Before assessing any upgrade, the freeze's own terms must bind the analysis:

▎ No new platform principles until the PB-004 retrospective. Clarifications legal, additions not. No further architectural expansion. C3's sole success criterion: can AST-010 help deliver PB-004 with greater determinism, traceability, and honesty than before?

R-29 (Foundation Complete, 2026-07-08):

▎ No platform change shall be made unless PB-004 or a subsequent feature demonstrates that the current platform is insufficient.

Exception clause: The singular exception to the freeze is demonstrated insufficiency — and the burden of demonstration falls on PB-004 itself, not on predictions, harvests, or audits. PB-004 has not yet been implemented through the platform. The RED tests exist; the feature's end-to-end traversal does not.

This analysis exists because the freeze demands it: if any gap is structural enough to be freeze's own exception clause would authorize an upgrade. If no gap meets that bar, thefreeze holds — and that is success, not inaction.
