## One final observation

You've reached an inflection point.

Up to now, your main risk was **building the wrong architecture**.

That risk has been reduced substantially through the work you've done.

From this point onward, your primary risk shifts to **execution**: consistently delivering features while preserving the architectural quality you've established.

That's actually a good place to be. It means the architecture should now serve the product, and every new implementation slice should strengthen confidence in both the software and the engineering process.
