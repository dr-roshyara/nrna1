## One caution

I would resist the temptation to immediately design for ten future products.

One of the strongest principles you've established is **"evidence before architecture."** That principle should continue to guide KnowledgeOS itself.

PublicDigit should remain the proving ground. The next major objective is not to imagine every future capability, but to build the smallest working KnowledgeOS platform that can successfully bootstrap **one additional, substantially different product**. If that succeeds, you'll have the evidence needed to extract truly reusable platform capabilities instead of speculative ones.
