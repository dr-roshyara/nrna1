## 🎯 **The Big Picture: 3 Checkpoints, 1 Goal**

When you vote on PublicDigit, your vote doesn't just zip into a database. It passes through **three independent security checkpoints** — each checking one specific thing. This design means:

- ✅ **Simple for voters** — You just vote; we handle security behind the scenes
- ✅ **Impossible to exploit** — Each checkpoint is independent (compromising one doesn't break the others)
- ✅ **Transparent** — You understand exactly what we're checking and why

## 🔍 **What Happens at Each Checkpoint**

### **Checkpoint 1: 🆔 "Is This Link Real and Yours?"**

**What we check:**
- ✅ **Valid link?** — Exists in our system (not fabricated)
- ✅ **Your link?** — Belongs to your voter ID (not someone else's)
- ✅ **Still active?** — Hasn't been deactivated or revoked

**Real-world example:**
```
✅ VALID:  You receive voting link, click it → ACCESS GRANTED
❌ INVALID: Someone sends you a fake link → "Invalid voting link"
❌ STOLEN: Someone screenshots your link, tries to use it →
           "This link belongs to another voter"
```

**Why voters appreciate this:**
- 🟢 Stolen voting links cannot be used (you're protected)
- 🟢 Only YOU can use your specific link
- 🟢 If your email is forwarded, the link is useless to others

**Why election organizers love this:**
- 🟢 Can revoke a voter's link if they're no longer eligible
- 🟢 Can verify that every voter who voted was authorized
- 🟢 Complete audit trail of who accessed voting

---

### **Checkpoint 2: ⏰ "Are You Voting at the Right Time?"**

**What we check:**
- ✅ **Session valid?** — Haven't exceeded 24-hour window
- ✅ **Election open?** — Voting period is active
- ✅ **Deadline met?** — Election hasn't closed

**Real-world example:**
```
✅ ON TIME:   You click link during voting window → ACCESS GRANTED
❌ TOO LATE:  You try to vote after election ends → "Election closed"
❌ EXPIRED:   You wait 25 hours to vote → "Session expired"
```

**Why voters appreciate this:**
- 🟢 Clear deadline (no surprises)
- 🟢 One-day voting window (reasonable, doesn't expire unexpectedly)
- 🟢 Know exactly when voting starts and stops

**Why election organizers love this:**
- 🟢 **Strict election window** — No votes outside official times
- 🟢 **Regulatory compliance** — Clear proof of when voting occurred
- 🟢 **Prevents disputes** — "I tried to vote after the deadline" rejected automatically
- 🟢 **Automatic security** — Links expire without manual intervention

---

### **Checkpoint 3: 🏢 "Are You Voting in Your Organization?"**

**What we check:**
- ✅ **Right organization?** — Your voter record matches the election's organization
- ✅ **Authorized?** — You're on the voter list for this election
- ✅ **No cross-org voting** — Company A voters can't vote in Company B's election

**Real-world example:**
```
✅ CORRECT:  Acme Corp employee votes in Acme Corp election → ALLOWED
❌ WRONG:    Acme Corp employee tries Company B's link → "Organization mismatch"
❌ HACKED:   Attacker tries to vote in multiple organizations → BLOCKED AT CHECKPOINT 3
```

**Why voters appreciate this:**
- 🟢 Your vote only counts in YOUR organization's election
- 🟢 No mixing of elections or voter lists
- 🟢 Complete data privacy — Your organization's voter list is isolated

**Why election organizers love this:**
- 🟢 **Absolute data isolation** — No cross-organization contamination
- 🟢 **Multi-tenant safety** — Multiple organizations on same platform, completely separate
- 🟢 **Prevents insider threats** — Employees of different companies can't access each other's elections
- 🟢 **Regulatory compliance** — Data separation documented and auditable

## 📊 **What Happens If You Fail a Checkpoint**

| Failed Checkpoint | Error Message | Why It Happened | What To Do |
|-------------------|---------------|-----------------|-----------|
| **Checkpoint 1** | "Invalid voting link" | Link is fake, stolen, or deactivated | Contact election admin for new link |
| **Checkpoint 2** | "Session expired" | Waited more than 24 hours | Ask admin for a new voting link |
| **Checkpoint 2** | "Voting period closed" | Election ended | Wait for next election or contact admin |
| **Checkpoint 3** | "Organization mismatch" | Used wrong election's link | Verify you have the correct link |

**Important:** All failed attempts are logged. If you see these errors, our support team can diagnose the issue.
