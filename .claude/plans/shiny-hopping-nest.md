## 3. Objectives & Planning-Question Answers

- **Q1 — What evidence indicates a potential bounded context?** Four independent evidence lenses, each grounded in accepted artifacts: **(L1) Seam evidence** — the authority seam ("nothing may cite a view as authority") and relationship polarity (item 1 Q3); **(L2) Language evidence** — UL shifts: where the same term changes meaning or distinct vocabularies operate (M0 glossary; F-BCP-4's three-sense "register" is itself a specimen); **(L3) Responsibility clustering** — F6×F1 classifications (M5) as *input-not-verdict*: co-occurring responsibilities suggest, never decide (post-fold, L3 also carries the O-M5-1 grading assumption explicitly); **(L4) Identity/namespace evidence** — M4's register-namespaces and identity modes: shared governed registers suggest cohesion; disjoint namespaces suggest seams.
- **Q2 — Which artifacts provide it?** L1: item 1 Q3/Q6 + M1 polarity rows · L2: M0 glossary + item 1 UL-collision registers · L3: M5 §4 · L4: M4 Part 1. Plus: the Work-item adjacent-domain recommendation (M5) as the one *pre-staged boundary question*, and the EPIC-002 Bounded_Context_Discovery precedent as the house method template.
- **Q3 — How are competing boundaries evaluated?** Per candidate: independent evaluation under each lens → **convergence scoring** (a candidate supported by ≥3 independent lenses is strong; single-lens candidates are weak by construction — the anti-bias core of this design) → **Boundary Confidence grading** (§5 model — convergence remains the candidate-*naming* threshold, but confidence weighs evidence quality: three weak lenses do not outrank two strong ones; **no vote counting**) → competing partitions compared with strengths/weaknesses/evidence-coverage → rejected partitions preserved with rationale and reopening triggers.
- **Q4 — How are false boundaries rejected?** Three falsification probes per candidate, each with its pass/fail condition declared *before* the probe is run: **(a) removal test** — hypothesize the boundary erased and enumerate what breaks, citing artifact + row: the probe FAILS the candidate if no named invariant, vocabulary rule, or authority rule actually breaks (no citable break → no boundary); **(b) linguistic probe** — for each term crossing the line, does its recorded meaning genuinely shift (different M0/M1 definition, identity, or violation semantics), or is it the same language administratively split? FAILS if no term genuinely shifts; **(c) cohesion probe** — do the concepts inside share lifecycle/identity/authority behavior (M4 evidence) more with each other than with concepts across the line? FAILS if cross-line affinity equals or exceeds in-line affinity for a majority of member concepts. **Failing ≥2 probes falsifies the candidate** — recorded with its probe evidence as a rejected partition, never deleted. Two further instruments modulate *confidence* (they never substitute for the probes): **(d) Boundary Stability Test** — remove one evidence *source* (an artifact, not a lens) and re-derive: if the candidate boundary does not remain, it depends on documentation rather than domain reality → confidence decreased and the dependency named; **(e) Emergence Verification** — for every boundary reaching recommendation: state why this boundary emerged from the evidence, why an equally plausible alternative did not, and what evidence change would flip the preference (anti-confirmation-bias; the flip condition doubles as the reopening trigger).
- **Q5 — UL influence:** language is lens L2 *and* the tiebreaker — where lenses conflict, the observed language wins over structural elegance (evidence-first).
- **Q6 — OQ-2/OQ-7 handling:** surfacing protocol armed from step one with **pre-identified trigger points**: OQ-2 (incumbent) is expected to bite when candidate boundaries touch AKB/EKP-designed-system territory — the discovery proceeds on the *practice-based* system (the accepted executive finding) and surfaces the incumbent question rather than deciding it; OQ-7 (three roots) is expected to bite when candidates align with `docs/`/`architecture/`/`engineering/` territory — territorial alignment is recorded as observation, placement never decided. Both go to the Surfacing Register with impact statements; unblocked discovery continues.
- **Q7 — Sufficiency criterion:** a bounded-context *recommendation* requires: ≥3-lens convergence · survival of all three falsification probes · Boundary Confidence (§5) graded Medium or above, with the grading shown · a passed Emergence Verification (why-this-not-that recorded) · an explicit UL statement for the context · named evidence for every included concept · a recorded competing partition. Anything less is recorded as a **candidate seam**, not a recommendation — and the demotion itself is recorded, with what was missing.

## 4. Discovery Workflow (repeatable; designed for reuse — each phase carries its acceptance criterion)

**Phase A — Preparation:** verify gates (M5 baseline confirmed + folds applied); load the four lenses' evidence tables; arm the Surfacing Register. *Exit:* G-M6-0 evidenced in the artifact header · all four lens tables loaded with source citations · OQ-2/OQ-7 trigger definitions (Q6) transcribed into the Surfacing Register before any evidence is read.
**Phase B — Evidence collection:** per lens, independently, *before* any candidate is named (prevents candidate-first bias): seam inventory · UL-shift inventory · responsibility-cluster map · namespace map. *Exit:* four inventories complete; every item carries source artifact + row; gaps recorded as gaps, never filled by inference; "register" senses disambiguated at collection time (R-M6-8).
**Phase B½ — Evidence characterization** *(reviewer refinement)*: for every collected evidence item, before any candidate exists, record **(a)** what it objectively says · **(b)** what it does *not* say · **(c)** plausible alternative interpretations · **(d)** confidence (M0 rubric). This preserves the **Observation → Characterization → Interpretation → Classification** chain and prevents jumping from evidence to architecture. *Exit:* no uncharacterized item enters Phase C; items whose (c) alternatives diverge materially are flagged for extra scrutiny in Phase D.
**Phase C — Candidate identification:** intersect the four inventories; name candidates only where lenses converge; record single-lens signals as seams-to-watch. *Exit:* every candidate names its converging lenses and the exact evidence items behind each; zero candidates sourced from a single lens; the seams-to-watch list is non-empty or its emptiness is justified.
**Phase D — Competing-boundary analysis:** for every candidate, construct at least one competing partition (finer, coarser, or differently-cut) and evaluate both under Q3. *Exit:* no candidate without a genuinely argued competitor — a strawman competitor is a Phase-D failure, not a pass; per-pair strengths/weaknesses/evidence-coverage recorded.
**Phase E — Falsification:** the three probes per surviving candidate (Q4 a–c), then the Boundary Stability Test (Q4 d). *Exit:* per-candidate probe table with declared pass/fail conditions and outcomes; every falsified candidate preserved as a rejected partition with its probe evidence; stability-test dependencies named.
**Phase F — Confidence & assembly:** Boundary Confidence per candidate (§5 model) · **Emergence Verification per recommendation (Q4 e)** · Surfacing Register · threats · the boundary-recommendation set with rejected partitions preserved. *Exit:* every recommendation meets the full Q7 bar; everything below the bar demoted to candidate seam with the gap named; no recommendation without its emergence statement.
**Phase G — Review:** checkpoint (Critical Review class — T-2 will apply and must be declared in the checkpoint header; external review remains the higher assurance tier, noted in advance) → authority disposition → consolidation. *Exit:* G-M6-2 → G-M6-3 → G-M6-4 in order.

## 5. Evidence Model

Every claim carries: epistemic class (M2 §10 convention) · source artifact + row · confidence · residuals. Every candidate carries: lens-convergence score · evidence-quality grade · probe results · stability-test result · competing partition · UL statement · reopening trigger · (if recommended) emergence-verification statement.

**Boundary Confidence** *(reviewer refinement — replaces bare ≥3-lens convergence as the confidence measure)*: per candidate, confidence is the composition **lens convergence × evidence quality × cross-artifact consistency × falsification survival**. Convergence keeps its role as the candidate-*naming* threshold (Q3); confidence additionally weighs **evidence quality**, graded on the M0 rubric and the M2 §10 epistemic classes (Observed outranks Derived outranks Synthesized) — **three weak lenses do not outrank two strong ones; no vote counting.** Cross-artifact consistency is informed by the Boundary Stability Test (Q4 d): a candidate that survives removal of any single source scores high; one that collapses scores low and names its documentation dependency.

**Hypothesis discipline:** every candidate is a hypothesis until it survives Phases D–F *and* the authority disposes; the artifact will say so per candidate.
