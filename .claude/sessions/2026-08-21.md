### The discriminator — one mechanical shape, and no other

A disposition row that is (a) **UNLABELLED** (no superseded marker), (b) **SINGLE-remedy** (carries no two-branch shape — including a trigger that names ONE branch), and (c) shares **≥2 distinctive SUBJECT tokens** with a line declaring the same disposition **SPLIT** elsewhere → **WARN** (`DI-4` — AMD5's §8 Phase-7 row vs §4.4's RD-3 split). The same row **LABELLED superseded** → quiet (AMD6's repaired §8 row); the same row carrying the **two-branch shape** → quiet (it IS the current split); a split declared for a **different trigger** → quiet (AMD4: the split is `INV-R1`, not the Phase-7 mismatch disposition).

### The falsification lesson — the discriminator was REWORKED against the real corpus

The plan-summary discriminator did NOT reproduce the §6 matrix: the first back-test run produced **8 spurious warnings** (3 on AMD5, 5 on AMD6). Every rework decision was pinned to a specific observed line, then locked in as a regression test:

- **Split declarations live in HEADINGS and TABLE ROWS, not prose** — §4.4's `### 🔴 ⭐ … IS SPLIT. One trigger, two causes…` heading (AMD5 line 500), the RD-3 register (line 120) and the phase-table row (line 382) declare the split; prose *"RC-1's split"* (line 581) is a **mechanism** split. ⇒ markers narrowed to `is split · split into · one trigger, two causes · never one branch` — the bare word **"split" is NOT a marker** — and splits are recorded from **all** non-fence lines (headings before section update, table rows in addition to buffering, prose).
- **Branch-boundary words are the split's branch DISCRIMINATORS, not its subject** — `writer · switch · case · pre · post · demotion · branch · both` in a row's trigger (*"before the Phase-5 WRITER switch"*, AMD5 §8:706) share nothing with the split's subject (*"the Phase-7 mismatch"*) ⇒ moved into `STOP_WORDS`.
- **Amendment citations carry no identity** — `amd4 · rc-3…` appear in triggers AND split texts (every row cites the amendment that introduced it) ⇒ excluded by pattern `/^[a-z]{1,3}\d+$/`.
- **AMD6's branch rows are constituents of the split, not competitors** — *"Phase 7, ONLY on a POST-DEMOTION disposition"* (line 840) is one branch of the split, so a row whose **trigger** names one branch is classed **two-branch** (trigger-side marker check, not remedy-only).
