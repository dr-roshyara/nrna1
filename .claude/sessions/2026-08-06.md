## Decisions

- **Source Code Editing Policy adopted** (`.claude/CLAUDE.md`): automation for discovery/verification; **source edits deliberate and manual** — no `sed -i`, `perl -pi`, or regex sweeps. Prompted by a rejected `sed -i`; justified in practice, since adjacent lines held `voting-election/`, `ElectionNavigation/` and a correctly-cased `@/composables/useMeta` that a broad pattern could have damaged. Recorded as a **KnowledgeOS candidate at n=1** (`docs/pks/2026-08-05-manual-code-editing-observation.md`), not a standard.
- **End of Commission checklist adopted** (`.claude/CLAUDE.md`) — session log · CONTEXT · plan · story commit · clean tree · next action. Mandatory, not advisory.
- **Commit ID convention:** product work always carries `(PBDIGIT-nn)`; chores say explicitly that no story applies. **Never attach a story ID to work that story did not cause.**
- **Discovered work becomes a backlog item, never a sentence in a reply** — `PBDIGIT-28` created rather than leaving 6 findings as prose advice.
- `PBDIGIT-28` deliberately **excluded** from PBDIGIT-27: those sites need *intent decisions*, and deciding intent inside a bug fix would be inventing.
