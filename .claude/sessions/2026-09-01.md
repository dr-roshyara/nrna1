### Findings

1. **`C0` fails its own baseline** — 21/25 capabilities, 9/16 scenarios. It holds no power to turn an
   observation into evidence, so it cannot produce a verdict, cannot validate, cannot represent
   uncertainty or assumptions. The missing power is `Qualify : Observation × Policy ⇀ Evidence`,
   which the prior corpus lane already carried as **`G1`**. Independent rediscovery of a known gap.
2. **`DetectGap` is derivable** — `Gap := difference-decision(norm-comparison(K_t, I_t))`, donors
   `Determine` and `Discriminate`, neither redefined, `SMUGGLING = FALSE`. Robust across **8/8**
   model variants, including one built to protect it. Agrees with the corpus's own `𝒪?`
   (derived-evaluation) classification of `DetectGap` from Question 15.
   **Confirms prior constraint 4 by experiment: `Zero(K_t, I_Q, EC)` is a predicate, not a primitive.**
3. **14 irreducible semantic powers, 12 irreducible operators.** The kernel is minimal in its
   epistemics and non-minimal in its *packaging*. Exhaustive search over all 16 384 subsets found
   exactly **two** minimal kernels, **both of cardinality 13**.
4. **Reduction beyond `DetectGap` STOPPED** (protocol's critical stop condition, invoked partially):
   `Interpret`/`Represent`, `Determine` and `Select` each flip status under a single defensible
   change of semantic granularity. The instability is in the granularity, not the count.
5. **7 of 7 smuggling probes restored full coverage** — reduction by absorption is always available
   and always worthless; any minimality claim without a smuggling test is unfalsifiable.
6. **The randomized property suite is non-discriminating for capability loss** (vacuity audit):
   removing `Validate`/`Qualify` drives P10's guard to 0.00. Only `Challenge` produces a genuine
   signal — P10 fails at .5944 with guard activation .5944, i.e. in 100 % of worlds where it can.
