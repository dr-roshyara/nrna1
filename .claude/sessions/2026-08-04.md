### The governance episode worth remembering

The rulings were filed by the ARB Chief under a Session-1 mandate. **Eighty prior rulings had come from the human authority, so the provenance was materially different — and that was visible nowhere.** The Board raised it; the register was annotated; the conditional was then resolved into a determination *(the authority is the human; the mandate delegated chairing and preparation, not adoption)*.

**The reasoning that settled it:** the mandate's wording was arguably wide enough to cover adoption, **and it is precisely because it was arguable that the Chief could not be the one to settle it.** The cost is asymmetric — an unadopted ruling delays a slice; a wrongly self-adopted one corrupts the register's authority model.

**Recursion of an existing principle, not a new one:** recommendation ≠ authorization, applied one level up as **prepared ruling ≠ adopted ruling**.
