## Commissions

### First — `SCB-1`…`SCB-9` Governance ruling report

* ⚠️ **COLLISION 1 — `SCB-1` as written contradicts an ADOPTED rule.** `SCB-1` forbids moving a window forward; **`EM-GOV-004` permits correcting an ELAPSED window — and an elapsed window can only be rescheduled LATER, never into the past. So every elapsed correction IS a forward movement.** Governance cannot let a commission position override an adopted rule, so `SCB-1` is recorded in **the only reading consistent with the record**: it forbids moving a window forward **at will**, not the rescheduling of an elapsed or halted phase through governed recovery. ⛔ **Without confirmation, `SCB-1` and `SCB-9` cancel each other and no correction is possible at all.**
* ⚠️ **COLLISION 2 — `SCB-4` answers a different question from the one it names.** Its text rules that **the LIFECYCLE may not return to a completed phase**; the boundary asks whether **a window's TIME may be moved earlier**. **The lifecycle half is ruled; the schedule half is untouched.** **Cross-consequence: the lifecycle ruling REMOVES *"restart from an earlier phase"* from `EM-OPEN-042`'s candidate recovery paths** — recorded so it is not re-offered later.
* ⚠️ **`SCB-7` raises the two hardest questions in the set.** **(7.1)** It **inverts the principle adopted yesterday** — `EM-OPEN-047` settled that the service reports and Election Governance decides electoral meaning; **`SCB-7` gives the Service Provider approve/reject power over an electoral act, on criteria the commission forbids inventing.** Two readings offered: a **service gate** (feasibility/contract) or a genuine **electoral veto**. **(7.2)** **An extension is a correction, and `EM-VOC-005` makes a correction create a NEW opportunity superseding the old — so an approved extension would supersede the opportunity under which votes were ALREADY CAST, and no adopted rule says those votes remain valid.** Either extension must be an express exception to `EM-VOC-005`, or the status of the votes must be ruled. **Must not fall to Architecture: it decides whether an election's votes survive its own extension.**
* **`SCB-5` RULED under express delegation** — *a correction affects only opportunities whose validity or timing is actually affected; unaffected later phases keep their schedules and opportunities; nothing is superseded merely because an earlier phase was corrected.* **Circularity broken in the correct direction: this COMPLETES `EM-GOV-013`'s clause rather than deriving from it.** Criterion offered for confirmation: *affected* = window overlaps the corrected window, or validity depends on the corrected phase's completion.
* ⚠️ **Consequence of `SCB-3` + `SCB-5` together, stated because neither says it alone: delays can NEVER be absorbed.** A later phase may not be shortened, so **a delay cannot be compensated by compressing a subsequent phase — every delay propagates forward through the whole schedule.**
* **`SCB-2`:** `SCB-7` identified as its **carve-out**, not a separate subject; **`EM-OPEN-027` substantially answered** — with extension permitted, an "end tolerance" becomes a **pre-authorized extension** rather than a new power. Not assumed: whether extension applies equally to non-voting phases.
* **`SCB-3`:** the word *"normally"* implies an **exception path defined nowhere** *(unlike `SCB-7`, where it is named)* — recorded as an open edge, not invented.
* **`SCB-6`:** confirmed as **DERIVED** from `EM-GOV-005`/`D-2` — it confirms rather than creates; stated explicitly so Architecture need not conclude it.
* **`SCB-8` kept OPEN with the gap stated precisely:** the Manifesto **references credentials three times and defines them nowhere** *(§2 scope line · `EM-OPEN-006` · `Q-TEN-2`)*. **No adopted rule says what a credential is, who issues one, when it is valid, or what invalidates it.** `EM-OPEN-006` must be resolved with or before `SCB-8`.
* **`SCB-9`:** authority not reopened; the twelve-step recovery model confirmed consistent; **the three open parts (notice · staleness · cause) untouched by this commission.**

* ⚠️ **Finding that must precede Q1–Q7: post-specific acceptance implies PER-POST PROGRESSION, and the adopted model has only ELECTION-WIDE progression.** `EM-GOV-010` progresses **the election**; `EM-GOV-009` fixes **phase** windows; `EM-VOC-004` ties an opportunity to a **published schedule**, and schedules are per phase. **Nothing adopted is scoped to a post.** **The fork: either acceptance is post-specific while progression stays election-wide — so one refused post halts everything and Q6/Q7 answer themselves — or progression itself becomes post-scoped, a substantial extension of `EM-GOV-010` AND of the opportunity model, since a per-post acceptance corresponds to no opportunity that currently exists.**

* ✅ **`EM-GOV-020`(a) finally CLOSES the sharpened `C-1`.** The Chief's confirmation is an **attestation of process**, not an approval of outcome — *"I conducted the required consultation, attempted common agreement, and these representatives were established through that process."* **The Chief neither selects the deciders nor approves them**, and is accountable for conducting the process fairly and documenting it. **Consistent with the programme's spine: the Chief acts; the rules decide.**
* ✅ **Governance checked and reports a useful separation: clause (b) — *"the Chief must not treat an unresolved objection as agreement"* — HOLDS UNDER ALL THREE readings of "common agreement", so it does NOT depend on `EM-OPEN-060` and could be adopted now.** One piece can move while the rest waits.
* ⚠️ **(c) The confirmation's availability cannot be specified yet** — what makes it available is precisely what `EM-OPEN-060` has not decided. **The enforcement follows the rule; the rule does not follow the button** *(the PO's own principle, applied to their own sentence)*.
* ✅ **(d) The protocol evidence list needs NO new rule** — `EM-GOV-005` + `P-2H` already require all of it, **objections included even where outvoted.**
* ⚠️ **The three readings have VERY different deadlock profiles, and this is the finding:** **①** unanimity — **one holdout deadlocks `Voting Preparation`, and since that phase gates Voting, a single candidate can stop the election** · **②** predefined majority — no deadlock, **but a minority is represented by someone it rejected** · **③** *no unresolved objection after consultation* — **NOT a third option until *"resolved"* is defined: if resolution needs the objector's assent it collapses into ①; if it means only that the Chief finished the process, it collapses into a formality with no substantive test.**
* ⚠️ **Prior sub-question surfaced: AFFECTED BY WHAT?** A candidate may accept the committee as a whole while objecting to **their own** representative — **two different scopes of objection, and the three readings behave differently under each.**

### Fourteenth — representation structure recorded; `EM-OPEN-060` largely DISSOLVES; two conflicts found

* 🟡 **`EM-GOV-021` PENDING:** each entitled candidate confirms **exactly one** representative · a representative may represent **one or more** candidates · **the committee consists ONLY of persons confirmed by at least one candidate** · a proposal nobody selects **lapses** · a candidate may propose another person if the pool has none suitable, subject to eligibility. ✅ **`Proposed ≠ Accepted ≠ Committee Member`.** ✅ **Property secured: the Chief controls the PROPOSAL process but cannot ENLARGE the committee unilaterally.**
* ✅ **`EM-OPEN-060` LARGELY DISSOLVES — the new mechanism removes its subject.** **When each candidate selects their OWN representative, there is nothing to object to in one's own representation — a candidate is satisfied by construction.** The confirm/object model existed because the **Chief** proposed *your* representative; **that is no longer the mechanism.** What survives: may a candidate object to **another's** representative *(the model grants no standing)*, and is any **cross-candidate agreement required at all**? **The earlier *"affected by what?"* sub-question is answered.** ⚠️ **The deadlock risk also changes: no longer a holdout objector but a candidate who SELECTS NOBODY — which is `EM-OPEN-056`, not an objection.**
* ⚠️ **`EM-OPEN-062` — the finding I would most want seen: does a representative carry ONE vote, or one per candidate represented?** **Worked from the rule:** ten candidates converge on **X**, one selects **Y** → committee of two. **Under one-vote-each, Y holds 50% while representing 1/11 of candidates, and X's ten are diluted to a single vote.** ⚠️ **`C-2` is TRANSFORMED, not resolved: the outcome no longer depends on how many candidates stood, but on how many happened to CONVERGE — a quantity nobody controls or predicts.**
* ⚠️ **`EM-OPEN-063` — CONFLICT between two pending rules:** `EM-GOV-017` fixes the **required number of representatives at application**, while `EM-GOV-021` **derives committee size from candidates' choices**. **Five required but two chosen fails through nobody's fault; twelve chosen exceeds a fixed number.** **A number fixed in advance cannot bind a set produced later by independent choices.** Four resolutions recorded, none endorsed.
* **`EM-OPEN-061` opened for representative eligibility** — the PO's six candidates, none endorsed, **plus one addition: eligibility is needed on BOTH paths**, or the Chief's pool could contain persons a candidate-proposed nominee would be barred from being.

### Fifteenth — representatives are for trust, not authority; two questions close, one bigger one opens

* 🟡 **`EM-GOV-022` PENDING — and only HALF of it is new.** ✅ **The *no-silent-dissent* half is ALREADY DERIVED** (`EM-GOV-005` + `P-2H`: objections are recorded even where they change nothing). **Only *no-veto-power* is new policy.** ✅ **Naming discipline applied: *"moral veto"* is NOT used** — *veto* implies authority to prevent; the business terms are **right to object · right to withhold acceptance · right to raise a formal concern.** ✅ **The principle in one line: `no veto power + no silent dissent`.**
* ✅ **`EM-OPEN-062` DISSOLVED — there is no representative voting weight**, so *"one vote or one per candidate?"* has no subject. **The ten-converge-on-X asymmetry disappears with it: X is one trusted representative, not a ten-vote bloc.** *(Returns the moment any counting threshold is applied to representatives — reasoning preserved.)*
* 🟡 **`EM-OPEN-063` RESOLVED IN SUBSTANCE — the fixed-number concept is dropped.** ✅ **The distinction that resolves it: *required representation* (fixed at application — every candidate has exactly one) is NOT *committee size* (an outcome of Voting Preparation).** **A committee of one is not a defect — it means every candidate trusts the same person.**
* 🟡 **`EM-GOV-023` PENDING and adoptable on its own:** *eligibility applies regardless of who proposes the person.* **Without it a candidate could bypass eligibility by proposing outside the pool.**
* ⚠️ **`EM-OPEN-064` — THE FINDING, and it is larger than the two it replaces.** **If representatives cannot prevent progression, what makes an acceptance GATE a gate?** **Earlier and agreed: non-acceptance HALTS progression, and publication may not occur without the required acceptance — both presuppose acceptance is a MANDATORY CONDITION.** `EM-GOV-022` now says representative participation confers no authority to prevent progression. **Both cannot be read at face value.** **Three readings, none endorsed:** the gate is real but **someone else decides it** *(name them)* · the gates are **not gates but recording points** *(which retracts the earlier "no publication without acceptance")* · the gate is real with a **threshold that objection cannot breach** *(then what fails it?)*. ⚠️ **The load-bearing word is *"considered"* — considered by whom, with what effect? If no effect, it is not consideration; if it can block, it is a veto.** ⛔ **This decides whether the two gates exist as gates.**

### Sixteenth — weighted representation; `EM-OPEN-062` reopens with an answer; `EM-OPEN-064` reduces to one word

* 🟡 **`EM-GOV-024` PENDING — weight(R) = number of candidates who selected R; thresholds apply to AGGREGATE representation votes.** ✅ **Committee size becomes irrelevant to the outcome, and *"6 of 10 representatives accepted"* is meaningless once weights differ.**
* ⚠️ **`EM-OPEN-062` REOPENS — and Governance's own caveat fired exactly as written.** It was dissolved hours earlier on the basis that representatives cast no votes, **with the recorded proviso *"it returns the moment any counting threshold is applied to representatives."* That is precisely what happened.** **The preserved reasoning is what makes the reopening clean rather than contradictory** — an argument for recording why something dissolved, not merely that it did.
* ⚠️ **`EM-OPEN-064` SHARPENS TO A SINGLE DISTINCTION.** **A threshold that can FAIL is a threshold that PREVENTS progression** *(60/100 under two-thirds fails → acceptance fails → `EM-GOV-011` halts progression)*, which contradicts `EM-GOV-022`'s *"no authority to … prevent the election from progressing."* ✅ **The PO's own sentence contains the fix: *"the representatives don't INDIVIDUALLY decide the outcome."* Individually no; COLLECTIVELY yes — that is what a threshold is.** ⛔ **Wording offered, not policy:** *"No individual representative may prevent the election from progressing. The aggregate outcome is determined by the predetermined threshold established in the Election Rules."*
* ⚠️ **`EM-OPEN-065` — the DENOMINATOR is undecided.** A candidate who selects nobody contributes no vote. **Is a two-thirds threshold measured against ALL entitled candidates or only against those who selected someone?** **Under the second, non-selection is an abstention that makes acceptance EASIER; under the first it behaves as an objection.** **The worked example (7 candidates → 7 votes) cannot distinguish them, because everyone selected someone.**
* ⛔ **`EM-OPEN-066` — is the *Election Committee* the existing chief-and-deputy committee, or a NEW body?** **(a) If chief+deputy, then *"Election Committee alone"* means THE CHIEF AND DEPUTY ACCEPT THE PROCESS THEY THEMSELVES CONDUCTED** — reinstating self-authorization far more strongly than `C-1`: **the Chief would not merely participate in the decision on their own request but constitute it entirely.** **(b) If new, it does not exist in the record and must be established.** **`EM-GOV-025` cannot be adopted until this is answered.**
* 🟡 **`EM-GOV-025` PENDING — participation model ≠ decision rule**, with representatives **not mandatory** for every election, and the PO's explicit non-assumption recorded: **committee votes are not simply added alongside candidate representation votes** *(5 committee members beside 100 candidate votes would be a completely different weight)*.
