# Session 2 — Governance · 2026-08-16

## Summary

Governance ruling report on the nine frozen schedule-correction boundaries (`SCB-1`…`SCB-9`). Six positions registered as rulings with provenance, one adopted under express delegation, one confirmed as derived, two left open — and **two structural collisions surfaced that must be settled before the set can close.**

## Commissions

### First — `SCB-1`…`SCB-9` Governance ruling report

* ⚠️ **COLLISION 1 — `SCB-1` as written contradicts an ADOPTED rule.** `SCB-1` forbids moving a window forward; **`EM-GOV-004` permits correcting an ELAPSED window — and an elapsed window can only be rescheduled LATER, never into the past. So every elapsed correction IS a forward movement.** Governance cannot let a commission position override an adopted rule, so `SCB-1` is recorded in **the only reading consistent with the record**: it forbids moving a window forward **at will**, not the rescheduling of an elapsed or halted phase through governed recovery. ⛔ **Without confirmation, `SCB-1` and `SCB-9` cancel each other and no correction is possible at all.**
* ⚠️ **COLLISION 2 — `SCB-4` answers a different question from the one it names.** Its text rules that **the LIFECYCLE may not return to a completed phase**; the boundary asks whether **a window's TIME may be moved earlier**. **The lifecycle half is ruled; the schedule half is untouched.** **Cross-consequence: the lifecycle ruling REMOVES *"restart from an earlier phase"* from `EM-OPEN-042`'s candidate recovery paths** — recorded so it is not re-offered later.
* ⚠️ **`SCB-7` raises the two hardest questions in the set.** **(7.1)** It **inverts the principle adopted yesterday** — `EM-OPEN-047` settled that the service reports and Election Governance decides electoral meaning; **`SCB-7` gives the Service Provider approve/reject power over an electoral act, on criteria the commission forbids inventing.** Two readings offered: a **service gate** (feasibility/contract) or a genuine **electoral veto**. **(7.2)** **An extension is a correction, and `EM-VOC-005` makes a correction create a NEW opportunity superseding the old — so an approved extension would supersede the opportunity under which votes were ALREADY CAST, and no adopted rule says those votes remain valid.** Either extension must be an express exception to `EM-VOC-005`, or the status of the votes must be ruled. **Must not fall to Architecture: it decides whether an election's votes survive its own extension.**
* **`SCB-5` RULED under express delegation** — *a correction affects only opportunities whose validity or timing is actually affected; unaffected later phases keep their schedules and opportunities; nothing is superseded merely because an earlier phase was corrected.* **Circularity broken in the correct direction: this COMPLETES `EM-GOV-013`'s clause rather than deriving from it.** Criterion offered for confirmation: *affected* = window overlaps the corrected window, or validity depends on the corrected phase's completion.
* ⚠️ **Consequence of `SCB-3` + `SCB-5` together, stated because neither says it alone: delays can NEVER be absorbed.** A later phase may not be shortened, so **a delay cannot be compensated by compressing a subsequent phase — every delay propagates forward through the whole schedule.**
* **`SCB-2`:** `SCB-7` identified as its **carve-out**, not a separate subject; **`EM-OPEN-027` substantially answered** — with extension permitted, an "end tolerance" becomes a **pre-authorized extension** rather than a new power. Not assumed: whether extension applies equally to non-voting phases.
* **`SCB-3`:** the word *"normally"* implies an **exception path defined nowhere** *(unlike `SCB-7`, where it is named)* — recorded as an open edge, not invented.
* **`SCB-6`:** confirmed as **DERIVED** from `EM-GOV-005`/`D-2` — it confirms rather than creates; stated explicitly so Architecture need not conclude it.
* **`SCB-8` kept OPEN with the gap stated precisely:** the Manifesto **references credentials three times and defines them nowhere** *(§2 scope line · `EM-OPEN-006` · `Q-TEN-2`)*. **No adopted rule says what a credential is, who issues one, when it is valid, or what invalidates it.** `EM-OPEN-006` must be resolved with or before `SCB-8`.
* **`SCB-9`:** authority not reopened; the twelve-step recovery model confirmed consistent; **the three open parts (notice · staleness · cause) untouched by this commission.**

### Second — `SCB-7` resolved in substance: schedule freeze + exceptional extension

* ✅ **`SCB-7.1` RESOLVED, and the inversion Governance flagged is removed.** The Service Provider performs a **trust-and-service check on exceptional use of the software**, not an electoral decision — so `EM-OPEN-047`'s principle survives intact. **Governance's supporting analysis: a REJECTION changes nothing electorally (voting simply ends at the published time, the default), so only APPROVAL is state-changing, and approval permits something the Election Rules themselves contemplate as exceptional. No electoral authority is exercised either way.**
* **`EM-GOV-015` recorded PENDING** — voting schedule **frozen** on voting start · exceptional extension via Service-Provider check · no general right established · full protocol · **votes already cast remain part of the election.**
* ⚠️ **`SCB-7.2` — the fix needs one more sentence than was supplied.** **Calling it an *"exceptional extension"* rather than a *"correction"* does NOT escape `EM-VOC-005`**, which attaches to **any correction to a published voting schedule** — and an extension changes the published schedule. **A definitional relabel is not a carve-out; only an express exception is.**
* ✅ **DERIVED argument for continuity, decisive rather than preferential:** `EM-VOT-005` makes authorization **opportunity-bound**, so **a new opportunity would require a fresh Chief authorization mid-vote — which nobody has given — and voting would have to STOP at the exact moment the extension was meant to let it continue.** **The new-opportunity reading is operationally incoherent, not merely undesirable.**
* ⛔ **But narrowing an adopted rule is the PO's act.** Draft sentence supplied for the PO: *"Notwithstanding `EM-VOC-005`, an exceptional extension approved after voting has started continues the existing voting opportunity and does not supersede it. Votes already cast remain valid within it."* → `EM-OPEN-051`.
* **`EM-OPEN-052` opened:** the freeze is **voting-specific** — **nothing says whether a started nomination, counting or results phase is likewise frozen.** The reason for the voting freeze (irreversible votes) has **no analogue** in most other phases, so *no* may be the right answer — **but silence is not an answer.**
* **Cross-boundary consequence recorded: the freeze OVERRIDES `SCB-2`'s general permission to extend, for voting after start** — confirming `SCB-7` as `SCB-2`'s carve-out, and strengthening it from *"no ordinary authority"* to a **freeze**.

### Third — two acceptance gates: report delivered, both BLOCKED on a missing object

* ⛔ **Blocking finding: the required participants do not exist in the governance record.** Both gates turn on *"which representatives must participate"*. **The only election bodies in the record are chief and deputy; there is NO adopted concept of an election representative, observer, scrutineer or acceptance participant** — no definition, selection rule, appointment authority or eligibility criterion. **This is the `SCB-8` pattern again: a rule cannot be written about an object the record does not define.** **Everything downstream — quorum, threshold, abstentions, non-participation, unavailability — COUNTS PARTICIPANTS, so all of it is unanswerable until the class exists.**
* ✅ **Several questions are already answered and need no new rule:** **non-acceptance halts progression** — a mandatory condition for the next phase is unsatisfied, so **`EM-GOV-011` applies unchanged** · **who resolves a failed acceptance** is the **recovery** question (`EM-OPEN-042`), and answering it here would decide that for one case · **publication without acceptance is impossible** once the gate exists (`EM-GOV-008`) · **the protocol list is already satisfied by `EM-GOV-005`** and its negative half · **objection ≠ non-acceptance ≠ fraud is `P-2H` one level up** — a recorded disagreement is an entry in the decision history, not a termination.
* ✅ **Placement derived: an acceptance gate is a phase-specific rule under `EM-GOV-010`③ — a precondition of the NEXT phase, not a component of the previous phase's completion.** **Consequence: voting that has finished HAS legitimately completed even if acceptance is refused; counting is blocked by a different condition.** Merging them would record voting as incomplete when it was not.
* ✅ **Naming instruction earns its keep:** calling the gates *phases* would drag in `EM-GOV-009` (schedule fixed at application — gates would need windows) and `EM-GOV-010`① (scheduled conditions — gates have no schedule). **"Gates" keeps both rules intact.**
* ⚠️ **Two findings specific to Gate 2:** **publication is IRREVERSIBLE** (`PBDIGIT-60`: immutable fact, visibility separate, never "unpublish") — **so the gate can only sit before it; a gate after publication would be decorative** · **`ADR-T11` binds the gate's own design: acceptance must be possible with NO voter↔vote linkage**, whatever participants must examine.
* **Conflicts checked, none real:** *only the chief may publish results* vs a gate conditioning publication — **the Chief still performs it; acceptance is a precondition**, exactly as `EM-VOT-004` already gates acts the Chief performs.
* **`EM-OPEN-053` opened — a question the commission did not ask:** the gates have **no schedule**, so **nothing bounds when an acceptance decision must be made; an election could sit indefinitely awaiting an acceptance nobody convenes.** Same shape as `EM-OPEN-046`; decide together.

### Fourth — acceptance participant & decision model: three undefined bodies, not one

* ⛔ **The blocking finding grew.** The previous report found **one** undefined object; working the participant model finds **three**, possibly four: **Election Commission** *(does not exist — only chief and deputy are governed bodies)* · **Acceptance Representative** *(no definition, class, appointment authority or eligibility rule)* · **Party** *(appears nowhere in adopted governance material — a party-based representation model would add a fourth undefined object)*. **Every participant, quorum and threshold question either names an undefined body or counts undefined participants.** ⛔ **Governance will not define an electoral body — that is a constitutional-level decision about who holds electoral standing.**
* ✅ **§9's Service-Provider boundary needs NO adoption — it is already in force** via `EM-GOV-006` *(only an Election Rule creates a governed period; not implementation convenience)*, `EM-OPEN-047`'s resolution *(service reports, Governance decides meaning)* and `EM-GOV-008`. **The one place the SP does hold a gate — the pending exceptional voting extension — is bounded and does not breach it, since a rejection changes nothing electorally.**
* ✅ **§7 is DERIVED, and it is the SAME problem as `EM-OPEN-050`(b) with the same answer:** the participant and threshold rules in force when an acceptance decision was made **remain bound to that decision**, exactly as a service-policy version binds when a recovery period begins. **One principle, two applications — cross-linked so they are not solved twice and differently.**
* ✅ **Objection-even-when-the-threshold-is-met is DERIVED:** the record must hold **objections that did not change the outcome** — precisely `P-2H`'s *"a successful request never erases the refusals that preceded it"*, applied to acceptance.
* ⚠️ **A concrete cost of the unsigned act surfaced:** §6 requires that failure not be assumed to mean cancellation/suspension/rescheduling/fraud/abandonment. **The rule securing exactly that is `EM-GOV-012` — PENDING. Until adopted, §6's protection rests on nothing but this report.**
* ⚠️ **`B-1` is NOT derivable:** `EM-GOV-009` fixes the **schedule** at application and says nothing about **participants**. **The analogy is strong; the extension is new policy and must be adopted, not inherited.**
* ⚠️ **Three consequences raised before any threshold is chosen:** **`C-1`** making the Chief a participant means **the Chief partly decides whether their own request is permitted** — it partially undoes *"Chief requests, Rules decide"* · **`C-2`** per-candidate "equal representation" makes the **outcome depend on how many candidates stood** (20-candidate post vs 2-candidate post) · **`C-3`** **non-nomination can deadlock quorum indefinitely** if quorum counts *required* participants — and nothing bounds when the decision must be made (`EM-OPEN-053`).
* **`EM-OPEN-054` opened, unasked by the commission:** **one acceptance per election, or one per post?** Acceptance of *the voting process* is naturally election-wide; acceptance by *candidate representatives* is naturally post-scoped. **The participant model cannot be specified without it.**
* **Next Governance question named: electoral standing — who holds the business right to participate, and from what source. Everything else is downstream.**
