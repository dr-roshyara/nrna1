## Commissions

### First — `SCB-1`…`SCB-9` Governance ruling report

* ⚠️ **COLLISION 1 — `SCB-1` as written contradicts an ADOPTED rule.** `SCB-1` forbids moving a window forward; **`EM-GOV-004` permits correcting an ELAPSED window — and an elapsed window can only be rescheduled LATER, never into the past. So every elapsed correction IS a forward movement.** Governance cannot let a commission position override an adopted rule, so `SCB-1` is recorded in **the only reading consistent with the record**: it forbids moving a window forward **at will**, not the rescheduling of an elapsed or halted phase through governed recovery. ⛔ **Without confirmation, `SCB-1` and `SCB-9` cancel each other and no correction is possible at all.**
* ⚠️ **COLLISION 2 — `SCB-4` answers a different question from the one it names.** Its text rules that **the LIFECYCLE may not return to a completed phase**; the boundary asks whether **a window's TIME may be moved earlier**. **The lifecycle half is ruled; the schedule half is untouched.** **Cross-consequence: the lifecycle ruling REMOVES *"restart from an earlier phase"* from `EM-OPEN-042`'s candidate recovery paths** — recorded so it is not re-offered later.
* ⚠️ **`SCB-7` raises the two hardest questions in the set.** **(7.1)** It **inverts the principle adopted yesterday** — `EM-OPEN-047` settled that the service reports and Election Governance decides electoral meaning; **`SCB-7` gives the Service Provider approve/reject power over an electoral act, on criteria the commission forbids inventing.** Two readings offered: a **service gate** (feasibility/contract) or a genuine **electoral veto**. **(7.2)** **An extension is a correction, and `EM-VOC-005` makes a correction create a NEW opportunity superseding the old — so an approved extension would supersede the opportunity under which votes were ALREADY CAST, and no adopted rule says those votes remain valid.** Either extension must be an express exception to `EM-VOC-005`, or the status of the votes must be ruled. **Must not fall to Architecture: it decides whether an election's votes survive its own extension.**
* **`SCB-5` RULED under express delegation** — *a correction affects only opportunities whose validity or timing is actually affected; unaffected later phases keep their schedules and opportunities; nothing is superseded merely because an earlier phase was corrected.* **Circularity broken in the correct direction: this COMPLETES `EM-GOV-013`'s clause rather than deriving from it.** Criterion offered for confirmation: *affected* = window overlaps the corrected window, or validity depends on the corrected phase's completion.
* ⚠️ **Consequence of `SCB-3` + `SCB-5` together, stated because neither says it alone: delays can NEVER be absorbed.** A later phase may not be shortened, so **a delay cannot be compensated by compressing a subsequent phase — every delay propagates forward through the whole schedule.**
* **`SCB-2`:** `SCB-7` identified as its **carve-out**, not a separate subject; **`EM-OPEN-027` substantially answered** — with extension permitted, an "end tolerance" becomes a **pre-authorized extension** rather than a new power. Not assumed: whether extension applies equally to non-voting phases.
* **`SCB-3`:** the word *"normally"* implies an **exception path defined nowhere** *(unlike `SCB-7`, where it is named)* — recorded as an open edge, not invented.
* **`SCB-6`:** confirmed as **DERIVED** from `EM-GOV-005`/`D-2` — it confirms rather than creates; stated explicitly so Architecture need not conclude it.
* **`SCB-8` kept OPEN with the gap stated precisely:** the Manifesto **references credentials three times and defines them nowhere** *(§2 scope line · `EM-OPEN-006` · `Q-TEN-2`)*. **No adopted rule says what a credential is, who issues one, when it is valid, or what invalidates it.** `EM-OPEN-006` must be resolved with or before `SCB-8`.
* **`SCB-9`:** authority not reopened; the twelve-step recovery model confirmed consistent; **the three open parts (notice · staleness · cause) untouched by this commission.**
