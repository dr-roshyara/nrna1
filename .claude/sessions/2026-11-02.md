## XD-PROV amendment AM-1 (later on 2026-11-02)

**Decision (Principal):** "A + B with rule 1 narrowed", recorded as XD-PROV §5a, PROPOSED (ARB approval needed: it changes Identity's deletion authority).
- The person may end any of their own relationships (`resignation` for Membership).
- The person may ask Identity to delete their own account and Person.
- The original importer may ask Identity to delete a **never-registered** imported Person, only with an importer capability (no central organisation record), after **complete** fail-closed discovery, subject to retention and internal authority.
- Option C was rejected: self-registered persons would be undeletable.

**New open questions:** OQ-PROV-5 (importer capability design), OQ-PROV-6 (complete discovery for deletion), OQ-PROV-7 (the Person's own deletion lifecycle).

**Refined after the Principal Reviewer's safeguards:** AM-1.5 (Identity is the only executor; request ≠ execution); incomplete discovery → pending or rejected; Membership ending ≠ affiliation lifecycle; capability and discovery marked as unresolved design proposals for ARB review (including replay and transfer risk).

Not committed.

## XD-PROV AM-1 r2 (later on 2026-11-02)

**Decision (Principal: accept the recommended set), recorded as PROPOSED:**
- deletion of a never-registered imported Person happens by **confirmed request**: the importing organisation initiates; P's explicit confirmation authorises; Identity alone executes.
- The confirmation replaces the importer capability.
- Organisation A delivers an Identity-issued single-use, expiring token to contact details it already holds; Identity stores no contact data.
- No response → reminders, then expiry; silence is never consent.
- Complete discovery is kept, to protect other organisations' identity links.

**Caveats recorded:** email control is not proof of identity; confirmation does not override retention.

**Open:** OQ-PROV-8 added (token design); OQ-PROV-5 largely obsolete.

**Refinement and confirmation (later):**
- Delivery goes to a **verified** email held by the importing organisation; OQ-PROV-9 added (verified-email evidence).
- Two steps of a later workflow proposal were not adopted, because they conflict with r2 and §1:
  - "verify the original importer";
  - "the system sends the notification".
- **The Principal confirmed r2 as recorded.** Still PROPOSED.
