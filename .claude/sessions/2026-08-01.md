**THE FOUR-LEVEL MODEL — adopted, and it is more than a taxonomy:**

**Business Policy (Q-2/ARB) → Architectural Invariant (ARB) → Mechanism (engineering) → Implementation (engineering).**

My two-level invariant/mechanism split was right but incomplete — it left unstated *where the invariant came from* and therefore *who may change what*. **The four-level version turns "is this substitutable?" from a judgement into a lookup**, and only **level 3 is substitutable**. **All of G-1 was a level-3 substitution, which is exactly why it dissolved without touching the model. Had the collision been at level 2, no engineering ingenuity would have helped and the honest answer would have been to return to the ARB.** The model tells you which situation you are in *before* you go looking for a clever fix.
