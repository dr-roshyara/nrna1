## Problems

* 🔴 **The safeguard the ruling relies on may be inoperative.** `confirmSuspension()` sets `status='inactive'`; the eligibility predicate and the server-side entry gate reject only `status==='removed'`; `suspension_status` is referenced **zero times** on the voting path. **A confirmed-suspended voter appears to pass both gates.**
  * Calibrated `OBSERVED IN CODE`, **runtime `NOT VERIFIED`** — all 20 `election_memberships` rows are `none / active`, so **no suspended voter exists to measure**, and creating one is a data change outside a read-only commission.
  * Aggravating: **`'inactive'` is overloaded** (`markAsVoted()` sets it too), so the predicate could not distinguish suspended from voted even if it tried; and **`confirmSuspension` never checks confirmer ≠ proposer**, so the four-eyes control the dual-actor signature implies is unenforced.
* **A second vocabulary collision.** `suspend` appears 6× in the Constitution and **every occurrence is election-level** (governance hold, with `resume`). `admit`, `revoke`, `restore` appear **0 times**. So the same verb means *hold the election* constitutionally and *suspend a voter* in the application. *(First collision: `capacity_eligibility`.)*
* **Full Membership mode remains unreachable** (`IERVP-2`, `IERVP-3`) — so **half the approved ruling cannot be exercised or tested.** The ruling raises those two blockers' priority.
* Two tracked review files (`2026-08-08-denominator-reconciliation.md`, `2026-08-08-election-test-universe-manifest.tsv`) show local modifications I did not make. **Left untouched — not mine.** Likewise the untracked files of unknown ownership.
* 🔴 **INCIDENT — I rewrote a Session 1 commit, and repaired it.** To fix a wrong commission count in this file I ran `git commit --amend`. **Session 1 had committed in between**, so `HEAD` was no longer mine: the amend folded my session-log edit into their commit `3150b2f6` and rewrote it to `e8edd4b1`.
  * **Repaired** with `git reset --mixed 3150b2f6`; their commit is byte-identical again (1 file, 144 insertions) and carries its **original hash**. Verified **nothing was pushed** — no remote branch contains `e8edd4b1`, and the remote is many commits behind.
  * **Cause: `--amend` assumes `HEAD` is still mine. On a branch two streams commit to, that assumption is false.** The concurrency is exactly what the Parallel Workstream Isolation block exists to protect, and I defeated it with a convenience command.
  * **Rule for this repository, adopted here:** **never `--amend`, `rebase` or `reset --hard` on `election-review` while a second stream is active.** A follow-up commit is always correct and never touches another stream's history. **A cosmetic fix is never worth rewriting shared history** — I should have left the wrong count and corrected it in the next commit, or simply committed the fix separately.
