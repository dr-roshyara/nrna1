## Problems

* 🔴 **The safeguard the ruling relies on may be inoperative.** `confirmSuspension()` sets `status='inactive'`; the eligibility predicate and the server-side entry gate reject only `status==='removed'`; `suspension_status` is referenced **zero times** on the voting path. **A confirmed-suspended voter appears to pass both gates.**
  * Calibrated `OBSERVED IN CODE`, **runtime `NOT VERIFIED`** — all 20 `election_memberships` rows are `none / active`, so **no suspended voter exists to measure**, and creating one is a data change outside a read-only commission.
  * Aggravating: **`'inactive'` is overloaded** (`markAsVoted()` sets it too), so the predicate could not distinguish suspended from voted even if it tried; and **`confirmSuspension` never checks confirmer ≠ proposer**, so the four-eyes control the dual-actor signature implies is unenforced.
* **A second vocabulary collision.** `suspend` appears 6× in the Constitution and **every occurrence is election-level** (governance hold, with `resume`). `admit`, `revoke`, `restore` appear **0 times**. So the same verb means *hold the election* constitutionally and *suspend a voter* in the application. *(First collision: `capacity_eligibility`.)*
* **Full Membership mode remains unreachable** (`IERVP-2`, `IERVP-3`) — so **half the approved ruling cannot be exercised or tested.** The ruling raises those two blockers' priority.
* Two tracked review files (`2026-08-08-denominator-reconciliation.md`, `2026-08-08-election-test-universe-manifest.tsv`) show local modifications I did not make. **Left untouched — not mine.** Likewise the untracked files of unknown ownership.

## Third commission — G-1 measured at runtime (authorised)

**Authorisation:** Product Owner — *"you are authorized to suspend a voter"*, one controlled voter membership, development environment. Commission was explicitly *measure only; do not implement, do not amend the Constitution, do not refactor `status`.*

**Result: [`docs/publicdigit/reviews/2026-08-12-g1-voter-suspension-enforcement.md`](../../docs/publicdigit/reviews/2026-08-12-g1-voter-suspension-enforcement.md)** · commit `d930cc9e`

* **G-1 was half right, and the wrong half was the headline.** A confirmed-suspended voter **is** refused the ballot — **HTTP 403** at every real slug route — but not by the gate the ticket examined. **The voting path has two predicates of opposite polarity and I had found only one:** `ElectionVotingController` allows unless `removed`; `User::isVoterInElection()` denies unless `active`. Suspension writes `inactive` → passes the first, fails the second.
* **What survives:** the **entry surface** does not enforce suspension — it reports `isEligible: true` and **issues a fresh `VoterSlug`**. `OBSERVED AT RUNTIME`.
* 🔴 **Enforcement is incidental.** Nothing expresses *"a suspended voter must not vote"*; it works only because suspension writes `inactive` and one predicate demands `active`. **So `G-5` is load-bearing — "clean up the `status` overload" would, if `status` were left `active`, silently delete the only enforcement of the Chief's authority.** That warning is now in the ticket.
* **`G-6` WITHDRAWN.** Four-eyes **is** enforced (`ElectionVoterController:342` → `canConfirmSuspension`); measured proposer-refused / second-actor-allowed. **I had read the domain method and never checked its caller.**
* **Cache-staleness worry withdrawn** — write-side invalidation exists and is correct (`ElectionMembership::booted()`).
* **Acceptance criteria relabelled** as *consequences of the ruling*, not established constitutional rules, at the Product Owner's instruction.
* **New: `PBDIGIT-69`** — the voter-eligibility cache key is **tenant-unaware**. Measured both directions: a wrong-context read serves `false` to the correct context for **300s**; a correct-context read serves `true` across a **tenant boundary**. **This is the persistence mechanism behind `PBDIGIT-65`** and very likely behind the original *"not eligible"* report — it explains why the symptom was intermittent and self-healing.

**Method note worth keeping:** the control run was what validated the setup. Had I not set `TenantContext` explicitly, the whole experiment would have re-measured `PBDIGIT-65` and I would have "confirmed" G-1 for the wrong reason.
