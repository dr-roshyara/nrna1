The three-level formulation was folded in: **L1 governed terms — never paraphrase · L2 normative rules — never paraphrase independently, amend the source · L3 explanatory prose — may paraphrase if meaning is preserved and the paraphrase is marked.**

# Thirty-first entry (same session) — Rules 14/16/17 adopted, Rule 15 declined on the Authority's own standard

## Summary

The Chief Architect issued Rules 14–17 **and attached a caution**: new review dimensions should be adopted only where they repeatedly distinguish classes previously conflated, and confidence and evidence-origin specifically *"only if they demonstrably improve review quality over multiple review cycles."* **The caution applies to the rules being drafted, so I applied it to them** — which resulted in declining one of the four.

## What was adopted, and why

**Rules 14 and 17 — adopted without reservation**, because **neither adds taxonomy.** Rule 14 (declare scope; verdicts apply within it) and Rule 17 (category-first summaries) are *reporting* rules for Rule 9's already-adopted dimension. Supporting evidence: **§7.1 had already adopted the category-first presentation spontaneously**, before Rule 17 existed — the natural-presentation argument in its strongest form.

**Rule 16 — adopted on one cycle's concrete yield**, with the multi-cycle standard **recorded as not met**, justified because applying it produced a finding no other dimension surfaced (below).

## What was declined

**Rule 15 (per-finding confidence) — n = 0.** Across four review cycles and three self-audits, **not one case exists where a confidence label would have changed a classification, severity, or routing.** Every apparent instance had a structural cause exposed by a different dimension: IBC-M11 by **authority basis**, IBC-S2 by **resolving act**, IBC-m3 by **category**, AFV-F1/F3 by **external evidence** (the strategic model's own characterization columns).

Recorded fairly in **PMR-6**: the theoretical case is real — a reviewer's only current outlet for uncertainty is lowering *severity*, which is the very conflation Rule 9 prevents. But theoretical is not evidence. Also flagged: a **duplication risk**, since the program already grades confidence for *domain* claims (M0's rubric, MCR-5), and **a finding's correctness is largely determined by its authority basis**, leaving little room for a middle grade. A re-assessment trigger is named.

**PMR-6 is the first candidate in the register assessed and *declined* on the Authority's own evidential standard rather than merely held pending assessment.**

## What Rule 16 surfaced — uncomfortable in a useful way

Evidence origin across the 21 findings: **0 reviewer-knowledge · 6 artifact-local · 15 cross-artifact.**

The good result: **zero findings rest on reviewer knowledge**, so the review is reproducible by anyone with repository access — the property Rule 16 exists to establish, and it holds.

The uncomfortable one: **only 6 of 21 findings are artifact-local. IBC-1 read in isolation yields under a third of them.** That is a fact about the *contract*, not the review — **a boundary contract should be checkable against itself, its own Section 11 presupposes exactly that, and this one largely is not.** The cause is the same systemic weakness already found: it states categories where the record states content. **Rule 16 independently corroborates that finding by an entirely different route.**

**A consequence I had not previously stated:** conformance criteria evaluable only by a reviewer holding fifteen other artifacts are not criteria an implementation team can self-assess — so Recommendations 4–8 would raise the artifact-local proportion substantially, a benefit the review had not credited.

## Rule 14 applied

**Scoped verdict now explicit: Architecture PASS · Governance REVISE · Methodology OBSERVATION · Documentation REVISE · Implementation OUT OF SCOPE.** The unscoped "REVISE" invited the reading *architecturally wrong*; scoped, a revising author knows which sections to touch and which to leave alone.

## Decisions

None. Three rules adopted, one declined with evidence, one candidate registered, one review re-scoped without changing any verdict.
