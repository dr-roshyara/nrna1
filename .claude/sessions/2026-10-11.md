## Voting: `VerifyVoterSlug` guard (PBDIGIT-273) and the voter-slug backlog

### Summary
`VerifyVoterSlug` was hardened test-first, without a database, after plan approval and the Principal Reviewer's conditions. It is not committed.

### Completed
- **Read-only exploration** (3 agents) of the middleware chain, the slug lifecycle and test-database safety.
- **Plan** `docs/plans/20261011-1318-verifyvoterslug-hardening-plan.md`.
- **The guard:**
  - V-1 auth before lookup;
  - V-2 route type via `election.demo`, no fallback between types;
  - V-3 owner;
  - V-5 soft-deleted / inactive / `status=expired`;
  - V-4 `>=` boundary plus the cap `created_at + voting.time_in_minutes`;
  - V-6 election, non-null equal organisation, type;
  - logs and context without the raw slug.
- **Tests:** `tests/Unit/Http/Middleware/VerifyVoterSlugTest.php` (31 tests, database-free). The request-path test and the rewritten `ConstitutionalVotingProtectionTest` case are written, not run.
- **Independent review:** no blockers; three should-fix items handled or narrowed.
- **Documentation:** guide `developer_guide/election/voter_slug/`; backlog `PBDIGIT-273` … `278`. 276 was triaged and split; the `MiddlewareChainTest` path claim was refuted.

### Decisions
Human PO, 2026-10-11:
- the slug lifetime is fixed from creation, with the `>=` boundary on all steps;
- the cap is enforced in the guard now;
- scope is the core only;
- replacement is a new row (migration PROPOSED);
- replacement happens only at the voting entry;
- slugs are election-specific: only an entitled voter of that organisation and election receives one;
- **request-path and integration tests need an isolated test database.** The earlier `nrna_test` answer is withdrawn after the review.

### Problems
- Building an Eloquent `User` runs schema-introspection queries; the test uses `GenericUser`.
- The RED runs of the old middleware ran read-only `SELECT`s on `nrna_test`.
- `start()` reuses old or soft-deleted rows, which the guard rejects, so **deploy only with `PBDIGIT-274`**.

### Next Steps
- Principal Reviewer review → Human PO acceptance → commit authorisation.
- Isolated test database (needed for the request-path tests and for `PBDIGIT-274`).
- `PBDIGIT-274` plan, including approval of the migration.
