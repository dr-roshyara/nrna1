## Completed — WP-4 Integration Readiness Review
**Report:** `engineering/verification/reports/2026-07-31-wp4-integration-readiness-review.md` (7 deliverables).

- **The decisive fact, verified in code:** what crosses the context boundary is **`InboxMessage` carrying a raw payload array** — never a domain object, never the producer's event class. `DeterminationIssuedReactionHandler::handle(InboxMessage)` imports **only** its own context + Shared; a repo-wide grep for consumers importing a producer's `Domain\Events` returns **zero**. And the rule is **machine-enforced**, not aspirational: Deptrac treats cross-context as a violation by omission, in fail mode since PB-007 7D.
- **The producer-side hydrator's true role clarified:** `OutboxEventProcessor:141` is the **only** production caller of `hydratorFor()` — it reconstructs the producer's own event *inside the producing context* for event-bus dispatch. `ChallengeRoutedHydrator` therefore never crosses into the consumer, which is exactly why no consumer imports it. It is producer-side infrastructure, not a shared contract type.
- **Published-language review (unmodified):** no producer leakage · no consumer information embedded · still minimal (three facts, pinned by test) · context-neutral. **Observation, not a defect:** no current consumer needs `routedTo` — it is **producer-justified, not consumer-driven**, which is the correct reason for a published fact to exist.
- **Consumer responsibility map produced** (ownership, not classes): 8 of 10 responsibilities already exist — PM · local `ChallengeRef` · store · **both** duplicate-handling seats (inbox dedupe **and** PM idempotency) · `fromConsumed` · transport/ordering/audit continuity. Two are WP-4's.
- **THE PREREQUISITE (G-2) — the one finding that is not merely unwritten code:** PB-005's **F-1 ruling** binds — *the Domain never names messaging outcomes*; business conditions translate to inbox markers at one Application boundary. Contestation satisfies it (`ChallengeReactionOutcomeTranslator` + markers). **Adjudication has no equivalent**, and its PM entry points are `void` idempotent no-ops returning nothing a translator could read. WP-4 must decide that seam **before** writing the handler — because a PM taught messaging vocabulary cannot be un-taught without rework. No new ADR, no redesign: the rule and the precedent both exist.
- **5 risks recorded**, each with the mitigation already in place (Deptrac catches the classic first-integration mistake at the gate, not at review; "Registration ≠ Delivery" keeps the producer ignorant of consumers; the minting allowlist prevents a second mint).
- **Verdict: READY WITH ONE PREREQUISITE** — G-2 decided as WP-4's first act.

## Completed — Cross-Context Integration Contract Audit (six phases; contract only, no code)

**Contract:** `docs/architecture/Cross_Context_Integration_Contract.md` — marked **DESCRIPTIVE** (derived from implementation, not invented); elevating any clause to normative status is a separate decision it does not take.

- **Phase 1 — all chains, not one.** Exactly **three** consumers exist (`implements InboxHandler`): Election←`DeterminationIssued` · Contestation←`DeterminationIssued` · Contestation←`ElectionCorrectionApplied`. All three take `handle(InboxMessage)`.
- **Phase 2 — nine invariants discovered with evidence (R-1..R-9)**, incl. the two that matter most: **R-1** no consumer imports another context's `\Domain` (exhaustive 4×3 context-pair scan: **zero**) and **R-2** no consumer imports another context's **Infrastructure either** — `grep "Hydrator"` in consumer Application layers: **zero**. **R-3:** the hydrator is **producer-side** — `OutboxEventProcessor:141` is its *only* production caller. R-1 is **machine-enforced**: Deptrac treats cross-context as violation-by-omission, fail mode, 0 violations. The contract is already executable architecture.
- **Phase 3 — boundary object lifecycle** drawn end-to-end, showing precisely where each representation lives and that **only the payload + `InboxMessage` cross**.
- **Phase 4 — dependency verification: ZERO violations, zero architectural debt** across all three chains.
- **Phase 5 — THE AUDIT'S MOST VALUABLE CATCH.** The proposed contract answer *"Who reconstructs? Consumer (hydrator → local VOs)"* is **wrong on the mechanism**. Consumers reconstruct **directly from payload primitives** (`DeterminationId::fromString($this->stringField($message->payload, …))`) and **never touch a hydrator**. Had WP-4 followed the proposed answer, Adjudication would have imported `Contestation\Infrastructure\Outbox\ChallengeRoutedHydrator` — the codebase's **first cross-context Infrastructure dependency**, failing Deptrac. The audit prevented exactly the class of error it exists to prevent.
- **Second correction:** a local `RoutedTo` VO was proposed; **rejected** — `routedTo` is a plain producer string and **no consumer needs it** (`openFor()` takes only the challenge identity). Creating it would be a component without authority.
- **Phase 6 — the contract**, answering all eight governing questions; the sharpest clause: *what may never cross* now explicitly includes **the producer's Infrastructure, including hydrators**.
- **Consequence: WP-4 introduces no new architectural concept** — one `InboxHandler` + its registration, the fourth instance of an established, machine-enforced pattern.
