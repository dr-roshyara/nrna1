### The design in brief

**Carrier:** measurement records `(v, s, t)` — magnitude / provenance / time, **three separable kinds
of structure**, so reduction can destroy them independently.

**`Q` defined BEFORE the transformations** (§5): `(argmax_source, decile(total))` — **two components on

rather than an all-or-nothing flip.

**Chain, each consuming the previous output:** `T5` drop timestamps → `T4` round to 3 s.f. →
`T3` round to 2 s.f. → `T2` replace value by within-case **rank**.

### Two design decisions worth flagging

**1. Non-monotonicity is made POSSIBLE, not assumed.** Rounding creates **ties**; ranks cannot. So
`argmax_source` may fail at `R3` and **recover** at `R2`, while the decile component degrades
monotonically. **The design admits `H-C`; if adequacy turns out monotone, `H-C` dies — which is the
point.**

**2. The fixed decoder `O` is deliberately naive** — it always sums the numeric field. At `R2` that
field is a *rank*, so `Σ rank` is not a magnitude and `O`'s decile must fail **while `O*` may still
exist**. **This is the mechanism that makes the OPERATOR boundary observable separately from the
representation boundary** (§23 of the handoff).
