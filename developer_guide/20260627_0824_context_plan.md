## 1. ElectionConstitution ("God Aggregate")

This is the one thing that makes me uncomfortable.

The diagram labels

```
ElectionConstitution

(God Aggregate)
```

From a DDD perspective, I would avoid using the term **God Aggregate**, even informally.

A God Aggregate is usually considered an anti-pattern because it implies:

- excessive responsibility,
- poor scalability,
- broad transactional boundaries.

From your earlier architectural discussions, my understanding is that this is a **conceptual constitutional authority**, not an aggregate that will be implemented with transactional semantics.

I would rename it to something like:

- Constitutional Root
- Constitutional Authority
- Governance Constitution
- Constitutional Source of Authority

That better reflects its role and avoids confusion during implementation.
