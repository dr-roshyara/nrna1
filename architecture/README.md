# `architecture/` — the Architect's Workspace (Think)

> Part of the **Engineering Knowledge Platform (EKP)**. During the transition this folder keeps its role.

This tree is **working / exploratory** knowledge — architecture thinking, discovery, AI collaboration, evaluations, brain-storming, and future ideas. In EKP terms its documents are mostly `status: draft | discovery`, `authority: provisional`.

| Folder | Role | Typical lifecycle |
|---|---|---|
| `architecture/` (here) | **Think** — exploration & drafts | draft / discovery |
| `docs/` | **Official Truth** — ADRs, decisions, baselines | approved / baseline |
| `developer_guide/` | **Build** — operational guides | authoritative (operational) |
