## 2. Facts MCA-R2 must have, none of them resolved here

### 2.1 All three are CONTROLS — so the strengthened filter applies as written

**Each mandates an activity** *(establish applicability · verify the source · check the token)*. **They are not vocabulary, taxonomy or status elevations.** ***So unlike MCA-R1's candidates, no applicability adaptation is needed: the escape-based filter applies to all three directly.***

> **The governing filter: *a control becomes GOVERNED only after REPEATED operational evidence that its absence let defects ESCAPE.***

### 2.2 ⚠️ The question the counts do not answer — **ESCAPE vs COUNTERFACTUAL**

**The filter requires evidence that the control's absence let defects ESCAPE. Not every instance on the record is an escape:**

| Instance | Was it an ESCAPE, or a defect CAUGHT WITHIN the act? |
|---|---|
| **PMR-8's datum** *(MCA-R1)* | *The false rejections were **prevented**, not observed. Is a counterfactual an escape?* |
| **PMR-9's data** *(CON-1 · MC-1)* | *CON-1's mis-framing was caught by the **Authority**, i.e. by a later stage. MC-1's candidate was caught **within** the act* |
| **PMR-10's data** *(AFV · MCA)* | *The AFV collision **blocked an Authority recommendation**. The MCA collision **published a false claim*** |

***This is the assessment's central question and is expressly NOT answered here: does an instance count toward the filter when the defect was prevented or self-caught, or only when it ESCAPED the stage that owned the check? The filter's wording says "escape"; the register's counts do not record which instances were escapes.***

### 2.3 ⚠️ The circularity, and its resolution

**Resolution already determined (Authority, 2026-07-31):** ***"An MCA assessing PMR-8 or PMR-9 doesn't have to USE those proposed rules as binding procedure. It can evaluate them under the EXISTING certified methodology, treating them as HYPOTHESES."***

> **The assessment's question is therefore: *would adopting PMR-n improve the methodology, based on evidence gathered under the CURRENT methodology?***

**And the boundary CDR-R1 §1 established governs here too: an assessment may INTERPRET an existing control's scope (lawful); it may not MANDATE that interpretation as procedure (which is what PMR-8 proposes).** ***MCA-R2 may read the filter; it may not apply PMR-8 to PMR-8.***

### 2.4 ⚠️ RESIDUAL: the evidence is not procedure-neutral

**All three candidates' evidence was GATHERED in acts that already applied the proposed ordering** — *MCA-R1 applied applicability-before-evidence; CON-1 and MC-1 applied verify-before-classify; the MCA collision was found by a collision check performed after the fact.*

> ### ***So the ASSESSMENT can be procedure-neutral while the EVIDENCE cannot. MCA-R2 must state how it weighs evidence produced by the very practice under assessment.***

### 2.5 The filter's known blind spot, carried forward unresolved

**MCA-R1 §1 recorded, and no act has since resolved: *the filter measures ONE kind of value — defect prevention. A candidate whose value is comprehension, onboarding or scale is invisible to it.*** *Whether that is a correct narrowing remains a methodology-of-methodology question no assessment has authority to settle.*

### 2.6 Interactions between the three

| | |
|---|---|
| **PMR-8 ⟂ PMR-9** | **Both are ORDERING rules from the same party on the same shape of insight** — applicability-before-evidence *(assessments)* · source-verification-before-classification *(reviews)*. **Deliberately unbundled because CDR-R1 established that bundling distorts evidence counts (PMR-7 had to be split)** |
| **PMR-9 ⟂ RET-3** | *RET-3's "verify support before calling it a defect" is verification before **assertion**; PMR-9 is verification before **classification**.* **Whether RET-3 supplies a further datum for the stronger claim is an ASSESSMENT question** |
| **PMR-10 ⟂ the AFV backlog item** | **Orthogonal: the backlog item is BACKWARD-looking normalization (by alias only, since renaming is forbidden); PMR-10 is FORWARD-looking prevention.** *Only the forward-looking rule changes future practice, which is why only it is a PMR* |
