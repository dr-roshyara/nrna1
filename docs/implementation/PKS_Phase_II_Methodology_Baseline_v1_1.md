## 4. ⚠️ The Authority's item 3 is BLOCKED by §1, and the block is structural

**The instruction was: *"Run AFV-1 against the amended baseline… it checks whether the adopted text was incorporated faithfully and without unintended strengthening."***

**Three obstacles, each of which must be resolved by the Authority rather than by me:**

### 4.1 A naming collision across three distinct things

| Token | What it actually is |
|---|---|
| **AFV-1** | **A PROMOTED ARTIFACT** — the Architecture Fidelity Verification of the Model → Architecture transformation. ***It cannot be "run"; it is a finished record*** |
| **An AFV-CLASS verification of v1.1** | **A NEW act** — does v1.1 carry the adopted text faithfully? *This is what item 3 appears to intend* |
| **The PMR-2 discharge verification** | **A THIRD, unrelated act** — are AFV-1's four interpretive over-strengthenings instances of composite-confidence inheritance? |

***Three acts share the "AFV" token and only one of them is the artifact named. Commissioning "AFV-1" would be ambiguous across all three.***

### 4.2 There is no "incorporated text" to verify against

**A fidelity verification compares a derived text to its source. §1 establishes that the baseline is a DEFINITION BY REFERENCE, not a text.** ***What §2 above contains is the adopted wording itself, quoted from the register — so a verification of it would compare the register's text to the register's text.*** **That is not a fidelity check; it is a tautology.**

**A genuine AFV-class verification of v1.1 becomes possible only if the baseline is first CONSOLIDATED into a text.** **⛔ CORRECTED 2026-07-31: the reason given here — *"a structural change beyond this act's authority"* — was FALSE (see §1). Consolidation is AUTHORIZED as administrative per CDR §4.2; it is simply UNCOMMISSIONED.** ***The blocking fact is a missing commission, not a missing power — and the Authority's ruling that consolidation must not be undertaken merely to enable a verification stands unaffected, since it concerns the REASON for consolidating rather than the POWER to consolidate.***

### 4.3 What is verifiable now, and what is not

| Verifiable today, without consolidation | Requires consolidation first |
|---|---|
| **Did v1.1 add exactly the three adoptions and nothing else?** *(a completeness/scope check — and §2/§3 are written to make it checkable)* | **Was the adopted text incorporated faithfully into a baseline document?** |
| **Did v1.1 adopt anything by implication that the CDR deferred?** *(§2.3 addresses this explicitly)* | **Does the consolidated baseline strengthen anything beyond its sources?** |

> **RECOMMENDATION, offered and not acted on: decide whether SDM/EOP should be CONSOLIDATED into a baseline text. If yes, that is its own controlled-change act, and an AFV-class verification of it follows naturally. If no, item 3 collapses into the scope check in the left column, which is cheap and can be commissioned immediately.**

***I have not chosen between these. Choosing would be restructuring the baseline on my own authority — which is precisely what §1 says this act may not do.***
