## 5. Strategic Relationship Classification (Phase 2 — pattern, direction, evidence, confidence)

### R-1 — CBC-3 seam (Norm Custody half) → CBC-1: **UPSTREAM/DOWNSTREAM DEPENDENCY — criteria supply; PATTERN UNDETERMINED** (seam upstream) *(DAR-1: VF-1 accepted — the Customer/Supplier name is withdrawn because the pattern's negotiation condition is unevidenced; the live alternative is ACL-shaped consumption. Direction, evidence, grade, and U-1 unchanged.)*

**Observed:** CBC-1's own UL statement imports its criteria: *"criterion (a Rule or Invariant, used here, owned elsewhere)"* (M6 §7.4). The violation semantics that make a criterion meaningful are defined norm-side (L2-5: Rule-violation = governance violation; Invariant-violation = defect). **Why not Conformist:** CBC-1 does not adopt the norm *model* — it consumes norms as assessment inputs and wraps them in its own language (degree, Verdict, Finding); the model boundary stays intact. **Why not Shared Kernel:** no shared model element is co-owned; ownership is explicitly one-sided. **Confidence: Medium-High** (verbatim UL + verified violation-semantics evidence) — *provisional in one respect: the upstream partner is a candidate seam, not an accepted context (uncertainty marker U-1).*

### R-2 — CBC-1 → CBC-3 seam (Authorization Acts half): **CUSTOMER/SUPPLIER over a PUBLISHED LANGUAGE** (CBC-1 upstream supplier of evidence; authority downstream customer)

**Observed:** *"knowledge as input to authority, never as authority"* (P-8, verified); Verdicts *"issued by a gate or review, never by the author"* (M0 G-15, corrected citation per §14 Amendment 2); the Verdict token set is a closed-ish vocabulary — PASS · PASS AFTER CORRECTION · WARN · FAIL · INCONCLUSIVE · EMERGENT · CERTIFIED (item 1 K-15; L2-16). **The token set is the Published Language:** authority acts consume assessment outcomes through exactly this vocabulary and nothing richer. **The constitutional nuance is part of the pattern:** the supplier *informs* and never *binds* — evidence wins over memory, but authority alone disposes; the downstream customer retains full decision power. **Confidence: Medium-High.** *(U-1 applies: the downstream partner is the seam.)*

### R-3 — every knowledge source ({CBC-1 · CBC-3 seam · core}) → CBC-2: **CONFORMIST** (CBC-2 downstream), with a **CONSTRAINT: the relationship is unidirectional by constitutional rule — no return path exists in the authority/evidence direction** *(DAR-1: VF-2 accepted — the "Separate Ways in the authority direction" label is withdrawn as a pattern-usage defect; a pair cannot be simultaneously Conformist and Separate Ways. The underlying rule, its evidence, and its significance are unchanged; Separate Ways is reserved for R-5.)*

**Observed:** CBC-2 conforms totally to its sources: *"recomputed from sources, never hand-edited to disagree"* (item 1 Q4); *"if a diagram and a frozen artifact ever disagree, the artifact wins and the diagram is corrected"* (item 1 Q7.2); members carry **no independent semantic identity** (L4-8) — a Conformist so complete it holds no model of its own to corrupt. **The return path is constitutionally closed:** *"nothing may cite a view as authority"* (item 1 Q3.1, verified) — no context may consume CBC-2's outputs as evidence or authority. That prohibition is itself a modeled relationship fact: **a constraint making the relationship unidirectional**, by standing rule, not by accident. **Why not Open Host Service:** CBC-2 publishes renderings for *humans*, not a service model for contexts; no context builds on its outputs (may not, by rule). **Confidence: Medium-High** (the strongest verbatim-rule base in the model).

### R-4 — {CBC-1 · CBC-3 seam} ↔ CBC-4 Work Management (across the domain edge): **CROSS-EDGE DEPENDENCY — no coordination pattern asserted**, with an **unassigned translation obligation** *(DAR-1: VF-3 accepted on ground 1 — the Customer/Supplier name is withdrawn because the negotiation condition is unevidenced. Direction, evidence, the DoD interchange terms, the Medium grade, and U-2 unchanged. Ground 2 — whether Evans patterns may be asserted across a disposed domain edge — was expressly not adjudicated.)*

**Observed:** the 14-box DoD makes **five knowledge obligations conditions of a work item's completion** (K-13; M6 §7.4 — the coupling the Disposition ordered M7 to model). Direction is asymmetric: work items carry obligations *toward* knowledge; no knowledge kind depends on any work item (M6 §6.4 cohesion evidence: no shared identity scheme, lifecycle vocabulary, or authority behavior). **The DoD's knowledge boxes are the interchange terms** — a narrow, named vocabulary through which the downstream domain references upstream states. **The translation obligation:** "Approved" and "Verified" exist on both sides as **guarded homonyms** (work states, *never* authority acts or verdicts — M6 §7.4 UL; L2-9/L-5 typed as a work-item status vocabulary). Someone must own the translation so work-side homonyms never masquerade as knowledge-side acts — **ACL-shaped, and its home is undecidable from current evidence** because CBC-4 is outside PKS and its interior is not ours to design (**uncertainty marker U-2**; a genuine M8/ARB-facing question, recorded not resolved). **Confidence: Medium** (the adjacency itself is M5-Derived, carried at its accepted grade).

### R-5 — CBC-2 ↔ CBC-4: **SEPARATE WAYS**

**Observed:** boards, epic files, and WBS rows are CBC-4's own carriers (M6 §6.4); no evidence routes them through CBC-2's projection discipline; the derived-progress rule ("derived, never hand-written") is a work-side rule, not a CBC-2 membership fact. No coordination cost is justified by any recorded evidence. **Confidence: Medium** (an absence claim — graded as such).

**Patterns considered and not used anywhere, with reasons:** *Partnership* — no pair coordinates changes symmetrically; every evidenced flow is asymmetric. *Shared Kernel* — no co-owned model element exists anywhere in the record (the one candidate, "criterion", is explicitly owned one-sided). *Open Host Service* — no context exposes a service model for others to build on; CBC-2's outputs are constitutionally non-consumable as authority. *ACL as an assigned pattern* — the translation obligation at R-4 is real, but assigning the layer a home would design CBC-4's interior or invent a PKS component with no evidence; recorded as an obligation, not a pattern instance.
