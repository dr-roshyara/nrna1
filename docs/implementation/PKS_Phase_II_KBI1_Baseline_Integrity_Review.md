## 13. Deliverable — the FOUR-INSTRUMENT OPERATIONAL SEPARATION demonstrated by this case

**The Authority's recognition, adopted as the primary result of this disposition:** *"The key result is not that KBI-R1 was accepted. It's that the programme has now **empirically exercised four distinct governance instruments on a single case without collapsing them.**"*

| Instrument | Responsibility | What happened |
|---|---|---|
| **Review** | Establish evidence | **KBI-R1 identified the lapsed present-state classification** |
| **Authority** | Dispose the evidence | **ACCEPT — with no reinterpretation of the review** |
| **Historical Annotation** | Preserve the record | **Current-state lapse recorded without altering the historical artifact** |
| **Methodology Governance** | Evolve future practice | **NO ACTION — the archival principle belongs to record keeping, not work methodology** |

***Each instrument performed exactly one responsibility.***

**Why this is a demonstration and not a restatement:** **a framework can *assert* separation of concerns at any time; separation is only shown when a case arises in which collapsing the instruments would have been EASIER and the collapse did not occur.** *Here the shortcut was available and cheap — silently correcting the Status line would have taken one edit and produced a document that read as current.* **It would also have destroyed the historical assessment, taken an Authority act inside a review, and set a methodology precedent by accident. All three were avoided.**

> ***Conceptual separation is claimed in a framework document. OPERATIONAL separation is only ever demonstrated by a case.***

### 13.1 The discriminator, restated as an objective test

> ### **Is the statement LOAD-BEARING in explaining a later governance act?**

| Artifact | Load-bearing? | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **CCP-1** | **Yes** — execution plan | **Preserve unchanged** |
| **ERV-1** | **Yes** — explains Amendment 1 | **Preserve unchanged** |
| **AFV-1** | **No** — operational readiness classification | **Historical annotation** |
| **KBI-1** | **No** — promotion readiness classification | **Historical annotation** |

**Recorded as an improvement on the rule's first form: the initial formulation appeared to turn on VERB TENSE (future · present · historical). The programme has shown tense is NOT the discriminator** — all four artifacts contain forward- or present-tense claims and they divide two-and-two. ***A criterion that survives four cases and reverses the naive reading is objective enough for future cases to be evaluated against it.***

### 13.2 Archival vs methodology — why the separation must hold

| Concern | Question it answers |
|---|---|
| **Methodology governance** | ***How should FUTURE WORK be performed?*** |
| **Archival principle** | ***How should COMPLETED WORK be represented after time has passed?*** |

***Routing the archival principle through methodology governance would blur that distinction*** — and would have the specific consequence of making every lapsed status line a candidate methodology change.
