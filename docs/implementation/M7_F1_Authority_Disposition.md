### 2.1 Why the correction matters — it exposes the normative step the framing concealed

| If the convention WERE "for contested memberships" | What is actually the case |
|---|---|
| The finding would be a **plain omission**, and extension would be mere tidying | **The convention was established for REGIONS and SEAMS. Extending it to INDIVIDUAL CONTESTED MEMBERS is a transfer ACROSS OBJECT KINDS** |

> ### ***A region is a bounded area whose interior is undecided. A contested membership is a single element whose home is disputed. That a notation serves the first does not establish that it serves the second — and that gap is precisely the "ought" this Authority must decide, rather than inherit from the framing.***

**Recorded as the commission-design refinement predicted:** *an embedded factual assertion in a recommendation becomes an Authority fact by acting on it. This one was caught by verifying before deciding, and correcting it made the decision harder rather than easier — which is the point.*

## 4. DECISION — **ACCEPT — EXTEND CONVENTION**, on constitutional ground

**The convention shall be extended to represent Risk · Question · Exception record as recorded-but-unassigned participants.**

### 4.1 The ground, stated precisely

> ### **Not because consistency requires it — consistency across object kinds is exactly what the evidence does not establish (§2). But because the CONSTITUTIONAL RULE AGAINST IMPLICIT DECISION requires it.**

| The reasoning | |
|---|---|
| **1** | The three memberships are **recorded, unassigned** in the governed baseline — a state the Authority Disposition preserved deliberately |
| **2** | **⚠️ NARROWED at Authority direction, 2026-07-31.** ***Within the strategic model, a contested element that is not represented is indistinguishable from one that was never contested. The Authority determines that preserving this distinction IN THE STRATEGIC MODEL is preferable to leaving it implicit elsewhere in the corpus.*** **Superseded, retained:** ~~*"A contested element absent from the strategic model is indistinguishable, to a later reader, from one that was never contested"*~~ — *overclaimed: the information does not disappear from the CORPUS, only from the MODEL, and the narrowed form names the choice the Authority is actually making* |
| **3** | ***That indistinguishability is resolution by omission — and the constitutional rule forbids deciding an open matter by not mentioning it*** |
| **4** | **Extension therefore DECIDES NOTHING. It records that a question remains open, which is the opposite of resolving it** |

**⚠️ The asymmetry, acknowledged as the commission's structure requires:** **ACCEPT—Extend is the NARROW act** — it makes an existing openness visible. ***ACCEPT—Intentional Incompleteness would be the BROADER act, because it would assert that the omission was chosen, and no record shows that it was.*** **REJECT would leave a contested matter invisible in the authoritative relationship model.**

### 4.2 Why the object-kind gap does not obstruct the decision

**M7's legend already carries `⚠ = uncertainty marker` — a notation whose object is neither a region nor a seam but an *uncertainty*.** ***So M7's own apparatus is not confined to regions; the transfer required is smaller than §2's gap suggested, though it remains a transfer and is decided here rather than assumed.***
