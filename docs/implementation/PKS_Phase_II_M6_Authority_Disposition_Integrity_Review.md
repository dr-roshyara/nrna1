## 11.4 — Framework registration 2: **Institutional Trust** — a better name and a pass/fail rule

**The Authority's formulation is sharper than the one used at §10.4 and replaces it:**

> **REPLAY** — can every decision be reproduced solely from recorded evidence, rationale and commission history?
> **AUDIT** — can an independent reviewer reconstruct every Authority act **without external explanation**?
> **INSTITUTIONAL TRUST** — can the record be trusted **without knowing the individuals who drafted it**, because authority derives from **commissions, evidence and governance roles rather than personal credibility**?

**"Institutional trust" is the right name: it locates the property in the institution rather than in the reader's confidence.** *And the any-one-fails rule matters — the three are not a score to average.
