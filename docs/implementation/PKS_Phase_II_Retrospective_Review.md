## 3. Deliverable 5 — **RET-1 (MAJOR): pattern NAMES are written as rules, while the header correctly says they are not**

*Objective 1 + 5 · Evidence origin: artifact-local*

**§9's header is exemplary:** *"Observed patterns, each with its demonstration. **None is proposed as a rule; all are candidates for future promotion.**"* **That is exactly right.** The defect is that **four of the eleven pattern statements assert universals or superlatives that their own demonstrations do not support:**

| # | As written | What the demonstration supports | The gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P-2** | *"Interpretive layers fail; **evidence layers don't**"* | *All five substantive defects were interpretive; zero were in quoted corpus evidence* | **"don't" is a universal.** The evidence supports *"did not, in Phase II, across five defects, in one lineage"* |

**Contrast P-10, which shows the document knows how to do this correctly:** *"Withdrawal requires less warrant than assertion **(scoped — see §11)**"*, and §11 then carries the narrow DAR-1 wording verbatim with the note that it was **expressly not adopted** as a methodology principle. **P-10 is the model the other four should follow.**

**Recommended resolution — no pattern removed, no demonstration weakened:** restate P-2, P-4, P-9, P-11 in the **observed-scope form P-10 already uses** (*"in Phase II, across n=…"*), retaining every demonstration verbatim. **The claims lose nothing they can support.**

## 4. Deliverable 1 (cont.) — **RET-2 (MAJOR): §11 grades the candidates, which is the assessment the MCA exists to perform**

*Objective 4 · Evidence origin: cross-artifact*

§11 item 1 reads: *"**Recommendation:** promote by **evidence strength**, not by appeal — **P-3 and P-10 rest on n=2 and one scoped instance respectively and should be held at that strength; P-2, P-4, P-5, and P-6 have the broadest demonstrations**."*

**The two halves of that sentence are different acts, and only the first is an input:**

| Half | Act | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| *"promote by evidence strength, not by appeal"* | Recommends a **method** for the MCA | ✅ **Legitimate input** — and a good one |
| *"P-3 and P-10 should be held at that strength; P-2/P-4/P-5/P-6 have the broadest demonstrations"* | **Grades the candidates against that method** | ❌ **This is pre-assessment** — the comparative ranking the MCA is convened to produce |

**The Authority's own framing decides it: *the retrospective should prepare inputs; it should never appear to prepare outcomes.*** **Recommending the scale is preparing an input. Placing the candidates on it is preparing the outcome.**

**Stated fairly, because most of §11 is exactly right:** each pattern's **demonstration and its n are FACTS** and belong here — P-3's *n=2*, P-10's *one scoped instance* are inputs of the best kind. **The defect is the evaluative conclusion drawn from them (*"broadest"*, *"should be held"*), not the data.** *Supplying the n-values and letting the MCA rank is strictly stronger, because it cannot be accused of having pre-decided.*

**Recommended:** retain the method recommendation and every per-pattern n; **remove the tiering and the "should be held" verdict.**

# §11 — REFINEMENTS AND DISPOSITION *(Authority instruction, 2026-07-30)*

## 11.1 — Refinement 1 ACCEPTED: empirical vs normative over-generalization are DIFFERENT DEFECTS with DIFFERENT REMEDIES

**The Authority's refinement is adopted, and applying it did more than re-label: it reorganized RET-1 and RET-3 into one two-class finding, because the remedies genuinely differ.**

| Class | What it does | Instances | **Remedy** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **RET-1a — EMPIRICAL over-generalization** | Extends an observation **beyond the available evidence** | **P-2** *"evidence layers don't [fail]"* · **P-4** *"self-verification is blind to…"* · **P-9** *"the most productive instrument"* · **§12** *"is what a working governance process looks like"* | **ADD SCOPE.** *"In Phase II, across n=…"* — the P-10 form. **The claim survives at its true size** |
| **RET-1b — NORMATIVE over-generalization** | Stops describing and **prescribes a criterion** | **P-11** *"a control is **only** proven by holding against its principal"* · **§1/§7** *"the **only kind worth having**"* / *"a control that yields to its principal **is not a control**"* | **CHANGE CLASS, not scope.** ***Scoping a prescription does not fix it*** — *"in Phase II, a control is only proven by…"* is still a prescription. It must become a **Recommendation**, or go to the MCA, or be deleted |

**Why the distinction earns its place — it is not a taxonomy for its own sake:** **the empirical instances are repairable in place and lose nothing; the normative instances cannot be repaired by scoping at all.** *A retrospective may observe that a control held; asserting what kind of control is worth having is a methodology principle, and methodology principles enter only through MCA → CDR under Configuration Control.* **P-11's remedy is therefore reclassification, and its content routes to the same place PMR-1 and PMR-2 already went.**

**Recorded: this is the sharper form of the finding, and the Authority's refinement produced it.**
