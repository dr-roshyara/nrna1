## 1. The central question, settled FIRST — **ESCAPE vs COUNTERFACTUAL**

**The filter: *a control becomes GOVERNED only after REPEATED operational evidence that its absence let defects **ESCAPE**.***

**Read as written, it requires three things: the control was ABSENT · a defect OCCURRED · the defect ESCAPED.** **And "escape" already has an operative sense on the record — RET-1 §5.3: *"self-verification by the executing stage is 0-for-2 on defects **later stages found**."***

> ### **DETERMINATION: escape means the defect LEFT THE STAGE THAT OWNED THE CHECK. A defect PREVENTED, or CAUGHT WITHIN the act that produced it, is not an escape.**

**This is offered as a READING of the existing filter, not as a new rule** *(per CDR-R1 §1's interpret-vs-mandate boundary; MCA-R2 does not apply PMR-8 to PMR-8)*.

### 1.1 ⭐ The residual resolves as a consequence, not separately

**The commission carried: *the ASSESSMENT can be procedure-neutral while the EVIDENCE cannot — all three candidates' evidence was gathered in acts that already applied the proposed ordering.*** **Under the determination above it resolves cleanly:**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Evidence PRODUCED BY the practice** | ***yields counterfactuals*** — *the practice was applied, so nothing escaped* |
| **Evidence of ESCAPE** | ***is procedure-INDEPENDENT*** — *an escape is an observation of FAILURE, and failures are not products of the practice under assessment* |

> ### ***So the procedure-taint concern applies to exactly the candidates whose evidence is counterfactual, and not at all to those with escapes. The residual is not a separate difficulty; it is the same distinction seen from the other side.***

## 2. PMR-8 — applicability before evidence evaluation → **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE**

**Sole datum: MCA-R1, where the applicability distinction WAS made and false rejections were thereby PREVENTED.**

| Filter element | Present? |
|---|---|
| The control was absent | ⛔ **NO — it was applied** |
| A defect occurred | ⛔ **NO** |
| A defect escaped | ⛔ **NO. n = 0 escapes** |

> ### ***PMR-8's evidence is structurally self-defeating, in the same way PMR-1's was — and the parallel is exact. PMR-1 failed because routing was "consistent"; PMR-8 fails because the applicability step was PERFORMED. A candidate whose only datum is its own successful voluntary application cannot demonstrate escape.***

**VERDICT: INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE.**

> ### ⚠️ **Stated precisely (Authority refinement, 2026-07-31): this does NOT show that PMR-8 lacks value. It shows that THE CURRENT ADMISSION CRITERION — escape evidence — HAS NOT BEEN SATISFIED.**

***The candidate may still have preventive value; it has simply not met the programme's current threshold for admission. Those are different findings, and only the second is within an assessment's authority to make.*** *MCA-R1 §1 already records that the filter measures only defect prevention.*

**Discharge condition — a DATA condition, not a date:** **one instance in which an assessment's failure to establish applicability first let a false verdict ESCAPE into a decision.**
