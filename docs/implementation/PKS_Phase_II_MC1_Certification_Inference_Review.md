## 5. **MC-F1 — MINOR** · the composite asserts a convergence its own labels do not show

**§9 presents three rows:**

| Dimension | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| **Method Design** | **PROVISIONALLY CERTIFIED** |
| **Operational Evidence** | **SUPPORTED BY ONE EXECUTION LINEAGE** |
| **Composite** | **PROVISIONALLY CERTIFIED** — *"The two dimensions **converge on the same label** today, but they are different questions with different upgrade paths"* |

**The two dimensions do not carry the same label.** *One is a certification grade; the other is a statement of evidence standing.* **The claim of convergence is therefore imprecise at exactly the point where the artifact is most careful everywhere else — the seam between two deliberately separated dimensions.**

**Remedy: state that the dimensions are **compatible** (the design grade is bounded by the evidence standing), not that they *"converge on the same label."*** *No grade changes.*

**Minor because the substance is right and is stated elsewhere in the same section:** *"Full certification is not reco[mmended]"*, plus three enumerated things the evidence cannot support. ***The defect is in the sentence describing the relationship, not in the relationship.***

## 11. The Authority's caution on PMR-8 · PMR-9 · PMR-10 — recorded, and it is an instance of the programme's own rule

**Caution, quoted:** *"Three distinct methodological candidates arising from three reviews is useful evidence, but **each still needs to stand on its own empirical support.**"*

> ### ⭐ **Recorded observation: this caution is PMR-2's own prohibition, applied to the REGISTER.**

**PMR-2 forbids a composite claim from inheriting its strongest component's confidence. Three candidates arriving together invite exactly that error at register level — reading "three candidates from three reviews" as support for any one of them, when PMR-8 stands at n=1, PMR-9 at n=1, and PMR-10 at n=2.**

***A cluster is not a corroboration. The candidates share an origin, not an evidence base — and shared origin is the weakest possible correlation to mistake for independent support.***

**Practice consequence recorded, not adopted as a rule: each candidate's evidence count is stated in its own entry and is never aggregated. That is already the register's discipline (§2 of Register discipline); the caution confirms it rather than extending it.**
