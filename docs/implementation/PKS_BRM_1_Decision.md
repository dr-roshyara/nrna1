# **BRM-1 — Baseline Representation Model Decision**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Act** | **AUTHORITY DECISION** (2026-07-31), executing `PKS_BRM_1_Baseline_Representation_Model_Commission.md` |
| **Question** | *Should the PKS governance baseline remain reference-defined (Model B), or transition to a materialized baseline (Model A)?* |
| **DECISION** | ## **RETAIN MODEL B — the baseline remains REFERENCE-DEFINED** |
| **Governing standard applied** | ***Do the benefits of materialization justify introducing a duplicated-governance risk that the current architecture does not have?*** |
| **Status** | **ISSUED.** |

---

## 1. Rationale *(Authority's, recorded as given)*

| # | Ground |
|---|---|
| **1** | **The existing architecture is complete and citable** *(CDR §4.2: "the record is complete and citable as it stands")* |
| **2** | **No demonstrated deficiency requires abandoning the reference-defined model** |
| **3** | **Materialization introduces a governance risk — duplicated authoritative text — that is ABSENT today** |
| **4** | **The proposed benefits (navigation · onboarding · readability · tooling) have NOT been shown to outweigh that risk** |
| **5** | ***Future tooling or presentation artifacts can improve usability WITHOUT changing the constitutional representation model*** |

### 1.1 ⭐ The rationale rests on the BURDEN TEST, not on the programme's direction — and that matters

**§5 of the commission recorded five governance acts all reducing duplicated text, and flagged them expressly as *evidence of direction, not an argument for continuing it*, because otherwise governance becomes path-dependent.**

> ### ***The Authority's rationale cites NONE of them. Every ground above is a direct application of the burden test: completeness already exists, no deficiency is demonstrated, a risk is introduced, the benefits are unweighed, and the benefits are separately obtainable.***

**So the decision survives the path-dependence caution the commission itself raised.** ***Model B is retained because the change failed its burden — not because the programme had been moving that way.***

---

## 2. ⭐ The carve-out, and it is part of the decision

> ### **PRESENTATION is not CONSTITUTIONAL REPRESENTATION.**

**Ground 5 forecloses nothing about usability:** *future tooling, indexes, navigation aids, generated views, onboarding guides and presentation artifacts remain available* — **provided they do not become the baseline.**

| Permitted | Forbidden by this decision |
|---|---|
| **Derived, clearly non-authoritative views** that aid navigation | **A materialized document that carries governed statements as a second authoritative home** |

***The distinction is what makes the decision narrow: it rejects a change to WHERE AUTHORITY LIVES, not a change to how the baseline is READ.***
