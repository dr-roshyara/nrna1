## 1. A circularity that had to be cleared before any adoption could stand

**Two of the three adoptions below rest on MCA-R1's determination that the strengthened filter is *stated for controls* and misfires on definitions, taxonomies and status elevations. That determination is also the substance of PMR-8 — which is UNASSESSED.**

> ### **Question: is this CDR adopting candidates on the strength of an unadopted rule?**

**Determination: NO, and the distinction is precise.**

| MCA-R1 did | PMR-8 would do |
|---|---|
| **INTERPRET the scope of an existing control** — reading what the filter, as written, is about | **MANDATE that interpretation as a required PROCEDURE in all future assessments** |
| *Lawful: reading a governed rule's scope is what applying it consists of* | *A methodology change: it prescribes how future work is performed* |

***An assessment that must not interpret the rule it applies cannot apply it at all.*** **The adoptions below therefore rest on the EXISTING filter correctly read, not on PMR-8. PMR-8 remains unassessed and gains nothing from this act.**

**Recorded because a future reader would rightly challenge it, and because concealing it would breach the principle that a governance record's value lies in making its points of judgment discoverable.**

## 2. Decisions

### 2.1 PMR-5 — Assembly Fidelity Rule → **ADOPT**

> *"An assembly artifact shall not modify normative wording inherited from an authoritative source. Improvements to inherited normative language must be proposed and disposed, not applied in assembly."*

**⚠️ MISQUOTATION — annotated 2026-07-31 under ISV-F1 (CDR-R1 — where it CASCADED). This is NOT the register's authoritative wording.** **The authoritative text reads: *"…must be proposed AGAINST THE AUTHORITATIVE SOURCE and PROPAGATED THROUGH THE ESTABLISHED PUBLICATION CHAIN."*** ***Annotated, not corrected: this record states what this act actually quoted, and rewriting it would erase the evidence of the cascade. The operative baseline (SDM v1.1) carries the authoritative wording.***

**The only candidate in the register that clears the strengthened filter as written.** Repeated operational evidence of escape: **M8 Finding 1 · the eleven-site KC-13/KC-17 cascade · ADR-001 §13's duplication of §7.**

**ADOPTED into the Controlled Change / assembly methodology.**

| Consequence | |
|---|---|
| **Effective when** | **On issuance of the amended baseline — NOT on this decision.** *Decision ≠ execution* |
| **Applies to** | Assembly artifacts prospectively. ***It is NOT applied retroactively to the corpus: the eleven-site cascade was already remediated, and re-opening remediated work on a newly adopted rule would convert a methodology decision into an audit of completed acts*** |
| **Ladder status** | **Candidate → GOVERNED.** *First control in the programme to traverse the strengthened filter* |

**Recorded: the adopted wording is the reviewer's original formulation, preserved verbatim from the register. It was not improved in the course of adopting it** — *which would have been the Assembly Fidelity Rule's own prohibited act, performed at the moment of its adoption.*

### 2.2 PMR-4 — Elevate the Strategic/Tactical boundary rule → **ADOPT IN PART**

**ADOPTED: the rule's STATUS as a core methodology principle.** *Confirmed by MCA-R1 on evidence that it has already DECIDED cases — ADR-001's framing, IBC-1's relocation, DR-7's restatement as a reopening trigger.*

**NOT ADOPTED: the PRECEDENCE that a "core" label would ordinarily confer.**

> ***Elevation changes what may be CITED as a principle. It does not change what WINS when principles collide — no collision case exists, so precedence is untested and is expressly withheld.***

| Adopted | Withheld |
|---|---|
| The rule is a core principle of the methodology and is citable as such | **Precedence in conflict.** *Deferred until a collision case arises, at which point the ordering must be decided on that case's evidence, not inferred from the label* |

**Rationale for splitting rather than adopting whole: adopting an untested precedence would import a consequence no evidence supports, on the strength of a word.** *This is the same discipline that refused PMR-2's stronger positive form while accepting its prohibition.*

### 2.3 PMR-7 → **ADOPT IN PART, and the entry is SPLIT**

**MCA-R1 established that the entry bundles three propositions and that splitting is a decision, not an assessment. The CDR exercises it:**

| New identifier | Proposition | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| **PMR-7a** | **The two-level determinism VOCABULARY** — Level 1 textual (*"did any editor author text?"*) ⟂ Level 2 operational (*"did any editor infer an unstated obligation?"*); satisfying Level 1 does not guarantee Level 2 | ✅ **ADOPT** |
| **PMR-7b** | **Planning control:** a change item must specify the OBLIGATIONS an edit incurs, not only the words it writes | ⏸ **DEFER** — n = 1 (CCP-R1) |
| **PMR-7c** | **Verification control:** a plan-verification must TEST for those obligations | ⏸ **DEFER** — n = 1 (ERV-R1) |

**PMR-7a's adoption carries a binding constraint into the baseline, restated so it cannot be lost:** ***CI-1 completely protects Level 1 and was never Level 2's guardian. The vocabulary BOUNDS CI-1; it does not fault it.*** **Level 2 was not nameable in advance.**

**Deferral condition for 7b and 7c: a second instance PER CONTROL, separately.** ***The two existing data are one defect observed at two stages; a second observation of the same defect at the same two stages would not discharge either.***

**Identifier discipline observed: PMR-7 is not renumbered or retired. 7a/7b/7c EXTEND its identity — *identity is never reassigned, only extended*.**

### 2.4 PMR-2 — Composite confidence non-inheritance → **DEFER**

**The defect class is CONFIRMED** (M7 §9 passed three unsupported pattern claims, caught only downstream). **n = 1.**

**DEFERRED with the cheapest discharge condition in the register: verify whether AFV-1's four interpretive over-strengthenings are instances of composite-confidence inheritance. One verification decides this candidate.**

**⚠️ The verification is NOT commissioned by this act.** *Commissioning is a separate authority act, and a CDR that commissioned its own discharge conditions would be setting its own agenda.*

**The stronger positive form remains OUT OF SCOPE and undeferred:** *PMR-2 prohibits inheriting the STRONGEST component's confidence; it does not establish that a composite is only as strong as its WEAKEST. Only the prohibition is before the CDR.*

### 2.5 PMR-3 — Permanent capabilities vs situational controls → **DEFER on a DATA condition**

**DEFERRED. Condition: n ≥ 2 simpler contexts in which CCP/ERV-class controls were applied and their necessity could be assessed.**

> ***A deferral to a DATE expires without producing knowledge. A deferral to a DATA condition cannot be discharged by the passage of time.***

**Recorded as a structural determination, not a scheduling one:** **this programme has ONE context. A distinction requiring comparative data cannot be established by the party that possesses only one side of the comparison** — the same property that holds the Operational Evidence dimension at zero. ***PMR-3 is therefore not merely undecided; it is undecidable from here.***

### 2.6 PMR-1 — Finding-class taxonomy → **DECLINE IN PART · DEFER the residue**

| Portion | Decision |
|---|---|
| **The finding-vocabulary portion** | ❌ **DECLINE.** *Discharged: the Review Method now owns the finding vocabulary and the Observations vs Findings distinction. Adopting it would duplicate a control — and a duplicated control is worse than an absent one, because two homes for one rule permit divergence* |
| **The residue — routing classes** (editorial fold · governance decision · Authority act) | ⏸ **DEFER.** *Practised consistently and declared nowhere normative. Condition: evidence of an actual MIS-ROUTING* |

***The decline is recorded as a decline, not as a silent lapse: the candidate was not withdrawn by its proposer and is not being quietly dropped. It is declined because the gap closed by another lawful route.***

### 2.7 PMR-6 — Per-finding confidence dimension → **DECLINE**

**DECLINED, on MCA-R1's strengthened grounds: the need was real, and the programme met it with a STRUCTURAL distinction** (Observation vs Finding; directly-supported vs speculative) **rather than a three-point scale.** *A scale added now would grade findings that are already sorted by kind.*

**The proposer's own standard remains the operative test and is now failed for two independent reasons** (n = 0 at entry; superseded in substance since). ***Recorded as an APPEND; the original entry is not edited.***

## 3. Did the admission discipline hold? — the test the Authority set for this act

**The Authority framed the CDR as the point where the programme demonstrates *"whether it can convert supported candidates into governed practice without weakening the admission discipline."* The four distinctions named are checked individually:**

| Distinction | Held? | Where it was tested |
|---|---|---|
| **Recognition ⟂ agreement ⟂ adoption** | ✅ | **Nothing was adopted because it was agreed with.** *PMR-4's precedence was withheld despite the candidate being confirmed; PMR-8 gained nothing from Authority endorsement* |
| **Annotation ⟂ maintenance** | ✅ | *No artifact was maintained or annotated by this act. A CDR decides; it does not edit* |
| **Preservation ⟂ methodology** | ✅ | **PMR-5's adoption is prospective only** — *applying an adopted rule retroactively would convert a methodology decision into an audit of completed acts* |
| **Review family ⟂ governance domain** | ✅ | *This act operates wholly in the METHODOLOGY-GOVERNANCE domain and performed no review* |

**And the arithmetic of restraint, stated plainly because it is the evidence:**

> **Seven candidates. ONE adopted whole. Two adopted in part with their unsupported halves withheld. Two deferred on conditions that cannot be discharged by time. Two declined.** ***Six of seven candidates were argued for by someone in this programme, and six of seven were held short of full adoption.***

**⚠️ The counter-reading, recorded so the result is not self-congratulatory: a 1-of-7 adoption rate is ALSO consistent with a filter that is too strict to admit good practice.** *MCA-R1 §3.3 records both readings and adopts neither. This decision does not resolve it either — and deliberately: a CDR that judged its own filter would be certifying the instrument by which it decides.*
