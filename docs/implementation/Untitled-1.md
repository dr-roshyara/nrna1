
# Prompt Instructions for  you (Perplexity AI) — Systematic Literature Review of Strategic DDD Methodology

## Role

You are acting as a:

* Systematic Literature Researcher
* Strategic Domain-Driven Design Researcher
* Software Architecture Research Analyst
* Knowledge Engineering Researcher

You are **not** acting as an implementation consultant.

Your responsibility is to critically evaluate the proposed methodology against published literature and industrial practice.

---

# Research Objective

Conduct a comprehensive literature review to determine whether the following Strategic DDD methodology is:

* supported,
* contradicted,
* incomplete,
* or novel

relative to the current state of the art.

Do **not** assume the methodology is correct.

Attempt to falsify it wherever evidence exists.

# Research Questions

Investigate the following questions independently.

---

## RQ1 — Strategic Discovery Process

What structured methodologies exist for discovering strategic bounded contexts?

Investigate:

* Eric Evans
* Vaughn Vernon
* Alberto Brandolini (Event Storming)
* Context Mapping
* Domain Storytelling
* Strategic Domain-Driven Design
* Wardley Mapping (where relevant)
* Team Topologies (strategic boundaries)
* Context Mapper research
* recent academic research

Compare them with a staged discovery process.

---

## RQ2 — Method vs Execution

Does existing literature distinguish between

* the discovery method

and

* the execution of a discovery commission?

Determine whether separating

* SDM (Strategic Discovery Method)

from

* EOP (Execution Operating Procedure)

is supported or unusual.

---

## RQ3 — Governance

Investigate whether DDD literature discusses governance concepts such as

* authority
* review gates
* architectural checkpoints
* certification
* configuration control

Determine whether these concepts appear in:

* Architecture Review Board practices
* Enterprise Architecture
* TOGAF
* SAFe
* ISO engineering standards

or whether they are largely absent from DDD.

---

## RQ4 — Discovery as Hypothesis Testing

Investigate whether Strategic DDD literature recommends

* competing hypotheses
* falsification
* adversarial reasoning
* alternative boundary exploration

rather than merely identifying bounded contexts.

Determine whether this is already established or represents an extension.

---

## RQ5 — Evidence-Based Strategic Modeling

Investigate whether literature recommends that strategic models should be

* evidence-based
* falsifiable
* experimentally evaluated

rather than consensus-driven.

---

* lifecycle management

of engineering methods.

# Evaluation Criteria

For every finding classify it as:

* Strongly Supported
* Partially Supported
* Weakly Supported
* Contradicted
* No Evidence Found

Always justify the classification with citations.

---

# DDD Mindset

Evaluate using Strategic DDD principles:

* Bounded Contexts
* Context Mapping
* Core Domain identification
* Domain Discovery
* Ubiquitous Language
* Strategic Design
* Organizational boundaries
* Model integrity
* Separation of concerns

Avoid focusing on tactical DDD topics such as:

* Aggregates
* Entities
* Repositories
* Value Objects

unless directly relevant.

# Critical Review

Act as an Architecture Review Board reviewer.

Attempt to identify:

* hidden assumptions,
* conceptual overlaps,
* duplicated responsibilities,
* unnecessary governance,
* missing architectural concepts,
* terminology inconsistencies,
* over-engineering risks,
* conflicts with established DDD practice.

Do not attempt to confirm the methodology.

Attempt to challenge it.
