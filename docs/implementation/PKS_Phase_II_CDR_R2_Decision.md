## 3. PMR-8 → ⏸ **DEFER**

**MCA-R2: INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE — n = 0 escapes.**

> ### ⚠️ **Deferred, NOT declined — and the distinction rests on the Authority's own refinement: the assessment showed that THE CURRENT ADMISSION CRITERION WAS NOT SATISFIED, not that the candidate lacks value.**

***Declining would answer a question the assessment expressly did not answer. Deferral holds the candidate at exactly the standing its evidence supports.***

**Discharge condition — a DATA condition, not a date:** **one instance in which an assessment's failure to establish applicability first let a false verdict ESCAPE into a decision.**

**⚠️ And the structural difficulty is recorded rather than glossed:** *PMR-8's discharge requires a governance FAILURE that the programme is actively trying to prevent. **The condition is satisfiable only by the control's absence causing harm — which is what an escape-based filter demands of every candidate, and which is hardest to obtain for candidates that are already being practised voluntarily.*** *Whether that is a correct property of the filter or a defect in it is §4's question.*
