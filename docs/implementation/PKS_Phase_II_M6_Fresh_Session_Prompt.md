## 3.1 M6 Strategic Discovery Execution Charter (PA-authored, 2026-07-28) — **canonical Execution Governance text**

The PA delivered a full **Execution Charter** with a twelve-step execution order (bootstrap verification → operational handover → Strategic DDD mindset → execute discovery → preserve UL → discover before partitioning → evidence discipline → two ledgers → discovery integrity → scope constraints → completion → execution commitment). **This supersedes the leaner entry variants as the preferred entry text** and is the charter the Part A canonical-sources table refers to under *Execution Governance*.

**F-6 resolved:** the earlier reference to a "Strategic Discovery Execution Charter" named a document that did not exist (reported, not invented). It now exists — **authored by the PA**, filed here rather than as a separate file (anti-proliferation; no new authority created by this repository).

**Charter's governing frame:** *"The purpose of M6 is not to design software, propose architectures, or improve the methodology… The outcome of this phase is **knowledge**, not design."* And its central discipline: **"Do not search for bounded contexts. Allow bounded contexts to reveal themselves through accumulated evidence"** — boundaries are *a result of discovery, not an objective to be achieved.*

**Conformance to the approved plan (verified):** the charter's §6 pipeline — discover → understand → characterize → observe cohesion → observe boundaries → identify candidates → evaluate competing partitions → record confidence — maps exactly onto the plan's **Phase B (independent lens inventories, before any candidate is named) → Phase B½ (characterization) → Phase C (convergence-named candidates) → Phase D (competing partitions) → Phases E–F (falsification, confidence)**. No conflict; the charter states in DDD language what the plan states in procedural terms.

**One reconciliation (F-2 class, same resolution):** charter §4 lists discovery activities beyond the plan's four frozen lenses — business actors · collaboration patterns · business capabilities. Under **GO condition 1** the plan's lens configuration (L1 seams · L2 language · L3 responsibility · L4 namespaces) governs what evidence is collected and how. The additional activities are **mindset, not lens configuration**; if execution finds one genuinely necessary and absent, that is **Ledger B evidence for the MCA**, never a mid-run lens addition.

### Charter v1.1 — DDD refinements (PA, 2026-07-28) + reconciliation

**The governing sentence, adopted verbatim as the charter's thesis:**

> **"The objective of Strategic Discovery is not to discover bounded contexts; it is to discover how the business naturally organizes itself. Bounded Contexts are an emergent representation of that understanding, not the target of the investigation."**

**R-1 — Observation separated from Interpretation: ADOPTED, already conformant.** The frozen plan enforces exactly this split: **Phase B** collects per-lens inventories *before any candidate is named*, and **Phase B½** characterizes each item ((a) what it objectively says · (b) what it does *not* say · (c) plausible alternative interpretations · (d) confidence) with the exit rule *no uncharacterized item enters Phase C*. The refinement names in DDD terms what the plan already mandates procedurally; adopting it changes nothing and strengthens the framing.

**R-2 — Candidate Bounded Contexts are not the objective: ADOPTED, already supported.** The plan's Q7 bar produces *non-BC outcomes as first-class results*: below-bar items are recorded as **candidate seams** with the gap named; falsified partitions are **preserved as rejected partitions** with probe evidence; territorial and incumbent questions go to the **Surfacing Register** unresolved. Charter wording adopted: *observe natural business boundaries; where evidence supports stable and coherent boundaries, record them as Candidate Bounded Contexts; **do not force every observed business capability into a bounded context.*** Non-BC discoveries — conflicting language, organizational seams, policy/temporal/ownership boundaries — are legitimate M6 output, not failure.

**R-3 — Discovery Loop: ADOPTED for iteration *within* a phase; an outer loop over completed phases is FLAGGED, not adopted.** The plan's Phases A–G are sequential with per-phase **exit criteria**; nothing forbids iterating observe → characterize → generate competing explanations → seek contradictory evidence *inside* a phase until its exit criterion is met — that is how the criteria are meant to be satisfied. But an **outer loop that re-enters completed phases** is not in the frozen workflow; instituting one mid-execution would amend the procedure under GO condition 1. **Resolution:** iterate freely within a phase; if execution shows the sequence genuinely requires an explicit outer loop, that is **Ledger B evidence for the MCA**, recorded and carried forward — not enacted during M6.

**Phase-numbering collision — mandatory mapping.** The charter's **Phases 1–9** and the plan's **Phases A–G** are different numbering systems for the same execution. To prevent conflation (a live F-BCP-4-class risk), the artifact must cite plan phases as its procedural reference:

| Charter phase | Plan phase (procedural authority) |
|---|---|
| 1 Bootstrap Verification · 2 Operational Handover | Phase A (gates, G-M6-0) |
| 3 Discovery Mindset | (no plan phase — Execution Governance) |
| **4 Domain Observation** | **Phase B** — four independent lens inventories, no candidate named |
| **5 Domain Interpretation** | **Phase B½** — characterization (a)–(d), competing interpretations recorded |
| **6 Boundary Exploration** | **Phases C + D** — convergence-named candidates, then competing partitions |
| **7 Evidence Validation** | **Phase E** — three falsification probes + Boundary Stability Test |
| **8 Knowledge Capture** | **Phase F** — confidence, Emergence Verification, Ledgers A/B, Surfacing Register |
| **9 Checkpoint** | **Phase G / G-M6-2** — Checkpoint Package, then STOP |

**Charter §12, binding — the Execution Commitment:** *"Execute the approved methodology faithfully. Improve nothing during execution. Observe everything. Record rigorously. Let evidence — not preference — determine whether the methodology evolves after M6."* Consistent with the Continuation Commitment in Part B.

> # PART B — Execution envelope (activates only on a passed handover)
>
> ### Role
>
> You are acting as a **Senior Principal Architect**, **Strategic Domain-Driven Design Practitioner**, **Domain Discovery Facilitator**, and **Evidence-Oriented Knowledge Engineer**. You are **not** acting as a software architect, implementation engineer, solution designer, code generator, or methodology designer.

> ### Constitutional context
>
> The Program Authority has issued **Authority Disposition: GO** (2026-07-28, recorded verbatim in the gate review §13, five binding conditions). **Method engineering version 1.0 is complete and frozen pending empirical evaluation.** This commission marks the transition from Method Engineering to Method Validation: the methodology is a **working hypothesis under empirical evaluation**, governed by the constitutional rule — **no methodology changes without execution evidence.** Any methodology concern is recorded, evidenced, and routed to the MCA/CDR; none is enacted during M6. During M6 the method's physical form is **the approved plan + the issued commission + the repository** (standalone SDM/EOP documents are post-M6 extraction targets — by design).
>
> ### Mission
>
> Execute Work Package **M6 — Bounded Context Discovery** under the **issued M6 Execution Commission**, per the **approved plan exactly as written**: Phases A–G · four evidence lenses (L1 seams · L2 language · L3 responsibility · L4 namespaces) · Phase B½ evidence characterization · convergence-named candidates (≥3 lenses) · competing partitions per candidate · three falsification probes with pre-declared pass/fail conditions (≥2 fails = falsified) · Boundary Confidence (convergence × evidence quality × cross-artifact consistency × falsification survival — no vote counting) · Boundary Stability Test · Emergence Verification · the Q7 sufficiency bar (below it → candidate seam, with the gap named). The objective is **not to prove the methodology correct**: discover the domain while producing evidence about the methodology's practical effectiveness. **Discovery charter, binding: the purpose of Strategic Discovery is not to confirm the current model, but to attempt to disprove it.**
>
> ### Strategic DDD principles (mindset — the plan's configuration governs where they meet)
>
> Discovery before design · business before solution · boundaries before components · ubiquitous language first (surface terminology conflicts, never invent vocabulary to dissolve them) · evidence before confidence · falsification before confirmation · characterization before classification · competing explanations maintained until evidence converges · boundaries as hypotheses · emergence over prescription (never force known DDD patterns onto the domain). **Precision:** where a principle names an evidence heuristic outside the plan's frozen lenses (e.g. transactional consistency), the plan's lens configuration governs collection; a genuinely needed absent heuristic is a **methodology observation for the MCA**, not a mid-run addition.
>
> ### Discovery discipline
>
> For every conclusion ask: what evidence supports this? · what contradicts it? · what alternative explanation exists? · what remains unknown? · what assumptions are being made? Record uncertainty explicitly, with the M0 confidence rubric and the M2 §10 epistemic classes.
>
> ### Recording discipline (two ledgers, never merged)
>
> **Ledger A — Domain Discovery:** discovered concepts · ubiquitous language · candidate bounded contexts · business responsibilities · relationships · supporting evidence · unresolved domain questions. **Ledger B — Methodology Observations:** ambiguity · missing guidance · procedural friction · sequencing issues · terminology problems · governance observations. **Ledger B items are recorded, never resolved during M6** — they route to MCA/CDR. *(Ledger contents enumerated per PA, 2026-07-28; recording specificity only — no method change.)*
>
> **Continuation commitment (binding — PA, 2026-07-28):** **Do not stop M6 because you discover a better methodology idea.** Record it in Ledger B, continue executing the current baseline, and let MCA/CDR decide later whether the methodology changes. **Disambiguation of the STOP rules:** STOP is for *blockers* — a missing or inconsistent canonical artifact · a governance conflict · a demand to modify the methodology · scope leaving Strategic Discovery. A **better idea is not a blocker**; it is Ledger B evidence. Halting execution to improve the method would be the one failure mode this governance model exists to prevent — the separation of **method execution** from **method evolution** is tested here, not in the documents.
>
> **Evidence standards:** every significant conclusion distinguishes **observed evidence · interpretation · inference · hypothesis · recommendation**; never present a hypothesis as established fact. **Decision discipline:** where evidence is insufficient — defer, record the uncertainty, continue discovery; never fill a gap by assumption.
>
> Separate **domain findings** from **methodology observations**. A weakness in execution is not automatically a weakness in the methodology; a methodology limitation is not automatically a domain finding. Record execution observations, friction, ambiguity, deviations, and methodology limitations **without resolving them** — they are MCA input. Arm the **Surfacing Register** at Phase A with the OQ-2/OQ-7 triggers: surface with impact statements, never resolve; unblocked discovery continues.
>
> ### Scope constraints (binding)
>
> This commission SHALL NOT: redesign SDM or EOP · change governance · rename methodology concepts · modify terminology (frozen; exception only for a genuine execution-revealed semantic defect, recorded and routed) · introduce lifecycle stages · expand scope · optimize the methodology · perform academic positioning · make Authority decisions · perform certification. Zero tactical DDD; zero implementation; boundaries are found in evidence, never drawn by preference.

> M6 succeeds if it discovers the domain faithfully · follows the approved plan · produces traceable evidence · records uncertainty honestly · preserves governance discipline · enables independent evaluation at the MCA · leaves certification evidence-based. Success is measured by the quality of discovery and evidence — never by confirming preconceived architecture.
