## 4. Method Design Assessment

### 4.1 The CDR baseline for this dimension

**PROVISIONALLY CERTIFIED.** The CDR recorded a precise reason for not upgrading at adoption time: MCR-1's adoption *discharged the gating condition formally*, but "a rule adopted this hour has zero executions," so validating the changed design requires **at least one run under it**. The full-certification bar was restated as: *one execution under the adopted MCR-1 rule with the defect class not recurring.*

### 4.2 Evidence accumulated since the CDR — differentiated per adopted refinement

**Measured (from the four post-CDR acts: M7 · Validation Review · DAR-1 · M8):**

| Adopted refinement | Was it exercised? | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **MCR-1** member-set fixation | **By analogy only — not in its defect domain** | M7 fixed its participant/pair inventory at §4 before classifying, and no drift occurred. But **M7 contained no probing act**: there was no falsification probe over a candidate's member set, which is the situation MCR-1 governs. SI-2 records this as weak-positive and names the first genuine test as "the next probing act" |
| **MCR-2** candidate seam as a formal state | **Yes** | The state carried CBC-3 through its re-disposition and through M8 §6 with its required record-keeping (gap · triggers · lift-path) — no improvised vocabulary was needed, which was the defect MCR-2 addressed |
| **MCR-3** re-entry route for Phase-D partitions | **No — never invoked** | The preserved finer partition was cited three times (M7 §7.3, DAR-1, M8 §6) and the route was correctly *not* used. Zero executions; the route remains untested |
| **MCR-4** Evidence Restatement stage + two fold classes | **Yes, repeatedly** | The two-class discipline governed the Validation Review's output split and DAR-1's editorial-vs-substantive separation. Note the ordering: MCR-4 was *derived from* an execution that preceded its adoption, and has since been applied twice post-adoption |
| **MCR-5** independence-basis statement | **Yes, repeatedly** | Applied in M7, the Validation Review (including the evidence matrix), and M8's standing qualifier. SI-3: adoption cost ≈ zero, informational value real |
| **MCR-6** sequencing + records rules | **Not tested** | No new sequencing collision arose; the records collision (B-7) recurred and was handled by the established precedent rather than by a new rule |

**Also Measured:** four consecutive acts consumed the frozen record **by reference without a single request to reopen an upstream artifact** (M8 §11 item 3) — evidence about the record set's sufficiency and about the by-reference execution mode.

### 4.3 Analysis — does this evidence change the status?

**No, and the reason is narrow.** The gating condition names one specific event: a run under MCR-1 in which the member-set-drift class does not recur. **That event has not occurred**, because none of the four post-CDR acts performed a falsification probe over a candidate member set. MCR-1's discipline was applied *by analogy* at M7 (fixing an inventory before classification), which is genuine but weak-positive: a rule cannot be validated by an act that gives its defect class no opportunity to appear.

**Upgrade considered and rejected.** The steelman is real — three of six adopted refinements were exercised without friction, and the baseline demonstrably constrained decisions at four observable points (SI-1). But the CDR's condition was written about MCR-1 specifically, and MCR-1 is precisely the refinement that remains untested in its own domain. Upgrading on the strength of the *other* refinements would substitute available evidence for required evidence.

**Downgrade considered and rejected.** The candidate trigger would be §4.4's new observation — a genuine gap in the SDM's self-verification for modelling acts. It does not warrant downgrade: the layered process *caught* the resulting defects, disposed them, and left the structural model intact. A gap that the process absorbs is a refinement candidate, not a certification failure. Downgrading would also misprice the four clean executions.

### 4.4 New methodology observation — recorded, not assessed

**Observed:** M7's self-verification (§9) checked "relationships consistent with SDM" and **passed all five pattern names**; the Validation Review, applying definitional conformance, then found two unsupported names and one pattern-usage defect — all three subsequently accepted at DAR-1.

**Implication (stated, not resolved):** the SDM's self-verification instrument for a *modelling* act tests scope, traceability, and internal consistency, but **does not test whether an applied pattern satisfies the definition it invokes.** This is a defect class distinct from MCR-1's (which governs member-set fixation before probing).

**Routing:** recommended for admission to the **PMR register** at the next MCA-class assessment, with this checkpoint's record as its evidence trace. **This checkpoint does not add it to the register** — admitting a candidate is an act beyond a certification assessment's authority, and the register's own rule requires an evidence trace, which this section supplies.

**Relation to the recorded stage-compensation property:** this is a **third instance** (after the Critical Review catching execution's member-set drift and the Validation Review catching M7's pattern names — the second of which is this same event viewed from the other side). Counted honestly: **two distinct events, three viewings.** The property remains at n=2 and is still not generalized.

### 4.5 Recommended status

**MAINTAIN: PROVISIONALLY CERTIFIED.** Unchanged gating condition: **one execution under the adopted MCR-1 rule in which the member-set-drift class does not recur.** The next act capable of satisfying it is any commissioned act containing a falsification probe over a candidate member set — most plausibly an **MCR-3 bounded re-entry collection** for the preserved finer partition, which would exercise MCR-1, MCR-2, and MCR-3 simultaneously. *(Named as an observation about what would satisfy the condition — not a recommendation to commission it.)*

## 5. Operational Evidence Assessment

### 5.1 The CDR baseline for this dimension

**SUPPORTED BY ONE EXECUTION LINEAGE.** The CDR was explicit that **no decision at that table could move this dimension** — only new executions can: another architect, another corpus, M7-by-reference, or external review.

### 5.2 Evidence accumulated since the CDR

**Measured:** four further executions (M7 · Validation Review · DAR-1 · M8), plus this checkpoint. **All same-lineage.** Every one carries an explicit T-2 declaration; M8 §11 states the bound for the phase as a whole; SI-5 keeps the definition of the missing datum precise and records it as unclaimed.

**Volume accumulated. Independence did not.** The count of executions under the method rose from one work package to five acts; the number of *lineages* remains one.

### 5.3 Analysis

**No change, and the dimension is structurally unmovable by anything Phase II performed.** This is worth stating carefully, because it is the point at which a checkpoint could most easily inflate: it is tempting to read five clean executions as stronger operational support. They *are* stronger evidence of **executability** — and that evidence belongs to the Method Design dimension, where §4.2 credits it. This dimension measures **independence of the supporting evidence**, and its label ("one execution *lineage*") tracks exactly that. Five acts by one lineage is one lineage.

**One precision recorded for the future:** the dimension's label conflates nothing, but its *readers* might. "Supported by one execution lineage" is a statement about independence, not about quantity or quality of execution. Any future upgrade must point to a **second lineage**, not to a larger body of same-lineage work.

**Upgrade rejected** — no second lineage exists. **Downgrade rejected** — nothing weakened; the supporting evidence grew in volume and stayed honest about its bound.

### 5.4 Recommended status

**MAINTAIN: SUPPORTED BY ONE EXECUTION LINEAGE.** Unchanged upgrade path: a genuinely separated execution — another architect or lineage running an SDM work package against the frozen baseline with divergences recorded — or external review. **M7-by-reference did not deliver this**, contrary to the earlier staging assumption: it was executed by the same lineage, and the M7 commission's own framing correctly reduced its claim to *observations relevant to* independence rather than the datum itself. **That staging assumption is now closed as untrue on the evidence** — a small correction the checkpoint is the right place to record.
