## 5. Deliverable 8 — **M6R-1 (MAJOR)**: §14's correction names one section; two identical statements elsewhere are neither corrected nor pointed at

*Objective 8 (cascade) + quality E (*what supersedes it?*) · Evidence origin: artifact-local*

**§14's design is sound and explicit:** *"Nothing above this section has been edited — where an amendment corrects a printed statement, **the original stands as history and the amendment states the correction**."* **That design works only if the amendment names every corrected statement.**

**It names one: *"Correction to §7.5: the demoted-seam row's '4 of 7 members…' reads, corrected: 3 of 7; the gap statement 'Cohesion (probe FAIL)' reads, corrected…"***

**The same superseded claim survives, uncorrected and unnamed, in two further places:**

| Location | Text | Status after Amendment 1 |
|---|---|---|
| **§7's confidence table** | *"2/3 — **cohesion FAIL**"* · *"**Low-Medium → demoted**"* | **Superseded.** §14 tallies **3 of 7**, *"3 of 7 is not a majority. The pre-declared FAIL condition is **NOT met**"* |
| **§12's self-verification** | *"**one FAIL recorded and honored** rather than reasoned away ✅"* | **Superseded.** The FAIL was restated as not-met; **the demotion held on stability grounds instead** |

**Consequence: a reader consulting §7's confidence table or §12's self-verification finds a probe FAIL that the record has since restated as not-met, with nothing to indicate it.** *§14 knows this perfectly well — it states that "as printed, CBC-3 was demoted by a probe FAIL whose record did not support itself. As restated, the probe passes" — so the artifact understands the supersession and simply did not enumerate all its sites.*

**This is quality E's uncovered question in its home form — *what supersedes it?* — and it is the KC-13 cascade pattern: a correction applied at its named site while identical instances survive.** *KC-13 occurred nine times in the ADR; here it occurs twice, in the artifact that invented the characterization discipline the whole programme later used.*

**Fold classification, which decides the disposition path: this is an EVIDENCE-NEUTRAL fold (MCR-4 class 1).** **It changes no evidence, no probe result, no confidence grade and no conclusion** — the CBC-3 re-disposition was already taken *on the corrected record*, and the restated grounds are already recorded. **It propagates a correction the Authority has already disposed.**

**Proposed fold:** extend §14's correction list to name §7's confidence table and §12's self-verification, in the same *"reads, corrected"* form. **No text above §14 need be edited** — which keeps the standing intact.
