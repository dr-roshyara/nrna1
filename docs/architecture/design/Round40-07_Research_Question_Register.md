## Gap taxonomy (per review-chair guidance)

The findings fall into **three distinct kinds of gap** — kept separate so the next phase is clear about what each question *is*:

- **THEORY gap** — a possibly-missing or mis-bounded piece of governance *theory* (families, recursion, legitimacy structure). Resolved by **discovery** (F-REV / future families).
- **SEMANTIC gap** — one term, several meanings. Resolved by the **Semantic Inventory** (`Round40-06`) → later Ontology. *Recorded, not defined.*
- **COVERAGE gap** — a real entity/stage the model simply does not yet include (actor, lifecycle phase). Resolved by **extending coverage** when the relevant family is discovered.
