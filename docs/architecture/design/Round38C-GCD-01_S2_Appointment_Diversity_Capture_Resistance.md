## 1. The constitutional property (S-2)

Per 38C-15 C.4: *No single Assembly cohort or election cycle shall appoint a majority of any constitutional oversight body. Terms shall be staggered, non-renewable, and longer than any single Assembly term. Removal shall require supermajority authorization and shall be permitted only for defined constitutional grounds.* S-2 is the **appointment-diversity / capture-resistance** safeguard.

---

## 2. Context for the capability set (not mechanisms)

**Capture vectors S-2 must resist:** V-1 single-cohort majority · V-2 cumulative capture across cycles · V-3 renewal leverage · V-4 removal leverage · V-5 nomination control · V-6 vacancy/timing manipulation.

**NRNA aggravator:** MA holds ~15 appointment-bearing functions and is source-of-source for all trust roots — the appointment face of **Meta-CVI**.

**Discovery lens — Progressive Appointment Normalization (PAN):** capture accretes through individually lawful appointments until the body is aligned (lens, not doctrine). PAN is why a *drift-detection* capability is first-class below.

These shape *which capabilities are needed*; they do not prescribe mechanisms.

## 3. Pass 1 — S-2 Governance Capability Catalog (purpose only)

### GC-S2-01 — Appointment Distribution Capability
**Purpose:** Ensure multiple distinct sources participate in constituting an oversight body, so no single source solely constitutes it.
*Realizes:* plurality of origin. **Carries the load-bearing residual:** under a single sovereign, distribution is *functional* (different participating sources/timings), not *structural* in origin — the S-2 face of "managed, not eliminated." *(Addresses V-1, V-2, V-5.)*

### GC-S2-02 — Cohort-Limiting Capability
**Purpose:** Ensure no single Assembly cohort or election cycle can seat a dominating share of any oversight body, at a point in time or cumulatively.
*Realizes:* the "no majority by one cohort" core of S-2. Distinct from GC-S2-01: *distribution* = many participate; *cohort-limiting* = none dominates. *(Addresses V-1, V-2.)*

### GC-S2-03 — Tenure Protection Capability
**Purpose:** Insulate sitting members from appointment- and removal-based leverage during their service.
*Realizes:* independence-in-office. *(Addresses V-3, V-4.)*

### GC-S2-04 — Nomination Integrity Capability
**Purpose:** Prevent capture of the candidate-selection/nomination stage (controlling the shortlist captures the body even under diverse final appointment).
*Realizes:* integrity upstream of appointment. *(Addresses V-5.)*

### GC-S2-05 — Appointment Transparency Capability
**Purpose:** Make appointment provenance and body composition observable to constitutional actors.
*Realizes:* visibility (precondition for detection and challenge). *(Addresses detection across vectors.)*

### GC-S2-06 — Composition Drift Detection Capability
**Purpose:** Detect, over multiple cycles, that body composition is trending toward single-cohort alignment.
*Realizes:* defense against **PAN** as a first-class capability. **Capability coupling (Pass-1 Rule):** *requires* **GC-S4-01 (Challenge Invocation)** and **GC-S4-02 (Standing Protection)** — detection without a route to act is inert. *(This is the S-2↔S-4 coupling that was invisible at the mechanism layer; carries forward OQ-GCD01-03/04.)*

### GC-S2-07 — Vacancy & Succession Integrity Capability
**Purpose:** Prevent manufactured vacancies and ensure legitimate, non-capturable succession.
*Realizes:* continuity that cannot be weaponized. *(Addresses V-6.)*
