## Part 2 — Falsification (pre-registered, BEFORE F-PROC)

| Candidate observation | What would falsify it |
|-----------------------|------------------------|
| **M-01** composite is the unit of design | a family where **one mechanism consistently dominates** (single-mechanism solution is best) |
| **M-02** mechanism quality ≠ composite quality | a family where the **highest-quality mechanism always yields the highest-quality composite** |
| **M-03** behaviour is emergent | a family where the target behaviour is simply the **sum** of independent mechanism contributions |
| **M-04** novelty is in composition | a family requiring an **entirely novel mechanism** rather than a new composition |
| **M-05** orthogonal dimensions sufficient | a family that requires a **new design dimension** beyond D1–D5 (note: F-AUTH's D1–D5 are F-AUTH-derived; other families may need different/additional dimensions — see caution) |

**Disposition:** if F-PROC falsifies M-01 (a single mechanism dominates), the methodology *learns* rather than fails — M-01 narrows to *"composite evaluation is necessary for some capability families, not all."* That refinement is itself a valuable, recordable result.

## Part 3 — Two kinds of emergence (do not conflate)

| Kind | Definition | Lives in |
|------|-----------|----------|
| **Architectural emergence** | interaction *among mechanisms* creates new governance behaviour (e.g. D1+D2+D4 jointly resist capture) | the **evaluation template** ("Emergence" field = *architectural* emergence) |
| **Research emergence** | the *methodology itself* discovers a new design principle (e.g. the shift from evaluating mechanisms to evaluating composites) | **synthesis/validation docs** (here), never the per-mechanism template |

Conflating them would let a per-mechanism score masquerade as a methodology-level discovery, or vice versa. Architectural emergence is *measured per composite*; research emergence is *recorded as a methodological observation* (M-01…M-06).

## Part 6 — Observation lifecycle (prevents premature elevation)

Every methodological observation carries one state:

```
Observed → Replicated → Reinforced → Working Method Principle → General Principle
```

- **Observed** — seen in one family.
- **Replicated** — seen independently in a second.
- **Reinforced** — holds across three+.
- **Working Method Principle** — holds across all Pass-2 families without refutation.
- **General Principle** — claimed beyond NRNA (requires external evidence; far off).

**After F-AUTH (one family), M-01–M-06 are all at "Observed"** (M-05/M-06 additionally *process-adopted*). Nothing is past "Observed" yet — by design.

## Part 7 — Methodology dependency graph

```
M-05 (orthogonal dimensions)
```
