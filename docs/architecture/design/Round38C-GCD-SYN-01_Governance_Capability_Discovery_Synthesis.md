## Part 1 — What the five safeguards taught us about governance (evidence first)

*Deliberately answered before EGCP is mentioned.*

**Patterns that appeared repeatedly:**
- A recurring **lifecycle**: Authority → Exercise → Observation → Challenge → (terminal closure).
- A recurring **recursion** at exactly one point per safeguard (the GRP) — always a single-source self-application; never closable.
- A recurring **Observation→Challenge edge**: detection is inert unless it connects to a route to act.
- **Challenge behaving as shared infrastructure** rather than as a per-safeguard feature.
- A recurring **threshold/entrenchment** capability and a recurring **authority-distribution** capability.

**What never appeared (equally informative):**
- **No safeguard required an Appeal stage** — even S-5, the prime candidate, *rejected* it.
- **No safeguard lacked Observation** — none was purely preventive.
- **No safeguard could eliminate its GRP** — none closed its recursion.
- **No safeguard operated without a Challenge route** — all route to S-4.

**Which capabilities were shared (not safeguard-specific):**
- **Challenge** (GC-S4-01/02) — used by S-1, S-2, S-3.
- **Threshold / entrenchment** (GC-S1-02) — reused by S-3 (scope entrenchment) and S-5 (override threshold).
- **Observability** — a near-identical capability appears in *every* safeguard.
- **Authority distribution** — appears in S-1 (ratification breadth) and S-2 (appointment distribution).

**Which safeguard was structurally different:**
- **S-3 (Jurisdiction)** — the only safeguard that governs **boundaries *between* safeguards** (interactions, not actions). The lone "meta / relationship-oriented" safeguard. (PARTIAL/STRESSED against the lifecycle.)

**Which lifecycle assumptions survived vs were refined:**
- **Survived:** the five-stage spine (S-1/S-2/S-4/S-5); the GRP invariant; the Observation→Challenge edge; the no-Appeal finding.
- **Refined:** the terminal stage is **Resolution** (a *closure operator*) producing **{Confirmation | Correction}** — not bare Correction.
- **Stressed / open:** whether S-3's Competence Determination is an *Authority refinement* (EGCP holds) or evidence that S-3 sits at a *different level* (EGCP scope-bounded).

**Common properties:** each is a single-source self-application point; each is **ineliminable** under one sovereign; each is **surrounded and made observable, not closed.** **One invariant (GRP-01), five instances.** This is the structural echo, at the capability layer, of 38C-15's *"managed, not eliminated."* GRP-01 held in *all five* safeguards — including the one (S-3) that stressed the lifecycle. **Within the current evidence, GRP exhibits stronger empirical stability than the EGCP lifecycle** (it survived the case where EGCP did not fully fit). This does **not** make GRP universal — only empirically stronger *inside this research*: GRP may be the deeper invariant, with EGCP as the (mostly-fitting) surface structure.
