This is a **governance/value decision**, not an evidence claim. Two statements must be kept distinct, and only the first is asserted here:

- **Statement A (asserted):** *"The constitutional owner's preferred direction is Option B, with additional constitutional safeguards, and the program shall proceed on that basis."*

## 2. The decision

> The NRNA constitutional architecture adopts **Functional Independence (Option B)** as the governing constitutional philosophy.
>
> This decision is based on:
> - consistency with comparative constitutional practice,
> - preservation of a unified constitutional source,
> - reduced institutional complexity,
> - and the judgment that independence can be adequately protected through constitutional safeguards.
>
> The program recognises that **constitutional infancy** increases the risk of capture, erosion, and self-reference. Therefore Functional Independence shall **not** exist as a bare constitutional declaration. **Additional constitutional protections shall be specified** to preserve operational independence, challengeability, and constitutional observability.

---

## 3. What the evidence does and does not show

The decision is consistent with — but not compelled by — the 38C evidence chain. The evidence shows:

- Option B is consistent with comparative constitutional practice (F-08; Stream 2 corroboration).
- Option B carries **additional risk in constitutionally young organisations** (transferability / JC-03; maturity-as-variable, corroborated lens).
- Those risks can **potentially** be mitigated through additional safeguards (the five minimum conditions; condition 5 remains load-bearing — JC-02/JC-05).
- Option B remains viable. Option A remains viable. A phased model remains theoretically viable.

The final choice is therefore a constitutional value judgment, not a mathematical proof.

## 6. Open questions routed to 38C-15

The decision sets direction; it does not specify the constitutional detail. 38C-15 (ARB Ruling) must resolve:

1. **Permanent vs sunsetting safeguards (THE FORK).** Are the additional protections permanent, or do they relax as NRNA matures?
   - *Permanent* → no maturity gate required; EV-04.5 / maturity-governability question moots.
   - *Sunsetting* → the maturity-gate governability problem (EV-04.5 outcome (c): "maturity real but not objectively governable") **returns**, and 38C-15 must define an objective, challengeable transition trigger or reject sunsetting.
2. **Which safeguards** (derived from the five minimum conditions; condition 5 / structural-external-vantage explicitly addressed).
3. **Boundaries of Functional Independence** — what MA may and may not do to the independent functions.
4. **Allowed DDD discovery scope** — what strategic discovery is unlocked, and what remains gated.
