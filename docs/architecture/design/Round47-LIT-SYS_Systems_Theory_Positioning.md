## 1. Why systems theory applies

The certified architecture (`Round42-01/02`) is explicitly a **feedback control system**: setpoint = legitimacy, power supply = consent, sensor = detection (F-THR), comparator = adjudication (F-REV), actuator = correction-by-acceptance, plant = the election process (F-PROC), calibration = appointment (F-AUTH). Feedback loops, correction loops, trust anchors, recursion, emergence — these are **systems-theory primitives.** Positioning against that literature gives the architecture a second, independent theoretical vocabulary.

## 3. Viable System Model (Beer) mapping

The four families + substrate map onto VSM's five systems:

| VSM | Function | NRNA correspondent |
|-----|----------|--------------------|
| **System 1** Operations | do the primary work | **Voting / F-PROC** (cast/record) |
| **System 2** Coordination | anti-oscillation, scheduling | **Election Lifecycle Governance** (state machine) |
| **System 3** Control / audit | here-and-now regulation; monitoring | **Audit + Authorization** (operational control) |
| **System 4** Intelligence | environment, future, threats | **F-THR** (threat/capture detection, anticipation) |
| **System 5** Policy / identity | ultimate authority, identity | **Consent / the constituent (Trust-Anchor) + Constitution** |

**Finding (SYS-1).** The mapping is strikingly clean — and the most important alignment is **System 5 = identity/ultimate authority ↔ the constituent (consent) as the trust anchor.** VSM independently predicts that ultimate authority resides at the "identity" apex, corresponding to our consent-anchor result (the final anchor is the constituent, not any organ). *Correspondence, not proof.*

## 4. Requisite Variety strengthens the Meta-CVI explanation

**Finding (SYS-2).** Ashby's Law gives a crisper account of the program's dominant residual risk: capture resistance (the regulator) needs **variety ≥ the attacker's variety**; the **meta** capture vector (capturing the regulator itself) is where requisite variety *cannot* be met internally, because **Conant–Ashby** + second-order cybernetics say a regulator cannot fully model/regulate itself without an external vantage. This **strengthens** (does not change) the certified "Meta-CVI managed, not eliminated" finding and the F-4 residual: the residual is now explicable as a *requisite-variety / self-reference limit*, not merely an observation. **Routed as an explanatory note**, not a model change.
