## 3. Viable System Model (Beer) mapping

The four families + substrate map onto VSM's five systems:

| VSM | Function | NRNA correspondent |
|-----|----------|--------------------|
| **System 1** Operations | do the primary work | **Voting / F-PROC** (cast/record) |
| **System 2** Coordination | anti-oscillation, scheduling | **Election Lifecycle Governance** (state machine) |
| **System 3** Control / audit | here-and-now regulation; monitoring | **Audit + Authorization** (operational control) |
| **System 4** Intelligence | environment, future, threats | **F-THR** (threat/capture detection, anticipation) |
| **System 5** Policy / identity | ultimate authority, identity | **Consent / the constituent (Trust-Anchor) + Constitution** |

**Finding (SYS-1) — Partial correspondence (not equivalence).** Our architecture **resembles** VSM in that ultimate constitutional identity is represented by **the constituent** rather than by an operational body — *analogous to* VSM's System-5 "identity" role. **Beer did not discuss constitutional consent**, so this is a structural correspondence, **not** a claim that VSM predicts or proves our consent-anchor result. Phrase as: *"our architecture resembles VSM because ultimate constitutional identity rests in the constituent, not an operational organ."*

## 4. Requisite Variety strengthens the Meta-CVI explanation

**Finding (SYS-2) — explanatory interpretation (not proof).** Ashby's Law **provides an explanatory interpretation** for why the Meta-CVI residual may be unavoidable: capture resistance (the regulator) needs variety ≥ the attacker's; the **meta** vector (capturing the regulator itself) is where internal requisite variety cannot be met, which **is compatible with** the limitation Conant & Ashby and second-order cybernetics discuss (a regulator cannot fully model/regulate itself without an external vantage). This **offers an explanation for**, not a proof of, the certified "Meta-CVI managed, not eliminated" residual. The *constitutional* framing of Meta-CVI remains a **Novel** contribution (no direct equivalent found). **Explanatory note only**, not a model change.
