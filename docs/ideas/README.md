# 💡 Idea Incubator — Vision Parking Lot

> **This folder contains ideas, visions, and strategic hypotheses.**
>
> **Nothing in this folder is architecture, governance, or a decision.**
> Ideas move into Discovery only when a formal commission is opened.
> **They may also remain here forever.**

> ## **Ideas are free. Architecture is expensive.**
> Every idea is welcome here. No idea becomes architecture without passing
> through the normal discovery, evidence, and governance process.

## The four maturity states of this repository

| Folder | State |
|---|---|
| `docs/adr/` | **We decided.** |
| `docs/architecture/` · `engineering/` | **We built / validated.** |
| `docs/plans/` | **We are doing.** |
| `docs/ideas/` | 💡 **We might explore someday.** |

## Rules of the parking lot

1. **Every idea file carries:** the vision · why it is interesting · the evidence that would promote it · what is NOT allowed today · any canon guard (rulings it currently collides with).
2. **Sketches are welcome here** — this is the one place API payloads and five-year diagrams may live without being pre-solutions, because the disclaimer above strips them of authority.
3. **Ideas shrink or leave; they do not grow in place** *(the backlog rule applies: an entry becomes less detailed over time unless new evidence arrives)*.
4. **Promotion path:** idea → evidence accumulates → commission opened (scope · sponsor · questions · closure) → discovery → governance. Never idea → implementation.

## Why ideas die

An idea may remain here forever. Reasons include:

- never enough evidence
- the problem disappeared
- a better solution emerged
- the technology changed
- business priorities changed

> **Ideas do not fail by staying here. They fail only if promoted without evidence.**
