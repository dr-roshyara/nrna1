## Phase 1 — `OQ-1` **the carrier decision** · ⛔ GATES EVERYTHING

**The problem.** KnowledgeOS has **no declared mathematical carrier.** Every experimental result was
produced on `r = (value, source, tag, time)` with `|D| ≤ 6`.

$$\boxed{\textbf{No experiment can decide this, and running more on the synthetic carrier will not bring it closer.}}$$

**Why it gates:** the tactical DDD model cannot complete without it, and **every `[EXP]` in the
estate is scope-bound to a carrier that is admittedly not the real one.**

- **Deliverable:** a governance decision naming the carrier, **informed by — not derived from — the
  experiments.**
- **Owner:** Governance / the user.
- **My input (`V1`) — a carrier options paper, read-only, no selection:** enumerate every carrier
  actually in use across `brainstorming/` with occurrence counts; for each candidate, state what
  every existing `[EXP]` would inherit or lose. **Multiplicity surfaced, never resolved.**
- **Note:** a **measurable** Knowledge Space `KS = (𝒳,𝒜)` with a filtration **is** already defined
  (2026-09-02); only the probability law is deferred. That definition is a live candidate and must be
  in the options paper.
