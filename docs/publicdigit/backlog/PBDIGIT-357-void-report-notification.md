# PBDIGIT-357: a dedicated report of a voided membership record to the organisation

**Status:** Open (created 2026-11-16 from D-243; not in PBDIGIT-354's scope).
**Context:** Membership (the fact) · Communication / Organisation (the notice).

## Finding

- D-206 says a Void is "reported to the organisation and challengeable". D-243 (Q-8 A) meets that in S2-5:
  - the Void carries its recorder, authority answer and evidence reference;
  - its effect is visible through C1.
- **A dedicated notice is not built:** who receives it (registrar, board, the member?), through which channel, and how a challenge is lodged and tracked.

## What is needed (model first)

1. **Who is told, and how.** Recipients and channel. A Communication capability, or Organisation's notices (D-179 area).
2. **The contract.** A published Membership event (e.g. "record voided") would be a new Published-Language contract: design and ARB acceptance before any code, with an accepted consumer.
3. **The challenge.** How a challenge of a record made from evidence is lodged and resolved (D-148, D-159).

**Traceability:** D-206 · D-243 · PBDIGIT-354 S2-5a.
