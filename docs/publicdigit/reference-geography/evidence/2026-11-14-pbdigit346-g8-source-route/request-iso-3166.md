# Draft: ISO 3166-1, terms and licensing

**Step 1 (in a normal browser; iso.org refuses automated access):**
- read https://www.iso.org/iso-3166-country-codes.html and ISO's copyright and terms pages;
- save the pages, or the relevant sentences, with the date.

**Questions to settle:**
- (a) may the codes be used freely?
- (b) may the list (names plus codes) be stored and shown in a commercial platform?
- (c) what is the official data product, its price and its licence?

**Step 2: if the terms don't settle it, write to ISO** (the contact given on the ISO 3166 page, or ISO's copyright office).

---

Dear ISO 3166 Maintenance Agency,

[Organisation / sender name] operates PublicDigit, a digital governance platform for political parties and civil-society organisations (a paid service). We would like to:

1. **store** the current ISO 3166-1 list (English short names and alpha-2, alpha-3 and numeric codes) as source evidence;
2. **use** it as the authority for which countries and areas exist in the platform, with their codes, **attributed to ISO 3166-1**;
3. **keep it current** from ISO's updates, keeping the history.

Could you tell us whether this use is permitted, under which terms, and, if a licence is needed, which ISO product (for example the Country Codes Collection or a data service) and licence apply?

Kind regards,
[name, role, organisation, contact]
