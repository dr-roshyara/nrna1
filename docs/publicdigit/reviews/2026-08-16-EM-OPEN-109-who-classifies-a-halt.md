# Governance Analysis — `EM-OPEN-109`: who may determine that a halted condition is unsatisfiable?

**Type:** Governance analysis (Session 2) · **Date:** 2026-08-16 · **Commission:** *"Resolve `EM-OPEN-109` first · classifier independent of the Chief · invent no authority · test deterministic rule-based classification FIRST · keep `EM-OPEN-071` separate unless the analysis shows they cannot be separated."*
**⛔ `C-1`/`C-2`/`C-3` REMAIN PROPOSED, NOT ADOPTED. Nothing adopted here. `EM-GOV-037` untouched · Architecture ⏸️ · Session 3 🛑.**

---

## 0 · The result, stated first

> ## **`EM-OPEN-109` IS HALF-ANSWERABLE TODAY, AND THE HALVES DIVIDE ALONG THE CLASSIFICATION'S OWN GRAIN.**
>
> * ✅ **`C-3` (unsatisfiable UNDER THE RULES) needs NO authority at all — Option C already covers it, structurally.** **`C-3` asks a question about the RULES, and reading the rules is not the exercise of discretion.**
> * 🔴 **`C-2` (unsatisfiable IN FACT) can NEVER be reached by Option C — it asks a question about the WORLD, and no rule can observe a fact.** **The record establishes no authority competent to assert one, so this half cannot be closed without new policy.**

---

## 1 · Option C tested first, as instructed — and it works better than expected, for one grade

**Example B, decided today with no discretion whatsoever:** candidacy has legitimately completed → supplying a candidate now requires reopening it → **reopening returns the lifecycle to a completed phase, which `SCB-4` forbids** → **no permitted governed act exists** → the halt is `C-3`.

**Nobody exercised judgement. The rules answered it.** **And that generalises: `C-3` is *"is there a permitted governed act?"*, which is a closed question over the rule set, so it is decidable by reading — exactly the deterministic path the commission asked to be tested first.**

> ### ⚠️ **1.1 · The one qualification — and it is where `EM-OPEN-071` genuinely bites**
> **`C-3` is deterministic ONLY WHERE THE RULES SPEAK CLEARLY.** **Where the rule set is AMBIGUOUS about whether some act is permitted, *"is there a permitted act?"* stops being a reading question and becomes an INTERPRETATION question — which is `EM-OPEN-071` precisely.**
>
> **So the commission's separability test is answered, and with a precise boundary rather than a preference:** **`109` and `071` SEPARATE wherever the rules determine the answer, and they DO NOT SEPARATE where the rules are silent or ambiguous about the existence of a permitted act.** **`071` is therefore not a prerequisite of `109` — it is the fallback `109` lands in when determinism runs out.**

## 2 · Why Option C cannot be stretched to cover `C-2` — and the trap in trying

**`C-2` asks whether the world can supply the condition (has the appointing authority been abolished? has the sole remaining candidate died?). No rule observes a fact.** **The obvious workaround is a deeming rule:**

```
"If no appointment is recorded within period P, the condition is deemed unsatisfiable."
```

> ### ⛔ **THAT WORKAROUND IS THE EXCLUDED FAILURE MODE WEARING A RULE'S CLOTHING.**
> **Elapsed time cannot distinguish *the authority no longer exists* from *the authority has not yet got round to it*.** **A time-based deeming rule therefore converts INACTION into IMPOSSIBILITY by construction — the precise thing the classification test was built to defeat, and what `EM-GOV-012` and `EM-OPEN-047` forbid.**
> **Verified against the record: no rule in the adopted set deems anything unsatisfiable today. The trap is available but untaken, and Governance recommends it stay untaken.**

### 2.1 · ⚠️ And most apparent `C-2` cases are not `C-2` at all

**External silence is an AXIS-2 fact, not an impossibility.** **`EM-OPEN-102` already rules that for an external actor the election can only bound ITS OWN RESPONSE.** **So an unresponsive appointing authority is handled by bounded waiting — not by a finding that the world has made the act impossible.**

**Genuine `C-2` therefore reduces to a narrow residue: a POSITIVE external fact of record.** **Derived property for any future `C-2` rule, offered as a property and not as policy:** ***a `C-2` finding should cite a positive external fact; it must never be inferred from silence.***

## 3 · The four options, tested

| | Verdict |
|---|---|
| **A — Chief** | ⛔ **Rejected, and the rejection GENERALISES.** **The principle is not *"not the Chief"* but *"the classifier must not be the actor whose performance the classification would excuse."*** **Stated role-independently it also covers future responsible actors — representatives in the acceptance cluster, or whoever a later rule makes responsible — where a named-role prohibition would silently fail to.** |
| **B — Committee** | ⛔ **Structurally incomplete, and not by degree.** ⚠️ **A halt can occur before the Committee is constituted, and the Committee cannot classify a halt that precedes its own existence.** **So B can never be the WHOLE answer.** **Completeness test any authority-based answer must pass: does it cover the EARLIEST phases, or does it say explicitly that those phases have no `C-2` route at all?** |
| **C — deterministic rule** | ✅ **Covers `C-3` completely, today, with no new authority and no new policy.** ⛔ **Cannot reach `C-2` (§2).** |
| **D — independent authority** | 🔴 **THE RECORD ESTABLISHES NONE.** **Checked, not assumed: the Committee's ADJUDICATIVE power is expressly WITHHELD (`EM-OPEN-071`, deliberately not folded into `EM-GOV-029`) · the *organisational governance authority* appears only in a SPECIFIC role, appointing the Committee and filling its vacancies (`EM-GOV-058`) · the Service Provider is BARRED from consequences by an adopted rule, being permitted to set duration only.** **So D requires NEW POLICY, and Governance does not invent it.** |

## 4 · 🔴 A coupling the commission's sequence does not yet account for

> **The DANGER of granting `C-2` authority is not fixed — it is set by the still-open expiry question in `EM-OPEN-042`:**
>
> * **If expiry of the recovery period is itself terminal:** a `C-2` finding only makes the terminal outcome arrive SOONER. **The authority being granted is the power to SHORTEN A WAIT.**
> * **If expiry is NOT terminal:** a `C-2` finding is the SOLE route out of a permanently halted election. **The authority being granted is the power to END AN ELECTION.**
>
> **These are different powers, and they warrant different safeguards.** ⚠️ **So `109`'s `C-2` half cannot have its risk assessed while `042`'s expiry consequence is unknown.** **This does NOT disturb the instructed sequence for the `C-3` half, which is decidable now and independently — it means the `C-2` half and `042` are mutually informing, and Governance reports that rather than resolving it.**

## 5 · What Governance recommends — on structure only, no rule proposed

1. **Adopt nothing yet**, as instructed.
2. **`C-3` may proceed on Option C when the classification is eventually adopted** — no authority question stands in its way, and no new policy is required.
3. **`C-2` remains BLOCKED on new policy.** **Governance will not name an authority the record does not establish.**
4. **If a deterministic route to `C-2` is wanted, it must rest on a positive external fact of record — never on elapsed time** (§2).
5. **`EM-OPEN-071` stays separate**, per §1.1, **with its boundary now named rather than assumed.**
