# Decision Request — `EM-OPEN-042` reframed: does a blocked election enter a governed Suspension?

**Type:** Governance decision request (Session 2) · **Date:** 2026-08-15 · **Business language only**
**⛔ Nothing adopted. The PO's interpretation is expressly NOT treated as adopted, per instruction. No architecture. `EM-OPEN-039` untouched. Architecture ⏸️ · Session 3 🛑.**

## ⚠️ 0 · Read this first — "suspension" is already taken, and it means something else

**The Manifesto already carries an adopted, actively-questioned concept called *suspension* — and it is the suspension of an ELECTION MEMBER, not of an election.**

| Existing | Subject |
|---|---|
| **`EM-GOV-002`** *"The Election Chief may independently suspend an **ElectionMember** for an election-specific reason"* | a member |
| **`EM-GOV-003`** Chief suspension vs organisation-driven suspension are distinct acts | a member |
| **`EM-EO-004`**, **`EM-FM-003`**, **`EM-FM-004`** | a member's `ElectionMembership` |
| **`EM-OPEN-002`, `003`, `006`, `007`, `009`** — one/two-actor act · where the decision lives · credential effects · distinguishability at the gate · temporary or indefinite | **all five are about member suspension** |
| *Referenced architectural principle:* *"suspension freezes capabilities only; it does not mutate business facts"* | stated generally |

> ## **This is a THIRD vocabulary overload — and the most consequential of the three.**
>
> The earlier two (*eligible*, *outcome*) collided across two concepts. **This one would collide with an adopted rule that grants an authority.** If election-suspension enters under the same word, **`EM-GOV-002` — "the Election Chief may independently suspend" — reads as authorizing the Chief to suspend the ELECTION**, which nobody has decided and which the PO's own Q3/Q4 pose as open questions.
>
> **Five open questions would also become ambiguous overnight**, since each would need re-reading to determine which suspension it concerns.

**⛔ Governance's first request, before any of the nine questions is answered: name the two apart.** Candidate pairing offered and **not** adopted: **member suspension** vs **election suspension** — or a different word entirely for the election-level concept. *(ES-005.4 — consume or extend an existing concept, never create a second one wearing its name.)*

*Evidence, not authority: an election-level suspended condition also exists in the running system. Governance cites it only as evidence that the concept exists somewhere, never as authority for what it means.*

## 1 · The reframed question

> **When a mandatory condition prevents an election from progressing, does the election enter a governed Suspension — and what are the rules for resolving and resuming it?**

**Governance confirms the reframing is the better question.** *"What happens to the failed phase?"* asked about a part; the real subject is the **election**.

## 2 · Compatibility with what is already adopted — checked, and it holds

* **`EM-GOV-011` says progression *stops*. It does NOT say the election is suspended.** **Stopping and being suspended are not the same thing**, and nothing currently bridges them — that bridge is question **Q1** below, not an assumption.
* **No collision with *"no downstream phase is assigned an artificial outcome"***, because **suspension attaches to the ELECTION, not to each phase.** The PO stated this explicitly; Governance records the check rather than assuming it. *(A per-phase suspension marking would collide, and would be the wrong model for the same reason a per-phase outcome was.)*
* **`EM-VOC-005`/`EM-VOT-005` remain binding through any resumption:** no authorization, refusal or eligibility decision survives into a new opportunity.

## 3 · Two of the nine are already answered — DERIVED, not open

> **`Q9` — can any authority use Suspension to bypass an Election Rule? **NO.** DERIVED, already binding.**
> `EM-GOV-008`: **no individual authority may override a mandatory Election Rule.** `EM-VOC-005`/`EM-VOT-005`: **nothing carries into a new opportunity.** Therefore *rule fails → suspend → unsuspend → proceed on the old authorization* **is already impossible**, whatever else is decided. **Suspension is a recovery mechanism, not an authority override** — this needs no new ruling, only recording.

> **`Q8` — must every suspension, reason, corrective action, removal and rescheduling event be recorded? **YES.** DERIVED.**
> `EM-GOV-005` and its extended negative half: material events are recorded, history is preserved, **and nothing is manufactured for what did not happen.**

## 4 · One is largely derived, with a single open edge

> **`Q6` — does removing Suspension resume the old opportunity, or require a new one?**
>
> **DERIVED:** **if the schedule changes, `EM-VOC-005` forces a new opportunity** — a corrected schedule is always a new opportunity and always needs a new authorization. **If the schedule does NOT change**, no correction has occurred, and `P-2H` says a refusal never terminated the opportunity — **so the original opportunity may still be live.**
> **OPEN edge:** whether it is still live depends on **when an opportunity ceases to be valid** — `EM-OPEN-026`(a) / `EM-OPEN-028`, both open. **So: resumption onto a NEW schedule is settled; resumption onto the UNCHANGED schedule is not.**

## 5 · Genuinely open — PO/ARB decision required

## 6 · Two further observations, offered not decided

**① Suspension and the four-eyes question are the same question twice.** `EM-OPEN-035` asks whether four-eyes applies to publication and correction; Q3/Q4 ask the same of suspension. **Governance's earlier finding applies unchanged: a safeguard on one door only sends the traffic to the other.** **These should be decided as one authority model, not three separate ones.**

**② `EM-OPEN-009` should travel with this.** *"Is suspension temporary or indefinite?"* is currently a member-suspension question, **but the same question arises for the election** — and it cannot be asked coherently until §0's naming is settled.

**None on the substance.** Governance confirms the reframing, records what is already binding, and returns the rest. **Its one request is procedural: name the two suspensions apart before answering anything else**, because every answer given under a shared word will have to be re-read later to find out which concept it bound.
