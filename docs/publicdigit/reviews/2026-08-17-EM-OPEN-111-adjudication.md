# `EM-OPEN-111` Adjudication — the OPEN ∧ INOPERATIVE region (`EM-GOV-070`/`071` PREPARED)

**Type:** Governance adjudication (Session 2, commissioned as the next act, 2026-08-17)
**⛔ BOTH RULES PREPARED, NOT ADOPTED. The behaviour-freeze stands until adoption. §2b correction remains Architecture's separate act — this adjudication FIXES ITS SCOPE, it does not perform it.**

## Part A — Is `OPEN ∧ INOPERATIVE` a valid state? **YES, BY DERIVATION — no new policy**

Three adopted facts settle it: **① both classifications derive rule-faithfully from adopted text** (the standing accept, `066`, keeps the threshold achievable → `068` OPEN; the vacancy arithmetic, `065` → Inoperative — C-5's executed proof); **② no adopted rule excludes the combination**; **③ combined conditions are already REQUIRED to be representable** — `059`(b) mandates retaining `HALTED ∧ INOPERATIVE` as two facts. **The design §2b commentary was simply wrong, and only the commentary.**

**The clock-gloss tension dissolves on subject-scoping, also by derivation:** `068`'s *"no recovery period runs"* speaks of **the gate** — no period attaches to a gate or runs *on account of* its openness. `062`'s clocks attach to **election conditions**: *"the Committee-restoration clock runs only while the election is Election Inoperative."* In the combined region a clock runs **because the election is Inoperative, not because a gate is open** — two subjects, no contradiction. *(This is the specific-governs reading; `062` is the clock rule.)*

> ### **`EM-GOV-070` (PREPARED):** *"OPEN and Election Inoperative are independent conditions that may hold simultaneously: a gate's interval state derives from positions and achievability on recorded facts (`EM-GOV-068`), and Election Inoperative derives from vacancy arithmetic (`EM-GOV-065`). No recovery period attaches to a gate or runs on account of a gate's openness; each recovery clock attaches solely to its election-level triggering condition (`EM-GOV-062`). Where both conditions hold, the Committee-restoration clock runs because the election is Inoperative."*

## Part B — What does Inoperative permit? **READING A RECOMMENDED — and it is nearly derived**

**The decisive adopted words, `EM-GOV-059`(b):** Inoperative is *"an **additional operational condition preventing progression**, not a replacement for the halt's business meaning."* **The adopted text names what Inoperative does: it prevents PROGRESSION. It says nothing against member actions.** Three supporting derivations: **①** the `058` naming ruling made *Inoperative* describe a **condition**, expressly *"without implying anyone holds a power"* — reading B would convert the condition into a disability imposed on members, a larger thing than the name was ruled to carry; **②** `059`(c)'s *"restores the ability to make the decision"* was written for the arithmetic-blocked case and does not forbid a decision completing where standing votes keep it reachable; **③** **the manipulation test favours A symmetrically: cast votes stand for accept AND object (`066`), so vacancy-timing can erase neither support nor dissent — A adds no manipulation surface. Under B, inducing vacancies would silence the remaining member entirely.**

**What remains genuinely the PO's choice:** whether a gate decision *completing* during Inoperative is wanted as business behaviour. Under A: positions may be expressed and a gate may become decided while Inoperative; **progression still cannot occur until restoration** (`059`(b) — prevention of progression is untouched). Under B: a new guard, a new rule, a future authorized slice.

> ### **`EM-GOV-071` (PREPARED — Reading A form):** *"Election Inoperative prevents progression; it does not prevent the expression of Committee positions or the completion of an acceptance decision whose threshold remains reachable on recorded facts. A gate decided while the election is Inoperative takes effect on progression only when the election is again operative. Nothing in this rule alters `EM-GOV-059` or the recovery clocks."*
