## 3 · ⛔ THE NON-ADOPTION, RECORDED AS A RULE OF CONSTRUCTION

> **Adopting v4 does NOT constitute adoption of `EM-GOV-037` or of any representative threshold applicability floor.**

**`EM-GOV-037` is marked in the Manifesto as `PENDING — NEW POLICY REQUIRED`, with the exclusion stated in its own row**, so that no future reader can infer from *"the acceptance cluster was adopted"* that the representative threshold came with it. **It did not.**

## 4 · What is now in force

> ### ✅ **MODEL A — Committee-only acceptance — IS A COMPLETE AND OPERABLE ACCEPTANCE PATH.**
> Its rules are all adopted: the Committee's definition, independence, minimum size, vote type, threshold rule, denominator, vacancy filling, failure and cancellation, gate semantics and both clocks. **Its only outstanding dependency is the EXTERNAL organisational appointing authority (`EM-OPEN-066`) — not a Governance gap.**

> ### ⚠️ **MODEL C — Committee + representatives — IS DEFINED BUT NOT CONFIGURABLE.**
> All of its machinery is adopted — trustee model, selection, freeze, revocability, unavailability, resignation, denominator, attrition, recovery, substitution — **but `EM-GOV-031` requires a representative threshold that `EM-GOV-037` has not supplied.** **The channel is built and dormant.**

**Model B does not exist:** retired on evidence by `EM-GOV-048`, now in force.
