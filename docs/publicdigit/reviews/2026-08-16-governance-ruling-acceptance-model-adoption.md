# Governance Ruling Report — Acceptance Model Adoption

**Type:** Governance ruling (Session 2) · **Date:** 2026-08-16 · **Commission:** PO/ARB, *"Acceptance Model Adoption"*
**⛔ Business/governance only. No software, entity, API, UI or implementation decision. No threshold invented. Architecture ⏸️ · Session 3 🛑.**

**Authority:** the commission delegates adoption for §§1–3 (*"evaluate and, if consistent with the existing rules, adopt"*) and instructs a choice on §6. **Everything adopted below is labelled with which delegation carries it.**

---

## A · Adopted

**`A-1` · `EM-GOV-022` ADOPTED AS AMENDED** *(delegation §1)* — *"Candidate representatives exist to provide stakeholder trust, transparency and documented participation. **No individual representative** has authority to veto, cancel, suspend or otherwise prevent election progression. A representative may exercise the right to object, to withhold acceptance, or to raise a formal concern; such positions are recorded and considered according to the applicable Election Rules."*
> ✅ **`EM-OPEN-064` IS RESOLVED — by the commission's own corrected wording.** The word **individual** is now in the text, and §7 states the collective half explicitly: *the representative does not personally halt the election; the Election Rule does.* **The contradiction Governance raised is closed exactly as the offered wording proposed.**
> ✅ ***"Moral veto"* is not used.** ✅ **`no individual veto power + no silent dissent`** — and **the second half was already derived** (`EM-GOV-005` + `P-2H`), so only the first half was ever new.

**`A-2` · `EM-GOV-021` ADOPTED AS AMENDED** *(delegation §2)* — each candidate may select **at most one** representative; **selection is OPTIONAL**; a candidate selecting nobody contributes **no representation vote**; a representative may represent one or more candidates; **a person nobody selects is not a member**; a candidate may select from the Chief's pool or propose another eligible person. **`Proposed ≠ Selected ≠ Committee Member`.** **No fixed committee size.**

**`A-3` · `EM-GOV-024` ADOPTED** *(delegation §3)* — **weight(R) = number of candidates who selected R.** Thresholds express against **aggregate representation votes**, never against a count of representatives. ✅ **`EM-OPEN-062` RESOLVED.**

**`A-4` · `EM-OPEN-065` RULED — Option B** *(denominator = candidates who selected a representative)*. ⚠️ **Ruled as a CONSEQUENCE of `A-2`, not as a preference: the commission made selection OPTIONAL, and Option A would count a candidate who declined to participate as though they had objected — which turns an optional right into a trap.** ⛔ **If the PO wants Option A, optionality must be reconciled with it in the same act; one line overrides this.**

**`A-5` · `EM-GOV-023` ADOPTED** — **eligibility applies regardless of who proposes the person.** *(Conditions themselves remain open — `EM-OPEN-061`.)*

## B · Derived — already binding, no new policy

* **§7's progression logic in full:** threshold unsatisfied ⇒ acceptance condition unsatisfied ⇒ **progression halted** (`EM-GOV-011`), **and the halt does not by itself determine the election-level outcome** (`EM-GOV-012`, still pending).
* **§8's protocol list in full** — `EM-GOV-005` + its negative half + `P-2H`. **"An objection must not disappear merely because the threshold was achieved" IS `P-2H`** *(a successful request never erases the refusals that preceded it)*. **No new recording rule is required.**
* **§9's version binding** — *the applicable rule/version recorded with the election and with each acceptance decision* — is the **same principle** already derived for the recovery policy (`EM-OPEN-050`(b)): **the rules in force when a decision was made stay bound to that decision.** **One principle, third application.**
* **§10's separation of the two gates** — already established; they accept different objects.

## C · Still open

**`C-1` · `EM-OPEN-066` — the Election Committee does not exist.** ✅ **The commission RULES OUT the reading Governance had flagged: Chief and Deputy are operational partners and are **not automatically** the acceptance committee.** **Governance searched the record again: no Election Committee is defined — no membership, appointment, eligibility or independence rule.** **Registered as the missing authority definition, exactly as instructed.**

> ## ⛔ **Consequence: TWO OF THE THREE MODELS CANNOT BE CONFIGURED TODAY.**
> **Model A (Committee only)** and **Model C (Committee + representatives)** both name a body that does not exist. **Only Model B is usable.** **An election application offering all three would offer two options that cannot be satisfied.**

**`C-2` · A symmetry worth seeing: neither Model A nor Model B is complete, and in opposite ways.** **Model A has a THRESHOLD (≥2/3 of Committee members) but no BODY.** **Model B has a BODY (the representatives) but NO THRESHOLD** — the commission expressly forbids inventing one. **Model C has neither, plus an undecided combination rule.**

**`C-3` · The combination rule for Model C** — §5 forbids assuming Committee votes and representation votes can be added; the 50/50 example is **expressly not adopted**. **Undecided.**
**`C-4` · The Model B threshold** — undecided by instruction.
**`C-5` · `EM-OPEN-061`** representative eligibility conditions · **`EM-OPEN-060`** the residue *(standing to object to another's representative; whether cross-candidate agreement is required at all)*.
**`C-6` · `EM-OPEN-059`** — the Voting Preparation phase and its three amendments remain pending and unadopted.
