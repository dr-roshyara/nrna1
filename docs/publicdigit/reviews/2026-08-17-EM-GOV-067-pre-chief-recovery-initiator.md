## 1 · The question, and why scenario testing cannot answer it (the PO is right)

> **Who or what is responsible for initiating governed recovery when a halt occurs before any Election Chief exists?**

**Traced against the record, the answer space is almost empty by construction:**
* **No internal actor exists.** Chief and Deputy are appointed in the very phase that halted (`EM-GOV-026`); the Committee likewise; **the applicant is expressly excluded from operating** (`EM-GOV-027`).
* **No internal actor may be created.** No default authority may be invented (`EM-OPEN-049` safeguard); appointment power must not transfer inward on external inaction (`EM-OPEN-102` ruling).
* **And an initiator would have nothing to initiate.** The failed condition is the appointments themselves, which only the external organisational authority can supply (`EM-GOV-028`; the Chief-appointer wall, `049`). The only governed acts that exist — record the halt (`EM-GOV-011`), run the recovery clock (`062`), terminate at expiry (`063`) — **happen without anyone initiating them.**

> ## **SO THE GAP IS NOT A MISSING ACTOR. IT IS A MISSING SENTENCE — the record is silent where it should be express.**
> **Assigning an "initiator" who can only send reminders would create a duty without a power — the exact shape `EM-OPEN-102` already rejected for external actors.**

## 2 · `EM-GOV-067` (PREPARED) — close the gap by decision, not by silence

> *"Where an election's progression is halted before an Election Chief has been appointed, the recovery-initiation responsibility of `EM-GOV-014` Part 1 is not assignable to any person or body within the election. Recovery of such a halt occurs only through the competent organisational authority supplying the missing appointments. The halt, the awaited appointments and the applicable recovery period are recorded; expiry of that period has the consequence defined by `EM-GOV-063`."*

**This is the *"decided not to invent"* form the programme has used from the start: the silence becomes an adopted negative rule, so no future reader can mistake it for an unconsidered gap.** S-14's derivation becomes fully cited: *how the situation ends* was already determinate; *who could initiate* is now expressly **nobody inside the election** — and the two-reader criterion is satisfiable because the answer is a sentence of adopted text rather than an inference from absence.

⚠️ **Expressly NOT included, offered as a separable option:** a **notification duty** — someone informing the organisational authority that its election is halted awaiting appointments. Informing is not empowering (the Cell-② analysis), but *naming the notifier* is new policy (the Service Provider sits in service-policy territory; no election actor exists). **One line adds it if wanted; its absence changes no outcome, only visibility.**
