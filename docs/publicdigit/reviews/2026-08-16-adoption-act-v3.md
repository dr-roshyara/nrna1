# Adoption Act v3 — ready to sign *(three items)*

**Prepared by Governance (Session 2) · 2026-08-16 · Follows act v2, adopted earlier today**
**⛔ NOT adopted. This is the text of an act for the PO/ARB to perform.**

**Why v3 is small:** act v2 cleared thirteen items and left exactly three pending. **Two of those three are now unblocked**, and the third is adoptable in a form that locks the separation without inventing an organisational body.

---

## The act

> **PO/ARB ADOPTION ACT v3 — 2026-08-16**
>
> **1 · `EM-GOV-013`** — *A halted phase may be recovered through governed rescheduling. A reschedule creates new governed schedule conditions and requires protocol recording. Affected dependent phases must be reviewed and rescheduled where their validity depends on the changed predecessor schedule.*
>
> **2 · `SCB-1`/`SCB-9` scope** — *The Election Chief may not arbitrarily move a scheduled window forward. Where a phase has elapsed and the governed recovery mechanism requires the election to continue, the elapsed-window recovery rule may establish a new future window. **An elapsed-window correction under governed recovery is not ordinary schedule movement.***
>
> **3 · `EM-GOV-015` + the `EM-VOC-005` carve-out (`EM-OPEN-051`)** — the voting-schedule freeze and exceptional Service-Provider extension **as recorded**, together with: *"**Notwithstanding `EM-VOC-005`, an exceptional extension approved after voting has started continues the existing voting opportunity and does not supersede it. Votes already cast remain valid within it.**"*
>
> **4 · `EM-GOV-028`** — *The organisational governance body, or an authority expressly authorised by the organisation's governance rules, appoints the Election Committee and the Deputy Election Officer. The Election Chief has no appointment authority over either, and coordinates and facilitates the process, which is recorded in the election protocol together with its outcome. Committee independence from the Election Chief is mandatory.*
>
> **— Signed: PO/ARB, 2026-08-16**

## Notes on each item

**Item 1** was blocked only on item 2; nothing else in the boundary set constrains the recovery *mechanism*.
**Item 2** is the confirmation Governance asked for on 2026-08-16. **It preserves both principles rather than sacrificing one.**
**Item 3** is the single sentence that has blocked `EM-GOV-015` since it was drafted. ✅ **The continuity it establishes is DERIVED, not preferred:** a new opportunity would require a fresh Chief authorization **mid-vote**, so voting would stop at the moment the extension was meant to let it continue.
**Item 4** is adoptable **even though `EM-OPEN-066` stays open.** ⚠️ **It states who does NOT appoint — which is the operative constraint — and defers WHICH body does.** **Adopting it locks the separation now; leaving it unadopted leaves the Chief's non-appointment resting on a derivation.**

## ⛔ Standing constraint registered with this act

> **The organisational appointment authority is a FIRST-CLASS EXTERNAL GOVERNANCE DEPENDENCY.** **It is upstream of the entire election** — it appoints the Deputy, Election Appointment is a precondition for Administration, and the amended chain makes Administration a precondition for everything.
>
> ⛔ **Architecture must not work around it.** **No inferred appointment mechanism, no default, no "the organisation" placeholder, no treating the Chief as appointer by omission.** **If the authority is absent, the correct behaviour is that the election cannot be constituted — not that something else constitutes it.**
