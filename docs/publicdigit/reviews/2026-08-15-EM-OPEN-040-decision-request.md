# Decision Request — `EM-OPEN-040`: the outcome of an opportunity whose predecessor never completes

**Type:** Governance decision request (Session 2) · **Date:** 2026-08-15 · **Business language only**
**⛔ No recommendation. No option ranked. No architecture. `EM-OPEN-039` not decided · `EM-OPEN-026`(a) untouched · Architecture ⏸️ · Session 3 🛑.**

---

## The question

> **What is the business outcome of a scheduled phase opportunity that never became progression-permitted, because its predecessor never legitimately completed?**

## Where the question bites — worked through in the adopted vocabulary

Administration is scheduled 1–5 September and does not legitimately complete. Candidacy is scheduled 6–10 September.

On 6 September: **the scheduled time is reached** · Candidacy is therefore **eligible for consideration** · the Chief **may request** progression · **progression is not permitted**, because Administration has not legitimately completed · **the request is refused and the reason is recorded**. *(`EM-GOV-010`, `EM-VOC-008`, `P-2H` — the model works, and this part needs no decision.)*

**What is undecided is what eventually becomes of the Candidacy opportunity itself.**

## Why none of the five adopted outcomes fits — confirmed, not merely asserted

| Adopted outcome | Why it does not fit |
|---|---|
| **started** | it never started |
| **temporarily unable to proceed while still valid** | accurate **while** it is valid — **it is a condition, not an ending** (`EM-VOC-004` confirmed reading) |
| **expired unused** | *unused* implies it **was usable**. This one **was never permitted to proceed at all** |
| **explicitly cancelled** | **nobody cancelled it** — cancellation implies an act by someone |
| **superseded by a later governed schedule** | **only if a correction actually happens.** If nobody corrects anything, nothing supersedes it |

**Nothing in the record forces the case into any of these, and `EM-VOC-004` requires outcomes to stay distinguishable.**

## What is already binding on any answer *(DERIVED — these exclude no option)*

* **It must remain distinguishable** (`EM-VOC-004`). Collapsing *"never became permitted"* into *"expired unused"* destroys the difference between *nobody used a real chance* and *there was never a chance* — the precise information loss that rule exists to prevent.
* **It must be recorded** (`EM-GOV-005`), with the refusals that preceded it preserved (`P-2H`).
* **It must not silently revive** (`EM-VOC-004`: an ended opportunity is not reusable).

## The options

**① A distinct sixth outcome.** The case gets its own name. *(Illustrative candidates only, none recommended: "never became available" · "unrealised" · "blocked by predecessor".)* **Cost:** the outcome vocabulary grows, and every later reader must know six rather than five.

**② One of the five is ruled to cover it, with the distinction carried by the protocol instead.** The outcome would be *expired unused*; the *reason* lives in the recorded refusals. **Cost:** the outcome alone no longer tells the story — two elections that ended very differently would carry the same outcome word, and the difference would be recoverable only by reading the refusal history.

**③ It ends only when something ends it** — a correction supersedes it, or someone cancels it — and until then it simply remains *temporarily unable to proceed*. **Cost:** if nobody acts, the opportunity **never ends**, and the election has no terminal position. **This option needs an answer to "does anything end it eventually?"**

**④ Another rule you state.**

## Two dependencies, raised rather than assumed

**① *When* it ends is not answerable yet.** Whatever is chosen, the moment an unreachable opportunity reaches its outcome depends on **`EM-OPEN-026`(a) and `EM-OPEN-028`** — the post-schedule decision period's meaning and its expiry are both open. **Governance therefore separates the two halves: *which outcome* is answerable now; *when it is reached* is not.**

**② Upstream failure and downstream unreachability are different business events, and may need different answers.** In the example, **Administration** was available and failed its own conditions. **Candidacy** was never reachable. **`EM-VOC-004`'s outcomes plausibly describe Administration already** *(it was usable and went unused)* — **the gap is specific to the downstream phases.** **Governance flags that a single answer covering both may be answering two questions at once.**

## One further question raised, not part of this decision

**One failure makes every later phase unreachable** — candidacy, voting, counting and result publication all reach the same fate. **Does the ELECTION itself then have an outcome?** No adopted rule gives an election a terminal business position when it cannot proceed. **Registered as `EM-OPEN-042`; not part of `EM-OPEN-040` and not decided here.**

## Governance's position

**None.** Governance holds no preferred answer and constructs none. It states what is already binding, confirms that the five outcomes do not cover the case, separates the *which* from the *when*, and returns the choice.
