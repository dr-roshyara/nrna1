## 1. What is accepted (Decision A)

The Organisation strategic architecture of the baseline's §13, namely these architectural conclusions and only these:
1. Organisation owns its identity and registry (E-0).
2. Organisation owns its structure and unit belonging; Membership independently owns membership truth (E-5, E-4).
3. Units and organs are distinct and related only through the organ's mandate and scope (E-6).
4. Real-world act, evidence, assessment and platform record are distinct; PublicDigit creates no organisational authority (E-7).
5. The organisation's written rules are the source of authority; the competent body decides; Governance records authority; Organisation maintains the resulting structure (E-8), **without the D-29 exchange**.
6. Tenancy is a platform capability, not part of Organisation (E-11), **without deciding its owner**.
7. Authorization owns no business-domain truth (E-12), **without deciding its scope**.
8. Legacy records are classified by function (E-13), **without classifying any**.

## 2. Status of other decisions (Decision B; D-105 B-4)

- **D-79, D-80, D-97 … D-105 remain separate decision acts**, with their own identity, provenance and history. The baseline references them; this act does not re-ratify, absorb or rewrite them.
- **Working decisions D-1 … D-30** (including D-3, D-22, D-29), the §8.3 C8 working model, **PO directions BR-10, BR-11** and **D-102** (working) **remain provisional**.
- **The ratified Membership contract CR-01 (C1, C2; D-73, D-77)** is cited as it stands; this act changes no ratified document.

## 3. What acceptance does not mean

- **No tactical design is fixed** by this act; tactical DDD design of Organisation may now begin, governed by the accepted boundaries.
- **No implementation is authorised.**
- **Open items stay open:** challenge availability for organisational acts; tenancy owner (HD-8); Authorization scope (XD-4); FeePaid; committee ownership (DR-07a); vocabulary (O-14); statute-dependent UNKNOWNs.

## 4. Change control

A change of meaning to the accepted architecture requires a new Principal decision recorded in the decision record; corrections that change no meaning may be made with a header note.
