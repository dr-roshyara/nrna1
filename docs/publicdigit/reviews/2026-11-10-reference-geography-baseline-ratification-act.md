## 1. What is ratified

1. **Strategic baseline §C** of `docs/publicdigit/architecture/reviews/2026-11-09-reference-geography-strategic-baseline.md` (the sections after §C remain the record of how it was reached; §C governs).
2. **Tactical DDD design v3.11:** `docs/publicdigit/architecture/reviews/2026-11-09-reference-geography-tactical-design-baseline-v3.11.md`.
3. **The baseline coherence assessment:** `docs/publicdigit/architecture/reviews/2026-11-09-reference-geography-baseline-coherence-assessment.md`.

**Supporting evidence (not itself ratified):** `docs/publicdigit/architecture/reviews/2026-11-10-reference-geography-final-validation-report.md`; earlier tactical versions and assessments as design history.

## 2. Binding decisions unchanged

D-3, D-4, D-5, D-22, D-31, D-73 / CR-01, D-75 (with the XD-3 act), D-79, D-80, D-81 to D-92, D-84a. **This act changes the meaning of none of them.**

## 3. What ratification does not mean

- **It accepts the domain design, not proof of its technical implementation.**
- **Open implementation proof obligations:** PO-1 to PO-8 (tactical v3.11 §6) and the four-point Identity contract (§8). **PO-8 (Membership §38.2 moves timestamps) is a known non-conformance;** until it is resolved, cross-context historical queries involving Membership are not fully guaranteed. Membership's ratified architecture is not changed by this act.
- **Deferred, not decided:** L-4c, Q-2, Japanese classification, naming below level 2, level-1 region names, address ownership (the Organisation person-profile capability remains a recommendation).
- **No implementation is authorised by this act.** The implementation architecture requires a separate commission.

## 4. Change control

A change to the ratified baseline requires a new Principal decision, recorded in the decision record; corrections that change no meaning (typography, references) may be made with a note in the document's header.
