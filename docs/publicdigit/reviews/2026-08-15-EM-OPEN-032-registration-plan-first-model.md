# `EM-OPEN-032` Answered — the Plan-First Schedule Model, Registered

## 1 · The answer — and Governance's question was wrong

**Governance asked:** *one act for the whole schedule, or one act per phase?* **The answer is neither, because the question conflated two different granularities.**

> **ONE publication act, publishing the complete schedule established at application. That single act brings into existence ONE OPPORTUNITY PER PHASE.**

| | Granularity |
|---|---|
| **the publication act** | **one**, for the whole election schedule |
| **opportunities created by it** | **one per phase** — administration · candidacy · voting · counting · result publication |
| **what a correction supersedes** | **the affected opportunity**, not all of them |

**Governance records the framing error rather than quietly adopting the better answer.** A binary question can exclude the right answer, and this one did: it treated *how many publication acts* and *how many opportunities* as the same axis. **They are not, and the distinction is now the load-bearing part of the model.**

## 2 · The model as registered

At application: the **complete** schedule — every phase window, the official election time, and any applicable window allowance. Then **one governed publication act**. Thereafter every phase holds a predefined temporal commitment, and the Chief's authority is **to request progression when a scheduled opportunity becomes eligible** — never to set the time on the day.

> **`EM-GOV-009`, adopted at PO direction:** *"The complete election schedule is established as part of the election application. After official publication the schedule is an official election commitment and cannot be silently changed. Any subsequent change must be a governed, attributable, protocol-recorded schedule correction that supersedes the affected opportunity and creates the basis for a new opportunity."*
>
> **Plan first → publish → operate according to the published plan → governed correction only.**

**What it forecloses, in the PO's own terms:** *encounter a problem → change the schedule → click Proceed → bypass the original decision.* **The Chief cannot change the time at 10:00 and press Proceed to escape a failed condition** — that is a correction, which supersedes the opportunity and requires a new authorization (`EM-VOC-005`, `EM-VOT-005`).

## 3 · ⚠️ The consequence that raises the stakes elsewhere

**If the schedule is fixed at application and may thereafter change only by correction, then the correction path is now the ONLY path for every legitimate schedule change** — including entirely benign operational adjustment.

**`EM-GOV-004`'s six boundaries — moving a window forward · extending it · correcting one already elapsed · moving it backward · effect on credentials already issued · permissibility once votes are cast — are still NOT adopted.** They were open before; **under the plan-first model they carry the entire weight of schedule flexibility.** **Governance raises this as a consequence, not a criticism: the model is sound, and it makes those boundaries materially more urgent than they were an hour ago.**

## 4 · Two things the model implies but did not say — `EM-OPEN-037`

**(a) It generalises "opportunity" beyond voting.** `EM-VOC-004` is **voting-scoped by its adopted text**. Speaking of an administration, candidacy, counting or result-publication *opportunity* extends it — and since **an opportunity is a context awaiting an authorized progression request**, the extension **implicitly answers `G-PROG-1` and `EM-OPEN-030` for every phase: it makes all phases request-progressed.**

**That may be exactly the intent. It must not arrive by inheritance.** *(The same guard applied to the T∧A∧E∧R model and to `EM-OPEN-031`.)*

**(b)** Does the single act bring **all five** opportunities into existence **simultaneously**, each then awaiting its own scheduled time and its own request? **The model implies yes; no rule says it.**

## 5 · The window-allowance statements — recorded, NOT adopted

The PO stated, within the discussion: an allowance is **given per window at application time**, is **part of the predefined schedule rather than something the Chief decides on the day**, and **belongs to its opportunity — it cannot silently be changed or inherited by another opportunity.**

**Registered as PO positions stated in discussion. NOT adopted, deliberately.** They were expressed as agreement with a model, not as a ruling — and more importantly **`EM-OPEN-026`(a) is still open: until it is settled whether such a period is a GRANT of time or a BOUND on it, the thing these statements describe has no fixed meaning.** *(They map onto `EM-OPEN-026`(c)/(e) and answer `EM-OPEN-029` in substance; each would take one line to adopt once (a) is ruled.)*

## 6 · Standing instruction re-registered

**`EM-OPEN-022` is settled authority** — a schedule correction creates a new voting opportunity (Reading A, `EM-VOC-005`). **Governance does not reopen it and did not reopen it here.**
