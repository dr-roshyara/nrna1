## How to record a decision

Supply, for each gate:
- (a) **decision**: APPROVED / REJECTED / DEFERRED, with the chosen option;
- (b) the **name and role** of the decider;
- (c) the **date**;
- (d) optional conditions.

The record is then updated with that wording verbatim, and the contract amended as listed above.
