# PublicDigit Membership — Next Gate: Independent SF-1 Audit and G3 Readiness Plan

## 1. Roles and decision authority

Work exclusively on the PublicDigit Membership workstream.

- **Claude:** repository investigator, implementation engineer and evidence producer.
- **ChatGPT:** independent Principal DDD Architect, requirements engineer, semantic reviewer and reviewer of the proposed next-step plan.
- **Human PO/ARB:** decision owner for scope, business rules, risk acceptance, implementation authorization and formal acceptance.

Follow plan-first governance: investigate, produce evidence, propose a plan, wait for ChatGPT's review and explicit Human PO/ARB approval, then implement only the approved work.

## 2. Current baseline to verify

The latest supplied reports identify:

- Branch: `knowelegeos-modelling`
- SF-1 commit: `ce8bf3109db3acfcc7551c6baa2f609078b2f659`
- Parent: `76b1192de`

- G3: PENDING
- PBDIGIT-268: not accepted or closed
- Three unrelated staged renames: must be preserved
- Wider Membership test-suite errors: unresolved
- PostgreSQL persistence evidence: incomplete
- Business use of the new kernel: reported as 0%

These are report-derived facts. Verify the actual repository before relying on them.

## 3. Task A — independently audit the SF-1 commit

Conduct a read-only audit of `ce8bf3109`.

Verify:

1. Exact branch, HEAD, parent and worktree/index state.
2. The full commit diff, including all four reported files.
3. S1, S2 and S3 against the approved Human PO/ARB decision.
4. Preservation of the historical wording-review qualifier.
5. That the evidence distinguishes conditional authorization, prerequisite satisfaction, execution and formal acceptance.
6. That G3 remains PENDING and PBDIGIT-268 remains unaccepted and open.
7. That no excluded code, test, database, configuration, runner, ratified-act, C-AMEND-1, §14.5 or CQ-3 content changed.
8. That the three unrelated staged renames and other-session work remain unchanged.
9. That the untracked source review document remains untracked, as required by the decision.

Investigate the wording distinction between the Principal Reviewer's approval being described as “authorizing” the P1–P6 work and the source phrase “approved for”. Assess whether this is a material authority ambiguity or merely a wording refinement. Do not change the text without separate authorization.

Distinguish repository-verified facts from claims reported by earlier sessions. Do not rerun tests unless separately authorized.

## 4. Task B — prepare the G3 acceptance plan

After the read-only audit, prepare a proposed G3 evidence package and acceptance plan.

Include:

- The C-1 semantic contract and approved scope.
- The exact implementation commit and traceability records.
- Kernel and application test evidence, including captured exit codes where available.
- Differential, property and mutation-test evidence, clearly identifying what has been independently verified.
- The D-COR-2 oracle gap and any other unresolved C-1 evidence limitations.
- The wider Membership suite errors and their unknown ownership.
- PostgreSQL persistence evidence that remains unavailable.
- The independent review's recommendation against the C-1 acceptance criteria.
- Explicit options for the Human PO/ARB: accept with documented residual risks, defer pending evidence, or reject and require corrective work.

Do not recommend unconditional acceptance merely because targeted tests pass.

Keep acceptance of C-1 separate from acceptance of Slices 0, 1a and 1b. Any batched acceptance document must provide a distinct verdict, evidence set, conditions and residual-risk statement for each slice.

## 5. Task C — propose the shortest safe implementation path

Use the existing 17-step slice plan and the repository's actual blockers.

Prioritize:

1. Formal acceptance or disposition of the existing kernel and repository/concurrency slices.
2. Resolution of persistence test infrastructure and the PostgreSQL evidence gap.
3. Triage and ownership of the wider Membership test-suite errors.
4. The outstanding business decisions for Admit/End and the query/projection slices.
5. C-2, C-3 and C-4, each with its own approved scope and entry criteria.
6. Governance, Election, Finance and Audit integration.
7. Migration, per-organisation cutover, API/UI and legacy retirement.

Do not start these implementation tasks during this assignment. Identify dependencies and propose an order based on risk reduction and delivery of observable business capability.

## 6. Domain and architecture invariants

Preserve the established theory:

- Append-only Membership facts with derived current truth.
- Valid-time dates and recorded-time reconstruction.
- Non-overlapping membership periods and explicit re-entry semantics.
- Annul/Void affecting targeted fact liveness only under the approved C-1 contract.
- No implicit Membership-period consequences.
- Membership does not itself grant voting entitlement or service access.
- Election-Only mode remains valid where specified.
- Legacy migration follows the approved single-writer, reverse-projection and cutover strategy.
- No production cutover before the required governance and operational gates.

Treat the brainstorming corpus as research input, not automatically ratified policy. Identify unresolved assumptions and business/legal decisions explicitly.

## 7. Required output

Return:

1. **Audit result:** pass, pass with findings, or fail; include evidence and exact commit references.
2. **SF-1 findings:** especially any authority or provenance ambiguity.
3. **G3 readiness matrix:** criteria, evidence, gaps, severity and owner.
4. **Acceptance recommendation:** clearly separate technical findings from the Human PO/ARB's decision.
5. **Implementation roadmap:** ordered slices, dependencies, business decisions, test requirements and exit criteria.
6. **Risk register:** correctness, persistence, security, migration and cross-context risks.
7. **Plan-first deliverable:** proposed next-step plan with exact scope and verification steps.
8. **Explicit exclusions:** work that must not begin without separate authorization.

Do not invent completion percentages. If providing an estimate, state the denominator, weighting method and uncertainty. Distinguish built, tested, accepted, integrated and used in production.

## 8. Stop condition

Do not edit files, stage, commit, push, run tests or implement the roadmap.

Present the audit and proposed plan to ChatGPT for independent review. Wait for the Human PO/ARB's explicit decision before beginning the next implementation step.

G3 remains PENDING until the formal acceptance decision is made.
