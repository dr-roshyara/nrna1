## 2. Purpose

Determine whether ODA-01 v1.1 is a **reliable conceptual baseline** for the Principal's decisions M-1…M-10 and the ARB's readings M-11…M-17. In particular:
- are its corrections right?
- is its evidence traceable?
- is it internally consistent?
- did the corrections introduce new contradictions?

This is **not** a request for a new Organisation architecture.

---

## 3. Questions

1. **Correction register (v1.1 §0.2).** For each of K-1…K-14:
   - is the source verification correct?
   - is the correction adequate?
   - is the classification right?
   - did the correction miss any affected section?
2. **IR-ODA-01 itself.**
   - Are any of its findings wrong or overstated?
   - Did it miss material issues in v1.0 that v1.1 therefore carried forward?
   - Note: v1.1 §0.3 disagrees with K-11 on whether PO-ORG-MEM-01 counts as a decision. Assess that disagreement.
3. **Definition (v1.1 §3.1).** Is the candidate definition coherent, and complete against the distinctions in §3.2? Does it exclude any legitimate organisation type? Does it accidentally design inter-organisation relationships (BR-13a)?
4. **Constitution (v1.1 §5).**
   - Is the O-1 / O-2 comparison now balanced?
   - Is the four-way separation (source record, interpretation ruling, authority evidence, operational rule) sound and sufficient?
   - Is the `(document, article, version)` citation proposal compatible with the ratified evidence contract (RAT `:72`), or would it need a Change Request?
5. **D-4 tension (v1.1 §5.2).** Is it stated accurately, without changing D-4?
6. **Strategic DDD (v1.1 §11).** Is the provisional classification free of unsupported claims? Is the treatment of the Organisation-context scope fair?
7. **Geography (v1.1 §8).** Are the five concepts distinct and well evidenced? Are the scope options neutral?
8. **Registers (v1.1 §15).**
   - Is each M- and E-item assigned to the right authority (PO vs ARB vs external evidence)?
   - Are any decisions missing?
   - Is the dependency order sound (e.g. M-1 → M-2 → M-3 → M-4)?
9. **Consistency.** Do v1.1's sections contradict each other? Do they contradict ratified records or recorded PO decisions?
10. **Over-modelling.** Does v1.1 still propose model elements that the evidence does not need?
