## C. Findings

Severity: **B** blocking · **M** material · **m** minor.

### F-1 [M] The PO-ORG-MEM-01 decision record exists; v1.1 treats the wording as unrecorded
- **Location:** v1.1 §0.2 K-11, §0.3, §1 (last row), §2.1 (row PO-ORG-MEM-01), §5.3, §15.1 M-1.
- **Evidence:** R/`2026-11-03-organisation-po-org-mem-01-membership-ownership-decision-record.md:3, 11-30`. The status is "APPROVED by the Principal / PO, 3 November 2026 … recorded verbatim; ARB validation pending".
  - Decision A is a third wording, different from both PREP `:47-61` wordings. It says the Constitution "establishes the competent organisational organs, **offices and positions**", that "a competent organisational organ **or authorised office holder** makes Membership decisions", and "the Organisation's authority remains independent of the software".
- **Finding (a):** "wording unrecorded" is now false. M-1 changes from "record the decision with its exact wording" to "PO confirms that this record is verbatim and registers it". IR K-11 was right when written. v1.1 §0.3's disagreement with K-11 is now moot, but its substance stands: no *argument* may rest on a wording.
- **Finding (b), provenance caveat [I]:** the record was written by a Claude session (Session B) from a statement "in the strategic discovery session". "Verbatim" is the recorder's claim; there is no PO-signed source. It is the best available record, not independent proof. Its own §3 (consistent with ACT §11, "does not reopen") is the recorder's inference.
- **Finding (c), overreach in v1.1 §5.3 [I]:** v1.1 says "the business owner of the constitution is the Organisation (PO-ORG-MEM-01 A)". Decision A says only "The Organisation's Constitution (statutes)", a possessive. It concerns ownership of *Membership* and says the Constitution is the source of competence. It does not decide who owns the constitution *record*. The sentence should read "the Constitution is the Organisation's own (A, possessive); who holds the record is not decided".
- **Finding (d), effect on other points:**
  - Definition (§3.1): A supports "authority independent of the software", consistent with "does not depend on PublicDigit". But A says organ **or authorised office holder** decide. §3.1's "decides … through its own organs" is narrower than the PO's own ruling.
  - E-2 (offices outside organs): A names offices and positions as established by the Constitution and as competent to decide. That is *weak* PO-level support for offices existing alongside organs. It does not settle cardinality; evidence is still needed.
  - C-2: A again places "positions" in the Constitution by PO ruling. ACT §11 still does not mention positions. C-2 stands.
  - D-4: record §3 says D-4 is unchanged and that platform access is not organisational authority. This confirms v1.1 §5.2's tension; it does not resolve it.
- **Correction:** update K-11 / §0.3 / §1 / §2.1 / M-1; soften §5.3's first bullet; widen §3.1 to "organs or office holders".

### F-2 [M] The code-precedent evidence for O-1 is mislocated and incomplete
- **Location:** v1.1 §5.3 "For" (O-1), K-10, §2.3 "Absent".
- **Evidence:**
  - `ConstitutionalBasis` is in Governance (`app/Contexts/Governance/Domain/ValueObjects/ConstitutionalBasis.php`; article + optional text).
  - But `ConstitutionalArticleReference` is in **Membership** (`app/Contexts/Membership/Domain/Committee/Constitutional/Doctrine/`).
  - The same Membership folder holds `DoctrineArtifact` with `version`, `effectiveFrom` / `effectiveUntil`, `doctrineHash` and `DoctrineProvenance` (approvedBy, supersedes / supersededBy), plus a `DoctrineRegistryInterface` and an in-memory registry resolving by version. Scopes are national / regional / emergency / caretaker / election (`ConstitutionalScope.php`).
- **Finding:** v1.1 says article citations and delegation sit in Governance code (precedent for O-1). Half of that is Membership code. The Membership code is a **versioned, effective-dated, hash-pinned, supersession-tracked rule store**, which v1.1 §2.3 ("Absent: … statute document or version store") omits. It is plausibly platform "committee doctrine" rather than organisation statutes, in-memory only, legacy. It does not prove a statute store exists. But it is the closest existing shape of "(source, version, effective period, provenance)", sits in Membership, and cuts against the "Governance-only precedent" and against "no version store".
- **Correction:** state both locations in K-10 and §5.3. Say the precedent is mixed, not O-1-favouring. Note the Doctrine store's apparent nature ([I], unverified: platform doctrine vs organisation statutes) as an [OQ].

### F-4 [M] The `(document, article, version)` citation vs RAT `:72`: v1.1's answer is incomplete
- **Location:** v1.1 §5.1 "Citation", K-14, M-12.
- **Evidence:** RAT `:72` names `ruleRef`, `policyVersion` (authority evidence) and "rule (ref, version)" (provenance). In code, `AuthorityEvidence.php:19-25` holds `ruleRef` and `policyVersion` as **non-empty strings** (all four string fields validated non-empty). `Provenance::rule(string $ruleRef, string $ruleVersion)` is the same shape.
- **Finding:**
  - Carrying three parts (document, article, version) inside the existing `ruleRef` string plus `policyVersion` string needs **no schema or contract-field change**. So a Change Request is not strictly needed to *use* it.
  - But declaring that these two opaque strings denote a constitutional document / article and a version fixes a **referent** the ratification deliberately left open (K-14). That is an interpretation of a frozen contract (RAT `:3` "changes only via Architecture Decision / Change Request"). Making the citation structured (separate fields), or requiring it in the contract, **would** be a Change Request, as PREP OQ-12 says for contract changes.
  - v1.1 says only "any change … needs a CR". It does not answer the commission's question or say which of the two cases applies. M-12 (ARB adopts the citation) should say "ARB records the convention; structured fields require a CR".
- **New related fact for M-4 [I]:** both strings are mandatory non-empty. v1.1 §5.2 says D-4 acts could be recorded as "interim, non-constitutional provenance" but does not say what `ruleRef` / `policyVersion` would then hold before any statute exists. That is a concrete sub-question for M-4.
- **Severity:** M.

### F-5 [M] [F] labels that are inference
- (a) §5.2 "[F] every current organisation does [use PublicDigit without statutes]: no statute is on file (PRI A86; XD-15 proposal `:138`)". PRI A86 (`:414`) says "OPEN; statutes **not yet requested**". That is absence of a request, not a fact that no organisation has statutes, and not a fact about what organisations have. Also the "XD-15 proposal `:138`" anchor is not a resolvable path in the document. Relabel [I]; cite A86 literally.
- (b) K-4 treats the "structure epoch" as one of three stable meanings. The code path is `CommitteeStructureEpochContext::fromOrganisationStatus`, marked **`@deprecated Phase B temporary shim`** (`:28-29`, one caller). It maps `status == 'active'` to an epoch. Call it a temporary shim, not a meaning. Also: since nothing writes `active` (my grep: the only write is `GovernanceSetupController.php:102`; the policy `activateGovernance` has no caller in routes), the committee-creation gate `allowCommitteeCreation` can never be true through application code. v1.1 hints at "data change outside the code" but not at this consequence.
- (c) K-2 "forWorldwideOrg defined, **unused**": true for `app/`, but six test files call it (e.g. `tests/Unit/Domain/GeographicStructureWorldwideTest.php:15`). Say "unused in production code; covered by tests".
- (d) §2.1 quotes D-4 as "interim and replaceable by the Governance-owned implementation". PREP `:299` reads "marked interim and replaceable by Governance". The quotation marks should go or the text should match.
- (e) C-12 / K-9 call the four classifications "inconsistent". The architecture assessment `:104` says they are "at three scopes" (platform system vs subdomain vs candidate). "Inconsistent" is [I]; "differently scoped, unreconciled" is the fact.

### F-6 [m] Overstated "five distinct concepts" in §8
- §8.1 "Concepts: five" includes the **organisational unit**, which is not geographic and is O-16-pending. §12 then speaks of "five link kinds (§8.1)". The link kinds are four (operating area, unit responsibility area, jurisdiction, electoral area); GeoUnit is the target, not a link. IR §I counted three plus election regions. Fix the count.
- Operating area is listed as a concept while v1.1 itself says it "waits for evidence" / may be derived. Keep it out of the diagram (§4) until evidence, per R-11.

### F-7 [m] Four-way separation: sound, but not sufficient
- **Sound:** source record / interpretation / authority evidence / operational rule are genuinely different things and hold under either placement. I confirm this.
- **Not sufficient:** an organ's **resolution or decision record** (minutes, delegation instrument, appointment resolution) is a fifth thing. Neither authority evidence (a check result) nor interpretation (a ruling on meaning) covers it. PREP OQ-12 already asks about "a reference to the deciding organ's resolution". Record it as an [OQ] rather than declaring the set closed. Also "Interpretation ruling" has no evidence anywhere that rulings exist as artefacts (only questionnaire D5); label it [P], not a settled concept.

### F-8 [m] Definition (§3.1)
- Coherent, and much better than v1.0. "Collective body … own organs" **excludes** a body decided by an office holder alone (see F-1). "Under its own constitution (statutes)" conflicts with the open question about organisations without written statutes: the candidate text should already read "rules, written or not", or the OQ should state that the definition excludes them as drafted.
- Does not design inter-organisation relations: confirmed. "Within … external rules that bind it" admits federation members without designing federation; BR-13a respected.
- The platform-record exclusion rests on BR-12 (`BR:35`), which I opened: the bootstrap organisation is "infrastructure, never a Working Organisation". That makes M-3 largely a vocabulary question, not a fresh PO decision.

### F-9 [m] Internal consistency

- §1 / §2.1 / §15 M-1 stale after F-1.
