## A. Verdict

**Ready with corrections.**

Reasons:

1. **Decision fidelity is exact where Decision A is quoted, but not where it is applied.** v1.2 §2.2 reproduces Decision A word for word (I diffed POMEM `:20-30` against v1.2 `:153-163`: identical) and its "taken / not taken" table (`:167-174`) correctly limits the decision to **Membership** decisions. But the candidate definition (§3.1 `:196`), the distinctions (§3.2 `:222`), the model diagram (§4 `:254`), the structure section (§6 `:333`) and the glossary (§13 `:418`) apply Decision A's "competent organ or authorised office holder" rule to the Organisation's decisions **in general**, and label that as **[D] Decision A**. J-rec PO-ORG-MEM-01-J1 (`:28`, `:32`) now decides that Decision A applies to Membership decisions only and must not be widened into a general organisational decision-making principle. This is a Material finding (C-01). It does not invite an irreversible decision, because §3.1 is a [P] candidate, but it must be corrected before the Principal relies on the definition.
2. **The O-1 / O-2 / O-3 comparison is much better than v1.1 but still not fully balanced.** O-3 is restored and shared costs are partly attributed. But one argument against O-1 presents a pending ARB *request* (DR07 ARB-2) as an ARB *reading* (C-02), and two costs are still attributed unevenly (C-03).
3. **The register is mostly assigned to the right authority**, but ODA-M-18 (conflicts with D-1…D-5, E-1…E-5, GOV-END B1) is assigned to the ARB alone, whereas PREP routes conflicts with PO decisions to the PO and with D-4 to the Human ARB (C-04), and ODA-M-4a does not name the Human ARB capacity that J-4 now fixes (C-05).
4. **J-rec post-dates v1.2.** Several v1.2 statements are now stale: C-15 and V-1 describe a PREP mis-attribution that has since been corrected; ODA-M-1's closure should now rest on J-3 for Decision B (C-06). This is not an authoring error, but the Principal should not accept a baseline that describes a superseded state.
5. Nothing found is **Blocking**: no finding shows v1.2 deciding, authorising or changing anything it says it does not, and D-4 is not touched.
6. **The finish line is not reached**: conditions 2, 4 and 5 are not met and 1 and 3 are partly met (§F). This review cannot change that. In particular, it does not satisfy the commission's independence criterion.
