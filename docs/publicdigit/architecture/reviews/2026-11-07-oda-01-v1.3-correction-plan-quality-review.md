## 1. Executive assessment

[I] The plan's analysis is **largely correct**:
- the J-1 traceability is accurate;
- all 26 IR12 findings are accounted for;
- no correction changes an approved decision, the frozen evidence contract or the ratified Membership architecture.

[I] But **it is not yet safe to submit unchanged**, for six reasons:
1. **Two of its own identifier series collide.** "E-1…E-5" (Change Request items) collides with the ODA-E-n evidence register and with the PO's dues decisions E-1…E-5. "F-1…F-5" (do-not-change items) collides with the S-review's F-n findings. This is the same error class v1.2 corrected (V-2).
2. **It is stale against the working tree.** After a later Principal approval ("Complete D-4 Attribution Corrections and Documentation Repair", J-rec §4, §6), Session B corrected the further D-4 attributions in PREP. The plan still says they are uncorrected (§1, Group F-5, §9). PREP line numbers in the plan are HEAD numbers; the working copy is shifted after `:307`.
3. **Its authority column conflates three things.** It shows "Principal (plan approval)" for every Group A item. It does not separate (a) approval of ODA-01 wording, (b) ownership of the underlying decision and (c) validation by the ARB, Human ARB or Governance.
4. **It misses a recorded role fact.** [F] `.claude/MEMORY.md:11` records "Principal Reviewer = ChatGPT (advisory)" for Membership work. RAT `:3` says the Membership architecture was ratified by "the Principal Reviewer". The plan's question B-4 ("same role as the Principal / PO?") is therefore framed on a weak premise, and the Change Request authority (ODA-M-19) is more open than the plan suggests.
5. **"Disputed: none" is stated without criteria.** It should be "no finding is contradicted at source"; one sub-claim is an unsupported hypothesis (IR12-F21(b)).
6. **Two new register items duplicate existing questions.** ODA-M-21 duplicates the POMEM §3 / PREP §3.4 row 1 question, and ODA-M-22 duplicates PREP OQ-13. They should be registered as **aliases** of the existing questions, not as new ones (ES-005.4: never a copy).

**Readiness:** ready for Principal approval **after** the recommended changes in §10. None of them is substantive for the architecture.
