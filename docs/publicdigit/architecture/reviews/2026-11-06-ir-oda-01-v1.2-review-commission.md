## 2. The Principal's finish line (context for the reviewer)

The Principal considers the Organisation baseline architecture **established** only when:
1. the business domain and ubiquitous language are sufficiently defined;
2. the strategic DDD analysis and bounded-context boundaries have been reviewed;
3. the relevant PO and ARB decisions are recorded;
4. the model has been checked against real organisational evidence;
5. the Principal has formally accepted the baseline, with unresolved matters identified and governed.

This review serves condition 2 and prepares 3 and 5. It cannot satisfy condition 4: no statutes are on file.

## 3. Questions

1. **Decision fidelity.** Does v1.2 represent PO-ORG-MEM-01 Decision A exactly, in meaning and in scope?
   - It must neither narrow decision authority to organs, nor widen the decision into context ownership of the Constitution or a reading of ACT §11.
   - Check v1.2 §2.2, §3.1, §5.3 and §6.
2. **Corrections.** Is each row of the v1.2 §0.2 correction register right, including where v1.2 disagrees with the Sonnet review (S-F5(c), S-F5(d), K-8)?
3. **New findings.** Are V-1 (D-4 decided by the Human ARB, not the PO; C-15), V-2 and V-3 correct? Are their consequences stated without changing D-4?
4. **O-1 / O-2 / O-3.** Is the comparison now balanced and complete, with shared costs counted fairly and facts separated from judgement?
5. **Citation cases (i) / (ii) / (iii)** (v1.2 §5.1).
   - Is the line between using the citation, declaring what the fields mean, and structuring them correct against RAT `:3` and `:72`?
   - Is anything in v1.2 a silent change to the frozen evidence semantics?
6. **Definition.** Is the candidate coherent? Is its stated exclusion of organisations without written statutes honest and correctly routed (ODA-M-20)?
7. **Register.**
   - Do ODA-M-1…M-20 and ODA-E-1…E-6 have the right authority?
   - Is the mapping of the decision record's four pending ARB items (§15.3) complete?
   - Is anything missing or wrongly closed (ODA-M-1)?
8. **Consistency and over-modelling.** Does v1.2 contradict itself, earlier ratified records or recorded decisions? Does it propose model elements the evidence does not require?
9. **Finish line.** Against the five conditions in §2: which are met, which are partly met, and what exactly is still missing for each?
