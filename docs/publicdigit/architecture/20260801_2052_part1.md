# Part 1 — Programme Overview & Architectural Context

## 1.1 Executive Summary

This programme is building **PublicDigit**, a constitutional governance platform whose primary implementation domain is a secure, auditable election management system. The programme extends beyond application development: it also evolves a reusable engineering platform (**KnowledgeOS**) and a structured operational learning system (**PKS – Product Knowledge System**).

The long-term objective is not merely to deliver software, but to establish a repeatable engineering methodology in which operational experience continuously improves both the engineering process and future products.

The programme therefore consists of three mutually supporting but distinct domains:

| Domain | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **PublicDigit** | The production software being engineered. |
| **KnowledgeOS** | The reusable engineering platform, standards, methodology, governance, and engineering practices. |
| **PKS (Product Knowledge System)** | The structured repository of operational evidence, implementation observations, architectural lessons, and engineering knowledge generated while building PublicDigit. |

PublicDigit is the **primary product**.

KnowledgeOS is **not** an independent first project. It evolves incrementally by extracting reusable capabilities that have been validated through real engineering work.

PKS captures the operational evidence that drives this evolution.

---

## 1.2 Programme Vision

The programme follows a deliberate evidence-driven evolution model.

Rather than designing a general-purpose engineering platform in advance, the programme uses PublicDigit as the operational laboratory from which reusable engineering capabilities are extracted.

The architectural feedback loop is:

```
                PublicDigit
                     │
         Real Engineering Work
                     │
          Operational Evidence
                     │
         Product Knowledge System
                     │
        Reusable Engineering Patterns
                     │
              KnowledgeOS
                     │
      Improves Future Engineering
                     │
              PublicDigit
```

KnowledgeOS therefore grows only when operational evidence demonstrates that a capability is genuinely reusable.

This prevents premature abstraction and preserves architectural simplicity.

## 1.3 Programme Components

### PublicDigit

PublicDigit is the primary software product.

Its current implementation focuses on a constitutional election platform designed according to Domain-Driven Design (DDD), explicit governance, and strong architectural boundaries.

The system emphasizes:

- deterministic behaviour,
- auditability,
- constitutional governance,
- explicit authority,
- evidence-driven decision making,
- bounded contexts,
- evolutionary architecture.

PublicDigit is considered the authoritative source of operational experience.

---

### KnowledgeOS

KnowledgeOS is the reusable engineering platform.

It contains engineering methodology rather than business functionality.

Examples include:

- engineering standards,
- architectural governance,
- documentation standards,
- review methodology,
- verification methodology,
- reusable engineering tooling,
- repository governance,
- AI-assisted engineering workflows.

KnowledgeOS is intentionally evolved from proven engineering experience rather than speculative design.

Every capability promoted into KnowledgeOS must be justified by operational evidence.

---

### PKS (Product Knowledge System)

PKS captures structured engineering knowledge produced during development.

Examples include:

- implementation observations,
- architectural discoveries,
- verification reports,
- governance findings,
- documentation integrity findings,
- engineering lessons,
- reusable implementation patterns,
- operational evidence.

PKS exists because operational knowledge is considered a first-class architectural asset rather than disposable project documentation.

## 1.4 Core Engineering Philosophy

The programme is guided by several architectural principles that emerged repeatedly during implementation.

### Evidence before generalization

Engineering methodology is extracted only after repeated operational evidence demonstrates reuse.

The programme explicitly rejects speculative architecture.

---

### Evolution over prediction

Architectural abstractions are introduced only after engineering experience reveals recurring patterns.

The programme deliberately avoids designing generalized frameworks before operational need exists.

---

### PublicDigit as the laboratory

KnowledgeOS is not developed independently.

Instead:

- PublicDigit produces operational experience.
- PKS records operational evidence.
- KnowledgeOS extracts reusable capabilities.

This ensures that engineering methodology remains grounded in reality.

---

### Deterministic engineering

Where engineering work is deterministic, it should be automated.

Examples include:

- documentation placement,
- documentation link repair,
- repository validation,
- documentation classification.

Human governance should focus on semantic decisions rather than mechanical work.

---

### Operational evidence is a first-class architectural asset

Implementation observations are preserved rather than discarded.

Operational evidence is treated as architectural input.

Engineering experience continuously improves both the product and the engineering platform.

---

## 1.5 Governance Philosophy

Architecture is governed through explicit authority rather than implicit convention.

Several principles guide every architectural decision.

### Evidence precedes authority.

Architectural decisions are exercised only after sufficient evidence exists.

---

### Authority precedes recording.

Governance records document decisions.

They do not create authority.

---

### Recording precedes execution.

Engineering begins only after authorization has been formally recorded.

---

### Architecture does not approve itself.

Architects propose.

The Architecture Review Board (ARB) exercises authority.

Engineering implements.

---

### Semantic ambiguity returns to governance.

Deterministic engineering work is automated.

When multiple architecturally valid interpretations exist, engineering stops and governance decides.

## 1.6 Role of the AI

Future AI assistants participating in this programme shall act primarily as:

- Principal Software Architect,
- Domain-Driven Design specialist,
- Architecture Review Board advisor,
- Engineering governance reviewer,
- Senior software engineer,
- KnowledgeOS evolution advisor.

The AI is expected to:

- preserve architectural integrity,
- challenge unsupported assumptions,
- distinguish evidence from inference,
- protect bounded-context boundaries,
- prevent premature generalization,
- maintain consistency with previously accepted architectural decisions.

The AI shall not redesign accepted architecture unless explicitly authorized.
