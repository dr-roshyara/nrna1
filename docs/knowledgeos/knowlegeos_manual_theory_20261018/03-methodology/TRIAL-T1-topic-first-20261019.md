## 1. The trial, step by step

1. **Choose the topic** from the theory map: one open **factual or definitional** fork or open question (e.g. F8 "knowledge spaces: nested or adjacent", F11 "what 'execute' means"). Normative forks stay with the Principal. Write one line in the theory map: the topic and why it was chosen.
2. **Find the sources** by searching the allowed list with the concept register's terms **and their synonyms**. State the terms and the number of hits.
3. **Pick five sources** for the topic: the earliest relevant source, the latest, and three between, chosen by relevance to the question. Write one line on why these five.
4. **Read each in full, in date order.** Every source is opened and read whole, not only the matching passages. Each gets a read-log row with depth `topic-full` and the topic name. **Topic rows do not move the chronological reading position** (it stays after S0035).
5. **Write or revise the section** under Rulebook v1.3 §4: scope stated before each derived result; a later synthesis never confirms an earlier source.
6. **Anything else a source contains** that belongs to another topic, or contradicts the book, is added to the theory map as a new topic or open question. It is never forced into the trial topic.
7. **Concepts:** no concept from the trial becomes VALIDATED before the reviewer's verdict on that concept (step 8). Until then it is PROVISIONAL.

## 2. Review

8. **The independent reviewer** reads the five sources blind, then the new or revised section and the whole theory map, and answers the six questions of Rulebook v1.3 §6, including **question 6, coverage**: what in these five sources does the book leave out?
   - **Access, granted by this approval:** the five trial sources (full text), the new section, the theory map. The file list goes into the access record **before** the reviewer starts. Nothing else.
   - The reviewer gives **a verdict per concept**: VALIDATED, INVALID or PROVISIONAL, with the reason.

## 3. The comparison (one short note)

Compared with the last chronological group, **C6 (S0031–S0035)**, and its review:

| Measure | How |
|---|---|
| Theory gained | new or revised concepts, derived results, forks settled or opened |
| Validity | review findings by type: overstatement, logic, citation, omission |
| Coverage | the reviewer's question-6 findings |
| New topics found | count from step 6 |
| Effort | start and stop time per source (logged from now on), plus time for the section and for corrections |

The note ends with a recommendation: keep chronology first, switch to topic first, or test again. **The Principal decides.**

## 4. What the trial does not change

- Every allowed source is still opened and logged eventually. Sources read in the trial are later met again by the chronological reading; there they only need a check that the book covers them.
- No source is skipped. No efficiency gain is assumed: it is measured.
- Allowed list, sealed and hold-out exclusions, and all other rules of Rulebook v1.3 apply unchanged.
