## 2. Summary

- The map is well formed. Rows are typed domain relations (authorizes, writes, reads, informs, conforms), with direction defined by dependency. 16 of 21 rows are marked conditional, and they are kept conditional. The draft's "star" and "narrowed law" were withdrawn on evidence. **(B) is applied faithfully** in §10.3.6, and the text itself reports the discrepancy with the decision's consequence line.
- Under (1), the **one record and D's kind schema form a shared kernel owned by standing**. §10.3.6 L16–18 says so in substance ("true by construction"; "every other area's kinds are granted by standing's schema"), but **the map draws no row and no pattern for it** (SD4-01, high).
- **R6**: an evidence-kind rule violation takes the merged record out of D, so **every standing answer becomes a refusal**. A supporting area's rule can disable the core answer. The decision that G1 starts from kinds in the one record makes this relationship real, not hypothetical (SD4-02, high).
- **R12**: standing's answers depend on unrecorded inputs (covers, θ, identity link) whose source is unknown. This is a core dependency on an **unowned upstream** (SD4-03, high, open).
- **Admission depends on who adds kinds** to standing's schema (p2, p5), and that act is not yet recorded. So (B) currently discriminates by schema authorship, not by the area's model (SD4-05).

## 4. What holds

- **Direction is defined**, not assumed. "Y is upstream of X iff X depends on Y" on published language or state, and every row carries one of five domain-relation types (L21–24). This answers S0162's "relationships, not a pipeline" for the typed half (L137).
- **Conditional rows are kept conditional.** 16 of 21 are marked ◇ with the open question, the reason and what would resolve it (§10.4.4). Only R1, R2, R3, R8 and R19 are unconditional, and each rests on settled lines.
- **Pattern names are labels for stated relations, not reasons** (L26). This is the right discipline: the literature benchmarks, it does not justify.
- **R8 (S → WM)** is a sound customer/supplier relation with S's contract upstream (F22 = (C + O)), consistent with §9.16 R4a as verified.
- **R5/R10 split authority by question** ("who may record" against "what an entry says"; "stands" against "applies"). These are well-formed DDD boundaries within a relation.
- **Withdrawals on evidence** ("star", "narrowed law", draft R8/R9) leave the map honest about the layering risk (§10.4.5 L150).
- **§10.3.6 states its own costs**: standing admitted by construction, no area's state independent of standing's record-level operations (L16–18). This is exactly what a DDD review needs to see.
