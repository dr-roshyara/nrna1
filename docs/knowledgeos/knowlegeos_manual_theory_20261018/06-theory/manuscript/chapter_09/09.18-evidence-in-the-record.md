## 9.18.1 In plain language

Promise (b) says KnowledgeOS shows how well-founded a piece of knowledge is: **refuted**, **not attested**, or **supported by recorded evidence**, and, since T9-17 (b), **contested** when live evidence both supports and refutes. Today the record holds only acts of authority, so none of the four can be read from it (gap G1, §10.2.3).

This turn models evidence as **proposed entry kinds of the one designated record** (proposed at this turn; adopted 2026-10-31, T9-19), accepted under the evidence area's own rule, and finds:

1. **Three kinds** (proposed at this turn; adopted 2026-10-31, T9-19):
   - an **evidence entry** that supports or refutes an item's content and names its producer;
   - a **withdrawal of reliance** for a purpose;
   - a **restoration** of that reliance.

   **Correcting a mis-recorded entry is not a new kind:** it is standing's annulment (§9.18.4).
2. **Each class can be read from the record at a cut, under stated conditions** (§9.18.2, table):
   - the rule reads no order among evidence entries;
   - each entry is listed with its state and its cause, so a contradiction stays visible;
   - an undetermined input gives an undetermined class, named.
3. **"Not attested" needs no completeness rule** if it is read and shown as *not attested in the record at this cut*.
4. **Fault containment holds for the evidence area's own clauses, for values and for marks**, once the two couplings are removed (checks 33, 35, 36). Clauses that decide **what an entry is, or where it belongs**, are not containable. That residue is a **stated cost** of decisions (1) and (b).
5. **A separate evidence record is not shown to be necessary.**
6. **Four choices are the Principal's**, prepared one at a time (§9.18.10):
   - whose evidence counts (T9-12): **Decided (c)** (the Principal, decision record addendum 2026-10-29T11:27:44Z, `c5ef9de27`);
   - who may withdraw or restore reliance (T9-16; **decided (b)**, §9.18.10);
   - how support and refutation combine (T9-17; **decided (b)**, g1, §9.18.5);
   - adopting the kinds: **decided, all three adopted** (the Principal, 2026-10-31: "Adopt all three"; decision record addendum to be recorded by the Governance Engineer).

## 9.18.4 The kinds (proposed at this turn; adopted 2026-10-31, T9-19)

*Modelled in v22 (`W_FIELDS`, `in_D_W`, `append_W`, `wclass`). **Not admitted to D**: `in_D` still refuses them (check 34).* *Adopted since (the Principal, 2026-10-31: "Adopt all three"; decision record addendum to be recorded by the Governance Engineer): after the adoption "D" names two domains: **D_S**, standing's (Q1 answers on a record in D_S), and **D_W**, W's (the classes need D_W); the record's own clauses belong to both, and a breach of W's clauses takes a record out of D_W only (§9.18.6; Q-A; consolidation S0253–S0277, D-08) (`in_D_S`, `in_D_W`, both modelled since v22); the model's `in_D` stays the pre-adoption D, which check 34 shows.*

| Kind | Fields (besides recorder, status, store, attestation) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **evid**, evidence entry | actor (read as the **producer**), cited bases (the producer's grant), **about** (the item), **polarity** (supports / refutes), scope, act time | a recorded claim that an item's **content** is supported or refuted, by a named producer |
| **ewdr**, withdrawal of reliance | actor, cited bases, **target** (an **earlier** evidence entry), **purpose**, scope, act time | the evidence stays recorded; it is not relied on **for this purpose** (CON-0214 (b)) |
| **erst**, restoration | actor, cited bases **including the withdrawal**, target (the withdrawal), scope, act time | reliance restored; order **by citation** |

**Correction and withdrawal, restated (A-04, B F9).**
- **Annulment** of an evidence entry voids **the recorded assertion**: it was mis-recorded, or recorded without standing.
  - Under the classification of §9.18.7, annulling is **standing's** ("what counts").
  - Annulment is **reversible**: an annulment can itself be annulled (§9.13).
  - The draft's "correction is permanent" is withdrawn.
- **Content found wrong** is not a correction of the record. It is **a refuting evidence entry**, which the class shows beside the original (S0888: retraction does not delete).
- **Withdrawal** keeps the entry and its truth as a record, and stops reliance **for one purpose**.
- **What separates them** is the **scope** (one purpose, or every purpose and every reader) and the **cause shown** (v22 gives each entry's cause: "annulled by …", "withdrawn for …"). It is **not** reversibility.
- **erst is not shown necessary:** annulling a withdrawal gives the same class (A P17). It is kept as a proposed kind because it lets the evidence area restore reliance **without an act of standing's authority**. If the Principal prefers fewer kinds, annulment of the withdrawal suffices. *(T9-16 was decided (b) without a separate answer on this point; erst stays a proposed kind, as recommended with (b), and its adoption is part of the adoption question. Since 2026-10-31 all three kinds, erst included, are adopted, T9-19.)*

## 9.18.5 The five premises, G8, and the class rule

*For each: the result; necessary?; kernel or application?; what would disprove it.*

**(i) What an entry supports or refutes.**
- **Result:** every evidence entry has a **polarity** and an **object** (`about`: an item, its content). It has no field for standing, and no standing entry may take it as a basis (a clause of D_S). *(Defined here, consolidation S0201–S0225 B-F3: **D_S** is standing's domain, D's clauses for standing's kinds plus the record-level clauses (`in_D_S`); **D_W** is the evidence area W's acceptance clauses (`in_D_W`). "Area" and "the element outside every area" are Ch. 10's terms (§10.3.6): a forward reference.)*
- **Necessary?** Yes, for §10.2.3's definitions: without polarity, refuted and supported cannot be told apart.
- **Kernel or application?** The fields are part of **the evidence area's (W's) kind** (proposed at this turn; adopted 2026-10-31, T9-19). The kernel's append step **enforces** them for W.
- **Disproved by:** a class that needs a third polarity, or an object other than an item.
- *Open:* the version and the bearer (T9-14); S0294's second axis.

**(ii) Correction against withdrawal.** As restated in §9.18.4.
- **Necessary?** Yes, for one reason: the **purpose scope**. Withdrawal for p leaves q refuted (check 34).
- **Kernel or application?** Annulment is standing's kind; withdrawal and restoration are W's kinds (proposed at this turn; adopted 2026-10-31, T9-19).
- **Disproved by:** a case where purpose-scoped reliance is never needed. Then annulment alone suffices.

**(iii) Ordering at a cut, and U** *(restated; A-03, B F7)*.
- **Result:** g0, and g1 after it, read **no order among W's entries**. Withdrawal and restoration are ordered by citation, so a withdrawal and a restoration at the same act time still give a determinate class (check 34).
- **Act-time order reaches the class through standing's answers**, chiefly the producer's authorization. A tie there gives U, and the class is **U with the entry and the cause named** (check 34: an ending at the producer's act time gives U; under the tie rule "position" it gives a value).
- U is never shown as "not attested" (CON-0221).
- **The rule is Kleene-monotone:**
  - *(g0, before T9-17:)* a surely live refutation gave "refuted" whatever else was U; a surely live support gave "supported" only if no refutation might be live.
  - *(g1, decided; updated at C46, W-04: g1 since T9-17 (b):)* a class is shown only if every resolution of its U inputs gives the same class, so a surely live refutation with a possibly live support gives U ("refuted or contested"), not refuted (check 38).

  *(Argued, and tested on fixtures; not checked exhaustively.)*
- **Necessary?** Yes: without citation order, a withdrawal and a restoration at one act time would need a tie rule (precedent: §9.13.2 L48).

- **Disproved by:** a display need for recency between evidence entries ("latest wins"), which is a rival in T9-17.

**(iv) Who may record evidence** *(relabelled; A-08, B F13)*.

  - it keeps every recorded claim visible, with its cause;

## 9.18.6 Fault containment (the Lead DDD Architect's SD4-02, routed as input; B4)

*"Fault containment", to keep it apart from scope containment (§2.11, §3.8; B F11).*

**The question:** can a breach of W's acceptance rule be contained to W's entries, instead of taking the record out of D and refusing every standing answer? Within one kernel such an entry is refused at append (check 35); the case arises on **merged records or bypass**.

**Premises, made explicit (A-01 proof table):**
- **(c1)** Φ reads an evidence-area entry only for **its own** atom (its producer's authorization, and annulments of it).
- **(c2)** No standing entry takes a W entry as a basis (a **new clause** of D_S; adopted with the evidence kinds, T9-19; kept, with no guard on adoption (the Principal, 2026-10-31: "No guard, keep c2"; T9-20; consolidation S0253–S0277, C-06)).
- **(c3)** **No W entry takes a single-use grant as a basis** (W's rule, G8; adopted 2026-10-31, Q-A). *Reading, Int:* an evidence entry "is not an act in A1's sense" (O2), so it is not a **use** under the single-use rule (§9.13 v13, "every act under a single-use grant is a use"). The Principal's rule is unchanged. **If the Principal reads it to include evidence entries, (c3) fails, and the coupling returns for marks and guards.**
- **(c4)** **Designation is a record-level clause**, owned by the element outside every area (the Principal, `814b4608a`). For standing's membership, W entries are kept as **opaque members**: their identity, position, store and citations, with no W clause read (`opaque`, `in_D_S`).
- **(c5)** **Standing's values and marks are computed on its projection** (the record without W entries). Its membership is computed on the whole record (c4).

**Results** (Ded from (c1)–(c5); model v22):
1. **Values:** standing's values are the same with and without the W entries (check 33: 400 interleaved records, every act, two items, t = 0–8; W entries took T 191, F 989, U 7). *This is the sink property (c1)–(c2); for the sample, the equality is empirical.*
2. **Marks:** where the record is also in D_W, standing's marks are the same with and without the W entries (check 33: 311 of 311). The 89 records outside D_W, mostly W entries on single-use grants, are excluded by (c3) and not compared.
3. **Guards:** a W entry on a single-use grant is refused, and the grant stays usable by standing (check 35). So W cannot exhaust a standing grant.
4. **Designation:** the guard requires a store move to close a maximal W entry, and standing stays in D_S after such a move (check 36). *v21 refused it: that was the model placing a record-level clause under standing.*
5. **A W-clause breach is contained:** standing stays in D_S, and the record leaves D_W. *True by construction of the two tests (A-10, B F8).*
6. **Generic clauses are not containable**: identity, integrity, attestation, store, the presence and order of cited entries, and the designation chain.

**The criterion (A-09, B F16):** a clause is **generic** iff it decides **what an entry is or where it belongs**: its identity, its integrity, the store it is in, the record order it occupies, or what it cites. These are the record's clauses, owned by the element outside every area under T10-16 (b). A clause is **per-area** iff it is the acceptance rule of one area's kinds (§10.3.6 (d2)).
- An integrity failure makes the entry's **kind** untrustworthy, so it cannot be attributed to an area.
- A dangling citation from an intact entry **can** be attributed, but citations are record structure: a record with a citation to nothing is not one record. The criterion puts it with the record (A's P10 accepted as the criterion's consequence, not as an attribution failure).
- The model's `in_generic` omits the store-known clause, which `in_D` still checks for standing (A P11). It is noted for v23 if the model is extended.

## 9.18.7 Removing and annulling: record-keeping or "what counts" (the Principal's decision 1, `814b4608a`)

**Test:** does the operation change **any area's answers** (what counts)? Or only **what the record holds and where** (record-keeping)?

| Operation | Effect (book) | Classification |
|---|---|---|
| **designate** | moves the record; changes no value | **record-keeping**, the element outside every area (**decided** by the Principal) |
| **remove** (by tombstone) | drops content and keeps every required field; "the **values** at every cut are unchanged", only the marks gain "rests on removed content" (§9.16 L79) | **record-keeping**, the element outside every area. *Its authority is still evaluated as an act* (an unauthorized removal is refused, §9.13 v9), as a designation's is |
| **annul** | voids its target from the start, at any position; Φ reads it (§9.13 Θ_f ρ), so it changes values | **standing's**, "what counts". Annulment of an evidence entry is the same kind; W reads its effect (R5) |

**Reasons:**
- The test is the one the Principal's two decisions already apply: designation changes no value, and was placed outside every area; standing keeps "what counts".
- Removal changes no value either. The book already states it.
- Annulment changes values by definition.

**The distinction is clear under this test, so the question does not return to the Principal.**

*Limit:* a removal's authority is evaluated by Φ, which is standing's evaluator. So the element outside every area **relies on standing's answer** for the authority of its own operations (removal, designation). That is a dependency of the element on standing, recorded for the map (§10.4 R0).
