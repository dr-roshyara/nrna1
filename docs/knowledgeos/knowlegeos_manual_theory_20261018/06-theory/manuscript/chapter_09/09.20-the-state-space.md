*Terms used in this section.* **R**: the set of designated records (finite sequences of entries of the admitted kinds, with their fields, in record order). **V**: a state that assigns each item a vector of attribute values. **R+C**: R plus stored copies of derived values. **Q-quotient**: R modulo an equivalence that identifies records no requirement can tell apart. **DC-1–DC-7**: the disproof conditions of `RESEARCH-QUESTION.md` §3, in one line each: DC-1 an observer or guard needing information not in the record or the stated inputs (against R's sufficiency); DC-2 two different records every observer treats alike (against minimality: a quotient); DC-3 an attribute vector shown sufficient (against V); DC-4 a stored derived value a decided rule needs and cannot compute (against R+C); DC-5 an attribute with no assignable class, or two classes; DC-6 a step using an open fork as decided; DC-7 a result that also holds for an empty Req or a one-point 𝒮. **M1–M6**: the model's cases (`t902_state_model.py`): M1 item vectors not sufficient; M2 record order needed (same entries, different order, different Q1 at a cut); M3 act time needed apart from record order; M4 a stored derived value goes stale; M5 the recorder field changes no Q1 value; M6 the act times of roots and grants change no Q1 value. *Q-quotient* is used below only as DC-2's quotient. **Lifting rule**: how an answer about a claim is carried to the document or carrier that holds it (§9.12 B1); **aggregation rule**: how several entries' answers about one item combine into one (§9.4 C2) (the Professor's glosses). *(Restated at review: A-15, B F13; glosses added at the consolidation S0353–S0377, D-11.)*

## 9.20.1 In plain language

1. **What the kernel must remember is its record: the entries of the admitted kinds, each with its fields, in record order.** The core questions (current authority, lineage, the evidence class, conflicts among rules) and the guards can be worked out from that record, the call's own inputs, and a few stated inputs. This is close to true by construction, because the book defines those questions over the record; its content is the check, question by question, of what each one reads. *(Restated at review: A, C, B F4)*
2. **A table of attributes per item is not enough.** Lineage returns the entries themselves, so any state must keep the entries about items, in order. And two records can give an item the same standing, actor and grade and still answer differently after the same next entry.
3. **Standing and the evidence class are not stored; they are worked out.** A stored copy would go stale: an annulment voids a grant from the start, so values computed earlier change.
4. **Most of the attributes F13 lists are fields of entries, values worked out from the record, or inputs the kernel does not hold.** Several are **open**: they belong to kinds not yet admitted (registration, declassification), or they are a different property under the same name (a claim's provenance is not an act's; §1.3's grade is a mode of knowing, not the class). *(Restated at review: C F-5–F-7, A-07–A-10, B F5–F8)*
5. **The record is not the smallest such state.** Some recorded fields, such as a grant's act time, change no answer the book's questions give today. Whether the record is minimal depends on what three questions return, which the book has not fixed. *(Restated at review: A-03, B, C F-4)*

**Outcome: partially resolved** (§9.20.6).

**Th2. A state that forgets entries is not sufficient (Ded)** *(Restated at review: B F9, C, A-16, A-17)*.

- **§9.1 C4's toy state** has standing as a component; under C1 = constitute it is derived (Th1 step 1). C4's results concern the form of existence and minimality and stand; the toy state is not a candidate for 𝒮.

**Th3. A store of derived values adds no required distinction (Ded, with M4).** By Th1 every derived value is a function of the record and stated inputs; no decided rule reads a stored copy (the single-use guard **evaluates** CE* at append, §9.13.2). DC-4 is not met. **M4:** standing stored when the adoption is appended is T; after a later annulment of its grant, CE* is F, also at the adoption's own act time. A store needs recomputation over the whole history and adds an obligation (stored = derived) that the record does not have (CON-0141). *So R+C is R with a cache:* an engineering choice (EA), outside the theory's 𝒮.

**Th4. Act time is required (for the acts CE* orders; for roots and grants by D only, M6; scoped at the consolidation S0353–S0377, D-09), and record order is required relative to H2 and D (Ded; M2, M3)** *(Restated at review: C F-3, A-14)*. **The trivial case (DC-7).** A one-point 𝒮 answers every Q2 call with one value and every Q1 call with one value, so it cannot give T for a recorded adoption and F before it: a required, non-default answer is unreachable. ## 9.20.5 Minimality (DC-2): the record is not minimal for the observers as written; for Req's core it turns on three output forms

*Restated at review (A-03, A-05, A-06; B probes P2–P4, F10; C F-4, F-8, rigour note).*

- **The frame (Ext; C at review).** The smallest state is the set of reachable records modulo the coarsest right congruence that refines equality of all outputs: every observer at every call (including the cut), every reply, every guard verdict, after every finite extension. This is the minimal-realisation (Myhill–Nerode) construction, standard, not the book's own. A **minimal state** (a quotient) is not the same as a **minimal set of distinctions** (fields that may be dropped); both are kept apart below.
- **DC-2 pairs exhibited (models; Ded beyond them):**
  - **M6:** two records that differ only in the act time of the root (0 or −3), or of a grant (1 or 0), give equal Q1 values at every cut, item and act time tried, after none or one of the appended entries (418 of 418 cases each). *Ded:* the grant's basis reads its window and the act's own time, never the grant's act time; current authority reads only adoption and revocation times (§9.13 R5, current-authority). (A at review checked marks and guards too: equal.) *(R3, theory turn 1 (T9-22), §9.21.4, A-08: the Ded holds for grants based on a root; a delegated grant's act time is read, basis(g₁, g₂) testing it against g₁'s window, §9.13 L122, so a pair differing in it is separated by Q1's values)*
  - **M5, M5b:** two records that differ only in an adoption's recorder give equal Q1 values (322 of 322); under signed-as-other, the marks differ (M5b). *So M5 concerns Q1's values only.*
  - **Recorder or authentication status of a grant or root** (B, P2–P4: 2106 of 2106 each), and the recorder of a grant that is not single-use and signed as its actor (C, P2: Q1 values and marks 7028 of 7028, guard verdicts 156 of 156, g1 classes 74 of 74). *Ded:* the recorder is read only by the mark that needs signed-as-other (§9.13.2); entry identity excludes recorder and status (§9.16 L93), so no citation tells such entries apart, and merging treats them as **one** act; the owner check reads `app`, not the recorder. *(R3, theory turn 1 (T9-22), §9.21.4, C-01: the **status** of a grant or root in an answer's support is conveyed by Q1's marks, which read status over the support, §9.13 L137–138; so this item's claim holds for the recorder, and for status only of entries in no support)*
- **What would separate them, and why it is open.** Three output forms the book does not fix:
  - Q1's "acts the answer rests on" (§9.10 L34): as identifiers, or as whole entries;
  - Q2's scope: whether grants and roots are "items with a governed identifier" (§9.10 Q2), and whether it returns whole entries or their identities (A-05: by §9.16 L93 the M5 pair is one act);
  - Q3's "found (with the grant)" (§9.10 Q3): the grant's identity or the whole entry.
- **Conclusion.** For Q1's values, marks, the guards and g1, **the record is not a minimal state**: the act times of roots and grants and the recorder and status of grants and roots are distinctions none of them draws. **For Req's core, minimality turns on the three output forms**: with whole-entry outputs no such pair was found among these fields; with identity outputs such pairs exist. D already behaves partly as the quotient for recorder and status, through content-derived identity. *Minimality is open, with the deciding question named.* *(Decided since: Question O (c), 2026-11-11, §9.21.5: every entry is returned whole, so the record is a minimal state for Req's core over every field, independent of B-07.)*

## 9.20.6 Step 4: the conclusion

**Partially resolved.**
- **Established (Ded, within Th1's premises and scope):** the record (the admitted kinds, their fields, the generic clauses, record order, act times, removal state) is a sufficient state for Req's core, under either list of the core, and for the guards and O1–O4's effects (Th1, definitional). Any sufficient state must keep the entries about items, in order (Th2, from Q2 and Q1's act list). A store of derived values adds no required distinction (Th3). Act time is required (for the acts CE* orders; D-09); record order is required relative to prefix cuts and D (Th4).
- **Established against minimality (models; Ded):** for Q1's values, marks, the guards and g1 the record is not minimal (§9.20.5).
- **Classified (§9.20.7):** each F13 attribute, with the open rows named and the homonyms split.
- **Open:** minimality for Req's core (three output forms, §9.20.5); covers and the link as unversioned inputs (T10-11); held acts; the kinds outside D (T9-21; U6); item identity across versions (T9-14; G10); the relation of g1's classes and of current authority to Ch. 1's and Ch. 3's vocabularies (G7's F13 part; F6); the bearer (F37).
- **Why not "resolved":** sufficiency is definitional and scoped; minimality fails for part of the observers and is open for the core; held acts and the kinds outside D have no clauses.
- **Why not "unresolved":** sufficiency, the necessity of the entries (Th2) and of act time (Th4), and the place of derived values follow from decided definitions (C1 = constitute; D; W; Req's references).
