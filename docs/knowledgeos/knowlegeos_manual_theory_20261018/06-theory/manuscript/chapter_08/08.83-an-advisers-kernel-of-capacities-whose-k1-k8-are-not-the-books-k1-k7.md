## 8.83.1 In plain language

1. **S0356 separates recognizing what was said from deciding what is allowed and from executing it, and keeps ambiguity explicit between them.** It corrects the claim that the interpreter determines the correct meaning: the interpreter decides whether a candidate is admissible for the requested act. Its outcome set is the estate's attribution vocabulary, reused for meanings.
2. **S0356 cites a rule that development may tell the HPA research is needed but may never start a research track itself.** The rule is quoted by an adviser; the book has no record of it.
3. **S0357 names eight kernel "capacities" K1–K8.** They are not the book's K1–K7, which are laws and assumptions about the record. The same labels carry different objects.
4. **S0357's kernel decides whether a knowledge-state transition is admissible under "constitutional rules", without saying who acts.** The book's kernel rests on recorded acts of a holder. S0357 also offers a contradiction vocabulary different from the book's displayed classes.
5. **S0357's "minimum Kernel" is a list with the rule that everything else must earn its way in.** It is asserted, not derived; the book states no minimal kernel of KnowledgeOS either.

## 8.83.2 Claims and derivations

**C1. An interpretation/execution split; its outcomes reuse the attribution vocabulary (Src; Int, agreement).**
- **S0356:**
  - "> **AH-6 — Interpretation/Execution Separation:** · > KnowledgeOS should separate deterministic expression recognition and candidate generation from contextual governance interpretation and from authoritative workflow execution; ambiguity must remain explicit across the boundary rather than being resolved implicitly by the execution mechanism." (L159–160);
  - "> “The Governance Interpreter determines the correct meaning.” · That would be dangerous." (L66–68); "> **The Governance Interpreter evaluates candidate interpretations against applicable governance context and determines whether a candidate is admissible for the requested act.**" (L72);
  - "{MATCH | MISMATCH | AMBIGUOUS | UNRESOLVED}" (L55);
  - "But **AH-6 should remain a hypothesis until the existing architecture is checked against it.**" (L168).
- **Int:**
  - **Agreement with the evaluator's shape.** The book's CE* returns T, F or U with reasons (§9.13), and an unresolved case stays U rather than being forced. *AH-6's "ambiguity must remain explicit" and its correction from "correct meaning" to "admissible for the requested act" agree in direction: the evaluator decides admissibility, not truth.* AH-6 is the adviser's hypothesis (L168).
  - **A homonym.** MATCH, MISMATCH, AMBIGUOUS and UNRESOLVED are the attribution check's values in the estate (§8.71 C4; §8.77 C2), comparing a declared label with a registered one. *S0356 reuses them as outcomes of interpreting a meaning* (L55). The same four words name two different comparisons.
- **Not a new concept.**

**C2. A standing rule cited by an adviser (Src; Int).**
- **S0356:** "The HPA standing rule now explicitly says that development should **inform the HPA when research is needed, but never initiate a research track autonomously**. The SNF research track is closed and the real-language semantic/compiler question is parked in unauthorized OQ-4." (L143).
- **Int:** The rule is reported by an adviser, with no record of the act that adopted it in the window. *§8.45 C1 found an assistant's words recorded as the HPA's;* here the adviser cites the HPA's rule. Its content agrees with this book's own discipline (T9-21 and T9-02 wait for an authorised turn). *Agreement in content is not support for its standing:* within the window it is a report.
- **Not a new concept.**

**C3. A kernel of eight "capacities" whose K1–K8 are not the book's K1–K7 (Src; Int).**
- **S0357:**
  - "I would define **eight essential capacities**." (L52); "### K1 — Identity" (L54); "### K2 — Evidence" (L74); "### K7 — Constitutional Admissibility" (L194); "### K8 — Deterministic State Transition" (L236).
- **§9.15 (the book):** K1 is what is detectable from the record; K2 is "a single designated record" (§9.15 K2, L75); K3–K7 are the inputs, the per-kind clauses, the integrity check, CE*'s implementation and the evaluator version (§9.15 L76–80).
- **Int:**
  - **Two objects under one label.** S0357's K1–K8 are capacities the kernel must have (identity, evidence, provenance, context, contradiction, time, admissibility, determinism). The book's K1–K7 are laws and assumptions about the record that the kernel's guarantees rest on. *S0357's K2 (Evidence) is not the book's K2 (a single designated record); S0357's K7 (Constitutional Admissibility) is not the book's K7 (the evaluator version).* A reader who meets "K2" or "K7" in a later source must read which list it belongs to.
  - **Agreements by content.** S0357's "Representation equality must never automatically become identity equality" (L68) agrees with §8.18's distinction (byte equality is not identity, CON-0240, one direction); its K8 (determinism, L240) agrees with the book's K7 and replay. *Agreement within one lineage of advice, not support.*
- **Not a new concept.**

**C4. Admissibility without an actor; a second contradiction vocabulary (Src; Int).**
- **S0357:**
  - "> **Given a candidate meaning, its evidence, context, history and applicable constitutional rules, what epistemic state transition is admissible?**" (L44);
  - "> Is this proposed state transition constitutionally admissible? · Not: · > Is this statement true?" (L200–204);
  - "SUPPORTED · UNKNOWN · CONFLICTED · REJECTED · SUPERSEDED" (L160–164); "The exact final vocabulary still belongs to the remaining architectural decisions." (L167).
- **Int:**
  - **Admissibility, not truth:** *agreement* with A1's separation of authority from truth and with §9.13 (the kernel answers standing, not correctness).
  - **No actor.** S0357's admissibility is decided from "a candidate meaning, its evidence, context, history and applicable constitutional rules" (L44); no holder, act or grant appears among its inputs, and its eight capacities name none. *The book's kernel derives current authority from recorded acts of a holder (A1; §9.10's kinds of act).* So S0357's kernel is epistemic where the book's is a kernel of the record of acts. *Whether these are one kernel seen from two sides or two kernels is not decided in the window.*
  - **A second vocabulary.** S0357's five states (L160–164) differ from the book's displayed classes (refuted, supported, not attested in the record at a cut, U, contested; §9.18) and S0357 leaves its own open (L167). Recorded, not mapped.
- **Not a new concept.**

**C5. A minimum asserted, not derived (Src; Int, agreement).**
- **S0357:** "If we apply the **Reduction Test** aggressively, I would reduce the Kernel to approximately:" (L440); "Everything else should have to **earn its way inside**." (L463); "I would **not start coding the Kernel yet**." (L485); "> **The Kernel should be incapable of doing things that belong to interpretation.**" (L390).
- **Int:** The "minimum Kernel" (L442–461) is a list reached by an unstated test, "approximately" (L440). *It is asserted, not derived.* §9.19.8 states no minimal kernel of KnowledgeOS; a minimal kernel is well posed only for a fixed pool (§9.19). *Agreement in caution* (not coding yet, L485). The "capacity ceiling" (L390) states as a design rule what §9.6.4's relocation test checks per part: what the kernel need not do. *The adviser's "Reduction Test" is not defined in the window* and is not identified here with the relocation test.
- **Not a new concept.**
