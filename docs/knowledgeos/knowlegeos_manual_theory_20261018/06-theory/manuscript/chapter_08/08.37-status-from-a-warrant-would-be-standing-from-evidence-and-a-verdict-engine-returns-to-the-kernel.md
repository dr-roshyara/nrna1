## 8.37.1 In plain language

1. **One source says that a conclusion without a stated reason "remains a hypothesis".** This can mean three things. As a rule that decides standing from the reason, it breaks the book's basic rule that only acts confer standing. As a requirement that an adoption must cite its reason, it is a choice the Principal could make, against a clause that is only proposed. As a label on the content, it belongs to applications. A reason ("warrant") is not the same as evidence: an item with supporting evidence but no stated reason would show "supported" today.
2. **A "reasoning verification engine" with five verdicts is proposed as a new kernel capability.** It did not appear suddenly: the line moved checking into KnowledgeOS step by step. In the book, such checks are applications that write evidence and decide nothing. The kernel's answer is standing: T, F or U. None of the five verdicts is the book's "undetermined".
3. **"Relations are first-class"**: the relations that bear on authority (citations, covers, targets, holders) are the record. Relations between items ("supported by", "bounded by", "derived through") are required but not admitted: they have no home yet. **"A statement is limited by its context"** agrees for standing, through scope; limits on a statement's validity are content.
4. **"A challenge must not delete the challenged claim"** agrees: nothing is deleted. But the "conflict space" the source wants recorded has no home in the book.
5. **"Pseudo-evidence"**: inside the record, an entry citing a missing entry is refused. A fabricated outside document is another matter: if an authorised producer records evidence that rests on it, the display shows "supported".
6. **The chat relays its candidate wording from source to source, and again leans toward a technology ("knowledge graph", hedged as "probably").**

## 8.37.2 Claims and derivations

**C1. Status from a warrant has three readings; a warrant is not evidence (Src; Ded against A1; Int).** *(Restated at review: W-01, W-02.)*
- **S0223's H-KOS-Vyapti-001:** "KnowledgeOS SHALL preserve the warranting relation that connects evidence to conclusion. A conclusion without an explicit inference relation SHALL remain a hypothesis" (L69–71). The source separates evidence from warrant: "Why is smoke a legitimate indicator of fire?" (L27–63).
- **Ded:**
  - **Sentence 1** asks to *record* a relation. Under the proposal (adopted since, T9-19), an evidence entry has *about*, *polarity* and its producer's grant (§9.18); it has **no warrant field**. The warrant is therefore not held today. Its nearest home is the reasons gap (reasons for T and F not defined, §9.13 L166) or G7's row (§10.2).
  - **Sentence 2** makes a status depend on the relation. Three readings:
    - **(i) A value function** (standing computed from the warrant): breaks A1, only an act confers standing (§1.6; §8.33 C2).
    - **(ii) A guard on adoption** (an adoption must cite its warrant): standing still comes from the act, so A1 holds. It conflicts only with the clause (c2), then proposed (§9.18). *A choice for the Principal* *(decided: no guard; c2 kept, (the Principal, 2026-10-31: "No guard, keep c2"; T9-20))*, like the guard reading of §8.36 C1.
    - **(iii) A content status** ("hypothesis" as a label of content): the kernel has no content value (§8.36 C4 (iii)). *An application matter.*
  - **Not "not attested":** an authorised producer's live "supports" entry with no warrant shows "supported" under g0, where the source says "hypothesis". Displaying the source's status would need a class promise (b) lacks (supported, warrant not recorded) or a warrant field.
- **Relay** *(origin added at the consolidation S0201–S0225, C F-10)*: the line starts at S0217's H-KOS-Inference-001, "its authority is dependent on the validity of that chain" (S0217 L376–384), which is reading (i), a value function, contrary to A1. S0220 L288–290 has the same name as S0223, with only the first sentence. S0219 TARKA-002 asks that the relation be *established*. S0222 already carries "Inference Rule" and "General Rule" (L188, L235). One name carries three strengths.
- **Not a new concept.** *A fork: "supported, warrant not recorded" as a class, or a warrant field (with G7 and the reasons gap).*
