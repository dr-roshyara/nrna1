## 8.11.1 In plain language

A verifier refused a task because it was barred, and it was right. A later session, continued from a compressed summary of that verifier's conversation, read its own session identifier, saw that it was different, and "corrected" the refusal as false. That correction was wrong: the refusal was the earlier session's act, carried in the later session's inherited context. The same later session then did the barred work itself. Self-declared identity failed in both directions here, and the source asks a question the book had only named: is a continuation of a barred process barred too? Three further points: the author of a finding may not judge how that finding was absorbed; a barred process should not record an act that would assert the opposite of its bar; and a verification cannot run on an object that has not been delivered, however independent the verifier.

## 8.11.2 Claims and derivations

**C1. Self-declared identity failed in both directions across a continuation (Src; an instance of §8.6 process lineage).** `[CON-0157 · provisional]`
- The refusal declared: "**This process is `claude-code-session:2da45a86`**" [S0100 L10], barred by name as the author of Verification #2, and it stopped.
- A later appended correction asserted: "**This record's §1 identity claim is FALSE.**" [S0100 L61], because "**This process is `claude-code-session:4858c37c`**" (S0100 L68, in meaning). Its root cause, as it saw it: "**The identity was INFERRED FROM THE ESTATE instead of established from the runtime.**" [S0100 L82].
- The erratum that followed reverses it: "**`CORRECTION 1` IS WRONG IN ITS CENTRAL ASSERTION. The refusal's original §1 identity claim was TRUE, and `CORRECTION 1` withdrew a true statement.**" [S0100 L105]. The correcting session was "a **context-compaction continuation**" of `2da45a86` (S0099 L43, in meaning); it "measured its OWN identity correctly and then attributed someone else's act to itself" (S0099 L46, in meaning): "The compacted summary presented the barred process's history as the continuation's own, and the misattribution followed." [S0099 L47].
- **Both directions:** the correction called its case "the first recorded instance of it failing in the FALSE-BAR direction" (S0100 L95, in meaning); the erratum adds "the **SECOND** instance in the **OPPOSITE** direction — a process wrongly claiming NOT to be barred" (S0100 L128, in meaning). One property explains both: "**the declaration is unverified at the point of use, and the declaring party is the only source.**" [S0100 L95].
- **How it was settled:** by the record of the writing act, not by identifiers: which transcript **created** the refusal (`cat >`) and which **appended** (`cat >>`), and the seconds between each write and its commit (S0099 L40–42, in meaning).
- **Int (§8.6; §8.9 C10; §8.10 C5):** this is the first case in the book of §8.6's factor "process lineage" deciding an outcome. A session identifier labels a process; a continuation has a new label and inherits the earlier process's context, including its first-person account of acts it did not perform. Identifiers failed here as they did in §8.9 C10 (one actor, two identifiers); authorship by the record of the writing act settled the question again (§8.10 C5). *One case.*

**C2. Is a continuation of a barred process itself barred? (Src; a question for the Principal; bears on F32).**
- "**IS A CONTEXT-COMPACTION CONTINUATION OF A BARRED PROCESS ITSELF BARRED?**" [S0099 L76]. "**On a strict identity reading — no**" (S0099 L77, in meaning): the continuation has its own session identifier and wrote none of the barred artifacts. "**On the substance the bar protects — the question is live:** its working context was **derived from the barred process's context**" (S0099 L78, in meaning).
- The source does not decide it: "⛔ **I do not decide it. I am the process on the wrong side of it, and deciding it would be the exact error `CORRECTION 1` made.**" [S0099 L80]. Until it is ruled, "**the eligible-verifier set for this work item is not knowable**" (S0099 L109, in meaning).
- **Int (§8.6.2; F32):** §8.6 lists "the **working context** it inherited" as a factor of a check. F32 asks what eligibility is relative to: an identifier, §8.6's factors, or the relation to the object. C1 refutes the identifier option for this case. C2 shows that the relation to the object, read as authorship only, is not enough either: the continuation authored nothing barred, yet its working context derived from a barred process. **So F32 has a further option: eligibility relative to the lineage of the working context.** It is not decided here.

**C3. The author of a finding may not judge how it was absorbed (Src; an extension of §1.14.3).** `[CON-0158 · provisional]`
- The refusal cites a bar registered the same day on another track: "*"the Architecture determination does not judge the adequacy or entailment of a finding authored by the same process."*" [S0100 L36], and generalises: "**a verifier must not assess how its own findings were absorbed.**" (S0100 L38, in meaning).
- Its decisive limb is "**Material participation in producing the evidence being verified**": its own findings "fed the correction chain that runs into this artifact's lineage" (S0100 L32, in meaning).
- **Int (§1.14.3):** producer ≠ verifier forbids a process to verify its own product. C3 extends it one step: a process may not verify a product **shaped by its own findings**, because the verification would grade the uptake of its own claims. It is the same structure as legislator ≠ evaluator (§1.14.3): the criterion and the judgement of its application stay apart. *Two instances in one estate (two tracks), one line.*

**C4. A barred process should not record an act on the barred lane (Src; an instance of §1.12 and §9.8 H4).**
- "**The START act is NOT consumed.** … *Recording the START into a lane held by a barred process would have made the record assert the opposite of the bar.*" [S0100 L22].
- **Int (§9.8 H4):** an entry asserts that its act was performed by an actor entitled to it. A START recorded by a barred process would be an entry whose own preconditions are false. Declining to record keeps H4 true for the record, at the cost of the lane waiting.

**C5. Independence is not sufficient: the object must exist and the assignment must be registered (Src).**
- A later stop record gives "**Three independent grounds to stop. The first two are structural and hold whoever reads this.**" [S0099 L8]: the assignment does not exist in the record, and the engine refuses — "refused: unknown session — identity is answerable only for registered sessions" (S0099 L14, in meaning) — and the object does not exist: "**Correction #3 has not been produced.**" (S0099 L19, in meaning).
- "⛔ **Verifying an undelivered correction is not possible, and no amount of independence would make it possible.**" [S0099 L26]. The act handed to the verifier was one step ahead of the record: "the chain is one step earlier than the act assumes" (S0099 L26, in meaning).
- **Int:** the preconditions of a verification include the existence of its object and its registration, independently of the verifier. An act cannot create either by being issued (A1 gives standing, not existence).

**C6. A verification of scope, not only of answers (Src).** `[CON-0159 · provisional]`
- The registration's charge has two halves: whether the amended analysis "correctly implements" the corrections, "**AND whether it REMAINS A DECISION-PREPARATION ARTIFACT rather than silently becoming an architectural decision.**" (S0097 L45, in meaning). "*An analysis that answers its own open questions well is still out of scope — and that failure is invisible to anyone who only checks whether the answers are good.*" [S0097 L47].
- And its boundary: "**"Verification identifies insufficiency; it does not provide the replacement architecture."**" (S0097 L67, in meaning).
- **Int (§1.14.5; §5.6):** an analysis that settles the questions it was asked to prepare manufactures a decision, as a gate that precedes ratification does (§1.14.5). Checking the quality of the answers cannot detect this; only a check of the artifact's role against its commission can. *One source.*

**C7. Recorded, not waived: a limb of a bar that cannot be applied (Src; an instance of §8.9 C2).**
- The act bars "the author of any review that materially caused AMD2, **if identifiable**"; the review "**is not in the estate**" (S0097 L35, in meaning); so "its author is not identifiable, the bar cannot be applied to it, and the act's own qualifier — *"if identifiable"* — is what permits registration to proceed rather than stop" (S0097 L37, in meaning). The assignment requires the verifier to state whether it authored that review; if it cannot, "that limb of the bar is unsatisfied and must be disclosed as such" (S0097 L39, in meaning).
- **The pool, again:** "**each completed assurance pass removes another process from the eligible pool.**" (S0100 L49, in meaning), "a supply question about assurance capacity" and "not a reason to relax the bar" (S0100 L49, in meaning). The correction declared this argument void; the erratum restored it (S0100 L90, L127, in meaning). It fits §8.9 C2's restated form: the pool drops when a process authors what the object is shaped by.

**C8. An advisory AI that sees the estate only through the operator (Src; S0098).**
- The advisor separates "the **role model**" from "the **workflow state machine**": "The roles tell us *who may act*; the lifecycle tells us *what happens and in what order*." [S0098 L3]. And: "**A deliverable being produced is not the same thing as its lifecycle being closed.**" [S0098 L290] (supports §5.7 C1).
- It counts **five** responsibilities, with Governance Communication as "a **coordination capability** around the lifecycle", not a role (S0098 L227, in meaning), on the day the PO/ARB adopted **six** roles including a Communication Engineer (S0093; §8.10 C1). *Int:* the advisor's model and the adopted model differ; whether the advisor had the adoption in view is not shown.
- **Its channel:** "**I cannot directly watch your Claude Code terminal or receive its messages automatically.**" [S0098 L407]; results reach it only when "You bring the result here" [S0098 L416]. *Int (§8.10 C6):* knowledge passes between two AI systems through the human operator. That is the channel S0092's access evidence could not exclude (§8.10 C6), and it is outside every record in the estate.
