## 8.69.1 In plain language

1. **The PO/ARB's rulings, as registered, disagree on who verified the resolver.**
   - The V-8 determination says one process "performed the independent verification".
   - The authoring commission names that process as the verifier and a second process as the producer.
   - The earlier correction commission lists that second process as a verifier too.

   Two registered rulings support the verification report's own map, and one supports the registration the book found doubtful in §8.67. The process the doubtful registration called the producer is barred in one ruling and not mentioned in the other.
2. **The correction needed a lane, a lane needs a workflow record, and creating the record would have decided an open question.** Governance flagged the circle and did not choose an exit. The PO/ARB closed it by deciding the question first, so the record is now created "by authority".
   - That authority is an act outside the record. The record begins with no transitions and no grants.
   - This is the book's regress ending at a root act, and the open question of how a first record is lawfully begun.
   - The processes that register these acts have no lane themselves. They act "on the PO/ARB's direction", the pattern the ruling rejected for the correction.
3. **"Technical evidence: this process performed the verification. Governed role: not yet established."** The ruling refuses to give the verifier a role "just because its report exists". This is the book's line exactly: evidence is not an act, and a role is not granted after the fact by a report.
4. **The authoring commission appoints a "fresh Architecture process" with the identity left as a placeholder,** to be "declared by the appointed actor and verified". The estate's own rule says process identity is self-declared and cannot be attested. So the required verification has no mechanism, and the next step stays blocked. The eligibility rules bar processes by labels whose attribution is itself in dispute.
5. **Several statements agree with the book:**
   - the properties the verification proved are kept as conditions any correction must preserve;
   - "do not claim closure because text or code changed";
   - the author may report only "ready for re-verification", never accepted.

## 8.69.2 Claims and derivations

**C1. The registered rulings disagree on who verified (Src; Int; bears on §8.67 C1).**
- **S0318:** "| `8a525719` · `d1612e03` | **verifier** *(both named in the ruling; `d1612e03` is recorded as delivered — Governance has not independently established its role)* |" and "| `a8ce5a39` | **producer** of the implementation being corrected |" (L72–73).
- **S0319:** "| **Technical evidence** | ✅ `d1612e03` performed the independent verification — the report stands as technical evidence |" (L57).
- **S0320:** "MUST NOT be the AST-017 producer (`claude-code-session:8a525719`) · MUST NOT be the independent technical verifier (`claude-code-session:d1612e03`, or the verifier identity recorded in the verification artifact)" (L32), given as verbatim eligibility.
- **Int:**
  - **Two rulings, as registered, support S0315's map:** S0319 L57 (`d1612e03` verified) and S0320 L32 (`8a525719` produced; `d1612e03` verified). That is §8.67 C1's reading (a), under which S0316's map is wrong. *R3 note for §8.67 C1: reading (a) gains support from S0319 L57 and S0320 L32.*
  - **One ruling, as registered, supports S0316's verifier:** S0318 names `8a525719` as a verifier, with `d1612e03` (L73), and bars `a8ce5a39` as the producer (L72). S0318's registrar is `b64828fe`, the registrar of S0316. S0319 and S0320 are registered by `b51dba91`.
  - **The consequence.** Under S0320's eligibility, `a8ce5a39` is not barred. Under S0318's it is. The rulings' own texts are not in the window; only their registrations are. *Which text the PO/ARB issued, and whether S0318 or S0320 transcribes it faithfully, is not shown.* By §8.06 C3's second premise, a record that contradicts itself establishes nothing about independence. The decisive evidence is still the provenance of S0315's writing act (§8.10 C5).
- **Not a new concept.**

**C2. A circularity closed by an act outside the record; a record created with no act in it (Src; Int).**
- **S0318:** "correction needs a lane → lane needs a workflow record → creating the record decides V-8 → V-8 must not be decided by fiat" (L52–55); "⛔ **Governance does not choose the exit.**" (L57); "⚠️ **What must not happen: the correction beginning without either exit chosen** — that would resolve V-8 by omission, which is the same defect as resolving it by fiat." (L64).
- **S0319:**
  - "*\"KOS-SESSION-BOOTSTRAP-001 SHALL be registered as a governed work item in the existing workflow model before its correction work proceeds.\"*" (L18);
  - "*\"We should not resolve a governance question accidentally through implementation.\"*" (L26);
  - "The record is therefore created **by authority**, not by implementation fiat." (L35);
  - Option (b), a directed act without a lane, rejected: "*\"… the workflow engine should be the authoritative place for responsibility and authorization.\"*" (L37);
  - "Registered by: Governance — `claude-code-session:b51dba91` *(… this session's own bootstrap resolves `UNRESOLVED` (no governed lane attributes to it); the PO/ARB's explicit direction is the authority for this act …)*" (L3).
- **S0320:** "Workflow record CREATED (OPEN · transitions=0 · grants=0 · mutationOwner=null)" (L20).
- **Int:**
  - **The circle and its exit.** The circle is real: the act that would host the correction would itself decide the reserved question. The exit chosen is an act prior to the record, the PO/ARB ruling, which decides the question and authorizes the record's creation. *Agreement* with the book's regress. Authority within a record traces back to acts outside it, and the chain ends at root acts (§1.14.1; §8.51 C1). How the first act is lawfully made is the book's open genesis question (T1-17; N4-15).
  - **The creating act is not registered in the window (W, step 1).** S0319 names record creation as "the subsequent lawful step" (L81). S0320 reports the record CREATED (L20). No registration of the creating act appears between them.
  - **A record that begins empty.** The record is created with "transitions=0 · grants=0" (S0320 L20). Its authority is a ruling recorded only in prose registrations. *Within the record, nothing yet shows why the record exists.* That is the designation question of §9.17 R1: what makes this store the record. The answer here is an act the store does not contain.
  - **"Resolve by omission is the same defect as resolve by fiat"** (S0318 L64). *Agreement:* a reserved question answered without the reserving act is answered without standing, whether by an act or by inaction.
  - **Registration without a lane** (S0319 L3; S0320 L3). Both registrations are made by a process whose own bootstrap resolves UNRESOLVED, "on the PO/ARB's explicit direction". That is the pattern of the rejected Option (b), a directed act without a lane, used for the registrations themselves. Two readings:
    - (i) registering a ruling is a recorder's act, which needs no lane (§1.14.3: recorder ≠ decider);
    - (ii) the rejected mode survives at the registration step.

    The sources state neither. *Recorded.*
  - **A deadlock between gates.** This is the kind §8.65 C4 found in a deadlock argument and §9.16 R6 places as law 20a (AE) in applications. Here it is resolved by a ruling, not by the engine. *Agreement with R6's placement.*
- **Not a new concept.**

**C3. Evidence is not a governed role; no retroactive role (Src; Int, agreement).**
- **S0319:** "| **Technical evidence** | ✅ `d1612e03` performed the independent verification — the report stands as technical evidence | · | **Governed role** | ⛔ **NOT YET ESTABLISHED** |" (L57–58); "*\"I would NOT treat `d1612e03` as an established governance role yet.\"* … *\"Do not retroactively give it a workflow role just because its report exists.\"*" (L60).
- **Int:** *agreement* with A1. A report is evidence (O2), not an act that confers a role. A role exists only by a recorded grant. Granting one after the fact "just because its report exists" would derive standing from evidence: the guard the Principal declined for the kernel (T9-20), applied here to a role. It also *agrees* with §8.67 C1's "advisory technical evidence only" and with §1.12 (record ≠ act).
- **Not a new concept.**

**C4. An appointment without an identity; verification of an identity the estate holds unattestable (Src; Int).**
- **S0320:**
  - "> **Fresh Architecture process: `claude-code-session:<ACTOR-ID>`**" (L30);
  - "*\"The actual process identity MUST be declared by the appointed actor and verified through the existing governance mechanism. No process may adopt another process's identity.\"*" (L34);
  - "**Until that resolution, `REGISTER` cannot lawfully run** (a REGISTER transition carries the concrete `session` id + `executionContext`)." (L36);
  - "Governance has **not** REGISTERED a lane, adopted an identity, or fabricated one — doing so would violate … `INV-ATTR-1`/`INV-ATTR-2` (identity is evidence-only)." (L95).
- **S0315** (§8.67): identity is "self-declared, not independently attestable".
- **Int:**
  - **A grant to an unnamed holder.** The act appoints a role and its eligibility, not a holder. In the book a grant names its holder (§1.13), so this act authorizes nothing until a holder is named. The source says so itself ("REGISTER cannot lawfully run", L36). *Agreement.*
  - **Verification of identity without a mechanism.** The holder's identity must be "declared … and verified through the existing governance mechanism" (L34). The estate's invariants hold identity "self-declared, not attestable" (INV-ATTR-1/2; §8.67 C1). So no mechanism the estate recognises can verify the declaration. *The requirement and the invariant cannot both be met as stated.* Two readings: (a) "verified" means checked against the record (MATCH), which is attribution as evidence, not attestation; (b) a new attestation mechanism is presupposed. The source does not say which.
  - **Eligibility over disputed labels.** The bars name labels (L32) whose attribution is in dispute (C1). A rule that bars "the producer" by a label can bar the wrong process.
  - **Bars by identity, not by role (W, step 1).** Governance is barred as `b64828fe` (S0318 L74; S0320 L32), but the current Governance session is `b51dba91`. A bar on a session label does not carry over to a new session in the same role. Whether the bar is on the role or on the session is not said.
  - **Role withheld, then used (W, step 1).** S0319 says `d1612e03`'s governed role is "NOT YET ESTABLISHED" (L58). S0320 bars it *as* "the independent technical verifier" (L32). Used for exclusion only, this is consistent, but it names a role S0319 withheld.
- **Not a new concept.**

- **Int:**
  - **A must-do, not only prohibitions.** "SHALL be registered … before its correction work proceeds" (S0319 L18) is an obligation. *Agreement* with §8.42 C1's floor: must-dos alongside prohibitions.
  - **Verified properties as scope conditions:** *agreement* with append-only (H1′) in spirit. What a verification established is not undone silently by a later change.
  - **Closure needs evidence** (S0320 L64): *agreement* with the Principal's rule that a finding is not resolved because code or tests pass.
  - **The status rule** (L72): the producer bar (§1.14.3), stated as an output vocabulary.
  - **A decision stated with its scope** (S0319 L41–51): *agreement* with §8.50 C4's separation of what an act does from what it does not.
  - **"The workflow engine should be the authoritative place for responsibility and authorization"** (S0319 L37): a designation of the authoritative record (§9.17 R1), made by a ruling.

## 8.69.4 Challenges recorded

- **C1, rival:** S0318 lists both processes as verifiers because the ruling named both, so the rulings agree.
  - *Answer:* S0320 names `8a525719` as the **producer** (L32). The registered rulings then disagree on that label's role.
- **C2, rival:** the empty record is lawful because the ruling authorizes it.
  - *Answer:* agreed. The point is that its authority lies outside the record, which is the book's root-act case.
- **C4, rival:** "verified" means attribution MATCH.
  - *Answer:* that is reading (a), kept. Then "verified" is evidence, not attestation, and the requirement adds nothing beyond INV-ATTR.
