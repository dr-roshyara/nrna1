## 8.69.1 In plain language

1. **The PO/ARB's rulings, as registered, follow their registrars on who verified the resolver.**
   - The two rulings registered by one Governance process adopt the verification report's own map. One of them bars "the verifier identity recorded in the verification artifact", so it defers to that report's self-declaration.
   - The ruling registered by the other Governance process repeats that process's earlier map, which the book found doubtful in §8.67.

   So the evidence is one registrar line against another. Nothing in either bears on who wrote the verification report, which is what would decide the question. §8.67's weighing is unchanged.
2. **The correction had to run in a new lane. A lane needs a workflow record, and creating the record would have decided a question reserved to the PO/ARB.** The source calls this a circularity. It is an obligation whose only means requires a reserved decision: a deadlock between gates, not a cycle. Governance flagged it and did not choose an exit. The PO/ARB, who held the reservation, decided the question first, so the record could be created "by authority".
   - That authority is a root act in the book's sense: an act of the designated human authority, outside the record.
   - The record begins with no transitions and no grants.
   - The act that created it is not registered in these sources.
3. **"Technical evidence: this process performed the verification. Governed role: not yet established."** The ruling refuses to give the verifier a role "just because its report exists". This matches the book exactly. A report is evidence, not an act. The book keeps evidence entries out of the bases of standing, and the same rule keeps a report from being the basis of a role.
4. **The authoring commission appoints a "fresh Architecture process" with the identity left as a placeholder.** The identity is to be "declared … and verified" before the lane can be registered. The estate's existing check compares a declared label with a registered one. A registered label exists only after registration. So, read that way, verification can only follow the very step it is meant to unblock: an ordering gap parallel to the first one. The bars name session labels, and whether they bind the role or the session is not said.
5. **Several statements agree with the book:**
   - "do not claim closure because text or code changed";
   - the author may report only "ready for re-verification";
   - V-8 is decided with its scope stated, "governed" is kept apart from "adopted", and V-8 is then closed to reopening;
   - the properties the verification proved are kept as conditions any correction must preserve. The book has no direct home for this non-regression rule.

- **Int:**
  - **One line against one.** The rulings registered by `b51dba91` adopt S0315's self-declared map. **C2. A deadlock between gates, dissolved by the reserving authority; a record created with no act in it (Src; Int).** *(Restated at review: W-03, W-04, W-05, W-11.)*
- **S0318:**
  - "*\"The next Architecture actor should be freshly appointed by PO/ARB, **with a new lane and human START**.\"*" (L48);
  - "correction needs a lane → lane needs a workflow record → creating the record decides V-8 → V-8 must not be decided by fiat" (L52–55), headed as a "circularity" (L46);
  - "⛔ **Governance does not choose the exit.**" (L57);
  - "that would resolve V-8 by omission, which is the same defect as resolving it by fiat." (L64).
- **S0319:**
  - "*\"KOS-SESSION-BOOTSTRAP-001 SHALL be registered as a governed work item in the existing workflow model before its correction work proceeds.\"*" (L18);
  - "*\"We should not resolve a governance question accidentally through implementation.\"*" (L26);
  - "The record is therefore created **by authority**, not by implementation fiat." (L35);
  - registered by `b51dba91`, whose "own bootstrap resolves `UNRESOLVED` … the PO/ARB's explicit direction is the authority for this act" (L3);
  - record creation is "the *subsequent* lawful step … to be performed by Governance" (L81).
- **S0320:** "Workflow record CREATED (OPEN · transitions=0 · grants=0 · mutationOwner=null)" (L20).
- **Int:**
  - **A deadlock, not a cycle (W-04).** The chain is not circular. The obligation is the ruling's "new lane" (L48), and its only means (a record) entails a decision reserved to the PO/ARB. In deontic form: O(p), p → q, and q is reserved. That is a conflict between gates. The source's word "circularity" (L46) is kept as its word. *The book names the same structure a deadlock argument* (§8.62 C4, W-15; §9.16 R6, law 20a). The holder of the reservation dissolves it by making q.
  - **A root act (W-03).** The ruling is an act of the designated human authority, which in the book is the root: "acts of the human authority that the organisation's constitution designates" (§1.14.1 L161). Its authority-side form is "who may approve at day zero" (§1.14.1 L164). Here the root act is identified; N4-15 asks which act is root in general. *Agreement, in the weaker sense that the record's authority lies outside the record.* Genesis (T1-17) is a different question, an evidence prerequisite (§1.14.1 L163), and is not used.
  - **A record that begins empty.** The record has "transitions=0 · grants=0" (S0320 L20), and its authority is a ruling recorded only in prose registrations. *Within the record, nothing yet shows why the record exists*, which is §9.17 R1's question of what makes a store the record. The creating act itself is not registered in the window (S0319 L81; S0320 L20).
  - **"Resolving by omission is the same defect as resolving by fiat"** (S0318 L64). *Agreement:* a reserved question answered without the reserving act is answered without standing, whether by an act or by inaction.
  - **Who acts without a lane (W-11).** Only `b51dba91` is stated to resolve UNRESOLVED (S0319 L3; S0320 L3). S0318 L3 gives no lane status for `b64828fe`. There are three cases:
    - (i) a registration is a recorder's act and needs no lane (§1.14.3: recorder ≠ decider);
    - (ii) the rejected mode, a directed act without a lane, survives at the registration step;
    - (iii) the record-creating act is an act on the store, not a registration. Reading (i) does not cover it, and it is unregistered.

    The sources settle none of these. *Recorded.*
- **Not a new concept.**

**C3. Evidence is not a governed role; no retroactive role (Src; Int, agreement).** *(Restated at review: W-02.)*
- **S0319:** "| **Technical evidence** | ✅ `d1612e03` performed the independent verification … | · | **Governed role** | ⛔ **NOT YET ESTABLISHED** |" (L57–58); "*\"I would NOT treat `d1612e03` as an established governance role yet.\"* … *\"Do not retroactively give it a workflow role just because its report exists.\"*" (L60).
- **Int:** *agreement* with A1. A report is evidence (O2), not an act that confers a role. A role exists only by a recorded grant. Granting one "just because its report exists" would make a report the **basis** of a role. The book's (c2) excludes evidence entries from the bases of standing (§9.18), and the Principal kept (c2) when deciding T9-20. *(T9-20 declined a guard that would make evidence **necessary** for standing. S0319 refuses to treat evidence as **sufficient** for a role. The two do not conflict.)* It also *agrees* with §8.67 C1's "advisory technical evidence only" and §1.12 (record ≠ act).
- **Not a new concept.**

**C4. An appointment whose verification must follow the REGISTER it unblocks; bars by label (Src; Int).** *(Restated at review: W-06, W-07, W-08, W-12.)*
- **S0320:**
  - "> **Fresh Architecture process: `claude-code-session:<ACTOR-ID>`**" (L30);
  - "*\"The actual process identity MUST be declared by the appointed actor and verified through the existing governance mechanism. No process may adopt another process's identity.\"*" (L34);
  - "**Until that resolution, `REGISTER` cannot lawfully run** (a REGISTER transition carries the concrete `session` id + `executionContext`)." (L36);
  - "identity is evidence-only" (L95).
- **S0315:** the attribution check compares a declared label with the registered one: "Registered reviewer `REGISTERED1` vs running `INTRUDER` → `MISMATCH` … No identity supplied → `UNKNOWN`" (L109–111).
- **S0316:** identity "**Self-declared, not independently attestable**" (L23).
- **Int:**
  - **An act whose grant cannot yet be registered** (W-08). Under §1.13.1-1, the commission is the human act and the grant is what Governance registers pointing at it. The grant cannot be registered without a holder. Within the kernel (C1 = constitute), there is no authority until then. Outside the kernel, C1-a against C1-b is open (§1.13 L134). The source states the block itself (L36). *Agreement.*
  - **"Verified", two readings** (W-06):
    - (a) checked by the existing attribution mechanism (MATCH/MISMATCH; S0315 L109–111). This is evidence, not attestation, and consistent with INV-ATTR (S0316 L23; S0320 L95). *Better supported.*
    - (b) a new attestation mechanism is presupposed.
  - **An ordering gap under (a)** (W-06). MATCH compares against a **registered** label. A label is registered only by REGISTER, and REGISTER waits for the identity to be "resolved" (L36). So, under (a), verification can only follow REGISTER. The commission's order (declare → verify → REGISTER) can be met only if "verified" means checked after REGISTER. *A second ordering gap, parallel to V-8 (C2). Recorded as an open point.*
  - **Bars by label** (W-12). The bars name session labels (L32) whose attribution is in dispute (C1). Governance is barred as `b64828fe` (S0318 L74; S0320 L32), while the current Governance session is `b51dba91`. Whether a bar binds the role or the session is not said. S0319 withholds `d1612e03`'s governed role (L58), and S0320 then bars it "as" the verifier (L32). Used for exclusion only, that is consistent.
- **Not a new concept.**

## 8.69.4 Challenges recorded

- **C1, rival:** two rulings against one decide reading (a).
  - *Answer:* the two come from one registrar line, and one of them defers to S0315's own self-declaration. Neither bears on the provenance of S0315's writing act.

  - *Answer:* that is reading (b). The estate has no such mechanism, and its invariants say identity is not attestable. Reading (a) is the existing check, and under it the order must change.
