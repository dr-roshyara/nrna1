## 8.87.1 In plain language

1. **S0362 is the same text as the last part of S0361, word for word.** It is counted once.
2. **The first adviser of S0363 makes the kernel "the Admission Boundary" and its invariant that no knowledge claim exists without identity, evidence, justification, epistemic state, confidence and history.** In the book a claim's standing comes from a recorded act, and a claim with no recorded evidence is shown as "not attested"; the invariant would forbid what the book displays.
3. **The same adviser reads knowledge as "justified true belief".** The line had kept truth out of the kernel's question; the book's standing does not depend on truth.
4. **The reply finds confidence's place in the kernel unproven, and says the kernel may govern a whole lifecycle (contested, superseded) rather than only admission.** The book already has kinds for later changes: withdrawal of reliance, restoration, annulment.
5. **The reply proposes a test: remove a responsibility; if no invariant breaks, it is not the kernel's.** That is the book's relocation test in a different form.
