## 8.81.1 In plain language

1. **The full record of the work item is now reproduced: nine entries.** The verifier's REGISTER and the HANDOFF to it are recorded as made by "governance", while both refusals state that no process holds a Governance role on this work item.
2. **The CONTINUATION that restarted the work item is entered on the producer's session, not on the verifier's.** The producer's lane was continued and then handed off. Each lane's end, the producer's and now the verifier's, is a STOP that stops the whole item, so each next step needs another CONTINUATION.
3. **Two processes refuse to perform the adoption review**, each on its own bar and both because no Governance lane exists. The bars now extend along the whole chain: the producer may not verify, the verifier may not review for adoption, the correction author may not either.
4. **The PO/ARB said "Begin the governance adoption review"; both refusals say a direction that names a step does not appoint anyone.** One step earlier, the PO/ARB said "register yourself as independent verifier", and the verifier's REGISTER followed.
5. **The verifier's bootstrap, run without a label, still returned MATCH**: on this run the check read the identity from the environment.
