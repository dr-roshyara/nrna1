## 8.77.1 In plain language

1. **The workflow store, the record every lane and human START is written into, is excluded from version control.** The prose registrations about it are kept; the record they register is not. The book had found this inversion before: the record that outranks prose is the one that lasts least.
2. **The live ambiguity that the correction was said to explain truthfully is made of one assignment and one mention.** The resolver takes any hex-shaped token in a lane's free text as a label. One lane is assigned to the process; the other only names it in prose. So the "two matching lanes" were one, and the false ambiguity grows as lanes accumulate prose naming earlier actors. A label written before a full stop is captured with the stop, which could leave an actor unable to resolve its own lane. The failure is safe in direction; its reason is wrong.
3. **A fresh verifier refused for want of a lane, and named a second blocker: the implementation lane was never stopped.** It is the first process in the chain to treat the missing record of an end of work as a reason not to proceed.
4. **A new commission makes "Governance Engineer" and "Communication Engineer" operating responsibilities, not workflow roles.** That adds a fourth sense of "Governance". The process that has been recording as Governance throughout fits none of the named senses. It also delivered an advisory review without a lane, which the refusals' own wording permits.
5. **The new commission consumes the orchestration tool as the canonical mechanism, although that tool is neither verified nor adopted.**
