## 8.85.1 In plain language

1. **S0360 reviews a design document the window does not contain.** What it quotes of that document is its report. It also reports a decision ("OQ-5 just explicitly established …") with no record of the act.
2. **It separates what a person appears to mean (an intent) from an authorized request to change the record (a command), and gives the change itself to the domain, not to a pipeline.** That agrees with the book's separation of a recorded act from what precedes it.
3. **It refuses numeric evidence weights in the kernel and keeps mechanism scores, evidence weights, domain confidence and authority apart.** That agrees with §8.84's reading of the earlier adviser.
4. **It warns that one status list for every layer would be a mistake, and assigns AMBIGUOUS and UNRESOLVED to interpretation, NOT_AUTHORIZED to governance, and admissible or rejected to the domain.** That agrees with the book's finding that the same words were serving several mechanisms.
5. **Its own closing question asks what KnowledgeOS must own "to preserve the truth, authority, …" of a transition.** The earlier advisers, and the book, keep truth out of the kernel's question.
