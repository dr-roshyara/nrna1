## 8.60.1 In plain language

1. **Several earlier sections waited for the plan itself, because they had only its reviews.** Read now, the plan answers most of what they left open: the window between copy and switch now has its own reconciliation step; a conflict of content is never merged automatically, and the post-condition "superset of both inputs" drops the earlier wording "sequence sets", which fits a content reading without deciding it; the mirror case is settled differently (a write on the evidence side before the switch is not reconciled; it fails verification), except in the grants guidance, which stays one-directional; the inventory comes before the durable place, and the plan leaves that order open rather than reorder it; the migration covers every record, not two work items; the final check of the copy compares against the closed comparison object.
2. **One file, four acts.** The original plan, four amendments, a correction and a repair were written by three named processes and one unnamed repair session, which may or may not be one of the three, all inside one file. The plan labels most of its changes and names the producers in its header; one box labelled as an earlier amendment states the ruling of a later one without a label. In the book's terms it is either a sequence of entries or one item over versions, which the book leaves open.
3. **One sentence stands twice on one line, first as a current claim and then, quoted, as superseded.** The repair added the correction after the sentence instead of replacing it. A reader who stops at the first part reads the old claim as current.
4. **The plan says the human act is the authority and a grant would only be its record.** That is the book's second concept, "the record represents authority". The Principal decided the first, "the record constitutes authority", for inside the kernel only; outside it, which concept applies is open. Inside the kernel, an authority with no record is "not recognized". The commissions of AMD3–AMD5 are not registered, and no amendment's delivery is; AMD6's commission is.
5. **Several points agree with the book** and are already in it: storage location is not ownership; derived state is never a second authority; a removal is not a deletion only if a verified copy holds all of it.
