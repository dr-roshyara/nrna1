## 8.66.1 In plain language

1. **Standing is missing from two lists of the "core"**, in S0311 and in S0312. The drift starts one file earlier, in S0310. The book's decided promise puts standing at the core. "Agency" in those lists covers who acted, not whose act counts. S0312 predicts that "kernel compression" will leave a "surprisingly small" kernel, which is a claim about minimality with no requirement set. "The compiler can be replaced. The kernel cannot." is a matter of replaceability: the book's generic class and its stability test, not its delegability test.
2. **S0311 names the degenerate solution of its own convergence test.** A compiler that maps everything to one value converges perfectly. So S0311 adds a second test: different meanings must stay different. That is the book's point about constant answerers. Whether it also excludes a system that always abstains depends on how UNKNOWN is scored, which the source leaves open. The source defines Zero both as a refusal to commit to a parse and as a refusal to commit without evidence. The second reading is the guard the Principal declined. "KIR exists does not mean knowledge exists" agrees with the book.
3. **S0311 reports a toy simulation scored on contradiction cases.** The architecture it used had already placed contradiction handling outside the compiler. The source itself then calls removing contradiction from the compiler benchmark "one correction to our previous thinking". So the 0% on contradiction follows from scoring the compiler on something its own design assigned elsewhere. It cannot confirm the design. The report is graded Src, like the earlier ones.
4. **S0314 records sessions that could not choose their lane.** The resolver answered AMBIGUOUS with four candidates. The book counts such an answer as a success, and the sessions correctly refused to adopt another process's identity. S0314 locates the defect in legibility: who holds a lane was recorded only in free text, and one fact the record computes, whether a session was handed work, could not be read. The correction makes these facts readable and routes the case to a recorded governance act. It does this through one bounded, authorised exception to the rule that all interpretation goes through one interpreter. The pattern resembles the book's trivial system (prohibitions met, no work done). The cause, though, is an observer gap, not a missing must-do.
5. **S0314 calls its central result "measured": the outcome does not depend on the AI model.** Three premises make byte-identical output under two model settings hold by construction. The resolver makes no model call, as the source states. It reads no model-specific setting, which the source does not state. Its output is deterministic, which the source does not state either. The test pins the second and third. It does not compare providers. "Attribution is evidence only, never a grant input" agrees with the book. "Self-verified" beside "the producing process cannot verify its own output" uses "verify" in two senses.
