## 8.66.1 In plain language

1. **Two lists of the "core" leave out authority:** S0311's "KnowledgeOS Core Boundary" and S0312's "Constitutional Core". The extraction two files earlier had it. The book's decided promise puts standing at the core.
   - S0312 also predicts that "kernel compression" will leave a kernel that is "surprisingly small". That is a claim about minimality with no requirement set.
   - Its test for the compiler, "the compiler can be replaced; the kernel cannot", is the book's delegability question in other words.
2. **S0311 names the trivial system itself.** A compiler that maps everything to UNKNOWN or to SAME would "converge" perfectly. So it adds a test that different meanings must stay different. This is the book's point that a system doing nothing meets every prohibition.
   - It defines Zero as "refusal to introduce epistemic commitment without sufficient semantic grounds". For a parse, this agrees with the book. For standing, it would make evidence a condition, which the Principal declined.
   - "KIR exists does not mean knowledge exists" agrees with the book's separation of a record from an act.
3. **S0311 reports a toy simulation built "using the architecture we just agreed on".** Its 0% on contradictions is read as "good architectural evidence" that contradiction belongs to another layer. But the design had already put contradiction in that other layer. A failure in exactly that place is what the design guarantees, so it cannot confirm the design. The report is also graded Src, like the earlier runs.
4. **S0314 records that two governed sessions were stranded.** The resolver returned AMBIGUOUS, and both sessions correctly refused to adopt another process's identity. That is the book's trivial case in real operation: every prohibition met, nothing lawful to do. The correction adds lawful routes and weakens no rule, as the book's floor of must-dos asks.
   - It also notes a fact the record computes but no reader can obtain: whether a session was handed work.
5. **S0314 calls its central result "measured": the outcome does not depend on the AI model.** But the resolver makes no model call, so identical output under two model settings holds by construction. The test pins that property; it does not compare the models.
   - "Attribution is evidence only, never a grant input" agrees with the book.
   - "Self-verified" beside "the producing process cannot verify its own output" uses "verify" in two senses.
