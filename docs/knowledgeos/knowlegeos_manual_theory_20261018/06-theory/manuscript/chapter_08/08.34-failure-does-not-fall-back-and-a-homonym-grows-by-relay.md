## 8.34.1 In plain language

1. **The estate says a failed change leaves the last trusted state in force, and treats an "uncertain" state as the wrong outcome.** The book differs in two places. A bad entry on a merged record makes every answer on that record a refusal, earlier points included. And inside one kernel, an accepted act at the same time as another can turn a "yes" into "undetermined". The book keeps that on purpose.
2. **A word with two meanings gets stronger each time it is relayed.** EKS's "lifecycle record ≠ grant" became "evidence ≠ authority", then "means of knowing ≠ valid knowledge", and each step is cited as more support. None adds evidence.
3. **"Meaning is not valid knowledge"** agrees with the book's two separate values. The same chat, though, allows "assessment → authority" in its pipeline while listing "assessment ≠ authority" as an invariant. The two fit only if that step is a person's act.
4. **"Canonical knowledge is always historical"** is what the book's Q1 already does: it answers at a recorded point.
5. **"Make forbidden transformations impossible"** fits two of the book's classes: guards, and things that are not inputs at all. The book detects other violations instead. The kernel derives its answers from the record; a stored answer has no standing.
6. **The estate's motto, "prevents unsupported knowledge from becoming trusted knowledge"**, is the promise the Principal did not choose. Under the decided promise, a recognised item with no evidence is shown as recognised and "not attested", without a guarantee.
7. **The chat declares collection complete twice and keeps adding lenses.** It also numbers new candidates "INV-KOS-004…007" while saying "no INV-005…N added": two numbering series.

## 8.34.2 Claims and derivations

**C1. The book does not keep "the last trusted state": a breach refuses, and a tie can turn T into U inside one kernel (Src; Ded against §9.13; Int).** *(Restated at review: W-01.)*
- **S0212:** "Failure pauses time, not the world": "Trusted Snapshot T1 remains canonical" after a failed synthesis. The "wrong model" is "corrupt / rollback / **uncertain** state" (L106–132). The candidate INV-KOS-005: "Failed transformations should preserve the last trusted knowledge state and should NOT create partial authoritative states" (L441–447). Its "synthesis" is assessment in its own mapping (L151).
- **Ded, against the book, by reading of "failure":**
  - **(a) A content-level failure** (a synthesis that fails): the kernel has no value for content (§9.10 L35). Nothing to preserve or refuse.
  - **(b) A breach of D within one kernel:** refused at append; the record and its answers are unchanged (§9.13.2; PL-2). **Agreement.**
  - **(c) A breach of D on a merged record or a bypass:** the whole record leaves D. In the book's current wording, a record in D determines the answer at each of its cuts (§9.13.2 L62). Membership is checked on the whole record, also for an earlier cut (§9.13 v9). **So Q1 refuses every cut; the book does not fall back to T1.** Its reason: refusing is fail-closed; dropping a failing entry could turn the acts it targets to T (§9.13.2).
  - **(d) An unauthorised act:** PL-2 has no authorisation guard for adoption, revocation or annulment. The act is **appended** and evaluates F. **The record advances, the values stay**: agreement in values, not in the record.
  - **(e) A tie, inside one kernel:** an adoption y is T at cut s1. A revocation of the same item is appended with the same act time; no guard refuses ties. "Ordered before" is then U (§9.13.2, decided by the Principal: "acts at the same time are unordered; the answer is U"), so Q1 at s2 is **U**. S0212 names exactly this outcome, an "uncertain state", as the wrong model. **The book keeps the U on purpose** (CON-0221; PL-6). *Worked from the text by the reviewer, not run in the model.*
- **Int:** S0212's model agrees with the book only for (b), and in values for (d). It differs at (c) and (e), and in both cases by the book's design (fail-closed refusal; unresolved never laundered). *The draft's "only on merged or bypass records" is withdrawn.*
- **Not a new concept.**
