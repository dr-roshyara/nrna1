## 8.32.1 In plain language

1. **EKS says it cannot answer two things:** whether an actor holds a grant, and whether an act falls under a grant's scope. Those are exactly the two things the book's evaluator takes from outside as inputs. EKS gives "unknown"; the book gives an answer only once the inputs are supplied.
2. **When EKS separates "evidence" from "authority", its "evidence" is the log of acts.** In the book, acts and grants are both authority records. So that separation is not evidence for the book's separation of evidence from standing.
3. **EKS's guards keep its own record well-formed and prevent nothing in the world.** The book draws the same line: its guards prevent a second recorded use; a second actual use is prevented only if work waits for the record.
4. **The growth from 216 to 218 transitions is plausible, but nothing records it.** §8.31's "one record at later cuts" is too strong.
5. **The estate's mathematics proposals contradict the book in two places:** evidence driving the state, and "small changes give small effects". The one structure the book actually uses, an order of information over three values, is not the "lattice" proposed.
6. **"Expression is not meaning"** agrees with the book. But "semantic identity" is never defined, and in the book's design no area owns it.

## 8.32.2 Claims and derivations

**C1. The two facts EKS cannot answer are CE*'s two inputs (Src; Ded against §9.13; Int).**
- **S0206:** of six AuthorizationFacts, two are "permanently UNKNOWN":
  - "is the grant holder — UNKNOWN — grants carry no session/role linkage (D-2)";
  - "the intended act is covered by grant scope — UNKNOWN — scope exists only as a string, compared by equality" (S0206 L897–898).

  Its conclusion: "EKS cannot answer 'is this actor authorized to do this thing?' — and it says so, in code, deliberately, rather than guessing" (L900). And: "A Grant is evidence that permission was given. It is not a permission the system can evaluate" (L1244).
- **The book:** CE* takes exactly these two as **inputs**: "covers is a given relation between a grant's (activities, scope) and an act's (activity, scope)", and "the actor–holder link: an act's actor matches a grant's holder iff they are the same identity" (§9.13.2, Inputs). An undefined covers pair gives U with its reason (§9.13 R2 (ii)–(iii)). Their correctness is K3 (§9.15).
- **Ded:**
  - Given the inputs, a grant **is** evaluable in the book (G1, G2). Without them, the book gives U only for undefined covers pairs.
  - Its actor–holder link is identity equality, defined on D because D requires the holder field (§9.13.2). EKS's grants have **no holder field at all** (S0206 L397: six fields, none a holder), so they are not in D: Q1 **refuses**, rather than answering U.
- **Int:** the estate and the book agree on **where** evaluation stops. The estate reports it as UNKNOWN in its resolver; the book makes it a stated input and a refusal outside D. *Within the lineage:* the book's defects 5 and 7 (§1.14.1), which made covers and the actor–holder link inputs, were drawn from this estate.
- **A count inside S0206:**
  - "Three of the six are permanently UNKNOWN by construction" (L889; S-1 L1436, "three of six");
  - "2 of 6 AuthorizationFacts permanently UNKNOWN" (U-9 L1381; diagram L1497: "4 answerable . 2 permanently UNKNOWN").

  The table at L891–898 lists **two**. S0201 relays "three" (S0201 L292). *The table governs; "three" is a defect relayed once.*
- **Not a new concept.**

**C2. "Evidence ≠ authority" in EKS is the log of acts against the grants: a homonym (Src; Int).**
- **S0206:** "Two never-merged records in one consistency boundary — `transitions` (evidence) ≠ `grants` (authority)" (L1150; P-4 L1359). Its own vocabulary lists five senses of "Evidence", the fourth being "the transition log ('historical evidence')" (L342).
- **S0201** relays this as "a structural separation of evidence from authority", and cites it among the evidence for CONFIDENCE ≠ AUTHORITY (S0201 L157, L170).
- **Int:**
  - In the book, the **transitions** (REGISTER, HANDOFF, START, COMPLETE …) are recorded **acts**, and grants are recorded acts too. Both belong to standing's record (D's kinds, §9.13.2).
  - The book's "evidence" is something else: entries that support or refute an item's content and change no authority (§9.10 O2; §9.18).
  - **So the EKS separation is between two kinds of authority record, not between evidence and authority.** The part of S0201's support for "authority is not confidence" that rests on it rests on a homonym.
  - *This narrows §8.31 C1:* what the estate shows directly is "no assessment confers authority" (AP-1, unemittable verdicts; S0206 L1007). The two-record separation is not that.
- **Not a new concept.** Recorded for T10-18 (homonym evidence).

**C3. The record refuses; the world is assumed (Src; agreement with §9.16 R3a; Int).**
- **S0206:** "EKS enforces the well-formedness of its own record. It enforces nothing about engineering" (L879). "An illegal write is a REFUSED transition (exit 65, nothing appended) — the fold refuses; nothing is physically prevented" (L65). Authority is "*evidentiary*, not *operative*" (L1582).
- Its strengths share one shape: "every one of them is a place where EKS made a guarantee *structural* instead of *stated* … a rule was made impossible to break rather than important to follow" (L1453).
- **The book draws the same line:**
  - the guards prevent a **recorded** second use; an **actual** second use is prevented only under L9, record before use (§9.15 G4; §9.16 R3a; §10.5 PL-5);
  - what the record cannot see is a legitimacy assumption, not part of a value (§9.15 R3).
- **Int:** "structural instead of stated" is, in the book's terms, a **guard** (prevent) against a **mark** or an assumption (detect, or assume). The book decided that per law (§9.16 R3a). S0206's observation is that the estate's strengths are all guards and its weaknesses are all assumptions. *One estate; an observation, not a law.*
- **Not a new concept.**

**C5. Mathematics after the invariants: two of S0205's proposals contradict the book (Src; Ded against Ch. 9; Int).**
- **S0205's order:** "Existing systems → Discover invariants → Define knowledge concepts → Find mathematical representations → Choose useful formal models", "Not: Choose mathematical model → Force knowledge into it" (S0205 L7–25). It also says "Do not choose a mathematical model yet" (L433). That is §8.30 C5.
- **Against the book:**
  - **(a) Evidence driving state.** S0205's kernel candidate is S_{t+1} = T(S_t, E_t), with E_t "accepted evidence/event", and the lifecycle "Draft → (evidence submitted) → Reviewed → (governance approval) → Authoritative". It claims this "already matches observed behavior" (L41–80).
    - In the book, standing changes only by recorded **acts**, and an evidence entry "changes no authority" (§9.10 O2; §9.18 (c1)–(c2)).
    - The measured EKS has no such lifecycle: its work items have no terminal state (S0206 L457–468).
    - *The claim of "observed behavior" is unmeasured* (W step 1, S0205 item 2).
  - **(b) Continuity.** "A small evidence update should not unexpectedly cause a huge semantic jump" (L496). In the book, **CE* is not monotone in the record**: one appended annulment can change many answers (§9.16 R1, L80). The book does not prevent the jump. It **records** it and names its reason. *The defensible core of S0205 is its own line 658, "state evolution must preserve lineage."*
- **What the book does use:**
  - CE*'s values are ordered by **information** (U below T and F); the answer is the least fixed point (KK) of a monotone operator (§9.13 R2). That is a lattice-theoretic structure **over authority values**, not S0205's "maturity" order, whose diagram has no common lower bound (W step 1, S0205 item 3).
  - S0205's sheaf idea (local meanings combined only where consistent; L784–846) has its nearest home in §9.14: global consistency from pairwise checks when the effect class has Helly number at most 2. *An analogy, not used.*
- **Not a new concept.** *Mathematics as a reason for a concept: no (§8.30 C5).*

**C6. Expression ≠ meaning: it agrees with the book, but "semantic identity" is undefined and unowned (Src; Int).**
- **S0207:** "Expression ≠ Meaning is a supported hypothesis … a representation-layer concern, not a kernel dimension. The kernel should preserve semantic identity invariants while allowing expression to vary" (S0207 L15). Its reply proposes "INV-KOS-002 (candidate) Semantic Continuity Across Representation Transformation: KnowledgeOS SHALL preserve semantic identity when knowledge changes expression, representation, or medium" (L552–558).
- **Agreement:** with §1.5 (knowledge is not its carrier), §3.4, §9.17 R1 (the record is not its carrier) and §10.5.0 (meaning, not interface).
- **What the book's design gives it:**
  - The record keeps the identity of **entries**, content-derived (§9.16 R2).
  - An **item** k is "a governed identifier", taken as input (§9.10 L33).
  - Whether two expressions are the same item is the **catalogue's** question, and the catalogue is not admitted (T10-10 (1); gap G10).
  - **So INV-KOS-002 has no owner in the book's design**, and no test exists that an expression change preserves meaning.
- **Undecidable as stated (W step 1, S0207 item 15):** "semantic identity" is not defined: no identity criterion, no equivalence relation, no decision procedure.
- **Absence claim:** "Counter-Evidence: None found" (L211) states no search (CON-0120).
- **Homonym:** S0207's "semantic invariance principle" is a statistical-learning invariance, prediction stable across data environments (L79–93), not an invariant of a record.
- **Not a new concept.** Carried to T10-19 (the inventory of areas' rules) and gap G10.
