## 8.95.1 In plain language

1. **The summary records that the six-part aggregate was falsified as a default and that the smaller boundary proposed after it is unproven.** Within the book's record both hold, with the scope the last window gave: what is shown is worked out and needs no joint update; the boundaries the book needs join a check to the entry it guards.
2. **It separates who asserts, who sets the rules, and who keeps the record, and says responsibility is not the same as owning an aggregate.** Within the record that agrees with the book, and so does its closing principle: protect the integrity of admitted knowledge without deciding what is true.
3. **It says identity is given by the domain, never changes, and is "opaque" and not derived from meaning.** The book has two identities here: for an entry, identity is worked out from its content; for an item, it is a governed identifier. The summary's description fits the second, not the first.
4. **It leaves open whether evidence is shared between claims or copied into each, and what the admission contract is.** Within the record the book records each producer's claim of support or refutation per item; the evidence itself stays outside. The book's answer to the admission contract is its own record rules and checks.
5. **It says state is worked out from events, relations, assessments and decisions, offers a new list of states, demotes confidence and leaves event sourcing open.** Within the record the first agrees; the list is a sixth vocabulary in the conversation; confidence has no counterpart in the book.
6. **It lists what each lens contributed and keeps a "Zero" list of things not to confuse.** Within the record most of the Zero pairs are kept apart by the book; not assessed against low confidence has no counterpart.
