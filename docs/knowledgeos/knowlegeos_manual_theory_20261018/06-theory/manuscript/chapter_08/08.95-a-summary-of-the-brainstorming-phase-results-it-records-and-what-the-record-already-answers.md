## 8.95.1 In plain language

1. **The summary records that the six-part aggregate was falsified as a default and that the smaller boundary proposed after it is unproven.** Within the book's record both hold, with the scope the last window gave: what is shown is worked out and needs no joint update; the boundaries the book needs join a check to the entry it guards.
2. **It separates who asserts, who sets the rules, and who keeps the record, and says responsibility is not the same as owning an aggregate.** Within the record that agrees with the book, and so does its closing principle: protect the integrity of admitted knowledge without deciding what is true.
3. **It says identity is given by the domain, never changes, and is "opaque" and not derived from meaning.** Within the record the book has two identities: for an entry, identity is worked out from the entry's fields, so it is not opaque; for an item, it is a governed identifier, which the summary's description fits. Its later phrase that the kernel gives identity "only for admitted domain objects" conflicts with the book, where an item carries its identifier before adoption. *(Restated at review: W-02, W-03)*
4. **It leaves open whether evidence is shared between claims or copied into each, and what the admission contract is.** Within the record the book records each producer's claim of support or refutation per item; the evidence itself stays outside. The summary's contract admits a claim to knowledge after a test of sufficiency; the book separates accepting an entry (its record rules and checks) from giving standing (an authorized act), and has no test of sufficiency. *(Restated at review: W-01)*
5. **It says state is worked out from events, relations, assessments and decisions, offers a new list of states, demotes confidence and leaves event sourcing open.** Within the record the first agrees for events and decisions; relations between claims and stored assessments have no counterpart; the list is a further vocabulary in the conversation; confidence has no counterpart in the book. *(Restated at review: W-08, W-09)*
6. **It lists what each lens contributed and keeps a "Zero" list of things not to confuse.** Within the record most of the Zero pairs are kept apart by the book; not assessed against low confidence has no counterpart, and "withdrawn" agrees only for withdrawing reliance on evidence, since a claim's withdrawal has no kind. *(Restated at review: W-10, W-11)*
