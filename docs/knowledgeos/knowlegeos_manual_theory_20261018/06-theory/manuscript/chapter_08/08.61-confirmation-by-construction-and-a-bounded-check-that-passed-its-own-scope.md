## 8.61.1 In plain language

1. **The intake assessment finds that all 24 research-bearing files "confirm" what was already admitted, and none adds anything.** But the rule it applies says that under the closure a positive answer "routes to an existing article (confirmation), never a new row". With that rule no other result was possible, so the count of 24 confirmations is a consequence of the rule, not a finding about the files.
2. **The record says the HPA's commission "is that act" which the closure requires for further material, and in the same breath that it is "not a new-source admission".** An encyclopedia article and a translator's book inside the files are called "evidence behind already-admitted families". That is the family reading of "source" that §8.55 found, now applied to whole outside texts.
3. **The commission asked for "a timestamp of the saved time"; the record uses each file's modification time and says all 27 files were produced after the corpus sort of 21 August and after the closure.** One file is stamped 19 August. Either the modification time is not the time a file was produced, or the record's statement is too broad.
4. **The record describes the chat export of §8.57 as the "draft" of the grammar records, and the research extraction of §8.58 as their "evidence base".** That fits the reading that the records were made from the assistant's answers, adopted or commissioned, not written by the HPA.
5. **The Governance re-verification finds the repair complete and the chain "presentable" to the product owner, within its declared scope: completeness, provenance, lineage, and current/superseded document integrity.** In that last part it passes the line where one sentence stands both as a current claim and as superseded (§8.60 C3). It re-measures the disputed count a third time and confirms the repair's figure, and it treats a fresh technical review of the repair as the product owner's option, not as a required step.

## 8.61.2 Claims and derivations

**C1. Confirmation by construction (Src; Ded; Int).**
- **S0301:** "The frozen classifier (Source → Lens extraction → relevance test → Kernel / Mechanism / Representation / Reject) governs the answer; under closure a 'Yes' routes to an **existing article (confirmation)**, never a new row." (L11); "⛔ **No new register rows** — the frozen classifier + research closure: a 'Yes' routes to an existing article, never a new row. All 24 lens-bearing files are confirmation." (L63); "**Result: zero new candidates · zero new rows · zero kernel changes.**" (L51); "The batch is not new convergence but **confirmation of the existing one** … re-corroborated by these files" (L81).
- **Ded:** under the stated rule, every file's outcome is "confirms" or "reject"; "a new row" is excluded before any file is read. So "zero new rows" follows from the rule, whatever the files contain; it cannot be evidence that the files add nothing. *A result that holds whatever the input shows nothing about the input* (§8.42 C1).
- **Int:** "re-corroborated" (L81) counts confirmations produced by the rule as support: within one lineage (§8.31 C4), and by construction. The record also says "No file proposes a dimension, law, invariant, or meta-principle absent from the admitted material" (L13): a claim about the files, which the routing table is offered to support; the table's column is the rule's output. *The claim is the recorder's; the evidence it cites is not independent of the rule.*
- **Two counts not disjoint (from W's inventory):** #25 is counted among the "2 outside-candidate" files (L97) while its part (b) "confirms **Z-KOS-001**" (L47). *Recorded; the tally 24 + 1 + 2 counts files, not parts.*
- **Not a new concept.**

- **Int:**
