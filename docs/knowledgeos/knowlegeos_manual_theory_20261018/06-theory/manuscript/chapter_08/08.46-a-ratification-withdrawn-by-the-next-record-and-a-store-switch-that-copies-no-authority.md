## 8.46.1 In plain language

1. **One record says the architect's act has "frozen" the character statement; the record a quarter of an hour later calls that "the earlier reading", superseded, and holds the statement as only proposed.** The second record also calls the Constitution "ratified" where the first called it still proposed. Either the architect acted twice, or, as the second record itself says, what the first called an act was the recorder's reading. The records do not show which. It is the clearest case so far of prose that a later record treats as no act at all.
2. **A separate review checks a plan to move the governance record to a new store.** Its rules match the book's: copying records into a new store gives them no authority; authority moves only at the switch, and the copy is checked all-or-nothing before it. A passing check is evidence, never a release.
3. **Zero is given a formal code, Z-KOS-001, and a "SHALL" statement, but it is neither a kernel law nor an invariant.** That is a binding sentence that belongs to no category the line has, opened as a new namespace after the line said no new concepts could be added. The Quranic lens then calls Zero "the stable truth", the reading the Zero ruling had just forbidden.
4. **The Quranic lens asks first "who says it?"; the Zero lens said "do not ask who said it".** The book needs both, on different axes: who acted, for standing; what evidence and of what kind, for the class shown.
5. **Several points agree with the book:** "authority is not truth"; "reasoning plus evidence plus validation gives a candidate", not knowledge; "a rule that everything must follow Zero would make Zero an authority". Others restate the pattern of recent windows: a prevention-only description of KnowledgeOS, and a list of "dimensions" that includes events.
