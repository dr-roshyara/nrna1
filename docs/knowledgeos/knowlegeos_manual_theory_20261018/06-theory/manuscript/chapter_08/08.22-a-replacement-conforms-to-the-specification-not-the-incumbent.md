## 8.22.1 In plain language

Most of this window restates what the book already holds. Three points add something.
1. **Replacing an incumbent.** A replacement engine must be checked against the specification, not against the old engine's output. Matching the incumbent only shows that the two agree, and they can share a defect. A second chain of the corpus thus reaches the book's rule that a reference must come from somewhere other than what it checks.
2. **A checker is a reader.** It must follow the designated record, not define where the record is.
3. **An index position is not a source.** One window holds a byte-identical copy of a text read in the window before, so counting positions overstates the support.

### S0152: integrating deterministic assurance with the durability track

*S0152 decides nothing and says so: "This document decides nothing, commissions nothing, mints no identifier, adopts no model, and authorizes no implementation." (S0152 L19, in meaning). Its proposals are an adviser's.*

**C2. The checker's resolution follows the record; it does not define it (Src; Int for §9.17 R2).** *(No new concept: evidence.)*
- "Assurance root resolution must FOLLOW the governed evidence boundary; it must not DEFINE or OWN that boundary." [S0152 L146]. The checker is "an adapter over a resolved root, never the resolver of where evidence authoritatively lives" (S0152 L150, in meaning).
- **Int (CON-0261, K2-r).** A checker is a reader. Under K2-r, what it reads must be a cut of the designated record, so its resolution must follow the designation. A checker that defined its own root would be a reader with its own default, which is §8.18 C1's case (CON-0239). *Agreement within the estate's lineage.*

**C3. A blocking checker is a governance object (Src; evidence for §1.14.5-1).** *(No new concept: evidence.)*
- The checker stays warn-only, because "a blocking checker **is** a governance object and therefore requires authority" [S0152 L131]. This is §1.14.5-1, quoted: "Gates follow ratification; they never precede it".
- The checker's limit, as S0152 states it: "the checker reduces *dangling-reference* defects to zero and reduces *missing-act* defects by nothing" [S0152 L138]. Each report must state what it did not check (S0152 L133, in meaning).
- Both are §8.20 C12 and C13. They come from the same Track-2 chain, which cites the same reviewer's sentence (S0152 L132, in meaning; §8.20 C12 quotes S0143 L83). **One line, not two.**

**C4. A back-test of known findings falsifies reach, not detection (Src; attack on the source; Ded from CON-0253).**
- S0152 calls the planned back-test "a falsification experiment, not a theoretical exercise" (S0152 L95, in meaning). The checker must rediscover 17 known findings and stay quiet on the repaired artifact (S0152 L97–107, in meaning).
- *Attack.* By CON-0253 (VALIDATED), rediscovering known findings shows that a checker reaches them, not that it detects defects unknown to its author.
- *Ded.* The back-test can falsify **reach** (a known finding missed) and **specificity** (a false alarm on the repaired artifact). It cannot falsify a claim to detect what nobody knew. So "falsification experiment" is right for those two properties only. The estate's later report met the same limit (§8.20 C12, C15).

### S0154: one metric engine, language bindings, and a recorded direction

**C7. A replacement conforms to the specification, not to the incumbent (Src; Ded; a second chain for CON-0257).** *(No new concept: evidence.)*
- The adviser's design is "language-specific understanding, language-neutral calculation" (S0154 L117, in meaning). The old PHP calculator is kept as "a reference/conformance implementation" until a new engine shows equivalence (S0154 L470, in meaning).
- The recording session then reads the retirement condition against the act:
  - equivalence is verified against the declared conformance specification, not against the PHP calculator's output (S0154 L584, in meaning);
  - so "a future engine that merely reproduces the PHP calculator's results would not satisfy this condition" [S0154 L584];
  - "It has to conform to the specification, not imitate the incumbent." [S0154 L584].
- *Ded.*
  - Agreement with the incumbent shows only that the two agree.
  - If both share a defect, a check against the incumbent passes, and a check against the specification fails.
  - So the incumbent's output is a reference that comes from the kind of thing being checked: an implementation of the same metric.
  - This is CON-0257's condition, that the reference carries information originating independently of what is checked, met by the specification. It sits inside the carrier-change gate as its independence clause (§9.17 R4, CON-0265).
- *Lineage.* This is the LCOM4 chain, a different estate chain from the durability migration where CON-0257 was found (S0146). It is a second instance, but independence of authorship is not shown: both chains are the Principal's sessions.

**C8. The act's words, not the adviser's: the recorder kept them apart (Src; evidence for the C23 rule).** *(No new concept.)*
- The adviser's text said "Python" would be the planned authoritative engine (S0154 L533, in meaning). The act, as the recording session reports it, names no language, only "one authoritative implementation" (S0154 L578, in meaning). The session recorded the difference and stated that the language must not be read into the direction (S0154 L580, in meaning). This is the C23 rule applied in the corpus: the authority's words come first, and a gloss is labelled with its author.
- "A direction on the record is not authority to act" [S0154 L574]. It was registered as a document only, with "no grant, no assignment, no transition" (S0154 L574, in meaning). This is §8.17 C8: the record never manufactures authority, and recording is not authorizing.
- *Lineage.* The act is reported by the recorder; its text is not quoted in the source.

### S0158: events in the knowledge loop

*S0158 is an adviser's reading of an outside article on event-driven DDD. The article is not in the corpus. Its claims are the adviser's attributions, and the source's statement that "the article supports each of these concepts directly" (S0158 L664, in meaning) cannot be checked here.*

**C9. An event is not the record (Src; Int for §9.17 R1).** *(No new concept: evidence.)*
- "This means the event itself is **not the durable truth**." [S0158 L458]. The durable evidence and state are; the event is the fact that lets other contexts react (S0158 L460–462, in meaning).
- "That distinction protects us from accidentally turning the event bus into the KnowledgeOS database." [S0158 L464].
- **Int.** An event stream about the record is a projection of it: "a projection, never a source" (§8.17, from S0128). What carries the record is fixed by designation, not by which channel holds copies of its facts (§9.17 R1).

**C10. "Cannot be determined mechanically" is not "wrong" (Src; evidence for §9.13 and §8.20 C13).** *(No new concept.)*
- A semantic finding means "The system cannot determine the answer mechanically and requires an authority-bearing human judgment." [S0158 L151], and not that the architecture is wrong (S0158 L145–147, in meaning).
- Assurance reports "Mechanical invariants satisfied", never "Architecture is correct" (S0158 L217–231, in meaning).
- **Int.** This is the book's U, which is not F (§9.13: every U names its reason), together with §8.20 C13's scoped PASS.

**C11. Outbox and idempotent consumers (Src; evidence for §1.16.1 and §9.16 R2).** *(No new concept.)*
- A committed approval must not leave its event lost; the state and an outbox entry are committed together (S0158 L369–408, in meaning). This is §1.16.1's single atomic change: an act and the transitions it causes in one step.
- A consumer recognises a redelivered event by its identity and does not process it twice (S0158 L472–506, in meaning). This is content-derived identity (§9.16 R2) applied to consumers.
