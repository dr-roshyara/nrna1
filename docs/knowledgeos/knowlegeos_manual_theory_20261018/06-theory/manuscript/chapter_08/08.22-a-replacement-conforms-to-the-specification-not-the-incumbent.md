## 8.22.1 In plain language

Most of this window restates what the book already holds. Three points add something.

1. **Matching an incumbent is not evidence of conformance.** A replacement engine that reproduces the old engine's results only shows that the two agree, and they can share a defect. What decides conformance is a reference that **is** the intended result, a specification complete enough to fix every choice. The old engine's output does not come from the new engine, so it meets the book's independence condition (CON-0257). But that condition is not enough, and the missing part is the one the book dropped when it restated CON-0256.
2. **A checker is a reader.** It must follow the designated record, now traceable through designation entries from the root designation, and not set its own root.
3. **An index position is not a source.** One window holds a byte-identical copy of a text read in the window before.

### S0152: integrating deterministic assurance with the durability track

*S0152 decides nothing and says so (S0152 L19, in meaning). Its proposals are an adviser's.*

**C2. A checker's resolution follows the designated record (Src; Int for §9.17 R1–R2).** *(No new concept: evidence.)*
- "Assurance root resolution must FOLLOW the governed evidence boundary; it must not DEFINE or OWN that boundary." [S0152 L146].
- *A homonym, qualified.* S0152's "root resolution" is a lint scope: a `--root` parameter that may also read earlier commits of a plan (S0152 L100–102, L151, in meaning). §9.17's "resolution" is a location plus a predicate for reading the record. They agree only where the checker reads the record.
- **Int (CON-0261, K2-r; CON-0239).** For reads of the record, a checker is a reader, so its value must be a cut of the designated record. A `--root` parameter is CON-0239's hazard, a reader's own default or parameter, and nothing in S0152 enforces "follow".
- **The enforcing operation now exists:** resolve by following designation entries from the root designation, because each change of carrier is an entry of D (§9.13.2, kind designate; §9.17 R3 (c)).
- *Agreement within the estate's lineage.*

**C3. A blocking checker is a governance object (Src; evidence for §1.14.5-1).** *(No new concept: evidence.)*
- The checker stays warn-only, because "a blocking checker **is** a governance object and therefore requires authority" [S0152 L131]. This is §1.14.5-1, quoted: "Gates follow ratification; they never precede it".
- *Scoped.* S0152 says the checker "reduces *dangling-reference* defects to zero and reduces *missing-act* defects by nothing" [S0152 L138]. Both quantifiers overreach, so they are scoped here:
  - **"by nothing"** holds only for acts referenced nowhere. §8.20 C12 (W-08) records that a missing act cited somewhere was found (RD-7·a, as DI-5).
  - **"to zero"** is a design claim for a checker that had not yet run.
- These are the same Track-2 chain as §8.20 C12 and C13, which cites the same reviewer's sentence (S0152 L132, in meaning). **One line, not two.**

**C4. A back-test of known findings falsifies reach on those findings (Src; attack on the source; Ded from CON-0253).**
- S0152 calls the planned back-test "a falsification experiment, not a theoretical exercise" (S0152 L95, in meaning). The checker must rediscover 17 known findings and stay quiet on the AMD6 artifact (S0152 L97–107, in meaning).
- **What it can falsify.** By CON-0253 (VALIDATED), rediscovering known findings shows reach, not detection of the unknown. So the back-test falsifies **reach on the listed findings**, and nothing about defects unknown to the checker's author.
- **The quiet row falsifies nothing.** S0152 itself calls the AMD6 chain unreviewed (S0152 L164, in meaning), so an alarm there cannot be classed false. §8.20 C12 (W-09) records that the quiet row was later silent on DV-7, a defect in the checker's own class. A quiet row is evidence only within the implemented scope and against a validated key (CON-0253).
- **The exit criterion is ill-posed as written.**
  - The 17 include "RD-*" (S0152 L102, in meaning).
  - G-2 says RD-1, RD-7 and RD-3·b, the missing-act class, are caught only by human review (S0152 L132, in meaning).
  - For missing acts referenced nowhere, reach is zero by construction. So the criterion is unsatisfiable unless the 17 exclude them, and S0152 does not say which they are.
- **The experiment was later run narrowed without a record** (§8.20 C12: 5 classes, not 17).

### S0154: one metric engine, language bindings, and a recorded direction

**C7. Matching the incumbent is not evidence of conformance; the reference must be the intended result (Src; Ded; home: CON-0256 (i) and §9.17 R4's exactness).** *(No new concept: evidence for a clause.)*
- *Change over time, in order:*
  - The adviser's design is "language-specific understanding, language-neutral calculation" (S0154 L117, in meaning). The old PHP calculator is to stay as "a reference/conformance implementation" (S0154 L470, in meaning).
  - The adviser's own verification plan is a **differential test**: "PHP LCOM4 ≟ Python LCOM4", using the node set, edge set and metric evidence rule (S0154 L497–505, in meaning).
  - The adviser's draft record says equivalence is "against the accepted conformance evidence model" (S0154 L541–543, in meaning).
  - **The recorder** then reads the retirement condition as: equivalence is verified against the declared conformance specification, not against the PHP calculator's output, so "a future engine that merely reproduces the PHP calculator's results would not satisfy this condition" [S0154 L584].
  - *Label (the C23 rule):* this last step is **the recorder's reading** of an act the source does not quote. It is not the act's text.
- *Ded.*
  - Agreement with the incumbent shows only that the two agree. If both share a defect, a check against the incumbent passes, while a check against a reference that **is** the intended result fails.
  - So **matching the incumbent is not evidence of conformance**.
  - The recorder's "would not satisfy" overstates this. An engine that reproduces a **conforming** incumbent does conform. What fails is the evidence, not necessarily the engine.
- **Where it belongs.**
  - The incumbent's output does not come from the new engine, and the replacement cannot rewrite it. So it **meets** CON-0257 as stated. CON-0257 is therefore necessary, not sufficient.
  - The deciding condition is that the reference is exactly the intended state. That is CON-0256 (i), dropped in its restatement as CON-0257, and the "exact" premise of §9.17 R4.
  - Replacing an engine is not a carrier change, so CON-0265 is not its home.
- **Boundary.** The deduction holds for a specification that is complete and decidable, that is, one that fixes which methods and edges the metric counts. Where it leaves a choice open, the incumbent is in practice the specification for that choice. **Fork (open):** may the incumbent serve as the specification for choices the specification leaves open (§8.22.3)?

**C8. The act's language, not the adviser's: the recorder kept them apart (Src; evidence for the C23 rule).** *(No new concept.)*
- The adviser's text said "Python" would be the planned authoritative engine (S0154 L533, in meaning). The act, as the recorder reports it, names no language, only "one authoritative implementation" (S0154 L578, in meaning). The recorder states that the language must not be read into the direction (S0154 L580, in meaning).
- *Scope:* "kept apart" holds for the language. On the conformance test the recorder's reading (C7) goes further than the adviser's texts, and the act is not quoted. So which of the two the act carries is not shown.
- "A direction on the record is not authority to act" [S0154 L574]. It was registered as a document only (S0154 L574, in meaning). This is §8.17 C8.

### S0158: events in the knowledge loop

*S0158 is an adviser's reading of an outside article that is not in the corpus. Its statement that "the article supports each of these concepts directly" (S0158 L664, in meaning) cannot be checked here.*

**C9. An event stream not designated as the carrier is a projection (Src; Int for §9.17 R1).** *(No new concept: evidence.)*
- "This means the event itself is **not the durable truth**." [S0158 L458]. "That distinction protects us from accidentally turning the event bus into the KnowledgeOS database." [S0158 L464].
- **Scoped.** S0158 itself lists event sourcing as "Selective" (S0158 L662, in meaning), and under event sourcing the log **is** the record.
- **Int.** By §9.17 R1, which store carries the record is set by designation, not by the kind of store. An event stream that is not the designated carrier is a projection of the record: "a projection, never a source" (§8.17, from S0128).

**C10. "Cannot be determined mechanically" is not "wrong", for the unknowns the checker knows it has (Src; evidence for §9.13; a contradiction recorded).** *(No new concept.)*
- A semantic finding means "The system cannot determine the answer mechanically and requires an authority-bearing human judgment." [S0158 L151], and not that the architecture is wrong (S0158 L145–147, in meaning). This is the book's U, which is not F (§9.13: every U names its reason).
- **Scope.** This holds only for unknowns the checker recognises. An undeclared act yields a PASS (S0152 G-2, L132, in meaning): a silent U reported as PASS, which S0152 names as the top risk (S0152 L133, in meaning).
- **Contradiction between the sources, recorded:**
  - S0152 routes every assurance result through human architecture review (S0152 L37–60, in meaning).
  - S0158 routes "AssurancePassed" to Governance and on to approval with no architecture review (S0158 L331–350, in meaning). Its two-way routing lacks the silent-U case.

**C11. Outbox and idempotent consumers: a contradiction with §1.16.1, recorded (Src; attack on the source).** *(No new concept.)*
- **Contradiction.** S0158's consumer checks "Already processed?", then processes, then records a receipt (S0158 L485–501, in meaning). §1.16.1 rejects exactly that order. It quotes its source, "a check performed *before* an effect is not deduplication; it is a race", and holds that the check and the effect belong to one atomic change.
- **Identity.** The closer home is §1.16.6: an identity must be pinned to what was read. A content-derived identity (§9.16 R2) would merge two genuine emissions with equal content, so it is not the right home.
- **Outbox scope.** It makes the producer's state and its outbox entry atomic (S0158 L369–408, in meaning). It does not make consumers current: they lag.

## 8.22.3 Open questions

- **Fork (C7):** may the incumbent serve as the specification for choices the specification leaves open? If it may, matching the incumbent on those choices is conformance by definition. If not, the specification must be completed first.

The bounded-context set drifts between S0151 and S0158: Execution is added, and Intelligence and Administration are dropped.
