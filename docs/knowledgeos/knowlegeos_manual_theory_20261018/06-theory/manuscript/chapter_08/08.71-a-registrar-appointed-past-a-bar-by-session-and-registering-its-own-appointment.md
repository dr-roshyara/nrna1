## 8.71.1 In plain language

1. **The process that had been acting as Governance declares itself a candidate, and passes the bar "must not be Governance".** The bar names Governance by a session label, `b64828fe`. The candidate is `b51dba91`, so it passes, while disclosing that it recorded four governance acts on the same work item. Each of the three bars is passed only by a different label, and the book does not count a new label as independence. The PO/ARB then rules on the one question put to it, the independence clause, with a test of its own: participation disqualifies if it was authorship, verification, acceptance or ownership, and recording was none of these. The ruling does not revisit the Governance bar. It says nothing about the commission's word "fresh", which the book reads as "no prior participation"; on that reading the appointee is not fresh.
2. **The candidate's census says no prior participation is "recorded anywhere", and its own report lists four registrations.** The census searched only the workflow store. The registrations sit elsewhere. "Anywhere" means "in the store", which presupposes that the store is the record. What carried the question was the candidate's disclosure, not its census.
3. **The appointee records its own appointment.** Within the window, the only record of the PO/ARB's ruling is written by the process the ruling appoints. Three earlier processes in the estate refused to do this. Here the registrar says it acts on the PO/ARB's explicit direction, and the same record is the only witness to that direction. No law the book states forbids it. No record of a human act can be checked inside the record; what is special here is that the one witness has an interest.
4. **The appointment is made on a declared identity.** The candidate "verified" its identity by reading its own environment variable: a self-report. The commission requires the identity to be verified, but neither its own sequence nor the ruling's has a step for it, and the ruling neither performs nor waives it. So the requirement is neither met before the lane is registered nor withdrawn, within the window. If the attribution check later compares the running process's environment value with the label it declared, and the process has not been resumed under a new id, the match holds by construction. It confirms that the self-report is consistent, nothing beyond the label.
5. **The resolver's rule text uses identity only to deny.** Its authorization formula requires an identity match, while saying identity is "never a grant input". The two agree if the match can only take permission away, which is how the formula uses it. The resolver is "not adopted", and yet its rules reach every governed session through the harness pointers. Whether they have standing before adoption, or only reach sessions, is left open.
