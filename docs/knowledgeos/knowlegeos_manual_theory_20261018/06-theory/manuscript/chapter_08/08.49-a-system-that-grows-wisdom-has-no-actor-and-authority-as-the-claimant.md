## 8.49.1 In plain language

1. **The session now asks how KnowledgeOS can "grow wisdom" and become "a maturing intelligence system".** Its answers say "the system updates its understanding", "new understanding is adopted", "the system remembers why it believes". In the book the system does not believe or adopt: a person holding a grant adopts, and the record keeps the act. "Adopted" with no one adopting is the gap the book has seen before, now in the system's own voice. A "wise" output such as "future designs should change the cooling architecture" is a recommendation, and a recommendation is not an act.
2. **The session's own summary lists "Authority" as a dimension and explains it as "who is making this claim? what is their role? what is their basis?"** That is the person behind a claim, its source. The line's Constitution and the book mean something else by authority: an assignment by a recorded human act, which decides standing. The same word names the speaker and the right.
3. **At 11:28 the assistant proposes, as the next step, reducing everything to "the smallest constitutional kernel", which the record had already done before ten that morning.** Either the chat did not know the record's state, or it points back to a step already taken. The record's "verbatim-in-substance" quotations of the architect come from this chat, so the two tracks feed each other while being in different states.
4. **The independent review of the handoff protocol shows the discipline cleanly:** it is a distinct session, it says a recommendation can never become authority by deference, and it gives its view on the freeze as "advisory evidence; the owner decides". It also calls that view "my decision", a word it then takes back. And it reads the research lens intakes as having respected the freeze, which is the records' own account, not what this book found.
5. **Several points agree with the book:** "this is what we know, why we know it, what changed, what we still do not know" is close to promise (b); a knowledge object carries who said it, when and on what basis; "evidence does not become authority". Others go beyond it: "how certain we are" is a grade the book has not built, and compressing observations is a derived view, never a deletion.
