## 8.9.1 In plain language

A verifier can check whether a correction's claims about primary evidence are right without knowing who wrote it. It cannot, from the content alone, say who is answerable for it or under which authority it was made. Those are two questions, and the sources keep them apart. A verifier who has worked on the thing under test does not clear it; it recuses, and a recusal is not a clearance. When the work a safeguard protects keeps being touched, the pool of verifiers who could clear it can shrink. A rule about absence claims failed again on the document that stated it, one scope wider. A script that runs shows a behaviour, not a modelled capability. And two tracks that share one contract may not change it in passing: a change to the shared part needs an act of a third authority.

## 8.9.2 Claims and derivations

**C1. Content assessment and provenance binding are two questions (Src; an extension of §1.12).** `[CON-0150 · provisional]`
- S0090, on a correction with no assignment and no declared producer: "**Does the irregularity affect assurance? Two separate answers, which must not be merged.**" [S0090 L232]. First: "**Content assessment does not depend on provenance.**" [S0090 L233]. Second: "**What is unaffected is the truth of its claims; what is affected is the record's ability to say who is answerable for them, and under what authority.**" [S0090 L234].
- The verdict is split the same way: "provenance integrity: 🔴 FAIL** *(of the record, not of the analysis" [S0090 L238].
- **Scope (restated after review C16):** S0090 states it for a correction whose every claim "is a claim about primary evidence" [S0090 L233]. It does not hold for first-hand, self-declared content (S0091 L102, in meaning) or for claims about the producer's own exposure (S0091 L715, in meaning): there, the producer's identity bears on how much the content weighs.
- **Int (§1.12; §8.6 C3; Rulebook §1; PT-001):** §1.12 separates a valid history (the acts occurred) from a verifiable one (each act has a record). C1 is a related but distinct distinction for a **work product**: claims that can be re-derived from primary evidence can be checked by anyone, while **accountability** needs a declared producer and an authorizing act. This matches the Principal's rule that authorship neither makes a result wrong nor right, and adds what authorship is still needed for: answerability. *Two instances in one estate:* §8.6 C3's verification accepted on its substance (S0058) and this one.

**C2. Recusal is not clearance, and the pool of eligible verifiers can run out (Src; an extension of §8.6).** `[CON-0151 · provisional]`
- The first verifier built the work a safeguard protects, so "I therefore do not treat my `SB-1` verdict as independent." [S0089 L28]. The second verified that work and also recuses: "This report's `SB-1` row is a recusal, exactly as Verification #1's was." [S0090 L26]. It refuses to force a verdict: "forcing a `PASS` here would be exactly the misuse the amendment's `A1.7` warns against — a recusal read as a clearance." [S0090 L196].
- "two consecutive verifications have now been unable to clear `SB-1`" … "the pool of processes able to clear it shrinks each time Track 1 is touched." [S0090 L28].
- **Neutrality about the subject can be unattainable:** "the subject of this discovery is the role model, and every process in this estate occupies roles. Subject-matter neutrality is therefore unattainable by ANY verifier." [S0089 L26].
- **Ded (restated after review C16):** let E(o) be the set of processes eligible to verify object o, those with no authorship of o or of the work o protects. Then E(o) is **non-increasing** as work proceeds, and it drops only when an eligible process authors o or what o protects. In the observed case both verifiers were ineligible **before** they started, through earlier work on Track 1 (S0089 L28; S0090 L17–28, in meaning); verifying did not make them so. E(o) can become empty only if the population of processes is **closed**. Under eligibility by identifier it is not: a new session has a new self-declared identifier at no cost, which meets "no authorship" trivially. §8.6 C1 already holds that a new identifier is not an independence factor. Under §8.6's factors, the pool may be empty from the outset rather than run out. **Fork F32:** eligibility relative to what — an identifier, §8.6's factor set, or the relation to the object (TODO T1-19)?
- A neutrality bar that no process meets makes verification unperformable, which is why S0089 takes the weaker bar the assignment set, "Producer barred" [S0089 L26].
- **Int:** recusal is a fourth outcome beside pass, pass with notes and fail. Recording it as such keeps an unmet independence requirement visible instead of turning it into a verdict.

**C3. The absence discipline failed again, one scope wider (Src; an instance of §8.4 C3).**
- §8.4 C3 records the discipline S0091 proposed after its own truncated search: an absence claim needs "an exhaustive, untruncated search AND an explicit statement of what was searched" (S0091 L404). S0090 found that the amendment broke it in the same document: it "surveyed `*.puml` in one directory and stated a conclusion about **"the untracked material"** as a class. **Same failure mode, third occurrence, narrower scope**" [S0090 L85].
- The falsifier was an untracked 925-line document setting out all six roles, "cited by NONE of the three artifacts" [S0090 L78].
- S0090's method for checking the rule: "where it states an absence, I searched at a WIDER scope than it did" [S0090 L46].
- **Int:** a scope-statement rule is checked by searching beyond the stated scope. Stating the scope makes the claim refutable; it does not make it true. That is §8.4 C3 seen from the verifier's side.

**C4. A behaviour is not a modelled capability (Src; an extension of §6.4 C3 and §8.4 C4).**
- A blocking gate that is adopted and in use refutes the generalisation that "enforcement is absent" [S0089 L91]. The corrected status is "**live, narrow, unmodelled**" [S0089 L93]. S0090 quantifies "narrow": the gate is "the ONLY `governance_tier: 1` asset among all 16 registered assets" [S0090 L109].
- "**A script proves a behaviour happens; it never proves a capability is modelled**" [S0090 L249]. And "*A declared trace is a classification, not a decision*" [S0090 L250].
- **That status was itself withdrawn.** The producer's next correction found "live, narrow, unmodelled" "NOT ESTABLISHED" and replaced it with CONTESTED: "The sole Tier-1 asset may be a **correctly filed `CAP-09` constitutional-observation asset**, not a `C-14` instance" (S0091 L766, in meaning); "**Neither "absent" nor "live" is established**" (S0091 L767, in meaning). §8.4 C3 records the same sequence (absent, then live, then contested).
- **Int (§6.4 C3, §8.4 C4; restated after review C16):** four separate questions. First, **which capability is the observed behaviour an instance of?** That is a classification, and it needs an act. Then: does the behaviour occur, how widely, and has an act modelled it as a capability with an owner. A count of mechanisms answers the second and third; only adopting acts answer the first and the fourth (A1). *One line.*

**C5. A correction that does not reach where the reader reads (Src; the verification of §8.4 C5's case).**
- §8.4 C5 records, from S0091, a correction that "landed on the `C-5` row" through a scripted first-match replace. S0090 is the verification that found it: the correction pointer "for `C-14` is attached to `C-5`" [S0090 L124], so "**Two wrong statements are produced at once, in the estate's own decision surface**" [S0090 L127]. It calls this "an application defect, not a redesign and not a new overclaim" [S0090 L131].
- **Int (§8.5 C8; §8.7):** a correction that is not shown where the reader reads does not reach that reader. Its analysis can be right while its application is wrong, and the two are checked separately. *Not independent of §8.4 C5:* one incident, seen from producer and verifier.

**C6. A count that changes because governed work happened (Src; an instance of §8.8 C2).**
- Four measurement points give 39, 40, 40, 42 path-bearing references [S0090 L143–148]. "***A number that changes because the estate did governed work is not a defect in the measurement***"; the fix is "a stated observation point instead of a fixed number" [S0090 L151].
- **Int (§8.8 C2):** a count needs its measurement point. Here governed work, including the measurer's own recorded handoff, changed the count between measurements [S0090 L151]; the measurement itself did not.

**C7. Two tracks over one shared contract: the shared part is owned by neither (Src; for the boundary questions).** `[CON-0152 · provisional]`
- "**Neither track may silently change the shared L3 contract or accepted semantic decisions.**" [S0087 L19]. "**Any shared-boundary change requires a separate Architecture/PO/ARB act.**" [S0087 L20]. "**the shared boundary is not owned by either track.** Track 1 consumes it; Track 2 may not amend it in passing; a change to it is a third act by a third authority." [S0087 L22]. Verification stays separate per deliverable [S0087 L24].
- The rule is carried into the discovery lane: "**If discovery reveals a change to a shared architectural boundary: STOP · record the implication · return the required change to Architecture / PO-ARB as a separate act.**" [S0088 L46].
- **Not a case of K3 (restated after review C16):** Track 2 is "EKS / Knowledge Engineering Platform Evolution" [S0087 L12], the platform side, not an application; the two tracks are one programme in one repository. §6.6 C2 rejected the same reading for agent harnesses. So C7 is no evidence for K3 (§9.6.6a).
- **Int (§6.6 C8):** a **stated norm**, not an observed property: the shared part is owned by neither track and changes only by an act of a third authority, never as a side effect of a consumer's work. Whether the rule held is not shown by these sources. *Hyp (one source, one contract):* that a rule of this form is a kernel law for the kernel/application boundary.

**C8. A programme is not an assignment (Src; an extension of §5.6).**
- The act named four purposes for Track 2: "**a programme, not an assignment**. Manufacturing a single work item broad enough to cover it would be inventing scope the act did not define" [S0087 L36]. Governance created none.
- The commission that followed treats the six proposed roles as "**HYPOTHESES TO EVALUATE, NOT DECISIONS**" [S0088 L24], and classes every statement as observed, inferred, proposed, decided or open: "⛔ **Do not turn a proposal into a decision.**" [S0088 L40].
- **Int (§5.6):** what an act covers is fixed by the act. A recorder who widens it into a work item creates scope without an act. *One line.*

**C9. Instances bearing on earlier sections (Src).**
- **§6.7's sources were untracked at the time.** On 2026-08-19 the verifier found "**all four `.puml` files and both `README`s are untracked**" [S0090 L57]. S0089 classes an untracked diagram as "*evidence of NON-REGISTRATION*, not *evidence of NON-EXISTENCE*" [S0089 L81]. It also confirms that the context and container views decompose the engineering roles differently: "*Same membership, incompatible decomposition*" [S0090 L58].
- **A verifier recorded the START of its own lane:** "I **transcribed the PO/ARB's `START` act into the workflow record myself** (seq 9)" [S0090 L30]; S0089's verifier "**recorded my own STARTs**" [S0089 L24]. Both are further instances of the roles law of §6.5 C2 being breached in practice, disclosed by the breaching process.

**C10. Further points from the window (Src; added after review C16).**
- **One actor, two identifiers.** Verifier #1 declares itself `1c8b041b` [S0089 L13] and also "the **Track-1 implementation engineer** (`4c6c1dac`)" (S0089 L28, in meaning); S0090 calls `1c8b041b` that engineer (S0090 L17, in meaning). The sources do not explain the two identifiers. *Int:* C1's binding to a producer and C2's eligibility both assume a stable identity of a producer, which the record does not give (§5.5; F13a).
- **A self-report tagged OBSERVED, and counted as corroboration.** The discovery tags its role-fluidity finding "OBSERVED (first-hand, self-declared)" (S0091 L102, in meaning), and the first verifier "corroborate[s] `F-9` from independent first-hand evidence" (S0089 L106, in meaning): its own self-recorded STARTs. *Int (§1.12-3; §8.6 C4):* three self-reports of one kind are one line of support, not three observations.
- **Decision rights follow the nature of the activity.** The discovery's stated pattern: "the AI **decides** wherever the outcome is a derived fact of an executable check; it **recommends** wherever the outcome is a judgment; humans hold every gate where authority, value, or entrenchment changes" (S0091 L92, in meaning). *Hyp (one source):* a candidate law of who may decide; its home would be §1.6 and §8.6.
- **"Role" has three meanings:** BC-7's lane role, the personas' engineer role, and the authority matrix's decision right: "**Three meanings, one word**" (S0091 L169, in meaning). Beside the "authority" homonym of §6.6.
- **Corrections still not shown where the reader reads.** After the producer's second correction, earlier statements remain unmarked in the body (for example the old C-14 headline and the count "39"; S0091 L82, L101, L237, in meaning), although the correction says its edits were "verified after writing" (S0091 L648, in meaning). A recurrence of C5, from the same producer.

## 8.9.4 What does not hold

*First version, refuted at review C16:*
- **"E(o) shrinks with every verification, and in a finite estate it can become empty"** (C2's Ded; the second half of CON-0151 as stated). Both observed verifiers were ineligible before they started, and emptiness needs a closed population, which identifiers do not give. Restated in C2.
- **"This is the shape of two independent applications over one shared contract"** (C7). Track 2 is the platform side; one programme, one repository. Not a case of K3.
- **"Owned by neither"** without "track", and "changes only by its own act" (C7). S0087 names a third authority.
- **"live, narrow, unmodelled" as the corrected status** (C4). Withdrawn by the producer as not established; CONTESTED (S0091 L761–767).
- **"Content assessment does not depend on provenance"** applied to all of a work product's claims (C1). Only to claims that can be re-derived from primary evidence. And "one source": §8.6 C3 is an earlier instance.
- **"Measuring changes what is measured"** (C6). Governed work changed the count, not the measurement.
