## 8.102.1 In plain language

1. **Within the estate, the kernel is recast as the enforcer of the admission boundary, not the boundary itself.** Within the book's record that matches the gate in one sense: the record's rules checked when an entry is appended.
2. **Within the estate, the source finds that admission is only the first step and asks who owns what follows.** Within the book's record the question stays open: ending, revocation and annulment are recorded acts, but supersession, a challenge, reconciliation and withdrawal have no recorded kind, so nothing in the record carries them yet.
3. **Within the estate, the adviser warns that the "six-part invariant" may be only a storage decision.** Within the book's record that matches the book's finding on the six-part aggregate, in its three parts. Several of the analysis's "established facts" are not established in the book.
4. **Within the estate, contradiction is split into four kinds:** between states, between meanings, between evidence and between rules. Within the book's record three of them have a counterpart: contradictory entries give "undetermined" with a reason, live support against live refutation gives "contested", and a conflict between rules has its own theory. Only a contradiction of meanings has none.
5. **Within the estate, "identity" is said to be overloaded.** The book keeps entry, item and process identity apart for the same reason.

**C2. Admission against a lifecycle whose transitions D does not admit (Src; Int, within the estate and D)** *(Restated at review: W-01, W-02, W-08)*.

- **Int:**
  - **Within D the dilemma is open, not dissolved (W-01).** Ending, revocation and annulment are acts of D (§9.13.2), and their effect on an item is derived by CE* from the one record. But **no admitted kind is a transition of a claim's epistemic state** (§8.88 C1): supersession and suspension are not admitted (§9.13 L52; CON-0175, open); raising a challenge has no kind (§8.93 C4; CON-0211); reconciliation and a claim's withdrawal have no kind (§8.94 C4; §8.93 C4); and no kind relates two claims (§8.88 C2 (iii)). *So S0392's lifecycle transitions, KnowledgeSuperseded, KnowledgeContested and KnowledgeReconciled (L534), have no carrier in D, and who would own them is open.* What survives is §8.87 C4's form: within D the lifecycle question is answered by evaluation over the record, for the admitted kinds only.
  - **"Contested" is a homonym here (W-02).** S0392's KnowledgeContested is a transition entered by a challenge (L81, L534); its book home is the dispute flag, CON-0211 (provisional). g1's "contested" is a derived class on one item, live support against live refutation (§9.18.5); §8.88 C2 (iv) and §8.93 C4 already keep them apart.
  - **(B)'s lifecycle question is the one S0364 (A) then answers** (lineage), so §8.88 C1 and C4, which read S0364, are its downstream, not its precedent.
  - **"God Aggregate" (L646)** turns on ownership counted by responsibilities; the book counts by invariants and the test of what changes atomically (§8.94 C1), and owners in Ch. 10 are candidates, not decided (§10.3: every row S0).
