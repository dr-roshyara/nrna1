## 8.104.1 In plain language

1. **Within the estate, one research commission went to three providers, and an adviser counted their agreement as independent convergence.** The commission listed the topics the three agree on, so agreement on topics is its agenda. On positions the agreement is partly led by the commission's own questions and its required output form, and partly by shared reference works; and the adviser's grading of two of the replies is in places not borne out by their text. The third reply attacks the estate's own earlier domain model, which the commission as recorded does not contain; the second reply also brings a state vocabulary of its own.
2. **Within the estate, the third provider's report, which §8.103 could not read, is now read directly.** Several of its questions land where the book already stands: standing is conferred by a recorded act, so "conventional" describes it rather than refutes it; the kernel within D checks no sufficiency; the evaluator's three values are discrete by construction and are standing, not epistemic states. Identity through change, and who may set or amend the admission rules, remain open in the book.
3. **Within the estate, the report names five places where interpretation might hide in the kernel.** Within D none is a judgement the kernel makes, but for different reasons: two are enforced over recorded fields; one, the identity link, is an input that is not recorded and has no named supplier, so the question is moved outside the kernel, not answered; and two, sufficiency and assigning an item's identifier, are absent from D, which means the operation is not built, not that the reasoner is excluded.
