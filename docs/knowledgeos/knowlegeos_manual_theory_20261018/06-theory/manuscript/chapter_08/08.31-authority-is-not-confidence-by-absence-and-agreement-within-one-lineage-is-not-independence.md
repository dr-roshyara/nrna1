## 8.31.1 In plain language

1. **"Authority is not confidence" is shown in the estate by what is missing.** No system has a confidence value, so nothing can be confused with authority. That supports keeping the two apart; it does not show how they relate once confidence exists.
2. **"I don't know" is not "no", not "not authorised", and not "refused".** The book keeps these apart too, but its "no" (F) also covers "nothing is recorded". An "I don't know" that comes only from not having read everything is a defect, not an honest answer.
3. **The estate's two counts of undated transitions differ (218 against the book's 210).** Both say the same thing: almost no recorded act carries a time.
4. **One file first calls two AI-produced reviews "independent research streams", and later says they are not.** Agreement within one programme is one voice.
5. **"Mechanism" means two different things** in the estate and in the book.
6. **Of the estate's kernel candidates, the book's kernel already contains five:** authority as a recorded act, the record as the only state, a closed answer vocabulary, evaluation conferring no authority, and an honest "undetermined". **It lacks two:** supersession and register-scoped identity.

## 8.31.2 Claims and derivations

**C1. Authority is not confidence: the estate shows it by absence, and its three statements are one lineage (Src; Int).**
- **What the sources say:**
  - S0201: "CONFIDENCE ≠ AUTHORITY is the strongest-supported external principle", classed "ESTABLISHED (EKS/PKS)", "PARTIALLY ESTABLISHED (AIP)" (S0201 L21, L296).
  - S0202: "Authority is organizational, not epistemic. A proposition may be well-supported / unauthorized, or authorized / uncertain" (S0202 L281–291, in meaning).
  - S0203 relays S0201: "It is already observable architecture" (S0203 L117).
- **S0201's own qualification:** "the separation is established by *absence* of confidence, not by a modelled confidence axis" (S0201 L531, contradiction X-03). No system has a confidence concept (S0201 L264, L283–285). S0203 drops this qualification.
- **Int:** what the estate shows is that **no assessment confers authority** (EKS AP-1, a machine-unemittable verdict subset; S0201 L158, L292). That is the book's "knowledge feeds authority; it never holds it" (§0.6; §1.2). It is **not** a demonstrated separation of two modelled axes.
- The book's own separation is stronger in one respect and weaker in another:
  - **stronger:** promise (b) shows **two values**, current authority and the well-foundedness class, and §9.18 models evidence entries that change no authority (fault containment, values and marks; ce_model v22, check 33);
  - **weaker:** the class is a three-valued status, not a confidence. The book has no confidence either (§9.18 G7 open).
- **Grades differ for the same claim:** S0202's first review rates "Authority" as "Separate concern" with strength "High" (L614); its second rates "Authority ≠ Confidence" "Moderate", consensus "Emerging" (L1519, L1596). *A grade in these reviews is its producer's, not the field's.*
- **Not a new concept.** *Agreement within the lineage; not independent support.*

**C2. Four answers kept apart, and an UNKNOWN from not reading is a defect (Src; Ded against §9.13; Int).**
- **What the sources say:**
  - S0201's candidate C-10, "Honest-UNKNOWN as first-class answer": "unknown ≠ unauthorized ≠ denied" (S0201 L450).
  - S0203 extends it: "'I don't know' ≠ 'false' ≠ 'not authorized' ≠ 'denied'" (S0203 L219–226, in meaning).
  - S0204, for the R-1 session: "An honest UNKNOWN is valid; an artificial UNKNOWN is a defect", after "NOT FOUND ≠ NOT PRESENT ≠ NOT EVIDENCED ≠ UNKNOWN" (S0204 L79, in meaning).
- **Ded, against the book:**
  - CE*'s U is "the record does not decide" (§9.13 R2). Its F is "refuted, or not recognized": under C1 = constitute, "nothing recorded" gives **F**, not U (§9.13 R2a; §10.5 PL-6).
  - So S0203's split "I don't know ≠ false" maps onto CE* only as **U ≠ F**. **The estate's "not recognized because nothing is recorded" falls into CE*'s F**, beside "refuted".
  - That is the hazard T10-21 (1) records: Q1's F must not be shown as "refuted".
  - "Not found ≠ unauthorized" is already the book's (§1.17.3; §9.10 Q3).
- **"An artificial UNKNOWN is a defect" (Int):** an UNKNOWN that a full read would have resolved is the mirror of laundering. CON-0221 forbids turning an unresolved status into a resolved one. S0204 forbids **manufacturing** an unresolved status from not reading.
  - The book's rule is the same as §8.30 C1's: completeness is established, not asserted.
  - A refusal or a U that rests on an unread store is not the record's answer (CON-0212: "a failed read is a refusal, not F").
- **Not a new concept**; it sharpens CON-0212 and §8.30 C1. *(n = 1: a launch prompt states it; no case of an artificial UNKNOWN is shown in this window.)*
