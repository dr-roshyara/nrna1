## 8.31.1 In plain language

1. **"Authority is not confidence" is shown in the estate partly by what is missing.** No system has a confidence value. What is shown directly is that **no assessment confers authority**. That does not show how the two relate once confidence exists.
2. **The estate's "I don't know" splits by cause in the book:** an undecided structure gives U; missing knowledge gives F ("not recognized", since only recorded acts count); an unreadable record gives a refusal. An "I don't know" that comes only from not having read everything is a defect, not an honest answer.
3. **The counts of undated transitions (210, 216, 218) are one record measured at later cuts.** They all say the same thing: almost no recorded act carries a time.
4. **One file calls one pair of texts "independent research streams", and later denies independence for another pair.** It never examines either claim. Agreement within one programme is one voice.
5. **"Mechanism" means two different things** in the estate and in the book.
6. **Against the estate's eight kernel candidates, the book's kernel fully contains one** (authority as a recorded act). **It holds four in part or in form** (the record as the only state, a closed answer vocabulary, an honest "undetermined", and evaluation conferring no authority, which is only proposed). **It lacks two** (supersession and register-scoped identity), and has a seventh as a rule.
