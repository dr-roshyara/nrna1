## 8.62.1 In plain language

1. **The first Sanskrit-grammar record calls its text "the HPA's complete extraction".** That text is the first answer in this chat export. The export holds answers in an assistant's voice and no question. Its second answer is word for word the export the book read as S0295, so the same text sits in two corpus files and counts once. The third answer refers back to a sentence from S0295's word-order answer, which suggests the file is a selection from one chat. Three of the record's sentences about "storing" have no counterpart in the answer: "the kernel stores the semantic roots and rules", "not a content store", and "store the rule, not the content". They are the recorder's own.
2. **An adviser recommended "accept, with conditions" and drafted the note for the product owner to add.** The registration records that note as "the PO/ARB's own wording" and puts the adviser's reasons inside the quoted decision. What the act decides does not depend on who drafted the words. But the record's statement about the words' origin is a claim of provenance, and the files show a draft. A later appendix explains a sentence of the adviser's as the product owner's. It also adds a "final" wording, after the grant pointing at the record had been registered.
3. **The registration says the acceptance "closes" the chain's findings and "discharges" the bar on Phase 5.** The previous record had already routed both to this acceptance, and had restated the bar as lasting "until the PO/ARB's acceptance decision". The plan's own sentence asked for an independent review of the repair. The record does not say whether that condition was met, was moved to the acceptance earlier in the chain, or was waived by this act.
4. **The same open item is non-blocking for the acceptance and, by the record, blocking for the migration.** The quoted note says only "during migration preparation"; "before migration authorization" is the record's sentence, taken from the adviser. Read as the product owner adopting that condition, it is one instance of a criticality set by a decision. That needs the premise that the grant is a recorded entry, which the window does not show.
5. **The grammar answers say "the identity survived" a change of expression, but give no rule for sameness.** In the book, item identity is open. "Event time is not knowledge time" agrees only in part: the book's two times are those of the authority question, not of a claim's content.
