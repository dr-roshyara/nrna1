# Chapter 8: How the theory is validated

*How the corpus tests its own claims, and what those tests can and cannot show. The corpus claims, among other things, that KnowledgeOS is independent of any one product (Ch. 0 §0.2; CON-0027, invalid). This chapter collects the corpus's validation methods and their limits, as **candidate** methods for validating this book's theory (corrected after review C4 F-11; SEQ-0013).*

**Status:** opened 2026-10-19 with corpus source S0025 (the MVK bootstrap experiment). Methods already met elsewhere are referenced, not copied:
- falsification by projection into other products: Ch. 3 §3.7 (S0022);
- a lexical search cannot show the absence of a semantic coupling: Ch. 5 §5.2 (S0024).

**What this chapter is, and what it is not.** It was announced as candidate methods for validating this book's theory. §8.6–§8.11 are mostly a theory of verification acts in the estate (independence, recusal, eligibility, authorship). Whether Ch. 8 is a validation chapter or an assurance-governance chapter is the Principal's decision (decision brief DB-4); until then both readings stand. The candidate law the sections converge on is stated once, as Hyp, at §8.10 C7.
