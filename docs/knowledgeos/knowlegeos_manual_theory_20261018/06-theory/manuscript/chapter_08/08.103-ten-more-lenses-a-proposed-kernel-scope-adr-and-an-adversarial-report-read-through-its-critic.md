## 8.103.1 In plain language

1. **Within the estate, ten more lenses are proposed and then counted as confirming the kernel boundary.** The book holds again that lenses from one line counting their own agreement are one voice. One test the adviser draws from them, that two uses of a word with different behaviour must not share one concept, is the book's own practice; another, that nothing should be invented when a prerequisite is missing, agrees with the book.
2. **Within the estate, an adviser proposes an ADR fixing the kernel's scope.** Within the book's record its strongest point agrees with the book's first result: a list of prohibitions is not a list of capabilities, and a kernel defined by prohibitions alone is met by doing nothing. The ADR is a proposal, not a recorded decision.
3. **Within the estate, a third provider's adversarial report is read by an adviser,** who keeps two distinctions the book also keeps: the record's boundary is one of authority to record, not a claim about what knowledge is; and admission is not final. The question whether checking a contract needs interpretation has, within the book's record, the answer no for the record's rules and no counterpart for sufficiency. The adviser's "very strong" convergence of three providers is three answers to one line of prompts.
