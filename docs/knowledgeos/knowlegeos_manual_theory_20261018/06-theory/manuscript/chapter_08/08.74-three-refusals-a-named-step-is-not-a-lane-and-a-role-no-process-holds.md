## 8.74.1 In plain language

1. **Three processes were handed the next task, and each correctly refused.** One was asked to re-verify work already verified. One was the correction's author, asked to review its own correction for adoption. One was a fresh process with no lane. None acted on its prompt alone. The fresh one put it plainly: a step named in a sequence is not a lane.
2. **The re-verifier learned that the work was done from documents, not from the record.** The workflow record still shows the verifier's lane active, with nothing after it. The completion was in the verification report, its registration and a working-context file. The record again lacks an end of work.
3. **The record's recorder field for the verifier's REGISTER and HANDOFF is now reported: "governance".** The book left that open in the last window. The appointee appended both itself.
4. **The correction's author has a lane on the record, and the resolver says UNRESOLVED for it.** Three readings are possible, and the sources do not decide: the label as given did not match, the resolver skips handed-off lanes, or the lane's text holds no label it can find. UNRESOLVED shows only that no lane matched the inputs as given.
5. **The next step needs a Governance lane, and no process holds a Governance role on this work item's record.** *(R3, C82, §8.75 C5: a Governance lane exists on another work item, `KOS-OQ-001`, S0336 L39.)* Every REGISTER and HANDOFF on this work item is marked "recorded by governance", yet no Governance lane exists. Since S0323 the practice has been to record without a lane while refusing to review without one; earlier processes refused to record for themselves too. Whether that line is right is the Principal's question. It is not a deadlock: the PO/ARB's act can still lead on. But the exit the fresh process prescribes again has Governance record a human START.
6. **Smaller points.** The author's refusal to review its own work agrees with the book. The fresh process recommends itself as qualifying, on its own check, though its refusal is itself prior participation. One process could not reproduce an earlier fingerprint and fell back on file times and a source scan, which are weaker.
