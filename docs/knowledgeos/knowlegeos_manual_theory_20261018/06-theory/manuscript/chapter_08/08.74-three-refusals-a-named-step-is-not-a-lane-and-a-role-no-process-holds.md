## 8.74.1 In plain language

1. **Three processes were handed the next task, and each correctly refused.** One was asked to re-verify work already verified. One was the correction's author, asked to review its own correction for adoption. One was a fresh process with no lane. Each read the record rather than its prompt. The fresh one put it plainly: a step named in a sequence is not a lane.
2. **The record's recorder field for the verifier's REGISTER and HANDOFF is now reported: "governance".** The book left that open in the last window. The appointee appended both itself, so the record names Governance as recorder for two entries the verifier made about its own lane.
3. **The correction's author has a lane on the record, and the resolver still says UNRESOLVED for it.** "UNRESOLVED" is read in the estate as "no lane". Here it means only that no lane matched the label as given, in the state the resolver looks for.
4. **The next step needs a Governance lane, and no process holds a Governance role on the record.** Every REGISTER and HANDOFF on this work item is marked "recorded by governance", yet no Governance lane exists. Governance acts in a recording capacity outside the lane model. The fresh process refuses to act as Governance without a lane; the earlier appointee acted as Governance without one, to record its own lane. The estate's line between the two is the line between reviewing and recording.
5. **Smaller points.** The author's refusal to review its own work agrees with the book. The fresh process recommends itself as qualifying, on its own check. One process could not reproduce the store digests reported earlier and fell back on file times, which the book has found weaker.
