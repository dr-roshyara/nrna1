## 8.79.1 In plain language

1. **The implementation session said that recording its STOP was a human act for Governance, and then the STOP was recorded by the implementation role.** Whether a human decision stood behind it, the sources do not say.
2. **After the STOP, the work item is stopped, and only a recorded CONTINUATION restarts it,** a step the sequence declared at START did not contain. Whether the STOP also ended the producer's lane is not reported; if the lane stayed active, or the CONTINUATION reactivates it, the producer is shown as entitled to act again. *(Restated at review: W-05.)*
3. **The producer recorded the appointment of its own verifier and preserved the prompt the verifier will read first.** The producer bar forbids verifying one's own work; it does not forbid this. Beyond five words of the PO/ARB, who wrote the prompt cannot be shown. *(Restated at review: W-11.)*
4. **Both appointments name an actor that does not yet exist, a case the commissioned operating model does not cover.** The model handles a session with no appointment and a session with a named appointee; an appointment with a placeholder fits neither. The producer reports having implemented that model. *(Restated at review: W-01, W-03.)*
5. **The mechanism, asked what comes next while the item is stopped, says no fresh actor is required because the work "is already assigned".** The outcome is safe; the reason given is wrong for a stopped item. *(Restated at review: W-08.)*
