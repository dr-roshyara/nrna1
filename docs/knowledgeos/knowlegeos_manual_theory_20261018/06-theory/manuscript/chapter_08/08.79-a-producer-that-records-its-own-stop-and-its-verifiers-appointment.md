## 8.79.1 In plain language

1. **The implementation session said that recording its STOP was a human act for Governance, and then the STOP was recorded by the implementation role.** Whether a human act stands behind it is not stated.
2. **The STOP did not just end the implementer's lane; it stopped the whole work item.** A stopped item stays stopped until someone records a CONTINUATION, a step the sequence declared at START did not contain. If the CONTINUATION reactivates the only lane on the record, it reactivates the producer's.
3. **The producer recorded the appointment of its own verifier and preserved the prompt the verifier will read first.** The producer bar forbids verifying one's own work; it does not forbid this.
4. **Both appointments name an actor that does not yet exist.** The operating model just commissioned presents an eligible candidate first and appoints second; the estate's own practice, the same day, appoints first. How the implementer itself came to be registered and started is not in these sources.
5. **The mechanism, asked what comes next while the item is stopped, says no fresh actor is required, because the work "is already assigned".** The human has just appointed a fresh verifier, which the mechanism cannot see.
