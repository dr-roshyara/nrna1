## 8.35.1 In plain language

1. **"No knowledge state may ever be final"** is false in the book: a root act cannot be annulled. The later, weaker version ("revision stays possible and history is kept") agrees with the book.
2. **"When supporting knowledge changes, dependent decisions must be re-evaluated."** The book does this for **authority**: an adoption loses authority when a grant it cites ends. It deliberately does not do it for **evidence**: evidence changes no standing.
3. **"A derived conclusion must not be treated as an observation"** agrees with the book. Its answers are derived and never recorded as facts.
4. **The reasoning-systems literature keeps beliefs free of contradiction.** The book keeps a contradiction visible, as "undetermined" with its reason, until someone acts. Both refuse circular support; the book's rule is stricter.
5. **The chat moved its position because the human said so.** It first put the reasoning engine outside KnowledgeOS, then inside it, after the human's statement and with no new evidence. Then one reply called the framework "architecturally complete … with total confidence", and the next one rejected that.
6. **Lists marked "Established" still have no measurement behind them, and one text appears in the corpus twice.**

## 8.35.2 Claims and derivations

**C1. Revision keeps history, but the book has finals: a root act cannot be annulled (Src; Ded against §9.13; Int).**
- **S0217 (B):** "H-KOS-Revisability-001: No knowledge state SHALL be irrevocably finalized. The kernel MUST provide a governed path for revision (retraction, replacement, or refinement) for every knowledge object" (L368–374).
- **S0217 (C)** weakens it: "is slightly too strong … Some domains have terminal states: historical fact after archival closure, legal judgment after final appeal, mathematical proof". It restates it as "preserve the possibility of epistemic revision while preserving historical identity and provenance of previous states", with "Old state remains historically true: 'I was believed at time T'" (L570–607).
- **Ded, against the book:**
  - **A root act cannot be annulled**: a root is T by stipulation, and an annulment targeting it has no effect (§9.13 Θ_f; model check 16). So (B)'s absolute is **false** in the book.
  - **(C)'s form agrees:**
    - every other act can be annulled, revoked or ended by a recorded act;
    - an annulment voids its target from the start but stays recorded;
    - a removal keeps every field D requires (§9.13.2);
    - "I was believed at time T" is Q1 at an earlier cut (§9.10 Q1).
- **Not a new concept.**

**C2. Authority is coupled to its grants by evaluation; it is deliberately not coupled to evidence (Src; Ded against §9.13, §9.18).**
- **The sources:** H-KOS-Decision-001: "When a knowledge state changes, its associated decisions SHALL be flagged for re-evaluation, ensuring that actions do not outlive their justification" (S0217 L358–364). Restated: "Changes to supporting knowledge SHALL trigger epistemic re-evaluation of dependent decisions" (L516–528).
- **Ded:**
  - **For authority, the book already couples, and automatically:** an adoption's authority lapses when any cited grant's period ends, or an effective ending of it comes after the adoption (§9.13 Θ_f P, conjunctive over the cited grants). No flag is needed: CE* recomputes it at every cut.
  - **For evidence, the book refuses the coupling by design:** an evidence entry changes no standing value or mark (§9.18 (c1)–(c2); check 33).
- **Int:** under (b), a "re-evaluation" driven by evidence can only be a **display** beside standing (PL-14), not a change of it. The sources' "supporting knowledge" mixes the two kinds of support the book keeps apart: grants, which **authorise**, and evidence, which **supports content**.
- **Not a new concept.**

**C3. Inference is never recorded as observation: CE*'s answers are derived, and a stored one has no standing (Src; Int).**
- **The sources:**
  - "Derived conclusions SHALL remain distinguishable from authoritative knowledge" (S0216 L359–363);
  - "Observation ≠ Inference": "Model generated statement treated as observation — epistemic corruption", and "Derived knowledge SHALL preserve the reasoning path and premises" (S0217 L615–668);
  - S0215's "Derived belief treated as core belief" forbidden (L301).
- **The book:**
  - CE*'s answers are **derived** from the record and are never entries.
  - A stored derived value has no standing (CON-0141).
  - The reasons for a U are given (§9.13.3). Reasons for T and F are required but not yet defined (§9.13 L166).
  - *Agreement*, with the gap that the reasoning path is published for U only.
- **Not a new concept.**

**C4. Consistency maintenance against visible contradiction; acyclicity; context as scope (Src; Int).**
- **S0215** gives the truth-maintenance task as keeping conclusions "not known to be contradictory" with "no belief kept without a reason" (L40). Its forbidden transitions include "Contradictory beliefs without resolution" (L297) and "Circular justification" (L299). Its invariants include "Global acyclicity" (L178).
- **The book:**
  - **(i) Contradiction:** a contradiction gives **U with its reason**, and stays visible until a recorded act resolves it (§9.15 G6; I-4; §9.13 R2). "Without resolution" is the book's normal state for an undecided record, not a violation. For evidence, the rival "contested" class is T9-17's open question; S0237 and S0888 favour preserving conflict (§9.18).
  - **(ii) Acyclicity:** the book's D allows citations of **earlier entries only** (§9.13.2), which implies acyclicity. The Principal chose it over D_acyclic, the rival with the same guarantee. *Agreement; the book's rule is stricter.*
  - **(iii) Context:** S0216 (A)'s "cognitive worlds": "Contradiction is not always corruption. Sometimes it is context separation" (L318–338). The book's form of context is the **queried scope**: Q1 answers per scope x, through covers (§9.13 Θ_f ω). Two adoptions under different scopes do not conflict for either scope's question.
- **Not a new concept.**
