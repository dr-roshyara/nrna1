## 8.50.1 In plain language

1. **The session now says wisdom is not another kind of knowledge and cannot be stored; it is a process applied to knowledge, and "KnowledgeOS does not become wise".** That corrects its own earlier "maturing intelligence system" and agrees with the book: the system is not the actor. Some wording still has the system act ("do not promote the conclusion", "request evidence"); read with the book's rule, it means only that no one has adopted, so nothing changes.
2. **A second tool, writing a "research extraction", quotes the assistant's sentences from the previous answer as "[User's analysis]".** The pattern seen in the research records, an adviser's words attributed to the architect, now runs between tools. A third text maps its components to articles that do not match the Constitution's numbering (it calls Article 3 "Evidence" and Article 5 "Provenance").
3. **The operational line records the protocol's adoption by the product owner, quoting his decision verbatim.** But the session that registers the adoption is the protocol's own producer, and the independent review had said the producer must not perform the adoption's registration. The registration discloses this and says it does not count as the producer accepting its own work.
4. **The registration says: "Governance records this decision; it did not make it … the adoption's authority is the human act, not this record."** The book's rule is that within the kernel the recorded act constitutes standing, and that the act happened is an assumption about the world. The two fit if "constitute" is read at two levels: the record, in the kernel; the person's act, in the world.
5. **Several points agree with the book:** humans remain the knower and the decider; an uncertain conclusion is not promoted and stays unknown; a revision keeps what was believed before; "outside research is not KnowledgeOS architecture". Others go beyond it: confidence and "calibrated humility" are grades the book has not built; "Authority" again means an approved source; and the session again proposes the kernel reduction the record had already made.
