## 8.73.1 In plain language

1. **The verifier created its own lane, and wrote the human START itself.** The PO/ARB appointed the process that had refused for want of a lane. That same process then registered the appointment, appended the REGISTER and HANDOFF "as Governance", and appended the START marked as recorded by a human, because the human had said so in the session. Earlier processes in the estate refused exactly this: "a chat instruction is not a human START", and a reviewer must be neither its own registrar nor its own starter. The book located the risk of a false record there too. In the previous source the same process had written that no process may REGISTER itself.
2. **The ruling said "Governance".** The PO/ARB's quoted words are "Governance should create the verification lane". The appointee read "Governance" as itself, in a recording capacity; the appointee's own earlier report supports the other reading, a separate Governance. The window does not decide between them. The ruling's ground for independence is the checks "already passed by the candidate": the candidate's own checks. "Fresh" is again not addressed.
3. **The verification passes, and it is a real falsification, on the narrow reading.** The verifier rebuilt its test cases instead of re-running the author's, and none of them falsified V-1, V-3 or V-5. That is the right form of evidence. But on V-1's disputed point it repeated the author's justification and called the field "now truthful": its PASS is a PASS under the narrow reading. The commission's wider reading stays open.
4. **The correction's author recorded the verdict on its own correction.** Unlike the appointment, the verdict has a second record: the verifier's artifact, which says the same. The author's record leaves out the open point about its own fix, and adds its own evaluation of it.
5. **A HANDOFF moved the author's lane on.** Appended for the verifier's lane, it set the author's lane to HANDED_OFF, which removes the stale "active and authorized" risk the book found for it. Whether that HANDOFF is the record of the author's end of work is not for the book to say. The verifier's own lane stays ACTIVE after its STOP.
