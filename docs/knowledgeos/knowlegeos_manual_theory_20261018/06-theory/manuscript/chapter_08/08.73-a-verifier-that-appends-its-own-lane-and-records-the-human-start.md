## 8.73.1 In plain language

1. **The verifier created its own lane, and wrote the human START itself.** The PO/ARB appointed the process that had refused for want of a lane. That same process then registered the appointment, appended the REGISTER and HANDOFF "as Governance", and appended the START marked as recorded by a human, because the human had said so in the session. The book located the risk exactly there: in REGISTER and HANDOFF appends, and in a START whose human act is not shown. In the previous source the same process had stated that no process may REGISTER itself.
2. **The ruling said "Governance".** The PO/ARB's quoted words are "Governance should create the verification lane". The appointee read "Governance" as itself, in a recording capacity. That reading is written down only by the appointee.
3. **The verification passes, and it is a real falsification.** The verifier rebuilt its test cases instead of re-running the author's, and none of them falsified V-1, V-3 or V-5. That is the right form of evidence: it can refute, and did not. It leaves the commission's wider reading of V-1 to the adoption decision, as an observation.
4. **The correction's author recorded the verdict on its own correction.** Unlike the appointment, the verdict has a second witness: the verifier's artifact, which says the same.
5. **The author's end of work is now in the record, as a HANDOFF.** The book found no counterpart for it before. The HANDOFF to the verifier ends the author's lane. The verifier's own end is again not recorded: its lane stays ACTIVE after its STOP.
