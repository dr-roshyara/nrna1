## 8.20.1 In plain language

Two of these sources finish reviewing the migration plan, and three start an automated checker for the defects the reviews kept finding. Five points come out.
1. A record held in quarantine blocks every act that would need it decided or would destroy it, until someone with authority disposes of it. The hold must be recorded on the container as a state. A comparison will not do, since moving the record out would make the comparison pass (C1, C9).
2. Without a shared clock, writes in one store come before an event in another only if that event names them, directly or through a chain. So a store switch must name the old store's last state (C2, C8).
3. A store cannot contain a full, current statement of its own final state, because writing the statement changes it. The closing record must live elsewhere (C6). And a gate before a removal must check the copy that survives, not only the thing removed (C7).
4. A checker that rediscovers defects already found shows that it reaches them. Fresh defects planted after it was built show that it detects new instances. Neither shows it can find a kind of defect nobody has named, and no checker of declared structure can see an act that was never declared (C12, C15).
5. The review that argued for automation counted as "mechanical" three findings that it says, elsewhere, no checker can find. Recounted, its trend is weaker (C19).

**C1. An undisposed quarantine is a hold: it blocks any removal of where it lives until a disposition act (Src; for CON-0221 and §9.13's tombstones).** `[CON-0250 · provisional]`
- **RD-3·a.** Criterion 12 requires the runtime source to equal "manifest + RECONCILED delta" before removal, and criterion 15 makes CASE β bytes "NOT reconciled — deliberately" (S0141 L118–119, in meaning). The safe form: "β does not gate on re-verification at all — it hands Phase 7 to governance and the demoted store is RETAINED until governance disposes of the quarantined record." (S0141 L130, in meaning).
- **RD-3·b.** "The artifact never names a quarantine destination." (S0141 L134, in meaning). "in place is exactly what Phase 7 removes." [S0141 L134]. The compliance argument is "sound for the moment of quarantine and is not yet established through Phase 7" (S0141 L136, in meaning).
- **Int (CON-0221; §9.13 tombstones; §8.19 C26).** CON-0221 says an unresolved status is "never laundered into a resolved one". S0141 shows what that requires of the acts that come **after**. A quarantined record holds an undecided status. Any act that needs it decided (here, removal gated on reconciliation) cannot pass, and any act that destroys it violates preservation. So the undecided item is a **hold** on everything that contains it. Only a **disposition act** by the competent authority releases the hold. *Deduction:* preservation pending a decision needs three things: a **place** that the destructive act does not touch, a **hold** that blocks that act, and a **disposition** act that ends the hold. In D only the **negative half** of the hold exists by construction: an entry is removed "only by a recorded act, by tombstone" (§9.15 G3, quoted), so nothing is destroyed without an act. A positive hold state, "undisposed here", is not a kind of D, and the place does not arise, since D has no stores to remove. *(Narrowed in the Professor's adversarial pass; the draft said the hold and the disposition "exist by construction".)* *Recorded; no kernel change.*
- *The source's own balance:* the unsatisfiable gate "fails in the safe direction (removal refused, nothing destroyed)" (S0141 L377, in meaning). So it is a clarification, not an impossibility.

**C6. A store cannot record its own final state; the closing record must lie outside the closed store (Src; Ded; for CON-0251).** `[CON-0252 · provisional]`

Written **pre-switch**, it lands in the source, so "the source now differs from the copy again ⇒ reconcile again … ⇒ NON-TERMINATING" (S0142 L223–227, in meaning). - DV-4: the load-bearing premise is not in the argument. A reconciliation is a mechanical write into the copy. - *Attack on the source.* "a store's final state cannot be recorded inside that store" is too strong as stated. A store **can** record its state **as of the entry before** the record, for example a hash of its prefix, which is how a hash chain commits to its own history. What it cannot record is a state that **includes** the recording entry. In the estate the problem arises because the rule (DV-4) requires every write to be evidenced by an append in the **same** store, and the closing comparison must include that append. *Scoped:* no store contains a complete, current statement of its own final state. A rule that every write is evidenced in its own store has no fixed point at a close. *Recorded; no kernel change. D has no stores to close, so the point concerns records about records (§8.18 C1).*

A mutation made **after** the checker exists, by a party who did not write it (here the specification's author, not the implementer), meets that condition for **instances** of known classes. It does not for **classes** nobody has named. *Deduction:* validation has three grades.
  1. Reach: known defects rediscovered (S0143).
  2. Detection of new instances: fresh mutations by a non-author (S0144).
  3. Detection of new classes, which no checker of declared structure can provide (S0143 L83).

  The book's planted-gap test is grade 2 for its reviewers, with the plants made by the Rule Reviewer. *CON-0253 amended with the grades.*
- *Limit:* three mutations are a purposive sample. They show the checker is content-driven, and they give no rate.

**C16. A checker that reads a quoted enumeration as a defined one: use is not mention (Src; evidence for CON-0238).** *(No new concept: evidence.)*
- V-3: "S2 CANNOT DISTINGUISH DEFINING AN ENUMERATION FROM QUOTING ONE — a systematic false-positive class over review artifacts" (S0144 L54, in meaning). Over 83 review documents it failed 9, including every AMD review, whose citations of the plan's step 5 are all legitimate. And it failed this very report: "it parsed an enumeration I QUOTED as one I DEFINED" (S0144 L54, in meaning). Root cause: "the rule needs to know whether a document is the DEFINING carrier of an enumeration or a citing one." (S0144 L54, in meaning).
- **Int (CON-0238; §8.13).** CON-0238 separates senses of reference and holds that no reference asserts its referent's correctness. V-3 is the checker's version: a document that **mentions** an enumeration (quotes, cites) does not **use** it (define it). A rule that reads every enumeration as a definition makes every faithful citation look like a dangling one. *Recorded.* The book's own quote checker treats quoted text as mention by construction, since it checks a quote against its source and never against the quoting document.

**C17. Mechanical checks see what review passed, and review sees what the checks cannot (Src; for CON-0253 and §8.20 C12).** *(No new concept: evidence.)*
- V-2: "FIVE LIVE TRUE POSITIVES, and they had survived every review that passed over them" (S0144 L53, in meaning). "The implementation design passed the Governance review and three independent Architecture reviews with a malformed table in it." (S0144 L53, in meaning). It is "the programme's first operational evidence that is not a back-test" (S0144 L53, in meaning).
- **Int (§8.20 C12; CON-0253).** S0143 found the checker blind to omitted acts, which only review catches. S0144 finds review blind to structural defects, which the checker catches. *Deduction:* the two are complementary, each blind where the other sees, so neither can stand in for the other. *A note on this book:* §8.18 also cited S0131 L131, the malformed row, and neither the window review nor the C25 settlement noticed. The book cited the row's content, so no claim is affected (§8.20 C14).

**C18. An identifier minted but not committed is invisible to every collision check (Src; evidence for §1.10 I-0 and CON-0233).** *(No new concept: evidence.)*
- V-4: `EKS-06` "IS ALREADY TAKEN — and the brainstorming document still assumes it is free" (S0144 L55, in meaning). The mint "is UNCOMMITTED (untracked …), so the mint is invisible to git and to any collision check" (S0144 L55, in meaning).

That is CON-0233's inversion (the record that outranks is the least durable) for identifiers. The authority of an allocation is only as good as the durability of its record.

**C20. Judging a work under a model changed after it was produced is the same error as an author judging its own work (Src; evidence for H3b and §9.15 K7).** *(No new concept: evidence.)*

**C21. Report first, route later: an output becomes an act when something acts on it (Src; evidence for CON-0236 and CON-0127).** *(No new concept: evidence.)*

CON-0127 says a recorded shared factor refutes independence and an empty record cannot establish it, "hence Declared / NOT ATTESTED, never PASS".
