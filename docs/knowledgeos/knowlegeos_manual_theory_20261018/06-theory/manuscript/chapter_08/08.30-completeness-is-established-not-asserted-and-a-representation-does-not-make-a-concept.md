## 8.30.1 In plain language

1. **"We did not need to read the rest" must be shown.** One review accepted "not needed" without reasons. A second check then found that the unread part contained facts that changed two of the reconstruction's main claims.
2. **A numbered sequence with no gaps does not prove nothing is missing.** The estate reproduced the loss itself: in its trials, the surviving records were still numbered without gaps.
3. **Writing and keeping a record does not make you its owner.** Reading the project's knowledge does not make you its owner either.
4. **A neat mathematical form is not a reason to add a concept.** The concept must come first, from the domain; the representation comes after.

## 8.30.2 Claims and derivations

**C1. Corpus completeness is established, not asserted (Src; evidence for CON-0120 and the book's whole-reading rule).** *(No new concept.)*
- The reconstruction left the migration plan's body unread and called it "not needed for current-state reconstruction" (S0198 L39, in meaning). The gate review calls this "a conclusion without the reasoning", and states the discipline: "corpus completeness is established, not asserted". Otherwise the next phase would consume "an artificial UNKNOWN" (S0198 L44–46, in meaning).
- The verification found that the unread body opens with a "Current-state inventory — OBSERVED, measured" (S0199 L50, in meaning). It contains facts that "materially affect" two load-bearing claims of the reconstruction. So the clause is "contradicted, not merely unestablished" (S0199 L56, L72, in meaning).
- **Int.**
  - This is CON-0120 applied to a reading, not a search: a claim that the unread part does not matter is an absence claim about it.
  - The book's own rule (v1.4 §3) says to skim only purely operational sources and to read the rest whole. It takes this as its premise.
  - The case also shows why the rule names the kind of source rather than a guess about relevance. The unread part was assumed to be target-state design, and it opened with a current-state inventory.

**C2. Density is not completeness; one writer is true of the code paths, not a structural guarantee (relayed by S0199; agreement with §8.18 C4 (a), C1 and C13).** *(No new concept.)*
- S0199 relays the migration plan: "DENSITY IS NOT A COMPLETENESS PROOF." The density is "preserved BY the loss, because the clobbering writer reuses the sequence number the lost writer took". It also relays a reproduction: "23 / 30 concurrent-append trials silently lost a transition … EVERY survivor was DENSE and MONOTONIC." (S0199 L60, in meaning)
- "One writer" is true "of the committed code paths today" and "false as a structural guarantee": a reader component can hand the record directory to a program named by an environment variable (S0199 L65, in meaning). The resolver invariant is "ENFORCEABLE for writers and tooling, and ADVISORY for ad-hoc reads" (S0199 L68, in meaning).
- **Int.**
  - In C25 the book found that the migration's dense-and-monotonic test passes the very loss it is meant to catch (§8.18 C4 (a), from S0131 L121). The estate's own reproduction, as relayed, agrees. *The lineage is shared (S0131 and the migration plan belong to one track), so this is agreement within the line, now with a reported measurement. The reproduction is not shown here.*
  - "One writer is not one record" (§8.18 C1) and "the resolver is advisory for readers outside it" (§8.18 C13) are the same findings, restated by the estate after the reconstruction had dropped them.
  - The reconstruction repeated a claim that its own corpus had already withdrawn. This is an instance of §8.26 C2: a summary can misstate its source.

**C3. Operating or producing a record is not owning it; consuming knowledge is not owning it (Src; evidence for §1.14.3 and §8.28 C1).** *(No new concept.)*
- The gate review's annotation: "AIP mechanically operates / maintains / produces records ≠ AIP is the authoritative domain owner of the knowledge or authority itself" (S0198 L62–64, in meaning). And: "AIP consumes knowledge ≠ AIP owns knowledge ≠ AIP is the KnowledgeOS" (S0198 L83–87, in meaning).
- The verification judged the second annotation "NOT CONFIRMED as a defect": the reconstruction already listed knowledge governance under "does NOT own" (S0199 L142, in meaning).
- **Int.**
  - The first distinction is §1.14.3's role law (a recorder does not decide) and ARCH-INV-1 (the mechanism records authority, it does not grant it), stated about a platform.
  - The second is §8.28 C1's "the kernel owns the mechanism, not the domain meaning", seen from the consumer's side.

**C4. A phase that consumes its own unreviewed output confirms itself; a review is itself verified (Src; evidence for §8.06 and §8.18 C21).** *(No new concept.)*
- Version 1 of the launch plan, which combined two phases in one session, was rejected: "an unreviewed AIP interpretation consumed by the same session's landscape would create a self-confirming architecture interpretation" (S0197 L6, in meaning). The next phase runs only after review and acceptance (S0197 L7, in meaning).
- The gate review's findings were then verified by an independent session against a seven-step method. One step is "do not resolve an architectural question merely because the finding appears plausible" (S0199 L22, in meaning). The verification:
  - **upgraded** P3-F1 to "contradicted" (C1);
  - **downgraded** P3-F4 to "not a defect";
  - judged P3-F3's "almost every" a hedge relative to the 6/6 enumeration (S0199 L117, in meaning);
  - found a wrong section label in the review (S0199 L93, in meaning).
- If a session ends before its record is complete, "the reviewing session completes bookkeeping and records the anomaly", and "the deliverable text is never altered by the reviewing session" (S0197 L77, in meaning).
- **Int (§8.06; §8.18 C21).** This is the structure this book uses, a draft, an independent review, and a settlement that verifies the review's findings, found in the estate. The review was itself checked, and it was wrong in both directions. That is evidence for checking a review's findings before applying them (the "V (Professor)" column of each settlement), not for accepting them on the reviewer's authority.
- *Recorded:* the review is again headed "Reviewer: Human Principal Architect (performed via reviewing session)" (S0198 L8); compare §8.29 C3.

**C5. An elegant representation does not make a concept; semantic state and authority state are different dimensions (Src; evidence for §3.2 and §1.14; attack on the source's model).** *(No new concept.)*
- S0200 proposes "K = S + iA", with S the semantic state and A the authority state, on the ground that "What something says and whether the organization is authorized to treat it as true are different dimensions" (S0200 L36–46, in meaning). It warns: "We should not create a domain concept merely because mathematics gives us an elegant representation." [S0200 L394]. It proposes the idea as a research hypothesis with a falsifier: "If no, discard the model." [S0200 L448]
- **Int (§1.14; §3.2).** That what a claim says and whether it has authority are different is §1.14 (state is not authority). §3.2's dimension test applies to the source's five-component vector (S0200 L311–334): each component must be orthogonal to the others, and the set must be necessary and sufficient. The source asserts the components; it does not test them.
- **Attack (Ded, the Professor's).** The complex form brings operations the domain does not have:
  - In the book, authority is the value of CE*: one of T, F or U for an act at a cut (§9.13). That is a three-valued answer, not a magnitude. So "A" has no addition and no scale, and ΔK = ΔS + iΔA (S0200 L229–239) is undefined on it.
  - Multiplication of complex numbers mixes the two axes (i² = −1). No reading is offered for that, and one would be needed before the form could carry meaning.
  - The source's own conflict split, semantic disagreement versus authority disagreement (S0200 L257–263), can be stated without arithmetic. The book already has its authority side: a positional conflict is not yet a conflict of authority (CON-0240, §8.18 C3).

  So the representation fails the source's own test (L394) at its first step. This is recorded as an attack. The hypothesis is not adopted, and it is not refuted for magnitudes the source might define later (confidence, evidence strength).
