## 8.14.1 In plain language

A record that says "this was delivered at time T" never becomes false. Later events can only change what the record is good enough for, so withdrawing reliance on it is a decision about one purpose, not a correction of the past. A dispute is a separate flag that can sit on a record in any state. Raising one is cheap and resolving one is expensive, so who may raise a dispute has to be settled together with what the dispute suspends.

The authority declined to declare a capability existent when none of its own activities had ever occurred. Deciding to enforce a rule is itself a decision about what the rule's owner is. And "there is no record of it" counts as evidence only when the store the record would be in actually exists and was read.

## 8.14.2 Claims and derivations

**C1. Existence is not declared from a decided boundary alone (Src; evidence for §8.12 C7's performance limb).** `[CON-0208 · provisional]`
- S0111 recommended YES. Its "load-bearing argument" was that a decision fixing the receipt's authoritative claim "presupposes a subject for that artifact" (S0111 L46, in meaning), with "realization NONE" (S0111 L44, in meaning).
- The authority decided otherwise: "D2 = `NOT YET ESTABLISHED`" (S0112 L12, in meaning). Of the capability's five activities, "one occurs and is owned elsewhere; four do not occur at all" [S0111 L21].
- **Int (§8.12 C7).** §8.12 C7's sufficient condition has two limbs: an adopted invariant presupposes the capability, **and** the capability is performed. Here the first limb alone was argued and not accepted. So this is one instance of the estate requiring performance, not presupposition alone. It is consistent with C7 and does not show that performance is necessary.
- **A decision made while an open question stays open.** S0111 argued that existence could be decided now because its answer is the same under every resolution of the open scope question: "Existence survives either resolution" [S0111 L54]. *Int:* this is a decision by invariance over the open question's resolutions. The authority did not adopt the conclusion, so the sources say nothing about whether it accepted the form of the argument.

**C2. Enforcement decides category; a decision can be kept non-constitutive (Src; for §9.16 R3a).** `[CON-0209 · provisional]`
- "Selecting HALT or BLOCK for any tier decides the category by consequence." [S0113 L18]. The source rests this on the analysis: advisory today, "control-plane if it gates START" (S0113 L16, in meaning).
- A boundary specification framed as constitutive "would decide existence by the back door" [S0112 L14]. The remedy is a conditional form: the capability "would own the receipt lifecycle and state described above if and when C-10 is realized" (S0112 L69, in meaning).
- And: "enable any enforcing behaviour in the same act that defines it" is the one move Governance would not make (S0113 L91, in meaning).
- **Int (§9.16 R3a).** R3a chose prevention or detection per law by whether the effect can be undone. S0113 adds a structural consequence: choosing prevention puts the law's guard in the control path, which is itself a decision about where the law lives. For the kernel, this is what §9.16 R4 derived for single use: prevention needs a coordination point.

**C3. A historical claim never becomes false; invalidation withdraws reliance for a purpose (Src; compares with §9.13).** `[CON-0210 · provisional]`
- The receipt's claim "is a time-stamped historical fact, so it never becomes FALSE — only INSUFFICIENT or UNRELIED-UPON" [S0114 L15].
- So "STALE, SUPERSEDED and INVALIDATED do not contradict the receipt; they change what it is sufficient FOR" (S0114 L16, in meaning). The source's three-way cut:
  - **superseded** is a fact about the knowledge version, "act-independent";
  - **stale** is "relational", the receipt's sufficiency for one act;
  - **invalidated** is "an authority act" (S0114 L64–69, in meaning).
- Invalidation "cannot be 'reversed'": the real question is "whether RELIANCE may be restored" (S0114 L80, in meaning). And "invalidation is PURPOSE-SCOPED, not global" [S0114 L85].
- One instance: the reclassified verification kept its findings as evidence and lost only its use as assurance (S0115 L54, in meaning).
- **Int (§9.13).** CE* already separates the record of an act from its standing: an entry stays on record, and annulment, revocation and endings change only values. What CE* lacks is **purpose**. An annulment voids an act for every question, while S0114's invalidation withdraws one use and keeps another. Whether the kernel needs purpose-scoped standing is open (§8.14.3).

**C4. A dispute is a flag orthogonal to the lifecycle, and its effect needs a gate on who may raise it (Src; compares with §9.13's marks).** `[CON-0211 · provisional]`
- "DISPUTED is a FLAG over the lifecycle state, not a terminal state within it." [S0114 L19]. A receipt may be valid, superseded, invalidated or missing, and disputed as well (S0115 L60, in meaning).
- The effect proposed: "A dispute suspends the receipt's use FOR THE DISPUTED CLAIM ONLY." [S0115 L71].
- The asymmetry: a dispute is "cheap to enter, expensive to exit" (S0114 L96, in meaning). So "The effect and the "who may raise" gate must be decided together." [S0115 L76].
- "A dispute cannot make an unenforceable condition enforceable." [S0115 L78].
- **Int (§9.13).** CE*'s marks have the same shape: they are orthogonal to the value and change no value. A dispute that only marks would be safe against the asymmetry. One that suspends use changes standing, and then the raiser's authority must be evaluated, as for any act that changes standing. This is the kernel form of S0115's "decided together".

**C5. Absence is evidence only in a realized store (Src; bears on §9.13's C1 and §9.15 L4).** `[CON-0212 · provisional]`
- "absence of a record in an UNREALIZED store is not evidence of absence" [S0114 L27]. A retrieval failure "must NEVER be reported as M1", the missing case (S0114 L29, in meaning), since that "laundders an infrastructure failure into a governance fact" (S0114 L29, in meaning; the source's spelling).
- No enforcement on "an inference from absence alone" (S0115 L33, in meaning), and M1, the missing case, "is that inference" (S0115 L33, in meaning).
- A present receipt that cannot be bound to its session is NOT ATTESTED, never MISSING: calling it missing "asserts "no receipt", which the evidence does not support" (S0114 L34, in meaning).
- **Int (§9.13 C1; §9.15 L4).** CE*'s current-authority gives F when an item has no adoption on record. That F is an inference from absence, and §9.13 rests it on L4 (the record is complete). S0114 sharpens the premise. The F holds only if the record **is** the authoritative store and was **read**. A failed read must not yield F: it is a refusal, not an answer. CE* has no input for a failed read, so a Q1 implementation must keep "the record was not read" apart from "the record has no adoption". *Proposed for the next §9.13 revision; not applied in this window.*

**C6. The binding of a record to its subject is not attestable in the estate today (Src; bears on §9.15 L10).** *(No concept: an observation of the estate's state.)*
- "The platform cannot attest "a specified session execution."" [S0112 L20]. So identity binding "must NOT be placed inside C-10's authoritative state" (S0112 L24, in meaning). Even the missing-receipt check "cannot verify that a presented receipt belongs to the session presenting it" (S0113 L44, in meaning).
- **Int (§9.16 R4a; §9.15 L10).** (C + O)'s guard rests on L10: the kernel authenticates the application that submits an entry. These sources record that the estate cannot attest the identity of a session. They are about sessions, not applications, so they do not refute L10. But they show that L10 is a **real world assumption, unmet for the nearest analogue in the estate today**, not a formality. G4 under (C + O) holds only where L10 is met.

**C7. Prevention can stop everything when its condition is universal (Src; a parallel to F22's cost).** *(No concept: an observation.)*
- While no receipt exists, "Every session, for every required context version, is in state `Missing`." [S0113 L40]. So "Any blocking response to Missing halts the entire estate on the day it is enabled." [S0113 L42].
- An enforcing response needs a defined trigger, an authoritative trigger, an evidence source and an existing authority: "No row satisfies all four." [S0115 L25].
- A warning on every act "would be noise, not notification" (S0115 L84, in meaning).
- **Int (§9.16 R4a).** The same shape as (C + O)'s cost and §8.13 C6: prevention trades availability, and when the guarded condition holds everywhere the trade is total.

## 8.14.3 Open questions

- Does the kernel need **purpose-scoped standing**, so that reliance can be withdrawn for one use and kept for another (C3)?
- Should a dispute be a **mark** (no effect on standing), or an act that suspends use, with its raiser's authority evaluated (C4)?
- How does Q1 report a **failed read** of the record, as against an absent adoption (C5)?
- Is "the answer is the same under every resolution of the open question" an accepted form for deciding under an open question (C1)?
