## 8.93.1 In plain language

1. **The first answer says the commitment belongs to KnowledgeOS and that the kernel admits claims; the replies split this into who asserts, who may admit, and who keeps the result consistent.** Within the book's record the split is right for admitting and maintaining: an authorized person's recorded act admits, and the kernel evaluates it. Asserting and judging are separate roles in the book too, though the record has no field for who asserts an item's content. *(Restated at review: W-03, W-05)*
2. **The first answer says identity is given by the kernel at admission, never before, so as not to create identities for claims later refused.** Within the record an item can carry its identifier and entries before anyone adopts it; what has no trace is a refused input, not an identity. Whether changing a claim's content makes a new claim is open in the book too.
3. **The first answer says the state is worked out from events and not stored, but also that the kernel keeps an "assessment result" it did not make.** Within the record, for standing the reply is right: the kernel keeps an authorized person's decision without redoing its reasoning, and works out standing from it. For the evidence class the kernel works the result out itself, by a stated rule. *(Restated at review: W-02)*
4. **The replies separate four things behind "contested": a relation, an event, a derived state and a governance determination.** Within the record one recorded act is both the event and the determination, and the status is derived; raising a challenge and the relation between two claims have no kind yet. *(Restated at review: W-07)*
5. **The second reply says that when each lens is used as a vote the answers cannot converge, so lenses should produce questions and test cases, and the decision should come from invariants.** The book derives no result from a lens. Its test of evidence that supports a hundred claims agrees with the reply: within what the record holds, each piece of evidence is recorded per item, so no single large boundary is forced. *(Restated at review: W-08, W-09)*
6. **The replies list cases where something is missing.** Within the record most are answered: a candidate refused leaves no trace; evidence invalidated changes the class, not standing; a claim with no evidence has standing if an authorized act gives it and is shown as not attested; the evaluator always answers on a record in its domain. *(Restated at review: W-10)*
