## 8.72.1 In plain language

1. **The correction changed the canonical rule document, and its header still names the producer.** The correction's own file list includes the boundary proposal (S0324), edited in three places. The header still says it was registered by the producing session, and the status still says "self-verified". The question the book left undecided at §8.71 can now be answered from an allowed source's text: S0324, as read, is the producer's text with the correction's lines in it.
2. **The fix to "recorded human start act" changed what the field means, not what it is called.** It now reports whether the lane is ACTIVE now. A lane that started and then completed reports "no human start act". That error only ever withholds permission, so it is safe; but the name says something the field no longer measures.
3. **The appointee's identity was "verified" only after START, by its own run.** The correction's evidence reports the lane RESOLVED, ACTIVE, with an identity MATCH. That is the self-run check the book said holds by construction. And the record shows who appended REGISTER only as a role, "governance", so whether the appointee registered its own lane cannot be read from it.
4. **The author stopped, but the record did not.** The completion report says the session cannot continue and is not authorized. No STOP was recorded in the workflow store, and the lane is still ACTIVE, which the mechanism reads as authorized. The stop exists only in the commission's prose and in the report.
5. **The would-be verifier refused, correctly.** Its prompt said the lane was started; the record showed only the author's lane. It read the record over the prompt, and it refused to act without a lane of its own. It also noticed, independently of the book, that two records disagree about who verified the resolver before.
