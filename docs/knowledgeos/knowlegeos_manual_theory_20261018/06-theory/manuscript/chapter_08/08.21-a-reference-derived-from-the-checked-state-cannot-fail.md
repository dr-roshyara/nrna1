## 8.21.1 In plain language

This window closes the stretch before the S0150 consolidation. Four points come out.
1. A check is only as good as what it compares against. The reference must be fixed before the act it checks, must include every change the result should carry, and must come from the other side. A reference built from the result itself cannot fail (C1).
2. A tool that is implemented and verified is not thereby adopted, which is the other half of "adopted is not verified". And an aggregate of three-valued checks must not depend on the order in which they run (C8, C9).
3. A disputed figure from one source reappears in the next as "Observed", with a target anchored to it. A report's own instruction says "PASS" where its own table shows "INCONCLUSIVE" (C10, C11).
4. A ledger kept by its own author proves authorship and priority only inside the estate. To an outsider it needs a commitment held elsewhere and trusted time (C14).

Two sources were skimmed: a role diagram and an architecture proposal whose every point is already in the book (C7, C13).

**C1. A check whose reference is derived from the state it validates cannot fail: the reference must be closed before the act, and from the other side (Src; Ded; for CON-0243).** `[CON-0255 · provisional]`
- HO-1, on an unregistered authoring handoff: its sequence closes the enumeration **after** the final copy-side write it is meant to validate (S0146 L192–199, in meaning). The plan closes it **before**, and the write takes it as its target, "bring the durable copy to that state" (S0146 L207, in meaning). The source states the risk "at exactly its strength": "IF construct/close were read as deriving the object from the state produced by the write it is meant to validate, the check CANNOT FAIL." (S0146 L220, in meaning). But "The enumeration describes the SOURCE, so the strict reading survives" (S0146 L220, in meaning).
- TRAP 1: re-verifying against the **frozen manifest** "FAILS IN THE NORMAL CASE", since the manifest predates the reconciled delta and the pre-switch records (S0146 L54, in meaning). So the repair would "reproduce DV-2's defect inside DV-1's repair" (S0146 L54, in meaning).
- **Int (CON-0243; §8.20 C7, C11).** CON-0243 requires the gate before an irreversible act to compare the survivor against the reference. S0146 shows two conditions on **which** reference.
  - **Too early:** a reference fixed before the changes the survivor is meant to carry fails in the normal case. That is TRAP 1.
  - **Derived from the checked object:** a reference derived after the act, from the very state the act produced, cannot fail, because the check compares a thing with itself. That is HO-1.

  *Deduction:* the reference must be **closed before the act it validates**, must **cover every change the survivor is meant to carry**, and must be **derived from the other side**, here the source, not the copy. The third condition is what keeps the plan's own reading sound. It is §8.20 C11's "self-satisfying" bound claim, recurring in the choice of a reference.
- *Rival explanation.* HO-1 is a sequencing slip, not a principle: any competent implementer would read "construct" as "take from the source". *Answer:* the source itself states both readings, and its deduction rests on which one the text fixes. A principle that a careful reading usually satisfies is still a principle.
- *Falsifier.* A check whose reference is derived **only** from the checked object after the act, and which still detects some defect in the act. Unless the derivation adds information from elsewhere, that is impossible by construction. So the falsifier is a derivation that turns out to read an independent input. In that case the concept's third condition is met, not refuted.

### S0148: Track-2 Phase-1 author-side adoption, evidence report

*S0148 is the deliverable of the Phase-1 commission, under a plan "EP-01 APPROVED (Decision Authority, 2026-08-21, recorded 58e758b0)" (S0148 L7, in meaning). It names no producing process. Its epistemic classes are "Observed · Estimated · Unknown. Not mixed." (S0148 L13, in meaning).*

**C8. A fail-closed aggregate is strong-Kleene conjunction, and it must be a function of the set, not the order (Src; agreement with §9.13).** *(No new concept: evidence.)*
- The report's aggregate is "fail-closed … FAIL > INCONCLUSIVE > WARN > PASS" (S0148 L37, in meaning). A repair was "found by the fail-closed CLI test (an INCONCLUSIVE slice preceding a FAIL could mask it; now position-independent)" (S0148 L39, in meaning).
- **Int (§9.13).** §9.13, quoted: "A three-valued assignment gives each atom T, F or U. The information order: U is below T and below F. Strong Kleene connectives". Under conjunction, F ∧ U = F and T ∧ U = U. So with PASS = T, FAIL = F and INCONCLUSIVE = U, the estate's precedence **is** strong-Kleene conjunction, with WARN as an added level between U and T. The masking bug was an aggregate computed over the **sequence** of slices. Conjunction is commutative, so the repair restores what CE* has by construction: the verdict depends on the set of slice verdicts, not their order. *Agreement reached independently.*

**C9. Implemented and verified is not adopted (Src; the converse of CON-0231).** *(No new concept: evidence.)*
- "This is not an organizational-adoption declaration." (S0148 L27, in meaning). The status is "PHASE 1 ADOPTION IMPLEMENTED / VERIFIED" as the commission prescribes, and "whether it becomes the organization's standing author-side obligation is the separate human authorization §7 requests. This report adopts nothing." (S0148 L27, in meaning).
- **Int (CON-0231).** CON-0231: adopted is not verified correct. S0148 is the other direction: verified working is not adopted. Adoption is an act of the competent authority, and verification supplies evidence for it. Neither implies the other. *Agreement.*

### S0150: an IP-protection brainstorm

*S0150 is an unattributed brainstorm, an adviser's reply to the human, "authoritative: false", "proposed: false" (S0150 L13–14, in meaning). **C14. An internal ledger does not prove priority without an external anchor and trusted time (Src; attack on the source; for §9.15 K1, L6 and CON-0243).** *(No new concept: evidence.)*
- The claim: decision records, session logs and commit history "create evidence of authorship" (S0150 L112–118, in meaning). An "IP Creation Ledger" with a date and a hash means that "later you can prove: "This concept existed before the other party claimed it."" (S0150 L248–273, in meaning).
- *Attack on the source.* A ledger the claimant keeps and can rewrite proves nothing to a party who does not trust the claimant.
  - **Dates:** the entry's date is the claimant's word. This is L6, trusted time, which §8.17 C6 found vacuous in this estate (2 of 216 transitions dated).
  - **Hashes:** a hash proves that some content existed when the hash was **committed somewhere the claimant cannot alter**. That is CON-0243 and K1's "with an external anchor, yes". Without such a commitment, an internal hash can be produced at any later time.
  - **Commit history** is rewritable in the claimant's own repository (§8.17 C9).
  - **Authorship:** for authorship, CON-0196's classes apply. A commit correlates a write with an account, which is AUTHORED at best, while a session log's self-declared identity is ASSERTED.
  - *Scoped:* the records are evidence of authorship **within** the estate. Against an adversary they need an external timestamp or commitment.
- **Int.** This is the book's trust boundary turned outward. What the record shows is internal (§9.15 G1–G6). What it proves to an outsider needs the world assumptions, here L6 and an anchor of K1's kind.

**C15. The DDD test for a bounded context, stated a second time and differently (Src; for CON-0085).** *(No new concept: evidence; a tension recorded.)*
- The adviser cautions against turning "useful concept → immediately turned into a bounded context" (S0150 L575, in meaning), and gives a test: does the candidate have its own "ubiquitous language · lifecycle · invariants · authority · reasons to change · independently owned state" (S0150 L584–589, in meaning)? "If not, it probably shouldn't become another bounded context." (S0150 L592, in meaning).
- **Int (CON-0085).** CON-0085 admits a seam as a bounded context "only if supported by one or more of nine criteria". S0150's test is six questions, and its "If not" leaves open whether one, several or all must hold. So the two statements of the admission rule differ in content (six against nine) and possibly in form (one-or-more against all). *Recorded as a tension; not resolved here.* S0150 is a non-authoritative brainstorm, and CON-0085's source governs as the book's home. The separation of "protect KnowledgeOS" from "KnowledgeOS as an IP-governance product" as two goals (S0150 L496–499, in meaning) is the operating loop's "business first" applied to scope.
