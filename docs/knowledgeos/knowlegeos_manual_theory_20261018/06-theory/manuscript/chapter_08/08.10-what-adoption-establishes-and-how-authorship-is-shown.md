## 8.10.1 In plain language

Adopting a model of roles organises who does what. It says nothing about which capabilities exist, and the act says so itself. A verification that was true when made is not disproven by a later decision that changes the world; it is superseded, and the old answer stays as it was. Whether a capability exists and which kind of thing it is are two questions, and the answer to one cannot be derived from the other. "Authoritative" needs an object: authoritative for which claim. And when a verifier could not be excluded by its identifier, its authorship could still be shown from the record of who wrote the file. That evidence shows who wrote an artifact; it does not show that a process was separate.

## 8.10.2 Claims and derivations

**C1. Adopting a model is not evidence that a capability exists (Src; an extension of §8.4 C4).**
- The PO/ARB adopts "the six-role model as the AI Engineering Platform's engineering operating model" [S0093 L11], and the registration calls the non-equivalences "the load-bearing half of this act" [S0093 L26]: adoption does not establish role ≠ bounded context, capability, agent, platform service or organisational position (S0093 L28–36, in meaning). "⛔ **The adopted role model must not be used as proof that a capability or bounded context exists.**" [S0093 L46].
- **Both directions:** the verification grant already forbade using "a role title as evidence that a capability exists", and "This act closes the other: **adoption is not that evidence either.**" [S0093 L48].
- **Adoption of a model is not adoption of a document:** the untracked 925-line role document "is **not adopted by this act.** **Adoption of the model is not adoption of that document.**" [S0093 L55]. And "⛔ **Do NOT retroactively convert historical brainstorming documents into governed architecture.**" [S0093 L53].
- **Int (A1; §5.6):** an act confers standing on exactly what it names. Here the object is a model of responsibilities, not a claim about the domain and not any document that contains the model. So the act's object is fixed by the act, and the act states its own limits.

**C2. Superseded, not disproven: a finding is true of its time (Src; an extension of §1.16.6 and §9.8).** `[CON-0156 · provisional]`
- After the adoption, the thesis a verification was commissioned to test, that the model "is not currently established as governed architecture", is "**SUPERSEDED** by the adoption decision" (S0094 L75, in meaning). "**Superseded, not disproven.** The claim was true of the estate when Verification #2 examined it; a later decision changed the world, not the finding." [S0094 L77].
- "⛔ **DO NOT DELETE OR REWRITE THE HISTORICAL VERIFICATION RESULT.**" [S0094 L79]. "Supersession governs **what is to be verified next**; it does not edit what was found before." [S0094 L81].
- **Int (§9.8):** a finding is a statement about the state at the time it was made: in §9.8's terms, a value of state history at (t, s) with t = s = the time of the finding. A later act changes the state from then on, so it makes a new question, not a false old answer. Overwriting the old answer would turn a change of the world into a correction of the record (§1.12).

**C3. Whether a capability exists and which kind it is are two questions (Src; for the boundary questions).** `[CON-0153 · provisional]`
- "**Separate capability EXISTENCE from capability CATEGORY.**" [S0096 L13]. Existence takes "**YES · NO · CONTESTED · NOT YET ESTABLISHED**" [S0096 L17]; category, "bounded context · cross-context capability · stewardship · control-plane function · another explicitly justified category" [S0096 L18]. "⛔ **Do not derive one answer from the other.**" [S0096 L20].
- The error it corrects: "It has no aggregate of its own, therefore it is not a capability" is "a **category** test doing an **existence** test's work" [S0096 L22]. In the concrete case: "Absence of a `C-5`-owned aggregate may disqualify a separate bounded context but must NOT be used as proof that the capability itself does not exist." (S0096 L28, in meaning).
- **Int (RESEARCH-PURPOSE; §9.6, §9.7):** this is the separation the book's boundary test already makes between "is the capability necessary?" and "must the kernel provide it?". A failed placement test does not show that a capability is unnecessary, and a necessary capability need not have a home of its own. S0096 states the same rule one level down, for bounded contexts. *Two answers the boundary test can use:* CONTESTED and NOT YET ESTABLISHED, beside yes and no.

**C4. "Authoritative for what claim?" (Src; an extension of §9.5 C4 and the homonym of §6.6, §6.7 C6).** `[CON-0154 · provisional]`
- The context lifecycle is split into seven states: "`ContextPublished` · `ContextSelected` · `ContextDelivered` · `ContextAvailable` · `ContextAcknowledged` · `ContextApplied` · `ContextVerified`" [S0096 L34]. "⛔ **Receipt is not proof of application or compliance.**" [S0096 L35].
- The question is reformulated from "is receipt authoritative?" to "**authoritative for WHAT CLAIM?**", across "**delivery · possession · applicability · execution · compliance** authority" (S0096 L37, in meaning). "The old form invited a single authority claim to cover five different assertions." [S0096 L38].
- **Int:** "authoritative" is a relation between an artifact and a kind of claim, not a property of the artifact. Read that way, the homonym of §6.6 and §6.7 C6 ("authoritative state" as the canonical source of a state) is one value of the relation: authoritative for the claim "this is the current state". It is not standing conferred by an act (§1.14). *One source.*

**C5. Authorship shown by provenance, not by identifier (Src; for fork F32).** `[CON-0155 · provisional]`
- The registration of the third verification had judged one exclusion unenforceable: a report "**declares no session identifier for itself**" [S0094 L41], and "**A bar that cannot be checked cannot clear it.**" [S0094 L45].
- The verification found the premise false: "🔴 `50d55d26` is **not a session identifier at all**. It is a **git commit hash**." [S0092 L50]; "the exclusion was never missing an identifier — **it was being sought in the wrong namespace.**" [S0092 L62].
- It established the author by two routes: a write-class tool call on the file in exactly one session's transcript, and that session's own written disclosure [S0092 L66–69]. Two cautions: "**Window overlap alone proves nothing**" [S0092 L72], and "**A transcript search for authorship is self-contaminating; only the originating `tool_use` block is evidence.**" [S0092 L74].
- Its limits, stated by the verifier: the method "requires transcript retention, covers only tool-mediated acts, is **self-contaminating if searched naively**", and "attests **an artifact's authorship**, never **a process's separation**" [S0092 L350].
- **Int (§8.6 C1; §8.9 C2, F32):** F32 asks what a verifier's eligibility is relative to. Here it was decided by the **relation to the object**: authorship of the protected artifact, shown by the record of the act of writing it. Identifiers failed in both directions in this window: one producer declared none, and another declared two (§8.9 C10). *One case;* it does not decide F32, and it is evidence for the relational option.

**C6. Access evidence is short of corroboration (Src; an extension of §8.6, information lineage).**
- Whether the discovery reconstructed an unread document or converged with it independently was classed OPEN. The verifier measured access: the discovery lane's "first tool-mediated access to the 925-line document occurred AFTER both the discovery and Amendment 1 were delivered" (S0092 L175, in meaning).
- It keeps the class: "⛔ **This does NOT discharge `INV-ATTR-2`, and I do not upgrade the classification to `CLOSED`.**" [S0092 L179], because it "cannot exclude: exposure conveyed by the human operator · exposure in a different session" (S0092 L179, in meaning).
- **Int (§8.6 C1):** timing evidence can refute tool-mediated access; it cannot establish information independence, because information can reach a process by channels the record does not see. The verdict "OPEN, with the access evidence recorded" keeps the evidence and the class apart.

**C7. Six kinds of separation, and the single operator (Src; an extension of §8.6).**
- The amendment names "process separation · access separation · artifact isolation · independent execution · **organizational independence** · external attestation" (S0096 L30, in meaning), and: "⛔ **Do not treat organizational independence as established in a single-operator environment.**" [S0096 L31].
- A candidate rule, explicitly PROPOSED: "*A party subject to a governed control may not be the sole authority for issuing, accepting, or finalizing the control's verdict about its own compliance, separation, or conformance.*" [S0096 L51].
- **Int (§8.6 C1):** §8.6's seven factors are about the check and its information; S0096's six add properties of the environment (access, artifacts, organisation, an outside attestor). In a single-operator estate, organisational independence and external attestation are absent by construction, so every independence claim here is relative to the other four at most. The proposed rule is producer ≠ verifier generalised to every governed control; *Hyp, one source, not adopted.*

**C8. Three kinds of dependency (Src; supports §6.7 C5, CON-0147).**
- "Distinguish **semantic** dependency · **evidence** dependency · **implementation** dependency. ⛔ **Do not present build order as architectural ownership.**" (S0096 L55–56, in meaning).
- **Int (§6.7 C5):** this is the kind of typing §6.7 C5 asks for before the direction law can be tested. The law itself does not yet say which of the three kinds it is about; that has to be stated before a view can test it. *One source, the same programme.*

**C9. Instances bearing on earlier sections (Src).**
- **The third off-record verification in the estate.** The third verification's lane "has no `START` on the record"; "the human PO/ARB's `START` act **did occur** … but **no seq-15 transition records it.**" (S0092 L35, in meaning). It is the "**Second occurrence at the "Verification #3" slot specifically**" (S0092 L238, in meaning), after §5.7 C2's case. S0095 (§9.8, M2) records how the missing START was later transcribed at a later position. So the book now has three cases: §8.6 C3 (no lane), §5.7 C2 (registered, never activated, closed), and this one (registered, handed off, never started, then transcribed).
- **Delivery ahead of the record:** a correction "finished its work and said so in the artifact, but **its lane never handed off**" [S0094 L25]; "the work is real; the record lags it. It is disclosed, not smoothed." (S0094 L27, in meaning). An instance of §5.7 C1's duty half.
- **The observation point worked:** a recount differed from the correction's count "by exactly the governed work performed in between, with every delta attributable" (S0092 L143, in meaning). §8.9 C6's rule, tested once.
- **Corrections that reach the row but not the blocks that quote it:** the residue of a propagation failure "is in the *retention* markers rather than the live rows" (S0092 L213, in meaning). §8.9 C5's case, a fourth time.
- **The strength rule, stated in the corpus:** the verifier rates two residuals low because "Rating them MATERIAL would make the strongest statement exceed the evidence" (S0092 L399, in meaning). It is the book's own final rule (the project's "strongest statement must never exceed the strength of the available evidence"), found in the estate. *Not independent:* the estate and the book share the rule's wording.
- **Governed knowledge arriving outside the governed channel:** the cited review "is NOT present in this estate"; it is "the third external input in two days whose artifact is absent from the estate" (S0096 L60, L64, in meaning). The refinements are registered "on their own merits as PO/ARB instructions — not as findings inherited from a review" (S0096 L62, in meaning).
