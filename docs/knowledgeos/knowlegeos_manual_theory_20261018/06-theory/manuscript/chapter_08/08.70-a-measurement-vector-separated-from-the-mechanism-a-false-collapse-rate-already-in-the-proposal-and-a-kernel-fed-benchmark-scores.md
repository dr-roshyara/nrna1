## 8.70.1 In plain language

1. **The first layer is a literature review whose evidence cannot be checked from the file.** In its bibliography the title and the link of an entry often name different works, several quotations carry no citation, and one topic is rated both "Strong" and "Moderate". Its accuracy hypothesis, "74.2% → 85–90%" on a 120-case corpus, starts from a benchmark the book graded as a report without a transcript. It is the book's earlier projection pattern again.
2. **The second layer makes the proposal more modest, on the book's lines.** SNF is any normalization that passes tests, not one pipeline. A hash fingerprint shows that two representations are equal, not that two meanings are, and not that two pieces of knowledge are the same. The uncertainty of the interpreter stays on the interpreter's side. Its new "context" argument, though, may carry the knowledge base back in.
3. **The "missing" false-collapse rate was already in the proposal.** The first layer's non-collapse score counts the distinct pairs that stay apart. The new "false-collapse rate" counts the distinct pairs that do not: it is one minus the old score. The critique rejects the old score for its arbitrary threshold and then needs a threshold to define "collapse". What is genuinely new is the opposite error, equal meanings split apart, and a continuous separation measure.
4. **No single score: the metrics must each pass.** That agrees with the book: what blocks cannot be traded for what does not. But the selection rule uses the table's "Target" column while calling it "minimum". And it is not said how an abstention enters the error rates. A system that abstains on everything has no false collapse. Whether it fails depends on an abstention-precision criterion that has no formula. The gap the book recorded at §8.65 and §8.68 is narrowed only conditionally.
5. **The third layer agrees with everything and then draws the kernel deciding epistemic state from the metrics.** The metrics describe a mechanism over a test corpus. They cannot decide the state of one claim. The drawing contradicts the critique it endorses, the review's own table ("Truth: not calculated") and the first layer's "Do not design the kernel".

**C3. The "missing" false-collapse rate is the proposal's non-collapse complement; the threshold returns (Src; Ded; Int).**

- **Ded:**
  - **FAR_τ = 1 − NC_snf.** Over the same set D of distinct pairs, each pair has NSID > τ or NSID ≤ τ. So the fraction with NSID ≤ τ (FAR_τ) and the fraction with NSID > τ (NC_snf) sum to 1, if both count the same pairs. *(A's L190 sums over ordered pairs and divides by "distinct pairs", which may mean unordered pairs; read with the same count, the identity holds.)*

- **Ded:**
  - **The selection rule is the Target column.** L1476–1479 equal the Target column of L1483–1491, not the Minimum column, while L1476 calls 0.01 "(minimum)". A variant with FCR = 0.03 passes the table's minimum and fails the selection rule. *The two cannot both be the acceptance rule.*
  - **The joint vector excludes two degenerate maps** (W, step 1). A constant SNF map has FDR = 0, C_conv = 1, T_preserve = 1, but FCR = 1 and D_sep = 0. A map injective on surface strings has FCR = 0 and high D_sep, but FDR ≈ 1 and low C_conv. *No single coordinate excludes both; the conjunction does.*
  - **The trivial abstainer.** The source does not say how an abstention enters FCR, FDR, C_conv or D_sep. If abstained pairs count as neither collapse nor divergence (S0317's rule, §8.68 C3), a system that abstains on everything has FCR = FDR = 0. Then only A_abstain can fail it. Suppose A_abstain's precision is the share of abstentions on cases whose gold label is unknown, and a is the share of such cases in the corpus. The always-abstainer then has precision a, and fails "> 0.90" unless a > 0.90. A partial abstainer that abstains on all gold-unknown cases and on x others has precision a/(a + x), so "> 0.90" requires x < a/9.
- **Int:**
  - **No single score:** *agreement* with §5.4 C2 (criticality cannot be traded in a compensatory aggregate) and with §8.68 C3's hard gate, now extended from non-collapse to every coordinate.
  - **The gap narrows, conditionally.** §8.65 C3 and §8.68 C3 left open that the trivial abstainer passes. S0321's vector holds an abstention-precision criterion that would bound it, **if** the positive class is "gold unknown" and abstentions are measured over the whole corpus.   - **T^ambiguous has no metric.** T^alter is routed to D_sep or FCR (L1366); T^ambiguous appears only at L880–887. The class where abstention is right has no measurement.
  - **The thresholds.** S0317 called its thresholds "experimental — subject to calibration" (§8.68 C3). Layer C gives acceptance thresholds with no source and no such label. *A step back from the practice §8.63 C3 records.*
