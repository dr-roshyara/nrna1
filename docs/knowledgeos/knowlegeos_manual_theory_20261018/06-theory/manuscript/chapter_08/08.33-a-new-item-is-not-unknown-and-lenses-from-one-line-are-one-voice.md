## 8.33.1 In plain language

1. **The estate's lenses say a new item "begins as UNKNOWN".** In the book, an item with nothing recorded is "not recognized" (F), and "undetermined" (U) is kept for a record that cannot decide. For evidence the book shows "not attested", a third thing. Showing a new item as "unknown" would blur these.
2. **"The kernel does not compute authority" would reject the book's evaluator, if read literally.** The book's kernel works out current authority from recorded acts; what it never does is create authority. "Compute" means two things here.
3. **One prompt told the investigation what it would probably find, and the investigation found it.** The chat line also calls five lenses "converging", yet all five come from the same conversation.
4. **"A change in one dimension must not silently change another"** has one checked case in the book: evidence entries change no standing value or mark.
5. **"An invariant is a protection against a bad transformation"** is narrower than the book. The book's kernel also guarantees facts about its answers, and it detects some violations rather than preventing them.
6. **The estate ties its modal verbs to strength** ("shall" for established, "should" for candidates, "may" for hypotheses). The book ties strength to levels and labels instead.

## 8.33.2 Claims and derivations

**C1. A new item is "not recognized" in the book, not "unknown"; for evidence, "not attested" (Src; Ded against §9.13; Int).**
- **The sources:**
  - "A new knowledge object begins without: Authority, Evidence, Validation, Interpretation. It begins as: UNKNOWN", with "UNKNOWN ≠ Missing", "UNKNOWN = Known unresolved state" (S0210 L355–393; S0211 L353–388).
  - The Zero extraction's candidate H-ZERO-001: "KnowledgeOS should distinguish unresolved state from negative resolution" (S0208 L687).
  - S0211 renames that candidate "INV-CANDIDATE-004 UNKNOWN Preservation: Unresolved knowledge must remain explicitly unresolved" (S0211 §16, §20.1).
- **Ded, against §9.13:**
  - Under C1 = constitute, an item with no recorded adoption has current authority **F**: "refuted, or not recognized" (§9.13 R2a). That F "rests on the record's completeness" (L4), not on an undecided record.
  - **U** is kept for a record that does not decide: an undefined covers pair, a cycle, or a tie (§9.13 R2 (d)).
  - So the book resolves "nothing recorded" to F **by the Principal's decision C1**. It does not leave it unresolved.
- **For evidence**, the book's class for "nothing live" is **"not attested (in the record at s)"** (§9.18 g0). That is neither U nor "refuted".
- **Int:**
  - The estate's single "UNKNOWN" covers three things the book keeps apart: F (not recognized), U (undetermined) and "not attested". H-ZERO-001's demand, "unresolved ≠ negative resolution", is met for **evidence** ("not attested" ≠ "refuted"). For **authority** it is met only if "not recognized" is not shown as a negative resolution, which is the hazard T10-21 records.
  - S0210's own per-dimension example shows "Authority: Unassigned" (L420–421). That is closer to the book's F than its "UNKNOWN" is.
- **Not a new concept.** It sharpens T10-21 (1): Q1's F should be shown as "not recognized", and a new item should not be shown as "unknown".

**C2. "The kernel does not compute authority": a homonym of "compute" (Src; Int).**
- **S0208's extraction rejects** "Kernel calculates authority — Violates Authority ≠ Evidence; authority is governed, not computed" (S0208 L598), with "Kernel calculates truth" and "Kernel calculates confidence". Its prompt: "Kernel does not calculate knowledge … truth … confidence" (L446–448).
- **The book:** the kernel's evaluator CE* **computes current authority** from the recorded acts (§9.13). What the record never does is **manufacture** authority: "the record never manufactures authority" (§8.17 C8; ARCH-INV-1). Records **constitute** authority within the kernel (C1).
- **Int:** S0208's "computed" means **created by calculation**; the book's "computes" means **derived by evaluation** from recorded acts. Read with the first sense, S0208's rejection agrees with the book. Read with the second, it would reject CE*.
  - The estate itself (S0206 L1244) says a grant "is not a permission the system can evaluate", so the estate has no evaluator to reject.
  - *Recorded as a homonym* (T10-18); not a disagreement.
- **Not a new concept.**

**C4. Dimension independence: one checked instance in the book (Src; Int).**
- **The sources:** "Knowledge dimensions should evolve independently. A change in one dimension must not implicitly modify another" (S0208 L833, "probably the most important generalization"; INV-KOS-001 in S0211 §20.1). Allowed: "Evidence improves → Evidence state changes → Authority review requested". Forbidden: "Evidence improves → Authority automatically increases" (S0210 L121–143). S0209's filter: "Did authority change because evidence changed?" (S0209 L66–107).
- **In the book:**
  - **one checked instance** of that rule, for evidence against standing, is §9.18's fault containment. Evidence entries change no standing value (check 33: 400 interleaved records) and no mark where the record is in D_W, under premises (c1)–(c5).
  - **"Authority review requested"** is outside the record: an act by the people who hold authority (§10.4 R1).
- **Int:**
  - The estate counts "Evidence ≠ Authority" as **Established from EKS/PKS/AIP** (S0208 L813–823; S0209 L322). Its EKS basis is the split between lifecycle records and grants, which is the book's state ≠ authority, not evidence ≠ authority (§8.32 C2). The estate's "Established" therefore rests partly on that homonym.
  - The general rule over **all** dimensions has no check. "Dimension" is not defined, so it cannot have one yet (F13: the book's own attribute set is open).
- **Not a new concept.**

**C5. "An invariant is a protection against an invalid transformation": narrower than the book (Src; Ded against §9.15, §9.16).**
- **The sources:** "A KnowledgeOS invariant is not a fact about knowledge. It is a protection against an invalid transformation between knowledge dimensions" (S0209 L11, quoting the uploaded consolidation). The question becomes "Which transformations must the kernel prevent?" (S0208 L1036; S0210 L658).
- **Ded, against the book:**
  - **(i) The kernel guarantees facts about answers:** on a record in D, exactly one answer, T and F forced, every U with its reason (§9.15 G1); refutation from the record alone (G6). These are not protections against a transformation.
  - **(ii) Not every violation is prevented:** the book decided, per law, to **prevent** some violations and **detect** others (§9.16 R3a). For example, a second use across merged applications is marked, not prevented (§9.15 G4).

  So "which transformations must the kernel prevent?" is one of the book's questions, not its only one.
- **Not a new concept.**

**C6. Modal verbs by strength: a register rule of the estate (Src; Int).**
- **S0211:** "Strength controls language: Established → 'SHALL preserve' · Candidate → 'should preserve' · Hypothesis → 'may require'" (S0211 §20.2).
- **The book** ties strength to **levels** (S0–S3, the strategy §3) and to **labels** (Src, Obs, Ded, Int, Hyp). It has no rule tying modal verbs to them; it states that the strongest statement must not exceed the evidence.
- **Int:** the estate's ladder makes the strength readable in each sentence. *n = 1; recorded as a candidate practice for the register's wording, not adopted.* S0211 also records "No promotion" (L13) and "No new numbering is created" (§20.1), the same discipline as ES-005.4.
- **Not a new concept.**
