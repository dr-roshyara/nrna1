# P5 / Ch. 8 — step 2 review (whole-book review S0100)

**Process independence.** I am an AI session independent of the author as a process, not as a person. I do not know whether the model is the same. By the book's own §8.6 this means my independence is declared, not established.

**Blind comparison.** It covers only my five sampled sources: S0100, S0090, S0094, S0060, and S0054 (skimmed to L80 only). The rest of the part was reviewed non-blind. For the non-blind part I spot-checked about 150 source anchors across 40-odd allowed sources.

## 1. Assessment
- **Reliability.** Ch. 8 is reliable as a careful, mostly well-scoped catalogue of assurance lessons from one estate. Its quotes check out almost everywhere. Its settlement discipline is visible: INVALID forms are kept and restated, and "what does not hold" sections are present.
- **Largest risks.**
  - A residual contradiction in the headline claim of the last-settled section (§8.11 C1, F1).
  - The central concept, "independence of a check", has no link between its seven factors and its one formal model, and uses "independence" in more than one sense (F2).
  - About 73% of the body (§8.6–8.11 by word count) is estate-specific assurance theory, drawn from two work items of one programme. The index describes the chapter as methods for validating the book's theory (F4).
- **Nothing critical found.** No finding defeats a foundational concept or a chapter conclusion as stated.
