## 5.7.1 In plain language

A piece of work should be closed when the commissioned work is delivered and the record supports it, and not merely because prose calls it finished. In the estate, 15 of 19 open lanes were finished but not closed, so the rule has a duty half as well as a prohibition. A supersession is recognized only once it is recorded; inferring one is not evidence. A recommendation authorizes nothing. Once a model is accepted, the ARB says further work should be driven by new evidence or explicit pressure. And in BC-7's model, the party whose act is judged does not supply the standing it is judged against.

## 5.7.2 Claims and derivations

**C1. Closing work: a duty and a prohibition (Src; an extension of §6.6 C4).**
- The rule applied: "Close only work whose substantive deliverable and lifecycle evidence both justify closure; do not close merely because prose says it is finished." [S0079 L4]. And "no START or HANDOFF manufactured retroactively · no off-record execution converted into on-record execution" [S0079 L5].
- **The deliverable is the commissioned one, and a deliberate null counts.** A lane was closed after it "deliberately implemented nothing under the two-stage amendment — the intended outcome" [S0079 L32]. A lane that produced nothing because it never ran is not completed: completing the ineligible refinement lane "would assert it finished work it never did" [S0079 L57].
- **A defective lifecycle may be closed if the closure states the defect.** The verification #3 lane was never started, and its lane was still recorded COMPLETED [S0076 L53, L57]. The reconciliation kept it closed "with the defect stated in its own closure note" [S0079 L73] (C2).
- **The duty half.** A rule that only forbids closing is met by never closing anything. The drift that the reconciliation corrected was the opposite failure: "work completed in prose but not closed in the record" accounted for 15 of the 19 open lanes [S0079 L74]. *Count only* (entry 46); the lanes are the whole portfolio of one day, not a sample. So the rule has three parts:
  - **may close as COMPLETE** only if (a lane with no output may still be ended by CANCEL, S0079 L57; S0080 L47) the commissioned deliverable exists **and** either the lifecycle justifies closure or the closure states its lifecycle defect: deliverable ∧ (lifecycle ∨ defect stated). A lane that never ran and produced nothing may not be closed by stating "never ran" (S0079 L57); V#3 was closed because its report was delivered and accepted (S0079 L73; S0076 L59);
  - **should close** when the deliverable exists and the lifecycle justifies it: the duty half, which the 15 of 19 breached;
  - stating a defect is a **permission**, not a duty.
- **Int (§6.6 C4):** completion is a closure act on a record. It asserts that the commissioned work happened. A COMPLETE on a lane that never ran would be a false record of an occurrence, and §1.12 already separates the record from the act it reports.
- **Held by restraint, not by the mechanism.** The engine accepted a COMPLETE on a lane that was never started (C2). So C1 is kept by Governance's restraint, not enforced (CON-0102).
- *Hyp (entry 45):* that this is a general closure law. The evidence is one reconciliation on one day.

**C2. A verification that ran off the record: registered, never activated, closed (Src; a different subtype of §8.6 C3).**
- "**The Verification #3 lane was never STARTed on the record.** … The work ran off-record, and the closure therefore closes a lane the record never shows as active" [S0076 L57]. Governance recorded the lane COMPLETED [S0076 L53]. Nothing was back-dated, and "the acceptance stands on the report's substance" [S0076 L59].
- **Int:** this is the second verification in the estate accepted without a governed START, after verification #2 of BASELINE-001 (§8.6 C3, S0058 L49). **It is a different subtype.** §8.6 C3's case had no lane, no START and no grant. This lane was registered, never activated, and then closed. The source calls the two cases the same class; the book keeps the subtypes apart, because here the mechanism accepted a COMPLETE without a START.
- The acceptance carries the defect as a stated qualification: "permanent qualifications on this chain's assurance, disclosed at the moment of acceptance rather than after it" [S0077 L41]. **But its own summary contradicts that qualification:** "Four independent verification passes" and "the model was never accepted on its author's word" [S0077 L26]. Only three verification passes are recorded, and S0077 also says two of three independence links are compromised. That is a further case of caveats dropped at acceptance (§5.6 C6), and of counting passes instead of independent information (§8.6 C4). The count slip is a source defect.
- **Int (A1, corrected from "Ded (§1.12)"):** the verification's content is evidence. Its standing as an assurance step rests on the acceptance act, which names the gap.

**C3. A supersession is recognized when recorded, or when a later act makes it evident (Src; an extension of §8.5 C8 and I-7).**
- A stale verification lane is "Plausibly superseded …, but **that supersession has never been recorded**" [S0079 L62]. The ARB recommends "**SUPERSEDED/CANCEL unless evidence says it must run** — *"do not infer"* supersession" [S0080 L47].
- **Int (I-7):** supersession is a link, never an edit. **It is recognized when it is recorded, or when a later act makes it evident; such an inference counts as evidence.** This is an epistemic rule about recognition. Whether the record *constitutes* the supersession is the open fork of §1.13.1-2, left to the Principal. This is §8.5 C8's rule, that a supersession must be named where the reader looks, taken one step back: it must first be recorded at all.
- **The vocabulary has gaps:** "`CANCEL` is terminal in this engine — there is no un-cancel edge" and "The engine has no "retire" transition" [S0080 L46–48]. *Int:* a lifecycle whose vocabulary lacks a needed ending forces that ending into a governance registration outside the record. **This differs from §6.5 C2** (corrected from "C9"), where existing endings were never exercised; here the transitions are missing. An un-cancel would be a reversal, not an ending.

**C4. Capability first, people and agents last (Src; for the boundary questions).**
- The ARB's guard: the governing question is "**What capabilities does a sustainable AI Engineering Platform require?**", evaluated in the order "capability → ownership → bounded context / stewardship → human role → agent → shared platform service" [S0080 L28–33]. It is registered "before commissioning, so the discovery cannot drift into org design" [S0080 L36].
- *The order itself is not stable in the corpus:* a later pack gives bounded context / stewardship before ownership (§8.10 C10).
- **Int (RESEARCH-PURPOSE, §9.3 C4):** this order is **compatible with, not identical to,** the book's boundary questions. The ARB orders an organisation-design discovery and puts the shared platform service last. The book's three tests (necessary, must the kernel provide it, could an application provide it) are not an order of staffing. What the two share: who or what provides a capability comes after the capability.
- **And a recommendation authorizes nothing:** "a recommendation is never an authorization" (ES-001.2), so the four dispositions the ARB recommends "are NOT executed by this registration" [S0080 L4].

**C5. Stability of an accepted model (Src; a candidate rule of its own).**
- "once a bounded-context model has been accepted, subsequent work should be driven by new evidence or explicit change pressure, not by endless architectural polishing" [S0080 L57].
- **Hyp (one source), modality *should*:** a stewardship rule for accepted models. **It is not A1.** A1 says only an act confers standing; it requires no reason. This rule adds a justification condition, and its source states it as a norm about practice. "Explicit change pressure" is not evidence. *Int (the book's own norm, not the source's):* its trivial case, never reopening, would need a duty half too: reopen when new evidence that bears on the model arrives.
- **Two messages, one acceptance, again:** a five-act instruction and a fuller formal act on the same day are "Registered once, as the governing text of this acceptance" [S0076 L43]. That is the second case of §5.6 C6 (S0061 L5).

**C6. A figure handed on as a check criterion (Src; an instance of §8.8 C2 and P-8).**
- The measurement-provenance issue is routed on, stated as "*a figure stated without its method cannot be reproduced, and a figure handed onward as a check criterion inherits authority it has not earned.*" [S0076 L28].
- **Int (P-8, §1.14.4):** being used as a criterion is an act-free property, so it confers no standing on the figure. This is §8.8 C2's hazard put as a rule about authority. **A homonym:** the source's "inherits authority" means reliance in practice, not standing from an act.

**C7. A reconciliation adopted as "truth" (Src + Int).**
- The ARB: "**"The reconciliation itself is high quality and should now be treated as the current portfolio truth."**" Then: "Adopted. The 2026-08-18 reconciliation is the authoritative portfolio state." [S0080 L8–10].
- **The conflict:** C1's own rule is that prose calling something finished does not close it, and BC-7's I-10 is that the record outranks prose (S0078 L163). A prose synthesis adopted as the authoritative state seems to put prose above the record.
- **Int, the reading under which they are compatible:** the adoption gives standing to the reconciliation's **classification** (what still needs a decision), as a **dated** snapshot. The closures it describes are record acts. It is not a second store of state that keeps its standing as the record moves.
- *Candidate fork F28:* can a recorded synthesis hold standing as "truth" beside the record?

**C8. In BC-7's model, the party being judged does not supply its premise (Src; one source).**
- BC-7's validation input is "the fold, recomputed at check time", "**never supplied by the party being validated**" [S0078 L161]. In one sentence: "the party recording an act never supplies the standing against which the act is judged. That is the tactical form of I-10 (*the record outranks prose*) — one truth, one producer, and no self-supplied premise." [S0078 L163].
- **Int (§8.8 C1, §1.14.1):** §8.8 C1 covers non-consultation of a stored derived key (T-1b). This is the stronger half (T-1a): the validator recomputes its own premise, so nothing the party submits can stand in for its standing. It bears on "how can it be trusted?". *One source;* the source calls it structural (S0078 L169).

**C9. No context owns who produced a fact (Src; for the boundary questions).**
- "**BC-7 owns the *rules under which a lifecycle fact is admitted*** … **It does not own, and cannot know, who produced one** … **So no bounded context in the accepted model owns "the ability to produce a lifecycle fact."** That ability is a property of *access to the mechanism* — an operational and governance concern, not a domain-model one." [S0078 L222].
- **Int (§9.6, the invariant-owner test):** §6.5 and §9.6 record that the actual writer is unattested. This is the boundary claim behind it: writer identity has no owner and no operation that guarantees it. So an invariant about who produced a fact cannot be enforced by any context of the accepted model. Where it belongs (kernel, platform access, governance) is open; it bears on F22.

**C10. The accepted model kept a statement already shown false (Src; an instance for §8.7).**
- The corrected census row reports "**20 instances**" of keys the mechanism never reads, and says it corrects a false cell [S0078 L67]. The replacement text for T-1 in the same document still says "No non-canonical key appears anywhere in the estate." [S0078 L169].
- **Int (§8.7):** the model was accepted as refined and corrected (S0077 L11), and verification #3 passed it. So a correction and its verification together left a false statement inside the accepted invariant text. It is one counterexample to the claim that correction plus verification restores integrity.

## 5.7.3 Open questions

- Should the kernel's lifecycle vocabulary include an un-cancel and a retire transition, or are those endings governance acts outside the record (C3)?
- Is "reopen only on new evidence or explicit pressure" (C5) a kernel law for accepted models, or one ARB's practice? And what is its duty half?
- F28 (candidate): can a recorded synthesis hold standing as "truth" beside the record (C7)?
- Where does an invariant about who produced a fact belong, given that no context owns it (C9)?

## 5.7.4 What does not hold

*First version, refuted at review C14:*
- **"A lane with no output is not completed"** (CON-0144 as stated). Two counterexamples in its own sources: a deliberate null was closed COMPLETE (S0079 L32), and a never-started lane was closed with its defect stated (S0079 L73). Restated in C1.
- **A closure rule with only a prohibition.** Never closing meets it; the measured failure (15 of 19) was a missing duty to close.
- **"A supersession exists only as a recorded link"** (CON-0145 as stated). That settles the open fork of §1.13.1-2. Restated as a recognition rule in C3.
- **"This is A1 for models … an act needs a reason"** (C5). A1 requires no reason; the rule is a separate *should*.
- **"For the record the verification has no lifecycle", labelled Ded (§1.12)** (C2). The record holds a registration and a COMPLETE: a lifecycle with no activation. Restated as Int (A1).
- **"The order the book's boundary questions assume"** (C4). Compatible with, not identical to.
- **"§6.5 C9 records the same gap"** (C3). It is §6.5 C2, and a different gap.

*Settled after review C15 (Rulebook step 9, the review of this settlement):*
- **The restated CON-0144, "close when … deliverable and lifecycle both justify it, or when the closure states its lifecycle defect".** The defect clause had no deliverable condition, so a lane with no output could be closed by stating "never ran", which S0079 L57 refuses. Restated as deliverable ∧ (lifecycle ∨ defect stated), with the duty separate.
- **"A supersession counts only once it is recorded"** (plain language). "Counts" is constitution language and settled the open fork again; "is recognized".
- **C5's added duty as unlabelled.** It is the book's norm (Int), restricted to evidence that bears on the model.
