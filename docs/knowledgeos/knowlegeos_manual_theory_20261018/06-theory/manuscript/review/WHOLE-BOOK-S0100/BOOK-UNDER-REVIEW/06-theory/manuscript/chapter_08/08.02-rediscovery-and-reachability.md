# 8.2 Rediscovery: when existing knowledge cannot be reached

## 8.2.1 In plain language

Within one day the corpus found that several of its new results already existed elsewhere in the repository. It counted these "rediscoveries" and looked for their cause. The first cause it proposed, that the regions were not indexed, was refuted by its next case. For validation this matters twice: a claim of novelty needs a search, and a cause inferred from a self-counted series needs a counterexample test.

Sources: S0036 (Platform Capability Model) and S0038 (Engineering Knowledge Landscape), both 2026-08-02, generated.

## 8.2.2 Definitions

- **Rediscovery (Src).** The failure mode "*proposing before searching*" [S0036 L81]: producing as new what already exists. S0038 counts nine [S0038 L25], and its annotation adds a tenth [S0038 L21].
- **Three kinds of duplication (Src).** `[CON-0106 · invalid as stated]` (the source classes the same items twice; C3)
  - **healthy repetition:** "pointer + description, by design";
  - **historical evolution:** "successive refinements of one problem";
  - **accidental duplication:** the rediscoveries, colliding vocabularies, and "Constitution" used for at least five artifacts [S0038 L151–157].

## 8.2.3 Claims and derivations

**C1. A causal claim from a self-counted series, and its refutation (Src + Ded).**
- **The claim:** "all nine rediscoveries drew on UNINDEXED regions. Not one drew on an indexed region." [S0038 L212]. On it rests the recommendation that an index is "the **sole cause** behind 9 occurrences" [S0038 L229].
- **The refutation, in the same document:** "Occurrence #10 drew on an INDEXED region that `.claude/CLAUDE.md` names explicitly. *The cause is not only a missing index — it is also not reading the index that exists.*" [S0038 L21]
- **Ded:** "all nine are unindexed" is a correlation over a series the author both produced and counted. One counterexample defeats "sole cause"; it does not defeat "a cause". The supported statement is: **unreachability has at least two causes, a missing index and an unread one.**
- **Selection:** the rediscoveries were found by one sweep, by the author whose work they hit, and attributed by the same author. So the series is not a sample of all rediscoveries (Ch. 4 §4.1 C4–C5). The supported form of C1 is that a missing index does not explain every case.

**C2. A measured ratio, scoped (Src).**
- "Four of nine suspected gaps were not gaps. That ratio is the landscape's measurement." [S0038 L175]
- **Scope:** nine gaps, one sweep, one author. It says that in this sweep four of nine claimed gaps were not gaps *(restricted at the consolidation settlement S0036–S0065; the earlier wording said "often wrong" of the corpus)*. That is evidence for the book's own rule that a search miss is not absence; it is not a rate that generalises.
- **"Discoverable" is estimated, not counted:** "Estimated 25–35 %, and the estimate is structural, not counted." [S0038 L211]

**C3. Duplication is classified by two variables, not three kinds (Ded; after review C7).** `[CON-0106]`
- S0038 calls the two decision templates and the three bounded-context instruments "historical evolution" [S0038 L156], and then counts them among the symptoms of one failure [S0038 L222]. So its kinds overlap.
- **The two variables** that decide RC-9 are: does a copy carry **authoritative standing**, and is a **supersession** recorded between copies?
  - a copy without standing (a pointer with a description) is no breach;
  - two authoritative copies with a recorded supersession are versions, no breach;
  - two or more authoritative copies with no recorded supersession are a breach, whatever their history.
- A "refinement" without a recorded supersession is therefore a second authoritative home. This matches the consolidation review's scoped form of RC-9.

**C4. An inventory becomes reusable only with a model of meaning (Src).**
- "The repository inventory is an evidence-gathering activity. It becomes reusable engineering infrastructure only after a stable Engineering Knowledge Domain Model defines the semantic meaning of its contents." [S0038 L15]
- A classification by kind of artifact is "not yet semantically grounded" [S0038 L17]. This bears on F13: an attribute model has to come first.
