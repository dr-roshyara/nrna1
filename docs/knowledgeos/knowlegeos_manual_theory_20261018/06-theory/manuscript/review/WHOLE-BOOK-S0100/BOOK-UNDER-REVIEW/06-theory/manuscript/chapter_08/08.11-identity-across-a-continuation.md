## 8.11.1 In plain language

A verifier refused a task because it was barred, and it was right. A later session, continued from a compressed summary of that verifier's conversation, read its own session identifier correctly, saw that it was different, and "corrected" the refusal as false. That correction was wrong: the refusal was the earlier session's act, carried in the later session's inherited context. Every self-declared identifier in the episode was true; what failed was the attribution of an act present in inherited context. The later session then treated the bar as not applying to itself and did the verification. Whether that was a breach depends on a question the source leaves open and the book had only named: is a continuation of a barred process barred too? Further points: the author of a finding may not judge how it was absorbed; a barred process should not record an act that would assert the opposite of its bar, though nothing but its own judgement enforces the bar; and a verification cannot run on an object that has not yet been delivered, however independent the verifier.

## 8.11.2 Claims and derivations

**C1. A continuation attributed an act in its inherited context to itself (Src; an instance of §8.6 process lineage).** `[CON-0157 · invalid as stated]` (restated form here)
- The refusal declared: "**This process is `claude-code-session:2da45a86`**" [S0100 L10], barred by name as the author of Verification #2, and it stopped.
- A later appended correction asserted: "**This record's §1 identity claim is FALSE.**" [S0100 L61], because "**This process is `claude-code-session:4858c37c`**" (S0100 L68, in meaning). Its root cause, as it saw it: "**The identity was INFERRED FROM THE ESTATE instead of established from the runtime.**" [S0100 L82].
- The erratum that followed reverses it: "**`CORRECTION 1` IS WRONG IN ITS CENTRAL ASSERTION. The refusal's original §1 identity claim was TRUE, and `CORRECTION 1` withdrew a true statement.**" [S0100 L105]. The correcting session was "a **context-compaction continuation**" of `2da45a86` (S0099 L43, in meaning); it "measured its OWN identity correctly and then attributed someone else's act to itself" (S0099 L46, in meaning): "The compacted summary presented the barred process's history as the continuation's own, and the misattribution followed." [S0099 L47].
- **So the correcting session's self-declaration was false: self-declared identity failed in one direction.** The correction had called its case "the first recorded instance of it failing in the FALSE-BAR direction" (S0100 L95, in meaning); the erratum judges that claim "**true as a class, wrong in its instance**" (S0100 L128, in meaning). The erratum's "SECOND instance in the OPPOSITE direction — a process wrongly claiming NOT to be barred" (S0100 L128, in meaning) presupposes an answer to C2, which the companion stop record leaves open (S0099 L76–80). The source's diagnosis still applies as a risk: "**the declaration is unverified at the point of use, and the declaring party is the only source.**" [S0100 L95].
- **How it was settled:** by the record of the writing act: which transcript **created** the refusal (`cat >`) and which **appended** (`cat >>`), with the seconds between each write and its commit as corroboration (S0099 L40–42, in meaning). The transcripts are themselves keyed by session identifier; the timing only corroborates.
- **Identifiers are not episodes.** The correction says `2da45a86` "ended" at 08:49:17Z (S0100 L68, in meaning), yet the stop record shows it writing from 11:10:52Z (S0099 L44, in meaning); and `4858c37c`'s transcript is said to end at 10:42:21Z (S0099 L44, in meaning) while it declares the later erratum (S0100 L142, in meaning). So one identifier can cover resumed episodes, and the erratum's own self-declaration is not checked in the sources. This is the first evidence in the book for §8.6.2's factor "resume".
- **Int (§8.6; §8.9 C10; §8.10 C5):** this is the first case in the book of §8.6's factor "process lineage" deciding an outcome. A continuation has a new identifier and inherits the earlier process's context, including a first-person account of acts it did not perform. What failed was **attribution of acts in inherited context**, not the identifiers. *One case.*

**C2. Is a continuation of a barred process itself barred? (Src; a question for the Principal; bears on F32).**
- "**IS A CONTEXT-COMPACTION CONTINUATION OF A BARRED PROCESS ITSELF BARRED?**" [S0099 L76]. "**On a strict identity reading — no**" (S0099 L77, in meaning): the continuation has its own session identifier and wrote none of the barred artifacts. "**On the substance the bar protects — the question is live:** its working context was **derived from the barred process's context**", and the estate's own rule that identity is evidential only "cuts against settling this by `sessionId`" (S0099 L78, in meaning).
- The source does not decide it: "⛔ **I do not decide it. I am the process on the wrong side of it, and deciding it would be the exact error `CORRECTION 1` made.**" [S0099 L80]. Until it is ruled, "**the eligible-verifier set for this work item is not knowable**" (S0099 L109, in meaning).
- **Int (§8.6.2; F32):** C1 shows that identifiers do not track working context. Whether eligibility **should** follow working context is the open question itself. F32 therefore gains an option, **eligibility relative to inherited working context**, to be kept apart from **information lineage** (§8.6.2): anyone verifying a correction must read the findings it answers, so an option based on information lineage would leave no eligible verifier for any verification of uptake. Not decided here.

**C3. The author of a finding may not judge how it was absorbed (Src; an extension of §1.14.3).** `[CON-0158 · provisional]`
- The refusal cites a bar registered the same day on another track: "*"the Architecture determination does not judge the adequacy or entailment of a finding authored by the same process."*" [S0100 L36], and generalises: "**a verifier must not assess how its own findings were absorbed.**" (S0100 L38, in meaning).
- Its decisive limb is "**Material participation in producing the evidence being verified**": its own findings "fed the correction chain that runs into this artifact's lineage" (S0100 L32, in meaning).
- **Int (§1.14.3):** producer ≠ verifier forbids a process to verify its own product. C3 extends it one step: a process may not verify a product **shaped by its own findings**, because the verification would grade the uptake of its own claims. It is the same structure as legislator ≠ evaluator (§1.14.3). *Scope:* the estate applies it to direct uptake; read transitively it would bar almost everyone. *Hyp, an estate policy:* practice elsewhere also has the opposite case, a referee checking that their own comments were taken up. *Two instances in one estate (two tracks), one line.*

**C4. A barred process should not record an act on the barred lane (Src; an instance of §1.12 and §9.8 H4).**
- "**The START act is NOT consumed.** … *Recording the START into a lane held by a barred process would have made the record assert the opposite of the bar.*" [S0100 L22].
- **Int (§9.8 H4):** an entry asserts that its act was performed by an actor entitled to it. A START recorded by a barred process would be an entry whose own preconditions are false. Declining to record keeps H4 true for the record, at the cost of the lane waiting.
- **An invariant with no enforcer.** The bar is written by name (S0097 L27–31, in meaning), while "no gate reads process identity" (INV-ATTR-1; S0097 L41, in meaning). So the bar holds only through the barred process's own judgement and a human START chosen on self-declarations; the continuation of C1 acted while it was "never declared in any record" (S0100 L78, in meaning). *Int (§9.8 M5):* no operation in the model guarantees the bar; refusing everything satisfies it, and nothing obliges anyone to act. Its owner is unnamed.
- *Conduct vs premise:* the refusal "consumed no `START`, mutated no record" and "Nothing has to be un-done; only the premise was wrong" (S0100 L88, in meaning), said when its premise was thought false. An act's correctness on its premise is separate from the premise's truth.

**C5. Independence is not sufficient: the object must exist and the assignment must be registered (Src).**
- A later stop record gives "**Three independent grounds to stop. The first two are structural and hold whoever reads this.**" [S0099 L8]: the assignment does not exist in the record, and the engine refuses — "refused: unknown session — identity is answerable only for registered sessions" (S0099 L14, in meaning) — and the object does not exist: "**Correction #3 has not been produced.**" (S0099 L19, in meaning).
- "⛔ **Verifying an undelivered correction is not possible, and no amount of independence would make it possible.**" [S0099 L26]. The act handed to the verifier was one step ahead of the record: "the chain is one step earlier than the act assumes" (S0099 L26, in meaning).
- **Int:** the preconditions of a verification include the existence of its object and its registration, independently of the verifier. An act cannot create either by being issued (A1 gives standing, not existence). *Time-indexed:* "Correction #3 has not been produced" was true at 13:15 local and overtaken ten minutes later, when Correction #3 was committed (git `c38396244`, 2026-08-19 13:25). Superseded, not disproven (§8.10 C2, CON-0156).
- *A related registration check:* existence is checked apart from correctness: "Precondition 2 was checked for **existence and evident implementation**, not for correctness" (S0097 L21, in meaning).

**C6. A verification of scope, not only of answers (Src).** `[CON-0159 · invalid as stated]` (restated form below)
- The registration's charge has two halves: whether the amended analysis "correctly implements" the corrections, "**AND whether it REMAINS A DECISION-PREPARATION ARTIFACT rather than silently becoming an architectural decision.**" (S0097 L45, in meaning). "*An analysis that answers its own open questions well is still out of scope — and that failure is invisible to anyone who only checks whether the answers are good.*" [S0097 L47].
- And its boundary: "**"Verification identifies insufficiency; it does not provide the replacement architecture."**" (S0097 L67, in meaning).
- **Int (§1.14.5; §5.6):** the source calls such an analysis "out of scope", not a decision. An analysis has no normative effect by itself (§1.14.5-3: recommending is not a normative effect); it can take effect only through an adoption that nobody registered (§1.14.5-13 (a)). So the risk is a decision made **implicitly**, by adopting an analysis that already settled the questions. Checking the quality of the answers cannot detect this; only a check of the artifact's role against its commission can. *One source.*
- **A counter-instance in the same window:** the session that wrote the findings `F-1`…`F-7` then produced Correction #3 (S0100 L130–135, in meaning; git `c38396244`), "compounding the seq-21 producer mismatch" (S0100 L135, in meaning): a verifier supplying the replacement for its own findings, against the boundary just quoted.

**C7. Recorded, not waived: a limb of a bar that cannot be applied (Src; an instance of §8.9 C2).**
- The act bars "the author of any review that materially caused AMD2, **if identifiable**"; the review "**is not in the estate**" (S0097 L35, in meaning); so "its author is not identifiable, the bar cannot be applied to it, and the act's own qualifier — *"if identifiable"* — is what permits registration to proceed rather than stop" (S0097 L37, in meaning). The assignment requires the verifier to state whether it authored that review; if it cannot, "that limb of the bar is unsatisfied and must be disclosed as such" (S0097 L39, in meaning).
- **The pool, again:** "**each completed assurance pass removes another process from the eligible pool.**" (S0100 L49, in meaning), "a supply question about assurance capacity" and "not a reason to relax the bar" (S0100 L49, in meaning). The correction declared this argument void (S0100 L90, in meaning); the erratum says it does not follow that it is void and that it stands (S0100 L127, in meaning), while restoring nothing: "I withdraw no verdict, invalidate no artifact and restore nothing" (S0100 L140, in meaning), since standing is "a **Governance / PO-ARB act**" (S0100 L140, in meaning). *So the record holds a claim, its withdrawal and the withdrawal of that withdrawal, with no act yet deciding standing:* truth and standing come apart (§1.14.1-12). It fits §8.9 C2's restated form: the pool drops when a process authors what the object is shaped by.

**C8. An advisory AI that sees the estate only through the operator (Src; S0098).**
- The advisor separates "the **role model**" from "the **workflow state machine**": "The roles tell us *who may act*; the lifecycle tells us *what happens and in what order*." [S0098 L3]. And: "**A deliverable being produced is not the same thing as its lifecycle being closed.**" [S0098 L290] (supports §5.7 C1).
- It counts **five** responsibilities, with Governance Communication as "a **coordination capability** around the lifecycle", not a role (S0098 L227, in meaning), on the day the PO/ARB adopted **six** roles including a Communication Engineer (S0093; §8.10 C1). *Int:* the advisor's model and the adopted model differ; whether the advisor had the adoption in view is not shown.
- **Its channel:** "**I cannot directly watch your Claude Code terminal or receive its messages automatically.**" [S0098 L407]; results reach it only when "You bring the result here" [S0098 L416]. *Int (§8.10 C6):* knowledge passes between two AI systems through the human operator. That is the channel S0092's access evidence could not exclude (§8.10 C6), and it is outside every record in the estate.

- **It names the gap the window then shows.** Among what is "still missing" it lists "Role identity / provenance" and "Who really executed an action?" (S0098 L337, in meaning), about a minute before the refusal of C1 was written. *Int:* written by a different system through the operator, this is possibly the window's only second line on the identity gap, though not independent of the operator.
- **Two defects in its model:** its state machine lists transitions (HANDOFF, START) as states (S0098 L235–266, in meaning), against the record's behaviour, where a lane is CREATED after REGISTER and HANDOFF (S0097 L5, L75, in meaning); and it puts ACCEPT and START authorization under Governance (S0098 L101–108, in meaning), where the registration requires a recorded human act (S0097 L77, in meaning).

## 8.11.3 Open questions

- For the Principal: is a context-compaction continuation of a barred process barred (C2)? The source leaves it to the PO/ARB.
- Does F32 need the option "eligibility relative to inherited working context", kept apart from information lineage (C2)?
- Who owns a bar written by name when no gate reads process identity (C4)?
- Should "the author of a finding may not judge its uptake" (C3) be a kernel law beside producer ≠ verifier (Hyp)?
- Should every verification include a scope check (C6)?

## 8.11.4 What does not hold

*First version, refuted at review C18:*
- **"Self-declared identity failed in both directions"** (C1; CON-0157 as stated), with the plain language "did the barred work". Both self-declarations were true; the source itself judges the false-bar instance wrong; the second direction presupposes C2. What failed was attribution of an act in inherited context.
- **"C1 refutes the identifier option" and "authorship is not enough"** (C2's Int). Both presuppose that eligibility should follow working context, which is the open question.
- **"An analysis that settles the questions it was asked to prepare manufactures a decision, as a gate … does"** (C6; CON-0159 as stated). An analysis has no normative effect by itself; it risks an implicit adoption.
- **"The erratum restored it"** (C7). It says the argument stands and restores nothing; standing is Governance's act.
