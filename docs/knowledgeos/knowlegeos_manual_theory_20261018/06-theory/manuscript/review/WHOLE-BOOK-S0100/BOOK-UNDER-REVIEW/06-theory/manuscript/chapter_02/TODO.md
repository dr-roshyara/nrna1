# Chapter 2: TODOs (undefined, ambiguous or inconsistent concepts)

*Rule: the Principal's "Undefined Concepts and TODO Tracking" (2026-10-18). File authorized by the Principal.*

*A TODO is closed only by evidence or an authorized decision, never by a proposed definition alone.*

**Status values:** Open · Under Research · Awaiting Human Decision · Resolved.

**Issue types:** undefined · ambiguous · inconsistent · disputed · awaiting validation.

**Distinctions kept:**
- undefined ≠ invalid;
- not found ≠ proven absent;
- unevidenced ≠ contradicted;
- proposed ≠ validated;
- recommended ≠ decided.

**Closing a TODO** requires its resolution criteria to be met, and its resolution is recorded with provenance.
