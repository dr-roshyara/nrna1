## S0241: KnowledgeOS Constitution v1.0 (depth: full)
1. **The kernel.** Eleven articles, each said to be "a negation" that forbids one epistemic collapse. They are "a selection and compression" of 29 register rows (25 candidates + 4 Established): "fewer laws, not fewer truths" (l.13, l.29). Each is "independently failure-tested", and removing one "opens a named corruption" (l.29). This is reported, not shown. [I] Several articles also contain positive obligations, not negations: Art 1.5 "Knowledge SHALL be a relationship", Art 6.1 (justification path), Art 10.1 (preserve agency). So "each is a negation" does not hold as stated.
2. **Identity (Art 1, l.33–37).** Representation and expression are not meaning. Similarity, even perfect coextension, is never identity. Context is part of identity: a claim without its frame is "incomplete" and "never valid beyond" the frame. Knowledge is a relation of knower, process and object.
3. **Dimension (Art 2, l.43–45).** Knowledge has independent epistemic dimensions. A value moves between dimensions only by an explicit, governed transition. No single scalar may replace the structure. [I] The dimensions are never listed or defined, so the article has no fixed domain (open question).
4. **Authority (Art 3, l.51–53).** Authority is never constituted by evidence, assessment or a source. Authority is *assigned*: "a recorded reference to a human act". The means of acquisition must be preserved (sensor ≠ expert ≠ AI ≠ document).
5. **Decision (Art 4, l.59–60).** Observation and knowledge do not constitute a decision. Knowledge informs action and never executes it. The knowledge–decision boundary is "governed, never implicit".
6. **Projection (Art 5, l.66–67).** A projection is never its source. Projections are regenerable and carry no authority beyond their source's.
7. **Verification (Art 6, l.73–77).** Entry needs a preserved justification path: authentic evidence + acquisition method + inference rule + reasoning path. Pseudo-evidence is not evidence. "Generation SHALL NOT be justification": AI output is a candidate. Reasoning is a governed transformation. Observation does not become inference.
8. **The three state articles.**
   - Failure-State (Art 7): REJECTED is preserved as an explicit state and is never silently discarded.
   - Contradiction (Art 8): CONFLICTED knowledge coexists until a governed resolution. Challenge does not destroy identity. Resolution is forward-only.
   - Unknown (Art 9): UNKNOWN is first-class. Unknown ≠ Absent ≠ False. Structured uncertainty is not flattened.
9. **Agency and History.**
   - Agency (Art 10): "who observed, reasoned, validated, decided" and under which authority. "Who knows?" is always answerable.
   - History (Art 11): revision never deletes. Knowledge has temporal validity; freshness ≠ truth and expiry ≠ absence. Supersession is forward-only.
10. **Kernel boundary (Ch III, l.123–140).**
    - The layers are Kernel (PRESERVE), then Engine Layer and Mechanisms (GOVERN), then Representations and Implementation (ENABLE).
    - The Engine Layer includes "verdict vocabulary" (l.129).
    - The kernel "does not do the reasoning". No lower layer may violate an article; a violation is "a corruption".
    - Implementation is out of constitutional scope.
11. **Negative boundary (Ch IV, l.148–154).** KnowledgeOS is not a repository, an LLM, a truth oracle, a similarity engine, or an autonomous decision maker. Each refusal is tied to the articles it would collapse, for example LLM → Art 1, 6, 11. The LLM lacks "persistent epistemic identity" and "historical belief evolution". A system that becomes any of the five "has ceased to be KnowledgeOS".
12. **Amendment discipline (Ch V, l.162–173).**
    - Engineering evolution in the lower layers is free, provided no article is violated.
    - Articles, character, negative boundary, kernel/engine boundary and Ch V itself change only by an amendment ratified by the HPA. "No article SHALL be weakened."
    - Amendment may refine wording, fix an inconsistency, or add a strengthening clause on evidence. It may never add concepts, sources or rows.
    - [I] This writes the closure of the corpus into the constitution and entrenches it against amendment. A missing concept could never be added (defeasibility question).
13. **Character text differs internally.** The Preamble (l.11) lists 7 preserved items: identity, context, evidence, reasoning, uncertainty, agency, history. Art I.1 (l.19) lists 8: it adds *authority* and the "governs how knowledge is created…" clause. Art I.2 says the three clause refinements are "absorbed" but do not supersede. [I] The two lists differ (small source slip, or a silent change).
14. **One row in two articles.** INV-KOS-Pramana-001 is rendered in Art 3 and in Art 6. H-KOS-Relationship-001 is rendered in Art 1 and in Art 10 (l.39, 55, 79, 109). [I] One row supports two laws, which weakens the claim that the laws are independent.
15. **Status.** "PROPOSED · EVIDENCE-BASED · NON-AUTHORITATIVE, pending HPA ratification" (l.5, 179). Upon ratification it is the governing instrument, and later phases "SHALL NOT extend it". Art I.3: "Mathematics describes, never defines."

## S0243: Reference Architecture v1.0 (depth: full)
1. **Governing principle (l.14–16).** "The architecture is a set of boundaries that make the constitutional forbiddances structurally impossible", "regardless of how any engine… is implemented". [I] This is a universal claim. The source only asserts it; it does not show it.
2. **The Constitution as constraint (l.12).** The Constitution is not architecture; it is the constraint system the architecture must obey.
3. **One-to-one mapping (§2, l.54–66).** Each article maps to one boundary, one kernel service and one named forbidden flow. Example: Art 1 → Identity boundary, where "identity is only ever assigned, never derived". [I] S0241 Art 1 says identity is *preserved*. "Assigned" is S0241's wording for *authority* (Art 3.2). Carrying "assigned" over to identity may extend the Constitution.
4. **Eleven kernel services (§3).** Each is "a gate or a record, never a reasoner". Examples: Decision Interlock "terminates knowledge flows at recommendation"; Verification Gate. [I] Trivial model: an interlock that terminates every flow, and a gate that admits nothing, satisfy all eleven "never" columns. The services have prohibitions but no positive capability (no "must-do"), apart from "records/assigns".
5. **The Verification Gate is the keystone (l.83, 90).** It is the "only admission path" into knowledge. It is also said to realize Art 7, 9, 1, 10 and 11. [I] This is the single point on which the "structurally impossible" claim rests.
6. **Kernel and engine division (l.44, 96).** Engines propose, transform and evaluate. They never admit and never hold kernel records. The Validation Engine *proposes* a verdict; the Gate *decides*.
7. **Engines (§4).** Validation, Reasoning (Vyapti-warranted inference), Contradiction/Debate (A claims, B counters, C validates, a mediator proposes), Fallacy Detection, History/Revision, Intent Classification (Vāda/Jalpa/Vitaṇḍā), and the LLM.
   - [I] S0244 l.40 requires every engine to come from the Ch III lists. "History/Revision Engine" and "Reasoning Engine(s)" are not in the S0241 Ch III engine list (only "reasoning pipelines", listed under mechanisms). S0243 l.174 claims full compliance anyway.
   - Eleven kernel "services" are a new kind of architectural object. Whether that counts as "extending" is open.
8. **LLM placement (l.106–108).** The LLM is a governed generation mechanism "at the mouth of the Verification Gate". It never writes to the store and "may propose; it may never possess". Its two structural absences "are now placement, not weakness".
9. **Verdict vocabulary has moved layer.** It is in the Representation layer (l.36, 122), described as "an engine product, not a kernel law". S0241 Ch III puts it in the Engine Layer (l.129).
10. **Layer count (l.22).** The text says "four layers below the kernel, three of them in constitutional scope". The diagram shows three layers below the kernel, and the Mechanisms layer of S0241 is missing. [I] Source defect: a count slip, and Mechanisms is silently dropped or merged.
11. **Canonical path (§6, l.132–155).** PROPOSAL → PROVENANCE → GATE, with three branches:
    - VALIDATED: entry, with identity, authority, agency, state and timestamp recorded;
    - QUESTIONABLE: held, "never FALSE, never deleted";
    - REJECTED: preserved.

    Then LIFE: challenge, forward-only revision, projection, advisory use. [I] Three problems:
    - (a) Identity is assigned only on VALIDATED entry. But REJECTED and QUESTIONABLE states, and challenged claims (Art 8.2), also need identity. That is a gap.
    - (b) No branch yields UNKNOWN, although l.90 says an unverifiable claim is UNKNOWN. QUESTIONABLE stands in for it, and the relation between the two is undefined.
    - (c) "Authority recorded" at gate admission conflicts with S0241 Art 3.2 and IV.3 (authority is assigned by a human act, and knowledge stays non-authoritative until governance assigns authority).
12. **Representations (§5).**
    - Evidence Record.
    - Context Tuple (Entity, Property, Context, Relation, Time, Authority): "a representation of Avacchedaka; the principle is Art 1".
    - Epistemic State Model, an open list ending "(…)", which mixes UNKNOWN/ABSENT/FALSE with VALIDATED/QUESTIONABLE/REJECTED/CONFLICTED.
    - Absence Taxonomy (four-fold Abhāva).
    - Typed Epistemic Graph `{identity, context, evidence, reasoning, authority, agency, history}`.
13. **Self-certification (§8, §10).** The completion gate says "YES on both": it satisfies the Constitution and does not extend it. [I] This is a self-assessment, not independent evidence.
14. **Status.** PROPOSED, pending HPA review (l.6, 200). The Traceability section calls the Constitution "(ratified)" (l.197). Next phases: Logical → Implementation Architecture → Systems.

## S0245: Vedic Mathematics placement ruling (depth: full)
1. **Kind of ruling (l.4–5, 19).** It is a placement ruling, not a new artifact. It is judged against the "frozen" Reference Architecture, under which an outside idea "cannot create a new constitutional layer", only enrich an existing engine or representation. [I] It calls S0243 "frozen" (l.4, 11), but S0243 is still PROPOSED (l.123 here). It applies an architecture that has not yet been reviewed.
2. **Placement model (l.13–17).** Kernel = PRESERVE, Engines = GOVERN, Mechanisms = GOVERN, Representations = ENABLE, Implementation = ENABLE. [I] This restores the Mechanisms layer that S0243's diagram dropped, so it follows S0241, not S0243.
3. **Central distinction (l.29).** Vedic mathematics "does not define what knowledge IS. It defines how a solver can transform information efficiently". So it is not a constitutional principle and it belongs in the Engine layer. This applies Art I.3 of S0241.
4. **Constitutional identity paraphrased in six items (l.29).** Identity, evidence, authority separation, verification, contradiction, history. [I] This list omits Dimension, Decision, Projection, Failure-State, Unknown and Agency. It is a lossy paraphrase, not the eleven articles.
5. **Five mappings into existing engines and representations, none new (l.58–64).**
   - Base Method → Reasoning Engine, "invariant discovery". Its output is a candidate invariant that goes to the Gate, never admitted directly, because of Art 1.
   - Vertically and Crosswise → relationship reasoning and the typed graph.
   - Complement → Fallacy Detection and Validation, asking "what would make this false?", tied to Art 7 and Art 9: "claim survival under challenge".
   - Base selection → Context Tuple (frame selection).
   - Sutra compression → reasoning-pattern representation: "a pattern, not a truth".
6. **Homonym (l.60).** In "base = invariant", "invariant" means a stable computational base, not a constitutional invariant (INV-*).
7. **Classification table (l.72–77).**
   - As philosophy: rejected for the kernel.
   - As knowledge theory: "too strong".
   - As techniques: engine mechanism.
   - Sutra as template: representation mechanism.
   - Base selection: context technique.
   - Complement: validation/failure technique.
8. **Four misunderstandings rejected (l.83–86).** Elegance ≠ truth, speed ≠ quality, pattern similarity ≠ identity (Art 1), beautiful formula ≠ authority (Art 3).
9. **Final statuses (l.100–106).** Research COMPLETE, Architecture "Candidate Mechanism Source", Kernel and Constitution NO CHANGE. A "Vedic-Inspired Reasoning Pattern Library" is deferred to the Logical Architecture and must still pass the no-new-concepts rule.
10. **Engines considered and not added (l.115).** Pattern Discovery, Complement Reasoning and Context Selection were not added to the engine list. The boundary "EXTERNAL RESEARCH ≠ KNOWLEDGEOS ARCHITECTURE": absorb ideas without letting them redefine the system (l.116).
11. **Epistemic status (l.117).** "Every classification above is a recorded HPA ruling, not an inference". [I] The record is "verbatim-in-substance" (l.3), and the Article cross-references in it may be the recorder's additions. How much is ruling and how much is gloss cannot be separated from this text.
12. **Gate (l.123).** The ruling does not open the review gate of the Reference Architecture.
13. **Unsourced category (l.64).** "Reasoning pattern representation" is not one of the S0241 Ch III representation names (only "typed encodings" comes close). [I] This is a possible strain on the sourcing rule (S0244 l.40).
