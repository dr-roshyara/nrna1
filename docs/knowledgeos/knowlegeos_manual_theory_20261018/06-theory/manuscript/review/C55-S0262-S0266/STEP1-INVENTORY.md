## S0262: Moksha lens, "state transformation of the knower" (brainstorm, AI voice, 736 lines). Depth: full

1. [S] Main thesis (l.10–14): Moksha is "not primarily a knowledge object" but "a state transformation of the knower". Open question: is a knowledge system only about storing correct information, or does it also need a state in which distortion, attachment, bias and confusion are reduced?
2. [S] Meta-goal (l.656–658): "A trustworthy knowledge system must not only accumulate knowledge; it must reduce the distortions that prevent knowledge from being correctly understood." The final insight (l.716–723) is to preserve the *process* by which confusion is reduced. The system "protects the possibility of knowing" and is "not a machine that knows".
3. [S] Zero is reinforced but stays outside the kernel (l.726–736): "Zero = the state where distortion is minimized"; it "does not become another kernel dimension"; it is a "foundational epistemic attitude / meta-principle". [I] Homonym: within this one source, Zero is a "stable reference frame" (l.319), "Zero neutrality" (summary table l.704), "the state where distortion is minimized" (l.729), and "the underlying purpose remains" (l.352). Those are at least two different senses: an invariant frame of reference, and a goal-state of minimal distortion.
4. [S] New candidate (l.181–211): "Epistemic Avidyā Detection", meaning wrong-model detection. The question changes from "what do we know?" to "where could our model of reality be wrong?". The problem is "wrong identification", not missing information: a temporary state mistaken for permanent truth (l.151–160). It is "not a kernel object", a "reasoning capability", classified as a reasoning mechanism.
5. [S] Ontology lens (l.78–135): what changes is the perception of reality, not reality. Knower ≠ Known. Worked example: "the system is down" is stored as Observation (HTTP 500), Interpretation (possible outage) and Conclusion (under investigation). Classified as an existing principle.
6. [S] Attachment means inability to update belief (l.219–233). Rule: "Knowledge must be revisable without identity destruction" (l.262). It links to AGM and TMS and to "Transformation ≠ Deletion" (l.268), classified as existing.
7. [S] Karma maps to history (l.372–413). Each state carries previous state, change, reason, actor and evidence: "current knowledge = historical evolution", not a snapshot. [R] The book's "weighted average": the present is shaped by previous moments and shapes the future (l.398).
8. [S] Ego dissolution (l.480–508): "creator = truth" and "authority = truth" are dangers. "Who said it ≠ whether it is true", i.e. Authority ≠ Evidence, classified as existing.
9. [S] Inner harmony (l.548–572): Moksha does not mean "no contradictions exist"; the relationship to contradictions changes. Contradictions become objects of reasoning, not system failures. A mature state means evidence, reasoning, context and uncertainty are "aligned".
10. [S] Oneness (l.425–470): "not 'Everything is one' but 'Knowledge requires relationship discovery'". This is the opposite of a document repository. Classified as a mechanism.
11. [S] Five refusals (l.586–650): not enlightenment, not a consciousness engine, not a truth oracle, not the removal of uncertainty (a mature system can say "Unknown"), not the replacement of humans (the system is not the final knower).
12. [S] Revised pipeline (l.676–694): Reality → Observation → Expression → Interpretation → Bias/distortion detection → Evidence validation → Reasoning → Knowledge evolution → Wisdom/application. [I] Distortion detection is placed *before* evidence validation, so its inputs are interpretations rather than validated claims. The order is asserted, not argued.
13. [S] Method (l.18–22): the source separates book-derived ideas from the architectural interpretation and from what must NOT be adopted. [R] The uploaded book: Moksha is "freedom from all bonds"; Yoga is the expansion of the limited "I" (l.24); wrong mental maps, "Mumbai with a map of New York" (l.167).
14. [I] Source defect: the summary table (l.700–708) has 7 rows for 8 lenses. It drops the Ontology lens and adds "Liberation", which is not a lens. Avidyā is called a "reasoning capability" in l.205 and a "Reasoning mechanism" in the table; Oneness is called a "Knowledge graph / reasoning mechanism" in l.470 and a "Mechanism" in the table.
