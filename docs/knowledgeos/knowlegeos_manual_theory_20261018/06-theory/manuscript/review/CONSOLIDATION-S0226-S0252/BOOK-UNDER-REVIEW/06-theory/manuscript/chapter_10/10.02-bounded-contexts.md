## 10.2.0 The research question, the admission rule, and what this turn does not do

**Question.** Which areas of responsibility in KnowledgeOS have their own model and language? For each: a responsibility statement, its concepts (CON-IDs), the rules it is responsible for, what it receives and publishes, its evidence and its level. The Principal adds two duties:
- say which context is responsible for the display of well-foundedness;
- show that each displayed class can be read from the record alone, or name it as an open gap.

**The admission rule is a fork, kept open (§2a; the Principal's instruction 3).** Three statements are in the book, and each is a **necessary** condition with **no sufficiency condition** (§6.2 C1; §8.21 C15; map L89: "admission is underdetermined"):
- **CON-0085** (INVALID: no sufficiency condition): a seam becomes a bounded context "only if supported by one or more of nine criteria" (S0033 L29, in meaning). S0033 itself rejected candidates that met a criterion: Knowledge Validation, with "invariants only", was classed a capability family (S0033 L83–86, L128–131, in meaning).
- **S0150's test:** six questions, "ubiquitous language · lifecycle · invariants · authority · reasons to change · independently owned state"; "If not, it probably shouldn't become another bounded context" (S0150 L584–592, in meaning). It is a rejection test, posed for one candidate.
- **S0168's ten criteria** (S0168 L1040–1051, in meaning; §8.24): a third statement, phrased as "evidence increases". *It is not scored here, because the reviewers did not open it and the Professor scored it nowhere (A-16); recorded only.*

**How this turn uses them (Int, the Professor's method; restated at review, A-01, A-03, B-02):**
- each candidate is scored item by item on **one grid**: the six questions of S0150, plus whether any of CON-0085's nine criteria is evidenced;
- "authority" is scored under **two readings, applied to every candidate**:
  - **(α) decision rights:** the candidate decides something, in its own right;
  - **(β) its own rule of who may record what.**

  Under (α), the standing candidate scores **no**: the mechanism "never grants" authority (ARCH-INV-1, §1.14.2-1), and a refusal for a missing permit "is not a judgment" (§1.14.2-4);
- a result reads "**not excluded** under reading X". It never reads "admitted".

**Taken as given:**
- §10.1's classes under (b);
- the boundary test's results (§9.6–§9.17) for the kernel line, shown per candidate and **not chosen anew**.

**Not done:**
- ownership (turn 3);
- the map (turn 4);
- the published language (turn 5);
- any technical interface, code or EKS file;
- any candidate drawn from present structure.

## 10.2.3 The display of well-foundedness

**Responsibility:** **candidate 2 derives** the class and its reasons; **candidate 3 shows** it unaltered.
- *Conditional on the fork (A-11):* under "all six" with reading α, neither 2 nor 3 is a context. Then the derivation would be a capability in 1's record or of an application, and the showing a role of every consumer.
- The duty itself does not depend on the fork, only where it sits.

**What the classes are (definitions, Int; not deductions, restated at review, A-07, B-05).** Let E be a designated record of evidence entries. For an item k at a cut t:
- **refuted(k, t):** some entry of E up to t records a refutation of k, and that entry is not corrected and not withdrawn for the purpose at hand by t *(a withdrawal counts only from a holder of a grant over the evidence's scope: T9-16 (b), §9.18.10)*, whatever support of k is recorded;
- **contested(k, t)** *(added, T9-17 (b))*: some refutation and some support of k are both live, under the same proviso. The conflict is shown, not resolved; resolving it is a recorded act (a correction, a withdrawal, or an act of standing);
- **supported by recorded evidence(k, t):** some entry records support for k, under the same proviso, and no refutation of k is live. *It shows that recorded evidence asserts support, and by whom. It does not show adequacy (§0.1), nor whether the producer checked their own work (CON-0242).* Hence no guarantee, as promise (b) says;
- **not attested(k, t):** neither, relative to E.
- *Undetermined inputs (gap G9, in part):* when an entry's liveness is U, the class is shown only if every resolution of the U gives the same class; otherwise U, with the classes it could resolve to (§9.18.5 g1; check 38).

**Premises these definitions need (named, not yet met):**
- (i) each entry carries a polarity, refutes or supports, and its object: k's content, not k's standing;
- (ii) correction (false from the start) and withdrawal of reliance (true, but not relied on for a purpose) are separate entries with different as-of effects (CON-0214);
- (iii) order and ties at a cut;
- (iv) each entry's validity rests on its producer's authorisation (G4);
- (v) for "not attested", a completeness rule for evidence (§9.15 L280: "Does evidence … need its own completeness rule?").

**"The record" (A-17):** the Principal's words are "the record alone". Whether E is part of D's record or a second designated record is exactly G1.
