## 8.33.1 In plain language

1. **The estate's lenses say a new item "begins as UNKNOWN"**, and that unknown is not "missing" and not "denied". That is the open-world view. The Principal decided the closed world for authority: in the book, an item with nothing recorded is "not recognized" (F). The difference is a decision, not a matter of display.
2. **"The kernel does not calculate authority" agrees with the book.** What the source rejects is authority calculated from evidence or scores. The book's kernel works out current authority from recorded acts and never reads evidence.
3. **One prompt told the investigation what it would probably find, and the investigation found it.** The chat line also calls five lenses "converging", yet all five come from the same conversation.
4. **"A change in one dimension must not silently change another"** has one checked case in the book, in one direction only: evidence entries change no standing value or mark. The other direction is coupled on purpose, and the book's own dimension model (Ch. 3) already has fixed links between dimensions.
5. **"An invariant is a protection against a bad transformation"** is narrower than the book. The book's kernel also guarantees facts about its answers, and it detects some violations rather than preventing them.
6. **The estate ties its modal verbs to strength** ("shall" for established, "should" for candidates, "may" for hypotheses). The book ties strength to levels and labels instead.

  - **For evidence,** "nothing live" is "not attested (in the record at s)" (§9.18 g0), and that is kept apart from "refuted". H-ZERO-001 is met there.

**C4. Dimension independence: one checked instance, in one direction; the book's own model has fixed links (Src; Int).** *(Restated at review: W-02, W-03.)*
- **The sources:** "A change in one dimension must not implicitly modify another" (S0208 L833; INV-KOS-001 in S0211 §20.1). "Must not **silently** modify another"; Allowed: "Evidence improves → Evidence state changes → Authority review requested" (S0210 L121–143). The Zero prompt forbids co-change: evidence and authority changing together (S0208 L170–177).

  Three readings: **co-change**, **causation**, and **silent** causation.
- **The book's home is §3.1:** six dimensions, "independent except constraint set S". The book shows only that they are "not functionally dependent" (§3.1). Under S (for example r = Tacit ⇔ g = ⊥), a change in one dimension **must** change another. **So the unconditioned rule fails in the book's own model.** The estate's five dimensions (S, E, A, Temporal, Lifecycle) are not Ch. 3's six (F13).
- **What the book checks, and in which direction:**
  - **Evidence → standing: non-interference**, stronger than "not silently", since even an explicit evidence entry moves no standing. It holds under the **proposed** premises (c1)–(c5): evidence entries change no standing value (equality empirical on check 33's sample) and no mark within D_W. W's kinds are not admitted to D (§9.18).
  - **Standing → evidence: coupled by design.** An entry is live only if its producer is authorised (§9.18 g0; T9-12 (c), decided). So annulling a producer's grant changes the evidence class without any evidence entry.
  - **Co-change** is neither checked nor forbidden.
  - "Authority review requested" is outside the record: an act by the people who hold authority (§10.4 R1).
- **Int:** the estate counts "Evidence ≠ Authority" as **Established from EKS/PKS/AIP** (S0208 L811, L1127). Its EKS basis is the book's state ≠ authority, not evidence ≠ authority (§8.32 C2), so the "Established" rests partly on that homonym.
- **Not a new concept.**

**C5. "An invariant is a protection against an invalid transformation": narrower than the book (Src; Ded against §9.15, §9.16).**
- **The sources:** "A KnowledgeOS invariant is not a fact about knowledge. It is a protection against an invalid transformation between knowledge dimensions" (S0209 L11, quoting the uploaded consolidation). The question becomes "Which transformations must the kernel prevent?" (S0208 L1036; S0210 L658).
- **Ded, against the book:**
  - **(i) The kernel guarantees facts about answers:** on a record in D, exactly one answer, T and F forced, every U with its reason (§9.15 G1); refutation from the record alone (G6). These are not protections against a transformation.
  - **(ii) Not every violation is prevented:** the book decided, per law, to **prevent** some and **detect** others (§9.16 R3a). For example, acting under ended authority is detected, not prevented. *This narrows S0208 L1036 and S0210 L658 ("prevent"); S0209 already allows for detection ("never happen silently", L30; the filter as a "detection dual", L98)* (W-04).
  - **(iii) The trivial case:** a set made only of protections is satisfied by a kernel that does nothing. G1 ("answers exactly once") is a must-do, and so cannot be a protection only (cf. §9.1 C2).

  So "which transformations must the kernel prevent?" is one of the book's questions, not its only one.
- **Not a new concept.**

**C6. Modal verbs by strength: a register rule of the estate (Src; Int).**

The same document grades the hypothesis H-KOS-Relationship-001 ("New Research Hypothesis", §14) as "Candidate" (§20.1). So it does not show strength readable in each sentence. The book's own strength rule shares its wording with the estate (§8.10, "not independent"). *n = 1; a candidate practice for the register's wording, not adopted* (W-07). S0211 also records "No promotion" (L13) and "No new numbering is created" (§20.1), the same discipline as ES-005.4.
- **Not a new concept.**
