## 1. The question as the commission states it

> **Does Req's kind structure (U6's per-kind cut) and the decided theory determine the mechanism granularity, batching, and the three output forms; and where it does not, what exactly is left to decide?**

There are five objects. Each is to be classed **derived** (with the derivation), **narrowed** (to named options), or **a decision** (one question for the Principal):

1. **G**, the mechanism granularity: G-kind, G-act, G-coarse, G-guard (§9.19.4 L159–164).
2. **Batching**: whether one input may carry several entries (§9.19.4 L164; "undecided", §1.16.1).
3. **Q1's output form**: "the acts the answer rests on" (§9.10 L34), as identifiers or as whole entries.
4. **Q2's output form and scope**: "the entries about k up to s" (§9.10 Q2) for "items with a governed identifier". Are grants and roots items? Are whole entries returned, or their identities?
5. **Q3's output form**: "found (with the grant)" (§9.10 Q3), as the grant's identity or as the whole entry.

Then come §9.20.5's minimality, restated for the derived or decided forms; T9-22, closed or reduced to a decision; and the consequences for T9-03 and T9-04, stated, not settled.

## 4. What would disprove each answer (committed now)

- **DC-1 (against "derived").** A derivation that uses a premise outside §2, an open fork, or a reading the book does not state. Any such step voids the "derived" label: the object is then "narrowed" or "a decision".
- **DC-2 (against G1).** A requirement of Req, cut per act kind, that a G-kind pool and a G-act pool meet differently.
- **DC-3 (against B1 or G3).** A mechanism that appends entries of several kinds in one step from one input, and that satisfies C6's input-origin condition as written.
- **DC-4 (against B3).** Two runs, one with batched inputs and one with the same entries appended singly, that differ in some core observer's answer at some cut, in a guard verdict, or in the reachable set of records.
- **DC-5 (against "the form is fixed by the text").** A line of §9.10 or of a reference definition that admits both forms of the output, or that requires the other one.
- **DC-6 (against Q2-items).** An admitted kind whose entries carry a governed identifier for a grant or a root, or a decided rule that makes grants or roots items.
- **DC-7 (against restating minimality).** A pair of records that the restated forms are claimed to separate (or to identify) and that they do not. For identity outputs the test is the pair §9.20.5 found: entries equal except recorder or authentication status. For act times the test is §9.20.5's M6 pair, against the claim that act time is part of entry identity.
- **DC-8 (the trivial case).** Any claim that also holds for an empty Req, or for a pool of one mechanism, says nothing about KnowledgeOS, unless it is labelled as such.
