## 8.83.1 In plain language

1. **S0356 separates recognizing what was said from deciding what is allowed and from executing it, and keeps ambiguity explicit between them.** It corrects the claim that the interpreter determines the correct meaning: the interpreter decides whether a candidate is admissible for the requested act. Its four outcome words occur in the estate in two different mechanisms. *(Restated at review: W-01.)*
2. **S0356 says the HPA has a rule that development may report a need for research but may never start a research track itself.** The rule is an adviser's report; the book has no record of it. *(Restated at review: W-11.)*
3. **S0357 names eight kernel "capacities" K1–K8.** The book already uses "K" for two lists of its own: the kernel assumptions K1–K7 and the boundary test's assumptions K1–K4. S0357 adds a third list under the same letter. *(Restated at review: W-03, W-06.)*
4. **S0357's kernel decides whether a knowledge-state transition is admissible; an actor and a submitting provider appear only as context, and no holder's grant enters it.** The book's kernel derives standing from recorded acts and, since §9.18, grades from recorded evidence. S0357 also offers a contradiction vocabulary different from the book's displayed classes. *(Restated at review: W-04, W-05.)*
5. **S0357's "minimum Kernel" is asserted, not derived, and its own lists of what the kernel holds do not agree.** Its ceiling, that the kernel be incapable of interpretation, is stronger than anything the book derives. *(Restated at review: W-09, W-10, W, step 1.)*
