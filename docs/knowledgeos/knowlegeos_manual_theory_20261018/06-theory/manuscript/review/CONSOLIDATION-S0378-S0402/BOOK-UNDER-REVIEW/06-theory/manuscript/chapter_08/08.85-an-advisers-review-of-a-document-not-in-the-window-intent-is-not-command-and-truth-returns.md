## 8.85.1 In plain language

1. **S0360 reviews a design document the window does not contain; its details match S0358–S0359 closely enough that it is plausibly downstream of them.** What it quotes of that document is its report. It also reports a decision ("OQ-5 just explicitly established …") with no record of the act. *(Restated at review: W-05, W-08.)*
2. **It separates what a person appears to mean from an authorized request to change the record, and decides the authorization before the domain sees the request.** The first agrees with the book; the second does not: the book records an act and evaluates entitlement inside the kernel. *(Restated at review: W-01.)*
3. **It keeps numeric evidence weights out of the kernel and keeps mechanism scores, evidence weights, domain confidence and authority apart.** The book's kernel already holds a non-numeric grade and an admissibility gate for evidence, which S0360's layering would put elsewhere. *(Restated at review: W-06.)*
4. **It proposes, hedged, a status list per layer, and moves AMBIGUOUS and UNRESOLVED out of the domain into interpretation.** The book keeps the undecided value U inside the kernel; S0360 also calls ambiguity a domain condition while giving the domain only "admissible" and "rejected". *(Restated at review: W-04.)*
5. **Its key design question asks what KnowledgeOS must own "to preserve the truth, authority, …".** Preserving is not deciding; there is a tension with the line's "not truth" only if preserving the truth means adjudicating it. *(Restated at review: W-02.)*
