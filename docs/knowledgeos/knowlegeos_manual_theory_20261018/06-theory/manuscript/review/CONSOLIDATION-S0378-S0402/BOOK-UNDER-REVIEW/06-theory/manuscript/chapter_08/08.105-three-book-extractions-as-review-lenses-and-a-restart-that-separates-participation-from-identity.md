## 8.105.1 In plain language

1. **Within the estate, an adviser turns a classical logic manual into a lens for testing claims about the kernel's boundary**: is the definition too narrow, too wide, or impossible; is the reason established; does the claimed dependency hold both ways; what exactly is absent. The tests agree in form with how the book argues. The book already has the manual's absence vocabulary from an earlier source; its own "unknown" is narrower than the lens's: within D, nothing recorded gives F, and only undecided structure gives U.
2. **Within the estate, the adviser extracts nine principles from a book that puts knowledge first.** The most used, that knowledge is factive and that knowing does not imply knowing that one knows, concern knowledge in the philosophical sense, which the book does not record: the kernel decides standing in a record, not truth. The non-iteration point has firm counterparts in the book (D excludes self-supporting authority; a recorder may not cite its own output). The book differs on defeat: there, new evidence defeats knowledge; within D only recorded acts change standing, and nothing recorded gives F.
3. **Within the estate, a restart gave one working process a second identifier, and one manager's choice for one repair barred it on participation, not on identifier.** The book already had an earlier estate answer of this kind (§8.12 C1); this is a second case, on a new channel, and the general question stays open. The same record keeps that choice apart from a rule, so that no general prohibition is created by accident.
4. **Within the estate, the adviser extracts ten principles from an epistemology textbook.** Two have direct homes: what supports a claim and whether it is adopted are kept apart, as in the book; and a coherent body of claims from one source is not independent corroboration, the book's rule about one lineage, which the window before this one applied to the adviser's own three-provider matrix, made about half an hour earlier. Where a claim came from (its authorship) has no kind in D. The three extractions also contradict each other in two places.
