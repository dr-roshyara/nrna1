## 8.84.1 In plain language

1. **This adviser treats a sentence the first adviser wrote as the user's own description.** Its "Six Pillars" are that sentence's six nouns. What looks like a second opinion carries the first one's words. *(Restated at review: W-07.)*
2. **Its worked example starts the adoption review of a work item and marks every check PASS, though its own rules allow that action neither from the state it names nor from the state its record shows.** The second reply repeats the conflict: its transition rule can never be satisfied.
3. **It replaces one claim by another when its evidence outweighs the other's by half again, and it multiplies evidence weight by 1.2 when the author is in an authority register.** The first is a change of standing with no act, against A1; the second is a rival to the book's decision that only authorised producers' evidence counts. It also forbids a claim and its denial from standing together, which the first adviser required and the book's "contested" allows. *(Restated at review: W-01, W-05, W-06.)*
4. **Its kernel runs a work item's workflow and names itself as the acting party.** The first adviser put producing the next state inside the kernel and workflows outside it. *(Restated at review: W-03, W-04.)*
5. **It separates duties by role (an owner role may not be among the reviewer roles), not by process, and nothing enforces the rule against the actor.** The estate's bars are by session label. *(Restated at review: W-11.)*

- **Int:**
  - **(a) Supersession by weight breaks A1-recognized** (W-01). A claim is replaced when its weight exceeds the other's by 1.5× (L178): *a change of standing computed from a value, with no act* (§8.38 C1, reading (i)). Under A1-recognized a standing change is recognized only if a recorded act effects it (§1.6).
  - **(b) Authorisation as a multiplier, a rival to T9-12 (c)** (W-01). An author's presence in an "authority_register" multiplies the weight by 1.2 (L393–394): *"authority" here in §8.38 C5's sense of producer authorisation or trust, not a holder's grant.* The book decided T9-12 (c): only authorised producers' evidence counts (§9.18, G4). *That is a gate; S0359 makes it a multiplier, under which unauthorised evidence still counts.* S0359 also has a separate gate, a recognised author for admissibility (L350–352). A constitutional rule as the heaviest evidence (L322) joins the rule layer and the evidence layer, against S0357's "Evidence ≠ authority" (L91).
  - **(c) Contradiction that may not coexist** (W-05). S0357's K5 requires a claim and its denial to coexist, each with its evidence (L139–153). S0358 forbids any transition that introduces a contradiction with an entrenched claim (L209), and S0359 fails such transitions (L168–169), **for ADOPT and SUPERSEDE proposals only** (L162), with a third result, CONTRADICTION, "detected but resolution pending", or supersedes by weight (L178) *(scope stated at the consolidation S0353–S0377, E-07)*. *The advisers contradict each other (S0358 outright; S0359 for those two actions), and the book's "contested" (T9-17 (b), §9.18) sides with S0357.*
  - **Not a total rule, and a different object** (W-10). g1 combines support and refutation of one claim and is total (§9.18); S0359 compares two claims, A and B (L175–181), *so "rival" assumes a mapping between the two that is not stated.* S0359's rule leaves ratios between about 1.2 and 1.5 in no branch and does not handle B outweighing A; the multipliers can take a weight to 1.2·1.1³ ≈ 1.597, outside the declared [0, 1] (L311).
  - **Q4's open fork** (W-06). S0359's ten evidence types (L315–325), weighted by type (L381–390), are a source-kind vocabulary, an input to grading: *an adviser's argument on the open fork whether Q4's grade reads source kind or reliability* (§8.38 C1; D-07; G7, §9.18). Its recency multiplier (L399–400) is the "latest wins" rival that §9.18 names as what would disprove the rule; *as an adviser's design it is not a display need, so it disproves nothing.*
- **Not a new concept.**
