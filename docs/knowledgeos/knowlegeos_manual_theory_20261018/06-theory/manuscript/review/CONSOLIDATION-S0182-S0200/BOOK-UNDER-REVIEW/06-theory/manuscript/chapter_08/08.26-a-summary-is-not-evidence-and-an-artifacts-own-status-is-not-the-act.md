## 8.26.1 In plain language

These summaries add no new theory. They give three warnings about reading the corpus.

1. **A summary can break its own rules.** One summary defines "Established" as "backed by direct repository evidence", then marks positions, principles and an asserted practice as established.
2. **A summary can misstate its source.** One summary describes an earlier source as being about something it is not about.
3. **A document's own "status: proposed" is not the decision.** A decision act may have decided what the document still calls proposed. The act decides; the document's label is a claim about itself.

## 8.26.2 Claims and derivations

**C1. A status tag must meet its own definition (Src; Int for §8.25 C7; an attack on S0179).** *(No new concept.)*
- **S0179's rule:** "Established" requires direct repository evidence as current fact or decision (S0179 L16–20, in meaning).
- **S0179's tags:** it marks as ESTABLISHED two "principles" (S0179 L39–40, in meaning). S0177 tags a core invariant "[ESTABLISHED] … (candidate)" in one line (S0177 L108, in meaning).
- **Int.** A principle relayed from an adviser, and a candidate, are not "current fact or decision" backed by direct repository evidence. By S0179's own definition these tags overreach. S0178 tags the same relayed Track-2 principles as ESTABLISHED (S0178 L87–96, in meaning), which overreaches by S0179's criterion, since S0178 states none of its own (W-08).
- *Scope (W-08):* under the rival reading, in which the corpus counts as part of the repository, "the corpus holds principle X" could be a current fact. For a relayed ruling (D7, D8) the rival is not fully answered, so C1 holds firmly only for the candidate tag and is PROVISIONAL for the principles. *(Instances removed 2026-10-27, C33 review W-06: those resting on S0155 (exposed, never used) or on S0161, which is not on the allowed list.)*
- *Agreement with §8.25 C7, one line:* the tag records the summarizer's classification and adds no evidence.

**C2. A summary can misstate its source (Src; Int from §8.22 C7; evidence for §8.25 C7).** *(No new concept.)*
- S0181 summarizes S0154 as exploring LCOM4 for "the multi-language KnowledgeOS binding": "KnowledgeOS pieces (kernel, runtime, intelligence, studio) may live in different languages; the binding between them must stay cohesive and measurable" (S0181 L55–60, in meaning).
- §8.22 C7 records what S0154 is about: one cohesion-metric engine with language bindings, "language-specific understanding, language-neutral calculation", and a PHP engine compared with a Python one. It is about measuring code written in several languages, not about binding KnowledgeOS components to each other.
- *Verified against S0154 itself by the reviewer (W-09):* S0154 describes one LCOM4 engine with a binding per language for analysing source code (S0154 L60–64, L100–117, in meaning). Nothing in it binds KnowledgeOS components to each other. S0181 also omits that the act named no language (S0154 L578–580, in meaning). So a claim "the corpus says X", resting on a summary, must be checked against the source. A summary **can** misstate what its sources say (one instance here; B-09).

**C3. An artifact that is not itself the record of the act does not decide by its status line; an act decides only what it names (Src; Int for §8.17 and the C23 rule; restated at review, W-07).** *(No new concept.)*
- S0177 and S0179 record a tension: the durability ADR "is PROPOSED" ("recommends an architecture and decides nothing"), while "a separate PO/ARB DECISION ACT (2026-08-19)" decided B′ and R-CONFLICT (S0177 L55–61, L140, in meaning; S0179 L154, in meaning).
- **Int (§8.17 C8; §1.13; the C23 rule).** For an artifact that is **not itself the record of the act** (here an ADR), its status line is a claim the document makes about itself, and the act decides. *Scope (W-07):* a decision record's own entry is the act's record, so for it the status is the act. *What is reported, not shown:* S0177 cites the act "per the cross-reference digest" (S0177 L61, in meaning); the act's text is not quoted.
- **An act decides only what it names (W-07).** S0179's DC-2 recommends that the human "execute the approved plan" (S0179 L205, in meaning), while the same document records the plan as "PROPOSED, NOT executed" (S0179 L115, L206, in meaning). The act decided D1–D3 (B′, R-CONFLICT, placement), not the plan. *Deduction:* a summary must not transfer an act's status to an artifact the act did not name. This is a real contradiction inside S0179, and the draft missed it.

**C4. The most authoritative record was the least durable (Src; evidence for §9.17 R4 and §9.15 K1).** *(No new concept.)*
- "I-10 (record outranks prose) makes the most authoritative artifact the least durable → authority and durability are **inverted**" (S0177 L35–37, in meaning). The authority record lived in an untracked runtime directory (S0177 L31–34, in meaning).
- This is the estate problem behind §9.17 (turn 8): K1's record must survive, and a change of carrier needs CON-0265's gate. **One line** with S0160 and S0164.

**C5. The authority log is the source; folded state is a projection; authority is strongly consistent (Src; evidence for §8.23 C1 and C2).** *(No new concept.)*
- *Lineage corrected at the consolidation (A-13):* S0180 L32–41 cites the durability ADR (S0128), S0152 and S0172 (in meaning), not only S0158 and S0159; it is one line with them. The decision record of the durability decision is S0130 (on the allowed list; quoted at §8.17 C9).
- S0180 reports the current state as follows. The authority record is "an append-only transition log", "written synchronously by the executing component, not consumed as an event stream". Derived state is "folded, never persisted". Authority decisions are "strongly consistent today" (S0180 L32–41, L93, all in meaning).
- **A proposal, kept apart (W-10):** "An 'approve' that may silently fail to propagate is not an approval" (S0180 L78–81, in meaning) is tagged PROPOSED, not current state.
- This is §8.23 C2 (the record is the source, state is a projection) and §8.23 C1 (authority change needs strong consistency). It is reported, not shown, and **one line** with S0158 and S0159.

**C6. Two systems; "kernel" is specified in the book and not built in the estate (Src; routed to T0-03; restated at review, W-11).** *(No new concept.)*

So it is one concept at two statuses, specified and not built, not a homonym.
