## 4. Strongest counterexamples and rivals, collected

- **Against B1:** the R1/R2 pair (A-01), and S0053's negative scope with its multi-commit pin (A-02).
- **Against B2:** R0 under C1 = represent (A-05).
- **Against B3's counterexample:** a failed signature is an integrity or authenticity failure, so it bears on A1-recognized too, under §1.12-2 (A-06).
- **Rival to §9.12's structure:** split the inputs into (i) the act's coverage (positive list, negative scope, pin set), (ii) the lifting rule from carriers to claims, (iii) the integrity status of entries, common to every reading, and (iv) occurrence evidence, which only as-fact reads (refutations, marked absences). Under this rival, F37 changes (ii), A1-as-fact changes (iv), and C1 is fixed by the reading. That keeps more of §9.12's conclusions than it loses.
