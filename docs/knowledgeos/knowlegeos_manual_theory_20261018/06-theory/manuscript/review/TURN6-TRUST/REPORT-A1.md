## 4. The six questions of the brief, answered

1. **Step 8:** valid. It needs the union property of unfounded sets (A1-10).
2. **R2's derivation:** valid. The model's walk finds every cycle the U rests on (none is missed in 1,070 cases), but it also names cycles it does not rest on (A1-06).
3. **Refusal-reason definition:** "self-cancelling: target among the grants its own authority rests on, directly or through the grants it acts under" matches "any link of its own authorization chain" in content, and `u_reasons` tests exactly that positive reachability within the component. No U case is left without a reason. The definition is coherent as a description of reasons for U. The decision's "gives U" is not (A1-01).
4. **The D reason:** consistent with C1 in record order only. Backdated or retroactive grants and forward targets show that it does not exclude authorization, or annulment, before the fact in act time (A1-04). "Checkable at each new entry" conflicts with dangling tolerance (A1-03). The attribution is overstated (A1-05).
5. **Θ_f rows:** root annulment and revocation scope are consistent with `phi`, `basis` and `current`. Conjunctive P matches the code, but its gloss is incoherent at the boundary (A1-07).
6. **Dependants:** consistent, except §9.09 T1 (A1-09). Removing the "safe direction" claim also affects §9.13.7 and, through it, §9.15's reading of integrity, which I did not review (commission C).
