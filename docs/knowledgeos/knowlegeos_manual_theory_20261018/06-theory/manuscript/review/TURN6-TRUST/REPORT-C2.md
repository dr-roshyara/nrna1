The reduction to key binding and custody is complete for the act's attribution and content; it leaves out, with stated reasons: timing (C2-07), capacity and duress (C2-09), intent (covered at §9.15.4 bullet 3).
2. **Transcribed.** The regress is applied, but T2.2's premises are not carried (C2-10). "Located at the transcriber" is right when the affirmation is itself transcribed (S0095); it is empty for unauthenticated self-writers (C2-05).
3. **R4.** Valid and definitional (§3). Missing cut-relativity (C2-07).
4. **R5.** The D field list has no recorder (verified). Fail-closed is a labelling rule (C2-08). S0078 reading: faithful, but 6 of 37 documented, 37 of 37 inferred by the source, and a 35/35 figure in the same file (C2-05).
