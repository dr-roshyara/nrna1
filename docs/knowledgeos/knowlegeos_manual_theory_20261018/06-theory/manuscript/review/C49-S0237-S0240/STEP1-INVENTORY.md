## S0237: P4 Constitutional Invariant Map v1.0 (345 lines), depth: full

1. **Status and position (L5–7, L312, L345):** PROPOSED, non-authoritative, not adopted. P4 maps the candidate constitutional material. The kernel decision is explicitly deferred to step ⑤, after P5. Part 7's definition "is NOT the step-⑤ kernel decision" (L312).
2. **Register census (L88–131):** 4 Established (INV-001 Evidence≠Authority, INV-002 Observation≠Decision, INV-003 Assessment≠Authority, INV-004 Projection≠Source) plus 25 candidates. The count chain is 7→11→12→17→22→23→25 (UM-24..UM-35), then "ten consecutive zero-new-row results" through UM-46 (L101). Rule 1: the map adds and removes no row (L26, L131).
3. **Three discipline rules (L24–28):**
   - Rule 1: no new research concepts; new material is only a Candidate.
   - Rule 2: identity of a row is fixed by ID, lineage, registration and validation state, never by resemblance (Similarity≠Identity applied to the register itself).
   - Rule 3: every row carries a layer, one of Kernel Invariant (PRESERVE), Reasoning Mechanism (GOVERN) or Representation (ENABLE).
4. **Kernel vs engine (L261–271):** the kernel is constitutional rules that constrain. It does not reason, generate or validate. Engines (LLM, TMS, AGM, solver, human expert, Validation Layer) execute within it. "AI reasons. KnowledgeOS governs reasoning." Fallibilism applies to the kernel itself: it is "sealed against corruption, not against revision", and Revisability-001 governs the kernel's own design (L271).
5. **Eight families (L135–218):** Identity, Evidence, Authority, Transformation, Temporal, Reasoning, Contradiction, Agent. Each row has Dimensions, a Forbidden Collapse, Evidence, Strength, a Validation Target (all "P5") and a Layer. The rule form is "X → Y collapse forbidden".
6. **Canonical essence, head 43 (L36–54):** "preserving identity, context, evidence, reasoning, uncertainty, agency, and historical evolution". The companion maxim is "preserves the possibility of knowing" (L42). Three clause refinements (existence §29, reasoning UM-45, identity UM-46) are ruled "absorbed": refinement, not supersession. The ruling says "head 43 unchanged … no new formulation" (L54).
7. **Five non-identities (L68–82):** repository, LLM, truth oracle, similarity engine, autonomous decision maker. Each is tied to a forbidden collapse (Storage→Knowledge; AI Output→Knowledge; Confidence/Freshness→Truth; Similarity→Identity, with Coextension-collapse as its limit case, "one axis, one target"; Knowledge→Action skipping authority).

## S0238: P5 evidence-validation launch prompt (90 lines), depth: full

1. **Transition claim (L11, L81):** the HPA declares the research phase "formally closed" and stops corpus expansion. The question changes from "What can KnowledgeOS contain?" to "What can KnowledgeOS not lose and still remain KnowledgeOS?"
2. **Validation principle (L29):** Evidence + Domain Independence + Necessity Test + Failure Test. "A candidate becomes constitutional only if removing it causes epistemic corruption."
3. **Five per-candidate tests (L33–37):** Identity, Evidence, Reasoning, Transformation, Agent, each with a canonical corruption example.
4. **Seven-way required classification (L41):** KERNEL, ENGINE, MECHANISM, REPRESENTATION, DOMAIN-SPECIFIC, REJECTED, OPEN.
5. **Mandatory separation (L45):** a constitutional principle is not an implementation. Topology, type theory, graphs, TMS, AGM and LLMs are mechanisms or lenses, "not automatically kernel laws".
6. **Special validation areas (L49–53):**
   - Avacchedaka: universal, or a modelling technique?
   - Abhāva: a universal requirement, or a mechanism?
   - Coextension: is Similarity≠Identity constitutional, and could KnowledgeOS exist without it?
   - LLM: what does it fundamentally lack? Five capabilities are offered, with "validate, do not assume".
7. **Output schema (L58):** ID | Candidate | Evidence | Domain Tests | Failure if Removed | Classification | Decision.
8. **Final deliverable (L62–65):** an adopted core ("the smallest possible set"), deferred mechanisms, rejected concepts, and the minimum system that can "truthfully be called KnowledgeOS".
9. **Forbidden actions (L69):** no new dimensions or sources; no architecture, databases, APIs, classes or implementation.
10. **Completion gate (L73):** "Smaller. Clearer. More enforceable."
11. **Sequence after P5 (L77):** Kernel Decision → Constitution v1.0 → Reference Architecture → Implementation Model.
12. **Change over time (L21):** the context restates head 43 *without* "authority". It does not use S0237 Part 7's definition with authority added.

**[I] judgements:**
- The removal test is a test of *necessity* only. Nothing tests *sufficiency*: whether the surviving set is enough for "trustworthy knowledge evolution" is never asked.
- Under single removal, two rules that cover the same corruption would each pass as unnecessary. So the test cannot by itself prove minimality, and it interacts with consolidation.
- The gate states the desired direction ("Correct direction: Smaller") before any validation. This expected outcome could bias the result.
- The prompt is the HPA's instrument, recorded "verbatim-in-substance" (L9). It is therefore paraphrase, not verbatim.

## S0239: P5 Constitutional Invariant Decision Matrix v1.0 (256 lines), depth: full

1. **Eleven-law "Adopted Constitutional Core" (L184–202):** Identity; Dimension; Authority; Decision; Projection; Verification; Failure-state; Contradiction; Unknown; Agency; History. The claim is minimality: "each law's removal opens a distinct corruption, and no law's failure is covered by another law's removal-test" (L202). The core is explicitly *not* adopted here; it is a recommendation to step ⑤ (L6, L186).
2. **Census (L121–133):** 26 KERNEL/SURVIVES (4 Established plus 22 candidates); 2 ENGINE/DEFERRED (Intent-001, Dialogue-001); 1 MECHANISM/DEFERRED (Lifecycle-Reasoning-001). There are 0 DOMAIN-SPECIFIC, 0 REJECTED and 0 OPEN rows; the two OPEN items are "not row classifications". Consolidation flags: NonInterference→INV-KOS-001, Context-001→Context-002, Uncertainty-001→H-ZERO-001, Temporal→Revisability. These are "rendered as single laws, not deleted".
3. **Method (L22–27):** the four prongs, with Necessity redefined as "already covered by another surviving row (redundant)?". The launch prompt's necessity was "removal causes corruption" (S0238 L29). This is a change in meaning. [I]
4. **Classification definitions (L41):** KERNEL = survives the failure test, is a principle, and is domain-independent. REJECTED = fails necessity, contradicts evidence, or is implementation. OPEN = undecidable on current evidence.
5. **Principle/device split, used repeatedly (L63, L65, L72, L100, L103):** the principle is KERNEL; the tuple, graph, record schema, verdict vocabulary or detection machinery is REPRESENTATION or ENGINE.
6. **Special rulings §3.1 (L141–159):**
   - (A) Avacchedaka: the principle is universal and the tuple is representation.
   - (B) Abhāva: the requirement is universal, consolidated into the Unknown law; the four-fold taxonomy (prior, destroyed, mutual, absolute) is a mechanism.
   - (C) Similarity≠Identity is constitutional, and KnowledgeOS "could NOT exist without it": "there is no degree of observable match at which the corruption is absent" (L158).
7. **LLM ruling (L163–173):** only 2 of 5 capabilities are *fundamental absences* (persistent epistemic identity, historical belief evolution, "by construction"). Provenance, contradiction management and justification accountability are degradations that scaffolding can approximate. The constitutional justification rests on those two, which map to laws 1 and 11. Distinction: fundamental absence vs degradation.
8. **Completion gate (L245):** "SMALLER": 29 rows → 11 laws. "Every law is a negation … directly testable."
9. **Deferred and rejected lists (L204–231):** the deferred items are the verdict vocabulary, fallacy detection, debate pipeline, lifecycle stages, intent classification, evidence schema, context tuple, state model, Abhāva taxonomy, hypergraph, and TMS/AGM/type/topology. The rejected items are the 16 padārthas, Sanskrit terminology as kernel vocabulary, storage choices, Ontology-001 or a full taxonomy, extensional identity, the single score, black-box generation, and anonymous knowledge.
10. **Minimum system (L235–237):** "A system that applies the eleven laws — and refuses every collapse they forbid … Below the eleven there is no KnowledgeOS."
11. **Change over time: strength labels upgraded (L61–119):** many rows that S0237 labelled "Candidate (tier-3)" are now "STRONG". Examples: INV-KOS-002, Relationship-001, Justification-001, Inference-001, Fallacy-001. No new evidence is cited, and L253 says "only EKS/PKS/AIP tier-1/2 evidence promotes". [I] This conflicts with the KERNEL definition, which requires Evidence and Domain Independence.
12. **Context-001 evidence (L62):** Context-001 is credited with "STRONG saturation (§33, SAME-ID reproduction)". In S0237 the §33 SAME-ID reproduction belonged to Context-002 (L124, L141). [I] Evidence is transferred between rows.
13. **Domain Tests column (L61–119):** the column lists the five validation questions (①–⑤), not tests across the three systems. [I] The domain-independence prong is asserted, not shown per row. Yet the census reports 0 DOMAIN-SPECIFIC, although S0237 L251 records C-8 as PKS-owned and F3 asymmetry.
14. **Minimality claim vs row mapping (L192, L195, L190/L199):** the eleven laws share rows. Pramana-001 renders into law 3 *and* law 6; Relationship-001 into law 1 *and* law 10; law 8's "resolution is forward-only" overlaps law 11. Lifecycle-Reasoning-001, though classified MECHANISM/DEFERRED, is still listed as rendered in law 6 (L195). [I] The claim that "no law's failure is covered by another" is asserted, not demonstrated; no removal-wise independence table exists.
15. **Verdict vocabulary drift (L100):** L100 gives "VALIDATED/QUESTIONABLE/REJECTED". S0237 L195 gave VALIDATED/QUESTIONABLE/CONFLICTED/INVALID/UNKNOWN. [I] REJECTED is also the law-7 state name, so it is a homonym across engine verdict and epistemic state.

**[I] Trivial-solution test against the "negation" property (L245, L235):** every law is a must-never or a preservation duty. An empty system, or one that refuses every claim, satisfies all eleven vacuously. The "minimum system" definition has no must-do capability: no command to admit, revise, resolve or decide. This is a central open point.

**[I] Labels and status:** the "Adopted" core is not adopted (L186). The header says PROPOSED (L6); the footer says "COMPLETED … gate OPEN" (L256).

## S0240: Step ⑤ Kernel Decision (108 lines), depth: full

1. **Decision 1 (L11, L21–39):** the HPA accepts the eleven-law core "as the constitutional candidate boundary". All eleven are affirmed; none is rejected or added. They become "SELECTED KERNEL LAWS".
2. **Status (L5):** the laws remain "PROPOSED · EVIDENCE-BASED · NON-AUTHORITATIVE · NOT ADOPTED" until Constitution v1.0 formalizes them. Distinction: selected ≠ adopted.
3. **Decision 2a (L45–51):** NonInterference merges into the Dimension law. The final wording is "No value passes from one dimension to another without an explicit, governed transition." [I] This *permits* governed cross-dimension transitions, which qualifies "dimensions evolve independently". Independence becomes "no implicit transition", a weaker and different claim from the state-form row.
4. **Decision 2b (L53–59):** Context-001 and Context-002 are declared "the same constitutional content: meaning requires its boundary". The clause reads: "carries its delimiting conditions (its semantic frame) and is never valid beyond them." The tuple stays representation. [I] S0237 kept these as two rows and never listed them as a compression pair in §3.9. The "same content" ruling first appears at P5/step ⑤.
5. **Decision 3, scope freeze (L63–72):** exactly eleven laws, none added, removed or weakened; "No new concepts. No new rows. No new philosophy." Head 43 stands, together with the three adjudicated refinements. The kernel/engine boundary is fixed and the five non-identities carry over unchanged.
6. **Basis (L3, L17, L104):** HPA quotations. "P5 compressed the research corpus into a constitutional candidate boundary." The reduction came from "separation of levels and consolidation": "fewer laws, not fewer truths."
7. **Five strongest validations carried forward (L39):** Similarity≠Identity ("an epistemic identity law, not a database decision"); Avacchedaka; Abhāva; the LLM two-absence boundary; the deferred mechanisms.
8. **What it does not do (L76–78):** no Constitution, no design, and no reopening of P4/P5 classifications. It is "a selection and a freeze".
9. **Change over time: law 6 row list (L32):** law 6 lists 7 rows. S0239 L195 listed 8, including Lifecycle-Reasoning-001, which is now dropped. The failure signature also loses "black-box generation".
10. **Change over time: law 10 (L36):** law 10 gains "'Who knows?' is always answerable".
11. **State transition (L86–95):** Research → P4 → P5 → 11 candidate laws → SELECTED (11) → Constitution v1.0.

**[I] judgements:**
- **Fallibilism.** "Kernel is now selected and fixed" (L84) and "no law … weakened" (L67) sit against S0237 L271, which says the kernel is open to revision under Revisability-001. The freeze has no stated revision condition.
- **Who decides.** The document records an HPA ruling, but was "produced per the HPA's recommendation" (L3). Acceptance is inferred from quoted assessment ("successful completion") rather than recorded as an explicit act on each law.
- **Reopening.** Decision 2a's wording changes law 2's content compared with S0239 L191, while the document claims to reopen nothing.
