   - Called "a much stronger definition" than listing entities (3823).

   - Rule: "authority reference exists ⇒ authorized" is rejected (879–885).
7. **The AdmissionContract is normative, not neutral** (3413–3447).

9. **Relationality test** (1076–1078; Q-CN-4, 2255–2259; 2182–2197).
   - Test: if a property exists only through a relation to something outside the Kernel, must that relation sit inside the consistency boundary, or need the Kernel only preserve its externally supplied representation?

10. **Contradiction ≠ difference** (2126–2152, 970–978).
    - A ≠ B may reflect a different perspective, context, scope or time, or genuine incompatibility.
    - Rule: admitting a conflict requires "an already-authoritative determination" of incompatibility "under the applicable scope/context" (2152).
    - CONFLICTED and ConflictRecord are kept, not changed (948–966, 2148). Linked to F-CM-1 "ambiguity ≠ contradiction" (976).
11. **Admission ≠ epistemic finality** (3304–3339).

12. **Retraction / withdrawal (C-15)**.

13. **Context constitutive vs metadata** (837–867).
