## S0087 — Two-track parallel authorization (Governance registration, 2026-08-18)
1. **A shared boundary owned by neither party.** Neither track may silently change the shared contract or accepted semantic decisions. Any change to the shared boundary is "a third act by a third authority" [S0087 L19–22]. [I] This is a kernel candidate: ownership of an interface sits outside the parties that use it.
2. **Consume vs evolve.** Track 1 only consumes accepted decisions. Track 2 "may evolve the EKS platform independently", but only up to the shared boundary [S0087 L15]. Independence is bounded, not absolute.
3. **Verification lanes stay separate when work runs in parallel.** Neither track verifies its own output [S0087 L24].
4. **A programme is not an assignment.** Governance refused to create a work item broad enough to cover four purposes, because that "would be inventing scope the act did not define" [S0087 L36]. Authority does not grow by aggregation.
5. **The capability-first guard was registered before commissioning:** capability → ownership → context/stewardship → human role → agent → service, with people and agents last [S0087 L32].
6. A posture is enforced by its grant, not by a declaration [S0087 L28].
7. A deferred question is carried forward as an input. The BC-7 role-model question was deferred to ADR-AIP-04 [S0087 L38, L44].
8. **Boundary of the act:** it creates nothing. Recording an authorization is separate from creating work [S0087 L42].

## S0088 — Commission KOS-AIP04-DISCOVERY-001 (2026-08-18)
1. **The ordering is the method:** capability → ownership → BC/stewardship → human role → agent → platform service → technology [S0088 L62–63].
2. **Proposed responsibilities are hypotheses, not decisions.** The six-role model must not be treated as established fact [S0088 L68, L80].
3. **Five-class epistemic scheme:** OBSERVED · INFERRED · PROPOSED · DECIDED · OPEN. "Do not turn a proposal into a decision" [S0088 L84].
4. **Operational boundary rule.** If discovery finds a shared-boundary change: STOP, record the implication, return it as a separate act [S0088 L90]. The rationale is that discovery is "exactly the activity that notices a shared boundary is wrong" [S0088 L92].
5. **A settled context is not reopened; its deferred question is used as an input** [S0088 L96].
6. **"Which are genuinely distinct"** and **"reasons to change"** are investigation criteria. They prefigure the bounded-context test [S0088 L74].
7. Deliverable bans: no agent creation, no silent role adoption [S0088 L102].
8. The producer must not verify its own proposal. The next actor is the verifier, then the decision authority [S0088 L108].
9. Placement is derived from the subject, not inherited from sibling paths [S0088 L52].

9. **"Consumption" is reclassified from OBSERVED to INFERRED.** No mechanical link exists [S0089 L135].
10. **A time-varying count (39 → 40)** is treated as a strengthening of F-8, not a weakening [S0089 L99].

## S0091 — ADR-AIP-04 Discovery Proposal, with Amendment 1 and Correction #2 (process 5e1dd9ee, 2026-08-18/19)
1. **Invariant asymmetry, the "core structural result":** every invariant BC-7 owns is mechanically enforced, and almost every invariant outside it is declared only. "Assurance is strong where executable, weak where prose — and a role model is prose" [S0091 L176–192]. It is claimed to have survived both passes [S0091 L588, L841].
2. **The basis of the absence claim is replaced.** It moves from scarcity of the titles (104 files) to the absence of an **adopting governed act** (ADR, decision record, capability map, registry) [S0091 L719–727]. [I] Validity is tied to an adopting act, not to occurrence.
3. **Four classes of evidence with different weights:** governed · untracked · persona · executable lane role [S0091 L425–433, L729–736].
4. **Authority attaches to the grant, not to the role.** Roles are assignment-scoped (R8). "The record never manufactures authority". "registry ≠ authority" (I-4) [S0091 L61, L182, L266–267].
5. **Decision rights follow the nature of the activity, not the actor.** The AI decides derived facts of executable checks, recommends judgments, and humans hold gates on authority, value and entrenchment [S0091 L92]. [I] A candidate kernel rule for who may decide.
6. **Separation is declared, not attestable (INV-ATTR-2), so C-5 is unowned.** "A process cannot attest its own separation" [S0091 L95–96, L185, L250, L257]. W-4 is read as C-5 "manifesting on the production side" [S0091 L796].

The same document also contains the capability-first guard and the BC-7 rule [S0091 L713].
8. **"Controlling the lifecycle is not owning what flows through it"** [S0091 L157]. Governance is a cross-cutting capability set with one strictly separated duty, acceptance [S0091 L103].
9. **"Role" has three meanings:** lane role · engineer persona · decision right. Six roles would add a fourth [S0091 L169–172].
10. **Authorship ≠ derivation ≠ enforcement** (F-7) [S0091 L100]. The status of C-14 goes absent → live, narrow, unmodelled → **CONTESTED** [S0091 L761–769].
11. **Knowledge distribution is distinct from the knowledge lifecycle and from communication composition:** "Recording a rule is not sufficient". Proposed invariant I-K1: the executing session holds the current rule at START [S0091 L70, L188, L310–313].
12. **I-K2: evidence identity is independent of storage location.** "Historical location is evidence; canonical future location is architecture" [S0091 L189, L216].
13. **Bounded-context test** (own language, invariants, responsibility, reason to change, relationship). C-19 fails it on audience-driven change and becomes a stewardship [S0091 L111, L124, L204, L307].
14. **Absence-claim discipline** (exhaustive, untruncated search plus a statement of scope). It is extended by a scope-verification rule: a truncated search, an over-general induction and a first-match replace are one root, "scope assumed rather than verified". The rule's scope excluded documents, but its rationale did not [S0091 L397–404, L652–669].
15. **The EKP/PKS boundary is deliberately not decided** (OQ-D) [S0091 L218, L335]. A real owner with an unbuilt capability (BC-1) is distinguished from an absent owner [S0091 L220].
16. **The self-history disclosure is used both as a conflict and as evidence**, tagged "OBSERVED (first-hand, self-declared)" [S0091 L19–20, L102].
