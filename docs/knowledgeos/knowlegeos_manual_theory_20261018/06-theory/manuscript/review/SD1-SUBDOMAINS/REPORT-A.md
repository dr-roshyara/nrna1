Object: `06-theory/manuscript/chapter_10/10.01-subdomains.md` and `10.00-index.md`, both drafts before review. All paths below are relative to W unless they start with `docs/`.

## Summary (10 lines)
1. **Critical:** the test in §10.1.2 cannot tell core from supporting. "Needed by the core" implies "usable(k, t) cannot be delivered without it". So any specific, necessary area meets the core condition, and supporting ⊆ core. Under the test as written, items 3, 4 and 10 come out core.
2. Item 1 says §9.6 "places authority recording and the evaluator in the kernel". Item 9 calls F22 "the kernel plane". The book contradicts both: F22 = (C + O), and where CE runs is still open.
3. "visible(k, t) [is] exactly what CE* answers" overclaims. §1.17 treats visibility as a presentation obligation, and §0.1 says visible covers the well-founded value too.
4. Premise 3 assumes an answer to T0-02 (the mission), which is disputed and awaiting a human decision. The fork in item 2 is premise 3 asked again.
5. Premise 1 accepts the "if" half of S0035 L72 and rejects its "then" half ("engineering governance/knowledge would be its Core Domain") without giving an argument.
6. Core and kernel are said to be different (premise 2), but items 1, 7 and 8 and the AI/robot evidence argue from kernel results anyway.
7. Two areas are missing. The book's own §0.7 makes context assembly "the central act", which is a rival core. S0196 L248 lists Rule, Invariant and Term as core, which is a rival to item 3.
8. The Ch. 10 placement is right, and there is a stronger reason than the three given: TARGET-TOC puts Ch. 6 under Part VI, "Applications".
9. Verdicts on the 11 classifications: 1 VALIDATED, 6 PROVISIONAL, 4 INVALID as derived (table below).
10. The largest risk is that "one core area" is read as a result, when it is really the test's informal "delivers alone" criterion.
