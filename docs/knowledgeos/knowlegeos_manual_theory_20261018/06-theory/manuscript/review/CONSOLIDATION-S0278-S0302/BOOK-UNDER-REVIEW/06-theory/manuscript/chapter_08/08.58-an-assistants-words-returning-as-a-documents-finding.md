1. **The research extraction marks several findings "[User's analysis]".** Most of them are, word for word or nearly, sentences of the assistant's third answer in the chat export of §8.57 (for example "We should not claim: 'All meanings can be represented perfectly.' The assistant then reviews the extraction and praises "the document" for a principle ("The words can move. The relationships cannot.") that an assistant's answer had stated minutes earlier by file stamp. One line of texts confirms itself.
2. **The extraction again names articles that do not match the Constitution:** it calls Article 4 "Mechanism" (the Constitution's Article 4 is "Decision") and Article 1 "Relationship" (a clause of Article 1, as §8.50 found).
3. **The semantic layer is the normal form of §8.56 and the semantic language of §8.57, now with research on parsers that ignore word order.** The settled readings apply, and several lines fit the reading in which structure decides identity and the normal form replaces the expressions ("all map to the same semantic object"; "Expressions become replaceable projections"). The review draws "Knowledge Identity" above the layer, which is compatible with identity being fixed by an act but does not show it. The answers again write confidence as one value, and again say a perfect language "violates [IT]".
4. **The review says "Semantic correctness ≠ Truth": a grammar gives meaning structure, not truth validation, and evidence, provenance, authority separation, uncertainty and revision are still needed.** The negative half agrees with the book: a well-formed claim is not thereby supported or adopted. The positive half, what truth needs, has §8.48's two readings: a truth the system holds, which the Constitution refuses, or the reality claims are about.
