## 8.61.1 In plain language

1. **The intake assessment finds that none of the files adds a new row.** But the rule it applies says that under the closure a positive answer "routes to an existing article (confirmation), never a new row". With that rule, "zero new rows" was the only possible result, so it is a consequence of the rule, not a finding about the files. How many files confirm and how many are rejected is not fixed by the rule.
2. **The record says the HPA's commission "is that act" which the closure requires for further material, and in the same breath that it is "not a new-source admission".** An encyclopedia article inside one file is called "evidence behind an already-admitted family", the family reading of "source" that §8.55 found; the book already counts that file there. The translator's book in another file belongs to an intake the closure record itself counted as handled before the closure.
3. **The commission asked for "a timestamp of the saved time"; the record uses each file's modification time and says all 27 files were produced after the corpus sort of 21 August and after the closure.** One file is stamped 19 August, and three are stamped just before the closure. For the three, the closure record itself dates their intake before the closure, so the record's statement is too broad. For the 19 August file it is not settled, and the book's own reading of that file's name as its time is affected.
4. **The record describes the chat export of §8.57 as the "draft" of the grammar records, and the research extraction of §8.58 as their "evidence base".** "Draft" says nothing about who wrote the file: the HPA may have written a draft, adopted one, or commissioned one. The readings of §8.57 stay as they were.
5. **The Governance re-verification finds the repair complete and the chain "presentable" to the product owner.** Its scope includes "current/superseded document integrity", which it applies to whole documents and commits; in that sense the line where one sentence stands both as current and as superseded passes, since the old wording is shown and labelled. Under the plan's own sentence-level rule ("one canonical current definition per section") it does not. The check also passed a site the review had named and the repair did not change. It re-measures the disputed count a third time, and treats a fresh technical review of the repair as the product owner's option, although the plan bars Phase 5 until the DV-1 repair is independently reviewed.
