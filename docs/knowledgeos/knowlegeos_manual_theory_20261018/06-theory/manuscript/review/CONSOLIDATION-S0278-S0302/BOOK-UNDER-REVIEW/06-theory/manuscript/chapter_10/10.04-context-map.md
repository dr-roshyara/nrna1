## 10.4.0 The research question, the participants, and what this turn does not do

**Question.** For each relationship among the areas of §10.2: its direction, which side is authoritative, how they are integrated **in domain terms**, the language both sides share, what changes downstream when the upstream changes, and its evidence. Then the direction law, handled as a candidate law (§2b).

**Participants (from §10.3.6):**
- **the context:** S Standing (admitted, under (p2), stipulated);
- **pending:** W Well-foundedness (G1) *(since T9-19, 2026-10-31: the evidence kinds adopted; candidate 2 admitted at S0, for review; consolidation S0253–S0277, D-03)*;
- **not admitted today** (no owned kind; T10-10 decided (1)): N Norms (G11); C Catalogue (G10, G11; its exclusive rule doubtful). Both stay participants as areas whose content the others read;
- **areas that are not contexts:** M Method, WM Work management and A Assessment methods (applications). **D Delivery is a role** that any consumer takes; rows with D are therefore **display rules on consumers**, not relations between two areas (B-14);
- **outside:** AU, the people who perform authority acts (including the holders of amending acts, R-a); U, the users and AI agents; P, the products whose knowledge is held (PublicDigit is the one instance).

**Direction and type (A-13; CON-0147).**
- **X depends on Y** iff some rule of X cannot be stated, or some answer of X cannot be computed, without Y's published language or Y's state (§10.4.3).
- **Y is upstream of X iff X depends on Y.** Every row is written **upstream → downstream** in this sense, and nothing else.
- Each row is **typed** by one of: **authorizes** (an act that is Y's), **writes** (appends entries under another's acceptance rule), **reads** (uses Y's answers), **informs** (supplies; never binds), **conforms** (shows Y's values unaltered). CON-0147 (validated) requires relations from which the dependency can be identified; the type says which.

**Pattern names** (customer/supplier, conformist, published language, anti-corruption layer, separate ways) are used **as labels for a stated relation only**. DDD literature is a benchmark, not a reason; it was not opened.

**Not done:** the published language itself (turn 5); any technical interface, transport or format; operational failure.

## 10.4.3 The direction law, a candidate law (§2b)

**Statement under test (verbatim, §6.6 C8):** "applications depend on the knowledge system, never the reverse".

**Its meaning (Int):**
- **Depends:** as in §10.4.0, on Y's published language **or** state.
- **The knowledge system** is read as the **kernel**: what the boundary test placed there (§9.6–§9.17), authority recording's contract and the evaluator's rule. *Where CE runs is open (§9.6 L219).*
- **Applications** are areas outside the kernel.
- **The law:** the kernel depends on no application.

**Its falsifier (as drafted):** a kernel decision that needs information **only an application holds** and that **does not arrive as a recorded input**.

**Is the falsifier met? (Ded, at review; A-06, B-09, B-10):**
- **Its first half is met by K3.** CE*'s answers depend on covers, the identity link, θ and the root designation, and none is a recorded entry of D (R12).
- **Its second half is open.** No source shows that an **application** holds those inputs; their source is open (§9.13 L327).
- **The draft's "narrowed law"** ("what it needs from outside arrives as inputs accepted through its own steps") **is withdrawn as invalid.** It replaced the definition, it names no acceptance step, and every read is "through its steps", so it had no falsifier.
- **The falsifier is also nearly vacuous** (B-10). The line between the kernel and an application is drawn by the same test the law would check: the kernel-primitive test **stipulates** a line between a recorded input and what it is about (§9.16 R7 (ii)), and the relocation test moves out of the kernel whatever would make a kernel answer depend on an application (§9.6.4). A violation can be removed by relocation. **The law is close to true by construction**, and that limits what any "for" evidence shows.

**A narrowed law, stated with its own falsifier (Int; untestable now):**
- **Narrowed law:** *the kernel's answers are a function of the designated record and of inputs that are themselves accepted by a specified, recorded step.*
- **Its falsifier:** *a kernel answer that changes when unrecorded application state changes, holding the record and the recorded inputs fixed.*
- **Not yet testable:** no input-acceptance step is specified. That needs the source of covers, θ and the identity link (§9.13 L327), and on whether norms gains kinds of its own (G11). Registered as T10-11.
