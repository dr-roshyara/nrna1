# 8.1 Testing reusability by attempting a second product

## In plain language

KnowledgeOS claims to be independent of the product it grew up in. The corpus tested that claim with an experiment rather than an argument. A fresh session, cut off from the rest of the repository, was given read access to exactly the 15 documents believed to be the reusable core (the "Minimum Viable Kit", MVK). It was asked to start a small, unrelated product: the inventory of a hardware shop. It had to **record every need it could not meet from those 15 documents** instead of looking elsewhere.

The verdict was FAIL. The report calls this "the most useful result this track has produced" [S0025 L182], because the defect was found **before** anything was extracted.

Source: S0025, an experiment report, 2026-08-02, generated, submitted to the Decision Authority.

## The method (Src)

| Element | What was done | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Isolation** | a session with no prior conversation context [L39] | the author "could not be the fresh session": having read the source product's documents, any attempt would be contaminated [L35] |
| **Boundary** | read access to exactly 15 file paths (244 KB), with an instruction to **record, not follow**, any other need [L40] | sufficiency becomes measurable |
| **Reference, not copy** | paths, not a bundle; this "makes reachability itself measurable" [L41] | it also obeys the ruling against materialized copies (BRM-1) |
| **Product** | "StockRoom", one paragraph of business need, "deliberately unrelated to elections" [L42] | a different domain |
| **Residual contamination, disclosed** | the session still inherited the project's `CLAUDE.md`; it was told to treat it as out of bounds and record any reliance. It refused two pulls and disclosed a third [L43] | the hazard is reported, not hidden |

**The reach register** `[CON-0058 · provisional]` (self-review only). Every recorded need outside the boundary is a "reach": 14 in all, 3 of them contamination [L62]. The register is the measurement, and it measures **insufficiency** (CON-0058): a recorded reach counts against sufficiency, while an empty register would only have been consistent with it, since a need met by an unrecorded channel leaves no reach. *One such channel is unrecorded by design:* the executing model's prior knowledge, which can meet a need without any file being read. Not every reach measures the kit: contamination reaches (#11, #12) measure the session, and reach #2 (a template that existed but was not indexed) measures the indexing (corrected after review C4 F-10).

## What it showed (Src)

**The kit did real work** [L45–60]: one bounded context with three others "deliberately rejected" as role separations; a vocabulary with two homonyms guarded in advance; three invariants; a decision record meeting all eight decision-model properties "authored with no template available"; five candidates rejected by the tactical principles. The most telling result: the tactical principles demoted a business wish "from invariant to mechanism" [L60].

**But it could not start the product lawfully** [L110–116]:
1. **the execution protocol cannot be met by one person at the start** ("The MVK forbids proceeding and supplies no lawful way to proceed" [L114]); team-size dependent [L129]. See Ch. 1 §1.14.3, RC-65;
2. **there is no genesis path** to a first architecture decision; see Ch. 1, RC-59/RC-64; Ch. 5 §5.2;
3. a strategic-DDD method seemed missing. *Later refuted by the author:* it exists but is coupled to PublicDigit (SD-1) and was outside the 15 files [L116].

The scope was then narrowed: "This `FAIL` invalidated the EXTRACTION BOUNDARY. It did NOT invalidate the platform." [L18].

**Other findings, placed elsewhere in the book:** two closed verdict vocabularies collide (Ch. 2 §2.4, RC-66); a deferred rule is silent (Ch. 2 §2.8.3, RC-67); a deferral whose trigger is said to have fired (Ch. 2 §2.11, RC-68).

## Three lessons for validating the theory

### 1. The brief can supply what the test measures `[CON-0060 · provisional]` (self-review only)

Reach #13: the **sequence** of the bootstrap came from the experiment brief, not from the kit. "Left to the MVK alone I would have begun at 'Idea → Implementation Plan' and never been told to model a context first. Disclosed because it materially flatters the result" [L77]. "The record is more favourable to the MVK than reality warrants" [L80].

**Int:** every experiment needs a brief. Contamination arises when the brief supplies the **object** of the test: here, the order of steps that the kit was supposed to provide. The same hazard applies to this book's own reviews: a reviewer given the book's conclusions first cannot test them independently. That is why the reviews here begin with a blind reading of the sources (Rulebook v1.3 §6). The reviewer's access to corpus material needs a separate approval (rule AR-11, given as a standing approval in `00-access/ACCESS-RECORD.md`).

### 2. Domain-free is not the same as portable `[CON-0059 · provisional]` (self-review only)

The three methodology documents in the kit were 73% of it by volume and "mostly non-portable case law". They had been chosen because they contained no election vocabulary. "Domain-free is not the same as portable" [L97]: a document can avoid one domain's words and still be one project's history.

**Int:** this is the experimental counterpart of S0024's method point that a coupling was "found by reading, not grepping" (Ch. 5 §5.2). Both say that a **lexical** test cannot establish a **semantic** property.

### 3. What one failure can and cannot show

- One failure refutes a **universal** claim: MVK sufficiency is "REFUTED at n=1" [L162]. **Ded, conditional:** a single counterexample suffices against "the kit is sufficient", **if the report is accurate**, and here on F-2 alone (no genesis path): F-3 was refuted by the author, F-1 is team-size dependent [L129], and the raw record is not in the repository (T8-01). A single success would not have proved sufficiency.
- **Builder, designer and judge are one party:** the author chose the kit's documents ("I listed them as MVK elements" [L97]), designed the experiment, and judges it, having declared that it could not be the fresh session [L35].
- One failure does **not** license new structure. The report proposes seven additions, each to an **existing** home, and puts **four of them (A, B, C, F)** to the Authority "as candidates", "not as amendments", because n=1 admits a candidate under the platform's own promotion ladder [L133–135, L173]. Its product-architecture section "DOES NOT ARISE": "A Bootstrap Engine would have automated a process that does not yet exist." [L151–155].
- **Int:** the same evidence bar appears in the decision docket ("authorizes a question, not a service layer", Ch. 5 §5.1) and in the n ≥ 2 rule for structure (Ch. 1 §1.8).

## What transferred

The execution protocol (zero translation), the tactical DDD principles, the CAP-001 template, ES-005.3's litmus, and "surprisingly" the restraint rules: "A governance kit that argues against its own expansion is the part most likely to survive contact with a new project — and it did" [L186]. Missing: "genesis · role scaling · acquisition · strategic method · one verdict vocabulary" [L187]. *(Review C4 F-10:* the body was not updated after the author refuted F-3 (a strategic method exists but is product-coupled): L130 still calls F-2 and F-3 "the firm findings", L169 "Close F-2 and F-3 first", and L187 lists "strategic method" as missing.) **Tension:** the scope correction calls the engineering process "VALIDATED" [L22], while the per-scope verdict for EEP conformance is FAIL [L108] and the EEP is "also the source of the blocking finding" [L86].

"The platform's reusability claim is no longer an assertion. It is a measurement — and the measurement says *not yet*." [L190]

## Limits (Src, with Int)

- **n = 1:** one product, one engineer, one attempt, "a deliberately small CRUD-shaped domain" [L128].
- **The raw record is not in the repository** [L10] (TODO T8-01).
- **Int:** the experiment tests the kit against **one** foreign domain. It is stronger evidence than S0022's conceptual projection (an actual attempt, not an imagined one), but narrower (one domain, not two).
