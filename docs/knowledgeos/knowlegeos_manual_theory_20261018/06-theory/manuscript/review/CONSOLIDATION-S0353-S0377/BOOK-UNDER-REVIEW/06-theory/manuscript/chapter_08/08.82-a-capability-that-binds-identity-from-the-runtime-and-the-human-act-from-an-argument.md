## 8.82.1 In plain language

1. **What was commissioned as an open interface for the human's decision is now filled by an argument the invoking session passes.** The capability reads the session's identity from the runtime, never from an argument; the START it writes is recorded as the human's, with the words it was given. *(Restated at review: W-02.)*
2. **The capability was built on this work item by a session that held no lane and whose label no record in the window names, while the item was stopped and without any entry on its record.** The producer bar cannot be checked against a process the record does not identify. *(Restated at review: W-03, W-07.)*
3. **The success criterion is that "Start the Governance adoption review" no longer ends in "no lane therefore refuse".** On the work item as it stands, the capability refuses anyway, because the item is stopped; the letter of the criterion is met, its purpose is not. *(Restated at review: W-01, W-08.)*
4. **The only grounds of ineligibility the source states are a lane on this work item or a bootstrap verdict other than UNRESOLVED.** Under those grounds, as specified, the capability would admit its own producer as the adoption reviewer, and the process the second refusal barred. *(Restated at review: W-04, W-05.)*
5. **In the producer's order, the capability is verified before the review; the reviewer's appointment record leaves that step out.** In either order the capability is unadopted when it binds the reviewer who would adopt it. *(Restated at review: W-06.)*
