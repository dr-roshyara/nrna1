## S0311 — semantic compiler / kernel boundary refinement, KOS-EV (930 lines). Depth: full

The file holds two AI turns, split by a lone "#" at l.427. Lines 1–426 answer an earlier document that I cannot see; it is quoted at l.35. Lines 428–930 report a toy simulation.

1. **Two-stage boundary (says, l.5–27, 391–422).** The Semantic Compiler does expression → meaning, giving Canonical Meaning / KIR. KnowledgeOS does meaning → epistemic state. "The compiler answers 'what does this expression mean?'; KnowledgeOS answers 'what, if anything, may we legitimately know'" (l.420–422). Offered as an answer to "what is KnowledgeOS and what is it not" (l.424).
2. **Kernel naming correction (says, l.31–68).** Do not call KOS-EV "the KnowledgeOS kernel". The kernel stays the domain/constitutional core of Reference Architecture v1.1. Epistemic Validation is "an enforcement mechanism operating at that boundary". The Core Boundary lists 7 items (l.54–60): Identity, Evidence, Context, Temporal state, Reasoning/justification, Contradiction, Agency. Typing them as aggregates, services or policies is deferred, for "DDD altitude discipline".
3. **Representation ≠ proof ≠ epistemic admission (says, l.300–324).** "KIR exists" does not mean "Knowledge exists". Called "one of the most important distinctions in the entire architecture". It is derived from a Gödel lens, with "no Gödel Engine" (l.290–296).
4. **Convergence and Separation / Semantic Non-Collapse (says, l.148–198, 773–791).** Equivalent expressions should give KIR(E1) ≡ KIR(E2). Materially different meanings should give KIR(E1) ≠ KIR(E2). Why non-collapse is needed: a compiler mapping everything to UNKNOWN or SAME would reach perfect convergence (l.172–182). [I] This is the trivial-solution test. It is mapped to Navya-Nyāya "coextension-without-identity" (l.198) and to "Similarity must not become Identity" (l.596).
5. **Zero, operational definition (says, l.256–284, 761).** Zero = "refusal to introduce epistemic commitment without sufficient semantic grounds". UNKNOWN follows when equivalence or distinction cannot be established, or context is insufficient. AMBIGUOUS/UNKNOWN follows for multiple readings. Examples are "Someone approved…" → Actor = UNKNOWN, and "may have" → Modality = POSSIBLE (l.729–758).
6. **Contradiction is placed in the epistemic layer, not the compiler (says, l.496–514, 556–558, 687–717).** It needs same subject, same predicate/domain, compatible temporal/context scope and incompatible propositions, which gives CONFLICTED. "Remove contradiction detection from the SCB as a primary compiler responsibility" (l.689) is marked "one correction to our previous thinking", a change over time.
7. **Contradiction inside the source [I].** l.708–710 still has the compiler output "A contradicts B", while KnowledgeOS states "A and B constitute a contradiction under C and T" (l.712–714). This conflicts with l.514 and l.689, unless it means the compiler only *represents* an asserted contradiction. The text does not say which.
8. **Five success properties replace "95% accuracy" (says, l.765–827).** They are Semantic Invariance, Non-Collapse, Epistemic Non-Invention, Identity Preservation (K1 → K2, continuity and history kept, K1 ≠ K2) and Contradiction Integrity. [I] Only the first three are compiler properties. Identity Preservation and Contradiction Integrity were just assigned to KnowledgeOS (item 6), so the list mixes the two layers.
9. **Toy simulation results (reports, l.428–452).** 10 families, 1,000 synthetic cases, "overall invariance 80.0%". Equivalence 66.7%. Distinction, context, transformation, temporal and unknown/ambiguity are 100%. Contradiction 0%. Abstention 80%. About 1.0 M cases/s, "almost no architectural significance". Caveat: "controlled toy simulation… not evidence that a production compiler will achieve these numbers" (l.430). Source arithmetic [I]: the unweighted mean of the 8 rows is 80.8% (command below), not 80.0%. The weighting is not stated. "Unknown/ambiguity handling 100%" and "Abstention detection 80%" look like near-identical measures with different results, and the difference is never defined.
10. **Over-claim set against the caveat [I] (l.518, 556, 900).** The text says "This validates the two-stage architecture", "the contradiction failure is actually good architectural evidence" and "does validate the architectural decomposition as a useful experimental model". This reads a failure as a confirmation, after the fact, from a toy model the source itself calls non-evidential. l.896 tones it down to "directional evidence only".
11. **Refined pipeline (says, l.636–683).** Surface Parser → Karaka-inspired Semantic Parser → Semantic Type System → Avacchedaka Context Binding → Canonical KIR → KnowledgeOS (Evidence, Identity, History) → Epistemic State with three outcomes: VALIDATED, UNKNOWN, CONFLICTED. [I] The core list here is 3 items against 7 at l.54–60. l.277 adds AMBIGUOUS, which is not among the three outcomes.
12. **Layer allocation of failures (says, l.904–926).** A language problem goes to the compiler. A meaning problem goes to semantic type/context. Knowledge, epistemic and contradiction problems go to KnowledgeOS. "That separation is exactly what we wanted DDD to uncover."
13. **Sanskrit as a hypothesis (says, l.238–252, 628–630).** "Can a grammar-inspired intermediate representation make surface variation irrelevant while preserving distinctions?" "A research hypothesis, not a finished solution." Freeze the research direction and stop adding lenses (l.328–348).
14. **Experiment design and measures (says, l.350–385, 831–888).** "No 95% target. No additive accuracy. No claim that it beats LLMs." The source lists 13 measures (l.369–381), against "the proposed eight" plus a ninth (l.150). The eight are not given. The design changes over time: "1,000 cases in families" (l.202–204, with 10 families in the simulation) becomes "1,000 semantic families, not merely 1,000 sentences" (l.839). "KOS-SCB v0.2" names both the simulation (l.428, 896) and the planned real experiment (l.837).
15. **Open questions.** Does a grammar-inspired IR reach convergence (66.7% in the toy)? What makes an expression family "equivalent" (≈) against "materially different"? Where does the line run between UNKNOWN and AMBIGUOUS? A testable "better than an LLM" claim is stated: maintaining identity-bearing, evidence-constrained epistemic state (l.426).

## S0314 — KOS-SESSION-BOOTSTRAP-001 Diagnostic and Activation Registration (93 lines). Depth: full

1. **Headline finding (says, l.8–9).** "The divergence is NOT model-dependent. Two AI providers, same repo, same records, same injected context, resolve the SAME governed session differently — and both cannot [be right]. The only difference is the env-level model endpoint. The defect is coordination legibility, not model capability." [I] Logical tension: if the endpoint is the only difference and the results differ, the outcome *does* depend on the model. The intended claim is probably that the *cause* is under-determination: with no deterministic resolver, interpretation is left to the model. That is a claim about the root cause, not "not model-dependent". The step from observation to conclusion is implicit.
2. **Measured facts (reports, l.17–25).** (1) The AST-016 resolver returned AMBIGUOUS, not operable, with 4 candidates. 3. **Root cause (says, l.42–51).** It is coordination legibility, not a rule gap. "The four refusals were the system working as designed… this correction adds the exit without touching the rules." [I] "Four" refusals is asserted. Item 2.5 does not count them, and "4 candidates" (l.19) is a different count.
4. **Provider-independence invariant (says, l.29, 38–40).** The bootstrap makes no model call, so identical authoritative state must give byte-identical JSON under either provider, pinned by S17. [I] S17 varies only an env *shape* ("Claude-shaped / DeepSeek-shaped env"). Because no model is called, the test is close to true by construction; it shows the env is inert, not that two model sessions behave alike. "A Claude-backed session resolves identically" (l.40) is stated, not shown. The matrix row says "must equal", a requirement, not a measurement.
5. **Identity as evidence, never a grant (says, l.46, 49, 60).** Process labels are token-scanned from free-text executionContext, all referenced labels are reported, MATCH / MISMATCH / UNKNOWN is computed, and the check fails closed on multiple labels, MISMATCH or UNKNOWN. (3) Cross-provider conformance (S17) is an acceptance criterion. Scope approved, with an explicit not-modified list (l.72).
