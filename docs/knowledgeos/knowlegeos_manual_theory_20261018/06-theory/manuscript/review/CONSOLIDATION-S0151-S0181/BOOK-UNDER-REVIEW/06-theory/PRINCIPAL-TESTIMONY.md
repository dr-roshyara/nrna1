## PT-001: three things kept apart

1. **Authorship:** an AI wrote the S0064 reviews (PT-001).
2. **Agreement:** the Principal agreed with them (PT-001). This is a human act on an AI-authored review, not a review written or performed by a human.
3. **Independent human verification:** none is shown. S0065 records the acceptance as made "without a separate independent verification of the v2 model" [S0065 L20].

**For the theory (Int, open, not decided):** the case is an instance of *AI authors, a human agrees*. Is an agreement an act in the sense of A1 (Ch. 1 §1.6)? Can it replace a review, or does it only adopt one? It is related to §1.12 (record ≠ act) and §1.14.3 (roles), and kept open in Ch. 5 §5.6.3.
