Reviewer B. Objects: §10.3.6 (the decision (B), the K2 condition, its application) and §10.4 (context map, draft). Labels: V = verified by me against a cited line; R = reported by the book, not verifiable by me; I = my interpretation.

## 1. Summary (10 lines)
1. K2 compatibility (K2-d, K2-w, K2-r) is shown, but nearly vacuously: K2 says nothing about entry kinds. K2 is not adjusted (V). K2 itself rests on CON-0261, PROVISIONAL (V, register).
2. The claim that (d1)–(d3) are "of the same form" as the grant-owner rule (§9.16 R4a) and the store-owner rule (§9.17 (c′)) is not shown. Those rules name an owner *application*, read from the record. (d1)–(d3) name an owner *context* per entry kind, and D has no context field (B-01, B-02).
3. Three of standing's ten kinds (consume, transfer, designate) are recorded by owner applications under existing rules. As worded, (d2) conflicts with that or collapses into K1 (B-01).
4. (d3) means a wrongly recorded entry of a W or N kind puts the whole record outside D, so S's answers change. That is a counterexample to §10.4.5 ("no relationship runs W→S, N→S as a dependency") that §10.4.3 does not list (B-03).
5. The header count is wrong. The table has 8 ◇ rows against the stated 9 of 13. R11 and R13 name W and N and are under-marked (B-08).
6. The direction law's meaning is not exact. Its definition counts "language or state", but its text excludes only state, and by its own definition S depends on N through K3 inputs. The "star" claim contradicts this (B-09).
7. The falsifier is nearly vacuous. It exempts anything that "arrives as a recorded input", and the book's kernel test (§9.16 R7 (ii)) and relocation test (§9.6.4) already stipulate that line. The "for" evidence is partly true by construction (B-10).
8. T10-10 omits a third option (a separate designated record, which the Professor's own K2 observation raises). G11 and T10-10 are used but not registered. The discrepancy with the decision's consequence line is correctly stated within the book (B-06).
9. Missing or unexplained relationships: N→WM (law 27), the people who hold amending acts → N and W, the user → S, and any translation boundary (S0162's second half). Evidence omitted from §2b: K1's outside commitment, the root designation, and S0066's authority direction (B-11 to B-14).
10. Verdicts: §10.3.6 PROVISIONAL (K2 part acceptable, rule form and application of norms need correction). §10.4.3 not accepted as stated. The map is acceptable only as S0 after B-01, B-03, B-08, B-09 and B-10 are corrected.

Process independence: reviewed on a different model from the Professor, with no shared context. This is process independence, not personal independence.
