9. **Agent pipeline** (L21): Observation → Claim → Evidence → Reasoning → Confidence → Unknown boundary → human validation if needed.
10. **Change in character** (L50, L66): from "manages knowledge" to "manages the boundary between knowledge and non-knowledge" ("epistemic operating system"), and from "protects knowledge" to "protects the possibility of knowing". This is the third derivation; the source calls it the "strongest convergence evidence".

## S0281: Session Completion & Next-Actor Handoff Protocol v1.1 (governance, 424 lines). Depth: FULL

This is a normative protocol, not a log or listing, so a full read was required.

1. **Thesis** (L9, L67): the human should "not need to reverse-engineer the next responsible actor". Reconstruction becomes recognition; approval replaces discovery.
2. **Coordination ≠ Authority** (L31–33, L215–217): the protocol answers "who **should** act?" and the workflow engine answers "who is **allowed** to act?".
3. **Mandatory report** (L73–109): 12 fields. "No field may be omitted"; NONE is allowed, blank is not. The report is advisory evidence that "registers nothing, grants nothing" (L111).
4. **Current State is consumed, not authored** (L103, L221). It is read from the single authoritative record, with states CREATED / ACTIVE / HANDED_OFF / COMPLETED / STOPPED / FAILED / CANCELLED.
5. **Deterministic routing** (L117–127): a mapping from (completing role, completed obligation) to next actor. Reviewer → Governance; Author → Independent Reviewer; Governance → PO/ARB; same responsibility → same role; no fit → PO/ARB.
6. **Constraints** (L131–134): no invented roles (escalate instead); the producer bar (never your own next actor for review, acceptance, approval or decision of your output); decision authority belongs to the human PO/ARB; the recommendation "assigns nothing".
7. **Self-continuation** (L140–156): YES/NO decided by rule, never by preference. Bars on continuing: a reviewer accepting its own review, an author approving its own correction, Governance acting as technical reviewer of its own subject, a producer verifying its own output.
8. **F1 amendment** (L158–191): "Can continue?" = "operational continuity only", "enough context to continue analysis". It is not ownership, authority, START, a human decision or acceptance. "Changes nothing in the outcomes above" (L160).
9. **Human authority boundary** (L197–207): the AI may recommend, explain, summarize and flag; it may not create a humanAct, approve itself, transfer authority, bypass REGISTER/HANDOFF/START/ACCEPTANCE, or decide, adopt, accept or certify. The report is "the *last* AI-authored artifact in a slice".
10. **Ownership** (L223): held for the successor on HANDOFF and passed only on START (G-3). HANDOFF vs COMPLETE (EKS-04) is presupposed.
11. **EKS-07 boundary** (L268–282): the protocol is an input EKS-07 may later consume or supersede, never an implementation of it. No identity attestation.
12. **Six verification requirements** (L288–297) and **four test cases** (L303–382). Case 4's YES must carry a reason grounded in a rule (L382).
13. **F3 amendment** (L386–400): the protocol complements the End-of-Commission checklist; both are binding; it is not a parallel process.
14. **Status and stop** (L404–418): ADOPTED OPERATIONAL PRACTICE, not accepted architecture. Next step ② says agent templates were applied after the PO/ARB confirmed the reading (L418).
15. [I] **Homonym in YES.** In §4's table (L146), YES = "same responsibility continues **and** no independence constraint applies" (a rule test). In F1 (L166), YES = "enough context to continue analysis" (a capability test). These are two different predicates. A reviewer has the context to continue, so it passes F1's YES but fails §4's YES. "Changes nothing" (L160) hides a redefinition.
16. [I] **Who accepts is unclear.** Reviewer → Governance is justified by "acceptance authority remains separate" (L123, L259). But §5 forbids "AI (the session)" to "accept … anything" (L205). If Governance is an AI session role, acceptance has no AI holder. The source distinguishes acceptance, registration and decision but never says who holds acceptance.
17. [I] **"Deterministic" depends on judgement.** The routing is called "deterministic" (L117), but the input "same responsibility continues / no constraint applies" (L126) needs judgement.
18. [I] **Trivial case.** A report with every field NONE meets the structure rule (L73), and the only must-do (produce the report) makes no demand on content quality.
19. [I] **Possible source defect.** The role vocabulary (L131) lists 7 names, or 6 if "Independent Reviewer / Architecture" counts as one. L424 says "six-role", yet the routing table treats Independent Reviewer and Architecture author as separate roles.
