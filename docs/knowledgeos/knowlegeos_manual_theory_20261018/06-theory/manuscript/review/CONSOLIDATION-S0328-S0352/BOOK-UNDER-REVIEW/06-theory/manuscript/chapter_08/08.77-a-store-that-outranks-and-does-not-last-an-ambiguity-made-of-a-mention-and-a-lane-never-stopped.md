## 8.77.1 In plain language

1. **The workflow store, the record every lane and human START is written into, is excluded from version control.** The prose about it is kept; the record it registers is not. The book has recorded this inversion several times (CON-0233).
2. **The live ambiguity the correction was said to explain truthfully is, by the implementer's report, one assignment and one mention.** So AMBIGUOUS there was safe, not the right answer. The resolver reads labels from free text, and both of its patterns can match mentions as well as assignments; only the REGISTER entry says who is assigned. The verification made "must stay AMBIGUOUS" its pass condition, so it confirmed the artifact.
3. **A fresh verifier refused for want of a lane, and named a second blocker: the implementation lane was never stopped.** It also says what would end it: a STOP transition, recorded by Governance. The book had left both open.
4. **A new commission makes "Governance Engineer" and "Communication Engineer" operating responsibilities, not workflow roles.** That adds a sense of "Governance". The book already classes the recording capacity the chain used as a declared recorder; its lane status is the Principal's question.
5. **The new commission consumes the orchestration tool as the one mechanism, though the tool is neither verified nor adopted, and it may not change it.**
6. **The same commission makes it the norm that the system, not the human, writes the record of the human's act,** with "never fabricated" as a rule nothing inside the record can check.
