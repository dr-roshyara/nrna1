## Summary (at most 10 lines)
1. §9.22 has the right overall shape: concepts and mechanisms are rejected as the unit, the record's type plus Req's references are kept, a K class is added, and law 1 is derived.
2. Its central marking claim does not hold under its own definition of F: "Req forces the laws about what the record keeps" (rows 2, 7, 8, 10a).
3. Under C6 result 7 (a), each answer is checked against its reference state by state. A step that silently deletes an uncited entry keeps the record in D: 1,841 of 1,841 probes; in 55 of them Q1's value changes with no act recorded. So Req does not force append-only, H1′ or "positions only grow" (DC-5 met).
4. The F marks rest on Req's text, "the same call gets the same answer", read across states. That is not the formal Req the section uses.
5. These laws therefore come from neither U-k (D is a set of records, and both records of a silent deletion are in it) nor U-r. They are premises of the type (§9.1 L99). The commission's limit asks for this to be reported as a result; §9.22.3 says instead that the pair carries all the laws.
6. Law 1: clause (ii) of the statement over-claims. With plural bases, a cited grant can be F and have no chain to a root while CE* = T (one probe). The derivation is valid only once (ii) reads "some cited basis".
7. Mark corrections: row 22's HP part was decided (a) under F36, so it is not App; row 11b is K7, not K6; row 27 is W while E1 stays deferred; row 26 is not yet expressible; row 23 is F under Req v3; row 30 is not order-independent for Q2 under (c); row 9b's detection covers consumptions only.
8. The senses of "primitive": under Question O (c), P-field's removal test is passed by every field, so it no longer discriminates; P-requirement is missing.
9. §9.22.6 decides nothing that is the Principal's.
