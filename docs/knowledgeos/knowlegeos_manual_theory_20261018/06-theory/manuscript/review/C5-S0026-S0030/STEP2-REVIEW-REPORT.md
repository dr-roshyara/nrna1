## Overall verdict

The four sections are careful, and they converge with my blind reading on about half the points:
- Domain rests on absence.
- Portability is a lattice, not a ladder.
- The decision-per-relation rule is applied incompletely.
- Evidenced is not measured.
- The register breaks its own standing rule, and a projection carries normative rules.

**Six results do not survive refutation as stated:**
1. **§3.8's "collapse without loss" counterexample is invalid.** Capability stands in three relations, but with three *different* partners. The criterion the section itself sets ("at most one relation per pair") is actually met by every listed edge.
2. **CON-0066 should not be VALIDATED.** "Only a capability executes" holds by stipulation. It is contradicted by:
   - S0026 itself (runtime assets execute, L59);
   - S0029, where the capability is a port and the adapters execute;
   - §6.1 C6, where a *person* realizes and executes a responsibility.

   The derivation "knowledge does not execute" quietly uses the circular premise it claims to reduce: S0026 sources its "exactly one" claim to I-11, which *is* "Knowledge does not execute. Capabilities execute."
3. **§6.1 C2's "a second product is a necessary condition" overreaches.** Non-identifiability from one image is lifted by prior constraints as well as by more data. §6.1 C3 performs exactly such a one-product split of SD-1. Two products do not suffice either.
4. **§5.3 C1's "pre-registered stopping rule" is contradicted by the register it describes.** The criteria were revised on the day of use ("OVER-APPLIED", "over-strict"). Rows were created at activation. The thresholds follow two opposite policies. S0030 is not a second instance, because it pre-registers measurements but no decision rule.
5. **"Two engines" is contradicted by §5.3's own count.** Incidents form a third category, evidence acting without a pre-registered criterion. The single "criterion-fired" activation was caused by an authority commission.
6. **§4.1 states the independence rule but does not apply it.** R-6 n=3, the explanatory-precision n=2 and MO-1's count of 1 all fail it. The proposing instance is counted as instance 1, which is the double use of data that §4.1's own statistics note warns against.
