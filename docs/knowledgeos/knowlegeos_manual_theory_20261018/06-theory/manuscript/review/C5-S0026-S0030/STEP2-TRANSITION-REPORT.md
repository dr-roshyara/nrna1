## Overall verdict

**PARTLY.** Each section moves the theory forward and mostly fits the theory map. The main results hold or can be repaired:
- the partial-order condition;
- the Szpilrajn remark;
- the {0,1}² lattice;
- non-identifiability from one instance;
- "evidenced ≠ measured";
- the activation two-engine split.

Six steps fail as written:
1. **The derivation of "knowledge does not execute" (§3.8) is circular.** Its premise CON-0066 rests on I-11, which already states the conclusion.
2. **The CON-0064 counterexample is invalid.** It argues from one element having several partners, not from one pair standing in several relations. By its own criterion, the listed edges can be collapsed into one labelled graph without loss.
3. **§3.8 contradicts Ch. 1 §1.5.** §1.5 classes scripts as capabilities because they execute; §3.8 says a script is still an artifact.
4. **§6.1 C3 picks one rule/binding split from a single product, which §6.1 C2 has just proved is not identifiable.**
5. **§6.1 C7 cites S0030 as support, but S0030 and S0027 are counterexamples to its premise.**
6. **§5.3 C2's resolution (inferred vs specified) does not survive** S0029 L43, which concedes that `init`'s own extension needs "the second adopter again". The better resolution separates claims by scope (local automation vs reuse claims).

Several "validated" labels also run ahead of their evidence: CON-0066, CON-0070 as applied, CON-0079 and CON-0071.
