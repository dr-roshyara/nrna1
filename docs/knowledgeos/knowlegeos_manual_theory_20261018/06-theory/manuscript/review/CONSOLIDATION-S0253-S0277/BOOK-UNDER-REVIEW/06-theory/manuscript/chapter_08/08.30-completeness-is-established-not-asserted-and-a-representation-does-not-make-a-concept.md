## 8.30.1 In plain language

1. **"We did not need to read the rest" must be shown.** One review accepted "not needed" without reasons. A second check then found that the unread part contained facts that changed two of the reconstruction's main claims.
2. **A numbered sequence with no gaps does not prove nothing is missing.** The estate reproduced the loss itself: in its trials, the surviving records were still numbered without gaps.
3. **Writing and keeping a record does not make you its owner.** Reading the project's knowledge does not make you its owner either.
4. **A neat mathematical form is not a reason to add a concept.** The concept must come first, from the domain; the representation comes after.

## 8.30.2 Claims and derivations

**C1. Corpus completeness is established, not asserted (Src; evidence for CON-0120 and the book's whole-reading rule).** *(No new concept.)*
- The reconstruction left the migration plan's body unread and called it "not needed for current-state reconstruction" (S0198 L39, in meaning). The gate review calls this "a conclusion without the reasoning", and states the discipline: "corpus completeness is established, not asserted". Otherwise the next phase would consume "an artificial UNKNOWN" (S0198 L44–46, in meaning).
- The verification found that the unread body opens with a "Current-state inventory — OBSERVED, measured" (S0199 L50, in meaning). It contains facts that "materially affect" two load-bearing claims of the reconstruction. So the clause is "contradicted, not merely unestablished" (S0199 L56, L72, in meaning).
- **Int (restated, C37 W-04).**
  - Reading "not needed" as an absence claim about the unread part **extends** CON-0120 (validated for searches) to a reading: Int, marked.
  - The case **agrees** with the book's whole-reading rule (v1.4 §3) without **testing** it. S0198's second option, scan and classify the body (L50, in meaning), would also have found the inventory, whose heading announces itself (S0300 L379). The failed guess was a guess about kind ("a migration plan is largely target-state", S0198 L44, in meaning), which shows that a document-level label can be wrong for a mixed document. *n = 1.*

  - *Two further points (C37 W-11):* an UNKNOWN produced by not reading differs from one produced after examining the evidence ("an artificial UNKNOWN", S0198 L46, in meaning); an UNKNOWN is itself an absence claim. The corpus-boundary rule, extra evidence "admitted by role, with a recorded finding" (S0197 L53, in meaning), is the must-do that keeps "completeness established" from being met by shrinking the corpus to what was read.

**C5. An elegant representation does not make a concept; semantic state and authority state are different dimensions (Src; evidence for §3.2 and §1.14; attack on the source's model).** *(No new concept.)*
- S0200 proposes "K = S + iA", with S the semantic state and A the authority state, on the ground that "What something says and whether the organization is authorized to treat it as true are different dimensions" (S0200 L36–46, in meaning). It warns: "We should not create a domain concept merely because mathematics gives us an elegant representation." [S0200 L394]. It proposes the idea as a research hypothesis with a falsifier: "If no, discard the model." [S0200 L448]
- **Int and attack (restated, C37 W-02; the Professor's, Ded).**
  - **S0200 merges what it claims to keep apart.** Its A is "how much organizational authority" (L86, in meaning), and its positions are approved, proposed and rejected (L91–103). Those are status values, so S0200's A axis is the book's *state* (§3.1, CON-0003), not its authority (§1.14). Its "semantic state" means content: home §1.4 ("detail does not confer authority"), and "state" is a homonym here. So the claim at L46 is sound (§1.14), and the representation does not realise it.
  - **ΔK is undefined on such an axis.** Differences need an interval scale, and status values are at best ordinal. So ΔK = ΔS + iΔA (S0200 L229–239) has no meaning as written.
  - **Nothing complex-specific is used**: no multiplication, no i² = −1. Every use is a point in a plane, so "complex" adds nothing over an ordered pair (the reviewer's inventory, R).
  - **What S0200 breaks is its own ordering rule** (L398–414: current architecture, then discovery, invariants, concepts, then representation). It proposes two kernels and a layering first (L356–388, in meaning). L394 forbids creating a concept from a representation, and S0200 creates none, so it does not break L394 (corrected at C37).
  - The semantic/authority split of conflict (L257–263) can be stated without arithmetic. Its authority side is CE*'s U for conflicting acts with no order between them (§9.13), and CON-0241 ("a positional conflict is not yet a conflict of authority", §8.18 C3) applies only inside one record.
  - The falsifier "without losing domain meaning" (L444) has no criterion; home §1.4 point 2 (C37 W-11).
  - The hypothesis is not adopted, and not refuted for the magnitude pairings S0200 itself proposes (C+iT, L166–172; E+iP, L345; the five-component vector, L315–334) where interval scales are given. S0200 concedes it "would probably move beyond complex numbers" (L313) to a "special two-dimensional projection" (L336), and uses its first axis in three senses (L77–82, L204–209, L330), all in meaning (I-12). Its two kernels and "EKS/PKS as applications" (L359–385) are framings for T0-03 and T0-03b.
