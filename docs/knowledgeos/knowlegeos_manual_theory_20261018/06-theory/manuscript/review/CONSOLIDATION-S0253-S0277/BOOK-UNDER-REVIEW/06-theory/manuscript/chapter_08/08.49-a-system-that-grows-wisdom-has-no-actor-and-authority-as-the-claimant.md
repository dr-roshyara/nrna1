## 8.49.1 In plain language

1. **The session now asks how KnowledgeOS can "grow wisdom" and become "a maturing intelligence system".** Its answers say a "wise system" will "update understanding", that "new understanding [is] adopted", that "the system remembers why it believes". Who adopts is not named. Three readings fit: the system itself (which the book rules out: only a person holding a grant adopts, by a recorded act); "the system" as shorthand for the people who run it; or wisdom as engine work, which leaves standing untouched (the same session puts everything beyond the kernel into engines and mechanisms). Wisdom formation is about content, so it lies outside promise (b). A "wise" output such as "future designs should change the cooling" is advice, which informs action as the Constitution allows.
2. **The session's own summary lists "Authority" as a dimension and explains it as "who is making this claim? what is their role? what is their basis? who knows?".** That is the knower and the source of a claim: what the Constitution calls agency ("'Who knows?' shall always be answerable") and what the book calls provenance. The book has a home for it; what is new is only the label. Its other uses of "authority" are a claim to truth (in its list of collapses) and an assigned grant (in the operational review).
3. **At 11:28 the assistant proposes, as the next step, reducing everything to "the smallest constitutional kernel", which the record had already proposed before ten that morning.** Three readings fit: the chat did not know the record's state; it points back to a step already taken; or both draw on a common earlier text (the session's summary uses the record's own wording of the character, the refusals and the layers). Under the second reading, adding the new lenses to the kernel would meet the Constitution's rule that amendment adds no new concepts.
4. **The independent review of the handoff protocol agrees with the book:** a recommendation, and deference to it, are no acts; its own verdict is advisory and the owner decides. It calls that verdict "my decision" and qualifies it in the same section. It reads the research-lens intakes as "freeze-enforcement history", treating the research closure and the product-phase freeze as one gate, which §8.48 C4 separates.
5. **Several points agree with the book:** "this is what we know, why we know it, what we still do not know" is close to promise (b); a knowledge object carries who acted, when and on what basis. Others go beyond it: "how certain we are" is a grade the book has not built; the speaker of a claim is not always the actor of the act that records it; and compressing observations is a derived view, never a deletion.
