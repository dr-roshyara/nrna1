## 8.38.1 In plain language

1. **"Knowledge is accepted or rejected by checking how reliable the producing process was."** Standing in the book does not work that way: only recorded acts confer standing, and correctness is explicitly not one of the things that can change it. Reliability does have a place in the book: the question "how well graded is the evidence for this claim?" (Q4), whose grading rule has not been written yet. Requiring a reliable, identified source before anyone may adopt would be a choice for the Principal.
2. **One proposed "confidence state machine" puts several things in one line:** what kind of source it was, whether it was accepted, whether it is challenged, whether it is under review, and a final verdict. The book keeps these apart, and it forbids a line that goes from "created" to "authoritative" with no act in between.
3. **"Trust by default; investigate only on doubt."** For standing, nothing is trusted without an act. For evidence, the empty value is "not attested". For the content itself the kernel holds no value at all, so "default trust" in content is an application's matter.
4. **"Never overwrite; keep the line of interpretation."** The book keeps every act about an item in order and can show it. An authorised removal can drop content, but it leaves a trace. What the book cannot yet record is that one statement interprets, extends or replaces another.
5. **The word "authority" has several meanings in these sources and the book:** the trustworthiness of a speaker, a legal capability to act, where authority comes from, and the book's recorded standing. They must not be mixed.
6. **"A term means something only within its context"** agrees with how the book works on itself. For the content the system holds, interpreting meaning belongs to applications.
7. **"Meaning comes before knowledge"** agrees with the book's "knowledge is not its carrier". But in both sources' final pictures, validation leads straight to knowledge with no one acting. In the book, only a recorded act does that.
8. **The sources write "ADR candidates", although their own line said not to; and one of them calls KnowledgeOS an "Epistemic Operating System", which an earlier reply had denied.**

**C2. One state chain carries several axes (Src; Int).** *(Restated at review: W-06.)*
- **S0224's "Knowledge Confidence State Machine":** "OBSERVED → INFERRED → ACCEPTED → CHALLENGED → UNDER REVIEW → VALIDATED / REJECTED" (L318–340).
- **Its KnowledgeEvent record** has "validity: verified/rejected" (L103–120), two values with no "undetermined" or "neither".
- **The book's strongest home:** §1.2: provenance has no state machine, and "no lifecycle may contain a direct edge from *created* to *authoritative*" (A1 restated). OBSERVED → INFERRED → ACCEPTED breaks both.
- **Int:** the chain mixes things the book keeps apart:
  - **(a)** the kind of source (observed or inferred): an open gap for content (G7's row; §8.35 C3);
  - **(b)** acceptance, which is standing: T, F or U by CE*;
  - **(c)** challenge: a refutation is an evidence entry, as proposed, shown in promise (b)'s display;
  - **(d)** UNDER REVIEW: a workflow state, an application's;
  - **(e)** a verdict (VALIDATED, REJECTED).
- **The verdict field, three readings:** (i) a stored derived value, which has no standing (CON-0141, provisional); (ii) a content assessment, an application's (§10.2); (iii) `validity` set by an authorised actor is **the record of an act**, and under A1 the act is what counts.
- **The chain** is a lifecycle, and a lifecycle state is not authority (§8.32). It is the seventh lifecycle vocabulary in §8.35–§8.38.
- *Model integrity (an observation, not promoted):* the chain is **one overloaded dimension**; each axis has its own home in the book, or a recorded gap.
- **Not a new concept.**

**C3. Default trust, per axis (Src; Ded against §8.33 C1).** *(Restated at review: W-07.)*
- **S0224:** "Normal: Trust → Action … When doubt appears: Doubt → Investigation → … Resolution" (L274–307). ADR-KOS-EPI-004: "Claims SHALL enter deeper verification workflows only when triggered by contradiction, uncertainty, or challenge" (L530–532).
- **"Trust" is a homonym:** in S0224 it is an agent relying on content; the book's T is standing.
- **Ded, per axis** (§8.33 C1; §8.36 C4 (iii)):
  - **(i) Authority:** an item that no act recognises is F (decision C1). Nothing is trusted *as standing* by default.
  - **(ii) Evidence:** an adopted item needs no evidence to keep standing; the display shows "not attested" (promise (b)). In this sense "not every fact needs review" agrees with the book.
  - **(iii) Content:** the kernel holds no value. Default trust in content is the consumer's, an application matter.
  - **The trigger:** when to investigate is a workflow (§10.2).
- **Int:** S0224 checks reliability on every claim (C1) and investigates only on doubt. A reading that reconciles them: classify every source always (EPI-002, the source-kind gap with G7), and investigate on doubt (an application). The source does not say so.
- **Not a new concept.**

**C4. No silent overwrite; per-item lineage is Q2; item links have no home (Src; Int).** *(Restated at review: W-08.)*
- **S0224:** "Original Decision → Interpretation → Extension → Challenge → Resolution", "Git for institutional reasoning" (L412–440). ADR-KOS-EPI-005: "preserve interpretive lineage rather than replace historical reasoning states" (L538–540).
- **S0225:** "Meaning lineage" (L399–440); ADR-KOS-SEM-004: "Changes in terminology SHALL preserve historical meaning transformations" (L526–528).
- **The book:**
  - *Agreement on no silent overwrite:* acts are appended, and an annulment voids from the start but stays recorded (§9.13.2).
  - *Per-item lineage:* Q2 returns every entry about an item in record order, including evidence (§9.10 Q2). On one item, challenge → resolution can be held as a refutation entry (proposed) and a later act.
  - *Qualified:* an authorised **removal by tombstone** drops content and keeps every required field; lost content cannot be restored (§9.13.2). Whether a deletion required by law extends to identities is open (§9.10). EPI-005 and SEM-004 are absolute; the book is not.
  - *Gap:* the record admits no link **between** items ("link" not admitted, CON-0188), and supersession is not admitted (Θ_f σ; §8.35 C1). That one statement interprets, extends or replaces another cannot be recorded today.
- **Not a new concept.**

**C8. Meaning before knowledge; no act in either flow (Src; Ded against A1; Int).** *(Added at review: W-04.)*

- **The book:**
