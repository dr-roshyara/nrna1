# 10.2 Bounded contexts: which areas have their own model and language

*Strategic-design turn 2 of 5 (the mandate, revision 1, §2 element 2 and §2a). Status: **draft, before review** (`review/SD2-BOUNDED-CONTEXTS/`). Builds on §10.1 under the promise the Principal decided, (b) (`e73b8e1cb`): current standing right and shown with its reasons, plus an honest display of well-foundedness (refuted, not attested, or supported by recorded evidence), without a guarantee. **Every line is S0 until a reviewer's verdict;** the ceiling column gives the lowest level a context's evidence would allow.*

## 10.2.0 The research question, the admission rule, and what this turn does not do

**Question.** Which areas of responsibility in KnowledgeOS have their own model and language? For each: a responsibility statement, its concepts (CON-IDs), the rules it is responsible for, what it receives and publishes, its evidence and level. The Principal's instruction adds two duties: **say which context is responsible for the display of well-foundedness**, and **show that each displayed class can be read from the record alone, or name it as an open gap** (§10.2.3).

**The admission rule is a fork, kept open (§2a of the mandate; the Principal's instruction 3).** Three statements are in the book; none has a sufficiency condition:
- **CON-0085** (INVALID: no sufficiency condition): a seam becomes a bounded context "only if supported by one or more of nine criteria" (S0033 L29, in meaning: semantic ownership, transactional consistency, lifecycle independence, invariants, ubiquitous-language divergence, team autonomy, deployment autonomy, integration characteristics, performance constraints) (§6.2).
- **S0150's test:** own "ubiquitous language · lifecycle · invariants · authority · reasons to change · independently owned state"; "If not, it probably shouldn't become another bounded context" (S0150 L584–592, in meaning). Whether one, several or all six must hold is not said (§8.21 C15).
- **S0168's ten criteria** (S0168 L1040–1051, in meaning; §8.24): a third statement, recorded only.

**How this turn handles the fork (Int, the Professor's method; for review):** each candidate is scored against **both** readings. Under CON-0085 it passes if at least one criterion is evidenced. Under S0150, the score is counted out of six, and three readings are distinguished: at least one, most (four or more), and all six. A candidate that passes under every reading is **robust**. A candidate that passes under some readings only is **conditional on the fork**, and is marked so with §2a's five items. **No reading is selected.**

**What this turn takes as given:**
- §10.1's classes under (b);
- the boundary test's results (§9.6–§9.17) as the kernel/application line. It does not choose them anew (the mandate, §2 row 2).

**What this turn does not do:**
- ownership of invariants (turn 3);
- the map (turn 4);
- the published language (turn 5);
- any technical interface;
- any code or EKS file;
- any context drawn from a folder or a module.

**BC-K2 Well-foundedness (evidence and its status).** *Core (the display's content); the methods that produce evidence are BC-A3.*

**The check against S0162's warning.** The contexts above do **not** form a chain:
- BC-K1 and BC-K2 are **independent producers**: standing and well-foundedness are kept apart.
- BC-K3 is their **common consumer**, and BC-K4 and BC-K5 **supply** both.

A layered chain would have made BC-K2 depend on BC-K1's answers or the reverse. Neither depends on the other. *(That neither depends on the other is a claim for the map turn, §10.4, to test.)*

## 10.2.3 The display of well-foundedness: can each class be read from the record alone?

**Responsibility (instruction 2):** **BC-K2** decides an item's class and its reasons; **BC-K3** shows the class without change. Both are needed for the promise. Neither can be bypassed: BC-K3 has no means of deciding a class, and BC-K2 has no means of showing one.

**Reading each class (Ded, given the premises named):** let E be a record of evidence entries, each citing the item it is about, with its provenance and its time, and let withdrawals and corrections be recorded entries too (CON-0214's two operations). For an item k at a cut t:
- **refuted(k, t)** holds if some entry in E up to t refutes k and is not withdrawn by t. It is an **existence claim over recorded entries**, so it is **readable from the record alone**, provided refutations and withdrawals are entries and not prose.
- **supported by recorded evidence(k, t)** holds if some entry in E up to t records support for k, is not withdrawn by t, and k is not refuted at t. It is likewise **readable from the record alone**. *What it shows is that recorded evidence asserts support, and by whom. It does not show that the support is adequate "to the degree its use needs" (§0.1). That is why the promise carries no guarantee: the class is honest exactly because it claims no more.*
- **not attested(k, t)** holds if neither of the above does. It is an **absence claim**. It can be read from the record alone **only if E is the designated record of evidence for k**, so that absence within it is decided relative to a stated scope (CON-0120; §9.17 K2's designation). Otherwise it reads "not attested **in the records read**".

**Open gaps (named, not solved):**

| Gap | What is missing | Consequence | What would close it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **G1** | **The book's record has no entry kinds for evidence or verdicts.** The kinds the evaluator's domain admits are acts of authority only: root, grant, adopt, revoke, annul, endg, consume, remove, transfer, designate (§9.13; the model's kind list, `tmp/tools/ce_model.py`) | none of the three classes can be read from the record the book has modelled. They need a record of BC-K2's own, with its kinds, designation and integrity | a model of BC-K2's record (its entry kinds, its designation, and the one-sidedness rule), reviewed. A turn-3 or turn-5 question, or a theory turn of its own |
| **G2** | **Precedence between supporting and refuting entries.** "Refuted takes precedence" above is the Professor's choice (Int, for review). The book's one-sided rule, CON-0127, is about independence only | an item with both kinds of entry could be shown either way | a stated rule, reviewed. The honest display may need to show **both** (refuted, with recorded support) |
| **G3** | **Per what?** A well-foundedness class may differ by evidence dimension: subject, epistemic status, provenance, reproducibility (CON-0217) | one class per item may hide a refuted dimension | a decision on whether the class is per item or per dimension |
| **G4** | **Who may record evidence.** Accountability needs a declared producer and an authorising act (CON-0150). That act is BC-K1's kind of thing, recorded for BC-K2's entries | it is a relation between BC-K1 and BC-K2 | the map turn (§10.4) |

**Result for instruction 2:** each class is readable from a record alone **once G1 is closed**: two classes by existence, and "not attested" only relative to a designated evidence record. **Until then all three are an open gap (G1).**
