## 10.5.0 The research question, and what this turn does not do

**Question.** What does each context, above all standing, promise the others: which concepts it publishes, what each means, and under which conditions the promise holds? Each item traced to its claim and level (the mandate, §2 row 4).

**Meaning, not interface (the mandate, §1).** An item here is a **domain meaning and a promise**: what a consumer may rely on, and when. **No technical interface, API, transport, data format or mechanism** is fixed or approved.
- Where the book's model has a name (`in_D`, `ce_star_explained`), it is cited as **evidence**, not as an interface.
- Terms such as "tombstone", "attestation" and "signed-as-actor" are **decided design elements of Ch. 9** (B F17). They are kept with their meaning stated, and do not name a mechanism.

**Not a frozen contract.** S0633, a later source consulted out of order (BRANCH-02), warns against going from a research hypothesis to an architectural conclusion and then to freezing the kernel contract (S0633 L5, in meaning; L538: "Do not freeze yet"). At S0, the published language is the **current statement of the promise**. It is open to review, not frozen.

**Not done:** any technical interface; operational failure; the language of contexts that do not exist yet (well-foundedness before G1; *since T9-19, 2026-10-31, the evidence kinds are adopted and W's language is still to be written*).

**Words used here.**
- **Appender:** the recording application that submits an entry (`app`, L10).
- **Owner application:** of a grant or a store (§9.16 R4a, §9.17); distinct from the **owner context of a kind** (§10.3.6).
- **Accepted by the kernel** *(published name since T10-21, 2026-10-31; formerly "attested by the kernel")*: accepted by a kernel. This is distinct from W's class "not attested", meaning not evidenced.
- **Current authority:** what Q1 computes. It is not standing in the sense of §1.14.1-12 (T10-15).

## 10.5.2 The record's language and standing's language

*Read as S0069 R-2 reads it: what crosses **in** (the record, its kinds, and their acceptance: PL-0 to PL-5) and what crosses **out** (the questions and answers: PL-6 to PL-11). Then come the conditions (PL-12), the non-promises (PL-13) and the joint display (PL-14). **G-labels of §9.15 are written "§9.15 G1"; those of §10.2.3 "gap G1"** (A N-09).*

**Split under T10-16 (b)** *(the G1 settlement, applying T10-16 (b) and the Principal's decision on designation, `814b4608a`)* (the Lead DDD Architect's SD5-01, routed as input, accepted). The record and its kind list are owned by **no area**; the record-keeping rules (designation, merge, identity, integrity) belong to that element, and standing keeps "what counts". Removal is classified with record-keeping and annulment with standing (§9.18.7). So the items fall into two languages:

| Item | The record's language (the element outside every area) | Standing's language |
|---|---|---|
| PL-0 | the record and its kind list | — |
| PL-1 | the kinds `remove` and `designate`; the fields every kind carries | the kinds root, grant, adopt, revoke, annul, endg, consume, transfer |
| PL-2 | the guards on **every entry** (citations, identity, integrity, attestation); a removal's and a designation's guards | the guards of standing's kinds (single use, owner application, transfer) |
| PL-3, PL-4 | the form of the record; identity and merge | — |
| PL-5 | the store owner and designation | the grant owner |
| PL-6 to PL-13 | — | all |

*The element relies on standing's answer for the **authority** of its own operations (a removal or a designation must be T at append): a dependency of the record element on standing, drawn as R0′ in §10.4.*

### A. The record's language (the element outside every area)


**PL-0. The one designated record and its kind list** *(added; the Lead DDD Architect's finding A2, weighed in the settlement)*.
- **Meaning:** there is **one designated record** (K2; §9.17 R2; CON-0261), and a **list of admitted entry kinds** (D's list, §9.13.2). Under T10-10 (1) this list holds **every area's state**. Adding a kind is therefore what can **admit a context**.
- **Who adds a kind.**
  - *Book history, a fact:* every kind added since D was defined was admitted **by the Principal's decision**: consumption (v8), transfer (v10) and designation (v19) (§9.13.2 L34, L39, L48).
  - *The rule:* none is stated. The assignment of kinds to owners is **stipulated by the book, outside D** ((p2), §10.3.6), and a new kind receives exactly one owner ((p5)).
- **Who owns the element:** **no area (T10-16 (b), decided by the Principal, `382fa6d85`, addendum 10:46:12Z):** it is changed only by an authorised act outside every area; adding a kind is such an act, so no area can admit a context.
- **Removing and designating** belong to the element; **annulling** stays with standing (the Principal's decision on designation, `814b4608a`; removal and annulment classified at §9.18.7). *The T10-10 addendum's "removing, annulling and designating stay with standing" is superseded for designation and, by the classification, for removal.*
- **Source:** §9.13.2; §9.17 R2; §10.3.6; T10-10. **Level S0.**

**PL-3. The form of the record** *(restated; B F8, A N-02)*.
- **Meaning:** every change is an entry. Citations point only at earlier entries.
- **Removal leaves a trace:** a removal is an authorized, recorded act that the tombstone cites. The tombstone keeps **every field D requires**, its record position and its integrity result. **The content is not retained.** Answers resting on it are marked "rests on removed content" (§9.13 L59–61, the Principal after turn 7).
- **Promise:** that an entry was recorded, its identity and its required fields stay readable; a removal never leaves a dangling citation. *The draft's "what was recorded stays readable" overstated a tombstone and is withdrawn.*
- **Condition:** the record changes only through the kernel's steps (K1). A loss outside them is detectable from the record if a surviving entry cites the lost entry or a numbered successor survives (numbers never reused, K6; B-F6); otherwise only against an outside commitment (§9.15 K1).
- **Source:** §9.15 G3; §9.13.2. **Level S0.**

**PL-4. Entry identity and the merge rule** *(added; B F9)*.
- **Identity:** an entry's identity is derived from its content: the fields CE* reads, not the recorder or the status (§9.16 R2; law 29). *For grants, since v7 the owner and the recording application are also part of identity for the merge (§9.16 L319); whether R2's order independence holds under that extended identity is open (T9-18; consolidation S0201–S0225, N1).*
- **Merge:** the union of the records, joining copies with one identity.
  - Conflicting copies are refused.
  - A tombstone wins over a full copy.
  - The status is the weakest of the copies, so a mark is never lost.
  - The recorder is the set of recorders (§9.16 R2; law 30).
- **Promise:** appending the same entry twice changes nothing (law 9a).
- **Source:** §9.16 R2 (CON-0194, PROVISIONAL). **Level S0.**

### B. Standing's language

#### In: what an appender may rely on

**PL-1. The kinds and their fields.**
- **The ten admitted kinds** (§9.13.2) *(by the split table, eight are standing's language (B); removal and designation, the last two below, are the record's language (A), T10-16 (b); consolidation S0226–S0252, A-11)*:
  - root act;
  - grant issuance (holder; start and end, end = ∞ if open-ended; the owner application; optionally a **single-use** mark, set at issuance);
  - adoption;
  - revocation;
  - annulment;
  - grant ending;
  - consumption (only of a single-use grant; its only basis is the grant it consumes);
  - transfer (new owner; the recording application; the grant's last use);
  - removal (by tombstone, with `removed_by`);
  - designation (the new store; it cites every maximal entry of the old store).
- **Every kind also carries:** actor, cited bases, scope, act time, the recording application, a recorder, and an authentication status (signed-as-actor, signed-as-other, unsigned). Uses of a single-use grant name the previous use. *The full field list is §9.13.2's; this is its summary* (A N-03).
- **Not admitted** (a record holding one is outside D, and is not answered: Q1 refuses it):
  - supersession, suspension, the acts that set θ;
  - O1's other kinds: registration, link, retirement, establishment, declassification (§9.13 L52);
  - O3's absence entries (placed with standing at §10.3.3).
- **Ownership of kinds (T10-10 (1); T10-16 (b)):** the acceptance rule of eight kinds is standing's; `remove` and `designate` are the record element's. The assignment is the element's to make, by an authorised act outside every area; no such act is recorded, so it stays stipulated ((p2)); standing's admission no longer holds "by construction" of its own schema (§10.3.7; consolidation S0226–S0252, A-04). A future kind (for example evidence, gap G1) enters this language only with its acceptance rule and one owner ((p5)).
- **Source:** §9.13.2 (CON-0191, PROVISIONAL); §10.3.6. **Level S0.**

**PL-2. Acceptance: when an entry is appended** *(restated; A-04, A-05, B F5, F11, F12, F14)*.
- **Meaning:** an entry is accepted **only if** it keeps the record in D **and** passes every guard of its kind. The two conditions are not one test: the append guard and the static test of D agree **on citations** (§9.13.2); the single-use guard has no static counterpart.
- **Every entry:**
  - its citations name entries already present;
  - its identity is new (PL-4);
  - it passes its integrity check.
- **A use of a single-use grant:** accepted only if T at append; every recorded use counts; it must be recorded by the grant's **owner application** (§9.13 v12, v13; §9.16 R4a). **Grants not single-use get no owner check** (§9.13 L46; §9.16 R4c).
- **A consumption:** only of a single-use grant (v14).
- **A transfer:** accepted only if T at append; only from the grant's **issuer** or an authority above the issuer's scope, **never from the holder**; it names the grant's last use (§9.13 L46).
- **A removal:** the kernel tombstones only if the removal act is T (§9.13 v9).
- **A designation:**
  - the old store is the carrier in force, and the new carrier has never been a carrier;
  - it cites every maximal entry of the old store;
  - its authority is T;
  - it is recorded by the old store's **owner application** (§9.17 R3 (c), (c′); v20).
- **What the appender learns on refusal:** that the entry was refused. *The book states reasons for refusals of Q1 outside D, not for guard refusals*; a reason for each guard refusal is **not promised** (B F12).
  - *Evidence, the G1 settlement (SD5-06, routed):* in the model every refusal path of standing's guard returns a reason (17 of 17, `ce_model.py` v22 `append_guard`), and the proposed evidence rule does too (§9.18 G8). **Recommended:** publish "a refusal names the guard that refused" as part of both languages. *Not adopted: the Principal's decision of `814b4608a` authorises clarification, not new design.*
- **Attestation:** an accepted entry carries the kernel's attestation that a kernel accepted it. **Published as "accepted by the kernel"** (T10-21, the Principal, 2026-10-31; "attested" is kept for the evidence class "not attested").
  - The attestation is **assumed** unforgeable ((p3); §9.17 (c′)).
  - D checks that it is **present**, not that it is valid (§9.17 limit (i); A N-04). The attestation is the Professor's technical choice, for review.
- **The appending application** is trusted as declared (L10).
- **Limit:** one bad entry in a merged record puts the whole record outside D. A record in D determines the answer at each of its cuts (§9.13.2 L62; the older phrase "closed under prefixes" at L69 is superseded there, B F14).
- **Source:** §9.13.2; §9.16 R4a–R4c; §9.17 R3; §9.15 G3, G4 (CON-0191, CON-0194, CON-0193: PROVISIONAL). **Level S0.**

**PL-5. The owner rules** *(moved "in"; restated; A-05, A-06, B F5, F6)*.
- **Grant owner** (§9.16 R4a, the Principal's words): "The contract names one owner application per grant, fixed at issuance. A change of owner is a recorded, authorized act. The kernel refuses a use of a grant not recorded by its owner." **Scope:** single-use grants (§9.16 R4c; §9.13 L46).
  - *Cost* (the Principal's words, R4a): "a grant cannot be used while its owner is unavailable, unless ownership is moved". As extended by the Principal (§9.16 L228): a use needs **both** the owner and the kernel available.
- **Store owner and designation** (§9.17 (c′), the decision record's wording): "each store belongs to **one application**, and only that application may move the record to a new store. Conflicting moves are prevented, not only detected"; "the move is a recorded, authorised act citing every maximal entry of the old store; one owner application per store; a store is a carrier only once".
  - **Limit:** how a store's owner changes is **not modelled**. In v20, every store of a chain has the root owner's application, so no move to another application's store is reachable (§9.17 L166 limit (i); T9-11, open).
- **Promise, stated as the book states it (§9.15 G4):**
  - within one kernel, a **second recorded use** of a single-use grant, and a conflicting move, are **prevented**;
  - a **second actual use** is prevented only under **L9** (record before use);
  - across merged applications, a second use is **detected**: it is marked, not corrected, and Φ may give it T (§9.16 L183).
- **Conditions:**
  - K6 (one serialized step);
  - L9;
  - L10 (the declared appending application);
  - the root designation and the root carrier's owner are set outside D (L11);
  - the mover, that is the application that records a designation, declares its own application (§9.17 limit (ii)).
- **Open:** T9-07 (protocol of F22), T9-11.
- **Source:** §9.16 R4a–R4d (CON-0194); §9.17 (c′) (CON-0261); **both closed by the Principal as design sketches.** **Level S0.**

#### Out: what a reader may rely on

*Each question has its own answer form and its own promise (A-02, B F3).*

**PL-6. Q1, current authority.**
- **Question:** for an item k, a scope x, a valid time t, a cut s and a parameter version u: does k hold current authority? (§9.10 L29–39; A N-10).
- **Naming:** Q1 computes **current authority**. §1.14.1-12 separates it from **standing**; the Principal's act says "standing", read as current authority (Int, §9.10 L31). Whether standing in §1.14.1-12's sense is answered by anything is **open (T10-15)**.
- **Answer:** T, F or U, on D, for Θ_f, under C1 = constitute.
- **What the values mean** (§9.13 R2, R2a; B F4):
  - **T:** recognized: forced (it holds in every fixed point).
  - **F:** **refuted, or not recognized**: forced. **Shown as "not recognized as current authority", never "refuted"** (T10-21, the Principal, 2026-10-31; "refuted" is kept for the evidence class). It includes "nothing recorded, so nothing recognized" (closed world under C1; that F rests on the record's completeness, L4). **A consumer must not read F as "refuted".**
  - **U:** the record does not decide. It rests on an undefined covers pair, a cycle through a negative edge, or a tie.
  - **Refused** (undefined; "inapplicable"): the record is outside D. The failing entry or citation is named.
- **Refusal reasons for U** (§9.13.3):
  - (i) a cycle among undetermined acts, named as a self-annulling act, a self-cancelling act with its link, a mutual annulment, an annulment ring, or a negative cycle through named acts;
  - (ii) and (iii) an undefined covers pair;
  - (iv) two acts at the same act time whose order the answer reads.

  The reasons are sound; **not shown minimal** (§9.13 L166).
- **Promise (§9.15 G1):** on a record in D, exactly one answer; T and F forced; every U names its reason.
- **Not promised:** **reasons for T and F.** §9.10 requires "the acts the answer rests on", but the headline reasons for T and F are not yet defined (§9.13 L166, L338). The support used for the marks is a conservative closure, not a reason (A-03). Moved to PL-13.
- **Display:** a U is shown as U. *This is a display rule (§10.3.3 C, promise (b)), grounded there and not on CON-0221, which §10.3 excluded as a process rule (A-07, B F16).* A consumer **may decide fail-closed** (treat U as "not authorized" for its own action) while the answer is still shown as U (§9.13 defect 1).
- **Source:** §9.10 (CON-0188); §9.13 R2, R2a, §9.13.3 (CON-0191); §9.15 G1 (CON-0193). **Level S0.**

**PL-7. Authorization of a recorded act** *(added; B F2)*.
- **Question:** is a recorded act x authorized at a cut? (authorized(x), §9.15 G2; `act_explained`, `marks_act`).
- **Answer:** T, F or U, as PL-6, with refusal reasons and marks for one act (§9.13.3, v8).
- **Promise:** **entitlement of the named actor**, relative to the same inputs (§9.15 G2).
- **Source:** §9.15 G2; §9.13.3. **Level S0.**

**PL-8. Q3, authorization of a possibly unrecorded act** *(restated; A-01, B F2)*.
- **Question:** principal a, capability v, scope x, instant t, cut s: does a recorded, unended grant cover a hypothetical act (a, v, x, t) placed after the cut? (§9.10 L53–64).
- **Answer:** found (with the grant), not found, or undetermined. **"Not found" is not "unauthorized"** (§1.17.3). A separation-of-duties violation is a **separate, positive finding**.
- **Status:** a **construction** on D, for Θ_f (§9.13 R5). It is **Int and not modelled**; its marks are not modelled (§9.13 L342).
- **Promise:** **conditional** on R5's construction. Nothing is promised beyond it.
- **Source:** §9.10 Q3; §9.13 R5. **Level S0.**

**PL-9. Q2, lineage** *(restated; A-02, B F3)*.
- **Question:** the entries about k, in record order (§9.10 L41–50), under §9.11 R1's premises (placed with standing at §10.3.3).
- **Answer:** entries, **not** T/F/U.
  - §9.10 lists acts, links, evidence, marked absences and recorded losses.
  - **On D only entries of D's kinds exist** (no links, evidence or absence entries), so what Q2 can return is narrower than §9.10 says. *§9.11 R1's premises are not re-checked here* (B, limits).
- **Source:** §9.10 Q2; §10.3.3. **Level S0.**

**PL-10. The marks** *(completed; A-08, B F13)*.
- **Meaning:** an answer is **marked, not refused**, when it rests on:
  1. transcribed (signed-as-other) entries;
  2. unsigned entries;
  3. removed content;
  4. a removal not, or no longer, authorized;
  5. an inconsistent recording status (signed-as-other with recorder = actor);
  6. a use not recorded by its owner;
  7. a second use of a single-use grant (present without the guard).

  (§9.13.2 L51, L60; §9.13.3; §9.16 L265.)
- **Scope:** Q1 and one act's authorization (PL-6, PL-7) only. Not Q3.
- **Promise:** **sound** (every entry the answer rests on is in its support); conservative (it may mark more than needed).
- **Condition:** the marks are true of the entries only if the recorded status is true (§9.15 G1).
- **Source:** §9.13.2, §9.13.3; §9.16 R4a. **Level S0.**

**PL-11. One answer whoever asks** *(restated; A N-01, B F10)*.
- **Meaning:** two calls of Q1 with equal (k, x, t, s, u) get the same answer (law 21; K5 of §9.7, CON-0143).
- **Status:** a **stipulation** stated by the book, with the hidden condition **one root per estate**, for the Principal to confirm (register, CON-0143).
- **Holds:** at a **given cut** with **equal entry sets** (§9.16 R1).
- **Limit:** answering "now" across applications without a sealed cut is **not** order-free, because CE* is not monotone in the record (§9.16 L80).
- **After a recorded removal:** the **values** at every cut are unchanged; only the marks gain "rests on removed content" (§9.16 L79). *The draft's "a recorded loss can change an answer" is superseded for tombstones; it still holds for a loss that drops identities.*
- **Condition:** readers take cuts of a designated record (K2-r).
- **Source:** §9.10; §9.16 R1, L79–80 (CON-0143, CON-0194: PROVISIONAL). **Level S0.**

Within one kernel such an entry is refused at append (PL-2); the case arises on merged records or bypass. ◇ gap G1; **whether a breach can be contained to W's entries is the G1 turn's question** (routed finding B4).
