## 10.5.0 The research question, and what this turn does not do

**Question.** What does each context, above all standing, promise the others: which concepts it publishes, what each means, and under which conditions the promise holds? Each item traced to its claim and level (the mandate, §2 row 4).

**Meaning, not interface (the mandate, §1).** A published-language item here is a **domain meaning and a promise**: what a consumer may rely on, and when. **No technical interface, API, transport, data format or mechanism** is fixed or approved. Where the book's model has a name (`in_D`, `ce_star_explained`), it is cited as the model's evidence, not as an interface.

**Not a frozen contract.** S0633, a later source consulted out of order (BRANCH-02), warns against going from a research hypothesis to an architectural conclusion and then to freezing the kernel contract (S0633 L5, in meaning). At S0 the published language is the **current statement of the promise**, open to review. It is not frozen.

**Not done:** any technical interface; operational failure; the published language of contexts that do not exist yet (well-foundedness before G1).

## 10.5.2 Standing's published language (the admitted context)

*Read as S0069 R-2 reads it: what crosses **in** (the record's kinds and their acceptance), and what crosses **out** (the questions, the answers and their conditions). Each item: its meaning, its promise, its conditions, its source and register status, its level.*

### In: what an appender may rely on

**PL-1. The one designated record and its kinds.**
- **Meaning:** authority is recorded as entries of **one designated record** (K2; §9.17 R2). An entry is of one of **ten admitted kinds**: root act, grant issuance, adoption, revocation, annulment, grant ending, consumption, transfer, removal (by tombstone), designation. Each kind has its required fields; every kind also carries a recorder and an authentication status (signed-as-actor, signed-as-other, unsigned) (§9.13.2).
- **Promise:** a record whose entries meet D's list is **in D**, and only then is it answered (PL-4).
- **Not admitted** (a record holding one is outside D): supersession, suspension, the acts that set θ, and O1's other kinds (registration, link, retirement, establishment, declassification) (§9.13 L52).
- **Ownership of kinds (T10-10 (1)):** all ten kinds are standing's: their acceptance rule is standing's rule. **No other area has a kind today.** A future kind (for example evidence, G1) enters this language only with its acceptance rule and its owner ((p5), §10.3.6).
- **Source:** §9.13.2 (CON-0191, PROVISIONAL); §9.17 R2 (CON-0261, PROVISIONAL). **Level S0.**

**PL-2. Acceptance: when an entry is appended.**
- **Meaning:** an entry is appended iff it keeps the record in D: its citations name entries already present, its identifier is new, it passes its integrity check (§9.13.2, "the append guard and the static test agree on citations").
- **Further guards** (each refuses, and says why):
  - a use of a **single-use grant** is accepted only if T at append, and every recorded use counts (§9.13 v13; G4);
  - a use of a grant **not recorded by its owner application** is refused (PL-5);
  - an **unauthorized removal** is refused: the kernel tombstones only if the removal act is T (§9.13 v9);
  - a **move** of the record not recorded by the old store's owner is refused (PL-6).
- **Promise:** an accepted entry is **attested** by the kernel that accepted it.
- **Conditions:** the attestation is **assumed** unforgeable (§9.17 (c′)); the appending application is trusted as declared (L10).
- **Limit stated by the book:** D is closed under prefixes but **not under appending**: one bad entry puts the whole record outside D (§9.13.2, "availability").
- **Source:** §9.13.2; §9.15 G3, G4 (CON-0193, PROVISIONAL). **Level S0.**

**PL-3. The form of the record.**
- **Meaning:** every change is an entry; an entry is removed only by a recorded, authorized removal, by tombstone, keeping identity, kind and the fields citations rely on (G3; H1′). Citations point only at earlier entries (§9.13.2).
- **Promise:** what was recorded stays readable as having been recorded; a removal never leaves a dangling citation.
- **Conditions:** the record changes only through the kernel's steps (K1); loss outside them is detectable only against an outside commitment (§9.15 K1).
- **Source:** §9.15 G3; §9.13.2. **Level S0.**

### Out: what a reader may rely on

**PL-4. The questions and the answer vocabulary.**
- **Questions** standing answers (§10.3.3 placement):
  - **Q1, current authority:** for an item k, a scope x, a valid time t, a cut s: does k hold current authority? (§9.10 L29–39). *Naming, stated by the book:* Q1 computes **current authority**, which §1.14.1-12 separates from **standing**; the Principal's act says "standing", read as current authority (Int, §9.10 L31). **The published word is "current authority"** (§10.5.5);
  - **Q3, authorization:** is a recorded act covered by a recorded grant at a cut? (§9.10 L53);
  - **Q2, lineage:** over the record of acts, under §9.11 R1's premises (placed with standing at §10.3.3).
- **The answer vocabulary is closed:** **T**, **F** or **U**, with the acts the answer rests on (§9.10 L35; §9.13.3).
- **Promise (G1):** on a record in D, exactly one answer; its T and F are **forced**; **every U names its reason**.
- **Refusal reasons for U** (§9.13.3): (i) a cycle among undetermined acts, named as a self-annulling act, a self-cancelling act (with its link), a mutual annulment, an annulment ring, or a negative cycle through named acts; (ii) and (iii) an undefined covers pair; (iv) two acts at the same act time whose order the answer reads.
- **Outside D:** the question is **refused**, naming the failing entry or citation (§9.13.2; G1).
- **Unresolved stays unresolved:** a U is never shown as T or F (CON-0221, PROVISIONAL, as a reading of PL-4; §10.3.3 C, display).
- **Source:** §9.10 (CON-0188, PROVISIONAL); §9.13.3 (CON-0191); §9.15 G1, G2 (CON-0193). **Level S0.**

**PL-5. The marks.**
- **Meaning:** an answer is **marked, not refused**, when it rests on **transcribed** (signed-as-other) or **unsigned** entries, on **removed content**, or on a use **not recorded by its owner** (§9.13.3, v6, v10).
- **Promise:** the marks are **sound**: every entry the answer rests on is in its support; they may over-mark (conservative) (§9.13.3).
- **Conditions:** marks are true of the entries only if the recorded status is true (G1, turn 7).
- **Source:** §9.13.3; §9.15 G1. **Level S0.**

**PL-6. The owner rules and designation.**
- **Grant owner** (§9.16 R4a, the Principal's words): "The contract names one owner application per grant, fixed at issuance. A change of owner is a recorded, authorized act. The kernel refuses a use of a grant not recorded by its owner." *Its cost, also the Principal's words:* "a grant cannot be used while its owner is unavailable, unless ownership is moved."
- **Store owner and designation** (§9.17 (c′), the decision record's wording): "each store belongs to **one application**, and only that application may move the record to a new store. Conflicting moves are prevented, not only detected"; "the move is a recorded, authorised act citing every maximal entry of the old store; one owner application per store; a store is a carrier only once".
- **Promise:** within one kernel, a second use of a single-use grant and a conflicting move are **prevented**; across merged applications, **detected** (T9-10; G4).
- **Conditions:** the root designation and the root carrier's owner are set **outside D** (L11); the mover declares its own application (§9.17 limit (ii)); L10.
- **Source:** §9.16 R4a (CON-0194, PROVISIONAL); §9.17 (CON-0261, PROVISIONAL). Both are **closed as design sketches** by the Principal. **Level S0.**

**PL-7. One answer whoever asks.**
- **Meaning:** two calls with equal (k, x, t, s, u) get the same answer (§9.10 Q1, identity; law 21; K5 of §9.7, CON-0143).
- **Promise:** at a given cut with **equal entry sets** (§9.16 R1).
- **Conditions:** readers take cuts of a designated record (K2-r); a recorded loss since then can change an answer, which then rests on the tombstone or is U with the loss named (§9.10 Q1).
- **Source:** §9.10; §9.16 R1; CON-0143 (PROVISIONAL). **Level S0.**

**PL-9. What standing does not promise.**

- **Held acts** (law 18): not a kind of D.
- **Timing:** no bound on when an answer is given (operational, out of scope).
- **The present estate's record:** not in D, so not answered (§9.13.2).

## 10.5.5 Checks against the mandate's failure modes

- **"A technical interface fixed":** none. The model's function names are cited as evidence only.
- **"Convenience redefining a concept":** every meaning is quoted or cited from Ch. 9. **One naming risk is flagged, not resolved:** promise (b) speaks of "current **standing**", and the Principal's act says "standing"; what CE* computes is **current authority**, which the book separates from standing (§1.14.1-12; §9.10 L31). Publishing "standing" for Q1's answer would redefine a concept by convenience. **This draft publishes "current authority"** and records the gap: whether standing in the sense of §1.14.1-12 is answered by anything is open (T10-15).
- **Conditional relationships stay conditional:** W's language (◇ G1), N's and C's content (◇ G11, G10).
- **The unrecorded inputs and the open translation boundaries are shown:** PL-8 (covers, θ, the identity link: R12, T10-11); T10-14 (R18: product terms into items and rules, no translator named; R12: no input-acceptance step).

## 10.5.6 Is it published? (S0039's test)

**S0039's test:** a language counts as published only when **a second consumer** relies on it.
- **Standing:** the book's consumers of PL-1 to PL-9 are WM (writes), D (shows), N (reads rule standing) and, after G1, W (reads producer authorisation). **None exists as a separate consumer in the estate's record**, which is not in D (§9.13.2).
- **Result (Int):** PL-1 to PL-9 are the **promised** language. That any consumer relies on it is **not attested**. This is consistent with S0 and with S0633's "do not freeze yet".
