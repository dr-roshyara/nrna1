# 3.1 The six dimensions of a knowledge item

## In plain language

The corpus describes every piece of knowledge by six independent properties. Two documents can say the same thing in different forms; one rule can be approved yet unenforced; a claim can be official yet only a hypothesis. Each of these is a different question, and **mixing them up is the source of repeated mistakes** in the corpus's own history.

## The model (Src)

The corpus's "canonical meta-model" (2026-08-02) states [S0016 L58–63]:

> KnowledgeOS instance = NATURE × REPRESENTATION × GOVERNANCE-STATUS × AUTHORITY-SCOPE × EVIDENCE-STATUS × OPERATIONAL-STATE

| Dimension | Question it answers | Values (as given) | Origin | Concept |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **nature** | what kind of thing is it? | decision · definition · constraint · description · finding · measurement (· skill) | RQ-002 [S0016 L41; GKC, Ch. 0 §0.3] | `[CON-0001 · validated]` |
| **representation** | how is it encoded? | executable · recorded · conversational · tacit | RQ-002 [S0016 L42] | `[CON-0002 · validated]` |
| **governance status** | where is it in its life? | the 8 executable states draft · discovery · reviewed · approved · baseline · frozen · superseded · archived | RQ-002 + `statuses.yaml` [S0016 L43; S0012 L119] | `[CON-0003 · invalid]` |
| **authority scope** (this book: *canon scope*) | how far does it reach? | context-local · project · platform · constitutional (· runtime) | RQ-002 + placement scopes [S0016 L44] | `[CON-0004 · validated]` |
| **evidence status** | how strongly is it held? | observed · inferred · hypothesized | new [S0016 L45] | `[CON-0005 · validated]` |
| **operational state** | does it bite? | enforced · advisory · unexercised | new [S0016 L46] | `[CON-0006 · invalid]` (partly valid: MM-4 open, whether the value belongs to the artifact or to the rule it carries [S0016 L154]; T3-09) |

**Where the two new dimensions come from.** The corpus admits evidence status because "ten rediscoveries and four falsifications happened because claims were not graded". It admits operational state because it is "what '0 traversals' and my '0 of 11 enforcing' error both measured badly" [S0016 L45–46]. Neither existed as a field anywhere when it was admitted ("NOT a field anywhere"; "never formalized").

**This resolves the lead of Ch. 1 §1.8** ("6 dimensions (2 new, 1 rejected on test)"): four existing dimensions, two new (evidence status, operational state) and one rejected (intent status, §3.2) [S0016 L58]. The identification in Ch. 1 RC-17a, there marked Int, is confirmed at its primary source.

## Independence shown by use (Src → Ded)

The corpus tested the model by classifying six real artifacts without changing it [S0016 L121–135]. Two rows show that governance status and operational state vary independently:
- a methodology constitution is **frozen** and **enforced**;
- a capability is **realized** but only **advisory**.

**Ded:** one such pair shows the two dimensions are **not functionally dependent**: neither value determines the other. It does not show **full independence** (that every combination occurs), which would need more cases. The corpus calls it "the orthogonality proof"; this book reads it as the weaker result.

This is the meta-model's form of Ch. 2 §2.9–2.10: how strongly a rule binds is not what enforces it.

## Why governance status is marked invalid here `[CON-0003 · invalid]`

The dimension is well sourced and its values are executable. It fails the **consistency** check:
- the book's Ch. 1 §1.2 shows that GKC's governance status mixes **standing** (canonical, superseded) with **lifecycle** (draft, archived);
- the corpus itself found that the authoritative lifecycle document draws "idea" and "research" as statuses, while the executable enum defines them as types [S0012 L118–125].

As one dimension it is therefore **partly valid** (TODO T3-01). The corpus's own remedy separates provenance from standing in the `authority` field (PM-1, Ch. 1 §1.2), but leaves governance status as stated.

## Review C3 notes (2026-10-18)

- **The test needed values outside the declared sets** (F-10): "accepted-with-refinements" [S0016 L130] is not one of the 8 statuses [S0020 L27]; SCOPE "product" [S0016 L129–130] is missing from CON-0004's value list. The criterion is "every existing document" [S0016 L10], but six artifacts were tested, one of them the document itself [L132]. The source's own rule, "If any row required changing the model, the model would be wrong" [L123], is arguably triggered.
- **Sufficiency was never tested** (F-11): the test table has only "Orthogonal?" and "Necessary?" columns [S0016 L39]. Reading the six-artifact test as a sufficiency test is **Int**.
- **The file is multi-temporal** (F-12): its body hierarchy [L102–106] is contradicted by its later annotation [L30, L33]; the body was not revised.
- **Governance status, restated reason** (F-09): the inconsistency is shown in scope by values outside the 8 [S0016 L128, L130] and by a second vocabulary, ADR-M, for the same position [S0018 L31, PM-3].
