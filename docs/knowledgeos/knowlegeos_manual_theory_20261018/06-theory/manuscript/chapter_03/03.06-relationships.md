# 3.6 How the concepts relate, and which relations may never change

## In plain language

Sections 3.1–3.5 describe what a knowledge item is and which vocabularies exist. This section asks how the platform's concepts **relate**: what comes from what, what contains what, and who owns what. One source (S0021, a generated candidate submitted to the Decision Authority) gives a full answer, and it ends with a distinction the rest of the book will need. Some relations are **invariants** that no project may change. Others are **implementation choices** that may be changed without breaking the model.

## The chain from purpose to artifact (Src)

A reviewer proposed Mission → Strategy → Principles → Capability. The author tested each step against the repository [S0021 L26–33]:

| Layer | Evidence named by the source | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **engineering strategy** | "12+" charter documents (eleven are named) | admitted `[CON-0035 · invalid]` |
| **engineering principle** | six registers with their own identifier series (AIP, AP, PGP, MC, SD, DR) | admitted `[CON-0036 · invalid]` |
| **design policy** (`DP-n`) | every capability in the Capability Catalog bound to one design policy | admitted, "DISCOVERED" `[CON-0034 · invalid]` |
| engineering domain as owner of capabilities | "No artifact links any `CAP-n` to a `PD-n`." | **rejected** |

Resulting chain [S0021 L37–42]:

```
MISSION → STRATEGY (charter) → PRINCIPLE → DESIGN POLICY (DP-n) → CAPABILITY (CAP-n) → KNOWLEDGE → ARTIFACT
```

**Why the three layers are marked invalid here.** The source admits them, but:
- Strategy's kind ("purpose→governance bridge") and Principle's and Design Policy's kind ("governance") are **not among the eight kinds** the commission defined: purpose · domain · capability · knowledge · representation · container · runtime element · projection [S0021 L48, L54–56] (TODO T3-11);
- design policy is "observable in the Catalog but never declared as a concept"; adopting it is open (RO-1 [S0021 L166]; TODO T2-07; Ch. 2 §2.9, RC-46).

## Kind and owner of each concept (Src)

The source gives thirteen concepts, each with a kind and an owner, all graded OBSERVED [S0021 L50–64]. The owners:
- vision and mission: the sponsor (the mission "enacted, unratified");
- strategy: ARB / sponsor;
- principle: the Decision Authority, sponsor + ARB, or the platform, depending on the register;
- design policy: the Decision Authority, via the catalog;
- capability: **its design policy**, not a domain;
- artifact: "its `owner` field";
- knowledge space: the space's own owner (for the knowledge platform, one named individual);
- PKS and product: the product team;
- projection: the space that generates it;
- **runtime adapter: "no owner found"** (open, RO-4 [S0021 L169]).

**Int:** owner appears here as an **attribute** of every concept, and for artifacts as a field. That places owner outside the six dimensions of §3.1, which bears on the open question of where intent's second argument lives (§3.2; TODO T3-02).

## Four relations (Src)

"`contains` = a container relation · `references` = a pointer · `generates` = produces a new instance · `depends on` = cannot be understood without." [S0021 L79; E-S0021 Q12]

Selected rows of the source's table [S0021 L81–95]:

| Concept | may contain | may generate | depends on |
|---|---|---|---|
| design policy | nothing | capability | principle |
| capability | nothing | evidence, assessments | design policy, knowledge |
| knowledge | nothing | **nothing** ("it is represented, never generative") | principle (for rules) |
| artifact | nothing (a leaf) | nothing | the knowledge it represents |
| knowledge space | artifacts, **other knowledge spaces** | projections | its constitution and declared scope |
| PKS | product artifacts, "never reusable Method" | product projections | engineering knowledge, product |
| projection | nothing | nothing | the space that generates it |

**Two internal tensions** `[CON-0037 · invalid]` (TODO T3-12):
1. The table says knowledge generates **nothing** [L89]; the same file later restates I-11 because "knowledge does participate in generation" [L115]. The table was not updated.
2. The table **permits** a space to contain other spaces [L91]; the same file says "containment vs adjacency unevidenced" (H-3 [L159]). A permission is not evidence, so nesting stays open (§3.3, CON-0011; TODO T3-04).

## Knowledge does not execute (Src)

The restated invariant I-11: "KNOWLEDGE DOES NOT EXECUTE. Capabilities execute. Knowledge CONSTRAINS AND INFORMS generation." [S0021 L115; E-S0021 Q20] `[CON-0039 · invalid]` (partly valid after review C4: Ch. 1 §1.1 draws the knowledge/capability line by "who executes", so the statement is close to definitional, a circularity recorded as SEQ-0012; standing: generated candidate; the restating review is not identified).

- *Counterexample attempt:* a rule enforced by a lint check. The check is a capability's realization and executes; the rule informs it. The statement holds.
- **Int:** this is the meta-model's form of "knowledge feeds authority, never holds it" (Ch. 1): knowledge neither holds authority nor executes; acts hold authority, capabilities execute.

## Invariants vs implementation choices (Src)

"An invariant may never be changed by a project; a choice may be changed freely." [S0021 L99; E-S0021 Q14] `[CON-0038 · validated]`

- **Choices** ("changeable without breaking the model" [L117]): which folder a space maps to; Markdown; YAML and Mermaid; the graph's declared scope ("a DECISION, not an invariant" [L124]); Claude Code as the runtime adapter (n=1); frontmatter; identifier formats; the form of projections [L119–128].
- **Invariants:** eleven, I-1 … I-11 [L103–115]. Among them: knowledge is represented by an artifact and is never the artifact (I-1); a capability protects "EXACTLY ONE invariant (one `DP-n`)" (I-2); a space has a declared boundary (I-3); one authoritative artifact per topic and context, lint-enforced (I-5); the runtime never owns knowledge (I-6); a PKS never contains reusable method (I-7); governance precedes automation (I-8); governance status is independent of authority (I-9); a constitutional principle is "void-overriding" (I-10; Ch. 2 §2.5, RC-47).
- The source's claim: "A future adopter may change every choice and none of the invariants — that is what makes this a platform rather than a repository layout." [L130]

**Counterexample attempt (for CON-0038).** I-4, I-7 and I-11 have all been reworded, breached or put to the ARB [L108, L111, L115, L168]. None was changed **by a project**; each change came through review or is routed to the ARB. The distinction holds as defined. "Freely" means without breaking the model, not without governance: changing the graph's scope is still a governance act (§3.5, S0020 L35).

**What the list does not meet (Src vs claim):**
- the heading says "each traced to a governed statement" [L101], but I-1 is traced to RQ-002 (unadopted, MM-1 [L170]), I-4 to DP-2 (of CAP-002, deferred [S0020 L100]), and I-11 to an unidentified review;
- I-7 is "stated and BREACHED" [L111] and RO-3 asks whether to repair or withdraw it [L168];
- I-4 is reported "FALSIFIED" in the file's header [L7] but "NOT false — INCOMPLETE" in its row [L108]: a later layer of the file contradicts an earlier one. The book follows the row, which repeats S0012's diagnosis (Ch. 1 §1.14.4, RC-25, RC-48);
- the I-numbers collide with the book's own Ch. 0 list (TODO T0-01).

**Int:** the invariant/choice split answers, for the platform model itself, the question the operating loop asks of every change: what must remain stable, and what may evolve. It is the first statement of that kind in the corpus read so far.

## The success test (Src)

"If any row needed a new top-level concept, the model would be incomplete." [S0021 L134] Eleven existing things were placed; "All eleven place." [S0021 L150]

Two limits:
- the test includes the document itself [L148], as S0016's test did (§3.1, review C3 notes);
- several rows place an artifact as carrying **Evidence** or a **Binding** [L139, L144, L147]. Evidence is not one of the thirteen concepts, though capability and product "generate" it. Whether evidence is a kind of knowledge is not stated.

The test surfaced a candidate twelfth invariant: "a Capability may read across a space boundary, but may not own what it reads" [L151] `[CON-0041 · invalid]`. The source itself holds it back: n=1 capability (TODO T3-13).

## The PKS corrected (Src)

"A **PKS contains** artifacts; it is not below them", accepted as "a real error in my model" [S0021 L19]. A PKS is "a product-scoped knowledge space" [L61] `[CON-0042 · invalid]`. Its own invariant I-7 is breached, and its declared boundary is not stated; S0020 places the PKS outside the graph's declared scope [S0020 L96] (TODO T3-14).

## Where each thing belongs (S0024; revision RC-61)

The Architecture Fitness Assessment sorts the platform's material into kernel, PKS and business product [S0024 L124–149]:
- **kernel:** "Only Tier-1 items — domain-free ∧ binding-free ∧ evidence-free" [L137; E-S0024 Q20], e.g. the standards ES-001…006 (ES-005.1 restated product-neutrally), the closed verdict vocabulary, the tactical DDD principles, and "the Reference Architecture, once ADOPTED" `[CON-0057 · validated]`, as a **necessary** condition only; the tiers are defined in S0028, and admission also needs standing (Ch. 6 §6.1, C4–C5). *Links (review C4 F-26, SEQ-0015):* the kernel is an open question in Ch. 0 §0.9 (Q2); the bootstrap experiment says the Kernel "remains HYPOTHESIS" [S0025 L153]; and the list includes the EP-01/02/03 role model, which the experiment treated as source-project property (reach #12);
- **a product's PKS:** "the BINDING and the EVIDENCE layers": the product's language, contexts and context map, its input contract, its identifier registers, its case law [L143–145];
- **never a separate product:** the runtime adapter ("replaceable ≠ reusable." [L128]), a product PKS (an instance, "one per product, by definition" [L129], which conflicts with S0022's finding that "one PKS per product" fails for configurable products; Ch. 3 §3.7), evidence records (the protocol is platform-side; the records never are), and internal subsystems: "Promoting one to a product would encode an internal design decision as an ownership boundary" [L132].

**Decomposition is not materialization.** An earlier ruling (BRM-1) "retired extraction-by-COPY. It did not retire extraction-by-DECOMPOSITION." [L185]; "The output of decomposition is a classification, not a document set" [L189]. **Int:** in §3.4's terms, a decomposition changes what is known **about** knowledge items without creating a new carrier.
