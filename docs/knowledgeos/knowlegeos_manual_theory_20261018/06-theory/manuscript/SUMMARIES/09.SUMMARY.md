## Block 1: §9.1–§9.9 (the scaffold and the first boundary tests)
- **§9.1:** the kernel scaffold has preservation with refusal, plus required capabilities. ∅, refuse-all and noop fail the finite test, and minimal kernels depend on the pool. C6 gives the kernel type: input origin, guards, and transition and trace invariants.
- **§9.2–§9.5:** corpus evidence.
  - Portability of parts is not sufficiency of the whole.
  - Capabilities are not kernel operators (CON-0111).
  - A read rule discharged everywhere constrains no state (CON-0167).
  - A recorded rule must reach the session that acts.
- **§9.6–§9.7:** boundary tests.
  - Recording authority: F22 was decided as (C + O).
  - The evaluator of standing is necessary, and its execution can be delegated. Result 4 is a common, versioned value for the values shown to change answers.
- **§9.8–§9.9:** history and trust as primitives.
  - History is derived from record properties, with Lemma D for cuts that cross no recorded loss. The independence of those properties is refuted (CON-0148).
  - Trust is an assessment, under a conditional reduction (CON-0160, restated as CON-0182).
