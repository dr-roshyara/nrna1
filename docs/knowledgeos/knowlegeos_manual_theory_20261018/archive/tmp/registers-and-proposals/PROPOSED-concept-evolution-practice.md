# Concept evolution record (research working practice)

**Status: PROPOSED.** A recommended practice, not ratified. Prepared 2026-10-18 from the Principal's note "treat the corpus as a history of evolving ideas". It is kept in the session scratchpad: no repository change was made.

## 1. What already exists (canonical discovery first)

The book partly does this already. The practice should **extend** these parts, not duplicate them:
- **one-page method, step 3:** "a later document is not automatically right";
- **idea register classifications:** UPDATE OR REFINEMENT · CONTRADICTION · REPEATED IDEA;
- **status marks** Src / Ind / Ded / Int / Hyp, kept apart from governance standing;
- **worked instances already in the book:**
  - V3 REV 1 withdrawing the "five names" claim and OQ-1 as phrased (Ch. 0 §0.2; Ch. 1 §1.14.4–5);
  - C1, AMS L51 against IA L99–101: two concepts kept, not resolved (Ch. 1 §1.13.1);
  - "evaluability" corrected to "required consistency guarantee" (Ch. 2 §2.9; an evolution **within the book**, not in the sources).

**What is missing:** one place per concept that follows its history across documents, with **governance status** kept separate from research support.

## 2. The four distinctions, mapped to the book

| Principal's distinction | Book's existing device |
|---|---|
| historical truth (what was proposed) | **Src** with an exact location; never paraphrased into agreement |
| research conclusion (what evidence supports now) | **Ind / Ded / Hyp**, with the seven obligations per claim |
| governance status (who authorized it) | standing and the act behind it (Ch. 1 §1.6). For sources: the document's own declared standing (e.g. AMS "governs nothing"; V3 REV 1 "rulings are decisions") |
| current theoretical position | the chapter's text, with alternatives preserved where unresolved |

## 3. Classification of a change between documents

The note's four classes, plus three that occur in this corpus:

| Class | Test | Example in the book |
|---|---|---|
| resolved evolution | explicit rejection, justified, approved by a body with standing in that scope | V3 REV 1's ARB rulings: approved inside the 08-16 programme. **Their standing outside that programme is a separate question** |
| research unresolved | B proposed; evidence incomplete or competing | C1 (constitutes vs represents) |
| governance unresolved | research favours B; no valid decision | most PROPOSED source rules (e.g. PC's override rule) |
| unexplained contradiction | both applicable, nothing decides | OQ-37 / OQ-38 items |
| **stale reference** (added) | a later-**dated** document still uses A because it was written **before** A's revision. That is not disagreement | batch-4 F25: PC (10:34) and DR (10:40) predate V3 REV 1 (11:36) |
| **term drift** (added) | the same word names different concepts, or different words name one concept; no position changed | "lifecycle", the seven names for three concepts (Ch. 1 §1.17.5) |
| **misreading by the book** (added) | the conflict exists only in the book's reading of a source | batch-4 F6 ("duplicate" vs SI's "potential duplicate"), corrected |

**Safeguards:**
- the order of documents is **established, not assumed**: from timestamps or explicit references, and stated with its evidence;
- "evolution" may not be used to explain away a real contradiction. A change counts as evolution only if the later document **says** it changes the earlier one, or if the relationship is derived and marked **Ded**;
- **a source's own approval** (e.g. "approved inside Track B") is reported as that source's claim about itself, not as a valid decision. Whether its approval is valid and applies here is the governance question.

## 4. The record (one row per concept that is rejected, replaced or materially revised)

The nine fields from the note:
1. identifier and original wording;
2. source and status;
3. rejection or revision, with source and reason;
4. replacement and rationale;
5. evidence, counterexamples, assumptions;
6. scope;
7. current status: proposed · under investigation · supported · approved · superseded · unresolved;
8. dependencies in the book;
9. open questions and the next responsible action.

**Proposed form:** a small table `CONCEPT-EVOLUTION.md` beside the idea register. The register keeps per-source ideas; the table keeps per-concept histories, and each row cites register ids. This is a light record, only for concepts that actually changed, consistent with "records stay proportionate".

**Status vocabulary caution.** "approved" means approved by a body with standing **for this theory**. Today no such approval exists for any source concept. Every "approved" in the sources is the source's own scope (e.g. ARB inside the 08-16 programme; Track B). So, read strictly, every row would today be at most "supported".

## 6. Assessment

- **Benefit:** it stops two errors seen in this work: reading a newer document as automatically right, and reading a stale reference as a disagreement (F25).
- **Risk:** a second register drifting from the first. That is why rows cite register ids, and why rows exist only for concepts that changed.
- **Applies to:** authorized background sources and the book's own revisions. **The restricted corpus stays out of reach** until authorized; nothing here authorizes reading it.

**Decision requested:** approve, amend or decline. Until then it stays PROPOSED and is not applied.
