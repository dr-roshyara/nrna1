## 4. Contradictions and unresolved questions

### 4.1 The authority definitions (the Principal's question). **Status: UNRESOLVED, hypothesis strengthened**

- **[Ind]** `docs/knowledge/schema/authorities.yaml` header: "Authority answers: how much should I trust this, and where did it come from? It is INDEPENDENT of `status` (lifecycle)." That matches S0010 L88 exactly.
- So in the platform schema, "authority" is a **field of a knowledge artifact** (trust plus origin; values authoritative / derived / generated …).
- S0008 SC-5 (L96) [Src] flags exactly this: "`authority` = provenance × standing in ONE field (its own header asks two questions)".
- The book's "authority" (act × scope × time, §1.6; AMS L49 "a recorded permission for a named activity within a stated scope") is a **permission to act**.
- **[Ded]** These have different bearers (artifact vs actor or grant) and different arity, so they are **not equivalent**. The evidence now favours **term drift** (one word, at least two concepts) over a contradiction about one concept.
- **Test still outstanding:** does the corpus ever apply the provenance × standing sense to actors, or the act × scope × time sense to artifacts? Not found in S0001–S0010.
- **Not decided:** which term keeps the word "authority". That is a ubiquitous-language decision, reserved.
