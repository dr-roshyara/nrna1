**[Ded]** There are **two classes of freeze**:
- **ruled freezes** (R-27, R-37, R-38: DA/ARB, with ruling IDs and, for R-37, a lifting condition);
- **declared freezes** (no named authority).

The sources conflict on whether the second class is legitimate: S0034's "sole authority" implies a declared freeze without the DA/ARB has none. **Not resolved from evidence alone.** It needs the rulings register, or a later source that reports how the declared freezes were authorized.

## D. The meanings of "authority"

| Sense | Locations so far | Explicitly distinguished by the source? |
|---|---|---|
| **(a) permission to act** (a grant, a decision right) | AMS L49; S0034 L53 ("sole authority to … freeze"); S0037 L227 (R-46) | partly: R-46 separates approval from execution authority |
| **(b) a document's trust and origin** (the `authority` field) | S0018 L72–96; `authorities.yaml` | **yes**: S0018 decomposes it into provenance × standing |
| **(c) a justification order** | S0026 L83 | yes, as one of four "partial orders" |
| **(d) NEW candidate: a governing body / authority system over a domain** ("three authority systems"; "Decision Authority"; "sponsor + ARB") | S0019 L98–107; S0037 L23, L199–218 | the sources treat these as **owners** of domains |
| **(e) NEW candidate: an authority flow between contexts** ("the knowledge flow and the authority flow run in opposite directions"; the product is "authoritative over the platform's right to evolve") | S0039 L131–133 | not distinguished from (c) |

**[Ded]**
- (d) is (a) seen from the bearer's side (a body that holds decision rights).
- (e) is plausibly (c) at the level of contexts.

So the evidence supports **three distinct concepts** (permission, trust/origin, justification or governing direction) with (d) and (e) as variants, **not five independent ones**. *This is my derivation, not a source statement.*

**No source so far reconciles them.**
