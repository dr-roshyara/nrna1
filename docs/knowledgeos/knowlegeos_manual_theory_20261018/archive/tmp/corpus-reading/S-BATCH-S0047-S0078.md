- (i) By 08-17 the corpus shows **freezes with a traceable authority**: a PO/ARB commissioning act (B2), and an amendment by a PO/ARB acceptance act (B3). There are still also the earlier unauthorized ones (S0017 L55; S0041 L105).

## C. External authority

**No source in S0047–S0078 treats law, regulation or any external obligation as authority.**

| Source / location | Kind | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| S0067 L116 | [Src] | "**Project Governance, the external upstream domain** … the origin of all policy and all authority". Here "external" means **external to the platform software**. It does not mean external to the organisation |
| S0062 L133 | [Src] | "HUMAN PO/ARB (authority origin — **outside the software**)" |

**[Ded]:** the corpus places the origin of all authority in the PO/ARB. That **leaves no room** for an external obligation as a source of authority. This is an **implicit closure**, not a ruling. Proposed extensions remain only S0022 F-3.
