# Appendix — Glossary (one meaning per term)

*Created 2026-10-18 after audit H-01, H-09 and C-02. It is the reference for the cross-chapter consistency check at every five-file review. Source terms with different meanings are listed with this book's term.*

| Term in this book | Meaning | Not to be confused with | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| **claim** | the atomic unit of knowledge: content + coordinate + provenance + status + scent | a document (a bundle of claims) | Ch. 0 §0.3 |
| **provenance** | where a claim came from, and by which act (*authored-by*); immutable | standing | Ch. 1 §1.2 |
| **standing** | what the organisation currently treats a claim as (provisional, authoritative, historical, superseded …); changed only by recorded acts | RM-1's "standing obligation" (= **persistence**, binds always); lifecycle phase | Ch. 1 §1.2; Ch. 2 §2.0 |
| **lifecycle phase** | where a subject is in its life (draft, reviewed, frozen, archived …); a **scope dimension** of the subject | standing; validity | Ch. 2 §2.8.6 |
| **governance status (GKC)** | GKC's dimension defined as "lifecycle position"; **mixes** standing (canonical, superseded) with lifecycle (draft, archived) | — | Ch. 0 §0.3; Ch. 1 §1.2 mapping |
| **validity (period)** | the time interval in which a rule, exception, grant or other claim binds; for a claim that states no period, this book uses the **convention** [*t*ₐ, ∞) (Int; Ch. 1 §1.14.1) | standing; lifecycle phase; a grant's **act window** | Ch. 2 §2.2, §2.11; Ch. 1 §1.14.1 |
| **effective (rule)** | standing *adopted* ∧ t ∈ validity: **current authority without the grant conditions**; what can be checked for rules today | "in force by date"; current authority | Ch. 2 §2.11; Ch. 1 §1.14.1 |
| **evidential grade** | how strong the evidence for a claim is (observed · inferred · hypothesized · unknown) | standing | Ch. 1 §1.3 |
| **canon scope** | GKC's fourth dimension: where a claim's canonicity holds (context-local, project, platform, constitutional) | grant scope | Ch. 0 §0.3; Ch. 2 §2.8.4a |
| **grant scope** | what an actor may act upon (SC's "authority scope", S-2) | canon scope; subject scope | Ch. 2 §2.8.1–2.8.2 |
| **subject scope** | what a statement or rule is about (S-1) | evaluation scope | Ch. 2 §2.8.1 |
| **evaluation scope** | what a mechanism actually looked at (S-3) | subject scope | Ch. 2 §2.8.1 |
| **authority (valid act)** | a permission for a named **activity** within a **scope**, conferred by a valid human act; the **grant record** represents it and is evidence of it, not the authority itself; see **valid (act)** | ownership; relevance; existence; its record | Ch. 1 §1.6, §1.13.1, §1.14.1 |
| **ownership** | who must maintain something | authority | Ch. 1 §1.11 |
| **binding strength** | how strongly a rule obliges (mandatory, recommended); set by the issuing authority | enforcement consequence | Ch. 2 §2.9 |
| **enforcement consequence** | what a mechanism **actually does** today when a rule is broken; set by configuration or act; distinct from **hardness** (what is intended). **Given premise P-E (not adopted)**, a block is admissible only if the check is executable, an act that is **currently authoritative** at the time of the block authorizes it (directly, by specifying hardness, or by adoption as mandatory only under reading (i) of reserved decision 5), and the block stays within that act's scope, period and rule version. A confirmation request to a person holding a covering grant, **recorded as an act**, is not a block | binding strength; hardness; realization level | Ch. 2 §2.9; Ch. 1 §1.14.5 |
| **realization level** | how far a rule has travelled toward executable form (stated → contract → typed → enforced → verified) | enforcement consequence | Ch. 1 §1.9 |
| **rule** | a standing (persistent), authoritative obligation within a defined scope and period; deviation is non-conformance unless an authorized exception permits it (RM-1, Track B) | decision; recommendation; permission/access control | Ch. 2 §2.0 |
| **exception** | a separately authorized deviation from a rule within a defined scope and validity period (RM-4) | an edit to the rule | Ch. 2 §2.11 |
| **conflict** | two rules whose **mandatory** effects cannot be jointly satisfied somewhere in their **effective** applicability | tension (recommended effects); overlap | Ch. 2 §2.3 |
| **unspecified (⊥)** | some value is intended but unknown (SC-2) | not applicable; ∅; "all" | Ch. 2 §2.8.4, §2.8.6 |
| **not applicable** | the dimension cannot apply to this kind of subject; governed per subject kind | unspecified; NOT_APPLICABLE (analysis result) | Ch. 2 §2.8.6 |
| **NOT_APPLICABLE (analysis result)** | PC's undefined analysis result. Two readings: (i) the analysis cannot be applied to the pair (E6-like; refusing a HARD gate is correct); (ii) the rules provably cannot meet (E1; licenses "no conflict"). Open; AI recommendation: report (ii) as proven consistent by disjointness | not applicable (E7); unknown | Ch. 2 §2.9 |
| **indeterminate** | three-valued result CANNOT DETERMINE | undecidable (computability) | Ch. 2 §2.8.4 |
| **lifecycle (other uses)** | the GKC knowledge cycle (Ch. 0 §0.4); the RM-3 rule path (= standing transitions); exception standing (granted, withdrawn, expired); authority endings (AM-1, plus consumption) | — | listed so that "lifecycle" is never used bare |
| **representation (form)** | GKC's dimension: the form a claim takes (Executable, Recorded, Conversational, Tacit) | carrier standing; presentation | Ch. 0 §0.3; Ch. 1 §1.14.4 |
| **canonical / derived carrier** | the standing of a *store*: the repository is canonical; database, graph, search and vector stores are derived and rebuildable | standing of a claim | Ch. 1 §1.14.4 |
| **presentation / projection** | an audience-facing view of knowledge (ADR, report, AI context); a sink from which nothing flows back | representation (form) | Ch. 1 §1.14.4 |
| **current authority (of a claim)** | derived, never stored, not the same as standing: the three-valued (strong Kleene) value of §1.14.1, the **least fixed point** over all acts: some adoption act authorized through a cited basis (act windows, actors, activity, scope) and not annulled; *t* within its validity; no effective, authorized revocation or supersession of the claim after it; the basis intact under the **ending policy P**. Evaluated over two time axes (act time; record position) under a **record view ρ**. Open alternatives κ, χ, ω, σ, π, υ, ι (final review C1–C9, briefs 1–6). Answer = **headline + all reasons**: current · cannot determine · not current, causal order (not validly made / never adopted › revoked › superseded › authority ended › suspended › no longer valid › not yet valid) | standing (de jure; recognized); workflow state ("Approved"); effective | Ch. 1 §1.14.1, §1.16.2 |
| **level error** | assigning one name or field to concepts of different levels (tiers; standing vs lifecycle) | a labelling problem | Ch. 0 §0.2; Ch. 1 §1.2 |
| **authority gate** | a mechanism that checks whether an actor may act (act × scope × time); its **report** of a missing record is evidence; the **STOP** that follows rests on INV-A3 (stated in V3, no adopting act located) or premise P-R, not on the authority axiom alone | conformance gate | Ch. 1 §1.14.5 |
| **conformance gate** | a mechanism that checks an artifact against rules; needs rules with applicability and effect; **given P-E**, may block only when a valid act authorizes that block (Ch. 1 §1.14.5); may always evaluate and report | authority gate | Ch. 1 §1.14.5 |
| **pinned reference** | a reference to another claim or record at a fixed version, so the referring claim's meaning cannot drift without an act | a live reference (needs detection or prohibition) | Ch. 1 §1.16.6 |
| **referent time (Hyp)** | when the state referred to is read: pinned at the decision · at read time · self-contained | required consistency guarantee (atomicity) | Ch. 1 §1.16.6 |
| **required consistency guarantee (atomicity)** | how tightly a condition's check must be bound to the change it governs (ATOMICALLY_REQUIRED · SAME_CONTEXT_TRANSACTION · EVENTUALLY_CONSISTENT · ANALYTICAL); fixes where the check may live (EVT-1). Not the same as when the condition can be checked | hardness; enforcement consequence; realization level | Ch. 2 §2.9 |
| **global invariant** | a condition over the whole knowledge space (e.g. one authoritative home per claim); preventable only where all writes pass one serialization point (physical or logical), otherwise detectable only | an invariant one object can own | Ch. 1 §1.16.7 |
| **may-read (Hyp) / visibility** | whether an actor or an assembled context may contain a claim: the **visibility** vis(c) of §1.7, seen from the grant side (a read grant covering the claim's class). Derived items are bounded by their sources' visibility, but that bound is necessary, not sufficient: inferred relationships may need a stricter classification | standing (is-authoritative) | Ch. 1 §1.7, §1.17.5 |
| **withheld (E8, Hyp)** | empty because the reader may not see what exists; may reduce to E3 narrowed by policy | not looked at (E3); no knowledge (E5) | Ch. 2 §2.8.8 |
| **valid (act)** | an act whose actor holds a cited, covering grant for the act's **activity** and scope, inside the grant's act window; **plus a recorded reason** under reading (a) of reserved decision 4, or under (c) for specified kinds; recognized by the organisation once recorded (I-2) | well-founded; verifiable; its record | Ch. 1 §1.6, §1.13.1, §1.14.1, §1.17.2 |
| **well-founded (decision)** | its recorded reasons **cite evidence** that supports it, from a context whose coverage is stated. Under reading (b) a valid act can be ill-founded and keep its standing until revised; under (a) an act with no recorded reason is not valid; under (c) that holds for specified kinds | valid; verifiable; "reasoned" (= a reason is present) | Ch. 1 §1.17.2 |

| **verifiable (act or record)** | recorded in enough detail (approver, reason, scope, period) for a third party to check validity and well-foundedness; a property of the record | valid; well-founded | Ch. 2 §2.11; Ch. 1 §1.17.2 |
| **quality obligation** | the name for reading (b) of I-2's "reasoned" part: a breach is a recorded defect, not a cause of invalidity. A reading under reserved decision 4, not an adopted rule | validity condition | Ch. 1 §1.17.2 |
| **ending policy P** | the set of grant-ending kinds (expiry, consumption, revocation, supersession, annulment, suspension) that carry over to claims adopted under the grant, possibly per kind of claim; **reserved decision 1**; no value assumed. An ending caused by the adopting act itself (e.g. the act consumed the grant) never counts against that act's own product | act window; validity | Ch. 1 §1.14.1 |
| **P-E (premise)** | "no mechanism may produce the normative effects of an act, or change the standing of a claim, unless a valid act authorizes it"; normative effect = changes what someone may or must do (permit, forbid, block, oblige); including "permit" reaches mechanisms that fail open; **not adopted** (reserved decision 3) | ARCH-INV-1; INV-A3 | Ch. 1 §1.14.5 |
| **hardness** | whether a rule is **meant** to block (HARD · SOFT · ADVISORY), set by a decision; may differ from what the mechanism does today | enforcement consequence; binding strength | Ch. 2 §2.9 |
| **record view ρ (L-RT)** | which state of the records an evaluation uses: as they stood when each act was done, or as they stand now. Decides whether a later **annulment** voids earlier acts from the start. **Reserved**; written as an explicit parameter so it cannot be settled silently | ending policy P | Ch. 1 §1.14.1 |

| **de jure standing / recognized standing** | what valid acts have made of a claim, recorded or not / what the organisation's records show; they coincide when every valid act is recorded | each other | Ch. 1 §1.2 |
| **epistemic support** | how far a statement may be relied on as true: observed within its coverage; a valid deduction as far as its premises; induction and hypotheses less; it confers no standing | standing; normative effect | Ch. 1 §1.3 |
| **normative effect** | a change in what someone may or must do (permit, forbid, block, oblige); only claims with standing as norms have it; distinct from a **standing change** | epistemic support; standing | Ch. 1 §1.3, §1.14.5 |
| **root act** | an act of the authority the organisation designates as the source of grants; authorized by stipulation; ends the regress of authorizations. **Who** the root is must be recorded by the organisation | grant | Ch. 1 §1.14.1 |
| **override** | whether a block may be lifted case by case: none · by a **recorded act**. Not a degree of hardness (a HARD gate may allow an override and still default to refusal), but defined only where the gate actually blocks. A UI confirmation is a report, not an act | hardness; confirmation request | Ch. 2 §2.9; Ch. 1 §1.14.5 |
| **design policy (DP-n)** | the named policy a capability executes; the anchor between a principle and a capability; 1:1 with its capability (corpus S0008) | the capability (which executes it); its script (a realization) | Ch. 2 §2.9 (RC-13) |
| **execution asset (AST)** | a registered runtime asset (prompt template, instruction document, hook) with a runtime moment and lineage; where a prompt belongs (corpus S0008) | a capability; knowledge | Ch. 1 §1.9 (RC-14) |
| **metadata / knowledge** | *descriptive* facts about an item ("what exists", e.g. its type) vs *prescriptive* information on its use (trust, owner, audience, review) (corpus S0010) | knowledge vs carrier (§1.5); method/binding/evidence (portability) | Ch. 1 §1.5 (RC-18) |
| **kind (archetype)** | a densely populated region of the knowledge dimensions, used as a category (domain, process, capability, knowledge, artifact, runtime asset, container, projection, purpose); a candidate, not a primitive (corpus S0009) | a dimension value (e.g. "product" = scope; "governance" = normative nature) | Ch. 0 §0.3 (RC-16) |
| **model amnesia** | re-deriving what the organisation already knows; the corpus's name for its central failure (= knowledge vaporization) | missing knowledge (not recorded at all) | Ch. 0 §0.1 (RC-1) |
| **survival ≠ sufficiency** | every element of a set can be portable while the set as a whole fails | — | Ch. 1 §1.8 (RC-8) |
| **operating-unowned** | an asset with operational effect and no owner: a defect. Contrast **candidate-unowned** (not adopted, correctly ownerless) | — | Ch. 1 §1.11 (RC-10) |
