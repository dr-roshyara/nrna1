## What this review shows about the method (trial observation)

- **Self-corrections are new claims.** Of the five items, three of my corrections were themselves defective (items 1, 2, 4). In two of them, the defect was an overstatement of exactly the kind the corrections were meant to remove.
- **The look-backs did not catch these; the independent reviewer did.** Targeted review (E-8) found what the producer's checks missed, at modest cost: a few minutes and about 92,000 reviewer tokens.
- **The round-2 corrections have not been independently reviewed.** By the same logic they need it before they are relied on, at least for 1a/1b (formula), 2a/2b (premise P-E and the generalized conclusion), and 3a (the I-2 choice).
