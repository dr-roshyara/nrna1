## 0. In business language

The **record of a blind review** and its comparison with the programme's own conclusions. The method:
- an **isolated AI session**, with no conversation context and with the programme's conclusion documents blocked;
- told to "ignore our conclusions" and rediscover the architecture from the evidence;
- the author did not review its own work: "I could not be the reviewer — I authored every conclusion under test."

**Results:**
- **12 conclusions reproduced blind.** These include the reusable kernel list, the knowledge platform's individual owner, product primacy, and "designed-for-reuse ≠ demonstrated-reusable".
- **4 sharpened.** Separation of duties is real as a process but not as people; the manual-adoption failure becomes direct evidence *against* the architecture's stance that "protocol + human authority suffice without resident mechanisms"; the constitution in force is unratified; the placement rules are breached.
- **3 corrected:**
  - "0 traversals" is false as generalized: the back-edge ran about 3 times;
  - the whole domain-model track never consulted the platform's own **frozen six-context map** (model amnesia occurrence #11);
  - "governance precedes automation" was over-granted as an invariant.
- **2 new findings:**
  - the one executable knowledge platform has a **consumption model already falsified** by its own evidence (E-1), still pending;
  - real work (CAP-001) was authorized by commissions **never minted** in the register, so "the register is provably not the complete record of authorizations".

**The strongest result:** the blind reviewer prescribed, as the missing test, exactly the kernel-bootstrap experiment that had already been run, without knowing it. Its prediction matched the measured outcome.

**Recorded "freeze":** the discovery corpus is frozen as historical evidence. Corrections are made by **new records**, never by rewriting the originals.
