## 0. In business language

The **gate** between discovery and writing the architecture. Every finding of the previous review phase (Phase B) is put into exactly one of four boxes. A finding that spans two is split: a defect and the decision it needs are different things.

**A · Validated (12 conclusions, each found twice by blind paths).** Examples:
- KnowledgeOS is a governed hypothesis whose gate is shut;
- product primacy governs;
- the knowledge-platform context is real, and owned by one named individual;
- governance is the strongest domain;
- the reusable kernel is listed, but "designed-for-reuse ≠ demonstrated-reusable";
- method is a distinct body (by scope exclusion);
- three knowledge ontologies coexist unreconciled;
- enforcement is runtime-side and capabilities advisory;
- the invariant set: human authority · produced-never-asserted · append-only · one-rule-one-home · fail-closed · burden-reversed · one-owner;
- the manual-adoption failure of a protocol is evidence against protocol-only governance.

**B · Factual corrections (4).** The key one:
- **"0 traversals" is retired.** The agreed fact is that evidence flowed back to the platform about **3 times informally** and about **2 times through the formal ladder**; PublicDigit → PKS: none.
- **"Governance precedes automation" was over-granted as invariant I-8.** The Reference Architecture's own note leaves it **deliberately unnumbered**, an "invariant-in-waiting". It is to be listed as a **candidate**.

**C · Process defects (8):**
- **model amnesia**: proposing before searching, now 11 times. It becomes a candidate **design requirement** for the platform;
- **work was authorized by commissions that never minted a ruling**, so "the register is provably not the complete record of authorizations";
- false gate evidence recurring;
- placement rules bypassed;
- backward links fixed case by case;
- **separation of duties is real as a process but unevidenced as people**: role separation is simulated by sessions, and "this limit is stated NOWHERE";
- the frozen context map lags reality;
- the over-evolution cost metric is not computed.

**D · Decisions required (10), each with its authority.** Three gate the next phase:
- the falsified consumption model E-1, plus the ontology reconciliation, block the knowledge-model section;
- the frozen-map amendment blocks the context map;
- the over-evolution check decides whether the next phase may run at all.
