## Contradictions and limits

- **Tiers overlap:** an artifact that is both binding-coupled and case-law-diluted fits tier 2 and tier 3 (L131 "2/3").
- **"Evidence-free"** means free of product case law, not unsupported by evidence. The name invites the wrong reading. [I]
- **The P1 annotation** (L95–101) says P1 was re-derived, not discovered, and lacks an `Ambiguous` class that the older Principle/Form framework has (Q6, Q7). So the decomposition is **not exhaustive** as stated.
