## 0. In business language

The research agenda for KnowledgeOS, under a strict rule: **no research without a named architectural question.** Every research item must serve an open decision that already has a number.

Each outside source is checked with **five fixed questions**. The last is "what decision should change?"; if the answer is none, the source is not recorded.

**The top-priority topic is Knowledge Governance:** how knowledge becomes trusted, who may change its standing, how corrections spread, and **who retires knowledge**. The key observation:
- the platform can retire *documents* (superseded, archived);
- nothing says **when a rule stops being true**;
- retirement has never actually been observed.

The states knowledge needs (candidate, approved, current, deprecated, historical, retired, superseded, archived) are states of **knowledge**, not of documents.

Other active topics:
- reconciling three ontologies;
- whether evidence status becomes an enforceable field;
- what to do when written rules alone fail to be used;
- how domain boundaries evolve;
- how AI should consume governed knowledge.

**Deliberately avoided:** generic RAG, vector databases, prompt tricks, agent frameworks.

A **freeze order** on 2026-08-03 limits work to three streams: Knowledge Governance, Operational Evidence and Knowledge Architecture. There are to be no new domains, capabilities, layers, folders or terms until evidence requires them.
