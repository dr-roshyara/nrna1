## 0. In business language

The "concept" counterpart of S0005: it checks whether the **outside world supports** 17 KnowledgeOS concepts, with the architecture held fixed. Sources are graded [FETCHED] (read on the web that session) or [CANONICAL] (established literature, used only for its core thesis).

**Strongest results:**
- **"Model amnesia"** is the long-known problem of **architectural knowledge vaporization** (Jansen & Bosch, 2005).
- The **PKS** matches what industry since 2025 calls "context engineering".
- The **enforcement gap** (context is governed, but what AI agents then do is not) is industry-wide.
- Several further ideas match standard practice: authority as **provenance plus standing** (W3C PROV; lineage ≠ "certified"), derived projections becoming authoritative only by **attestation**, and **evidence earns / governance grants** (technology readiness levels, GRADE).

**Only partly supported:**
- **"governance precedes automation"**: the literature separates the two but does not *order* them;
- "one capability, one invariant": analogy only.

**Still unproven hypotheses:**
- the DP-n layer;
- **generating** a governed PKS (n = 0);
- whether the kernel suffices as a set;
- the specific mission text;
- the governance-grade PKS framing.

Five challenges from the literature are recorded, not absorbed. The most important: pure **codification** of knowledge fails when its use is personal and contextual.
