## 0. In business language

The question was not "should authority get a state machine?" but "what independent dimensions describe how engineering knowledge evolves?". Ten existing progression mechanisms are inventoried:
- document status;
- the authority field;
- the promotion ladder;
- methodology maturity;
- decision-record status;
- capability lifecycle;
- the engineering protocol;
- observation protocols;
- identifier lifecycles;
- evidence counts.

They are **not ten lifecycles but four kinds of change, plus one thing that never changes:**

| Kind | Moved by | Can it be reversed? |
|---|---|---|
| **governance-act** progression | a decision by a named role | only by a further act |
| **evidential** progression | accumulating evidence; "nobody can decree it" | only by refutation |
| **work-execution** progression | time and effort; it has a clock | no; work concludes |
| **composite** trajectory | crosses the others (the promotion ladder; the capability lifecycle) | — |
| **provenance** (non-progression) | nothing: an immutable fact of origin | meaningless |

**The key sentence:** "a GOVERNANCE-ACT progression can be granted. An EVIDENTIAL progression can only be earned." No ruling can make an observation "replicated"; no evidence can make a document "frozen".

**The flagship finding:** the `authority` field asks two questions in one, "how much should I trust this **and** where did it come from?". Its values split accordingly:
- *generated* and *derived* are **provenance**, which never changes;
- *authoritative*, *provisional* and *historical* are **standing**, which changes only by governance acts.

So "authority = an immutable PROVENANCE fact × a mutable STANDING judgment. Attestation changes standing. Nothing changes provenance." A signed financial statement is *derived* in provenance and *authoritative* in standing at the same time, without contradiction. The earlier question (LG-1) dissolves, and the remaining question is whether standing changes need roles and guards (PM-2), which is a question about **acts**.

**Also:**
- the attestation chain (derived → reviewed → attested → released) is **qualification**, not authority;
- status consults authority at one point (a guard), "coupling by guard, not by fusion";
- the platform's central mechanism is named: "evidence EARNS; governance GRANTS; promotion requires BOTH".

## 11. [I] Interpretation (kept apart)

- **[I-1] This is the primary source of the book's §1.2.** "Authority = an immutable provenance fact × a mutable standing judgment" fixes three things the book had only from secondary citations:
  - the pair reading (T1-31);
  - provenance is descriptive (a fact), which is exactly the review C1 finding F-03 / TODO T1-44;
  - standing changes only by acts (the §1.6 axiom).
- **[I-2] "Granted vs earned"** is the cleanest corpus statement of the two-key rule (RC-7):
  - governance acts cannot produce evidence;
  - evidence cannot produce standing.

  The two keys are **causally** distinct, not just procedurally.
- **[I-3]** "Standing changes by governance acts, which the platform records as act-logs (rulings, dispositions), not as workflows" ties standing to the **record of acts**. It sits on the act-vs-record line (RC-29): a standing change recognized from the act log is subject to the log's completeness.
