## 0. In business language

This document tests the **relationships** between KnowledgeOS concepts against outside literature and standards. The concepts themselves (the nodes) are held fixed. Ten relationships are graded by how strongly outside evidence supports them.

**Most relationships are standard engineering:**
- knowledge represented by artifacts (as in library science, FRBR);
- policy decided separately from enforced (the XACML standard);
- the product team as the platform's customer (Team Topologies);
- evidence earning candidacy and governance granting promotion (Deming's Plan-Do-Study-Act cycle).

**What KnowledgeOS uniquely bets on** is that it can **generate** a governed product knowledge space (PKS). That edge has zero instances inside the platform and none found outside.

After review, the bet was broadened to the whole loop: discover → model → govern → generate → evolve the PKS → guide product engineering → produce software → collect evidence → improve KnowledgeOS. Along that loop:
- the **tail** is observed: software was built and evidence collected;
- the **head**, generation, is not.

Broadening makes the bet harder to prove, and the author says so.
