## 0. In business language

The ARB's secretary turns ten open decisions (plus one new one) into a **docket**: an agenda of one-page packages, each giving the decision, why it exists, evidence for and against, the risk of adopting and of rejecting, what depends on it, who decides, and a recommendation. "Recommendations only — every decision is the named authority's." The docket is ordered by **gating effect**: the decision that controls whether the others are worked comes first.

The eleven packages:
1. **D-9:** compute the platform-cost metric and run the over-evolution check of AIP-14. *Amended:* first decide **how** platform cost is measured (D-9a), then run the check (D-9b). The docket's own supporting evidence ("20+ platform-only documents in 48h") assumed the answer (document count) and is **downgraded** to an illustration.
2. **D-5:** ratify the enacted mission (sponsor). A text is proposed.
3. **D-1:** dispose the falsification of the knowledge platform's consumption model (E-1).
4. **D-8:** reconcile three competing knowledge ontologies; recommended: RQ-002 as the frame.
5. **D-3:** amend the frozen context map through the existing procedure.
6. **D-2:** mint CAP-001's missing authorization with disclosure, plus a forward rule.
7. **D-4:** ratify the proposed standards, the Reference Architecture and the Metamodel.
8. **D-7:** issue the canonical list of governed registers.
9. **D-6:** state that role separation is simulated by fresh sessions.
10. **D-10:** admit "model amnesia prevention" as a requirement candidate.
11. **New:** a "Platform Architecture" workstream, packaged but **deferred**.

## 11. [I] Interpretation (kept apart)

- [I-1] The docket is a concrete **instance** of the decision-readiness the corpus names in S0017: evidence graded, options separated, unknowns carried, the decision left to a named authority. It is one docket, generated by the platform about the platform; it does not show that "77 rulings arrived shaped".
- [I-2] **Presupposing evidence:** an item counts as evidence for a decision only if its meaning does not depend on the answer to that decision. The docket applies this to itself (L34). This is a general rule of decision preparation, stated here once.
- [I-3] **The self-check (L103):** CAP-001 verifying the ruling that authorizes CAP-001 is circular **for verification**, not **for authority**. The authority comes from the DA's act (Ch. 1 §1.6). The capability can check that the ruling is well-formed; it cannot be the reason the ruling is valid.
- [I-4] **INCONCLUSIVE as the trace of a missing act (L127–130):** when the governing list does not exist, the only operating capability answers "no criterion exists". The absence of an authority act appears as an *undetermined* result, not as a *negative* one. This is the three-valued reading of Ch. 1 ("cannot determine").
- [I-5] **"One failure authorizes a question, not a service layer"** (L168) is an evidence bar: the strength of evidence limits the size of the change it can justify.
