## 0. In business language

A **deliberate attempt to break** a candidate model, the "Engineering Knowledge Domain Model" (D-1…D-7). Its proposed domains are governance, method, runtime, workflow, capability, evidence and PKS. The tests run against real artifacts, not by reasoning. Result: **the model does not survive intact.**

**Broken or weakened:**
- **Method (D-2)** claimed its own decision series and independent evolution.
  - The decision series turns out to sit under a **shared authority** (sponsor + ARB).
  - The whole series was **written in one day**: separate in subject, not in time.
- **Runtime (D-3)** claimed a separate vocabulary protected by a translation layer.
  - There is **no artifact** holding that vocabulary, only one citing source.
  - The translation (deny/ask/allow) is practically the **identity**, and "an Anti-Corruption Layer that performs an identity mapping is not an ACL. It is a restatement."
- **"PKS is a projection" is falsified.** Existing PKS documents are commissioned, reviewed, scored 9.9/10 and accepted, which is the lifecycle of an *authoritative* artifact. "You do not endorse a projection at 9.9/10. You regenerate it."
- **The "context type" (D-7)** was inferred from one instance. "A type that has never been instantiated is a description, not a discovered construct."

**Survived:**
- **Governance (D-1)** survived every attack.
- **Method** survives on a better ground: an explicit **mutual scope exclusion** ("Governance discoveries … MUST NOT appear here"). "A shared authority that forbids itself from mixing two subjects is evidence FOR two models".
- The rejection of **Workflow** as a domain stands: it has the same owner as governance.

**Principle:** **falsified is worse than unevidenced.**

**Unanswerable from the record:** *why* the sponsor separated method governance. "The artifacts record the act, never the reason."

**Verdict:** revise and re-test; **not canonical**.

## 11. [I] Interpretation (kept apart)

- **[I-1]** U-1, "the artifacts record the act, never the reason", is direct corpus evidence on the book's **reserved decision 4** (what "reasoned" means in I-2). In practice, acts are recorded without reasons. Under reading (a), reasons as a validity condition, such acts would have no standing. The evidence does not decide the reading. It shows the practical cost of each reading.
- **[I-2]** Three corpus tests for a real boundary emerge:
  1. it is **instantiated** in an artifact or tool, not only asserted in prose;
  2. any translation across it is **non-trivial**;
  3. it is **maintained by an explicit rule** (scope exclusion), not by filing accident.
- **[I-3]** The projection test is a **lifecycle test**. Whether an artifact is derived or authoritative is decided by what happens to it (regenerated vs accepted), not by its label. This matches the book's "representations never gain authority" (§1.14.4): an artifact that was accepted by an act is not a projection, whatever it is called.
