## 0. In business language

A new commission asked for a full "strategic architecture discovery" of the platform: mission, vision, domains, bounded contexts, context map, lifecycle, knowledge flow, capability model, and the runtime, PKS and product boundaries.

The author **declined to redo the work**. The questions had already been answered and frozen, and twelve answers had been checked by a blind reviewer a day earlier. Repeating the discovery would itself be an instance of the problem KnowledgeOS exists to solve: "model amnesia", forgetting what the organisation already knows.

So the document is a **coverage map**. Each question points to its existing answer, with a grade: Confirmed, Candidate, Hypothesis or Open Question. Main points:
- The mission is enacted but not ratified.
- The vision is only a hypothesis.
- Runtime is rejected as a domain.
- The context map is deliberately incomplete.
- The knowledge-lifecycle loop as a whole is a hypothesis.

The commission's success test was "If PublicDigit disappeared, what of KnowledgeOS would survive?" It had already been **run as an experiment**:
- every element of the candidate kernel survived on its own;
- the **set** of elements failed to bootstrap a new product.

The lesson: **survival ≠ sufficiency**.

Only one genuinely new open question emerged (R-1): where does a **"Prompt"** belong in the capability model?

## 11. [I] Interpretation (kept apart)

- **[I-1]** The document applies the platform's thesis to itself. Refusing to redo frozen work is a **governance act about knowledge reuse**, and KnowledgeOS's purpose is stated as a cure for "model amnesia".
- **[I-2]** "evidence EARNS · governance GRANTS · promotion requires BOTH" is a **two-key promotion rule**: evidence is necessary, not sufficient, and authority is a separate key. This matches E-S0001's "ownership is conferred by granting". Recorded as consistent, not as derived.
- **[I-3]** "survival ≠ sufficiency" is a **compositional** finding: elementwise portability does not imply that the set is portable.
