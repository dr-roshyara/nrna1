## 0. In business language

An outside research input (a Perplexity research pass, plus a reviewer's summary of it) was checked against KnowledgeOS's existing knowledge, using a fixed five-question review protocol.

**What the outside research shows:**
- Industry already governs which knowledge an AI agent may retrieve: permission checks at the retrieval step, and provenance as a control mechanism.
- "Knowledge Operating System" is not an established market category.
- Three KnowledgeOS ideas have no outside equivalent and should be protected rather than renamed: Method / Binding / Evidence, the P4 lifecycle, and the Portability Ladder.

**The main strategic result is a clearer relationship.** KnowledgeOS **governs** retrieval technology (RAG, knowledge graphs, vector search) and does not compete with it: KnowledgeOS sets the policy, and retrieval engines enforce it.

**One gap was raised: provenance** (where knowledge came from). The check showed provenance already exists in the corpus, spread out. Notably, authority itself is defined as *provenance × standing*. So provenance is not made a new domain; it enriches an existing research stream.

**Nothing is adopted:**
- the outside implementation material (schemas, events, pipelines) is refused for this phase;
- a proposed positioning sentence and five principles go to the decision-makers.
