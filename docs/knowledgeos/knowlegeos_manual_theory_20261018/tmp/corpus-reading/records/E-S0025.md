## 0. In business language

The question: can the **Minimum Viable Kit** (MVK: the 15 documents that a "Product Boundary Discovery" named as the reusable core of KnowledgeOS) set up a **second product** on its own? The test: a fresh, isolated session with read access to exactly those 15 files, asked to start a small, unrelated product ("StockRoom", a hardware shop's inventory), and to **record every time it needed something outside the 15 files** instead of fetching it.

- **The kit produced a lot:** a capability, one bounded context (three others rightly rejected), a guarded vocabulary, three invariants, a decision record, a test boundary, a capability on the CAP-001 template, and five rejected design candidates. The tactical DDD principles did real discriminating work in a domain they were not written for.
- **But the verdict is FAIL:** 14 needs fell outside the kit, 3 of them contamination. Three failures are structural:
  1. the execution protocol cannot be satisfied by one person at the start (it forbids one party being engineer and reviewer, forbids skipping, forbids implementing before approval);
  2. there is **no path from nothing to the first architecture decision** ("genesis");
  3. the strategic-DDD method was missing (later corrected: it exists, but is coupled to PublicDigit).
- **The most important line:** the **sequence** of the bootstrap came from the experiment brief, not from the kit. So the result flatters the kit; "The true state is worse than FAIL suggests".
- **Meta-finding:** "A platform that governs CHANGE does not automatically govern GENESIS — and the gap is invisible from inside a mature project, because genesis happened before the governance existed." It could only be found by attempting a genesis.
- **Lessons:** "Domain-free is not the same as portable": documents without election words can still be one project's case history. Two "closed" verdict vocabularies collide. A deferred rule leaves a silence. The restraint rules transferred best.
- **Proposals:** seven additions, each to an **existing** home (no new standard), put to the Authority **as candidates**, because n=1 admits only a candidate. Re-run the experiment as a repeatable instrument.

## 11. [I] Interpretation (kept apart)

- [I-1] **The experiment is the corpus's first empirical test of a reusability claim**, as opposed to S0022's conceptual projection. It measures sufficiency by the **reach register**: every need outside the boundary is evidence of insufficiency. The method also shows its own main hazard: the brief can supply what the test claims to measure (reach #13).
- [I-2] **Genesis was found by attempting a genesis.** This parallels S0022's masking finding: some failures are invisible from inside the one product that was used to build the model.
- [I-3] **F-1 and the book's process independence.** RC-30 says the book's independence is process independence unless a different person is evidenced. EEP §2 speaks of **parties**. If "party" means role-holder in a session, fresh sessions satisfy it (with the limits D-6 asks to state). If "party" means person, a solo team cannot proceed lawfully. Proposed addition C (role scaling: "depth scales… the sequence does not", L141) would settle this; it is a candidate only.
- [I-4] **A deferred rule is silence.** "a deferred rule cannot be cited as authority" (L74) places a deferred rule on the same footing as no rule. In Ch. 2 §2.8.3's terms, a deferred rule yields **silence**, not a ruling.
- [I-5] **Two closed vocabularies with no scope** are a **genuine** conflict in Ch. 2's sense: both bind, they overlap, and no scope, specificity or supersession separates them.
