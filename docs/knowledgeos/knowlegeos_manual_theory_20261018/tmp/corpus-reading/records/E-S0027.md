## The theory found

1. **An evidence entry separates epistemic kinds.** Five fixed sections: observation, provenance, claims affected (and not affected), interpretation, and promotion decision, which is always left to the Decision Authority (L113–121). The reason given: "an entry mixes three epistemic kinds unless the sections are fixed" (Q8).
2. **Two streams.** Stream A records what happened, per event, append-only. Stream B holds learning candidates (recurring patterns), "generated PERIODICALLY from Stream A · reviewed by the DA · NEVER written directly" (Q12). "Patterns are extracted FROM entries by a distinct act, after several exist — never written INTO them." (Q9)
3. **Counting an edge of the model.** The edge "guides product engineering" (R-6) reaches n=3 records: planning, implementation and discovery (L34, L61, L99). But "the edge is evidenced, not measured": there is no unguided baseline (Q3).
4. **The counted class must be fixed in advance.** The n=3 count holds only because the edge's canonical wording is activity-level. "Had R-6 been implementation-scoped, the count would have been trimmed to n=1 — the check was real." (Q10)
5. **The back-edge (evidence → platform change)** is formally traversed for the first time at instrumentation level: "formal n=1, instrumentation-level" (Q5, L80, OE-KOS-3, 2026-08-04). The informal canon-level n≈3 "stands separately". Guides-edge and improvement-edge must not be conflated (Q4).
6. **Coverage of an obligation.** A safety net (the reminder hook) saw only some file areas, so an obligation (write the developer guide) was skipped where the net was blind. "reminder-coverage must track obligation-scope, or the obligation silently narrows to what the net can see" (Q7). The gap was found by the user, not the platform (Q6).
7. **Promotion needs independent recurrence.** Single occurrences are recorded, not promoted. A modelling heuristic (MO-2) waits for "recurrence across MULTIPLE bounded contexts" (Q11). A volume trigger (10–15 candidates) is a watch signal, not policy (L195–201). "entries are never created to fill the register" (Q14).
8. **A discovery that refuses to decide.** OE-KOS-4: the discovery found "a missing business decision, not a missing if-statement" (Q15) and wrote no plan.

## Contradictions and limits

- **Back-edge counts disagree.** The book (Ch. 1, from S0014, 08-03) has "≈3 informal, ≈2 formal". This register (08-04) calls OE-KOS-3 the **first** formal traversal. The two sources use different definitions of "formal" (here: deficiency → recorded evidence → platform-machinery change with records). Same problem as idea 4.
- **Selection.** All three R-6 records were chosen and written by the platform's own process, and judged by its reviewers. [I] Without a baseline or an independent recorder, the count cannot separate "the architecture guided the work" from "guided work was what got recorded".
- **"Activity-level" checked against the canonical wording** (L175): good practice, but the wording itself is in a document not read here.
