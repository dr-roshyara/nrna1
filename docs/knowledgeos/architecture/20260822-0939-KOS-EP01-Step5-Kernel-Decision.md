## 3 · Decision 2 — the two consolidation wordings resolved

### 2a · NonInterference → Dimension Law (law 2)

The OVERLAPPING ruling (P4 §3.9, deferred to P5) is resolved: H-KOS-NonInterference-001 is the **transition-form** of INV-KOS-001 (Dimension Independence). They render as **one law**. Final wording (frozen into law 2):

> **Dimension Law** — *Epistemic dimensions evolve independently. No value passes from one dimension to another without an explicit, governed transition; no dimension collapses into another (no single-score knowledge).*

Rows rendered: **INV-KOS-001 · H-KOS-NonInterference-001.** The "no implicit transition" clause is NonInterference's content, now stated inside the law — no separate row, no lost content.

### 2b · Context-001 → Context-002 (law 1's context clause)

H-KOS-Context-001 (semantic frame preservation) and H-KOS-Context-002 (Avacchedaka — delimiting conditions) are the same constitutional content: **meaning requires its boundary.** They render as **one clause of the Identity Law**. Final wording (frozen into law 1):

> **Context clause of the Identity Law** — *Context is part of identity: a knowledge object carries its delimiting conditions (its semantic frame) and is never valid beyond them. A claim without its frame is incomplete.*

Rows rendered: **H-KOS-Context-001 · H-KOS-Context-002.** The delimiting-conditions **tuple** (Entity·Property·Context·Relation·Time·Authority) remains a REPRESENTATION technique, deferred — the *principle* is the law, the *tuple* is one way to express it.
