- R-30: V7 AMBIGUOUS — "this log becomes the only living rulings home" moves the authoritative location without a "supersedes" verb, so it may or may not be a supersession (the old proposals are kept); "§6 … closed" listed as not-modelled alongside the redirect (ANNOTATE).

- R-34: V1 AMBIGUOUS — it creates three new classification categories and a supersession; the stated purpose ("log must not grow faster than the software") may or may not count as demonstrated need / sufficient evidence. "Accepted as grandfathered" coded ACCEPT by verb, although the object is not completed work.
