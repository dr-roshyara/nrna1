# Coding notes

General conventions (apply to all records): V1 is coded UNTESTABLE ("does not apply") when no §C guard is engaged, or when a guard might be engaged but the record doesn't say whether it was satisfied (F1). V6 is coded on the text rather than PRE-R43, because the records are numbered X1–X8, not R-numbers. V6 is coded VIOLATED when no deciding authority is named: V6 tests exactly what the text says, so "silence is not evidence" doesn't turn it into UNTESTABLE.

- X1: V1 is hard. APPROVE/CONFIRM have no guard, but "No-as-health-indicator added" may be new vocabulary (the demonstrated-need bar), and the record doesn't say whether that need was shown. So V1 is UNTESTABLE. "ARB reconfirmed: stop extending" is coded CONFIRM (no change) and not as a new norm.
- X2: The word "supersede" appears only inside the content of Policy 1. The record performs no supersession, so V7 is UNTESTABLE, although a strictly literal reading of F6 would make V7 apply. "Consequence accepted" isn't completed work, so it goes under not_modelled rather than ACCEPT. V2 is SUPPORTED because the gate is recorded as satisfied but Tactical DDD isn't started. The new invariants and vocabulary may trigger the demonstrated-need bar, which isn't stated, so V1 is UNTESTABLE.
- X3: "VODP adopted as permanent governance" isn't stated as PREPARED, so it is ADOPT-DECISION (it could also be read as CREATE-NORM). "Special standing recorded" is kept under not_modelled (verb "recorded") and not treated as a change made by CONFIRM. If it counted as a CONFIRM change, V3 would be VIOLATED. The ARB is named only as the future route for a reversal and not as the decider, so V6 is VIOLATED.
- X4: The headline is the whole record. M3 is treated as the successor slice to M2, and M2 is accepted in the same record, so V1 is SUPPORTED. That depends on M3 counting as a "successor slice".
- X5: V1 is AMBIGUOUS. The refusal to promote cites Phase 16 and the parked criterion, but §C's RAISE bar (≥2 instances) is met by the stated two slices. On one reading, the refusal concerns an act §C permits (VIOLATED). On the other, it concerns promotion without the DA's authorization, which is outside the evidence bar. "In force / binding" is the PA's content, so it is coded as ADOPT-DECISION and not as a separate CREATE-NORM.
- X6: "Corrected" isn't coded as CORRECT-TEXT because the record doesn't say the correction is same-day or of its own text. "Frozen are dimensions…" is read as a state description, not a FREEZE act. V5 is AMBIGUOUS: it's unclear whether the corrected generalizations were earlier rulings, and the "evidence" is a thought-experiment (falsifying test) rather than materially new evidence. V4 is SUPPORTED because the record attributes the correction to the ARB and not to evidence alone.
- X7: The adopter isn't named ("operationally adopted"), so V6 is VIOLATED. The closed set of four outcomes and the protocol may be new categories or a methodology extension. The record denies only that the clarification is a new category, and demonstrated need isn't stated, so V1 is UNTESTABLE. "WP-6's own record opened" is an outcome record and not a work package, so it goes under not_modelled rather than OPEN-WORK.
- X8: "Authority formulation" names a role label and not a specific body, so authority_named is true but V6 is AMBIGUOUS. V5 is AMBIGUOUS: it's unclear whether the withdrawn statement was an earlier ruling, and whether the 19/21 re-certification counts as materially new evidence. V1 is SUPPORTED on the reading that the cited re-certification amounts to "sufficient evidence" for SUPERSEDE. That call is contestable.
