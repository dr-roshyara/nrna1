## Defects (all mine unless noted; disclosed; outputs kept)
1. **STRICT is unreachable by construction (formal reasoning).** v1_e, v1_x, v1_h and v1_c are unknown on every START event (v1_h and v1_c are each known on one record only), and STRICT requires all other fields to be known. "No strict witness" in r1–r3 therefore **carries no evidential weight**. The reviewer: 4 disjoint target pairs would otherwise exist.
2. **The pipeline removed the negatives (coding / evidence scope):**
   - the R06/R07 merge deleted the only same-operation ACT negative (PREPARE-ISSUE [1,1] pre-merge);
   - the 1ay-n rule of coding not-occurred operations as UNMAPPABLE (R29, R31, R22) conflates polarity with operation, which the instrument carries separately;
   - A′ R-A2 / R-A3 plus coder choices turned many v1 REFUSED outcomes into ACT_OCCURRED of other operations.

   **"Act-level guards non-identifiable" is therefore valid only *under this coding and pipeline*, not "in the corpus".**
3. Unparseable 1ax corrections were applied to junk keys and listed as applied (r1).
4. The mapping silently overwrote 1ax corrections (R04 about_actor).
5. Composite targets were not expanded: 2 of the 14 POSSIBLE are artifacts.
6. **Duplicate or level-mismatched fields remain:** v1_a (vs actor / stated_by), v1_r (vs operation), v1_k (derived from target), v1_s in ACT (possibly conditioned on the outcome).
7. **r3's fixes were verdict-proximal** (specified after the sealed expectation failed, from a diagnostic that printed the blockers). **r3 is EXPLORATORY.**

## What the chain establishes (descriptive, same-family)
- Under A′ coding and normalization, ACT is **23+ / 1−**: act-level contrasts **cannot be tested on this coded dataset**.
- NORM START-EXECUTION is **5+ / 5−**, with up to **12 genuine** same-operation, same-actor, opposite-polarity pairs that differ in target. They are confounded with time / the authorizing ruling, and 2 are within a single ruling.
- **The same target flips polarity across successive rulings with all coded fields equal** (R31 → R32; R33 → R34). **Target alone does not index polarity; an intervening norm-making act does.**

## Methodological conclusion (INFERENCE; the reviewer concurs on the substance)
- **The inherited strict single-variable pair-witness method (v1 R3, carried into 1ay) is inadequate for this corpus.** Structural unknowns, multi-field confounding and very few negatives make it unable to deliver evidence either way.
- **The informative structure is dynamic:** norms change **by acts** (a ruling that authorizes a previously unauthorized target; an adoption that brings prepared rulings into force). The discriminating signal is *"polarity changed by an intervening act"*, a **transition**, not a static variable.
- **Next (1az):** the identifiability analysis on **norm dynamics**: sequences of (norm state, norm-changing act, new norm state) per target. H-D and H-B are compared on what they predict about those sequences.
