# Six gaps in SCHEMA-r2 (from a reliability study; coder identities removed)

Two careful coders, applying SCHEMA-r2 to the same 36 records, disagreed. In most disagreements the schema admits **both** answers. The gaps:

- **G1 R-A3 scope:** does a register/matrix row's own classify / recommend count as a performed (norm-making) act? (R15–R21; R27 "applies")
- **G2 Level of the governed act** (§2 `about_operation`): is the norm about the meta-act (e.g. *promote*: ELIGIBLE / INELIGIBLE) or about the substantive behaviour (REQUIRED / STATUS_CONSTRAINT)? (R15–R21)
- **G3 Priority, ACT_REFUSED vs ACT_OCCURRED:** when a span both declines something (R-A2) and itself performs a register act (R-A3). (R01, R26; also R29, an unchosen option)
- **G4 PREPARED / unadopted rulings and ACT_ATTEMPTED:** undefined. (R05)
- **G5 REQUIRED vs STATUS_CONSTRAINT:** undefined boundary. (R10, R23)
- **G6 Shared spans and R-A3's second sentence:** where a norm sits when two records share one span (R06/R07), and when the stated content of a performing row is "additionally a NORM" (R02, R30).
