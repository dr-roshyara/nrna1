# The scoping questions (human decision record, verbatim intent)
**Goal:** does an independent record exist that could establish **which norm (ruling / authorization) was in force at a given time**? "Independent" means maintained by someone or something other than (a) the norm-maker, (b) the executor of the work, and (c) the ruling that asserts its own effectiveness.

For each candidate channel, answer only:
1. Does it exist?
2. What does it record?
3. Who or what controls it?
4. Are its timestamps independently generated?
5. Can it establish the norm in force at time t?
6. How complete is it?
7. How independent is it?
8. Can it be reproduced and independently verified?

**Rules:**
- Existence or scoping only; **no broad archaeology**; do not acquire the underlying evidence yet.
- If independence cannot be established: **INDEPENDENCE_NOT_DETERMINABLE**. **Never a forced negative.**
- Do not classify a candidate channel's own writers as executors by construction (a known earlier defect).
- A self-attested adoption record is **not** independent merely because it has a timestamp.
- **The research programme's own artefacts (under docs/knowledgeos/) are never candidate channels** (self-contamination).
