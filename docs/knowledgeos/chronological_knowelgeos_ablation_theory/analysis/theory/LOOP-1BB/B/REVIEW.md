# REVIEW of SPEC.md / SPEC.json (adversarial)

Inputs used: TASK.md, HYPOTHESES.md, IDENTIFIABILITY.json, CONFORMANCE-CASE.json, STATE.md, SPEC.md, SPEC.json. Nothing else.

## Verdict: **PASS_WITH_LIMITATIONS**

The spec does most of what TASK.md asks, and does it carefully:
- It keeps to the scope IDENTIFIABILITY.json allows: it drops P3 and P6 and states that P2, P4 and P5 are one-sided.
- It removes the draft's unexplained "≥3" and "2 pairs" minimums and gives a reason for each replacement.
- It replaces the "complete act log including unregistered ones" with a closed channel.
- It adds windows, and it hedges the feasibility section.

It does not reach PASS. Its headline deliverable, the "single smallest discriminating acquisition", is defective as stated:
- One of its three outcome branches does not separate the hypotheses (D1).
- The three items are not enough to trigger the falsifiers the spec itself defines (D2).
- It leaves out the outcome where no WP-4C-1 work exists (D2).
- Its claim to be the only small two-sided acquisition contradicts the spec's own P5 entry (D3).

The closed-world register is also under-defined (D5), and the P7 window starts at a recording time (D4).

All of these can be fixed without redoing the per-prediction work, so the verdict is not FAIL. **The ranking and the smallest-acquisition section should not be used until D1–D5 are fixed.**

## Answers to the eight attacks (short)
1. **Discrimination:** P3 and P6 are dropped correctly. Including P4 and P5 as one-sided tests is defensible: force without adoption is a sub-case of IDENTIFIABILITY's type (i). But the *combinations* the spec relies on do not discriminate (D1), P7-HD cannot fire in its only episode (D12), and P8-HD repeats P1.
2. **Sufficiency and minimality:** fields are missing (D8), and the norm in force is not established independently for P7 and P5. A few fields are unnecessary (D14; R-87 in C1 only because of D6).
3. **Attestation:** self-reported material remains (JSON `self_report_residue`), and some windows start at recording times (D4).
4. **Episode minimums:** every "1" is justified. The P1 census and the P8 tendency census are under-specified rather than arbitrary (D10).
5. **Falsifiers:** most have windows. The closed-world register is not well defined (D5). Several falsifiers need an absence of *work*, and there is no closed world for work.
6. **Feasibility:** the feasibility section is hedged, but the attestation fields make existence claims without evidence (D11).
7. **Contested case and status mediation:** C1 will probably come out UNDETERMINED as written (D9). The status-mediation upgrade is only partial (D13).
8. **Ranking:** the claimed advantage of the smallest acquisition rests on D1–D3. It becomes HIGH only on the condition that WP-4C-1 work exists.
