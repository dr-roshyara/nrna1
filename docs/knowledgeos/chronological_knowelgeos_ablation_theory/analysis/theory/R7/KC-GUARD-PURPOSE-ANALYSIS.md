## 3. Hypothesis and refutation attempts

### 3.1 Hypothesis (as tested in r1)

**H1′.** Across the source texts, D1 is stated uniformly as "reads both sides". On that definition, the option groups differ only in D3 at the guard rows (K1), so "purpose" adds no independent decision variable.

**The evidential weight of the D1 texts is limited, and is weighed symmetrically with Q2 (K1 in §9):**
- Q1 (D1) and Q2 (the K1 (a) guard clause) are **one unratified [E] sentence pair** at T3:66. Neither has more standing than the other.
- T3 is not adopted. Its crossing table is "not adopted" (E2b:496).
- E3:115 is an [O] bullet citing T3.
- Q4 and Q5 are truth disclaimers. They do not exclude a crossing that tracks what a clause enforces.

So D1 is **uniform across the unratified source texts**, not settled. **Ratifying or revising it remains open to KC-1 (O2).**

### 3.2 7c read two ways

- **7c-purpose:** a crossing measures whether a constraint couples the sides. Under this reading D1 changes from "reads" to "couples".
  - It departs from the uniform source wording (Q1, Q4–Q6).
  - It is close to the "held" column of T3's relations table (Q7, Q49, Q50). That column lists the crossing constraint rows, contains no guard row, and cites R15 only under "not held" (Q8). Q6 keeps that table apart from crossings, but the table is itself unadopted (Q55).
  - As MKC:172 notes, applied to every row it fails under every principle, because typing and definition rows cross through what they type. It is therefore coherent only as a statement about guard rows.
  - **Result:** 7c-purpose is a **revision** of the unratified D1 text. It is open to KC-1 as O2; it is not ruled out by the sources.
- **7c-count:** keep D1, and let guard rows read only what they assert. This is K1 (b) / (c), i.e. C's definition (Q42) at the guard rows, up to the parse. It is consistent with D1.

### 3.3 D2 and D1

**D2 does not by itself determine D1. It selects the rows to which a K1 rule applies, under every option** (K2 in §9).
- **T3:** Q2 applies to "T-A guard form" rows, i.e. T2's tier.
- **K:** takes the guard role from the T2 label (Q52).
- **K-T:** takes it from the tier (Q53).
- **M and C:** apply one rule to all rows, but the KC inventory and the matrix evaluate their effect on exactly this row set.

**Orthogonality [E], conditional on K1 (a)** (K3 in §9). Under T3's verdicts, the four D1 × D2 cells are all occupied:
- constraint and crossing: R2, R3, R9, R22;
- constraint and internal: R1, R18;
- non-constraint and crossing: R14, R15, R16, R21;
- non-constraint and internal: R11, R17.

**But the "non-constraint ∧ crosses" cell is K1 (a)'s output.** It is not independent evidence that D2 and D1 are orthogonal; using it so would be circular. Under C with K1c that cell empties (apart from R15 under the R15ref parses; MKC §5.3). So whether D1 and D2 are orthogonal **at the guard rows is exactly the K1 question.** The ratified lists (Q32), and the F-LOG-0297 acceptance built on them, are **status evidence** for K1 (a) and are listed under O1 (§5.2). They are not a reason to choose by 13 / 8.

## 4. Model-integrity test

**Scope note (K6 in §9).** The test (orthogonal, necessary, sufficient) is meant for **model attributes**. "Purpose" is a **decision rationale**, not a model attribute. The test is applied here only to ask whether it is a separate **decision variable**.

| candidate | new / overloaded / value? | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **D1** what a crossing measures | existing (the K-c criterion, Q1); uniform but unratified | open to ratification or revision (O2) |
| **D2** constraint status | existing (T2 tier) | **selects the K1 row domain under every option**; does not by itself determine D1 |
| **D3** what a row reads | existing (K1–K13) | **K1 is the option-level coordinate** |
| **"guard-row purpose"** | not a new dimension: **redundant with K1, given D1 as written (O2 not taken)** | given D1, its value is fixed by the K1 value (R-d). It adds no decision variable. It remains a legitimate **rationale** for choosing K1 |

r0's "not sufficient" argument is dropped, because the same argument applies to D3 (C needs a parse). F4 rests on redundancy with K1 alone.

## 5. What the sources bear on, and what remains a human choice

- **B1.** D1 is uniform across the unratified texts: "reads", with truth disclaimers (Q1, Q4–Q6, Q24, Q26). No text defines a crossing as coupling. The counts are, however, **used** as a coupling proxy (Q37, Q39, Q40).

- **B3.** T2 states no D1 or D3 rule. It enters K-c only as the row selector.

### 5.2 Open: human choices

**O1. K1 at R11, R14–R17 and R21** (the option-level decision). The evidence:
- **For (a):**
  - rule 1's guard clause (Q2), unratified, and the same sentence pair as D1;
  - the ratified host lists (Q32) and the F-LOG-0297 acceptance of E3-REL, as **status evidence** (outputs of K1 (a));
  - the pairing column (Q19), which names the denied formula.
  - **Caveat:** none of these may be chosen *because* it preserves 13 / 8. Source of this caveat: the human's instruction of 2026-10-15, pasted advisory text that the human directed be followed: "do not choose hosting simply because it preserves the existing 13/8 result, or coupling simply because it produces a different result". **It is not a logged act.** F-LOG-0301 offers output preservation as a candidate criterion (Q64).
  - **The reads-based presupposition** of the VP11 / E2b text (guard rows "that cross groupings", Q33, Q60) fits (a)'s crossing verdicts. It is listed here as weak context, not a ruling.
- **For (b) / (c):**
  - T3's general denial handling (Q3; rule 5; A1), a consistency argument;
  - the coupling-proxy rationale (R-b), which rests on E-5; and VP11 / E2b's **deferral** of the guard rows' label, read as 7c semantics only under [E] E-4.

- **Neither:** T2's text (it states no reading rule); P9.
- **Verdict:** the sources do not settle O1. The conflict between the two sides is real.

**O2. Ratify D1 as written, or revise it** (e.g. to coupling, 7c-purpose). This would be a revision of "the K-c criterion" (E2b §A2.7, Q35), and it would change the stated meaning of E3-REL (Q26) and of T3 §1a (d) and (g).
