# 1. The discriminating criterion, derived first

`P-07`/`P-08` reasoned with *"same thing, changed state"*. ⭐ That formulation **presupposes** the thing
survives the transition — i.e. that the successor relation is the **identity relation**.

Let `≺` be *"is a successor of"* over identities.

$$\boxed{\begin{array}{ll}\textbf{item-level } (\tau 1..\tau 10) & \mathbf{\prec} = \mathrm{id} \quad\text{— the population of identities is INVARIANT; only fields change}\\[4pt] \boldsymbol{\tau 11} & \mathbf{\prec} \neq \mathrm{id} \quad\text{— the population of identities CHANGES}\end{array}}$$

⭐⭐ `[DERIVED]` **This is the exact characterization of what `P-08` omitted.** All ten members of
`𝒯_KOS` hold the identity population fixed. ⛔ **And it is a criterion, not a taxonomy** — the taxonomy
must now be derived, not assumed.
