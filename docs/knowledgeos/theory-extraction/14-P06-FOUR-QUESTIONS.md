## 0. ⭐ The central question first — is candidate identity DERIVED or ESTABLISHED?

`[EMP]` **The records show BOTH mechanisms, doing two different jobs.**

### Evidence that candidate identity is **derived from definitions**

| case | how the candidate distinction arose |
|---|---|
| **`𝒦 ≠ K`** | ⭐ **by containment** — `𝒦 = (K,H)` and `𝒦 = (K,C,T,E,A)` *contain* `K`. Recognised by inspecting the **definitions** |
| **`δ` vs `δ = 0.3099`** | from the definitions' **types** — function vs measured quantity |
| **`Zero` vs `0_i ∈ 𝒟_i`** | from the definitions' **shapes** — predicate vs algebraic identity element |
| **`Π`'s five** | from reading the **seven definitions** and applying dispositions |
| **`Σ` vs `Σ_Λ`** | from `𝔥_Λ`'s definition containing `Σ_Λ` |

$$\boxed{\textbf{6 of 6 candidate SPLITS were derived from definition analysis.}}$$

### Evidence that candidate identity is **established by naming, before or without definition**

| case | evidence |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐ **`ℐ`** | **named as `G-67`, never enumerated.** Four definitions exist and **0 of 7 candidates is established** — yet the whole estate treats `ℐ` as a thing, assigns it status, grounding, dependencies and a relation. **If candidate identity were derived from definitions, `ℐ` would not be a candidate at all** |
| ⭐ **`Qualify`** | **"named, typed, NO BODY"** — three lanes record it as a candidate *precisely because* its definition is absent |
| **`K`, `Zero`** | discussed under those names across hundreds of files **before** and independently of any particular definition |
| **the ratified 8** | `{Entity, State, Event, …}` declared as a **set of names** by act `C-022` — *"50 attack classes, no counterexample"* — candidate identity **stipulated, not derived** |

$$\boxed{\begin{array}{c}\textbf{NAMING establishes that a candidate EXISTS.}\\ \textbf{DEFINITION-ANALYSIS establishes DISTINCTIONS among candidates.}\\ \textbf{Two mechanisms, two jobs, both evidenced.}\end{array}}$$

`[EMP]` **Consequence: the `R-list` / `R-derivation` dichotomy is a false alternative as posed.** The
arrow is **derivational for distinctions and stipulative for existence.** Any model must support both,
and **neither reading alone is what the records show.**

## 5. ⭐ A structural observation: **D-with-promotion ≡ C**

If **Q-ii** is answered *"yes, at least for some candidates"*, D's only route is to let a candidate be
**promoted from a field value to an entity** when it acquires properties.

```
D + promotion:   glyph record ──► definitions ──► candidate field
                                                       │ promoted when it acquires properties
                                                       ▼
                                              a candidate ENTITY, cross-linked
```

$$\boxed{\textbf{That is Alternative C, arrived at incrementally rather than declared.}}$$

`[ARCH]` **Consequence:** the real choice may be **three-way, not four-way** — *(A restricted to
named candidates)* · *(B, accepting glyph-local candidates)* · *(C, whether reached directly or via
D+promotion)*. ⚠️ **Offered as an observation about the option space; it selects nothing, and it may be
wrong if promotion is refused.**
