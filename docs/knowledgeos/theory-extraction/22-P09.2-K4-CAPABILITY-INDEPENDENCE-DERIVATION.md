# 2. When are two obligations the SAME capability?

⛔ **Not by name. Not by implementation. Not because one mechanism could serve both.**

$$\boxed{\begin{array}{c}\textbf{Two obligations are ONE capability iff they share the same SEMANTIC RETENTION RULE —}\\ \textbf{all ten dimensions of §6 — and the parts CANNOT be independently varied.}\\[6pt] \boxed{\begin{array}{l}\textbf{DECOMPOSITION is justified iff each part can vary while the others are held fixed.}\\ \textbf{UNIFICATION is justified iff ONE rule preserves every required distinction WITHOUT hidden special cases.}\end{array}}\end{array}}$$

⚠️ **Two asymmetries, both binding:** a **common implementation is not** proof of semantic unity, and
**different implementations are not** proof of semantic separation. ⛔ **And minimality is over
DISTINCTIONS, never over capabilities, tables, records or entities** (§20 of the commission).
