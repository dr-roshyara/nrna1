## The rules that govern this stage

**Every statement carries three things**, as one compact line:

```
[<record> §"<anchor>"] · <EVIDENCE | DERIVED-PROPOSAL> · <status vector>
```

**A section may be short. It may never be padded.** Length is not a criterion for anything.

**An absence is stated, never silent** — as `NOT-EVIDENCED-IN-CAPTURE` or
`GENUINELY-UNDEFINED-AFTER-CENSUS`. *Reporting what is half-built as half-built and what is not
found as not found is the whole point of this stage.*

**This stage writes; it does not decide.** A question the earlier stages could not settle is written
as an open question, not resolved by good prose.

## The gate

Whatever this stage produces is **`PENDING GOVERNANCE REVIEW`** — never `FINAL`. The output is a
**recommendation**. This root does not ratify its own work.

## What is deliberately absent here

No JSONL records and no script. This stage is narrative by nature.
