## 1. The blocking open questions, in dependency order

### `OQ-1` — **The carrier.** `[OPEN]` · **blocks everything tactical**

KnowledgeOS has no declared mathematical carrier. Every result was produced on
$r = (\text{value},\text{source},\text{tag},\text{time})$ with $|D| \le 6$.

**What would close it:** a governance decision, informed by — not derived from — the
experiments. **No experiment can decide this**, and running more of them on the synthetic
carrier will not bring it closer.

**Why it blocks:** document 12 cannot complete a tactical model without it, and every `[EXP]`
in the set is scope-bound to a carrier that is admittedly not the real one.

### `OQ-2` — **`DECISION-02`: is $\varphi$ a semantic evaluation frame or an evidence partition?** `[OPEN]` · **blocks the composition rule and the $\delta$ transition**

**What would close it:** an adjudication. The experiments *constrain* it (documents 08) and
cannot decide it. It also decides which bounded context owns $\varphi$ (document 12 §2).

### `OQ-3` — **The transfer problem.** `[OPEN]`

Do the three separations survive on documents, claims, entities, relations, provenance,
contradictions, temporal states and evidence?

**What would close it:** one experiment on a non-synthetic carrier reproducing *any one* of the
separations. **Not attempted.** This is the largest scientific risk in the programme: every
result could be an artifact of small numeric multisets.

## 2. The open questions that experiments *can* close

### `OQ-4` — Adequacy vs Realization, **actually exhibited** `[OPEN]`

Never achieved (document 05 §3). Requires a level with
$\hat H(Q\mid R) = 0$ **and** $F < 1$ — a decoder deliberately weaker than the information, or a
constraint that bites at an adequate level. **Neither is hard; neither was done.**

`[REC]` **This is the highest-value experiment available**, because it is the one separation the
programme claims and has never shown.

### `OQ-5` — The 67 dead cells `[OPEN]`

Adequacy saturates for most $(T,Q)$ pairs. **Requires transformations that *partially* preserve
each $Q$** — a design change, not a sampling change. Nine generator regimes are the evidence that
sampling cannot reach them.

### `OQ-6` — Does the eliminability ladder reach $k > 3$? `[OPEN]`

$k = 3$ demonstrated for $n \le 6$; $n \le 6$ is a **design bound**, not a property of
eliminability.

### `OQ-7` — A valid interval for $\hat H$ `[OPEN]`

The bootstrap is `[DEFECT]` here. A valid interval requires resampling the **fiber structure**,
not the case list. Not attempted.

## 3. The two standing conjectures

`[CONJ]` **No `Zero`/preservation bridge exists for read-disjoint $(\Pi, Q)$.**
**Refutable by:** one substantive, replicating, gate-clean cell — $|MH\text{-}RD| \ge 0.01$ on
both splits, gate passed, at least one stratum CI excluding zero.

`[CONJ]` **Realization ⟹ Adequacy.**
**Refutable by:** a case set where the fixed decoder is right throughout while the fiber is
$Q$-heterogeneous — a decoder right by luck.

Both are stated with refutation conditions attached so they can be attacked. **Neither may be
cited as established.**

audit, bridge. **In none did it produce a kernel primitive**, and in each it produced a question
already answerable by experiment.

> **The lens has been consistently useful for framing and consistently empty of machinery. It
> has never once been the source of a result, and after five cycles that is evidence, not an
> accident.**

`[REC]` **Keep the strand, keep it external, and stop testing it for kernel candidacy.** Its
role is *naming what an experiment already earned*, and naming is enough.
