### Experimental Protocol Specification: `KR-REP-REDUCTION`

$$\mathfrak R(Q,\Pi) = \left( D, \{R_n\}_{n=1}^5, \{T_n\}_{n=1}^5, O, Q, \Pi \right)$$

#### 1. Chain Dynamics & Invariants

* **Directional Reduction Chain:**

$$D \xrightarrow{T_5} R_5 \xrightarrow{T_4} R_4 \xrightarrow{T_3} R_3 \xrightarrow{T_2} R_2 \xrightarrow{T_1} R_1$$


* **Strict Non-Monotonicity:** Entropy ($H(R_n)$), storage size, and element count are recorded independently per level. Monotonicity is treated as an empirical hypothesis, never an architectural assumption.
* **Support Discipline:** Every level $n$ declares its valid empirical support $N_n = \{i \in N : R_{n,i} \neq \mathrm{NULL}\}$ and completeness ratio $\mathrm{Comp}_n = \frac{\vert{}N_n\vert{}}{\vert{}N\vert{}}$.

#### 2. Vector Measurement Metric ($M_n$)

For each representation level $n \in \{1, \dots, 5\}$ evaluated over support $N_n$:

$$M_n = \left( A_n, F_n, \hat H(Q \mid R_n), X_Q(R_n), \hat H(R_n), N_{\mathrm{viol},n} \right)$$

* **$A_n$ (Admissibility / Contract Adequacy):** Proportion of samples in $N_n$ satisfying structural/contract invariants under $\Pi$.
* **$F_n$ (Operator Fidelity):** Empirical accuracy of answer recovery:

$$F_n = \frac{1}{\vert{}N_n\vert{}} \sum_{i \in N_n} \mathbf{1}[O_n(R_{n,i}) = Q_i]$$


* **$\hat H(Q \mid R_n)$ (Residual Query Uncertainty):** Conditional entropy measuring sufficiency of $R_n$ for $Q$.
* **$X_Q(R_n) = \hat H(R_n \mid Q)$ ($Q$-Relative Residual Representation):** Conditional entropy measuring representation payload unconstrained by $Q$.
* **$\hat H(R_n)$ (Representation Entropy):** Empirical marginal entropy of the representation space.
* **$N_{\mathrm{viol},n}$ (Fiber Violation Count):** Exact pairwise conflict count across $R_n$-equivalence classes:

$$N_{\mathrm{viol},n} = \sum_{r} \frac{\left( \sum_q c_{r,q} \right)^2 - \sum_q c_{r,q}^2}{2}$$



#### 3. Empirical Preservation Boundary ($n^*$)

The minimal adequate level $n^*$ along the tested sequence $\{T_n\}$ is formally defined as:

$$n^* = \min \left\{ n \in \{1..5\} \;\middle\vert{}\; \mathrm{Comp}_n = 1.0 \;\land\; A_n = 1.0 \;\land\; N_{\mathrm{viol},n} = 0 \;\land\; \hat H(Q \mid R_n) = 0 \right\}$$

If level $n^*-1$ causes $A_{n^*-1} < 1.0$, $N_{\mathrm{viol},n^*-1} > 0$, or $\hat H(Q \mid R_{n^*-1}) > 0$, then $R_{n^*}$ is confirmed as the **chain-specific preservation boundary**.
