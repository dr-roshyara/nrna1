The attached writing still treats **Zero as something we can bring into the R⁵→R² experiment as a relatively direct predictor**.

$$

and the mechanism experiment has shown that **relationality may enter through either \(T\) or \(\Pi\)**. The important object is therefore not simply "Zero causes adequacy."

It is the **interaction structure underlying the transformation/preservation pair**.

$$
D
\xrightarrow{T}

\text{preserved inquiry}

with an underlying interaction structure:

\mathcal I_{T,\Pi}(D,S)

and **Zero is one observable/judgment arising from that structure**.

The attached document actually hints at this, but doesn't make it the organizing principle.
