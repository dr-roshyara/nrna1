# 2. R0 — regression governance

## Result: **FOUND**

There are actually **two different historical claims**, and they must not be confused.

### Earlier corpus finding

P-09 explicitly recorded:

> the corpus did not establish monotonicity; direction language was zero.

That remains `[EMP]` and was not overturned by later governance. 

### Later governance act

`INTAKE-001` records the R0 branch:

> MD-018 permits no regression — the status chain is monotone forward-only.

The later reconciliation explicitly says this is a **governance stipulation**, not a corpus-derived fact. 

Therefore the historical chain is:

$$
\boxed{
\text{Corpus: monotonicity NOT EVIDENCED}
}
$$

followed by:

$$
\boxed{
R0:\text{ monotonicity STIPULATED BY GOVERNANCE}
}
$$

and not:

$$
\text{Corpus}\Rightarrow\text{monotonicity}.
$$

### Important recovered consequence

The distinction between **correction** and **regression** was subsequently made explicit:

* correction = warrant identifies a defect in the prior record;
* regression = warrant identifies a change in the subject.

The latter is prohibited under R0. 

**Status:** `[QUALIFIED]`, with the important documentary distinction `[EMP]` vs `[DER-S]`.
