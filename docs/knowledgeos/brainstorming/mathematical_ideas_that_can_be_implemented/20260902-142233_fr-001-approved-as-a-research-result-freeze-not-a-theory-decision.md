### The strongest part

I think the most important sentence in the freeze is:

> **“What is the minimal joint structure required to represent family-level epistemic dependence?”**

That is the right residual question.

It is fundamentally different from:

> “What formula gives us N_eff?”

The experiments have already shown why the latter is dangerous. You have tested:

1. a fitted scalar relationship that failed outside its design space, and
2. a structurally motivated pairwise construction that failed to represent coupling.

Continuing to search for a third scalar formula would risk **formula hunting rather than theory discovery**.

### One wording I would tighten

The frozen finding says:

> “Pairwise semantic/evidential distinguishability cannot carry family-level epistemic complexity by itself.”

I approve this, but I would interpret **“by itself” as load-bearing**.

The experiment does **not** establish:

$$
\neg\exists F(\sim_\Lambda)
$$

in every conceivable mathematical construction.

It establishes something narrower and stronger experimentally:

$$
\text{pairwise distinguishability alone}
\;\not\Rightarrow\;
\text{adequate representation of family-level burden}
$$

under the tested constructions/regimes.

That distinction is already largely protected by your `[EXP]/[NEG]` statuses, so I would **not reopen the freeze** merely to change the wording.
