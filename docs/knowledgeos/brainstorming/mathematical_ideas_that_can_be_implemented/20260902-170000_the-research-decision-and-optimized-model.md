# Part B — The Decision Layer

*What evidence cannot settle. Six items have reached this register; two are closed.*

## B.1 `DECISION-01` — **`[DECIDED]`** · non-evidential invariance

> ## **Semantic evaluation must be invariant under transformations that leave the evidential content unchanged.**
> ```
>         E ∼_evidential E′   ⟹   Comp(E) = Comp(E′)
> ```

**`C6`** (permutation invariance) and **`C7`** (frame-refinement invariance) are ratified as
**instances**, not as free-standing criteria. Ratifying the principle first prevents two criteria of
the same kind being accepted or rejected on separate grounds.

> ### ⚠️ And it does NOT decide that `majority` violates it.
> **Whether a variation is *non-evidential* is not settled by the principle** — it is settled by what
> counts as evidential content, which is `DECISION-02`. **The `C7` objection to `majority` is
> contingent.** This is why the composition choice is *necessarily*, not merely prudently, deferred:
> **the ratified principle cannot yet be applied.**

## B.2 `DECISION-02` — **`[DECISION REQUIRED]`** · the semantic status of a frame

> **Does `φ` identify semantically meaningful evaluation frames, or does it merely organize evidence?**

| | Option A — representational partition | Option B — semantic context |
|---|---|---|
| commits to | chunking is an artefact of recording | **frame individuation is part of the evidential content** |
| `majority` under `C7` | **VIOLATES** — it makes **representation granularity semantically observable** | does **not** violate |
| what it owes | — | a semantics for how frames relate and how weight is set |

> **Option B does not license `majority`.** It removes the `C7` objection only.
> **`frame count ≠ evidential weight` unless explicitly decided** — otherwise "majority" is
> *count-of-representational-partitions*, not *weight-of-evidence*.

⚠️ **`φ = {time, context}` is NOT promoted to a semantic primitive.** The operative wording — *"which
features must agree for two opposed items to be genuinely opposed"* — is a **detection criterion, not
an ontology.** It does not say what a frame is *for*, and every experimental result so far is
compatible with all six candidate readings.

## C.1 The design principle — six times evidenced

> DEFINE THE STRUCTURE.  DERIVE THE PROJECTION.
> A projection may be named, used and optimized — it may never be primitive.
> ```

`Sat_c` for the evaluation situation · `U` for `𝓑` · `Gap` for `𝓑` · pairwise for the joint object ·

## C.2 The evaluation representation

```
        Evaluation  =  Status / Polarity   ×   Typed Boundary / Reason
```

`[EXP]`, **scoped** to the tested representation language and `ℛ_req`. **The surviving factorization —
not an adopted representation.**

**Two complementary witnesses:** `K3` is insufficient for **contradiction**; a two-channel `Standing`
is insufficient for **boundary**. Measured, over 13 required distinctions:

| | preserved | collapsed | not-representable |
|---|---|---|---|
| Classical | 1 | 1 | **11** |
| K3 | 3 | **10** | 0 |
| **FDE two-channel** | **11** | **2** | 0 |
| **Structured** | **13** | 0 | 0 |

**First factor — candidate:** `Standing(S⁺, S⁻)`, independent support channels. It takes K3's ten
collapses to two and preserves **every** contradiction distinction. Its two failures are **both**
boundary distinctions.

**Second factor — required:** a typed boundary. **Measured, not asserted:** 10 distinct values over
21 conditions, **not** an identity encoding, 66 pairs collapsed, **not adequate alone (11/13)** —
failing exactly `Satisfied`/`Unsatisfied`. **So the pair really is a pair**, with a clean division of
labour: **`reason` carries the whole boundary family; `value`/`polarity` carries only the determinate
split.**

**Safe wording, per review:** *an explicit reason/boundary component is indispensable **within the
tested representation language and candidate fields***.

> ⚠️ **Boundary ambiguity affects EVERY `Standing` class, not only `(0,0)`.** `NotAssessed`,
> `Underdetermined` and `TheoryIncomplete` all sit in `(1,0)` — evidence *is* present, merely
> unevaluated, insufficient, or without an evaluator. **A positive Standing is as boundary-ambiguous
> as an empty one.**

## C.3 The saturation law — readings

**The `Zero` readings factor through `π_set : 𝒮_t → 𝒫({T,F,U})`, discarding which requirement,
multiplicity, reason, and every value outside `{T,F,U}`.** A single unrelated `U` masks everything —
and with 5 of 8 `Sat` classes non-executable, **saturation is the normal regime.**

> ### A reading must be a function of `𝒮_t`.
> If computed from a projection `π`, then `π` must be **reading-complete**:
> ```
>      ρ factors through π   ⟺   ~_π refines ~_ρ
> ```
> **Decidable and testable.**

**Constructive form:** `ρ : 𝒮_t → (verdict, Witness)`. A saturated *verdict* becomes harmless because
the **witness still moves**. **One fix for two problems** — adding `𝓑` while leaving readings
existential would recover the information and discard it again downstream.

## C.4 Composition — what is fixed and what is not

**Fixed by evidence:**

- **`φ ⊇ {time, context}`** — 18/90, and **non-additive**: `{time}` 0/15, `{context}` 0/15.
- **`union` excluded** — ignores the frame, collapses frame-separated cases onto contradiction.
- **`strict` excluded** — destroys genuine conflict.
- **`last-wins` excluded twice** — discards an earlier genuine conflict (`⊭ C1`), and is
  **order-dependent**: `E₁ = E₂` as multisets yet `lastWins(E₁) ≠ lastWins(E₂)`. **The second is
  deeper — not a failed requirement but failed well-definedness.**
- **Supersession is subsumed by temporal separation.** A system with a temporal frame qualifier needs
  **no separate supersession rule for this purpose.**

**Not fixed — and must not be fixed by the implementation:**

> **The rule choice is `[DEFERRED]`.** Two survive; **the design space is NOT established to contain
> only two** — five were enumerated because five were thought of, and no exhaustiveness argument
> exists.

**The third shape worth holding open** — `[PROP]`, **not architecture:**

```
    cross-frame divergence  →  EXPLICITLY UNRESOLVED
    Evaluation = ( frame-relative standings , divergence )
    …a SEPARATE determination operation decides if a frame-free conclusion is justified
```

**Stop requiring the composition operator to manufacture a frame-free `Standing` at all.** Both known
costs come from forcing a two-bit frame-free answer out of a situation that has none.
