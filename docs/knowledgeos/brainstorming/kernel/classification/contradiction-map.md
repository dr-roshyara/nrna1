## 4 · Provenance finding — the attribution in `171853` is not supported by the original corpus

`20260825-171853` reframes C-15 and attributes the model to *"**your earlier** conceptual model"*:

```
Knowledge Space (potentially unbounded / evolving)  →  projection at time t
```

**Tested against all 13 original-region documents:**

| Concept | Occurrences across the 13 original documents |
|---|---|
| **"knowledge space"** | **0** |
| "projection" | present (`110950`:10, `114530`:13, `113645`:7) |
| "at time t" / "knowledge-at-time" | **1** (`113645`) |
| **"retract" / "withdraw"** | **heavy** — `114358`:32, `111647`:24, `113410`:14, `123619`:11, `112855`:10 |

**First substantial appearance of "Knowledge Space" anywhere in the corpus: `20260825-103542`** — two
days after the original region closed.

**Reading:** the original region contains a **strong retraction thread** and **no Knowledge Space
concept**. The Knowledge-Space-projection reading of C-15 is therefore a **cross-generation
hypothesis** — original retraction thinking combined with later research — and must be labelled as
such. It is **not** a recovery of something the original architecture already contained.

**Stated limit:** absence of the *phrase* is not proof of absence of the *idea*; the early documents
may express it in other vocabulary. But the phrase-level evidence is unambiguous (0 of 13), so the
burden of showing otherwise sits with the claim, not against it.
