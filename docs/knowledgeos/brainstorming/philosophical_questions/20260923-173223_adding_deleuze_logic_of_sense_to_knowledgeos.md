## Part I: What Is Deleuze's Technique, Precisely?

Strip away the literary style. Deleuze's *Logic of Sense* is a **formal method** — a way of constructing and analyzing structures. It has **six operators**:

### The Six Operators

1. **The Series Operator** — Every structure is a **pair of heterogeneous series** (signifying/signified) in perpetual disequilibrium. One series is always in excess; the other always in deficit.

2. **The Paradoxical Element** — Between the two series circulates a **paradoxical element** (the empty square, the aleatory point, the floating signifier) that belongs to neither series and to both. It is always displaced from its own place.

3. **The Singularity Operator** — Each series is constituted by **singular points** (turning points, inflection points, points of fusion). These are pre-individual, impersonal, a-conceptual.

4. **The Event Operator** — The **event** is the sense that is produced by the circulation of the paradoxical element through the series. It is incorporeal, impassive, and neutral — neither active nor passive, neither individual nor universal, neither present nor past.

5. **The Surface/Depth Operator** — There are two **topological registers**: the **surface** (where events are produced, where sense is deployed, where language is organized) and **depth** (where bodies mix, where noise dominates, where language collapses into affect). The surface is fragile; it can be reabsorbed into depth.

6. **The Double Causality Operator** — Events have a **double causality**: a **real cause** (the corporeal mixtures that produce them) and a **quasi-cause** (the paradoxical element that distributes them). The quasi-cause is immanent to its effect, and turns the product into something productive.

This is the technique. Everything else in *Logic of Sense* is application.
