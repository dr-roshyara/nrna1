## Part A — Research Architecture Note (the DDD boundary of this work)

This experiment lives in its own **Semantic Research context**. Its concepts are research concepts and **none of them is a KnowledgeOS domain concept**:

| Research concept | What it is here | What it is NOT |
|---|---|---|
| `Expression` | a surface string submitted for interpretation | not knowledge |
| `CandidateSet` | what a producer returns: candidate IR(s) + confidence + uncertainty + abstention | not an identity claim |
| `Mechanism` | a replaceable policy: `interpret(Expression, Context) → CandidateSet` | not a domain service |
| `Measurement` | observed behaviour of a producer against declared gold | not a verdict about meaning |
| `ConsensusObservation` | agreement/disagreement across producers | **not evidence of truth** |
| `Experiment` | configuration + lifecycle + reproducibility metadata | not a governance act |

The four-part separation from Phase 1 is unchanged and remains the spine of the apparatus: **A** surface normalization · **B** candidate generation (producers) · **C** equivalence *evaluation* (`d_SNF`) · **D** identity authority — **which lives in the KnowledgeOS kernel and is deliberately out of scope**. Neither B nor C may become D.

Layering: semantic rules live in the domain modules (`ir` · `distance` · `mechanisms` · `evaluator` · `metrics`); corpus loading, JSON, subprocess, seeding and reporting are infrastructure (`corpus_1000` assembly, `run-competition`). No producer sees gold, the evaluator, a score, or another producer's output — except SNF-E over its own **declared** members.

## Part C — The metric: `d_SNF v0.2` (a research pre-metric)

v0.1 is **retained and callable** as the A/B baseline; v0.2 is the P5 metric. The repair is a **category floor**, not a bigger weight: a status-category mismatch is a boundary, so the total may not fall below the floor of its most decisive slot — the same reasoning Tier 1 already applied to predicate/negation/entity.

| Floor | Value | Repairs |
|---|---|---|
| EXPRESSED vs NOT_EXPRESSED | 0.50 | D-2 |
| EXPRESSED vs UNKNOWN | 0.55 | D-3 |
| NOT_EXPRESSED vs UNKNOWN | 0.45 | **D-3 — the distinction the HPA named** |
| role present vs structurally absent | 0.50 | D-2 |
| AMBIGUOUS vs NOT_EXPRESSED / UNKNOWN | 0.45 | D-2/D-3 |

**The HPA's four-way amendment, measured.** Acceptance threshold τ = 0.40; every pair must exceed it:

| pair | v0.1 | v0.2 |
|---|---|---|
| EXPRESSED vs NOT_EXPRESSED | **0.330 ✗ collapsed** | **0.500 ✓** |
| EXPRESSED vs UNKNOWN | 0.412 ✓ | 0.550 ✓ |
| **NOT_EXPRESSED vs UNKNOWN** | **0.247 ✗ collapsed** | **0.450 ✓** |
| EXPRESSED vs NEGATED | 0.650 ✓ | 0.650 ✓ |
| NEGATED vs NOT_EXPRESSED | 0.980 ✓ | 0.980 ✓ |
| NEGATED vs UNKNOWN | 1.000 ✓ | 1.000 ✓ |

The metric gate grew from 8 pairs to **14** and must pass before any producer runs (it did: 14/14).

**A new apparatus defect, found by the tests and fixed.** The Phase-1 report claimed `d(x,x) = 0` as a pre-metric property. **It was false for any IR carrying an UNKNOWN slot**: `W_UNKNOWN_UNKNOWN = 0.15` separated an IR from *itself* (measured `d(x,x) = 0.124`), because "two unknowns may differ" was applied without first asking whether the two IRs are the same IR. v0.2 restores reflexivity on identical semantic content and keeps the UNKNOWN-vs-UNKNOWN separation for genuinely distinct IRs. v0.1 is left defective on purpose — reproducing the baseline is the point.

**Honest limits.** `d_SNF` is a **pre-metric**: non-negative, symmetric, `d(x,x)=0` (v0.2). **The triangle inequality is not claimed** for either version, and the category floors make v0.2 no more metric-like than v0.1 in that respect. All thresholds (τ_eval 0.40 · τ_amb 0.55 · τ_conv 0.40 · τ_agree 0.30) and all floors are **research hyper-parameters, never architectural thresholds**.

## Part E — Arbitration experiment (D-4)

**A defect worse than "only one rule ran".** Inspection of the Phase-1 ensemble showed that `majority`, `abstain_conflict` and `veto` shared one promote threshold and one partial-agreement fallback: they were **behavioural aliases**. A sweep over those names would have produced identical numbers under different labels. The six approved policies are now pinned apart by test (`test_no_two_policies_are_aliases` compares decision signatures over every member-outcome pattern). `DOMAIN_CONDITIONAL` is **deferred, not implemented** — and a test asserts it stays that way.

Full sweep, SNF-E (1,100 cases; 990 pair cases; policy is the only variable):

| policy | C | NC | abstained (pairs) | A | H | correct abstentions | false acceptances | FA rate | pairwise false consensus |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| LEAST_DIVERGENT | 640 | 290 | 50 | 0.046 | 0.000 | 50 | 120 | 0.706 | 0.592 |
| QUORUM | 640 | 290 | 50 | 0.046 | 0.000 | 50 | 120 | 0.706 | 0.592 |
| MAJORITY | 640 | 290 | 50 | 0.046 | 0.071 | 50 | 120 | 0.706 | 0.587 |
| VETO | 594 | 223 | 163 | 0.148 | 0.057 | 50 | 120 | 0.706 | 0.587 |
| **ABSTAIN_ON_CONFLICT** | **640** | **245** | 95 | 0.114 | 0.000 | **80** | **90** | **0.529** | 0.587 |
| UNANIMOUS | 594 | 200 | 186 | 0.196 | 0.000 | 80 | 90 | 0.529 | 0.587 |

**Arbitration is a real risk–coverage dial, and disagreement carries information.** Strictness buys fewer wrong answers at the price of coverage: UNANIMOUS halves wrong pairs (290 → 200) and nearly doubles correct abstentions, while giving up 46 correct answers. On this corpus ABSTAIN_ON_CONFLICT reached UNANIMOUS's false-acceptance rate **without** the coverage loss. **That is a measurement, not an endorsement** — it holds for this toy corpus, these producers, one seed.

## Part G — False-consensus report (agreement ≠ truth)

- **Pairwise:** 5,090 agreeing mechanism-pairs, **2,990 false → 0.587**. Nearly three in five agreements are agreements on a wrong reading.
- **Highest false-agreement edges:** `SNF-A|SNF-B` 320 · `SNF-A|SNF-D` 320 · `SNF-A|SNF-E` 320 · `SNF-A|SNF-C` 260 · `SNF-B|SNF-C` 260.
- **The trap works exactly as designed.** In `false_consensus_trap`, both expressions carry the surface shape *"with the X"*; the gold declares X the INSTRUMENT in one and a companion (instrument unexpressed) in the other. A/B/C map *"with NP"* → INSTRUMENT by rule, so they agree with each other **and are wrong together, 90/90**. Only the producers with an abstention route escape: D abstains 67/90 (registry), E abstains 45/90 (policy).
- **Apparatus limitation.** *Strict* consensus (all resolved producers agree) fired **0 times** at n=6, because the null baseline always dissents. With a null baseline in the pool, strict consensus is degenerate; **pairwise is the usable observable**. Phase 1's strict figure (2/5) should be read in that light.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SNF-A | 640 | 50 | 0 | 120 | 290 | 0.046 | 0.706 |
| SNF-B | 640 | 50 | 0 | 120 | 290 | 0.046 | 0.706 |
| SNF-C | 640 | 50 | 0 | 120 | 290 | 0.046 | 0.706 |

The 50 out-of-world cases are the clean signal: **all five non-null producers abstain on 50/50** — so the world boundary is respected — yet on the 120 genuinely ambiguous cases they overwhelmingly commit.
- **Coverage-matched calibration (D-5) — the Phase-1 confound was real, and it reverses.** At the common coverage 0.834:

| mech | raw Brier | raw ECE | raw coverage | matched Brier | matched ECE |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SNF-A | 0.2408 | 0.1618 | 0.949 | **0.1742** | 0.0666 |
| SNF-B | 0.2271 | 0.1118 | 0.949 | **0.1700** | 0.0166 |
| SNF-C | 0.2198 | 0.1070 | 0.949 | **0.1700** | 0.0166 |

| SNF-E | 0.2033 | 0.0717 | 0.903 | 0.1875 | 0.0575 |

  In Phase 1 the abstainer (SNF-D) had the best Brier/ECE and the report flagged it as *possibly* an abstention artifact. On the harder corpus, **matching coverage removes the advantage entirely and inverts it**: the answering producers improve sharply when judged on their most-confident 83%, while D — already at that coverage — does not move and ends up worst calibrated. **D-5 resolved: SNF-D's Phase-1 calibration advantage was a selection effect, not calibration skill.**
- **Prudence vs inability, separated by construction.** SNF-D's abstentions are *registry-driven*: it abstains on 46/110 presence cases because an entity is unregistered, not because the meaning is underdetermined. Since `abstention_warranted` treats registry ignorance as warranted for SNF-D, **part of D's "correct abstention" count is true by definition** — a definitional artifact of the apparatus, recorded rather than removed. SNF-E is the only producer with **blind abstentions** (45): its policy abstains where the case was determinable.

**Pareto frontier** (maximise correct-resolution and correct-abstention rates; bound FA rate; no weighting, no total order):

| policy | non-dominated |
|---|---|
| ABSTAIN_ON_CONFLICT | **SNF-D, SNF-E** |
| UNANIMOUS · VETO | SNF-A, SNF-B, SNF-C, SNF-D |
| MAJORITY · QUORUM · LEAST_DIVERGENT | all five |

**The frontier depends on the arbitration policy** — so "which producer is best" is not even well-posed until a policy is fixed. Under the permissive policies nothing separates; only under ABSTAIN_ON_CONFLICT do A/B/C become dominated (same resolution rate as E, worse FA rate). **Appearing on a frontier is not an endorsement.**

## Part I — Final research report

### Established by evidence (this toy world, this corpus, one seed)

1. **The Phase-1 ceiling was an artifact of corpus scope.** The same producers drop from 80/80 to R ≈ 0.69 on a corpus built to include their failure modes. Scaling the old corpus 10× would have measured nothing new.
2. **`d_SNF` v0.1 collapsed two distinctions the research depends on** — EXPRESSED vs NOT_EXPRESSED (0.330) and NOT_EXPRESSED vs UNKNOWN (0.247), both under τ = 0.40. v0.2 separates all four states (0.45–1.00).
3. **The collapse had measurable consequences, not just theoretical ones.** Under v0.1 producers were credited CORRECT on 45/90 false-consensus traps; under v0.2, 0/90. A metric that dilutes presence hides exactly the failures an adversarial family is built to expose.
4. **The Phase-1 pre-metric claim `d(x,x)=0` was false** for UNKNOWN-bearing IRs (measured 0.124). Fixed in v0.2.
5. **Three of the six named arbitration rules were behavioural aliases.** The six approved policies are now distinct and test-pinned.
6. **Arbitration is a genuine risk–coverage control.** UNANIMOUS: wrong pairs 290 → 200, abstention 0.046 → 0.196, FA rate 0.706 → 0.529.
7. **Agreement is not truth, at scale.** 0.587 of agreeing mechanism-pairs agree on a wrong reading; the "with NP → INSTRUMENT" rule makes A/B/C wrong together on 90/90 traps.
8. **SNF-D's calibration advantage from Phase 1 does not survive coverage matching** — it was a selection effect (D-5 resolved).
9. **No producer represents the epistemic/semantic absence distinction at all.** All of them fail 110/110 on `unknown_vs_not_expressed`. The distinction exists in the IR vocabulary and now in the metric, and is absent from every mechanism.
10. **SNF-C's Pāṇinian role analysis shows exactly one measurable advantage**: `role_omission_ambiguity`, 60/60 handled where A/B/D/E fail 60/60 — recognising that an omitted kāraka is *underdetermined* rather than resolvable. It shows no advantage on any other family, and it fails the traps with A and B.
11. **All producers respect a declared world boundary** — 50/50 abstentions on out-of-world content — while committing on 120 genuinely ambiguous cases. Refusing the unknown *word* is easy; refusing the underdetermined *reading* is not.
12. **Everything is reproducible.** Two independent runs produced an identical sweep digest (`9ce9d670cb47`), differing only in elapsed time; corpus digest `e9fb867903fb`; seed 20260822.

### Suggested by evidence (directional — not established)

- **Abstention capability, not interpretation strategy, is what separates these producers.** A, B and C are outcome-identical on every family; they differ only in hedging rate. The differences that matter came from having a *route to decline* (D's registry, E's policy).
- **A structural inability to say "I don't know which filler"** may be the common cause of both the `unknown_vs_not_expressed` failure and the high false-acceptance rates.
- **Non-collapse looks harder than convergence.** Convergence families are at 100%; the failures cluster in distinction-preservation and underdetermination.
- **Fronted adjuncts break the surface layer, not the semantic layer** (`nominalization_reorder` 90/90 wrong for everyone) — probably a toy-parser limit, not a finding about meaning.

### Not established

- **Which SNF formulation is correct.** No winner; the frontier changes with the arbitration policy.
- **Any claim about natural language.** Toy world, one verb lexicon, hand-written gold. Nothing here transfers to real text, translation, metaphor, pragmatics or institutional meaning — those families were **excluded by decision**, not measured.
- **That `d_SNF` v0.2 is correct.** It is a repaired **research pre-metric** with declared hyper-parameters; the triangle inequality is not claimed, and the floors are chosen, not derived.
- **That modality/tense/quantifier differences are properly separated.** They are declared DISTINCT in gold but the metric treats modality as graded (0.10), so candidate pairs differing only in modality still register as agreement at τ_conv — a known and deliberate tension, unresolved.
- **Whether abstention should be priced, and at what rate.** The taxonomy separates the outcomes; it does not settle the cost.
- **Any generalisation from one seed.** CIs are bootstrap over cases, not over corpora or worlds.

## Threats to validity

1. **Toy world.** One verb lexicon, closed entity set, hand-written gold, deterministic surface parsing. Nothing generalises to real language.
2. **Author-designed corpus.** The same author wrote the families and the gold; the traps are traps *by construction*. This measures behaviour against a declaration, not against language.
3. **Producers unchanged.** P5 measured Phase-1 producers on a harder corpus; their failures may reflect toy-parser limits (`nominalization_reorder`) rather than semantic strategy.
4. **Floors are chosen.** v0.2's floors were selected to exceed τ, then the corpus was scored with them. They are not derived from data and were declared before the run, but they are a research choice.
5. **One seed, one run.** Bootstrap CIs are over cases only.
6. **`abstention_warranted` is partly definitional** for SNF-D (AH-3).
7. **Strict consensus is degenerate** with a null baseline in the pool (Part G).
8. **Pre-metric.** No triangle inequality; distances are not distances in the metric-space sense.
