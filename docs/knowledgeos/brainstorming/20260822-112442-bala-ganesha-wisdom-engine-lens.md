This is a very interesting lens because **Bāla Gaṇeśa (बाल गणेश)** represents a completely different dimension than Śiva, Śakti, Vedānta, or Leonardo.

Leonardo represents:

> **How a human mind integrates many perspectives to understand reality.**

Śiva–Śakti represents:

> **How unity and manifestation coexist.**

Bāla Gaṇeśa represents:

> **How intelligence begins: curiosity, discrimination, memory, humility, and adaptive wisdom.**

For KnowledgeOS this is extremely valuable because the question changes:

> Not only "How does KnowledgeOS store and integrate knowledge?"
> But:
>
> **"How does KnowledgeOS grow wisdom?"**
