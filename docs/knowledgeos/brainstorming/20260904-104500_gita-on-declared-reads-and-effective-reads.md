## 1. What happened this cycle

`Π_value_set` was *declared* to read the value field. Under `T_D_relational` its value is
computed from `rank`, and `rank` is produced by ordering values **within a tag group** — so
the observable was in fact carried by `tag`, the very field `Q` reads. It did not know this.
Neither did I, and I wrote it.

Then the corrected map was *still* wrong: `Π_arity` reads **no field by declaration**, and
after `T_C_dedup` it reads `v` — because arity is a function of which records survive, and
membership is decided by a field. **A perturbation test found this; reading the code did not.**

## 2. The verse that fits, and how far it fits

> **prakṛteḥ kriyamāṇāni guṇaiḥ karmāṇi sarvaśaḥ** ·
> **ahaṅkāra-vimūḍhātmā kartāham iti manyate** — *3.27*
>
> "Actions are performed in every case by the guṇas of prakṛti; one whose self is deluded by
> ego-sense thinks 'I am the doer'."

The structural point, stripped of its soteriology: **the agent's account of its own activity
is not the activity.** `Π` reports `{v}`. `Π` is in fact carried by `{v, tag}`. Its
self-description is sincere and wrong, and its wrongness is not visible from inside itself —
it is visible only from the transformation that constitutes it.

> **gahanā karmaṇo gatiḥ** — *4.17* · "the way of action is deep / impenetrable."

Not mysticism here: **dependency structure is not surface-readable.** Two of my three
provenance maps were wrong, and I was reading the code that produced them each time.

---

## 3. Where the analogy **breaks**, and it must be said

The Gītā's resolution is **renunciation of the fruit** (`mā phaleṣu kadācana`, 2.47) and
**seeing the non-doership of the self** (5.8–9, *naiva kiñcit karomīti*). That is a
resolution in the register of *disposition*.

**There is no such move available here.** The experiment's resolution was not disposition
but **perturbation**: change one input field, watch which output columns move, and let the
observed dependency overrule the declared one. **That is a mechanical test, not an insight.**
The lens supplies the *question* — "does this thing know what it reads?" — and supplies
**nothing** toward the answer. Treating the resolution as transferable would be exactly the
category error the discipline forbids.
