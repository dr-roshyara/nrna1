## 2. The locus that fits `KR-ZOOM-01` — **kṣetra revisited at a second resolution**

Cycle 6 mapped **kṣetra / kṣetrajña** (13.1–2) onto the field/frame split. `KR-ZOOM-01` puts that
split under recursion: *the observed value becomes a new field.*

The Gītā anticipates the move in **13.26** and **13.34**:

> **yāvat saṃjāyate kiñcit sattvaṃ sthāvara-jaṅgamam** ·
> **kṣetra-kṣetrajña-saṃyogāt tad viddhi bharatarṣabha** — 13.26
> "whatever comes to be, moving or unmoving, know it as born of the union of field and knower."

> **kṣetra-kṣetrajñayor evam antaraṃ jñāna-cakṣuṣā** — 13.34
> "the difference between field and knower **seen by the eye of knowledge**."

`[CORPUS]` **The structural point: what counts as field and what counts as knower is not fixed by
the objects — it is fixed by where the observing is done from.** That is precisely the anchoring
problem `KR-ZOOM-01` had to solve: the experiment's zoom is anchored to *the dimension that
dominated the observation*, and the audit's most consequential finding (`AUDIT-2`) is that this
anchor **determines what the next field can contain**.

### Where it lands

**It names the anchor, and the anchor turned out to be a defect.** `AUDIT-2` shows that anchoring
zoom to a single dimension makes the counterfactual **untestable by construction** — the exposed
structure *cannot* differ when a non-anchor dimension is removed. The lens says *the field
depends on where you observe from*; the experiment says *and if you observe from one point only,
you have decided the answer.* **The verse frames the defect. It did not find it — a perturbation
of the operator's own definition did.**

## 3. The verse that fits the cycle's most surprising number — **2.16**

`KR-ZOOM-01`'s most counterintuitive result: **zoom lost determination in ~7 % of cases and gained
it in none.** Refining the value made the question *less* answerable.

> **nāsato vidyate bhāvo nābhāvo vidyate sataḥ** — 2.16
> "of the unreal there is no being; of the real there is no non-being."

`[CORPUS]` Read structurally and *without* its metaphysics: **the verse asserts that what is real
does not come and go under a change of view.** The experiment measured the opposite for
*determination*: determination is **not** invariant under resolution change — it can be destroyed
by refinement.

> ### `[CORPUS]` **This is the first cycle in which the lens and the experiment DISAGREE**, and the disagreement is the useful part.
>
> The Gītā's invariance intuition is a claim about *being*. The experiment's result is about
> *answerability under a fixed contract*. **Conflating them would license "refinement never
> loses knowledge", which the data refute in 114 cases across two splits.**

`[REC]` **Record the disagreement.** A lens that only ever agrees is decorative.

## 6. Verdict — **seven cycles, and the pattern has now been tested to failure in the other direction**

> ### `[CORPUS]` **Seven cycles. Zero kernel primitives. Four namings. And now one place where the lens's structural intuition is flatly contradicted by data.**

Cycles 1–6 established that the lens *re-describes* rather than predicts. Cycle 7 adds the sharper
datum: **when the lens did make something resembling a prediction — invariance under change of
view (2.16) — the measurement refuted it.**

`[REC]` **The standing recommendation is unchanged and now better supported:** keep the strand,
keep it **external**, do not test it for kernel candidacy. Its value is naming what an experiment
earned. **Cycle 7 adds the corollary: it must never be used as a source of expectation**, because
its one expectation-shaped contribution was wrong.
