# Prioritization: What Must Be Resolved Before What

Every gap in `01_gap-register.md` classified into exactly one of seven categories (per the
authorization's §13). This is the decision-relevant synthesis of this whole stage.

## BLOCKING (must be resolved before a *unified* Stage-07 formalization could legitimately begin)

| Gap | Why it blocks |
|---|---|
| **GA-001** (Kernel identity) | A unified formalization cannot target "the Kernel" while four independently-produced families remain unmapped to each other, and two of them (A/C1/C2 vs. B) are shown to be different *kinds* of object |
| **GA-038** (no canonical `K_t`) | The corpus's own most explicit statement that state-formalization cannot proceed on a single, non-arbitrary structure without a selection act this reconstruction has not made and is not authorized to make |

**Both are BLOCKING only for a *unified*, cross-model formalization target — neither blocks a
formalization that stays within one model's own already-established, internally-consistent
sub-results** (e.g. Model B's own tested kernel-reduction result, or its frozen `Δ_t` definition,
could each be formalized further on their own terms without waiting for GA-001/GA-038).

## The single most important finding of this stage

**The programme's two BLOCKING gaps (GA-001, GA-038) are the same question asked from two directions**
— cross-model (which Kernel/State object, if any, is common across models) and within-Model-B
(which specific `K_t` variant, among Model B's own 9+, is canonical). Neither question is closeable by
more reading of the existing corpus: both models' own evidence, and this reconstruction's own repeated
adjudication passes (Phase 3, Phase 6), have already searched for a resolving map or a selection
criterion and found none. **This is not evidence that gap-analysis was performed insufficiently — it is
evidence that Stage 07 formalization, if it is ever authorized, cannot legitimately target "the
Kernel" or "the canonical state" as single objects without either (a) a new, dedicated, separately-
authorized formalization effort that attempts the structure-preserving mapping this reconstruction has
never attempted, or (b) explicit acceptance that formalization must proceed model-by-model, or
representation-by-representation, rather than unified.**
