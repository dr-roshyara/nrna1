evidence-sharing stress test). At least eight *other* named Kernel-structure candidates across the C1

### CT-3 — Five self-caught circularities within the `phase_measure_theory/` arc

immediately preceding file's formalization of Knowledge State/System State. **Status:** each
individual circularity is resolved at the point it is caught (a corrected definition replaces the
circular one) — recorded as a contradiction-and-repair pattern, not an outstanding tension, but
notable as a recurring structural weakness of this research method (defining a state partly in terms

### CT-4 — The `kernel/` vs. `phase_measure_theory/` chronology and content overlap, never reconciled

## The C1↔C2 relationship, tested against the fuller evidence (authorization's required section)

MD-007 (2026-09-01) held three possibilities open from an initial ~10-file sample: **(i)** `C1 → C2`
(evolution) · **(ii)** `C1 ∥ C2` (independent lines sharing vocabulary) · **(iii)** `C1 →
limitations/questions → C2` (a C1 gap motivates the epistemic question) — with an explicit falsifier
named for its own promoted watch-status finding: *"'Knowledge' is undefined in all ten C1 files while
load-bearing in every one... the falsifier is simple: any C1 document that defines knowledge."*

**This falsifier is decisively triggered by the fuller 732-file population.** C1-tagged files define
"knowledge" explicitly and repeatedly throughout Clusters D–H of `01_evidence-base.md` — e.g. seq
0434's five-term definitional ladder (Information=Raw_Data+Provenance; Knowledge=Information+…); seq
0445's `K=K_D+K_V` split; seq 2330's own mature definition ("a factive epistemic relation between a
participant and domain content..."). **MD-007's own watch-status finding is therefore falsified when
tested against the fuller population, exactly as its own falsifier specified — this is recorded as a
finding, not silently updated into the prior watch-status entry**, per this reconstruction's own
history-is-never-rewritten discipline.

**What this does, and does not, establish about C1→C2**: it does **not** establish option (i), (ii),
or (iii) as correct — MD-007's own rule stands: *"the relation between C1 and C2 is decided... from
documents that state the transition, not from the plausibility of a story that fits."* No C1 document
in this evidence base explicitly states "we moved from engineering to epistemics" or cites a specific
C1 limitation as the reason for asking "what is knowledge." **What it does establish is narrower and
more precise**: the empirical classification boundary between `engineering_knowledgeos` and
`epistemic_knowledgeos`, as actually applied across this corpus, does not track a clean split between
"builds infrastructure without theorizing knowledge" and "theorizes knowledge." The overwhelming
majority of sustained, mathematically serious knowledge-theorizing in this entire reconstruction
(Knowledge State, Ideal State, Discrepancy, Knowledge Ātma, the repeated defining and redefining of
"knowledge" itself) sits inside the `engineering_knowledgeos`-tagged population, not the
`epistemic_knowledgeos`-tagged one — which has exactly one member.

**This is reported as an observation about classification pattern, not a recommendation to
reclassify** — per the authorization's explicit guardrail, no membership change is proposed or implied
here; the question of whether this pattern reflects correct discipline (engineering work legitimately
needs a working theory of knowledge without that work thereby becoming "epistemic" in protocol.md's
sense) or under-application of the `epistemic_knowledgeos` tag is **left to a separately-authorized
classification-review phase**, never decided here.

**Verdict on MD-007's three-way framework**: **UNRESOLVED**, and for a more specific reason than
"insufficient evidence" — the framework's own operative distinction (C1 as engineering-without-
epistemics vs. C2 as epistemics) does not cleanly partition the evidence this phase actually found. A
future phase revisiting this question should not simply re-ask "(i), (ii), or (iii)?" without first
addressing whether the C1/C2 classification boundary itself needs governance attention — a distinct
question from the one MD-007 posed, and one this phase is not authorized to answer.
