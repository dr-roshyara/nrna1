## Contradictions (genuine, corpus-internal)

### CT-1. The 13-vs-8 kernel-cardinality tension is resolved; a second, textually distinct 14-vs-12 tension is not

M0037 proves four distinct cardinality-8 minimal kernels exist against C0's original 13-operator
proposal (M0030); M0099 formally reconciles this as "both are genuine minima under different
algebras" (§F, §P-1/P-2 of the concept register). **M0102, however, introduces a separate count —
"14 vs. 12" — for what its own text describes as a related but textually distinct minimality
question, and explicitly flags it as NOT reconciled with the 13-vs-8 result.** This evidence base
contains no record resolving 14-vs-12. **Status:** genuine, corpus-acknowledged open contradiction
— `unresolved_equivalence` between the two minimality counts, not to be silently merged with the
already-resolved 13-vs-8 case simply because both concern "kernel cardinality."

### CT-2. THM-1, THM-5, and THM-11 as originally stated in M0043 are refuted by M0043's own same-day self-audit (M0045)

Recorded in full in the concept register, §D. THM-1 required an added hypothesis to hold; THM-5 was
downgraded from theorem to a representation-relative axiom; THM-11 was found near-circular pending
independently specified semantics. **Status:** `genuine_contradiction`, internally resolved by the
corpus's own audit discipline the same research day — recorded here because M0043's *own*
self-description ("What v1.0 now proves") is not itself corrected in that later file, so a reader of
M0043 alone would not discover the retraction without also reading M0045.

### CT-3. M0057's "Zero is more fundamental than Sat" claim is explicitly rejected one file later

M0058's impossibility proof (Zero cannot be an oracle for unknown-unknowns) replaces M0057's
hierarchical Zero-over-Sat architecture with a symmetric one. **Status:** `genuine_contradiction`,
resolved within the same short chain (M0057→M0058→M0060) — recorded because M0060's "Zero Concept
v1.2" synthesis is the only place both the claim and its rejection are visible together.

### CT-4. M0099's own "general mathematical theory" self-description is downgraded by M0100, in the immediately following file

The Structure-First framework (§F) is canonized at M0099 with strong language ("the most important
document..."), then M0100 — read next in sequence — explicitly reclassifies the framework's status
from "general theory" to "a very strong explanatory hypothesis," and separately rejects M0099's
freshly-proposed `Zero = closure(B_π)` definition as premature (seven non-equivalent candidate
meanings of "closure" catalogued, none selected). **Status:** `genuine_contradiction` for the
theory/hypothesis status claim and for the `Zero = closure(B_π)` definition specifically; both facts
are carried into the concept register (§F) exactly as the corpus itself resolves them — canonization
of the *framework*, explicit non-canonization of *this specific status claim and this specific
definition*.

### CT-5. Two independent file-timestamp-vs-logical-content-order anomalies

(a) M0105 is timestamped/sequenced in a position that does not match its logical dependency on
M0103/M0104 (FR-001's proof and freeze) — its content presupposes conclusions that, by sequence
number, come after it. (b) M0110/M0111 show a similar mismatch relative to M0109. **Status:**
recorded as a corpus-integrity anomaly, not a mathematical contradiction — the *content* in each
pair is not in tension, only the apparent processing/logical order is. Not resolved within this
evidence base; flagged for the governed record (`14_decision-log/`), not silently smoothed over by
re-ordering the traceability table.

## Unresolved equivalences

### UE-1. The K_t tuple-structure family (nine+ distinct formulations)

Already registered in full at concept register §G: M0001 (7-component), M0006 (6-argument
function), M0009 (6-component atomic claim), M0043 (33-definition apparatus), M0048 (5th variant),
M0076 (6-component candidate), M0125/M0126 (11-component alternative with 20 unreconciled "core
invariants"), plus at least two further variants distinguishable within the M0283–M0338 tail. **No
corpus-internal act establishes a structure-preserving equivalence across this family.** Recorded
here as `unresolved_equivalence` per MD-017's default rule — not merged because several members look
superficially similar (all are "K_t-named state structures"), and not treated as evidence of
corpus incoherence either, since several *sub-chains* within the family (§A: M0005→...→M0132) are
genuinely `refinement`, not contradiction.

### UE-2. The M0125/M0126 eleven-component alternative and its twenty "core invariants"

A specific, sharper instance of UE-1: this particular tuple `K_t=(A_t,R_t,E_t,Σ_t,H_t,Z_t,L_t,T_t,
G_t,C_t,M_t)` appears in two session-handoff documents together with twenty named "core invariants,"
**and does not match any other K_t formulation this evidence base tracks elsewhere.** M0131 traces
this structure's genuine origin to Theory v1.1, later downgraded to candidate status in v1.2 — but
the twenty invariants themselves are never individually cross-referenced against the rest of this
evidence base's own established definitions. **Status:** `unresolved_equivalence`, open — not
resolved by M0131's traceback, which explains *provenance*, not *content-level correspondence* to
the rest of the K_t family.

### UE-3. Composition rules "majority" and "intraframe-only" (M0129)

Both survive KR-COMP-SEP-2026-09's testing without being shown necessary or sufficient. Recorded as
its own fifth status in the kernel-candidate table (§P-12/P-13, concept register) rather than forced
into `unresolved_equivalence` proper — flagged here because the *relationship between the two
survivors themselves* (are they equivalent under some transformation, or genuinely distinct
candidates that happen to both survive the same test?) is never addressed by the evidence base.

## Open questions (Model-B evidence alone cannot settle these)

**OQ-1.** Was the kernel extension M0032/M0033 propose (adding `RespondToEvidence`/`Maintain·
ApplyEpistemicStandards` to C0) ever carried through the same ablation-experiment discipline that
tested C0 itself (M0030/M0035/M0037)? No execution record for this specific extension exists in this
evidence base. (Concept register §C, §P-3.)

**OQ-2.** Was `KR-LINGA-YONI-2026-09-02` (the decisive 2×2-design experiment testing whether the
kernel's irreducible function corresponds to an "initiating" pole distinct from a "transformation"
pole, M0090–M0092) ever executed? M0091/M0092's own open questions ask this; this evidence base
contains no confirming record, in contrast to the structurally parallel KR-CONTR/KR-ORGASM/KR-COMP-
SEP protocols, each of which *was* confirmed executed. (Concept register §L, §P-14.)

**OQ-3.** Is ClosureEvent's irreversibility a **kernel** property, or merely a property of History/
Audit? M0080 names a decisive test design (`K_t,K_{t+1}`-identical-but-History-differs) but this
evidence base contains no execution record for it. (Concept register §K, §P-6.)

**OQ-4.** What is Zero Closure's actual evaluation predicate? M0057 separates it from Zero Lens as a
distinct, still-undefined operation; M0058's impossibility proof narrows what it *cannot* be (an
unknown-unknowns oracle) without supplying what it *can* be; M0060's synthesis leaves it formally
open. (Concept register §E.)

**OQ-5.** Which of the seven non-equivalent candidate meanings of "closure" catalogued by M0100 (in
the course of rejecting M0099's `Zero = closure(B_π)`) is the corpus's intended one, if any? Not
selected anywhere in this evidence base. (Concept register §F, CT-4.)

**OQ-6.** Is the full `KR-COMP-SEP-2026-09` execution writeup located anywhere in this corpus, or
does only M0129's summarized report exist? M0129's own record flags the full document as not
independently located during this pass. Bears directly on how much confidence to place in the three
composition-rule exclusions (§P-9/P-10/P-11) versus treating them as provisional pending the full
writeup.

**OQ-7.** Does a document titled "KnowledgeOS Verified Theory — Part XIV" genuinely continue the
13-part rewrite series (M0322–M0334), or does the series' own Part XIII (M0334) leave the next part
unwritten, with a same-numbered file (M0335) only coincidentally titled similarly? **This specific
question was previously investigated in a different governance context (the earlier math-lane
reconciliation, prior to this Phase-2 reconstruction), which is not treated as settled evidence
here** — Model B's own evidence base is re-examined independently for this reconstruction, and this
evidence base's own digest pass did not reach far enough into the M0335–M0338 continuation to
independently re-confirm the relationship. Recorded as genuinely open pending that direct check,
rather than carried forward from an unrelated pass.

**OQ-8.** Why does M0132 ("Gap Theory," internally labeled v1.0) cite research artifacts from
KR-CONTR, KR-CONTR-FDE, and the Zero-synthesis chain — all of which, by this evidence base's own
sequence numbering, are processed *before* M0132 but conceptually treated by M0132 as prior,
established inputs to a "v1.0" document? The version label and the citation pattern are not
obviously in tension (a v1.0 document can legitimately build on already-established prior results),
but the juxtaposition is unusual enough, relative to this corpus's otherwise consistent versioning
discipline (§O), to record as an open question rather than silently pass over.

**OQ-9.** Are the twenty "core invariants" named alongside the M0125/M0126 eleven-component K_t
alternative individually consistent with, a superset of, or in tension with the invariant
commitments implicit in the corpus's own axiomatic capstone (M0043/M0045/M0047)? Not addressed by
any file in this evidence base (see UE-2).

**OQ-10.** Is there a corpus-internal, non-arbitrary way to select among the K_t tuple family (UE-1)
a single canonical structure for any future formalization work, or does the family remain
irreducibly plural because each variant was built for a different sub-purpose (e.g. M0043's is
