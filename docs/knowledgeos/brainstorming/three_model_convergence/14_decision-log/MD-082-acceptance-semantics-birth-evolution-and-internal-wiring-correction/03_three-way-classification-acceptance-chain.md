# MD-082 §03 — Three-Way Classification: Object Identity / Semantic Responsibility / Computational Completeness

Per the mission's own explicit requirement, these three dimensions are never collapsed. Vocabulary:
object identity (`SAME OBJECT`/`SAME CONCEPT, REFINED`/`RELATED OBJECT`/`UNRELATED HOMONYM`/
`UNRESOLVED`); semantic responsibility (as in MD-081 §02, extended with `EXTERNALIZED RESPONSIBILITY`);
computational completeness (`defined` / `computable` / `empirically validated` — kept strictly separate
per this mission's own §7).

## `Sat(K,r,Γ)` itself

- **Object identity**: `SAME OBJECT` across `[Def 6.18]` and §6.27/§6.28's own reuse — `RECONSTRUCTED`
  at high confidence (§02), the strongest same-object case in the family.
- **Semantic responsibility**: `SAME RESPONSIBILITY` — determining whether a knowledge state satisfies
  a requirement, consistently, from birth (`step-023`) through every later reuse.
- **Computational completeness**: **`defined`** (a name, an argument list, and, via `[Def 6.18]`, a
  compositional formula) but **not `computable`** (the formula's own two inputs — `EC.Rules`'s content,
  `Det_r`'s body — are each undefined) and **not `empirically validated`** (zero invocations anywhere,
  MD-076, unchanged). This is the correct, three-way-separated restatement of what MD-078/079 called
  simply "Terminal Classification C" — `defined` without collapsing into `computable`.

## `Det(K,p,EC,Γ)` (§6.27) / `Δ_p`/`Zero_p` (§6.28, §6.73–74)

- **Object identity**: `SAME OBJECT` as each other (internally consistent within Part VI); `RELATED
  OBJECT` to Part III's own independently-derived `Det(K,p,EC,Γ)`/Theorem 3.1 (same name, same shape,
  no cross-citation between the two Parts — two convergent, non-identical formalizations of the same
  idea, per this reconstruction's own standing "convergence ≠ identity" discipline).
- **Semantic responsibility**: `SAME RESPONSIBILITY` as Part III's own version — proposition-level
  aggregation given per-requirement `Sat` values.
- **Computational completeness**: **`defined` and `computable` given `Sat`'s own values** — Theorem
  6.1's own proof is genuinely valid and requires nothing further once `Sat(K,r,Γ)` is available by any
  route (fiat, `Standing(p)`-style evaluation, or a hypothetical completed `Det_r`). **Not itself
  blocked** — this is the one part of the whole chain that is fully operational, exactly as MD-078's
  own Classification A already found, now confirmed at the computational-completeness level
  specifically, not merely the "defined" level.

## `EC.Rules`

- **Object identity**: `UNRESOLVED` — no internal structure is ever given, so there is no second
  formulation to compare it against; identity cannot even be posed.
- **Semantic responsibility**: `SAME RESPONSIBILITY` as `Policy_Det`'s own illustrated slot ("the exact
  policy belongs to the epistemic contract") — the two are explicitly, textually connected by the
  source itself, unlike most other candidate pairs in this investigation.
- **Computational completeness**: **not `defined`** — no source, anywhere in the corpus, gives
  `EC.Rules` an internal type or structure. This is the one place in this entire three-way matrix where
  the classification is uniformly negative across all three checked properties — but even here, the
  correct statement is **`SEMANTICALLY PRESENT BUT COMPUTATIONALLY OPEN`**, not `GENUINELY ABSENT AFTER
  CENSUS`: the *responsibility* is named and located precisely (§6.43's own words), only its *content*
  is missing.

- **Object identity**: `RELATED OBJECT` to `EC.Rules` (explicitly, textually connected — "the exact

- **Computational completeness**: **`defined`** as a case structure (four named outcomes, four named
  conditions) but **not `computable`** — "sufficient independent support" and "sufficient challenge"

  passage, never operationalized anywhere else in the corpus.

## `Admissible = Pre∧Inv∧Assurance∧Auth` (verification lane, `STEP-VERIFY-041-055.md` §42.9/§42.41)

- **Object identity**: `RELATED OBJECT` to `EC.Rules`/`Policy_Det` — a genuinely different bounded
  context (action-admissibility, not requirement-satisfaction; MD-081 §01).
- **Semantic responsibility**: `RELATED RESPONSIBILITY`, not `SAME` — governs whether an action/decision
  may proceed, not whether a knowledge state satisfies a requirement.
- **Computational completeness**: **`defined`**, and this phase reaffirms (reusing, not re-deriving) the
  verification lane's own prior adversarial finding: **not `computable`** — `Assurance`, one of
  `Admissible`'s own four conjuncts, is explicitly "REFUTED as definable" by the corpus's own governed
  audit work (`NG-1`). This is a case where the corpus's own prior work already performed exactly the
  `defined`-vs-`computable` separation this mission asks for, independently, before this reconstruction
  ever posed the question — worth noting as corroboration of the distinction's own validity, not merely
  its applicability.

## The "qualification rule" (`verification/gap-discovery/08-EVIDENCE-GAP.md`, EG-2)

- **Object identity**: `RELATED OBJECT` — "relevance to a named proposition under an explicit rule,"
  the closest match in spirit to `EC.Rules`'s own job found in the verification lane specifically.
- **Semantic responsibility**: `SAME RESPONSIBILITY` — closest of any candidate found to literally
  answering "what determines whether a requirement/observation counts."
- **Computational completeness**: **`defined`** ("well-formed," per the source's own words) but **not
  `computable`** — "its load-bearing predicate — relevance under a rule — is in the class the corpus
  itself marks as having no decision procedure" (EG-2, reused). A second instance of the corpus's own
  prior work independently applying this same separation.

## Summary: the pattern across every candidate

Every genuine formal-object candidate found for the acceptance/sufficiency responsibility — across five
phases of searching (MD-037–041's `Warrant`; MD-080's `Assessment`/`Verdict`; MD-081's `Admissible`/
qualification-rule/evaluation-rule; this phase's `Policy_Det`) — lands in the **same** cell of this
three-way matrix: `defined`, not `computable`, not `empirically validated`. This is not five independent
gaps; it is one recurring pattern, independently rediscovered at least five times, in at least four
different lanes, by different authors/sessions across the corpus's own history. That consistency is
itself evidence worth weighing: a responsibility this repeatedly *named* and this consistently left
*uncomputed*, across independently-authored threads that do not cite each other, is better read as a
genuine, structural, acknowledged-by-the-theory-itself open design question than as an oversight any
single further search is likely to close.
