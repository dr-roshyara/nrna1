# Complementarity Adjudication (H1–H4)

## The four hypotheses, tested against the 4 pairs

- **H1 — coherent composition exists.** Not found in any of the 4 pairs.
- **H2 — composition exists only after semantic alteration/reinterpretation.** Not tested (would
  require reconstructing missing specification, explicitly forbidden).
- **H3 — composition is impossible under the structures actually evidenced.** Not established either —
  Pair 4's own result (B's tested apparatus directly constrains C1's field's own adequacy) is evidence
  *against* flat incompatibility, even though no actual map was demonstrated.
- **H4 — composition cannot be tested because one or both sides are insufficiently specified.** **The
  correct verdict for 3 of the 4 pairs (1, 2, 3)**, and the partial verdict even for Pair 4 (the map
  itself is untestable, though a weaker, genuinely positive functional-adequacy finding was still
  obtained).

## Overall verdict on H_C (the core complementarity hypothesis)

**H4, predominantly, with one genuine partial exception.** The complementarity hypothesis is **neither
confirmed nor refuted** by this study — it remains exactly what MD-023 left it: a hypothesis. What this
study adds, precisely: **the hypothesis cannot currently be tested at the rigor the authorization
requires (§5's full input→operation→output→invariant chain), because Model B's own operator
specifications — within its own legitimate, non-KR-SIM-tagged evidence base — never reached that level
of formality in the first place.** This is not a failure of this study's method; it is a fact about
what the frozen source material actually contains, confirmed directly by raw-source check (`00`).

## The one genuine, evidence-grounded step forward

Pair 4 (`ConflictRecord` ↔ B's contradiction-representation line) produced a real, if narrow,
finding: B's own tested result constrains what an adequate conflict-tracking structure must contain,
and C1's own current `ConflictRecord` field does not meet that bar as currently specified. This is
**functional correspondence with a demonstrated adequacy constraint, not structural correspondence and
not composition** — the most precise, honest characterization this study can support.

## What would change this verdict

A future, separately-authorized effort that either (a) locates or produces a formal input/output
specification for B's own C0 operators within its own legitimate evidence base (not importing the
KR-SIM-tagged apparatus, which would require its own separate authorization to bring into scope), or
(b) attempts an actual composition design (architecture work, out of scope for both MD-023 and MD-024).
