# Pre-Registered Selection (documented before any composition test was run)

## Selection criteria (fixed in advance)

1. Highest formal specification quality available on each side (from `01`).
2. Strongest evidence the candidate represents the relevant DDD layer.
3. Strongest prior cross-model correspondence (from Phase 3, Phase 6, or MD-023).
4. Highest diagnostic value for distinguishing complementarity from incompatibility.
5. Ability to produce an actual structural composition attempt, not a terminology analogy.

## Candidate universe

Given `01`'s own finding that B's operator side has essentially no formal I/O specification anywhere in
its legitimate evidence base, an exhaustive B-operator × aggregate-field sweep would produce identical,
uninformative `NOT FORMALLY SPECIFIED ENOUGH TO TEST` results for the overwhelming majority of cells.
This is a **DIAGNOSTIC SUBSET**, not an exhaustive search — the candidate universe (13 B operators + 2
extension candidates, × 4 aggregate candidates × their component fields) is large, and this study
selects for diagnostic value per the criteria above rather than testing all combinations.

## The 4 selected pairs

| # | Pair | Selection rationale |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `Validate` (B) ↔ C1's P-3 (six-part aggregate) | Criterion 1/2: P-3 is the only aggregate candidate with a *tested* invariant (seq 0157); `Validate` is one of B's own tested-load-bearing operators (survives every ablation round). Best-specified pair available on both sides. |

Tests B's most operational content against A's most content-rich aggregate. |
| 3 | B's operator set generally ↔ C2's `𝒞=(D,P,T,C,I,E,R,H,Θ)` | Criterion 5 (coverage) — C2 is the only aggregate side with a named transitions component (`Θ`), the closest thing to an operator-shaped slot on the aggregate side; tests whether B's operators could plausibly populate `Θ`. |
| 4 (adversarial) | B's contradiction-representation research line (§J, tested, `M0114-127`) ↔ C1's P-5's `ConflictRecord` field | Criterion 3/4: this is the pair most likely to succeed if the complementarity hypothesis is correct, since B has *substantial, tested* content specifically about contradiction handling — deliberately chosen because its failure would be strong evidence against complementarity, and its success would be the strongest evidence for it |

**Not selected**: any pairing involving B's own untested extension candidates (`RespondToEvidence`,
etc.) or B's individually-unenumerated cardinality-8 minimal kernels — both lack even the operator-name-
level specificity the 4 selected pairs have.
