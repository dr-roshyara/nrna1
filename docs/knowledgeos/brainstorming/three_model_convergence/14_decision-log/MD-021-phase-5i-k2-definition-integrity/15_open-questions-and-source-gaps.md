# Phase 5I — Open Questions and Source Gaps

## Genuine source-research gaps (confirmed, not merely unfound)

1. **A single, reconciled `Assertion` schema** — the corpus's own two prose documents and three
   executable scripts do not agree, and no supersession/versioning marker exists to resolve which is
   authoritative.
2. **`Qualify`'s computable algorithm and single arity** — confirmed absent (Phase 5H), now further
   shown to have an unreconciled arity conflict (1-arg vs. 2-arg) with no document addressing both.
3. **`State`'s own projection target** — "the carrier" remains undefined corpus-wide (Phase 5H,
   reconfirmed this phase).
4. **Whether $\pi_1$ and $\pi_2$ (the two K-1→K-2 projections) are reconcilable** — genuinely new this
   phase; no document has ever distinguished them, so none has ever addressed their relationship.
5. **`Π`'s own denotation** (Policy vs. provenance) — a newly-disclosed ambiguity (`06`, discrepancy 5).

own prior characterization** of the K-1→K-2 projection (narrowing Phase 5H's "partial correspondence"

## What would close these gaps

A single governance act reconciling the `Assertion` schema (retiring or unifying the two competing
scripts); a formalized `Qualify` predicate with one settled arity; an explicit `State` carrier
definition; and — new to this phase — an explicit statement (or demonstrated bijection) reconciling
$\pi_1$ and $\pi_2$, or an explicit governance decision that only one of the two `Assertion`
characterizations is authoritative going forward.
