# Phase 5I — K-2 Ontological Status (the required 1–7 classification)

## Evidence summary feeding this classification

- K-2's own top-level structure, `K=(𝒜,ℛ)`, is **stable and undisputed** across every source (D1–D5,
  `01`) — no document proposes an alternative top-level shape for K-2 itself.
- The **content of `𝒜` (Assertion)** is where the instability lives: 5 materially distinct
  characterizations (`01`), reducing to 2 internally-consistent positions plus 1 outlier
  (`e_equality.py`'s own narrower parameterization) (`02`).
- The **operations** over K-2 (`𝒪_sem`) are stated to exist (19 candidates, Step 272A) but not
  re-examined this phase (out of scope — Phase 5H's own Target A was about K-1's operators, not K-2's).
- The **executable evidence is internally inconsistent with itself** in one specific, disclosed way
  (`03`'s intra-script finding) and **inconsistent across scripts** in the field-set question (`06`).

## Testing the required 1–7 classification

1. **SINGLE WELL-DEFINED OBJECT**: **Not supported** — the field-set conflict is real and unreconciled.
2. **SINGLE OBJECT WITH MULTIPLE REPRESENTATIONS**: **Not supported** — this would require the
   representations to be shown compatible via a stated transformation (per the authorization's own
   distinction in §6); no such transformation exists (`06`, relation 2: UNRESOLVED).
3. **EVOLVING VERSIONED OBJECT**: **Not supported as a confirmed classification** — plausible (`07`'s
   own C-hypothesis) but not evidenced; no version markers exist anywhere in the five Assertion
   records.
4. **FAMILY OF RELATED OBJECTS**: **Closest partial fit for the top-level structure** — `K=(𝒜,ℛ)`
   itself is stable, so K-2 is not a *family* at the top level; but its constituent `𝒜` sub-object is
   where the multiplicity lives.
5. **COMPETING OBJECT DEFINITIONS**: **Best-supported classification overall** — two internally-
   consistent, mutually-unreconciled characterizations of `𝒜` (D1/D3 vs. D2/D4), each paired with its
   own supporting code, neither aware of or citing the other's field set. This is precisely what
   "competing object definitions" describes: not a homonym (both concern the same named concept), not
   mere notation, not a demonstrated evolution — genuinely competing, unresolved formalizations.
6. **INTERNALLY INCONSISTENT OBJECT**: **Applies at a narrower scope than the whole-object level** —
   `t285_reconcile.py`'s own T-A/T-C sections disagree with each other about `Observation`'s status
   (`03`), which is a genuine internal inconsistency, but it is localized to one script's own two
   sections, not a property of K-2 as a whole (K-2's top-level `(𝒜,ℛ)` shape is not itself
   inconsistent).
7. **UNRESOLVED**: not the primary classification, since the evidence supports a specific, positive
   finding (5) rather than a blanket "insufficient evidence" state.

## Verdict

**K-2 = 5. COMPETING OBJECT DEFINITIONS**, specifically at the level of its `Assertion` constituent —
**with an additional, narrower finding of type 6 (internal inconsistency) localized to
`t285_reconcile.py`'s own two sections.** This is a research adjudication, evidence-backed at every
step above, not a design decision, and not an assumption drawn from the shared word "Assertion" (per
the authorization's own explicit warning in §9) — it follows from the demonstrated, re-executed
incompatibility of the underlying field sets and their independent, unreconciled code support.
