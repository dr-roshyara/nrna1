## Compound final classification

**Primarily D (admissibility/provenance block) combined with a confirmed-negative extension of the
Warrant search (no new B/C/A-level finding).** Neither letter alone is accurate: this phase is not
chiefly a scientific finding about Warrant (as B/C designated in MD-040/041) — its dominant event was
discovering that most of its own nominal search scope was inadmissible by provenance. But it is not
purely an admissibility block either (as D alone would suggest for a single blocked directory) — it
also *actively completed* a real, if negative, search over the landscape that remained after triage,
and that negative result (§3) is itself evidence, not merely an absence of access.

**Stated plainly: D+ — provenance triage dominates the phase; the residual search that could be
completed found nothing new on Warrant and confirmed rather than created the standing kernel-
candidate landscape.**

## Main-goal test

**Does this bring us materially closer to selecting a defensible minimal kernel or canonical theory?**
Not directly — no candidate was advanced, no gap was closed, no new computable Warrant rule was
found. **But it does two things of real, if modest, value**: (1) it converts an assumed-wide search
space ("eight directories") into a precisely-bounded one (three searchable, five firewalled/blocked),
which is itself progress toward knowing what remains to be done and by whom (a governance-level
provenance decision, not further ad hoc searching); (2) it hardens MD-040/041's own negative finding
on Warrant from "not found in the threads we happened to already know about" to "not found anywhere
this reconstruction can currently search" — the same kind of closure GA-038 itself already reached
for `K_t` canonicalization (a governance-decision-shaped gap, not a further-research-shaped one).
Separated cleanly per the five-way test above: mostly **unresolved/better-located**, nothing newly
**blocking**, nothing **established** at the level of a kernel or Warrant finding itself.
