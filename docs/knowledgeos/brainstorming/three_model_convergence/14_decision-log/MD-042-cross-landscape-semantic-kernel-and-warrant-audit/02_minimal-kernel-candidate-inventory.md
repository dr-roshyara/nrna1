## What this inventory does and does not establish

- **No new live minimal-kernel candidate was found** that survives its own source's scrutiny — every
  genuinely new item (F7) turns out to answer a different question ("what is Knowledge," not "what is
  the Kernel"), and every Kernel-labeled item found (F3, F4's new data point, F5, F6) is already known
  to this reconstruction under a name already on record.
- **GA-001 (Kernel identity) is UNCHANGED**: no pair of families found here reaches beyond the
  categories already assessed at Phase 5/6; no new structure-preserving-equivalence claim is
  established or testable from this material.
- **GA-038 (no canonical `K_t`) is UNCHANGED, and arguably reinforced**: F4's new data point is a
  further explicitly-non-canonical `K_t` variant, adding to (not resolving) the proliferation GA-038
  already names.
- **A fourth K-1/K-2 label collision (F7) is now on record**, alongside Phase 6's own F5 finding —
  strengthening, not newly creating, this reconstruction's standing caution against treating shared
  short labels as evidence of a shared object.
