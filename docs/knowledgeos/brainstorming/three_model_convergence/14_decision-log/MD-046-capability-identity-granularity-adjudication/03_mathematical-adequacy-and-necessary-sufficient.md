# MD-046 §3 — Mathematical Adequacy (Phase D) and Necessary vs. Sufficient (Phase E)

## Phase D — testing every candidate found against the 8 circularity-avoidance questions

No item found in the census (`01_...md`) rises to a genuine candidate identity/equivalence relation —
each is either a methodological prohibition (what not to do) or evidence about a different question
(aggregate atomicity within one already-fixed vocabulary). For completeness, the two closest
candidates are tested anyway:

**`S1-F016`'s atomicity-falsification test** (strong relatedness / weak atomicity as independent
axes): (1) independent of chosen names? — partially, it operates on already-named items without
requiring them to share a name; (2) independent of arbitrary decomposition? — **NO**, it presupposes
the items are already correctly individuated; it cannot tell you whether "Identity" and some other
undiscovered name denote the same thing; (3) distinguishes composite from atomic? — yes, that is its
whole purpose, but only among items already given as candidates; (4–8) not applicable — this test
answers "should these already-named things be one aggregate," not "are these two descriptions the
same capability." **Verdict: answers a different, though related, question. Not sufficient for
capability identity.**

**`S1-F037`'s three prohibitions**: these are constraints on method (don't decompose a primitive,
don't derive ontology from representation, don't rename to solve), not a relation. They can rule out
certain proposed identity criteria as illegitimate, but supply no positive test of their own.
**Verdict: necessary-sounding guardrails, not a sufficient criterion.**

**Conclusion**: no criterion found anywhere in the searched corpus, real or candidate, would let
`DetectGap` derivability or `Qualify` necessity be tested against the real 13-capability universe
without first making exactly the arbitrary decomposition choice the MinKer chain's own final rule
forbids.

## Phase E — necessary vs. sufficient, explicitly separated

**Necessary** (established, at least as constraints on any future criterion, by the corpus itself):
independence from the specific names/labels chosen (`S1-F037`); independence from which
implementation currently realizes the capability (implicit in the MinKer chain's own `≡_sem`
discipline); the ability to distinguish genuine atomicity-grouping decisions from identity questions
(implicit in `S1-F016`'s own distinct axis).

**Sufficient**: **nothing found in the corpus meets this bar.** A reflexive/symmetric/transitive
formal relation (which the MinKer chain's own `≡_cap` already is, formally) is not automatically the
correct *semantic* identity relation — this is exactly the gap the MinKer chain's own file 9 already
names by declining to adopt `c=(I_c,O_c,Γ_c,ℐ_c)` as final.

**What remains unspecified, stated precisely**: a test, applicable to any two capability descriptions
regardless of their names or current decomposition, that determines whether they denote the same
semantic contribution to the fixed (but itself still unspecified) KnowledgeOS contract. No such test
exists in the corpus this reconstruction has examined, admissible or newly admitted.
