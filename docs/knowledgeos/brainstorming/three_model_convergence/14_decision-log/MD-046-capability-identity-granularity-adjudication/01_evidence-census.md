# MD-046 §1 — Capability-Identity/Equivalence Evidence Census (Phases A/B)

Method: background mechanical census across `brainstorming/kernel/` (172 files), `reviews/kernel/`
(112 files, session1/session2, admitted narrow scope), `mathematical_ideas_that_can_be_implemented/`
(402 files, excluding a re-read of the already-known 10-file MinKer chain), `nrna1/verification/
zero-algebra/`, `nrna1/research/kernel-reduction/`. Firewalled directories untouched.

## Findings, classified on the required 8-level scale

**`reviews/kernel/session1/S1-F037-primitives-must-not-be-decomposed-and-boundaries-are-boundaries-
of-abstraction.md`** (2026-08-25, four days *before* the MinKer chain, independent research effort):
three corpus-native modelling prohibitions — *"Do not automatically turn every fundamental concept
into an aggregate of lower-level database constructs," "Do not derive the ontology from the
representation format," "Do not solve a primitive concept by renaming it."* Also documents a
**vocabulary-collision registry**: "Kernel" carries 4 senses, "projection" 6 senses, "boundary" a
7th collision. Records three *unreconciled* standing positions on whether Identity is primitive-and-
indefinable, self-so (not assignable), or merely administrative.
**Classification: RESEARCH INTERPRETATION (level 5)** — a real, source-grounded methodological
awareness of the exact class of risk MD-045 found, but a set of prohibitions (what not to do), not a
positive identity criterion.

**`reviews/kernel/session1/S1-F016-aggregate-hypothesis-falsified-atomicity-fails-on-every-pair.md`**:
a genuine falsification methodology — tests pairs of candidate aggregate members (Identity+Evidence,
Identity+Justification, Identity+EpistemicState, Identity+Confidence, Identity+History) for
"transactional atomicity requirement" independent of "semantic relatedness"; every pair scores strong
relatedness / weak atomicity, falsifying a single-aggregate hypothesis. Six independent same-corpus
arrivals at this conclusion logged (2026-08-19 through 2026-08-24).
**Classification: MACHINE-OBSERVABLE FACT (level 3), for a DIFFERENT question than capability
identity** — this tests whether several already-named items should be grouped as one transactional
unit (atomicity), not whether two differently-named descriptions denote the same semantic capability
(identity/equivalence). Related, not sufficient.

**`reviews/kernel/session1/S1-F008-nine-capability-minimum-map-derived-from-existing-law.md`**: a
second, non-overlapping capability enumeration (9 items: admission gate, contract-conformance
enforcement, identity assignment, evidence admission, justification, history recording,
representation-agnostic intake, epistemic-state determination, confidence assignment), claimed
"derived from existing law rather than designed" — but the cited law-artifacts sit outside this
folder, and the claim is recorded, not independently verified by this census.
**Classification: RESEARCH INTERPRETATION (level 5)** — a candidate vocabulary, own provenance
unverified, in direct, recorded, unreconciled tension with `S1-F007`'s "Kernel as god object, six
conflations" finding (already known from MD-042) — one argues the Kernel is too large, the other
implies a minimal 9-item map, pulling in opposite directions, neither adjudicated.

**`mathematical_ideas_that_can_be_implemented/20260901-222111_prompt-kernel-reduction-and-
minimality-experiment.md`** (the kernel-reduction experiment's own design prompt, 3 days before the
MinKer chain, already-admitted narrow-scope evidence): lists "capability decomposition" as one of
five axes to vary in a planned sensitivity/robustness test.
**Classification: DIRECTLY SPECIFIED (level 1, as a design intention) / NECESSARY-BUT-UNSPECIFIED (as
a resolution)** — this is an experimental-design instruction anticipating exactly the sensitivity
MD-045 found, not a criterion that resolves it.

**`reviews/kernel/session2/S2-F007-ab-divergence-may-be-an-ill-typed-comparison.md`**: an adversarial
review finding that an apparent "boundary divergence" between two Kernel proposals may actually be a
**difference in capability decomposition** ("architectural altitude"), making their reported
"intersection" ill-typed rather than a genuine membership agreement.
**Classification: RESEARCH INTERPRETATION (level 5)** — directly corroborates (within the same
`reviews/kernel/` research lineage, not independently) the general risk MD-045 already found from the
MinKer chain's own side: comparing capability sets across different decompositions can produce
false-positive or false-negative agreement.

Qualify, Hypothesize, DetectGap, Challenge, Validate, Revise, Determine, Select`) appears in a
kernel-capability context anywhere in `brainstorming/kernel/` or `reviews/kernel/` — a genuine
structural fact, not an absence of search effort.** This reconstruction's own separately-developed
kernel/capability research track (`reviews/kernel/`, `brainstorming/kernel/`) uses **entirely
different, non-overlapping vocabulary** (the 9-item "existing law" map; the Identity/Evidence/
Justification/EpistemicState/Confidence/History aggregate-member list) with **no name-level overlap
whatsoever** with the MinKer chain's 13 names, and **no place was found anywhere cross-referencing
the two.** No place discusses whether any of the 13 MinKer names might overlap with each other or
with this other vocabulary; no place discusses the atomicity of any of the 13 MinKer names
specifically.
