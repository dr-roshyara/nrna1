# MD-030 Claim Audit

MD-030's five central claims, each re-examined against this study's new evidence — not accepted by
default.

## A — "The executable rule supplies the information MD-029 was missing."

**Holds, precisely as MD-030 itself qualified it.** MD-030 (`08`) already distinguished "supplies
in the narrow technical sense" from "closes the gap in the sense that matters" (untracked, self-
declared experimental, internally contested) — that qualification stands unchanged. **New context**:
this study finds the *same* information is *also* independently stated (common-cause, not
independent-confirmation — `06`/`11`) in narrative-lane text with materially better provenance. So
Claim A's underlying fact (the executable lane does encode this information) was always true as
*machine encoding*; it is now also true, via a separate chain, as *narrative specification*
(`08`) — a stronger status than MD-030 could establish on its own evidence.

## B — "The executable lane is inadmissible."

**Holds, unchanged.** Nothing in this study's evidence bears on the executable lane's own git-
tracking status (still zero), self-declared experimental status (still `[EXP]`), or internal
contestation (`V6` still exists, still untouched by `06`'s own silence on it — `07`). MD-030's
inadmissibility finding was about *that specific directory's own provenance*, not about whether its
content happens to be corroborated elsewhere — this study adds corroboration, not provenance.

## C — "The executable lane contains an internally contested alternative."

**Holds, unchanged and independently re-confirmed.** `07` re-examined `V6` directly against `06` and
found no resolution — `06` neither engages with nor rules out the alternative. `V6` targets the
identical derivation slot (the `warrant-assessment`/`Verdict` step) with a materially different input
set (`+Defeater`) — a genuine semantic alternative, not a cosmetic variant, confirmed again here.

## D — "No MD-024–029 finding depends on the executable lane."

**Holds, unchanged.** `06-composition-rules.md` was equally unadmitted and unread by every one of
MD-024–029 (it is narrative-lane material, distinct from what MD-030 characterized); reading it now
does not retroactively create a dependency in frozen prior work. No frozen artifact is touched by
this finding.

## E — "The executable lane is potentially necessary."

**Narrowed, not simply upheld.** This is the one claim this study's own evidence meaningfully revises.
For the *specific* field MD-030 was assessing (`Validate`'s input carriers), the executable lane is
**no longer the only, or even the best-provenanced, candidate source** — `06` (narrative, same
git-provenance tier as the two already-admitted files) supplies the identical content via a
narrative-only chain (`03`/`08`). A future admission decision aimed at closing *that specific gap*
could reasonably target `06` alone, without needing the executable lane at all. **The executable lane
remains relevant and non-redundant for two things `06` does not supply**: the *computed* C0-vs-
`C0_plus` reachability consequence (`baseline.json`, `minimal_kernels.json` — MD-030 `05`) and the
existence of the `V6` alternative itself (`06` says nothing about it — `07`). For those two items,
"potentially necessary" still holds; for the input-carrier specification itself, it is now better
described as "relevant but no longer the sole candidate — `06` is available as an alternative,
better-provenanced source for that specific field."

## Net effect

Four of five MD-030 claims (A, B, C, D) are confirmed unchanged by this study's new evidence. One (E)
is narrowed: the executable lane's necessity is now specific to the computed/robustness findings it
alone provides, not to the input-carrier specification itself, for which `06` is now the stronger
candidate.
