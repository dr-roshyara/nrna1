## New objects discovered, opened as TheoryState trajectories

1. **`(Q,𝒦)` — Knowledge Space Theory's own formal apparatus** (Doignon & Falmagne, externally and
   verifiably cited) — tracked as **reported external-source content**, explicitly not adopted as this
   reconstruction's own object; the corpus's own discipline (which this phase confirms is followed
   faithfully here) treats it the same way: "KST gives us a rigorous framework for one class of
   knowledge-state modelling problems... not a complete framework for KnowledgeOS."
2. **`κ(K_t)` — the minimal-representation/Kernel function**, and its associated recognition predicate
   `Φ`. Born `225609`. Status: unresolved, explicitly named as the segment's own central open question
   (what are the necessary/sufficient characteristics, and what is the smallest unit preserving them).
3. **`Knowledge Trajectory` (`K_{≤t}`)**, replacing "Knowledge Lifecycle" — born `225949`, explicitly
   chosen to avoid implying a software-artifact-style created→active→obsolete→deleted sequence.
4. **The "intersection method" for Kernel candidacy** (`Kernel Candidate ≈ ⋂_{R∈Regimes}
   Requirements(R)`) — born in the Pritchard-extraction content (`222915` part 2), explicitly paired
   with a caveat that the Zero-Lens/removal test is still separately required (something absent from
   all current regimes might nonetheless be fundamental).
