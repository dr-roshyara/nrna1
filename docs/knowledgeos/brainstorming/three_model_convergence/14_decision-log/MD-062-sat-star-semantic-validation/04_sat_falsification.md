## Summary

**Four genuine counterexamples (E1/E2/E3/E5), one non-defect (E4), one pass (E6), two neutral
findings (E7/E8).** No case shows `Sat*` returning a *wrong* answer against a stated ground truth —
every counterexample shows a **missing distinction**, not an **incorrect** one. This is the basis for
distinguishing Gate C from Gate D in `07`.
