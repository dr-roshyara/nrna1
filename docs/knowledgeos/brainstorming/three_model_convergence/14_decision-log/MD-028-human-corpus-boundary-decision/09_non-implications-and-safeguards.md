# Non-Implications and Safeguards (mandatory)

A **YES** decision on DQ-1 does **NOT** mean, and must never be read to mean:

- Model-B membership established.
- Historical authorship established.
- Provenance upgraded from `RECONSTRUCTED PROVENANCE` to any stronger evidence level.
- Mathematical correctness, well-definedness, or closure established for any admitted content.
- Equivalence, identity, or convergence established between any two models or Kernel candidates.
- Canonical status established for any admitted document.
- A model selected.
- Stage 07 (formalization) opened.
- K-1/K-2 governance changed in any way.
- Any governance authority created retroactively — the authority gap named in `07` remains exactly as
  open after a YES decision as before it, unless separately and explicitly resolved.
- Automatic authorization of the composition retest named in `06` — that remains its own, separate,
  future authorization even if DQ-1 is answered YES.

A **NO** decision does **NOT** mean the underlying provenance connection (M0030's own citation) is
false — only that this reconstruction will not use the material, regardless of whether the connection
is true.

A **DEFER** decision does **NOT** authorize interim use of the material under any option while deferred.
