# Mathematical and Research Implications

## What admission would make possible

If DQ-1 is answered YES (Option B or C), the previously missing operator-contract evidence
(input/output types, preconditions, postconditions, per-operator invariants — `04-operator-
contracts.md`'s own content) would become admissible for a **future, separately authorized**
specification-sufficiency/composition experiment — specifically, a retest of MD-024's own Pair 1 or
Pair 2, which both stalled precisely on the absence of this material.

## What admission would NOT prove, mathematically

Admission is a provenance/governance act. It performs **no mathematical proof** of:

- well-definedness of any operator under the newly-admitted contracts;
- closure under composition;
- invariant preservation;
- equivalence or identity between any two Kernel candidates;
- canonicality of the atom vocabulary or the contract table;
- uniqueness of any minimal kernel;
- correctness of the `research/kernel-reduction/` lane's own conclusions (several of which, per MD-026
  `04`, were already superseded by that lane's own later work);
- convergence, in any form, between Model B and any other model.

**This document does not run, and does not authorize, that future experiment.** It only names what
becomes possible, not what would be found.
