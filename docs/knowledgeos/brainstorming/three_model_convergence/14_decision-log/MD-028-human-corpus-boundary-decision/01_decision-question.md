# The Decision Question

**DQ-1:** *May `docs/knowledgeos/research/kernel-reduction/` be admitted as an admissible
Model-B-adjacent evidence lane, for the specific and limited purpose of resolving the operator-
specification gap MD-024 identified and MD-025 located — without that admission being read as an
established finding that this material was historically part of Model B, that it was authored as
Model B's own reconstruction output, or that its research content is thereby validated, correct, or
canonical?*

This question concerns **admissibility only**. It does not ask, and this package does not attempt to
answer, whether `kernel-reduction/` genuinely *was* Model B's historical output — the evidence
(`02`/`03`) supports a plausible but reconstructed connection, not a documented one, and no amount of
governance decision changes that evidentiary fact. What governance can decide is whether this
reconstruction may *use* the material going forward, under an explicitly bounded, explicitly labeled
provenance status.
