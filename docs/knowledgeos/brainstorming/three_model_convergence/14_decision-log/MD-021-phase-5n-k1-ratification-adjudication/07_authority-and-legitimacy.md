# Phase 5N — Authority and Legitimacy (re-tested specifically for D-FA-6/D-FA-4, per the authorization's §13)

## The six required sub-questions

1. **Does the document claim HPA has authority?** **Yes** — seq 0764's own header: "Authority: HPA
   (Highest Project Authority)."
2. **Does the corpus establish the scope of that authority?** **Partially** — seq 0764's own preamble
   states it is ruling on "the FA-9 Ratification Packet," suggesting a scope limited to that packet's
   own 7 determinations plus 2 riders — not an unlimited, general authority over all KnowledgeOS
   matters.
3. **Does another source corroborate it?** **Yes, functionally** — seq 0927 (the governance-decision
   protocol document, Phase 5K/5L) and multiple `phase_measure_theory/` documents (Phase 5K's own `03`
   finding: ~15 files) consistently treat HPA as the acting reviewing/ruling entity — a *pattern* of
   corroboration, not an *independent charter*.
4. **Does the governance protocol establish how authority becomes legitimate?** **Yes, in the abstract**
   — seq 0927's own `Authority(a,scope)` vs. `LegitimateAuthority(a,scope,source,validity)` distinction,
   and its own Authority Chain formula (`ValidRatification(d) ⟺ Mandate(a) ∧ ...`) — but this is a
   *definitional* framework, not itself a grant of legitimacy to HPA.
5. **Is there an independent source establishing HPA's organizational legitimacy?** **No** — re-searched
   this phase (`03`), no charter, mandate, or delegation instrument found anywhere in the corpus.
6. **If not, what governance status can nevertheless be assigned to the document's own act?** **A
   genuine, internally consistent, corpus-native governance act — self-authorized, not externally
   validated.** This is neither dismissed as meaningless (per the authorization's own caution against
   automatically invalidating an internal act for lack of external legitimacy) nor treated as
   externally binding (per the same caution against the reverse error).

## Verdict

**D-FA-6 and D-FA-4 are genuine governance acts within the corpus's own internal, self-consistent
governance practice.** Their *content* (what they actually ratify or leave open) is not in doubt —
re-confirmed directly from the primary text (`04`). Their *external, organizational* authority remains
unestablished — unchanged from Phase 5J/5L/5M, now specifically re-tested against these two
determinations rather than restated generically.
