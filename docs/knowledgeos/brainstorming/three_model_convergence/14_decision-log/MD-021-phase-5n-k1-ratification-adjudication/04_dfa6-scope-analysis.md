# Phase 5N — D-FA-6 Scope Analysis (this phase's central finding)

## Full text, re-quoted verbatim (seq 0764, lines 70–77)

> ### D-FA-6 — K_t naming register
>
> **RULING:** ACCEPT
>
> **Reasoning:** Qualified naming (formal `K_t` / `ALT-09` [H] / L3 engineering usage) is adopted.
> This resolves the ambiguity in the concept-evolution record without introducing contradictions. No
> formal object changes; this is a terminology policy.
>
> **Consequence:** The naming register is authoritative. Future usage must qualify according to the
> register.

## A. What is the object of the ruling?

**A naming/terminology register** — explicitly, in the document's own words: *"this is a terminology
policy."* Not the 8-primitive structure itself.

## B. What does "ACCEPT" mean, per the document's own vocabulary?

Cross-checked against the other 6 `D-FA` rulings (all read in full, Phase 5H): every `ACCEPT` in this
document is paired with a `Reasoning` section stating what specifically is accepted and a `Consequence`
section stating what becomes authoritative. **"ACCEPT" is used consistently to mean "the specific
proposition named in the Reasoning/Consequence sections becomes authoritative"** — it is not a blanket
endorsement of everything associated with the topic heading.

## C. Smallest proposition unquestionably established

**"The term `K_t` is the qualified, authoritative name to use going forward, among the competing
labels `K_t`/`ALT-09`/`L3 engineering usage`, for whatever object those labels already informally
denoted."** This is a naming-convention ruling.

## D. What is explicitly NOT established, per the document's own words

**"No formal object changes."** This is the single most direct, unambiguous piece of evidence in this
entire investigation: **the ruling's own text explicitly disclaims making any change to, or
ratification of, the object itself** — only its name.

## The companion finding: D-FA-4's own treatment of the object

D-FA-6 addresses *naming*. **D-FA-4 is the determination that actually addresses the 8-primitive
kernel's own standing as an object**:

> ### D-FA-4 — Three kernel traditions
>
> **RULING:** ACCEPT
>
> **Reasoning:** The layered structure (11 articles = L1; **M₄₉ = L2 candidate**; 8 formulations =
> [H]) correctly preserves the three traditions without forcing unification. **Membership at the
> object level remains open (OQ-2).** This is the correct epistemic posture — no CONTRADICTS exists
> between the three streams.
>
> **Consequence:** The kernel traditions are layered, not merged. **OQ-2 remains open.**

**This is the ruling that bears directly on whether the 8-primitive object itself (M₄₉, per `02`'s
own reconstructed identity) is ratified.** Its own verdict: M₄₉ sits at **"L2 candidate"** tier —
below the ratified "L1" state model (which D-FA-1 separately, and explicitly, closes: *"The state
model is formally closed at L1"* — but D-FA-1's own "state model" concerns the epistemic-status
ladder/admission-axis, a **different construct** from M₄₉'s own Entity/State/Event/... primitive
tuple, per direct comparison of the two rulings' own subject matter). **D-FA-4 itself explicitly
leaves "membership at the object level... open (OQ-2)."**

## Verdict

**No ruling in seq 0764 ratifies K-1's own 8-primitive object.** D-FA-6 ratifies its eventual name
only, and says so in its own words. D-FA-4 classifies the object itself as an open, candidate-tier
item, and says so in its own words, via an explicitly-retained open question (OQ-2).
