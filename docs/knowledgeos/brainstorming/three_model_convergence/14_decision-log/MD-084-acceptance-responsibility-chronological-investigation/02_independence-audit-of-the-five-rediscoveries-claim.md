# MD-084 §02 — Independence Audit: MD-082's "Five Independent Rediscoveries" Claim

MD-082 §03 stated: *"Every genuine formal-object candidate found for the acceptance/sufficiency
responsibility... lands in the same cell... independently rediscovered at least five times across at
least four different lanes, by non-cross-citing authors/sessions."* Per this mission's own explicit
instruction, this claim is audited rather than accepted.

## Finding: `Admissible`/`NG-1` and the qualification-rule/`EG-2` are not the same document or session

Verified directly this phase: `05-NEW-GAPS.md` (2026-09-02 22:40) sits inside a dated addendum
subdirectory, `gap-update-2026-09-02/`; `08-EVIDENCE-GAP.md` (2026-08-30 20:58) sits in the base
`gap-discovery/` directory, three days earlier. Neither cites the other — a direct grep for
`"NEW-GAPS"`/`"NG-1"`/`"Admissible"` inside `08-EVIDENCE-GAP.md`, and for `"EVIDENCE-GAP"`/`"EG-2"`/
`"qualification"` inside `05-NEW-GAPS.md`, returned zero hits either direction.

**But they are not fully independent either.** Both sit inside the same continuing governed audit
initiative (`verification/gap-discovery/`, the same overall programme this reconstruction has cited
repeatedly — e.g. `16-MASTER-GAP-REGISTER.md`, `CANONICAL-UBIQUITOUS-LANGUAGE.md`, both already found
by MD-081 to cross-reference the qualification-rule finding by its own `G-14` gap ID). This is
**common-lineage repetition within one continuing verification programme** — two separately-dated
observations, plausibly by the same overall author/methodology, not two independently-authored
discoveries in the strong sense the "independent replication" language implies.

## Corrected lineage count

Collapsing `Admissible` and the qualification rule into one lineage (the verification lane's own
gap-discovery programme, which happens to have produced two dated, non-cross-citing but methodologically
continuous observations of the same underlying pattern), the corrected count is **four genuinely
distinct lineages**, not five:

1. The `Warrant`/"surviving a defeater" thread (its own dedicated document series).
2. The `Assessment` thread (external-literature-extraction files) — and see below, a further
   cross-check with (1).
3. The verification lane's own `gap-discovery` programme (containing both `Admissible`/`NG-1` and the
   qualification-rule/`EG-2` as two dated sub-observations).
4. T21's own `Policy_Det` (Part VI §6.43).

**A further cross-check, surfacing a genuine relationship between two of these four**: this
reconstruction's own much earlier work (MD-040, pre-dating MD-067, already frozen) found that the
`Warrant` thread itself has "a second, independent-but-connected research thread (M0032/33/36, a
Titelbaum-epistemology-derived cluster) that also proposes `Warrant` as a named-but-undefined tuple
component — and directly names/engages the kernel experiment by its own ID, confirming the two threads
are in conversation, not independent confirmation." Since MD-080's own `Assessment` finding is *also*
Titelbaum-derived (the `20260901-233713_titelbaum-for-theory-discovery-explicit-epistemic-assessment-
state.md` file), lineages (1) and (2) above may themselves share a common root in the corpus's own
Titelbaum-literature-extraction cluster — **not re-verified to full certainty this phase** (would
require re-opening MD-040's own specific M0032/33/36 files and cross-checking their exact relationship
to the `Assessment`-bearing files MD-080 found), but flagged explicitly as a plausible further
collapse, rather than left as an implicit assumption of independence.

## Corrected classification

**Not `independent replication`.** The correct classification, per the mission's own §10 vocabulary, is:
**`common-lineage repetition within one continuing verification programme`** for lineages (3)'s own two
sub-observations, and **`possibly common-lineage, not independently re-verified`** for the relationship
between lineages (1) and (2). Only the relationship between `{Warrant, Assessment}` taken together,
`{the verification lane's gap-discovery programme}`, and `{T21's own Policy_Det}` is confirmed
genuinely independent (different lanes, different document series, no cross-citation, different
authorship contexts as far as this reconstruction can determine).

## What this does and does not change

**Does not weaken the underlying finding**: even at the corrected count of three-to-four genuinely
distinct lineages (rather than five), every one still independently lands in the same cell — `defined`,
not `computable`, not `empirically validated` — for the acceptance/sufficiency responsibility. The
consistency of the *outcome* across genuinely distinct lineages is real and still evidentially
meaningful, just should be stated at its correct, more conservative count. **Does change**: MD-082's
own specific phrase "at least five times" is corrected forward to "three to four genuinely distinct
lineages, with one further plausible but unverified common-root relationship" — MD-082's own text not
edited.
