# Final Verdict

## Primary verdict

**C — SOURCE-GROUNDED MOTIVATION FOUND, BUT NO FORMAL DEFINITION.**

## Why C, and not the other four

- **Not A/B (definition found, full or partial)** — no document, across the entire 17-file, 2,043-
  line population, states what distinguishes "surviving" a defeater from not surviving one in
  operational terms (`04`). Not even a partial predicate is given beyond the input-carrier
  requirement already known from `12` alone.
- **Not D (no definition found)**, chosen as the *bare* alternative — this would undersell what was
  actually found. The population contains substantial, repeated, source-grounded *motivation*: two
  explicit open-question entries (`17` Q-6/Q-7), two falsifiers explicitly framed as "real" and
  "open" (`15` F-9/F-10), a direct audit-response clarification of V6's own evidentiary role (`18`
  §3.3), a named (if different) invariant (`18` §5.3's `I9`), and a domain-rationale aphorism (`13`).
  **C captures this richness; D would flatten it into a bare negative.**
- **Not E (search scope insufficient)** — the search was comprehensive: every remaining file in the
  series, read in full, not sampled, with every relevant passage quoted and classified (`02`/`03`).
  The scope was more than sufficient to support a confident negative-for-definition,
  positive-for-motivation finding.

## Necessary admissibility caveat, restated per this study's own scope discipline

The population this verdict is based on is **not currently admitted** (`01`). This verdict is a
characterization finding about what a future admission decision would be admitting, not an
adjudicated conclusion resting on already-admitted evidence — exactly the distinction MD-030 and
MD-034 already modeled for their own respective discoveries.
