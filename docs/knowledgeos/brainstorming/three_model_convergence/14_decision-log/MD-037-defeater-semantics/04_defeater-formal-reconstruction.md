## What this study explicitly does NOT invent, per the authorization's own binding list

- **The meaning of `Defeater`** — left exactly as `04`'s own atom table and `06`'s own derivation
  table state it (a carrier produced from `Claim`/`Hypothesis` via `adversarial-negation`); no
  further content is added by anything found in the wider series.
- **What it means to "survive"** — genuinely undefined everywhere searched. No passage in any of the
  17 files distinguishes defeating, rebutting, answering, neutralizing, or merely processing a
  challenge.
- **Whether one or multiple defeaters are possible** — not addressed anywhere.
- **Whether a surviving defeater is necessary or sufficient for a `Verdict`** — necessity is asserted
  (under `V6`); sufficiency is never addressed.

## Net formal status

**"Surviving a defeater" remains an undefined term used consistently, but never formally specified,
across every document in the series that uses it** — `12`, `15`, `17`, `18` all use the identical
phrase or its close variants without ever cashing it out operationally beyond the input-carrier
requirement MD-036 already reconstructed from `12` alone.
