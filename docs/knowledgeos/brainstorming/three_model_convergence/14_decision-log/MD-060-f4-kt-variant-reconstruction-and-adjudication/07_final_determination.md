# MD-060 §07 — Final Determination

## Gate

### Gate B — `Sat(K_t,r)` cannot yet be instantiated, decomposition-independently or otherwise.

**Reasoning, at the strength the evidence supports**: reconciling the `K_t` variant family
(MD-059's own originally-proposed next step) is real, necessary work — this phase performed it,
found 12 distinct formulations, one conditional formal equivalence (V1↔V3), two same-document
structural correspondences (V2a↔V2b, V6a↔V6b), and one demonstrated cross-cluster incompatibility
(V1/V3↔V7's `Σ_t`). **But `G-K3` (`06`) shows this reconciliation, however far it were carried, would
not by itself make `Sat` computable** — no variant in the entire census, including the two that ship
with their own `Sat`-shaped signature, has ever been given a body. **Gate B holds independently of
`K_t`-variant reconciliation's own progress.**

## Smallest remaining research input

**A concrete body for `Sat(K_t,r)`, constructed for at least one `K_t` variant (any one — reconciling
the family first is not a precondition, per `G-K3`).** This is narrower and more directly actionable
than MD-059's own "reconcile the 9+ variants" framing, and it is now the corpus's own most literal
gap: two independent sources (M0043, M0048) each write `Sat(K,r)` as a symbol and neither supplies
what it computes.

## Correction to MD-059's own framing, disclosed (MD-059's own text NOT edited)

MD-059 identified "a decomposition-independent body for `Sat`, blocked on `K_t`'s own unresolved
component semantics" as the smallest missing primitive — correct as far as it went, but this phase's
broader primary-source sweep shows the blocker is **more general**: `Sat`'s body is missing
independent of which `K_t` representation is used, including the deliberately abstract V4a and the
independently-proposed V5. **Reconciling the variant family remains valuable** (it resolves `G-K1`/
`G-K2`/`G-K4`/`G-K5`, real open items in their own right) **but is not, on its own, the path to Gate
A** — a correction worth recording precisely, since treating "reconcile the variants" as the whole
answer would have quietly converted a documentary/typing gap into the wrong single blocker.
