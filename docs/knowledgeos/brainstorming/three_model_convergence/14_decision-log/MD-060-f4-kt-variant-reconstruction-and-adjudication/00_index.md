## 3 · Central result

**The corpus supplies a genuinely richer variant population than Model B's own register counted**
(12 distinct formulations once same-document internal variants are counted individually, against the
register's own "9+" — a disclosed refinement, not a contradiction, of `03_model-b_mathematical/
02_concept-register.md` §G/UE-1). **No corpus-internal act establishes equivalence across the
family.** One genuine, demonstrable exception exists (M0001 vs M0009, both probabilistic — a
conditional formal equivalence, detailed in `02`). **The semantic-core hypothesis is partially
falsified**: a weak common core survives (time-indexing, purpose-relative epistemic-state
referent); a strong common core (mutual information-preserving inter-translatability) is falsified by
a concrete, demonstrated obstruction between the probabilistic cluster and the categorical
multi-component cluster (`04`).

**The most consequential finding, refining MD-059's own framing**: `Sat(K_t,r)`'s own SIGNATURE is
stated relative to the deliberately abstract `K_t∈𝕂` (M0043) — not tied to any specific tuple — and a
*second*, independent source (M0048) proposes its own tuple (5-component) alongside its own `Sat(K,r)`
signature, **also without a body**. **This generalizes MD-059's own diagnosis**: the blocker is not
"reconcile the variants and `Sat` becomes computable" — it is that **no variant, of any arity or
structural type, has ever been given a concrete `Sat` body anywhere in this evidence base.**
Reconciling the `K_t` family is therefore **necessary but not sufficient** for Gate A.
