## Is "`Sat` collapse" requirement satisfaction, proposition support, or another relation?

**Genuinely ambiguous from the text itself.** Two readings, both defensible:

1. **`Sat` here is M0043's own `Sat(K_t,r)`**, and `value∘Eval_c` names a specific *(unspecified
   elsewhere)* candidate computation for it that loses a "Reason" distinction — consistent with the
   table's own willingness to mix `K_t`-native concepts (the `K` row) into the same list.
2. **`Sat` here is a general/informal "satisfaction of a claim by evidence" notion**, closer in kind
   to `Standing(p)`'s own support evaluation, sitting among the table's other Contr/Zero-family rows
   (`Zero`, `Gap`, `Balanced`, `U`) — consistent with `§8.5`'s own *"E. CONTRADICTION SHOULD BE
   REPRESENTED OUTSIDE `Sat`"* verdict option, which places `Sat` squarely inside the
   contradiction-evaluation discussion.

**Neither reading is favored by direct textual evidence strong enough to exclude the other.**
