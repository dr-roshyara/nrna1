## Explicit statement: do not force A

Per the mission's own instruction, no single verdict is given for "the whole family." The evidence
genuinely supports **A for the derived/aggregation layer** (`Req`'s shape, `Δ`/`Zero`/`Det` given `Sat`
values, the `Determination⇏Decision` separation), **D for the base-case computation layer** (`Det_r`'s
own body, `EvalReq`'s own body, `standard`/`EC.Rules`, `AcceptanceCondition`), **C for the contract/
codomain layer** (`EC`, `𝕊_sat`/`𝒮_sat`, the 2-arg/3-arg `Sat` split), **E for the object-identity layer**
(`r`, `Γ`, bare `Eval`), and **B for one genuinely strong but non-identical alternative construction**
(`Sat_c`/`Eval_c`, and `kos/inquiry.py`'s own working `Sat`). This is a materially richer and more
precise picture than MD-076/077's own single Classification C for "the `Det_r`/`EvalReq` chain" —
neither MD-076 nor MD-077 is overturned; both remain correct for the specific narrow object they
addressed (`Det_r`/`EvalReq`'s own body, and the absence of later corpus-native resolution of that
specific gap). This phase's own broader finding is that the *surrounding* family is not simply "less
developed" than that narrow gap suggested — it is differently, and in most cases more severely,
unresolved (proliferation, not absence, for `r`/`Γ`/`EC`), while a few specific derived results
(`Req`'s shape, the separation principle, the aggregation logic) are genuinely, robustly complete.
