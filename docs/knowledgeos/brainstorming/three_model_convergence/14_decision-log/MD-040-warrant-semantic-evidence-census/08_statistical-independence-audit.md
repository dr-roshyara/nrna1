## The corrected discipline, applied to this study's own central discovery

Thread 1 (kernel-reduction) and Thread 2 (`M0036`/Titelbaum) are **not** independent evidence for
the "Warrant is undefined" finding, despite arriving at their vocabularies (atoms/derivation-rules vs.
tuples/pipelines) separately. **`M0036` itself directly names and discusses the kernel experiment

in conversation, not two blind, unconnected research efforts. **Classification: corroboration within

responding to the other.** This is a stronger connection than the "same author-layer" finding already
established for the kernel-reduction lane's own internal documents (MD-031/037) — here it is
explicit textual cross-reference, not merely shared authorship inference.

## What this means for the evidentiary weight of the combined finding

**The fact that both threads independently propose `Warrant` as a named-but-undefined component is
not two data points — it is one thread's own extension of, and engagement with, the other's already-
identified gap.** `M0036`'s own text treats the kernel experiment's `Qualify` discovery as a starting
point and explicitly reframes the whole kernel question around the missing `Assessment`/`Warrant`
layer — i.e., `M0036` may be *reacting to* an already-felt gap in the kernel-reduction thread, not
independently rediscovering it. This does not weaken the finding (the gap is real either way) — it
means the finding should be reported as **one connected line of research recognizing the same open
problem twice**, not as independently-replicated evidence.
