# Phase 6 — Adjudications and Contradictions

## The Kernel case, treated with full strictness

Four independently-produced Kernel-candidate families now exist across the whole reconstruction:
Model A's four-way `unresolved_equivalence` family; Model B's tested, representation-dependent
cardinality-8 minimal-kernel family; Model C1's eight-way `unresolved_equivalence` family (UE-1, the
single largest such family found anywhere in this reconstruction); and Model C2's single, untested
candidate. **No two of these four families are shown structure-preserving-equivalent, in whole or in
specific pairs, anywhere in this reconstruction's own evidence.** The one specific candidate
correspondence that looked promising on naming grounds (A's seq 0219 citing something called "K-1,"
matching C1's own canonical "K-1" name) was checked directly against raw source and **does not hold** —
0219's own text contains no such citation; the apparent link exists only in a reconstruction-layer
synthesis, one step removed from source.

**Explicit distinction from the K-1/K-2 Kernel-object family (Phase 5A–5N, out of scope here).** This
phase's own authorization requires this stated plainly: the "K-1" label used within Model C1's own
register (§L, `KnowledgeAggregate+ConflictRecord+VerificationPort`, sourced from `kernel/` seq
0165/0167) is a **different, unrelated object** from the "K-1" label used throughout Phase 5A–5N (the
8-primitive tuple `𝒦=(E,S,T,O,P,R,Π,A)`, sourced from `phase_measure_theory/` seq 0630). Both labels
happen to be "K-1," produced independently, in different corpus lineages, at different times, for
different structures. **This is itself a genuine, corpus-wide naming collision, worth recording as a
finding in its own right** — not resolved, adjudicated, or even compared here, since doing so would
require reopening the frozen 5A–5N sub-sequence, explicitly out of scope for this phase.

## Representation-dependence check (required for any row reaching STRUCTURAL CORRESPONDENCE or above)

**No row in `02_extended-correspondence-matrix.md` reaches STRUCTURAL CORRESPONDENCE or above** — the
strongest classification reached is PARTIAL CORRESPONDENCE (Rows 1, 4, 5, 6) and FUNCTIONAL ANALOGY
(Row 7). Per the authorization's own instruction, the representation-dependence check therefore applies
formally to none of them, but is worth stating as a standing caution for any future phase that might
attempt to strengthen Row 4 (State: C1↔B) past its current level: Model B's own evidence already proves
kernel minimality is representation-dependent (P-2, ESTABLISHED); the same caution would apply to any
future claim that C1's and B's `K_t` variants are "the same" merely because a chosen representation
makes them look alike — the shared *vocabulary* (Row 4's actual finding) is not, by itself, evidence of
shared *formal content* under any specific representation.

## Contradictions found, kept explicit

**No new genuine contradiction between C1/C2 and A/B was found** — every candidate correspondence
examined settled at PARTIAL CORRESPONDENCE, FUNCTIONAL ANALOGY, or UNRESOLVED, none at INCOMPATIBLE.
This differs from Phase 3's own A↔B pass, which found three INCOMPATIBLE rows (Invariant, Equivalence,
Knowledge/epistemic-status value-set). The absence of a new INCOMPATIBLE finding here is itself
reported honestly: it reflects that C1/C2's own evidence, where it has a counterpart at all, tends to
sit at a *categorical* or *notational* level of similarity to A/B rather than making a specific,
falsifiable claim sharp enough to be shown incompatible — a weaker, not a stronger, evidentiary
position than Phase 3's own A↔B rows in several cases.

## Two internal C1-population findings surfaced by this extension, not previously stated this way

1. **C1's own §L (8-way kernel proliferation, mostly untested) and B's own §L (the four-way
   conflation diagnosis) independently diagnose the same underlying failure mode** — repeated
   redefinition of "Kernel" relative to adjacent concepts, without a standing rule preventing
   recurrence. Recorded in the non-convergence document as a shared methodological finding, not a
   domain correspondence (per the same discipline Phase 3 applied to its own "production-time
   self-correction" parallel).
2. **The `kernel/` directory is confirmed, by the Row 1 raw-source check, to be a genuine
   cross-classification boundary region** — files there (0165, 0219) carry both `gita`-primary and
   `c1`-secondary tags. This is consistent with, and now directly evidenced for, Phase 4's own
   `04_boundary-observations.md` finding that classification boundaries in this corpus do not track a
   clean content split. Not a reclassification recommendation — an observation, per the standing
   guardrail every phase in this reconstruction has honored.
