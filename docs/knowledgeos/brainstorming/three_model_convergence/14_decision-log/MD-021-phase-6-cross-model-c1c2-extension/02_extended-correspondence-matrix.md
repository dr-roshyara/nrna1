**Proposed relationship type (surface):** both are unresolved, multi-candidate families of *typed
domain-object* (aggregate/tuple) Kernel proposals, unlike Model B's operator-set family — a categorical
match, not a content match.

**Raw-source check performed** (consequential/specific claim): does A's own seq 0219 (which cites "K-1"
in its per-file synthesis alongside 0216 and Kernel Candidate v0.2/v0.3) actually cite C1's own K-1
(seq 0165/0167, `KnowledgeAggregate+ConflictRecord+VerificationPort`)? **No.** 0219's raw text
(`kernel/20260824-112051-...md`, 449 lines) contains neither "K-1," "KnowledgeAggregate," nor any
citation to seq 0165 or 0167 anywhere. The comparison exists only in the reconstruction's own per-file
synthesis layer (`01_source-analysis/per-file/0219.yaml`), one interpretive step removed from source —
it is a reconstruction-layer juxtaposition, not a source-stated citation. **This specific candidate
correspondence does not hold at any level stronger than coincidental naming.**

**Evidence for (categorical level only):** both families are dominated by DDD-style aggregate/tuple
proposals (nouns: what objects exist), never operator sets (verbs: what actions are performed) —
matching the exact categorical split already found between A and B in Phase 3's own Row 1.

**Evidence against:** no specific A candidate is shown identical, structurally corresponding, or even
cited by, any specific C1 candidate, once the one plausible-looking naming match is checked and fails.
Both families remain internally unreconciled on their own terms (UE-1's 8-way, §F's 4-way).

**Preserved differences:** A's candidates trace to `kernel/` files classified `gita`-primary
(0219, and also 0165, both carrying `c1` as a *secondary* tag — see boundary note below); C1's
canonical K-1 statement (0167) is `engineering_knowledgeos`-primary with no secondary tag. The
`kernel/` directory itself is confirmed, by this check, to be a genuine cross-classification boundary
region — several of its files (0165, 0219) carry both `gita` and `c1` tags — not cleanly separable into
"Model A territory" and "Model C1 territory."

**Required assumptions:** none available to advance past the categorical-level finding.

**Adjudication status:** **PARTIAL CORRESPONDENCE** at the categorical/representational level (both
propose the same *kind* of Kernel object); **UNRESOLVED** at the specific-candidate level, with the one
promising-looking lead (K-1 naming) explicitly checked and found not to hold.

**Confidence:** Moderate for the categorical claim (now a third independent data point after A and B's
own categorical split); none for any stronger candidate-level claim.

a positive correspondence.

## Row 4 — State: C1 ↔ B

**C1 concept/structure:** the `phase_measure_theory/` `K_t`/`Δ_t` proliferation (§F, seq 0446–0492,
main corpus) — six distinct component-count formulations of `K_t`, `Δ_t` ("Discrepancy," renamed after
a non-symmetry proof), the transition `K_{t+1}=δ(K_t,e_t)`, and at least five self-caught
circularity/consistency repairs within the same arc.

**B concept/structure:** the `K_t`/`I_t`/`Δ_t` triad (§A, math lane, M0001–M0132) — nine+ independently
motivated tuple structures, `Δ_t` eventually frozen as `Δ_t={r∈R_t:Sat(K_t,r)=0}`.

**Source references:** C1 — `04_model-c_kernel-ddd/02_concept-register.md` §F. B —
`03_model-b_mathematical/02_concept-register.md` §A, §G.

**Raw-source check performed:** does either lineage cite the other? Checked both directions — C1's
`phase_measure_theory/` per-file records (0446, 0469, 0481) contain no reference to any `M0xxx` file;
a search of the math-lane per-file records for the specific seq numbers 0446/0461/0464/0469/0479/0480/
0481 returns only coincidental digit matches in unrelated file paths (M0057, M0330), not citations.
**No citation link exists in either direction.** These are two independently-numbered, non-cross-
referencing corpus lineages (main-corpus `engineering_knowledgeos` vs. math-lane) that both,
independently, converge on the identical variable names `K_t` and `Δ_t`, and both exhibit the identical
research-process signature: multiple superseding component-count revisions, each caught and repaired
by an in-arc self-audit rather than an external test.

**Evidence for:** this is a stronger finding than the A↔B "State" row Phase 3 already recorded (Row 2,
that document) — Phase 3's own A↔B pairing found *comparable proliferation pattern* with *no shared
vocabulary* (Model A's components are Region/Method/Identity-type; Model B's are Requirement/Assessment/
History-type). Here, by contrast, the vocabulary itself — `K_t`, `Δ_t` — is literally shared, with zero
citation, across two lineages within the same overall corpus.

**Evidence against:** no specific C1 `K_t` formulation is shown identical, or even structurally
mapped, to any specific B `K_t` formulation — this register does not have the component-level detail
for either lineage's six/nine variants to attempt that mapping here (both registers summarize rather
than fully enumerate every variant's field list), and doing so would require a dedicated, separately-
scoped formalization pass, not this adjudication.

**Preserved differences:** B's `Δ_t` is eventually **frozen** to one canonical definition (M0132); C1's
own evidence base shows no analogous freeze for its own `Δ_t` — the instability remains open at the
end of C1's own evidence population.

**Required assumptions (to advance past PARTIAL CORRESPONDENCE):** a demonstrated component-level
mapping between at least one C1 `K_t` variant and at least one B `K_t` variant — not attempted here,
not available from either register's own summary-level content.

**Adjudication status:** **PARTIAL CORRESPONDENCE** at the notation/research-process level (level 2–3
of the six-level ladder: the shared vocabulary and shared instability signature are conceptual/
functional correspondences, verified independent of citation); **UNRESOLVED** at the structural/formal
level (no component-level map).

**Confidence:** Moderate-high for the notation-level finding (independently verified, no citation
link, genuinely surprising); none for any structural-equivalence claim.

## Row 7 — Representation: C2 ↔ B

**C2 concept/structure:** regimes as structure-adding functors `F_R(𝒞)` (metric/topological/
probabilistic/logical/measure), Shani's invariant ("no regime may silently redefine a core semantic
concept").

**B concept/structure:** Structure-First (§F) — induced equivalence `∼_π`, the Representation Adequacy
Principle (`Adequate(π,Q)⟹D_Q⊆Preserved(π)`), the tested-and-rejected Hilbert-space representation.

**Source references:** C2 — `04_model-c_kernel-ddd/02_concept-register.md` §M. B —
`03_model-b_mathematical/02_concept-register.md` §F, §I.

**Evidence for:** both are, at core, a guarding principle against representation-induced semantic
corruption — B's Adequacy Principle ("a representation cannot recover distinctions its projection has
already destroyed") and C2's Shani-invariant ("no regime may silently redefine a core semantic
concept") state functionally the same warning, in different formal vocabularies, from entirely
unconnected sources (a math-lane ablation-experiment programme vs. a single synthesis document citing
an external philosopher, Shani).

**Evidence against:** B's apparatus is tested (the Hilbert-space rejection is an executed, ratified
governance act); C2's regime-functor apparatus is proposed, worked through four regime types, but not
tested against any adversarial case within its own single-file evidence base. No formal map is shown
between B's `∼_π`/Adequacy and C2's `F_R`/Shani-invariant.

**Adjudication status:** **FUNCTIONAL ANALOGY** (level 3 — shared guarding role, not shared formal
structure), the same classification level Phase 3 gave the A↔B "Representation" analogue (Row 3, that
document, for A's lens-discipline vs. B's Structure-First) — here supported by n=1 on the C2 side, so
weaker in evidentiary weight even at the same classification level.

**Confidence:** Low-moderate (thin population, but the functional match is precise).

## Row 8 — Knowledge-definition: C1 ↔ A, C1 ↔ B

**C1 concept/structure:** repeated, explicit "knowledge" definitions throughout §D–H — e.g. seq 0434's
"Information=Raw_Data+Provenance; Knowledge=Information+…"; seq 0445's `K=K_D+K_V`; seq 2330's own
(C2's) "a factive epistemic relation between a participant and domain content."

**A concept/structure:** no dedicated "define knowledge" formalism found in A's own register — A's
evidence is organized around pramāṇa (valid means of knowledge *acquisition*), not a definition of
knowledge itself as an object.

**B concept/structure:** B's own K_t/Δ_t apparatus (§A) defines "knowledge state" formally but does not,
in this register's own record, propose a prose *definition* of "knowledge" as a term in the way C1's
files do.

**Evidence for/against:** this is recorded primarily as a **non-convergence**, not a correspondence —
C1 (and C2) contain sustained, repeated, explicit knowledge-definition content that neither A nor B's
own register shows an equivalent of. This directly extends Phase 4's own finding (`03_contradictions-
and-open-questions.md`, the MD-007 falsifier discussion) that the *overwhelming majority* of sustained
knowledge-theorizing in this whole reconstruction sits inside the `engineering_knowledgeos`-tagged
population, not `epistemic_knowledgeos`, A, or B.

**Adjudication status:** **INCOMPATIBLE-shaped absence** — not a term-collision (Row 7/8 of Phase 3's
own matrix), but a genuine content asymmetry: C1 (and singly, C2) do the definitional work; A and B do
not attempt it in comparable form. Carried to the non-convergence document as a model-specific
structure, not forced into a weak correspondence row.

**Confidence:** Moderate (register-level finding, not independently re-verified against every cited
seq this turn).
