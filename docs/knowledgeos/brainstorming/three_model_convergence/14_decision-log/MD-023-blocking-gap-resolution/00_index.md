# MD-023 — Blocking-Gap Resolution Study (GA-001, GA-038, GA-002/003)

**Status: COMPLETE.** Executed under separate, explicit authorization (2026-09-08), with three
pre-negotiated clarifications applied throughout (see §Clarifications below). **Not Stage 07** — this
study investigates whether GA-001/GA-038 are resolvable; it does not formalize, canonicalize, or select
a model. **Not Phase 5O** — the K-1/K-2 governance-frozen track remains untouched.

## Primary research question

Are GA-001 (Kernel identity) and GA-038 (no canonical `K_t`) genuinely resolvable scientific/formal
questions, or do the existing evidence and admissible formal methods demonstrate that unified
Kernel/state formalization is currently unjustified and the programme should proceed model-by-model?

**Answer, stated up front (full reasoning in `02`–`06`): neither is resolved by this study. GA-001
narrows to a specific, falsifiable hypothesis (DDD complementarity, not identity) rather than remaining
a flat "unresolved." GA-038 is found to have `NO CORPUS-JUSTIFIED CANONICALIZATION CRITERION` among the
10 tested — with one precise, evidence-grounded exception: the corpus's own successful precedent
(`Δ_t`'s freeze, M0132) shows canonicalization in this programme has historically been achieved by
explicit governance ratification of an already-derived sub-result, not by a general mathematical
selection principle. Persistent non-convergence at the object level is recorded as a legitimate result,
consistent with the study's own governing principle.**

## Clarifications applied (per authorization)

1. **Recurrence language corrected.** Nowhere in this study is repeated cross-model appearance
   described as "confirmed," "independently confirmed," or "statistically replicated." The corrected
   formulation — *"the distinction recurred consistently across the examined instances; this recurrence
   is not independent or statistical confirmation"* — is used throughout. Stage 06's own frozen wording
   (`02_cross-model-gaps.md` GA-001, "confirmed a fourth time... a fifth") is **not edited**; the
   correction is recorded here only.
2. **Scoped correspondence testing, selection criteria pre-registered before any test was run** — see
   `02_kernel-correspondence-analysis.md` §1 for the criteria and §2 for the 4 selected pairs, chosen
   for diagnostic value (formal-specification quality, existing correspondence strength, potential to
   resolve GA-001, potential to falsify the aggregate/operator divide, coverage across A/B/C1/C2) —
   never for convenience or expected outcome.
3. **Four-state obstruction taxonomy used throughout**, never collapsed: `NOT FORMALLY SPECIFIED
   ENOUGH TO TEST` / `PARTIALLY TESTABLE` / `TESTED — NO MAP FOUND` / `UNRESOLVED` (reserved for cases
   where testability is adequate but the result genuinely cannot be determined either way). **No
   under-specified candidate was reconstructed into a fuller formal specification to make a test
   possible** — where structure was missing, this study names exactly what is missing and whether the
   raw corpus appears to contain it, and stops there.
