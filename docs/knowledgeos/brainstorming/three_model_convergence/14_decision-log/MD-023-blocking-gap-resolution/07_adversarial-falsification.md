# Adversarial Falsification

For every positive claim this study makes, the observation that would prove it wrong — required before
any claim is treated as settled.

| Claim | Falsifier |
|---|---|
| The aggregate-vs-operator-set divide is genuine, not merely apparent (`02`§7) | A demonstrated structure-preserving map from any operator-set candidate to any aggregate candidate, under a stated representation — none found, but not exhaustively searched (only 4 pairs tested) |
| Even within the aggregate category, no candidate corresponds to another (`02` Pairs 3/4) | A future, dedicated field-by-field re-specification of any two aggregate candidates from raw source, showing an actual shared invariant this study's register-level comparison missed |
| `NO CORPUS-JUSTIFIED CANONICALIZATION CRITERION FOUND` for `K_t` (`04`§2) | A corpus document, not located by this study, that applies one of the 10 tested criteria (or an 11th, untested one) successfully to the full `K_t` family — this study's own search was limited to the criteria list given in the authorization, not exhaustive of all conceivable criteria |
| The `Δ_t` freeze is a governance act, not a mathematical selection (`04`§3) | A demonstrated proof, not found in M0104 or elsewhere in this evidence base, that `Δ_t={r∈R_t:Sat(K_t,r)=0}` is the *unique* structure satisfying some independently-motivated formal property — M0104's own "freeze-as-constraint, not conclusion" language is direct textual evidence against this, but a later document this study has not read could in principle supply it |
| The `K_t`/`Δ_t` recurrence is best explained by reconstruction-process reuse (`05`§2) | Direct evidence of authorship — session logs, timestamps finer-grained than this reconstruction has access to, or an explicit cross-reference this study's citation search missed — that shows independent, unrelated authorship of the two lineages |
| The complementarity hypothesis (`06`§2) | An attempted composition (B's operators applied to an A/C1/C2-style aggregate) that fails for a structural reason, not merely an unattempted one — this study did not attempt the composition itself, so this falsifier is untested in both directions |
| The failure-mode asymmetry (Pair 1, `02`§3) corroborates the categorical divide | A third falsified candidate (beyond C0 and P-3) whose failure mode does not fit either the "incompleteness" or "soundness" pattern — this study examined only 2 falsified candidates total (the only 2 that exist in the current evidence base, per `01`'s own summary) |

## Self-check: did this study manufacture convergence anywhere?

No. Every positive-sounding finding (the `Δ_t` non-symmetry parallel, the complementarity hypothesis,
the failure-mode asymmetry) is explicitly labeled as either a narrow, specific, evidence-bounded
observation or a hypothesis requiring further, separately-authorized testing — none is used to declare
any two Kernel or state candidates identical, equivalent, or canonical.

## Self-check: did this study manufacture non-convergence anywhere?

No. Where a positive structural finding was available (the `Δ_t` non-symmetry correction, genuinely
shared across two lineages) it was reported as such, not suppressed or downgraded to preserve a clean
"nothing converges" narrative.
