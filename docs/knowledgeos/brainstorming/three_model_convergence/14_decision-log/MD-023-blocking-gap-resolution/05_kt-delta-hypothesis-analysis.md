# The `K_t`/`Δ_t` Recurrence Hypothesis (GA-002/GA-003)

**Corrected framing, applied throughout (clarification 1)**: the recurrence of `K_t`/`Δ_t` notation
across Model B's math-lane thread and Model C1's `phase_measure_theory/` thread **recurred consistently
across the examined instances; this recurrence is not independent or statistical confirmation.** Two
data points, zero citation link, one shared notation family — that is the full evidentiary weight
available.

## §1. Six candidate explanations, tested against available evidence

1. **Notation coincidence.** Weak — `K_t` and, especially, `Δ_t` (a specific, non-obvious choice —
   "Discrepancy," explicitly *not* "Distance," in both lineages independently) are unusual enough
   variable-name choices that pure coincidence is a poor fit for two independent occurrences of the
   *same* non-obvious naming decision (§4 of `03`).
2. **Shared methodological pattern.** Strong — both lineages exhibit the identical research-process
   signature (proliferating component-count revisions, each caught and repaired by in-arc self-audit,
   §04 of `06_gap-analysis`'s own GA-052 discussion). This explanation does not require the two
   *objects* to be related, only the two *research processes* to share a discipline.
3. **Conceptual correspondence.** Plausible but unproven — both `K_t` families do address "current
   epistemic state," a genuinely shared concept, but this alone does not distinguish this pair from
   Model A's own Knowledge Vector family, which shares the concept without sharing the notation.
4. **Structural correspondence.** Not established — `02_extended-correspondence-matrix.md` (Phase 6)
   already found this at UNRESOLVED, and `03`'s own §5 here confirms no component-level map has been
   attempted or is evidenced.
5. **Consequence of a common mathematical constraint.** Weakly supported by one specific finding: both
   lineages independently proved the *same* non-symmetry result for their own gap-quantity
   (`Δ_t`≠symmetric distance) and corrected for it the same way. This suggests that **if** a state/gap
   formalization is attempted with even minimal rigor, the non-symmetry of "gap from current to ideal"
   is a discoverable mathematical fact independent of which lineage discovers it — a genuine, if narrow,
   mathematical-constraint explanation for *that one specific* convergence, not for the broader `K_t`
   naming match.
6. **Consequence of the reconstruction process itself.** **The most parsimonious explanation for the
   naming match specifically.** Both lineages sit inside the same overall corpus
   (`docs/knowledgeos/brainstorming/`), produced under what this entire reconstruction has repeatedly
   found to be a continuous, session-like authorship process (Phase 5J's own repository-history finding:
   a single bulk-import commit reflecting when research was checked in, not written; the corpus's own
   repeated self-audit discipline appearing identically across unrelated threads). A single underlying
   author/process working on two nominally-separate tracks reusing its own prior notational habit
   (`K_t` for "knowledge state at time t," an entirely natural choice once used once) requires no
   coincidence and no deep mathematical necessity — only ordinary notational memory.

## §2. Conclusion

**Explanation 6 (reconstruction-process reuse) is the best-supported reading of the raw naming match
itself; explanation 5 (shared mathematical constraint) is independently, separately supported for the
one specific `Δ_t`-non-symmetry finding; explanations 2 (methodological-pattern) is independently well
supported as a separate, additional fact about both lineages.** None of the six explanations is proven
in the sense of eliminating the others — they are not mutually exclusive, and the evidence supports a
combination (process-level notational reuse, PLUS one genuine shared mathematical discovery about
non-symmetry) rather than a single clean answer.

**What this study does NOT conclude**: that the two `K_t` objects are the same formal structure, that
one derives from the other, or that this finding resolves GA-038. It remains exactly what Phase 6 left
it: a **PROPOSED CROSS-MODEL HYPOTHESIS**, now with a sharper internal decomposition (naming-reuse
component vs. shared-mathematical-fact component) rather than one undifferentiated claim.

## §3. What would upgrade this beyond hypothesis status

A full field-by-field enumeration of both `K_t` families (the missing structural information named in
`03`§1/§2) followed by an attempted structure-preserving map — explicitly not performed in this study,
per its own binding constraint against reconstructing missing specification.
