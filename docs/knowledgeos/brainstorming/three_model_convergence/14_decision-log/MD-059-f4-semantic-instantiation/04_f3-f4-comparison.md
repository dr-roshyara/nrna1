# MD-059 §04 — F3 ↔ F4 Comparison (§7/§8 of the authorizing prompt)

## The attempted comparison

`F3 ≈_{Q,𝒪} F4 ?` — testing whether `Obs_{Q,𝒪}(F3) = Obs_{Q,𝒪}(F4)` for some shared `Q,𝒪`.

## Why the comparison cannot be posed, even in principle, from current evidence

- **F3's `Obs`** (MD-050): a projection of `Reach(Ops(K))` — its codomain is a set of **reachable
  atoms** (F3's own carrier/atom table, e.g. specific derivation-rule outputs).
- **F4's `Obs_F4`** (`02`): a function `r ↦ Sat(K_t,r)` — its codomain is a set of **satisfied
  requirements** (`R_t`).

**These are different types.** An atom is not a requirement, and no document anywhere in either
Model B's own evidence base or F3's own source connects the two — no map `atoms → requirements` or
`requirements → atoms` is stated, proposed, or even flagged as a gap by either lane. **Positing a
shared `Q,𝒪` for both would require inventing that bridge, which this phase's own hard boundary
forbids** (§00 §7 — no invented semantic-interpretation function).

## What this finding actually establishes, precisely

This is **sharper than MD-058's own finding** ("5 of 6 candidates unavailable, so 15/15 pairs
UNRESOLVED by default"). Here, **both sides of one specific pair are partially instantiated**, and
the comparison *still* fails — not from missing material on either side, but from a **missing
inter-candidate bridge** (a third, distinct kind of obstruction, alongside "missing primitive" and
"unresolved registry"). This is itself a scientifically useful, precisely-diagnosed negative result
(§13, condition C/D of the authorizing prompt).
