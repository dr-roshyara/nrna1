# MD-044 §4 — Adversarial Hypotheses (Phase G) and Statistical Discipline (Phase H)

## Phase G — hypotheses, evidence-led

**H1. MinKer is merely another notation for an already-existing kernel candidate.** — **NOT
WELL-POSED AS STATED.** MinKer is a criterion/operator, not a candidate object; it is not the same
type of thing as F1–F8, so it cannot be "merely another notation for" one of them. Neither confirmed
nor refuted — a category clarification, not a verdict.

**H2. MinKer genuinely supplies a new semantic minimality criterion.** — **SUPPORTED.** The explicit,
source-endorsed rejection of operator-count/`Complexity(K)`-based minimality in favor of
`⪯_sem`/`⪯_cap`-based ordering is a real, if under-specified, criterion not previously on record in
this reconstruction's own F1–F8 inventory.

**H3. MinKer defines minimality but does not establish existence.** — **SUPPORTED**, directly per
M0239's own ledger and its own §6 text.

**H4. MinKer establishes existence but not uniqueness.** — **NOT SUPPORTED** — the premise is false;
existence itself is not established either (see H3).

**H5. MinKer can distinguish existing candidates (F1/F3/F4/F5/F6).** — **NOT SUPPORTED.** No
computation was attempted against any of them; the toy execution covers only a 3-capability universe
unconnected to any named F1–F8 object.

**H6. MinKer cannot currently be executed because `𝔎_adm`, `𝔠_KOS`, `⊨`, or `⪯_cap` remain
insufficiently defined.** — **SUPPORTED**, the central finding of `01_...md`'s dependency table.

**H7. MinKer resolves GA-038.** — **NOT SUPPORTED.** `K_t` is never named anywhere in the 5-file
chain; MinKer's own applicability to the `K_t` question has not even been asserted by its own source.

**H8. MinKer merely reframes GA-038.** — **PARTIALLY SUPPORTED for GA-001** (a more principled,
semantic-equivalence-based reframing of the general kernel-identity question exists) **but NOT for
GA-038 specifically** (MinKer does not engage `K_t` at all, so it does not even reframe that
question).

**H9. MinKer is mathematically stronger than the previous governance-only `Δ_t` selection
precedent.** — **NOT SUPPORTED.** Per `03_...md`'s Phase F finding, MinKer is the same shape (a
formal place requiring a governance choice), not evidently stronger.

**H10. MinKer still requires an external governance decision before becoming canonical.** —
**SUPPORTED**, directly, per the source's own explicit "Pending Formal Governance Review" / "Canonical
Implementation... is an engineering and governance choice" language.

## Phase H — statistical discipline, applied

**The discovery of MinKer is not treated as an independent confirmation, replication, convergence, or
proof of correctness.** It is one additional, formally relevant artifact within an already-admissible
research lane (`mathematical_ideas_that_can_be_implemented/`, `KR-SIM`-tagged boundary material).

**The five files are one continuous same-session editorial dialogue (~17 minutes), not five separate
confirmations.** Where the same formulation repeats across files (e.g., the `Kernel≠Representation≠
Reasoning≠Zero≠Governance≠Execution` boundary statement appears near-verbatim in files 1, 2, and 3),
this is characterized as **provenance dependence within one continuous session**, never as multiple
independent sources converging on the same conclusion. Where a claim changes across the sequence
(the uniqueness bullet), this is characterized as the source's own **self-correction**, not a
contradiction requiring adjudication by this study, and not evidence of instability in the underlying
mathematics.

**The F3 (kernel-reduction) vocabulary/finding resonance** (`03_...md`) is explicitly classified as
**at most within-lane corroboration** — both sit in the same broader math lane — never as independent
statistical confirmation, consistent with this discipline.

**No existence, uniqueness, or convergence claim is asserted or implied by this study beyond what the
source's own final ledger (M0239) states in its own words.**
