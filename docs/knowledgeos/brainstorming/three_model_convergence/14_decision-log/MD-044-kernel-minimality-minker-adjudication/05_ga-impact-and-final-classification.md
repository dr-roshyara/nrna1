# MD-044 §5 — GA-001/GA-038 Impact and Final Classification

## Phase E — GA-001

**Does MinKer provide a corpus-justified criterion capable of distinguishing the competing Kernel
families?** **CRITERION AVAILABLE BUT NOT EXECUTABLE.** A genuinely more principled criterion now
exists (reject syntactic/operator-count minimality; use semantic-capability-simulation-based
minimality instead) — a real conceptual advance, source-endorsed and internally consistent. But it
cannot be EXECUTED against any concrete pair of F1/F3/F4/F5/F6 candidates, because its own load-
bearing inputs (`𝔎_adm`, `𝔠_KOS`/`⊨`, `⪯_cap`'s `Trace`-based semantics) are each `NECESSARY BUT
UNSPECIFIED`. **GA-001 itself: UNCHANGED** — no pair of families is newly shown structure-preserving-
equivalent or newly shown incompatible.

## Phase E — GA-038

**Does MinKer provide a legitimate corpus-internal criterion for choosing among competing `K_t`
variants?** **UNCHANGED, more conservatively than GA-001.** Unlike GA-001 (a generic kernel-identity
question this criterion was at least designed to address), GA-038 is specifically about `K_t`, and
`K_t` is never named anywhere in the 5-file MinKer chain — its applicability to that specific
question has not even been asserted by its own source, let alone operationalized. **GA-038:
UNCHANGED, and arguably reinforced** — the existing evidence (`03_...md`, Phase F) shows MinKer would
face the identical governance-choice requirement `Δ_t`'s own precedent already established, so even a
future successful application to `K_t` would not, by this source's own logic, resolve GA-038 without
an additional governance act.

1. **Is MinKer formally defined?** Yes, as a formula (`MinKer(𝔠_KOS)=Min_⪯sem{K∈𝔎_adm\|K⊨𝔠_KOS}`),
   but its own inputs are not (see 2–5).
2. **Is `𝔎_adm` formally defined?** No — `NECESSARY BUT UNSPECIFIED`.
3. **Is `𝔠_KOS` formally defined?** No — `NECESSARY BUT UNSPECIFIED`.
4. **Is `⊨` formally defined?** No — chained to 3.

   simulated" is explicitly flagged by the source itself as still informal (`Trace(K,h)` not yet
   formalized) — so `⪯_sem` is defined only in terms of an admittedly-still-open primitive.
6. **Is MinKer computable/executable?** Not against the real candidate universe or any F1–F8 object.

   reconstruction needs.
7. **Is kernel existence established?** No — explicitly `NOT YET PROVED` per the source's own final
   ledger.
8. **Is kernel uniqueness established?** No — explicitly `NOT YET PROVED`; the source's own earlier
   overclaim on this point was self-corrected within the same session (see `02_...md`).
9. **Can MinKer distinguish F1/F3/F4/F5/F6?** No — never attempted; not executable at that scale.
10. **Does MinKer resolve GA-001?** No — criterion available but not executable.
11. **Does MinKer resolve GA-038?** No — `K_t` is never engaged by this material at all.
12. **Does MinKer select a canonical Kernel?** No — explicitly and repeatedly disclaimed by its own
    source ("Canonical Implementation... is an engineering and governance choice, distinct from
    semantic minimality").
13. **Does MinKer require governance?** Yes — explicitly, per the source's own "Pending Formal

    principled, source-endorsed definitional shape for what "minimal kernel" should mean, rejecting
    operator-count minimality decisively) — not as a resolution of any open gap, and not as an
    executable procedure.

## Final classification

**C — MINIMALITY FRAMEWORK ONLY, REQUIRED SEMANTICS MISSING.**

Mathematical justification: `MinKer` is a well-typed formula over three inputs (`𝔎_adm`, `𝔠_KOS`/`⊨`,
`⪯_cap`), each of which the source's own final self-assessment (M0239) explicitly leaves open or
unproved. This is not **A** (established and executable — the load-bearing inputs are missing, not
merely awaiting a computational run). Not **B** (established but not yet executable — "established"
would require existence/uniqueness themselves to be proven facts about a well-defined object;
instead, the object's own defining inputs are unspecified, which is a category below B). Not **D**
(no material advance — the semantic-vs-syntactic minimality reframing is a genuine, source-endorsed
conceptual advance over what was previously on record, even though unexecutable). Not **E**
(contradictory/incompatible — the internal "uniqueness" claim-then-correction is a normal, healthy
same-session peer-review sequence, not an unresolved contradiction; by the chain's own end (M0239)
the ledger is internally consistent).

**My prior expectation was not a desired outcome; this classification follows the evidence chain in
`01_...md` through `04_...md`, not a preference.**
