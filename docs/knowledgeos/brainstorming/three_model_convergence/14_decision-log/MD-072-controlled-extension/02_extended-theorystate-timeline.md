series (MD-069). They document a **structurally parallel, vastly larger, independently-governed**

decisions — and neither ratification event (`ABK-1` nor `GN-31`) ever touches the F4 chain's own
tracked objects.** The F4 chain's own zero-governance-adoption finding (MD-069 §04) is not merely
unremarked-upon — it stands in contrast to two real, documented ratification events elsewhere in the
same corpus.
