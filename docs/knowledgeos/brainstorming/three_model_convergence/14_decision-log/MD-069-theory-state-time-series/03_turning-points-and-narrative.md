## Derived Theory Evolution Narrative (phases named from evidence, not assumed)

The mission's own example nine-phase list is explicitly not assumed correct. The evidence itself
supports **five** phases, not nine — several of the mission's suggested phases (e.g. a distinct
"kernel development" phase, a distinct "governance and ratification" phase) do not correspond to
material changes in the *tracked chain itself*; kernel and governance activity in this corpus belongs
almost entirely to the unrelated-homonym branches (T14/T15/T17), not to the `EC_t→Sat→Δ_t` lineage.

### Phase I — Conceptual Formation (T0–T4, Sep 1 14:59 – Sep 2 00:45)

`K_t`, `EC_t`, `Req`, `Δ_t`, `Zero` each born independently, in loosely-connected precursor forms,
across a single continuous ~10-hour authorial arc. No formal proof exists yet. This phase ends the
moment all five objects are drawn into one connected structure.

### Phase II — Canonical Formalization and First Repair Attempts (T5–T13, Sep 2 00:46 – 17:53)

The defining event: T5 supplies a complete, internally-consistent (if not fully reconciled) formal
chain, immediately followed by a sustained, honest research effort (T6/T7/T9/T10/T12/T13) to fill the
one gap the chain itself could not close at birth — a computed body for `Sat(K_t,r)`. Every attempt in
this phase is either self-falsified (T9's CE-1), demoted (T10), or explicitly rejected and retired
(T12). This phase ends not because the gap closes, but because a same-day governance register
(`[04-11]`, technically Phase III) reaffirms it is still open.

### Phase III — Branching and Divergence (T11, T14–T17, Sep 2 10:46 – Sep 4)

The corpus's own research attention fragments into at least four largely non-interacting branches
(ZeroLens, `ℛ_req`/ABK-1, Zero-Algebra, "Theory v1.2 FROZEN") — each internally rigorous, each reaching
its own closure/ratification events, **none of which engage the canonical chain's own unresolved `Sat`
gap**. This is the corpus's own longest phase by elapsed time (nearly two days) and produces the
richest governance activity in the whole graph (T14's ABK-1 ratification) — but none of it is
load-bearing for the object this reconstruction has been tracking since MD-057.

### Phase IV — Re-derivation and Closure (T18–T22, Sep 6 00:16 – 10:00)

A fresh session, in one continuous ~10-hour arc, independently re-derives the entire canonical chain
from first principles and, this time, supplies a genuine computed body for `Sat` (T21) — with proved
theorems at nearly every stage and eight further domain-specific instantiations. This is the corpus's
only phase in which the specific gap identified at the end of Phase II is actually closed.

### Phase V — Silence (T23, Sep 6 09:44 onward)

The corpus does not return to Phase IV's own result. No adversarial review, no citation, no
contradiction — simply no further engagement, for the entire remainder of the traversed queue (through
Sep 9). One partial, independent echo exists (`[06-22]`–`[06-25]`) but does not constitute review.

## What this narrative explains, per the mission's own required question

**What changed, why it changed, what it enabled, what it broke, and what remained unresolved:**

- **What changed**: `Sat` moved from an unspecified oracle (T5) through several falsified concrete
  proposals (Phase II) to a genuinely computed, proved function of independently-typed inputs (T21).
- **Why it changed**: the corpus's own repeated, honest self-critique (Phase II's own reviewers)
  consistently rejected every shortcut (entailment, three-valued ad hoc classes) that did not actually
  connect `Sat` to `EC_t`'s own purpose-relativity — a discipline that ultimately produced T21's own
  cleaner separation of evaluation (`Eval`) from determination-rule (`Det_r`).
- **What it enabled**: proved theorems throughout the downstream chain (`Δ_t`, `Zero`, `Determination`,
  and — crucially — a proof that `Decision` is *not* automatically determined even once `Sat` and
  `Determination` are both settled).
- **What it broke**: nothing explicitly — but it silently dropped `r`'s own earlier structured fields
  (GAP-001) and the explicit `App` gate (GAP-003), architecturally absorbing both roles without ever
  saying so.
- **What remained unresolved**: whether T21's own `Sat` definition would survive the kind of adversarial
  review every other major claim in Phase II and Phase III received — genuinely unanswerable from the
  corpus as traversed (GAP-004), because Phase V never happened.
