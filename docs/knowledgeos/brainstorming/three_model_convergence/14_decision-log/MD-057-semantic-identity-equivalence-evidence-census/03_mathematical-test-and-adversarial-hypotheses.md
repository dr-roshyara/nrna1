# MD-057 §03 — Mathematical Test Battery and Adversarial Hypotheses

## Mathematical test battery, applied to the Cluster-1/2 candidates

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| **Identity vs. "same"** | `≡` and `≈` are shown corpus-distinct as *typed slots* (design-level), not shown identical or distinct *semantically* — the source's own distinction, kept exactly as strong as stated. |
| **Semantic equivalence with actual interpretation** | The one candidate formula (`261.21`/CLOSURE-4) has an interpretation (match on `q∈Q, o∈𝒪`) but is self-labeled a candidate, not adopted. No interpretation exists for `≡_K` proper. |
| **Concrete `Obs(c)`/`Beh(c)` for F1/F3/F4/F5/F6** | **Still none** — this candidate apparatus (`𝒪`, `𝒯`, `𝒪_K`, `≡_K`) belongs to a *different* candidate family (the `phase_measure_theory` `K_t`/step-25x apparatus) than any of F1–F8/K0 as registered in this reconstruction. No document connects it to F1/F3/F4/F5/F6 by name. MD-049's own finding (zero hits for the required Trace/Obs/behavior vocabulary against F1/F3/F5) is **unaffected and unextended** by this phase. |
| **`c⊨C_KOS`** | Not tested here — no admissible `C_KOS` instance was in scope for this candidate family either. |
| **Minimality ordering / irreducibility** | **Tested, by the source itself**: `{≡}` is computed as the unique size-1 cut over a 29-node dependency graph — a genuine, executed minimality result, but for *which symbol to resolve first*, not for a kernel object's own minimal representation. Not comparable to F3's own `Reach(S)`-based minimality result (MD-050) — different formalism, different object. |
| **Representation-independence / circularity check** | The source explicitly performs this check on itself and catches two near-misses (a "vacuous PASS" where a total order trivially satisfies join laws; an over-strong reading of `join-semilattice`) — disclosed in `REFINED-STEP-291` §14/self-check log. This is evidence of **methodological rigor in the source**, not evidence resolving GA-001/GA-038. |
