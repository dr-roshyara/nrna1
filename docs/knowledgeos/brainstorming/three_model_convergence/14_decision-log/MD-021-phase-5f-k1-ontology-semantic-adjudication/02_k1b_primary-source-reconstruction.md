## The disambiguation this phase adopts

**Both readings are carried forward, not collapsed into one.** Section `09`'s final matrix reports
**both** pairs explicitly: "K-1 ↔ Phase-5C's K-1-B" (Reading 1, resolved to formal equivalence) and
"K-1 ↔ verification-lane K-2" (Reading 2, resolved to partial correspondence via the corpus's own
formal projection test). **This phase does not silently choose one reading and discard the other** —
the authorization's own text plausibly intended Reading 2 (given its explicit "two-component
formulation... Assertion expansion" description), but Reading 1 is this reconstruction's own prior,
already-published terminology (Phase 5C), and abandoning it silently would itself violate this
reconstruction's own standing discipline against silently repairing or reinterpreting prior findings.
