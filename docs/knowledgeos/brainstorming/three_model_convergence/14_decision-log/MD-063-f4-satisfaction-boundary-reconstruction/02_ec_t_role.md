# MD-063 §02 — Q2: The Role of `EC_t`

## Does the corpus define `EC_t → requirement/acceptance condition → Sat(K_t,r)`?

**NOT EVIDENCED IN INSPECTED CORPUS**, as an explicit formula — searched M0043 (the source of `EC_t`
itself), M0047 (the source of the concrete requirement tuple), M0048 (a same-day review of M0043,
per MD-061's own finding), M0125/M0126, M0132. No source writes a rule computing an acceptance
condition, or `Sat`'s own value, from `EC_t`'s four arguments.

## One suggestive, unconfirmed terminological adjacency — disclosed, not adopted

`EC_t=EC(S_t,G_t,Q_t,C_t)` (M0043 `[DEF-19]`: Subject, Goal, Query, **Context**). M0125's own
`Boundary=(Facet,Condition,Context,Provenance)` (§1.2/§8.2) **also uses "Context" as one of its own
four fields.** No formula anywhere connects `EC_t`'s own `C_t` argument to `Boundary`'s own `Context`
field — this could be the same concept reused, a coincidental reuse of an ordinary English word, or a
genuine homonym. **Recorded as a lead for a future phase, not resolved or adopted here** — exactly
the discipline Phase D (`03`) requires.

## Verdict

`EC_t → Sat` relation: **NOT EVIDENCED IN INSPECTED CORPUS.**
`EC_t`'s own `Context` argument vs. `Boundary`'s own `Context` field: **UNRESOLVED — a named,
disclosed candidate connection, not invented, not confirmed.**
