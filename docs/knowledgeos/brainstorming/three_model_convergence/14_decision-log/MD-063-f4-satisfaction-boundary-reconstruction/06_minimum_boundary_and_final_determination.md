1. **Strongest direct evidence**: M0125's own commissioning of M0127, and its own native `Sat`-
   collapse diagnosis (`04`) — the single most consequential finding of this phase.

   applies to *requirements*, not only propositions.

   evidence.** The single most valuable next research act — still reconstruction, not construction —

   object. That single resolution would determine whether the `Reason`/`Provenance`/`Context`
   apparatus transfers to `Sat(K_t,r)` as evidenced fact, or remains a structural analogy one notch
   stronger than M0127's own (per MD-062).
