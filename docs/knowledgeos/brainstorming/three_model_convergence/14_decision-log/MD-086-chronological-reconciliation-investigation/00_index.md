# MD-086 — Chronological Reconciliation Investigation: `EC`, `r`, `Γ`, `Sat`

## Purpose

User accepted MD-085 and redirected to a narrowly-scoped reconciliation question, distinct from every
prior missing-search phase: **can the competing corpus-native formulations of `EC`, `r`, `Γ`, and
`Sat` be reconciled historically into identity/refinement relationships using only corpus evidence,
without inventing mappings or choosing a canonical formulation?** Explicit warning against exactly the
failure mode this phase must avoid: inferring a projection/refinement relationship from field-overlap
or proximity alone, when the source never states one.

## Method

Reuses MD-078–085's own already-established evidence as the base population; performs new, targeted
primary-source verification specifically where a reconciliation claim depends on exact structure that
was not previously checked at this level of precision. Two targeted checks performed before drafting:
(1) whether `T5`'s own source ever explicitly relates `Sat(K,EC_t)` to a universal quantification over
`Sat(K,r)` — checked directly, **not found**; (2) whether Part VIII's own 4-field `Γ` is ever stated as
a projection of Part II's own 7-tuple `Γ` — checked directly, **not found; zero cross-reference either
direction**. Both results require correcting prior framing (§02).

## Central finding, disclosed first

**A genuinely new, previously-uncredited structural correspondence for `EC`**: T21's own 6-field
`EC=⟨Req,Rules,Scope,ER,TR,AR⟩` (Part I/II, 2026-09-06) shows striking near-identical field *names* to
`step-023`'s own 7-field birth `EC=(Purpose,Requirements,EvidenceRules,UncertaintyLimits,ConflictRules,
TemporalRules,AuthorityRules)` (2026-08-27) — four of six T21 fields (`Req`≈`Requirements`,
`ER`≈`EvidenceRules`, `TR`≈`TemporalRules`, `AR`≈`AuthorityRules`) match closely in name and evident
intent. This was not previously isolated in MD-078/081's own broader "seven competing, never
reconciled" framing. It does **not** amount to proven identity (no explicit citation exists either
direction, and `T21`'s own `Rules`/`Scope` fields have no clean counterpart), but it is the single
strongest field-level structural correspondence found anywhere in the `EC` family — see §01.

**Two corrections to this reconstruction's own prior framing**, both disclosed and recorded forward,
neither editing the original frozen text: `Γ`'s MD-081 "`SUBDIVIDED`" responsibility classification
overstated what the source actually supports — no stated projection semantics exist anywhere; the
correct classification is `ALTERNATIVE FORMULATION, no stated correspondence`, not `SUBDIVIDED`. `Sat`'s
contract-level and per-requirement forms are never explicitly related by any universal-quantification
statement in the corpus — this reconstruction has never claimed otherwise, but this phase closes the
question definitively by direct check rather than leaving it merely unaddressed.
