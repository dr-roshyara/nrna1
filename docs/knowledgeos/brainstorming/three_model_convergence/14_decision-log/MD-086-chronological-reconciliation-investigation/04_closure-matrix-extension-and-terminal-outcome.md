## Terminal outcome

**`R-B` — some load-bearing competing formulations can be reconciled by corpus evidence; others remain
unresolved.** Not `R-A` (no family achieves full reconciliation). Not `R-C` (this would understate real
findings — genuine `SAME OBJECT`/`SAME OBJECT, REFINED` relationships were established for `EC`
(twice), `r` (once), and `Sat` (once, if structurally imperfect) — `identity/refinement relations were
established`, just not for the *majority* of pairs, and not uniformly across families). Not `R-D` (the
corpus does not explicitly frame these as deliberate alternatives the way it explicitly frames the
acceptance policy — silence and undisclosed drift dominate, not declared pluralism), **with one partial
exception**: the `Γ_I`/`Γ_R` naming choice (deliberately *not* reusing the bare `Γ` symbol) is itself a
disclosed signal of intended distinctness, closer in spirit to `R-D` for that specific pair alone.

**Family-by-family, restated precisely**: `EC` and `r` are genuinely `R-B` (some reconciled, some not).
`Γ` is closer to `R-C` on its own (no pair reaches `SAME OBJECT` or a source-stated refinement — every
relationship found is `RECONSTRUCTED` at best). `Sat` is `R-B`, with the important qualification that
its one source-claimed refinement (Part III→Part V) does not survive close mathematical inspection.

## Is the theory mathematically closed as a family of historical formulations, even without one canonical formulation?

**Partially, and unevenly.** `EC` and `r` each have at least one genuinely closed sub-lineage
(`EC₆`↔`EC₇`; `r_B`↔`r_H`) sitting alongside several genuinely open cross-lineage questions. `Γ` has no
closed pair at all — every relationship in that family remains reconstructed or unresolved. `Sat` has
one closed-but-structurally-imperfect claim and is otherwise open, including the specific,
previously-unexamined question of why the theory itself abandons its own 3-arg innovation twice. **A
theory can be "mathematically well-defined as a collection of explicitly related historical
formulations" (per the mission's own framing) only where those relations are themselves explicit — and
by that standard, only `EC₆`↔`EC₇`, `EC₀`↔`EC₈`, `r_B`↔`r_H`, and (with a flagged caveat) Part III↔Part
V `Sat` meet the bar. Everything else remains either `RELATED OBJECT, RECONSTRUCTED` (plausible, not
proven) or genuinely `UNRESOLVED`.**
