**RECOMMENDED OPTION — NOT RATIFIED: Option E (Preserve Competing Models), as an interim governance
position — with Option C named as the most promising *content-level* candidate for a future,
separately-authorized reconciliation attempt, should governance choose not to remain at Option E
indefinitely.**

## Why Option E is preferable, on the evidence available

- It is the only option with **zero information loss** relative to the current evidence base (`13`).
- It carries the **lowest governance complexity** — an acknowledgment act, not a selection act — and
  is the only option not requiring a governance body to accept one research thread's own content over
  another's without independent grounds to prefer either.
- It is **directly consistent with the corpus's own governance principle**, found this phase (seq
  0927): `GC = NOT CLAIMED` does not block further work — `Step 284 permitted with GC=NOT CLAIMED`.
  Preserving both variants, unreconciled, is not a failure state under the corpus's own explicit rule;
  it is an anticipated, legitimate one.
- It matches this reconstruction's own standing evidentiary discipline, applied consistently since
  Phase 5C: default to `UNRESOLVED` absent demonstrated equivalence, never merge on resemblance alone.

## What evidence does NOT support Option E

- It does not make progress on `State`, `Qualify`'s algorithm, or `Π` — these remain exactly as open
  as before.
- It creates a deferred, not eliminated, cost: **any future point of actual use** (an implementation,
  or a further research phase requiring one concrete Assertion instance) will still need to choose,
  and Option E supplies no criterion for that eventual choice.
- It leaves the DDD "elevated anti-corruption concern" (`11`) unresolved for as long as the
  deferral lasts.

## What it changes / what it preserves

**Changes**: nothing in any frozen artifact. **Preserves**: every existing record, in full, exactly as
found.

## What historical artifacts remain untouched

All of them — D285-1, D285-6, D285-7, Step 272A, Step 272B, seq 0927, and all three executable scripts
remain byte-identical, as they have throughout Phase 5F–5K.

## Risks Option E creates

Indefinite deferral risk (a governance body may simply never revisit this); the risk that some future
consumer of K-2 makes an ad hoc, undocumented choice between variants under time pressure, without the
benefit of this phase's own comparative analysis.

## What additional evidence would change this recommendation

- A document establishing `LegitimateAuthority` for a specific decision-maker (`03`) would materially
  change the calculus, since a real ratification act could then responsibly select among A–D with
  accountability attached.
- A resolution of the `Π` denotation (`09` of Phase 5J) would strengthen Option D specifically, since
  its own weakest point (the `Π`-avoidance judgment) would become unnecessary.
- An implemented `Qualify` algorithm, for any of the 4 known arities, would strengthen Option C
  specifically, since its own central justification (the well-evidenced Observation→Evidence
  relationship) would then also become computable, not merely structurally motivated.

## Why Option C is named as the standing content-level candidate, without being the primary recommendation

Option C carries the **strongest single-relationship evidentiary basis** of any option (3 independent
sources for its own core claim) and the **lowest information loss** among the options that actually
*change* something — but it introduces one unverified new invariant, and untested-against-any-code
status, that Option E avoids entirely by changing nothing. **If and when governance decides Option E's
deferral should end, this phase's own analysis (`07`) is offered as the most evidence-grounded starting
point for that future decision — not as a substitute for it.**
