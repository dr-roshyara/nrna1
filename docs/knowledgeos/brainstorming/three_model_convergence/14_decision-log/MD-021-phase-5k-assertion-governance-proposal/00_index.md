## A remarkable corpus find, load-bearing for this whole phase

**The corpus itself already contains a governance-decision protocol document that anticipates almost
exactly the discipline this phase is asked to follow.** Seq 0927
(`step_283_missing-part-governance-decision-protocol-authority-provenance-and-closure-criteria.md`,
2026-08-31) defines a formal $Prepare \rightarrow Decide \rightarrow Ratify \rightarrow Record$
governance act sequence, an explicit `Authority(a,scope)` (theoretical) vs. `LegitimateAuthority(a,
scope,source,validity)` (organizational-fact) distinction, a full authority-provenance chain, and a
Governance Decision Register table format. **This phase adopts that corpus-native structure directly**
for its own governance decision record (`15`), rather than inventing a new one — the corpus's own
research had already, independently, worked out the same discipline this reconstruction's human
collaborator has been re-deriving through the MD-021 phase sequence.

**One critical, precisely-stated warning inside that same document is directly relevant to Decision
D7 (governance authority)**: the document explicitly uses *"HPA is the ratification authority"* as a
**negative example** of an overclaim a verifier must not make "unless the organizational corpus
already establishes that fact." **This phase found no such independent establishment anywhere** — "HPA"
functions, behaviorally, as a recurring reviewing/ruling actor throughout much of the corpus's later
research (e.g., seq 0764's own "HPA RULING — GN-31," and this same document's own "HPA SUPERVISORY
RULING"), but its own organizational legitimacy is never independently verified, and the corpus's own
governance-protocol document explicitly declines to assert it. **Decision D7 is therefore recorded as
`GOVERNANCE AUTHORITY NOT EVIDENCED`, precisely as the authorization anticipates.**
