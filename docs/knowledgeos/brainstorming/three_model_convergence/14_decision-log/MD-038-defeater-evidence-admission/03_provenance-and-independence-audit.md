# Provenance and Independence Audit

## The correction, applied systematically

Every claim of "corroboration," "confirmation," or "convergence" made in MD-037 is re-examined here
against the correct standard: same-lineage corroboration, never independent replication.

| MD-037 claim (as written) | Corrected classification |
|---|---|
| "Direct, independent corroboration of MD-036's own separation" (`00_index.md` headline) | **Corroboration within a common provenance lineage.** `18` and `12` share one author-layer (`18` §0's own ledger, MD-031). Their agreement is one lineage's internal consistency, not two independent sources converging. |
| "`18`'s own independent restatement" (`06_epistemic-status-audit.md`) | **A restatement from within the same lineage**, not an independent one. |
| "Matching MD-036's own independent conclusion (reached without access to this material)" (`00_index.md`) | **This specific phrasing is defensible and is NOT corrected** — it describes MD-036 (the *study*, an analytical process this reconstruction performed) as not having had access to `18`, which is true and different from a claim about the *source material's* own statistical independence. The distinction matters: MD-036's own reasoning process was independent of `18`'s text; `18`'s own text is not independent of `12`'s. |
| "the first time... a source document itself... has stated the common-authorship fact outright" (`02_search-frame-and-provenance.md`) | **Accurate as stated** — this is a claim about *what kind of evidence* (direct statement vs. inference), not a claim about statistical independence, and needs no correction. |

## Why this distinction matters for the admission decision

If `18` were treated as "independent" corroboration, an admission decision might be tempted to treat
its agreement with `12` as *additional statistical weight* behind MD-036's own conclusion — as if two
separate research efforts had converged. **They did not.** One author-layer produced the whole
kernel-reduction series (protocol authorship aside — see below), so `18`'s own agreement with `12`
is better understood as: *the same research effort did not contradict itself over time*, which is a
real, positive fact (self-consistency is not nothing), but a categorically weaker claim than
independent replication would be.

## The one genuine multi-party structure in this series, and its limits

`18` §0's own provenance ledger names **three** distinct layers: Protocol (an external "ChatGPT"
author), Execution (Claude Code CLI — the layer producing both narrative and executable output), and
Audit (also "ChatGPT," reviewing the Execution layer's own output). **This is the closest thing to
genuine multi-party structure in the series** — the Audit layer is, by the ledger's own account, a
different author from the Execution layer. Does this mean `18` (the Audit-response document) counts
as independent of `12` (an Execution-layer document)?

**Partially, and this needs precise treatment, not a blanket answer.** `18` is Execution's own
*response* to an external Audit — meaning its content is Execution's own synthesis of a critique from
elsewhere, not a verbatim independent document. The specific passage this study relies on most (§3.3,
"V6 was never the evidence... V6 is the contrast case") is Execution's own defense of its earlier
finding, written by the same layer that wrote `12` — **not** independent audit content itself. The
Audit layer's own original critique (referenced but not itself part of this series — "the external
audit... 2,410 lines," not admitted, not read by this study or MD-037) might carry a stronger claim
to independence, but it was never inspected. **Classification: `18`'s own defense of V6 remains
within-lineage (Execution-authored); the underlying Audit critique it responds to is a genuinely
separate, but unread and unadmitted, source.**

## Net finding

No claim in this study, or carried forward from MD-037, should be read as independent statistical
confirmation of anything. The corrected standard is applied to `08` (this study's own admissibility
recommendation section is written accordingly).
