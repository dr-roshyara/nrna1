# Phase 5E — Closure Verdict

## Population closure

**Supported.** P2 (237 files) and P3 (172 files) were both mechanically enumerated and fully
reconciled against their governed per-file records (0 gaps, 0 orphans in both). No sampling was
required or used.

## Document closure

**Supported.** All 409 P2/P3 documents received exactly one of the 8 required dispositions (`02`);
0 documents remained in `AMBIGUOUS / REQUIRES OBJECT ADJUDICATION` after full inspection.

## Object closure

**NOT supported.** At least 11 new object-register entries were found (`03`), most consequentially the
K-1 through K-7 family and the partially-evidenced Models A-F comparison (`05`). While no new entry
reaches structural correspondence with an existing 19-object set members beyond the single K-1↔K-1-B
pairing (`06`), the underlying question — "does the corpus's Kernel-object population terminate, or
does each new population sampled continue to surface further distinct candidates?" — is **not answered
in the negative or the positive**: this phase found fewer *wholly new* competing Kernel-state tuples
than Phase 5D did (most of P2's Kernel-adjacent content refines, audits, or governs an already-fixed
8-primitive state rather than proposing new alternatives), but it did find a definitive
authority-status register (K-1..K-7) that had not been directly sourced before, which is itself new
information changing the shape of the object population.

## Provenance closure

**Substantially supported for the specific items investigated.** The KERNEL-OBJ-04 attribution
question is now raw-source-confirmed at evidence level 1 (`07`), the strongest evidentiary state this
reconstruction has reached for that finding. The K-1 family's own governance history is now
documented via seq 1006. **Not supported globally** — the broader P2/P3 provenance graph was extended
only for the specific edges found in `07`, not exhaustively for all 409 documents' own inter-document
relationships (a task of a scale beyond this phase's own proportionate scope, and not required by the
authorization's own closure criteria, which asks for "the strongest provenance chain available from
the inspected corpus," not an exhaustive all-pairs graph).

## Equivalence closure

**Supported in the sense required**: every plausible pair this phase actually identified received an
explicit adjudication state (`06`) — none left silently unaddressed. **Not supported in the sense of
"all possible pairs have been checked"** — an all-pairs check across 19+11=30 objects was not
attempted; only pairs with a specific, evidenced reason to compare were adjudicated, per the
authorization's own "for every plausible pair" instruction (plausibility, not exhaustive combinatorial
coverage, is the trigger).

## K-1 closure

**Substantially supported.** The K-1 through K-7 family, and its relationship to K-2/K-3/K-6/K-7 as
named in Phase 5D's own audit, is now accounted for via an authoritative, raw-source-confirmed
register (`05`). The one remaining open question is whether K-1 (seq 1006) and K-1-B (Phase 5C's
KERNEL-OBJ-02, seq 1008) are the *same* object — resolved to structural correspondence, not formal
equivalence or identity.

## Minimality closure

**Supported.** Every substantive minimality-adjacent claim found in P2/P3 was dispositioned as
lexical/informal/semi-formal/formal or explicitly none-confirmed (`08`); 0 reach the formal level.

## Overall verdict

**PARTIAL CLOSURE — SPECIFIC POPULATIONS REMAIN OPEN.**

Population and document closure: **CLOSURE SUPPORTED** for P2 and P3.
Object, provenance (globally), and full equivalence closure: **CLOSURE NOT SUPPORTED** — genuine,
consequential findings remain (the K-1..K-7 register, the Models A-F comparison, the Knowledge-Space
foundation family) that were not previously part of any frozen register, and their full relationship
to the existing 19-object set is not exhaustively adjudicated.

**This is not a forced binary answer** — it reflects that document-level exhaustive inspection is a
different, and now-satisfied, kind of closure than object-level equivalence closure, which remains
open exactly where the evidence itself is incomplete.
