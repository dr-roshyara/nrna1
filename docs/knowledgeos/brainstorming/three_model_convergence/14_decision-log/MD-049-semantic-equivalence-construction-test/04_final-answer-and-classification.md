# MD-049 §4 — Required Final Answer

## Phase 7 — statistical discipline restated

This is a diagnostic formal study over three pre-registered pairs, not a statistical sample. No claim
of replication or generalization is made from these three pairs — they were selected because they are
this reconstruction's own most-characterized candidates (MD-042–048), not as a representative sample
of any larger population. The selection rule (already-identified candidates with the most existing
characterization) is stated here explicitly, as required.

## Required final answer

> **Does the existing semantic framework provide enough structure to test whether differently defined
> Kernel candidates are semantically the same, without circularly defining identity in terms of
> itself?**

**NO.**

**Exact smallest missing mathematical object, precisely named, not invented**: a concrete
instantiation of the observable-behavior function `Obs`/`Beh_𝔠` for at least one real candidate — i.e.,
an actual mapping from a named Kernel candidate (F1, F3, or F5) to a concrete trace/observable
description in the shape the source's own `Tr_K(s,n)`/`Obs_𝔠` formulas already define. **The formulas
exist; no one has ever filled them in for any real candidate.** This is a narrower, more precisely
targeted gap than "capability identity is missing" (MD-046/047) or "the breakthrough didn't resolve
this" (MD-048) — it is the exact next construction step, one level more concrete than either.

**Why the current framework cannot proceed without it**: `≡_sem` and `⪯_cap` are both *derivable*
formulas (§1) — they compute correctly once given two `Obs`/`Beh_𝔠` instances to compare. Every
downstream object (`MinKer`, `MinKer_/≡sem`, irreducibility, minimality) depends transitively on this
one input. Supplying it for even one candidate — without inventing a capability taxonomy, without
choosing a decomposition — would be a genuinely new, bounded, well-defined research task; supplying it
requires a modelling decision this study is not authorized to make.

## What this study establishes that MD-046/047/048 did not

MD-046/047 asked "does a capability-identity criterion exist" (answer: no). MD-048 asked "did the
breakthrough supply one" (answer: no). **This study asked a narrower, more actionable question — can
the existing MACHINERY be operated on the existing CANDIDATES — and found the machinery is sound (§3,
Phase 5) but has never been fed any real input (§2).** This is progress: it converts "capability
identity is missing" into "one specific function, for one specific candidate, needs to be
constructed" — a smaller, better-bounded gap than any prior phase in this arc named.
