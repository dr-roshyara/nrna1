## Primary audit question, answered up front (full evidence in `11`/`12`)

*Does the evidence actually justify Phase 5K's recommendation of Option E, or has Phase 5K introduced
an implicit governance preference that exceeds the evidence?*

**Answer: Phase 5K's recommendation is CONFIRMED WITH QUALIFICATIONS (verdict 5L-B).** Two specific,
material overstatements were found and corrected below; the core recommendation (Option E as a
defensible interim position, not ratified) survives, but its stated justification requires narrowing
in two places, and one genuinely new, consequential finding outside Phase 5K's own direct scope was
uncovered.

## Three headline findings

1. **"3 independent sources" for the Observation→Qualify→Evidence relationship is an overstatement,
   corrected this phase.** Re-checked directly: none of seq 0630 (step-049), seq 0795 (step-170), or
   Step 272A cites either of the other two. But all three are undated-independent in the statistical
   sense that matters less than this: they are three checkpoints in **one continuously-numbered
   research programme** (`phase_measure_theory/`'s own "Step N" series, spanning 2026-08-28 through
   2026-08-30), a corpus this reconstruction has itself repeatedly found to read like a continuing
   session transcript. **Corrected finding: three separate documents, no citation chain, but not
   demonstrated to be independently-arrived-at in the sense "independent sources" implies** —
   downgraded to "three separate, uncited restatements within one continuous research programme."
2. **Option E's own "preferable" framing smuggled a governance/architectural preference that Phase 5K
   did not fully disclose as such.** "Zero information loss" and "lowest governance complexity" are
   real, defensible, narrow claims — but Phase 5K's own recommendation language moved from these
   narrow claims to a broader "Option E is preferable" conclusion without fully accounting for the
   downstream costs the user's own counter-argument names (deferred complexity, ambiguity, translation
   cost at first use). **Corrected finding**: Option E remains defensible as a *low-risk, reversible*
   interim position, but Phase 5K's own language should not have been read as "Option E is the best
   architectural choice" — only as "Option E is the lowest-commitment choice," a narrower and more
   defensible claim.
3. **A new, consequential finding outside Phase 5K's own direct scope**: D285-1's own foundational
   citation for K-1's ratification — "`C-022` in `claim-registry`" — **cannot be verified anywhere in
   the corpus.** The only "C-022" found elsewhere (`step-152`'s own "Constitution v1.0" document) is
   about an unrelated topic (trace identity), and the file most plausibly named "claim-registry" (Step
   239, "kernel-genealogy-and-claim-registry") contains neither "C-022" nor the 8-primitive content
   D285-1 attributes to it. **This does not change K-2's own governance-unresolved status** (Phase 5L's
   own scope), but it means the implicit contrast this reconstruction has repeatedly drawn — "K-1 has
   real ratification, K-2 has none" — rests on a citation that is itself unverified. Flagged, not
   adjudicated (K-1's own status is outside Phase 5L's authorized scope).
