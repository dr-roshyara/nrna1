**Sources**: seq 0630 (step-049, 2026-08-28), seq 0795 (step-170, 2026-08-29), Step 272A (seq 0911,

### Independence test, per the authorization's §4

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Shared authorial origin | **Not independently verifiable, but strongly suggested by shared context** — all three sit within `phase_measure_theory/`'s own continuously-numbered "Step N" series, a corpus this reconstruction has repeatedly found to read as a continuing session transcript (Phase 5D/5E's own findings) |
| Copied text | **No** — re-checked this phase; the three documents' own wording for the O→E/Qualify relationship differs across all three (an equation in seq 0630, a gated transition with named conditions in seq 0795, a 3-argument function in Step 272A) — not verbatim duplication |
| Common predecessor | **Not found** — no document cites a fourth, earlier source that all three might independently derive from |
| Same research session | **Plausible, not provable** — the 3-day span (Aug 28–30) and continuous Step numbering are consistent with one extended, multi-day research session, but no explicit session-boundary marker was found either confirming or denying this |
| Same generated synthesis | **No** — each document's own content is distinct, not a copy-with-variation of a single synthesis |
| Common conceptual dependency | **Yes, and this is the load-bearing finding**: all three depend on the same underlying conceptual move (distinguishing raw Observation from qualified Evidence) — this is a **shared conceptual commitment**, not independent re-derivation from first principles each time |

### Direct citation check, re-performed this phase

`grep` confirms: seq 0795 does not cite "step-049"; Step 272A does not cite "step-170" or "step-049."
**No citation chain exists.**

## Verdict — the corrected formulation

**"3 independent sources" overstates the finding.** The correct, defensible formulation is:

> **Three separate documents, within one continuously-numbered research programme, restate a
> compatible Observation→Evidence-via-Qualify relationship without citing each other and without
> verbatim duplication — this is evidence of a *stable, recurring conceptual commitment* across the
> programme's own multi-day span, not evidence of three *independently arrived-at* confirmations in
> the statistical sense the word "independent" implies.**

This is a genuine downgrade in evidentiary framing, though **not a reversal of the underlying fact**:
the relationship itself (Observation→Evidence via Qualify) remains the best-attested single
relationship in this entire investigation — it is the *characterization of that attestation's
strength* that required correction, not the relationship's existence.

## Consequence

Phase 5K's `07`/`10`/`14` (Option C's own justification, the evidence ledger, and the recommendation)
each use "3 independent sources" language. **This finding narrows, but does not eliminate, Option C's
own evidentiary basis** — addressed directly in `05`/`11`.
