## Verdict

**Options A–E are not exhaustive in the abstract, but are sufficiently complete relative to what the
evidence supports.** One genuine, minor gap is named: **Candidate F-prime — "Observation and Evidence
as separate knowledge objects, not fields of Assertion at all."** This candidate is **not** added to
the option set (that would exceed this phase's own audit-only scope), but is recorded as a legitimate
omission for any future phase to consider, since it is logically available and was not previously
named anywhere in Phase 5C–5K.
