# Claim Audit — MD-031 and MD-032 Re-Examined

## MD-031's claim: "the input-carrier field is CLOSED BY SOURCE, via a chain built from `04` + `06`"

**Confirmed, and extended.** This study, reading `03`/`04` cold and independently, reaches the same
input-carrier conclusion (`03`) via the identical chain, and additionally closes several fields
MD-031 did not examine (atom, output carrier, non-reducible responsibility, corpus-support rating,
state effects) — none of which contradicts MD-031's own scope, which was narrower by design (focused
specifically on the input-carrier/executable-rule comparison).

## MD-031's finding: "`06` is silent on `V6`"

**Confirmed, and extended to the full admissible population.** MD-031 checked only `06`. This study
checks all three admitted files and finds the silence is total — `03` and `04` are equally silent on
`V6`, though (newly established here) they are **not** silent on variants generally: `03`/`04`
explicitly name `V1`, `V4`, `V5`. This is a materially more precise finding than "the admissible
lane never discusses robustness variants" — it does; it just never names this particular one.

## MD-031's verdict: E — PARTIAL CONVERGENCE

**Not disturbed.** This study's findings sharpen the boundary of what MD-031 called "partial" (now
precisely: the happy-path contract is closed; preconditions/postconditions/failure semantics and V6
are not) without changing the overall verdict — MD-031's own scope (the narrative-executable
relationship specifically) is a different question from this study's own (specification sufficiency
for composition research), and neither supersedes the other.

## MD-032's explicit disclaimer: "admission does NOT establish... composition sufficiency... V6
resolution"

**Directly confirmed by this study's own findings**, not merely restated: this study independently
establishes that composition sufficiency (in the "fully specified operator" sense) is not yet
reached (`03`, `06`) and that `V6` remains unresolved (`04`) — MD-032's own disclaimer was accurate
in advance of the evidence this study gathered, not merely a cautious hedge.

## Net effect

No prior claim from MD-031 or MD-032 is contradicted. This study's contribution is precision: a
census-based (not summary-based) confirmation of exactly which fields are closed and which remain
open, and a sharper, corrected characterization of the admissible lane's own relationship to
variant-testing generally (it does discuss variants — V1/V4/V5 — just not V6).
