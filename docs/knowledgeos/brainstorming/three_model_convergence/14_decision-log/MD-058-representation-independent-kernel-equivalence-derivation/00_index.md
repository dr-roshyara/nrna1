## 4 · Central result, stated at the strength the derivation actually supports

**A representation-independent relation was derived — `K₁ ≈_{Q,𝒪} K₂` (parameterized observational
equivalence) — as the necessary consequence of the already-established requirement set, but only
under an explicit, previously-implicit sub-requirement this derivation itself surfaced (𝒪's own
representation-neutrality, `R1a`), and only as the WEAKEST member of a family the requirements do not
uniquely pin down.** It was successfully instantiated for exactly one candidate (F3, reusing MD-050's
own `Reach(Ops(K))` construction) and could not be instantiated for F1/F4/F5/F6/K0 without inventing
semantics — which this phase declines to do, per its own hard boundary. **Success condition: a mixed
B/D result** (§06) — a minimal candidate relation IS derived with explicit open assumptions (B), and
the cross-candidate comparison fails for a precisely identified reason — missing `Obs`/`Beh`
primitives for five of six candidates (D) — not because the relation itself is undefined.
