# DDD Boundary Audit — Five Distinct Boundaries, Kept Separate

## Filesystem boundary

`docs/knowledgeos/brainstorming/` vs. `docs/knowledgeos/research/` — a real, machine-observable
directory split. **Established.**

## Research-lane boundary

`kernel-reduction/`'s own self-label ("research experiment"), its own falsification/negative-results/
supersession sections, and its own experiment ID suggest a distinctly organized research process.
**Strongly supported as a research-lane distinction** — this is organizational evidence (an index, a
revision history, a naming scheme), not merely asserted.

## Model boundary (Model-B membership)

M0030's citation + same-day dating supports a *relationship* between this lane and Model B's own
admissible evidence, but **does not establish that `kernel-reduction/` IS Model-B evidence** — it
establishes a plausible common origin, no more. **Relationship supported; membership not established.**

## Bounded-context boundary (DDD sense)

**This is where MD-026 overstated.** "Self-governing" implies independent authority/ownership in the
DDD sense — evidence for this would be: an explicit statement of ownership, an explicit context map
relating it to Model B, or an explicit statement that it is (or is not) part of the reconstruction
corpus. **None of these was found.** What was found — an index, a falsification section, a
supersession discipline — is evidence of *internal organization*, not of *bounded-context authority
relative to Model B*. **Corrected verdict: `PLAUSIBLE SEPARATE RESEARCH LANE`, not `SOURCE-ATTESTED
SEPARATE CONTEXT` and not `STRONGLY SUPPORTED SEPARATE RESEARCH LANE`** — MD-026's own "self-governing"
language is downgraded one full level on the required scale.

## Governance boundary

No document establishes who has authority to admit or exclude `research/` from the reconstruction
corpus. **Genuinely unresolved** — matches MD-026's own claim 11 finding, narrowed only in scope
(§01's claim 11: "none found in the documents read," not "none exists anywhere").

## Why keeping these separate matters

MD-026's own "Outcome C (separate research context)" verdict conflated the research-lane boundary
(strongly supported) with the bounded-context boundary (not established) into one combined claim. The
corrected position, carried into `06`/`07`: **the research-lane distinction is real and well-evidenced;
the DDD bounded-context/authority claim implied by "self-governing" is not.**
