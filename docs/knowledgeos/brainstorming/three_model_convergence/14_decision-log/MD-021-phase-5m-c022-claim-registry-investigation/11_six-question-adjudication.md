# Phase 5M — Six-Question Adjudication (per the authorization's §14, kept explicitly separate)

## Question 1 — Does the cited C-022 exist?

**As an identifier**: yes, once, at step-152 §152.26. **As a citation of K-1's own ratification
evidence**: no — nothing bearing that identifier concerns K-1. Answer: **NO, not for this subject.**

## Question 2 — Does it contain the claimed K-1 content?

**No.** The one artifact carrying "C-022" (step-152) does not mention K-1, `K_t`, the 8 primitives, or
attack-class testing.

## Question 3 — Does it establish ratification?

**Not the artifact bearing the identifier "C-022."** A genuine ratification act (seq 0764's own D-FA-6
ruling) exists, but it concerns *naming*, not the combined "8-primitive/50-attack-class" claim D285-1's
row presents as one fact.

## Question 4 — Does it identify an authority?

**seq 0764 does**: HPA, expanded this phase as "Highest Project Authority." **The "C-022" artifact
itself (step-152) does not** — it carries no authority marker at all.

## Question 5 — Is that authority legitimately established?

**No** — re-tested specifically in this citation's own context (`08`); HPA's own organizational
legitimacy remains self-declared, not externally verified, unchanged from Phase 5J/5L's own findings.

## Question 6 — Does D285-1 accurately cite it?

**No.** D285-1's own citation ("`step-049`; C-022 in `claim-registry`") bundles a genuine source
(`step-049`) with an identifier (`C-022`) and a document name (`claim-registry`) that, taken together,
do not point to any single existing artifact. The *content* D285-1 attributes to this citation is
**real and traceable — but to three separate documents, under none of which does the specific
identifier "C-022" or the specific phrase "claim-registry" actually appear with matching content.**

## Why these six answers must not be collapsed into one verdict

Answering "is the citation valid?" with a single yes/no would erase the genuinely important
distinction this phase found: **the underlying evidence is largely real** (Questions 1–2 concern the
identifier specifically; the *content* behind D285-1's claim is corroborated elsewhere, per `04`/`05`)
**while the specific citation mechanism is not accurate** (Question 6). A single collapsed verdict
would either overstate the problem (implying K-1's evidence base is fabricated, which it is not) or
understate it (implying the citation is merely imprecise, when it in fact points to two confirmed
unrelated artifacts).
