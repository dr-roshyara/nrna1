## Does the characterization demonstrate this is a distinct candidate family?

**On formal grounds, yes** — `K=(𝒜,ℛ)` is a real, structurally distinct object from every member of
F1–F8 and from K0. **On evidentiary grounds, the case for registering it as an established candidate is
actively weaker than K0's own** (MD-052), because — unlike K0, which was merely *unverified* — this
construction has an **identified internal contradiction**, confirmed by the thread's own second pass,
and its own terminal document explicitly declines to call it closed. **No label is assigned by this
phase.** Registering a self-admittedly-contradictory construction as a formal candidate (even
unlabeled-but-tracked) would need the user's own explicit decision, not a default outcome of
characterization.

## Admissibility

**Population**: the entire `brainstorming/verification/` top-level directory — 71 `.md` files, spanning
`V0-theory-corpus-map.md` through `THEORY-STATUS-VERDICT.md` — read in full this phase, plus the 9
`spec/` files already admitted narrow-scope in MD-052. Same provenance tier as MD-052's own admission
(§01); no stronger and no weaker.

**Recommended scope, mirroring MD-052's own narrow-admission discipline**: admit for further
characterization/comparison research only — not as an established kernel candidate, not merged into
F1–F8, no composition test, no GA-001/GA-038 resolution, admission ≠ adoption. Given the volume, a
finer-grained admission (e.g. only the terminal-state documents, or only the load-bearing construction
documents) is also a legitimate option, and is offered as an alternative below.
