## What changes, mechanically

specification/composition/variant research is: `03-capability-model.md` + `04-operator-
contracts.md` (MD-028-DQ-1) + `06-composition-rules.md` (MD-032) + `12-randomized-results.md` (this

**`V6` is now present in the admissible population** — the single most consequential mechanical
change this decision makes. MD-033's own "V6 is not a live blocker" reasoning (which rested
explicitly on V6's absence from admissible evidence) no longer applies unmodified; any future study
drawing on the newly-admitted evidence must treat V6 as a live, source-evidenced alternative to be
adjudicated, not as an absent question.
