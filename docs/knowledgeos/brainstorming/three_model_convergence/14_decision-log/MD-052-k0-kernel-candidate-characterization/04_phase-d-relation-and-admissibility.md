## Does the characterization demonstrate K0 is a distinct candidate family?

**On formal grounds, yes, provisionally**: K0's apparatus (typed frames, functions, predicates —
closer to a multi-sorted algebraic signature) is not a restatement of any of F1 (flat primitive
list), F3 (atom/operator/carrier signature), F4 (a specific `K_t`/`Δ_t` tuple family), F5 (a DDD
aggregate design), or F6 (a single narrative document) — see Phase C Claim 1. **On evidentiary
grounds, the case for treating it as a scientifically *established*, independently-corroborating new
candidate is weaker**: its single strongest, most consequential claim (`K_t`-representation-
independence) is admittedly unverified even by its own author, and its central negative finding about
F1's own object is not independent corroboration in the strong sense — it shares an upstream source.

**This phase does not assign a candidate label.** Per the authorization, that decision belongs to the
admissibility decision below, not to this characterization.
