# MD-052 Phase C — Hostile Audit of Seven Named Claims

Discipline: the goal is what the source establishes, not whether a result agrees with this
reconstruction's own prior findings. No claim is refuted merely for being unfamiliar or inconvenient.

## Claim 1 — "K0 is a genuine kernel candidate rather than another representation of a previously known kernel."

**Verdict: PARTIALLY TRUE, and the true part matters.** K0's own formal apparatus (7 typed frames —
sorts, functions, predicates, e.g. `Ω:W→O`, `ev:𝒦×R×EC→𝒮_gap`, `δ:𝒦×ℰ⇀𝒦`) is a **different kind of
object** than F1's flat 8-primitive noun list, F3's operator/atom signature, or F5's DDD aggregate —
it is closer to a multi-sorted algebraic theory than to any of them. It is not "F1 relabeled." But K0
does not arrive at this apparatus in a vacuum — it explicitly evaluates and *rejects* F1's own
candidate object (§Phase A) as insufficient, positioning itself as an alternative answer to the same
underlying question ("what is the smallest set of things the theory's results actually need") rather
than a wholly unrelated question. **Not confirmed identical to any of F1/F3/F4/F5/F6; also not shown
to be addressing a different question than they are.**

## Claim 2 — "Its results are actually independent of any particular `K_t` representation."

**Verdict: NOT ESTABLISHED — and K0 itself agrees.** As found in Phase B, K0 tags this claim
`⚑VERIFIER INFERENCE, to be adversarially checked at Level 1`, and the programme's own checkpoint
trail shows the session **stopped before that check ran**. This is the single most consequential
claim in the document and it is, by its own author's admission, an unverified hypothesis, not a
result.

## Claim 3 — "The five-way kernel distinction prevents the naming collision with F1 rather than merely renaming it."

**Verdict: NEITHER — it is orthogonal to the question.** K0's own §1 table is a real, useful piece of
*internal* hygiene (it keeps K0 from conflating itself with the 049-tuple, the DDD shared kernel, the
constitutional kernel, or the K1–K8 capacity list, within K0's own document). But K0 was written
2026-08-29 — **before this reconstruction's own F1–F8 inventory existed** (built starting
2026-09-07) — so it cannot have "prevented" a collision with a taxonomy it never saw. Using K0
alongside F1–F8 today does not inherit any collision-prevention from K0's own table; it adds a
**sixth** taxonomy layer on top of the fifth (F1–F8) and the several K-1-label collisions this
reconstruction has already found (MD-042). Reconciling them is unstarted work, not a solved problem
K0 hands over.

## Claim 4 — "The irredundancy/removal tests establish necessity only, not sufficiency."

**Verdict: TRUE, and K0 says so itself.** Verbatim, `K0` §2.4: *"Sufficiency for the FULL claimed
theory is NOT asserted."* This phase's own Phase B additionally notes the necessity claim itself is
weaker than it may first appear — the removal test shows each frame is necessary *for A4's own
specific proof of a specific theorem*, not that no alternative proof route through a different frame
combination could exist. K0 does not overclaim here; if anything it is more conservative than its own
removal-test table might suggest to a fast reader.

## Claim 5 — "T-K1–T-K10 really have the stated proof status."

**Verdict: CONSISTENT WITH WHAT IS SHOWN, on a conceptual read; not independently re-derived.** Every
theorem's labeled status (PROVEN / PROVEN UNDER ASSUMPTIONS / PROVEN with a qualifier) matches what
its own `A4` proof text actually argues — e.g. T-K3's "under assumptions" label correctly reflects
that its proof explicitly invokes KA1/KA2/KA5; T-K7's qualifier correctly reflects a genuine
correction the document makes to its own first-pass claim (an interleaving counterexample the
document itself constructs and then re-qualifies with continuity). On a conceptual pass, no proof
raised an internal red flag (T-K1/T-K2 are elementary composition/quotient facts, correctly stated;
T-K7's invariance-of-domain argument correctly identifies the standard topological obstruction;
T-K6a's contradiction argument is a direct and valid two-line construction). **This phase did not
symbolically re-derive any proof line-by-line** — that would be a substantially larger undertaking
than characterization, and is not claimed here.

## Claim 6 — "The three missing mechanisms really are prerequisites rather than merely the programme's chosen formalization strategy."

**Verdict: NOT CONFIRMED — they look at least partly artifact-of-formalization.** The identity
calculus (KA3) is needed specifically because P5 (Evidence frame) chose to represent evidence as a
multiset normalized by a given equivalence `~` and preorder `≺`; η is needed specifically because P4
(Gap frame) chose to type `ev` over an explicit `EC` argument. A differently-formalized kernel need
not necessarily reproduce either requirement in the same shape — and, concretely, **this
reconstruction's own F3 (kernel-reduction, MD-050/MD-051) supplies no comparable "identity calculus"
or "η" gap at all**, because its own formalization (atoms/carriers/`Reach`) does not require one in
that form. This does not mean K0's three gaps are illusory or unimportant *for K0's own construction*
— they plainly are load-bearing there — but the claim that they are prerequisites of *any* adequate
KnowledgeOS kernel, independent of formalization choice, is not established by anything read this
phase.

## Claim 7 — "The impossibility/identifiability results are genuinely applicable to the KnowledgeOS kernel question rather than only to the programme's formalized abstraction."

**Verdict: SPLIT — the mathematics is general; its applicability to KnowledgeOS specifically is
unverified.** T-K1/T-K2 (observational impossibility; identifiability = factorization through `Ω`)
are standard, elementary facts about any system with an observation function `Ω:W→O` — genuinely
portable mathematics, not an artifact peculiar to K0's own notation. **But** whether KnowledgeOS's own
corpus concepts actually instantiate `W`/`O`/`Ω` the way K0 assumes is a separate question this phase
did not check — `P1`'s own source anchor ("031 §17–18") was not opened (Phase B). The theorems are
real and general; their *grounding* in KnowledgeOS's own primary corpus material, rather than in K0's
own restated abstraction of it, remains to be verified.

## Summary posture for Phase D

Nothing found in this audit refutes K0. Nothing found in this audit confirms K0 as a resolved or
independently-corroborated result either. The honest posture: **a real, disciplined, partly
self-critical piece of mathematics, built one methodological step removed from primary KnowledgeOS
corpus grounding, whose single most important claim (`K_t`-representation-independence) is an
admitted, never-checked hypothesis, and whose central negative finding (the 049-tuple/F1 object is
insufficient) is not independent of this reconstruction's own already-existing, separately-reached
finding about the same object (Phase 5N: N2/"L2 candidate"/OQ-2-open) — it is a second angle on it,
built from a shared upstream source, not a second, unrelated confirmation.**
