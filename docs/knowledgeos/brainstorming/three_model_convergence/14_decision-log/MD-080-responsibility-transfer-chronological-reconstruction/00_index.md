# MD-080 — Responsibility-Transfer Chronological Reconstruction

## Purpose

User accepted MD-079 as a valid bounded composition finding but rejected its conditional necessity
verdict as terminal. Governing rule restated: **RECONSTRUCT FIRST → RECONCILE LATER → CANONICALIZE
LAST.** The question is not "how do we complete T21's `Det_r`/`EvalReq` route" but **"what did the
theory actually become over time, and where did the semantic/computational responsibilities of
`Det_r`/`EvalReq`/`EC.Rules`/`standard`/`Acceptance` and the rest of the family eventually end up —
completed elsewhere, moved, refined, split, absorbed, replaced, deliberately externalized, duplicated,
or genuinely never completed?"** Explicit new discipline: **object identity and semantic-responsibility
identity are tracked separately** — two different objects may perform the same responsibility; a name
match is neither necessary nor sufficient for a responsibility transfer.

## Method

No frozen artifact reopened. MD-067–079 reused as the frozen evidentiary baseline for the T5/T21
material already verified. New work this phase: (1) direct verification of two specific,
already-flagged-but-never-fully-chased leads from this same reconstruction's own much earlier phases
(pre-MD-067) — `M0127`'s own `Standing(p)` construction (flagged in MD-063 as evaluating something
structurally close to, but distinct from, `Sat(K_t,r)`) and the `Warrant`/"surviving a defeater"
investigation (MD-037–041, already exhaustive, reused not redone); (2) targeted searches for
`Assessment`, `Verdict`, `criterion`, `validation rule`, and related candidate successor-objects across
the math lane and the `kernel/`/`verification/` lanes; (3) construction of the required registries.

## Central finding, disclosed first

**`Det_r`'s own intended responsibility — evaluating a specific requirement/proposition and producing a
verdict — has a genuine, rich, formally-typed, empirically-tested predecessor: `Standing(p) =
(S⁺(p),S⁻(p),R(p),P(p),Ctx(p),Cond(p))`, born 2026-09-02 (`M0127`), four days before T21.** Its
six-component structure (positive/negative support, reason, provenance, context, condition) is
strikingly close in shape to `Eval`'s own worked-example output vector
`⟨Support,CounterSupport,Uncertainty,Conflict,Dependencies,Assumptions,Justification⟩` (Part VI, T21).
**`Standing(p)` was empirically validated** (M0127's own already-established result, reused from this
reconstruction's own MD-061–063: "preserves 12/14 scenarios versus Classical 2/14... a deliberately
strong, six-component semantic-equivalence criterion") — this is materially stronger evidence than
anything T21's own `Eval`/`EvalReq`/`Det_r` ever received (zero invocations, per MD-076). **And yet
`Standing(` occurs zero times anywhere in the T21 rewrite** — confirmed by direct grep across all 21
parts. T21 does not cite, extend, or reuse it; the responsibility was not transferred, it was
independently re-attempted and re-abandoned.

`EC.Rules`'/`standard`'s own responsibility — a checkable acceptance/policy rule — has **no demonstrated
successor anywhere**: the `Warrant`/"surviving a defeater" investigation (MD-037–041, reused) already
exhaustively searched for exactly this kind of object and found no formal definition anywhere;
`Assessment(...)` is confirmed, by direct source inspection, to be external-literature-extraction
vocabulary (Titelbaum, I.J. Good, Rice — each paper's own borrowed notion, inconsistent argument lists
across papers, no native KnowledgeOS formal object); `Verdict(` occurs zero times as a formal function
anywhere in the corpus.
