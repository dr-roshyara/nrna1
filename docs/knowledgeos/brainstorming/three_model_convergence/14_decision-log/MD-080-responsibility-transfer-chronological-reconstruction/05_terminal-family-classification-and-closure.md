## Explicit statement: the family is not forced to one verdict

The single most important correction this phase makes to the picture as it stood after MD-079: **the
`Det_r`/`EvalReq` gap is real at the object level (D) but the responsibility it was meant to carry is
not a genuine corpus gap at all (B) — it has been discharged, more than once, by different objects the
corpus itself never connected to T21.** This is precisely the distinction the mission's own §4 asked
this phase to maintain, and collapsing it in either direction would misstate the evidence: calling the
whole family "D" ignores `Standing(p)`'s own real, tested completeness; calling it "B" without the
object-level qualifier would wrongly suggest T21's own equation is actually usable.

## Smallest genuinely remaining semantic/computational gap

Narrower than MD-079's own finding, now that responsibility-transfer has been checked: **there is no
remaining gap in whether the *responsibility* can be discharged** (it demonstrably can, several ways).
**The remaining gap is entirely in whether any of the existing discharges can be shown, by the corpus
itself rather than by invention, to be what T21's own `r`/`EC`/`Γ` intended** — and no source anywhere
states that connection. This is not a research question this reconstruction can resolve by further
reading; it is a genuine absence of a corpus-native bridge, which no amount of additional census
changes.
