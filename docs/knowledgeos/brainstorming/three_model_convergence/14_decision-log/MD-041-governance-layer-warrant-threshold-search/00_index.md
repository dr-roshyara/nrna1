`Γ` = "satisfaction rules" per requirement, a `Closed(EC)` predicate requiring every requirement to
have a semantic definition and satisfaction rule before the contract is valid. It does **not** supply
a concrete satisfaction rule for the kernel-reduction sense of "warrant" (assign warrant to a
`Claim`/`Hypothesis` given `Evidence`) — its own worked examples (`RestoreTestPassed`,
`ArchitectureApprovalGranted`) are drawn from a different domain (organizational/production-migration
assurance), not epistemic claim-assessment. The document's own closing section (§25E.41) opens a
further, still-unresolved "Governance Conflict Algebra" as the next research step — the author's own
assessment is that contract *derivation* (resolving conflicting governing sources) remains "the next
real mathematical barrier," not solved here.
