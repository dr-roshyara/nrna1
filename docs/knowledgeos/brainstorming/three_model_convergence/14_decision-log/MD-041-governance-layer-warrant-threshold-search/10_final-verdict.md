# Final Verdict

## Classification scheme (constructed for this study's own question, consistent with MD-039/040's
own style)

A — Governance-layer threshold found and computable
**B — Governance-layer container/structure found, threshold content missing**
C — Governance-layer concept named but not structured
D — No governance-layer treatment found
E — Evidence insufficient

## Primary verdict

**B — Governance-layer container/structure found; the threshold content itself remains missing.**

## Why B, and not the other four

- **Not A** — no source, in any of the three threads examined, supplies a computable satisfaction
  rule for a `Validation`-type requirement (`05`).
- **Not C** — this would understate what seq 0583 supplies. It is not merely a named concept; it is
  a genuinely developed formal structure (`EC=(R,Γ,A,V)`, `Closed(EC)`, `EvalContract`, a proposed
  Ubiquitous Language, `CandidateRequirement ≠ ContractRequirement`) — real DDD-adjacent structure,
  the richest found anywhere in this MD-036–041 sequence (`06`).
- **Not D** — a governance-layer treatment plainly exists and was found (`03`).
- **Not E** — the search, though bounded, produced a clear, well-evidenced, chronologically-anchored
  answer.

## What this means for the original question

**Yes, a governance-layer specification exists — of the *concept* and its *container*, not of the
*threshold itself*.** The corpus separately, and more precisely than either kernel-reduction or the
Titelbaum thread managed on their own, distinguishes `Validation` from `Governance` as coordinate
requirement types and gives `Validation` a formal slot (`Γ`) for a satisfaction rule — but never
fills that slot with content for any epistemic-claim-assessment case, and its own author explicitly
names the remaining gap (conflict/authority resolution among governing sources) as unsolved.

## Net effect on the original "surviving a defeater" question

**Unchanged — still no computation rule.** What has changed is the *map* of where such a rule would
plausibly need to live, if it is ever specified: inside a `Validated(r)`-typed requirement's own `Γ`
component, within an `EC`, evaluated by a process this document names but does not build.
