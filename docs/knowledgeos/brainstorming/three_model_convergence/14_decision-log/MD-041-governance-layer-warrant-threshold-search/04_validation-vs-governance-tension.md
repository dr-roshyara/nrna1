## Reading the tension precisely — not resolved, but characterized

Kernel-reduction's own claim conflates what seq 0583's own formal vocabulary keeps apart: "warrant"
(assessing whether a claim is sufficiently supported) maps most naturally onto seq 0583's
`Validated(r)` category, not its `Authorized(r)` category — and seq 0583 treats these as distinct by
design (§25E.14, §25E.23's own Ubiquitous Language list keeps `ValidationRequirement` and
`GovernanceRequirement` as separate terms). **Two of the three threads (the math lane and this
newly-found thread) agree that warrant/validation and governance are not the same thing; only
kernel-reduction's own DDD document conflates them.**

## What this does NOT establish

This is **not** grounds to declare kernel-reduction's own claim wrong, or to select one thread's
vocabulary as authoritative over another's. Per this session's own established discipline (MD-023
onward): a majority among three independently-developed threads is not proof, and the three threads
address genuinely different scopes (kernel-reduction: an epistemic-operator minimality experiment;
`M0032`: a Bayesian-epistemology-derived critique; seq 0583: an organizational/production-migration
assurance framework). The correct, bounded conclusion is that **the corpus does not speak with one
voice on whether "warrant"/"validation" belongs inside or outside governance** — a genuine, now
three-way-documented open question, not a two-against-one vote settling it.

## Relevance to the kernel-reduction lane's own "the standard is governance" claim

Given seq 0583's own vocabulary, kernel-reduction's own claim is more precisely restated (using the
newly-found framework's own terms, without asserting they are formally the same object across
threads — see `05`) as: *the warrant threshold might be better modeled as a `ValidationRequirement`
within an `EpistemicContract`, not folded into `Authorized(r)`/governance at all.* This is offered as
an observation this study's own evidence supports, not as a correction to kernel-reduction's own
frozen text (unmodified, per `11`).
