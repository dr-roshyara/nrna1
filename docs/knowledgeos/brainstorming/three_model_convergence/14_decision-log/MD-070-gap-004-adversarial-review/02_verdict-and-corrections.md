# MD-070 §02 — Verdict and Corrections

## Calibrated final verdict

**GAP-004 disposition: DOWNGRADED, not REFUTED.**

The Theory-00-21 definition `Sat(K,r,Γ)=Det_r(EvalReq(K,r,EC,Γ),EC)` is not mathematically wrong, is
not withdrawn anywhere in the corpus, and is not a repeat of the earlier retired attempts in kind (it
does not collapse under the CE-1 factivity obstruction that killed `Sat_c`, and it does not commit
M0136's specific FOL-entailment error). It is a genuine structural advance: for the first time in the
876-file traversal, the tracked chain has an object with the *right type signature* — a name
(`Det_r`), a domain (`𝒱 × EC`), and a codomain (`𝕊_sat`) — sitting where an unnamed, unspecified
"interpretation/evaluation step" had stood since T5.

But Finding 5 (§01) is decisive: **the theory that proposes this definition never once executes it.**
A definition that names its own missing computation without ever computing it, even in its own
flagship illustration, has not closed the gap MD-057–068 were tracking — it has re-described the gap
more precisely, with a name and a type. This is real progress (a corpus-wide first), but it is not
the closure that MD-067 ("upgraded toward A") and MD-069 (T21 as "CORPUS-SUPPORTED, DEFINED, PROVED")
credited it with.

**Verdict, in the vocabulary this reconstruction has used throughout: T21's terminal state moves from
"DEFINED-BUT-UNREVIEWED" to "DEFINED (type-level), NOT COMPUTED — reviewed and found structurally
sound but operationally vacant."** GAP-004 itself moves from UNRESOLVED/UNRECORDABLE to **CLOSED WITH
QUALIFICATION**: the adversarial review MD-068 called for has now been performed, and its answer is
that the definition survives scrutiny as a type-level contribution while failing to survive scrutiny
as a computed closure of the F4 chain.
