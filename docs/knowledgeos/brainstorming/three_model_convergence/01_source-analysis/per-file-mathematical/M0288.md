## Summary

Clarifies that setting a dimensional component to zero (d_i=0) means 'the influence of dimension i is treated as neutralized under the current model,' not that d_i does not exist. Proposes an explicit influence-valued encoding (+/0/-/? for supporting/neutralized/opposing/undetermined influence) as observations or modeling assignments rather than intrinsic properties. Shows the zero assumption need not be stable across time: Influence_i(K_t,Q)=0 followed later by Influence_i(K_{t+1},Q')!=0 is not a contradiction, since the state, question, or relations may have changed (Zero_i^t -> NonZero_i^{t+1}).
