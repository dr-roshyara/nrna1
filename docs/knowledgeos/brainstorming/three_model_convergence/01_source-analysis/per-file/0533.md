## Tier 2
**TRIGGERED — LANDMARK: "Computational Closure 4 — Evidence," delivering the corpus's first fully
axiomatized theory (EVD-1..EVD-11).** Opens: "Evidence is not a property of a document. Evidence is a
relationship between an observation and a proposition under a context and an evaluation rule." Defines
`Evidence(O,P,C,R)`, yielding `Evidence≠Observation`, `Evidence≠Source`, `Evidence≠Support`,
`Evidence≠Truth`. Establishes `Source→Observation→EvidenceRelation→EpistemicAssessment` and demonstrates
Evidence is strictly relational: `Evidence(O,P_1)≠Evidence(O,P_2)`.

**Formalizes Evidence** `E=(O,P,R,C,T,Q,Π)` and separates `Evidence≠EvidenceQuality`. Works through six
evidence categories, delivering `Evidence(PersonBelieves(P),P)≠Evidence(PersonVerified(P),P)` and
`LLMOutput≠EvidenceOfTruth` (though LLM output can itself be evidence of what the LLM generated/believed).
Delivers an explicit "what is NOT evidence" catalog: mere assertion, unsupported opinion,
`ModelConfidence≠Evidence`, popularity, `RepeatedEvidence≠IndependentEvidence`, circular reasoning. Delivers
a governance distinction: `Constitution≠EvidenceOfDomainFact` — instead `Constitution→Constraint`,
`Constraint+Observation→ComplianceAssessment`.

**Delivers the file's central innovation**: evidence quality is a VECTOR, not a scalar —
**`Q_E=(R,Re,D,I,F,X,P)`** — "much more faithful than confidence=0.87." Establishes
`EvidenceQuality=f(E,P,C,R)` and delivers a major refinement: evidence comparison is a PARTIAL order
(`E_1⪰_{P,C,R}E_2`), admitting genuine incomparability. Formalizes an **Evidence Dependency Graph**
`G_E=(V_E,E_E)` with typed edges, resolving the corpus's recurring independent-evidence problem
(0528/0530/0532). Delivers corroboration without additivity (`Support(E_1+E_2)≠Support(E_1)+Support(E_2)`)
and contradictory-evidence preservation (`Conflict(E_1,E_2,P)` retains both, for Zero to detect).

**Delivers a rigorous conclusion theory**: `Conclusion=Inference(Premises,Rule,Context)`, introducing
**four formally distinct conclusion types** — Deductive, Inductive, Abductive, Computational/derived — and
a dedicated `Conclusion` object `C_n=(P,Γ,R,κ,Σ,Π,t)`. Delivers `LogicalValidity≠EmpiricalTruth`. A
four-observation worked example (Nexus API/container/LLM/architecture document) shows identical evidence
supports different propositions differently, and that a normative document is never evidence of a factual
claim.

**Connects Evidence to Ideal State**, delivering **`EpistemicCorrectness≠DomainAdequacy`**: "KnowledgeOS
can know something accurately while that thing is undesirable" — a profound sharpening of the corpus's
Discrepancy theory. Proposes an Evidence lifecycle, an "Epistemic Evidence" bounded context, and five core
functions (IdentifyEvidence/EvaluateEvidence/CompareEvidence/Aggregate/Infer/Conclude). Sharpens Support as
a RESULT of assessment (`Support(P)=Assess(ℰ_P,P,C,R)`), never a stored property.

**Delivers a thirteen-row computational-closure status table** extending 0532's nine-row dashboard, and
**the file's deepest formal result**: "KnowledgeOS does not compute Truth directly" — it computes a fully
backward-traceable epistemic state (`K_{t+1}=δ(K_t,Observations,Evidence,Rules,Context)`), proposed as
potentially the system's defining characteristic.

**Resolves two genuine design decisions**: (A) absence is not automatically evidence of absence —
`NoObservation≠EvidenceOfAbsence` unless the observation mechanism is sufficiently complete; (B) Bayesian
probability is a PLUGGABLE inference policy, not KnowledgeOS's foundational epistemic model.

**Closes freezing the corpus's first axiomatic theory**: **EVD-1 through EVD-11** (relational; grounded in
observation/provenance; proposition-relative; multidimensional quality; context/policy-dependent
comparison; independence≠repetition; contradiction preserved; evidence≠truth; conclusions need explicit
inference rules; conclusions must be traceable; and, most important, **no scalar confidence value may
replace the underlying evidence structure**). Names the next closure target: **"Inference and
Conclusion."**
