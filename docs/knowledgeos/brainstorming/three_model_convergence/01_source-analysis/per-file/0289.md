## Tier 2
**TRIGGERED.** Direct continuation and deepening of 0288's per-agent-projection model. Central move:
`S_t^A ≠ S_t^B` does **not** imply `Reality_A ≠ Reality_B`. Explicitly rejects an over-strong
non-contradiction requirement — legitimate disagreement can arise from different time, aspect, Level of
Abstraction, context, or incompleteness (worked "service healthy/unhealthy" example resolved via
time/layer/scope differences) — refining the principle to *semantic reconcilability*, not syntactic
agreement. Introduces **epistemic orientation** `O(S_t)=(scope,purpose,LoA,regime,time)` as a new required
field, letting two states be compared by their orientations before their sentences. Names the **Projection
Compatibility Principle** and separates **four distinct senses of "truth"** in a multi-projection system
(local consistency / cross-projection compatibility / truth orientation / reality adequacy). Reframes
knowledge evolution as coarse→refined→expanded→qualified→corrected→recontextualized rather than
false→true (a worked five-stage service-latency example), and formalizes "progress" as a six-dimension
vector rather than a scalar comparison. Closes with two boxed provisional principles — "KnowledgeOS does
not own the Knowledge Space; it preserves and relates the projections... through which participants engage
with it" — self-described as among the strongest formulations reached so far, and an open question: should
Orientation be an explicit Kernel primitive, or fully derivable from participant+purpose+boundary+LoA+
regime?
