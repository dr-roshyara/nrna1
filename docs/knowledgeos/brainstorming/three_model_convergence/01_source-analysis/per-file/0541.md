**TRIGGERED — LANDMARK: full specification of the Operational Independence Test.** Opens adopting an
explicit incremental discipline ("proceed one step at a time"), beginning with "Step 1: Operational
Independence." Distinguishes True Independence (unprovable) from Operationally Established Independence
(relative to a declared model): `Ind_ρ(e_i,e_j,G,C)→{Independent,Dependent,Unknown}`. Defines the
inspectable metadata tuple `M(e)=(Source,AcquisitionEvent,Timestamp,Actor,Method,ParentEvidence,
Transformation,Location,Content,Context)`.

**Delivers a first fully deterministic rule**: provenance-graph adjacency `e_i⤳e_j` alone establishes
Dependent, requiring no LLM judgment. **Delivers a common-ancestry finding**: two LLMs reading the same
document must default to Unknown, since `CommonAncestor(e_i,e_j)⇒Independent≠Confirmed`. **Delivers an
important negative finding**: `DifferentSource⇏Independent` (two Reuters-sourced documents aren't proven
independent merely by different URLs). Delivers the corresponding positive case: two genuinely separate
sensors can be established as `OperationallyIndependent` under policy.

**Delivers the file's central representational innovation**: an independence evidence VECTOR
`I(e_i,e_j)=(i_S,i_A,i_P,i_M,i_T,i_C)` (Source/Acquisition/Provenance/Method/Temporal independence +
Common-ancestor status), each ∈{Confirmed,Rejected,Unknown}, explicitly rejecting a collapsed
`IndependenceScore=0.87` since it "would obscure why the evidence is considered independent."

**Formalizes the decision rule** (Dependent if established; Independent only if ALL policy-required
conditions hold; Unknown otherwise) and delivers a precisely-stated central principle: the three-valued
`𝕀={I,D,U}` beats `{True,False}` since `Unknown≠False` and `Unknown≠True` — "absence of evidence for
independence is not evidence of dependence, but neither is it evidence of independence." Delivers a DDD
`IndependenceAssessment` domain object with two governing invariants (`Dependent⇒¬Independent`,
`Unknown⇒¬ConfirmedIndependent`).

**Works through six concrete test cases**: exact duplicate→Dependent; LLM transformation→Dependent; two
LLMs reading same document→Unknown; two independent sensors→Independent; two sites copying one
source→Dependent/Correlated; missing provenance→Unknown. Delivers the central provisional law:
**"Independence must be established, not assumed."**

**Delivers the file's most important statistical nuance**: **`OperationallyIndependent⇏
StatisticallyIndependent`** — two sensors with separate acquisition/hardware may still share the same
environmental bias, so operational independence is only "evidence about possible statistical
independence," never proof; a statistical model must separately justify its own conditional-independence
assumption before Bayesian multiplication applies — "exactly why Evidence Architecture and Statistical
Inference Policy must remain separate," sharpening 0538/0539's Kernel/Policy boundary with a rigorous
statistical justification.

**Concludes**: operational independence is "computable in a qualified sense" via Provenance/Dependency/
Common ancestry/Acquisition events/Sources/Methods/Context, but "must not claim universal statistical
independence merely from metadata." **Closes naming "Step 2"**: testing the Evidence Identity/Equivalence
Calculus with concrete examples (hashing, canonicalization, semantic comparison, LLM assistance).
