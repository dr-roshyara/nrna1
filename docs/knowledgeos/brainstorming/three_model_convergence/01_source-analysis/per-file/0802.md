evidence-to-conclusion; Decision owns governance; Authorization owns permission) and three compact
non-implications: `Knowledge(C)=Established⇏Authorization=Granted`; `Determination=Feasible⇏

**§177.28-177.30 upgrades the architecture** into an explicit sequence of semantic transformations, and

**§177.31-177.36 delivers the file's central challenge to "truth" itself**: KnowledgeOS should not

Approved(x,t), Authorized(x,t)`.

Determination→Decision→Authorization→Execution→Outcome→Observation'`** — "no longer merely a three-zone

seven-term non-collapse chain and "the strongest principle so far": **"a governed knowledge system should
preserve why a proposition is considered valid, for whom, under which conditions, and at what point in
time."**
