## Tier 2
**TRIGGERED — landmark self-correction**, single layer, no duplication. Identifies that the 0371-0373
Evidence→Reason→Fact chain was "still too deterministic": both Evidence (`E|C,t`) and Reasoning (`R|E,C,t`)
are themselves conditional. Worked non-conclusive-evidence example (server on port 8081 as evidence, not
proof, for "Nexus is running"). Reveals that `A⇒B` actually hides an assumption set `C={c1,...,cn}`, so the
true structure is `(A∧C)⇒B`, not unconditional `A⇒B`. Consolidates into a fully conditional determination
`D(p|E,R,C,t)`, noting the crucial subtlety that probability is conditional **on the reasoning model itself**
(`P(p|E,model,assumptions,C,t)`, not merely `P(p|E)`). Changes Fact from "p is true" to `Fact_t(p|C,R,E)`,
explicitly permitting context-relative validity across time.

Decomposes uncertainty into **four explicitly distinct, non-collapsible types**: Evidence uncertainty
`P(E|p,C)`; Model/reasoning uncertainty `P(R|E,C)`; State uncertainty `P(S|E,C)`; Proposition uncertainty
`P(p|E,R,C,t)`. Mathematically operationalizes 0364's overlapping-elements finding (`O~S1,S2,S3`). Explicitly
refutes Probability=Uncertainty via an inter-observer-disagreement example (`P_A(p|E,C)≠P_B(p|E,C)` for
identical evidence but differing models). Produces a fully conditional five-stage pipeline with explicit
conditioning at every arrow.

**The file's most consequential result**: explicitly **withdraws** the prior assumption (implicit since
0366) that the ideal Knowledge state `K_t*(p)` must resolve to `{0,1}`. The ideal state may itself be
conditional and irreducibly probabilistic — `K_t*(p|C_t)=0.73` — **without this meaning Knowledge is
incomplete**: it may mean the proposition genuinely remains uncertain even given everything knowable.
Crystallized as **"Ideal Knowledge ≠ omniscience,"** explicitly called "a very important conclusion." This
directly yields a **new two-gap distinction**, resolving a latent ambiguity in every prior `K_t*/K̂_t`
formalization since 0366: **Gap 1** (epistemic incompleteness, `K̂_t<K_t*`) vs. **Gap 2** (irreducible
uncertainty, even `K_t*` itself is uncertain) — explicitly named "fundamental." Reformulates the research
target once more: "under what conditions does evidence, combined with conditional reasoning and existing
determinations, warrant assigning a proposition a particular epistemic state?", with probability as only
one of several possible regime realizations. Closes naming a ten-area mathematical comparison programme
(Bayesian probability, conditional probability, Bayesian networks, Dempster-Shafer, fuzzy/many-valued
logic, non-monotonic logic, defeasible reasoning, argumentation theory, epistemic logic, temporal logic,
measurement theory) to find what is invariant across representations.
