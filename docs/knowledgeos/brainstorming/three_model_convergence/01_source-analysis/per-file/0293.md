## Tier 2
**TRIGGERED.** Searle's *Construction of Social Reality*, read as planned back at 0279, adds an entire
**social/institutional layer** to the Knowledge Space model — of unusually direct relevance given the
broader project's own PublicDigit voting-platform domain (elections, voter/candidate/officer status,
constitutional authority, governance ratification). Formalizes brute facts vs. institutional facts, the
central mechanism **X counts as Y in context C**, **deontic power** (rights/obligations/authorizations)
distinguished from causal power, **collective intentionality** (distinct from a union of individual
beliefs, connecting to Fagin's common/distributed knowledge), and the **descriptive-vs-constitutive/
performative** speech-act distinction ("Alice is President" vs. "Alice is hereby appointed President" —
the Kernel must not confuse the two). Iterated status functions produce non-flat institutional structure
(a worked election chain: person→voter→candidate→winner→president→power→appoint→new status). A four-way
relation typology (natural/semantic/epistemic/institutional) is called "one of the cleanest classifications
we have found." Extends the Knowledge Space to three parallel spaces (Natural/Semantic/Institutional)
converging into Epistemic Space, proposes **StatusFunction(X,Y,C)** as a new candidate institutional
primitive directly compatible with the project's constitutional/governance architecture, and closes with
ten named Zero-Lens principles (ZL-S01..ZL-S10). Explicit verdict: "this book should materially influence
the KnowledgeOS model" — the clearest connection yet between abstract Kernel-discovery research and the
concrete voting-platform domain.
