(remote Knower, cannot directly observe, needs to know "what is happening") vs. **Arjuna** (Knower+Actor,
needs to know "what to do") — the same KnowledgeOS serves both via its Sañjaya/Sārathi capabilities
respectively. Refines the working formula: **Krishna = Knowledge**, explicitly ≠ KnowledgeOS (the system
that makes it operational) and ≠ the Ideal State (**I_t=f(K_r,Purpose,Context,Knower)**, not Krishna=I_t).
Gives "activate" a precise operational meaning preserving human agency.

Introduces **"contextual manifestation"**: Ω→K_context→K_accessible→Understanding — the infinite horizon
becomes accessible piece by piece, never as undifferentiated infinity. **Central methodological
contribution**: explicitly and deliberately **refuses to formalize** the deeper proposition "Krishna can
itself be the Lord" as an architectural rule — "the Lord Lens should investigate rather than assume" —
crystallized into a named **"Zero Principle for the Architecture"**: "do not collapse distinctions until the
model gives sufficient basis to collapse them," applied to four explicit non-collapses (Krishna≠KnowledgeOS;
KnowledgeOS≠Knower; IdealState≠Krishna; LordLens≠KrishnaLens). Closes characterizing KnowledgeOS as "a
servant to the Knower, not a source of authority."
