## What Is This Document About?
The docket converts a scattered open-question inventory into a **single ordered agenda**, sequenced
by *gating effect* rather than by importance or ease. Its organising claim is that the bottleneck
has shifted: *"from finding answers to deciding which validated answers become canonical."*

Package 1 (D-9) is placed first because it gates the docket itself: a standing rule says two
consecutive platform-only iterations trigger an over-evolution review, and the document observes
that **the tripwire cannot fire if the metric is never computed.** The REV 2 amendment then does
something unusual and important — it turns the same scrutiny on the docket's own text. The
supporting-evidence line for Package 1 ("~20+ platform-only documents in 48h") **presupposed
document count as the metric**, which is precisely the undecided question. That line is
**downgraded from trigger evidence to illustration**, and D-9 splits into **D-9a (define the
measurement basis)** and **D-9b (run the check)**, with an explicit recommendation to adopt a basis
of *delivered capability + effort* and to **reject raw document count**, on the reasoning that one
strategic decision may produce many small governance documents while a large implementation may
produce few.

The remaining packages cover: ratifying an enacted-but-unwritten mission; disposing a recorded
falsification of the knowledge-platform's *consumption* model while retaining its metadata layer;
reconciling **three competing knowledge ontologies in one context** (D-8) with a recommendation to
adopt one as the frame and scope the other two under it; amending a frozen context map through its
own unused amendment procedure; retroactively minting an authorization with disclosure — noting the
recursion that *the capability would check the ruling that authorizes the capability*; ratifying six
standards currently PROPOSED, with the observation that *"the platform governs by a proposed
constitution"* — the exact over-claim class it polices elsewhere; issuing a canonical register list;
stating publicly that role separation is **session-simulated**; and admitting *model-amnesia
prevention* as a requirement candidate at n=11.

Package 11 is added and then **deliberately not launched**. Its opposing-evidence cell is the
sharpest reasoning in the file: launching a new platform workstream while the over-evolution
tripwire is unbuilt *is* the exposure under examination; and the word **"services"** collides with an
adopted stance that the platform is *"an engineering governance architecture — NOT a software
system… no orchestrator, workflow engine, state machine… only after operational evidence
demonstrates the governance model is insufficient."* The recommendation is DEFER, with "service"
to be **declared** as responsibility-grouping unless the software-machinery evidence bar is
separately cleared.
