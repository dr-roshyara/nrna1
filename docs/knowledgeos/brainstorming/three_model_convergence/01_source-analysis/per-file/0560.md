## Tier 2
**TRIGGERED — LANDMARK: full DDD-grounded semantic theory.** Executes Step 17, opening with a warning
against a naive giant-taxonomy ontology, and the governing principle **`KnowledgeOS does not own all
meaning`** — it owns the mechanisms by which meanings are declared, scoped, related, compared, governed.
Delivers `Word≠Concept` via the "Backup" example (five distinct meanings across contexts), and
`LexicalIdentity≠ConceptualIdentity`, framed as preventing "semantic false merging."

Formalizes `Concept=(ConceptID,Meaning,Context,Constraints,Relations,Terminology)` and contextual meaning
`Meaning(c,C)` — "exactly what DDD bounded contexts formalize." **Formalizes Bounded Context**
`BC=(Concepts,Terms,Rules,Relationships,Context)` via a three-way "Customer" example (Sales/Identity/
Billing), and the symmetric case `Different terms⇏Different concepts`. **Adopts Ubiquitous Language**
(`Term→Concept` per context) directly as KnowledgeOS's semantic-boundary mechanism.

Delivers `SemanticDefinition∈KnowledgeState` (even definitions are provenance-bearing assertions) and
`Ontology≠InstanceKnowledge`. Formalizes an ontology model `𝒪=(C,R,𝒜,𝒦)`. Delivers
`RelationshipType is semantically significant` (a bare edge could mean is-a/part-of/depends-on/causes/
etc.), formalizes typed relations, then full relation epistemology `r=(Source,Type,Target,Σ,Evidence,
Provenance,TemporalScope)`.

**Delivers the file's central governance-separating result — `Semantics≠Governance`**: ontology answers
"what is this thing?"; constitution/policy answers "what must happen?" Extends to
`DescriptiveSemantics≠NormativeRules`, proposed as a core invariant. Delivers a clean three-layer
architecture: Concept/Assertion/Rule. Formalizes a semantic universe `ℳ=(𝒞,ℛ,𝒜,𝒫,𝒢)`, explicitly kept as
distinct bounded concerns rather than one monolith, and contextual concept identity
`ConceptID+ContextID`, rejecting `GlobalConceptMeaning`.

Delivers Cross-context mapping/Semantic Alignment (`Alignment(C_1,C_2,M)`, Support=Weak/Moderate/Strong),
and **`SemanticSimilarity≠SemanticIdentity`** — no automatic inference of equivalence from apparent
similarity. **Delivers the semantic LLM-boundary statement**: an LLM proposes `CandidateSemanticMapping`;
the kernel evaluates context/definitions/constraints/evidence/authority — **"LLM proposes; KnowledgeOS
governs."** Formalizes stable Concept identity independent of terminology, `Concept≠Entity`, and entity
membership as an epistemically-graded relation.

Delivers contextual entity identity and Identity Resolution `Match(e_1,e_2,C)→{Same,Distinct,Unknown}`
(echoing 0552), and **`SemanticDifference≠SemanticConflict`** — disagreement across contexts is not
automatically conflict. **Reparameterizes the corpus's conflict model** by semantic model:
`Conflict=Conflict_{M,Ctx,ρ}(A_i,A_j)` — the same textual pair may conflict under one interpretation, not
another.

Delivers Ontology versioning and Semantic migration (Merge/Split/Rename/Refine/Generalize) and
**"No retroactive semantic rewriting"** — historical assertions retain their original semantic
context/ontology version (explicitly connected to Step 16). Delivers Semantic reasoning
(`𝒪,K⊨P`, worked Dog⊆Animal syllogism) with **`Inference is model-relative`** (echoing 0556's causal
model-relativity), and **Semantic contradiction** as a distinct species of conflict arising from explicit
ontological disjointness axioms (`SemanticConstraintViolation`).

**Extends Zero** to detect `SemanticDiscrepancy` (missing relationship, invalid type, undefined concept,
context mismatch, ontology violation, ambiguous identity). Extends Lord (glossary/expert/comparison/
candidate-mapping proposals for `UndefinedConcept`) and Sārathi (`SemanticConflict may require governance,
not algorithmic resolution` — `EscalateToDomainAuthority`).

**Delivers the file's central architectural deliverable — the Semantic Kernel**:
`SK=(ConceptRegistry,ContextRegistry,RelationRegistry,OntologyRegistry,MappingRegistry,
SemanticRuleRegistry)`, explicitly NOT a reasoning or decision engine —
`SemanticKernel≠ReasoningEngine≠DecisionEngine`. **Delivers eight tentative DDD bounded contexts for
KnowledgeOS itself**: Knowledge, Semantics, Identity, Causal Reasoning, Discrepancy, Decision, Governance,
Temporal History — "should not collapse into one enormous aggregate." Delivers aggregate-boundary
discipline (`Reference across contexts≠Ownership across contexts`), reformulating assertions as
`A=(SubjectID,PredicateID,ObjectID,ContextID,Σ,Evidence,T_v,T_k)`, and extends the bare RDF triple
`(S,P,O)` into `(S,P,O,Σ,E,Π,T,Ctx)`.

Delivers semantic identity persistence under concept evolution (splits explicitly recorded via
`Split(C_1,C_2,C_3)`) and semantic alignment as itself epistemic (Candidate→Evidence→Assessment→
AcceptedAlignment). Connects back to the corpus's original heterogeneous-input model with a worked
source→semantic-role table (Constitution→normative rule; ADR→architectural concept; Textbook→external
knowledge; Database→instance evidence; LLM output→candidate interpretation; etc.).

**Delivers the file's central summarizing invariant**: `Meaning is contextual, typed, versioned, and
governed`, and **`Term≠Concept≠Entity≠Assertion≠Rule`** — "perhaps the most important semantic invariant
of Step 17." **Delivers the file's central formal deliverable — fourteen canonical invariants (SE1-SE14)**:
Word≠Concept; Same word⇏Same concept; Different words⇏Different concepts; Meaning is context-dependent;

Delivers Step 17's compact mathematical core and a computational-viability assessment paralleling every
