## Tier 2
**TRIGGERED — landmark new-axis discovery.** Introduces **Dimension uncertainty**, a genuinely new axis
distinct from Value uncertainty, not previously separated in the corpus. Formalizes the ideal state's
dimension set `D={d_1,d_2,...}`, explicitly distinguishing (1) **Information uncertainty** — known
dimension, uncertain value (e.g. we know the certificate exists but not when it expires; probability can
model this) — from (2) **Dimension uncertainty** — not knowing whether a relevant dimension *exists at all*
(e.g. an undiscovered hidden dependency) — "fundamentally different," sharpening known-unknowns/
unknown-unknowns specifically at the level of ideal-state dimensions.

Reinterprets a "90-95% knowledge" claim precisely as `P(known dimensions are sufficiently complete)`, not
individual-fact correctness. Delivers a worked **Team A/Team B** demonstration: Team A knows few dimensions
with high accuracy; Team B knows many more dimensions with some uncertainty — `Knowledge(B)` can exceed
`Knowledge(A)` despite A's higher per-dimension accuracy, because B has broader dimension coverage.
Sharpens further: a team claiming lower completeness but higher *awareness of unknown dimensions* ("it
knows what it does not know") may possess **better** epistemic knowledge. Derives **Knowledge ≠ Quantity of
Information**, proposing (unformalized) `Knowledge ~ dimension-coverage + information-quality +
confidence-in-completeness`.

Formalizes probability at **two explicit levels**: Level 1 (value uncertainty, `P(X=2.69|O)`) vs. Level 2
(structural/dimensional uncertainty, `P(dimension coverage is complete|O)`) — explicitly warning against
assuming measure theory automatically solves Level 2 without first establishing what the random object
actually is, extending 0378's discipline to this new layer. Draws the deeper consequence that extraction is
not merely `P(X|O)` but a **joint** `P(D,X|O)` — first discover the relevant state space, then estimate the
state within it. Introduces **three explicitly distinct confidence types**: `C_fact` (determination
correctness), `C_coverage` (dimension-identification completeness), `C_model` (correctness of the
dimension-relationship model). Produces a five-stage pipeline (Dimension discovery → State extraction →
Determination → Knowledge), with probability operating at both discovery and extraction stages. Closes by
reformulating the central research question and naming a four-part focus — Dimension uncertainty + Value
uncertainty + Temporal change + Comparative determination — explicitly **before** any claim about what the
KnowledgeOS Kernel must contain, reinforcing 0382/0383's discipline.
