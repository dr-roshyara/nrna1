## What Is This Document About?
The commission explicitly rejects the prior file's (0019) inventory as a starting point — *"the
Landscape is OBSERVATIONAL EVIDENCE ONLY; it is not extended here"* — and states the discipline that
governs everything that follows: *"meaning first… no domain is derived from a folder, a filename, or
a markdown type."* Every candidate is tested against the same nine admissible justifications first
seen at 0010, graded on a Strong/Medium/Weak scale.

Of eight candidates, **D-2 Engineering Method is the model's principal discovery**: it is shown to be
a bounded context **distinct from** D-1 Engineering Governance on four independent grounds — its own
adopted constitution, its own decision series (ADR-M, distinct from ADR-AIP), its own validator and
baseline, and a vocabulary with **zero term overlap** with D-1's. The document states the stakes
plainly: *"treating governance and method as one domain would merge two independently governed models
— the classic conflation Strategic DDD exists to prevent."*

D-4 (Workflow) is **rejected outright** — no ownership, no vocabulary divergence, changes only as D-1/
D-2 change — with the finding stated as a naming trap: *"naming their union 'Workflow' creates a third
label for two existing owners."* D-5 (Capability) is **demoted to a knowledge kind, not a bounded
context**, on the explicit ground that a prior refusal (H-CAT-1, "not yet evidenced") has never been
overturned, and *"promoting it would overturn H-CAT-1 by preference rather than by evidence — the
exact move the nine criteria forbid."* D-6 (Evidence) **splits**, and the document states precisely
why a split is not the same as an ambiguity: canon already records the concept's owner as *"the
producing track; consumed by DA,"* and *"a single owner cannot be 'the producing track' and the
platform"* simultaneously — so the boundary runs **through** the concept rather than around it,
yielding D-6a (Evidence Protocol, platform-side, reusable, **never executed**) and D-6b (Product
Evidence, records, never reusable). D-7 (PKS) is **reclassified as a context type**, not a context: it
has one instance per product, and *"conflating the type with an instance is what let PublicDigit's
methodology end up inside its own PKS paths."*

§5's ubiquitous-language audit finds the corpus's most severe recorded collision to date: **"Baseline"
carries five distinct senses**, one of which (a methodology baseline, D-2) belongs to a domain the
prior inventory had not even indexed. §6 assesses folders **last, as projections**, and produces a
methodological result the document states explicitly is the whole point of the exercise: the same
misplacement (domain-free methodology sitting in a product path) that an earlier pass found by
**grepping for domain vocabulary** is here found by **asking who owns the knowledge** — and the
model-based method finds a **second** misplacement (D-6b, product evidence records sitting inside the
platform tree) that *"no vocabulary search could find, because records contain no methodology terms
at all."* This is presented as proof that *"the model had to precede the index."*
