## Tier 2
**TRIGGERED — LANDMARK: full typed-uncertainty taxonomy.** Executes Step 20, opening: informal terms
("unknown," "uncertain," "confidence," "probability") would eventually make the model inconsistent.
Delivers the governing principle **`Uncertainty is not one quantity`**:
**`Unknown≠Ambiguous≠Incomplete≠Imprecise≠Conflicting≠Probabilistic`**, and
**`LLM confidence≠calibrated probability`**.

**Delivers the foundational six-case worked example** (identical Nexus-version statement under six
distinct conditions: no evidence/Unknown; ambiguous referent/Ambiguous; missing attribute/Incomplete;
conflicting sources/Conflict; interval-only statement/Imprecise; measurement-based estimate/Probabilistic)
— `KnowledgeOS must preserve the type of uncertainty`. **Formalizes Uncertainty as a structured object**,
explicitly rejecting `U∈[0,1]`: `𝒰=(Type,Scope,Object,Representation,Source,Basis,TemporalScope,
Resolution)`, `Type` drawn from an eight-member taxonomy `{Unknown,Incomplete,Ambiguous,Imprecise,
Probabilistic,Conflicting,Indeterminate,ModelUncertain}`.

Formalizes Unknown (`Unknown(A)≠False(A)`) and **delivers the Open-World Assumption as an explicit
principle**: `Absence of evidence≠Evidence of absence`. Formalizes Incomplete knowledge (connects to the
Discrepancy model) and **Ambiguity as a set of known candidate interpretations**
(`Ambiguity(x)={c_1,...,c_n}`), explicitly distinct from Unknown, with the kernel forbidden from
arbitrarily resolving LLM-extracted candidates.

Formalizes **Imprecision as interval-valued, distinct from probability** (`IntervalUncertainty≠
Probability`), and Probability rigorously via a measure `P:ℱ→[0,1]`, delivering **`Probability is
model-relative`** — echoing 0556/0560's model-relativity theme. Delivers Bayesian probability alongside
the crucial caution **`Not every uncertainty should be probabilized`**, worked via a concrete example
where assigning P=0.97 to a constitutional approval requirement WORSENS the representation — the real
issue is `NormativeValidity` (reinforcing 0561's normative/descriptive separation).

**Delivers four distinct, non-conflatable senses of "confidence"** (statistical/subjective/model/epistemic
support) and the hard invariant **`LLMReportedConfidence≠CalibratedProbability`**. Formalizes Calibration
empirically and task/context-relative (`Calibration(Model,Task,Context)`). Formalizes **Conflict as a
distinct epistemic category, not a probability value** — `Conflict(A,¬A)` can coexist with a synthesized
`P(A|E)=0.8`, with the conflict preserved as provenance, never erased.

Formalizes Indeterminacy ("not derivable and not refutable under the current model," connecting to 0561's
four-valued reasoning results) and **`ModelUncertainty≠ParameterUncertainty≠MeasurementUncertainty`** —
three genuinely distinct statistical categories, worked through concrete examples (`ErrorInterval≠
Probability`). **Delivers type-dependent Uncertainty propagation**: deterministic interval propagation vs.
probabilistic distribution transformation vs. causal propagation are categorically different —
**`There is no universal uncertainty propagation formula`**, elevated to a major architectural principle —
a clean, explicit further instance of the corpus's recurring "no universal formula" pattern (0545/0546,
0554, 0556, 0561). Formalizes an Uncertainty object with `PropagationRule` singled out as particularly
important.

Delivers **`EpistemicStatus≠UncertaintyType`** (orthogonal dimensions) and **`Unknown does not imply
UniformProbability`** — no invented `P(A)=0.5` from mere binary possibility. Delivers **`No numerical
confidence without a defined basis`** (five legitimate bases named, LLM felt-confidence excluded by
default).

Delivers Evidence uncertainty vs. proposition uncertainty and **Source uncertainty via 0562's Bayesian
reliability formalism** (`Rel(S,A)~Beta(α,β)`), creating a hierarchical statistical model. Delivers
Uncertainty propagation through inference as rule-semantics-dependent (deterministic/probabilistic/causal).

**Connects decision-making under uncertainty directly to Sārathi (0557)**: expected-utility formulas valid
"only when the uncertainty has a defensible probabilistic model"; otherwise interval analysis, minimax,
robust optimization, or escalation. Formalizes Risk (`Risk(a)=f(Likelihood,Impact,Uncertainty,
Reversibility)`) and delivers **`Unknown probability must not be replaced by an arbitrary probability`** —
unknown likelihood + severe impact should trigger human escalation. Delivers
`RequiredEpistemicStrength=f(ActionRisk)`, connecting Step 20 to Step 19 (0562). Delivers a formal robust
decision criterion `a*=argmax_a min_{P∈𝒫} EU_P(a)`, extending 0557's robust/minimax theory.

**Delivers Uncertainty reduction as an explicit action type** (`a_info:K→K'`, ideally `U(K')<U(K)`, not
necessarily to zero), and **generalizes 0557's EVSI/VOI into a system-wide Value of Information
framework**: `VOI=EU(with info)-EU(without info)-Cost`, delivering **"Active Epistemic Management"** as "a
significant distinction from ordinary RAG systems."

**Extends Zero** (`UncertaintyTooHighForPurpose`, worked "Supported but Not decision-ready" example,
`Supported≠Sufficient`). Extends Lord (system-of-record query, human confirmation, independent
measurement — goal reframed as `reduce uncertainty enough for the intended purpose`, not to zero). Extends
Sārathi: `Ready(K,P)=SufficientEvidence∧AcceptableUncertainty∧NoCriticalConflict∧ApplicableGovernance`.

Delivers a typed extension of the discrepancy model (`d_uncertainty`: MissingValue/AmbiguousIdentity/
InsufficientPrecision/UncalibratedProbability/ConflictingEvidence/ModelUncertainty), a richer assertion
epistemic-state structure `EA=(Status,Support,Uncertainty,Conflict,Provenance,TemporalScope)`, and **a
structured truth space rejecting bare True/False**: `TruthStatus(A)=(Support(A),Refutation(A),
Uncertainty(A),Commitment(A))`, explicitly permitting simultaneous nonzero support and refutation — "not
necessarily a bug."

**Delivers the file's central architectural-humility principle — "No forced resolution"**: KnowledgeOS
must be permitted to legitimately return `Unresolved` — "the system must be able to represent 'we do not
know' without converting it into an invented answer." Delivers a full Uncertainty lifecycle (Unknown→
Evidence acquired→...→Commitment decision), contrasted with naive "retrieve→answer" RAG.

Delivers **four proposed DDD bounded contexts** (Epistemic/Statistical/Decision/Evidence Context),
preventing probability concepts from "contaminating the entire domain model," and a Ubiquitous-Language
caution against a single shared "confidence" field across contexts, echoing 0560's semantic theory.
Formalizes two distinct domain objects: `UncertaintyAssessment=(Type,Target,Basis,Scope,Representation,
Provenance)` vs. `ProbabilisticAssessment=(RandomVariable,Distribution,Model,Prior,Posterior,Calibration)`.

Delivers a three-tier computability assessment (deterministic/statistical/AI-assisted), with the caution
`AI extraction does not make an uncertainty claim mathematically valid`.

**Delivers the file's central formal deliverable — fifteen canonical invariants (U1-U15)**: Unknown≠False;
AbsenceOfEvidence≠EvidenceOfAbsence; Ambiguity≠Unknown; Imprecision≠Probability; Conflict≠Probability;
EpistemicStatus≠UncertaintyType; LLMConfidence≠CalibratedProbability; no numerical confidence without a
defined basis; not every uncertainty should be probabilized; every uncertainty type has explicit
propagation semantics; unknown probability must not be replaced arbitrarily; conflict must preserve
competing claims; decision thresholds are governance/policy, not universal constants; the system may
legitimately return Unresolved; uncertainty must be represented per its actual semantic type.

**Step 20 verdict**: THEORETICALLY RESOLVED AT THE FRAMEWORK LEVEL, with the file's single strongest
architectural decision: **"KnowledgeOS shall never force heterogeneous uncertainty into a single scalar
confidence value at the kernel level"** — `Structured Uncertainty→Derived Decision Metric`, never
`Uncertainty→One Universal Confidence Number`.

Delivers a consolidated fourteen-stage sources-to-outcome pipeline, cross-cut by `Identity+Semantics+Time+
Evidence+Provenance+Authority+Reliability+Uncertainty+Rules+Governance` — "approaching a closed
mathematical architecture rather than a collection of philosophical definitions."

**Closes by identifying a remaining gap despite the near-closure framing**: the transition from knowledge
to action and the meaning of "Action" itself are not yet FULLY formalized — "what is an Action? What is a
Decision? What is a Goal? What is an Outcome?" **Opens "Step 21 — Goals, Intentions, Decisions, Actions,
