## Tier 2
**TRIGGERED — landmark critical review**, the sharpest epistemic-discipline correction in the 0374-0378
research burst. Endorses an "uploaded framework"'s research-not-architecture discipline while diagnosing
its central defect: the proposed `Observation→Evidence→Fact→...→Knowledge` sequence is "still too simple."
Reformulates the pipeline `O_t→E_t(p|C,M)→R_t(p|E,F,C,M)→D_t(p)→F_t`, and names the file's central
diagnostic finding: **"Determination is the missing mathematical object in the current framework."**

Challenges `P(F|O_t)` as too simplistic, proposing `P(F|O_t,C_t,M_t,F_<t,A_t)` with explicit assumptions
`A_t`. Challenges "established facts" as wrongly treated as deterministic: `certainty of the rule ≠
certainty of its premises ≠ certainty of the conclusion` — crystallizing **the combination problem**.
Formalizes **two explicitly distinct uncertainty layers**: Layer 1 (extraction, `O→F`) vs. Layer 2
(propagation through reasoning — given `P(F1)=0.8` and `F1⇒K`, `K`'s epistemic state is **not**
automatically `P(K)=0.8`) — "more fundamental than simply Bayesian versus fuzzy versus Dempster-Shafer."

**Downgrades** the framework's proposed "invariants" (uncertainty representation, combination rule,
conflict handling, etc.) to mere "dimensions along which frameworks differ" — proposing a strict
`I(X)=I(Y)`-under-transformation criterion. Marks a proposed "common algebraic structure" (lattice/
measure-theoretic) as premature: `P(p)`, `μ(p)`, `μ_fuzzy(p)`, `Acceptable(p)` may represent genuinely
different epistemic properties, "exactly the danger we have been trying to avoid." Proposes a **strict
five-step ordering** before any invariance claim (epistemic object → determination operation → what each
regime represents → equivalence conditions → what survives) — only then ask what is invariant.

Proposes a **multidimensional epistemic-state hypothesis**: `𝓔_t(p) = (proposition, support,
justification, status, uncertainty, time)`, with an explicit per-regime dimension mapping (Bayesian→
uncertainty; Argumentation→support/attack; Non-monotonic→defeasible status; Epistemic logic→knowledge
relations; Temporal logic→temporal truth; Measurement theory→numerical attributes) — these may be
**different dimensions** of one state, not competing scalar representations. Retraces "Is Knowledge
probabilistic?" through four refinements to a deeper question: "What exactly is being represented by the
probability?" Produces a **sixteen-row research-target matrix**, closing with the pivotal meta-question:
are these different **lenses** on one epistemic state, or lenses on **different dimensions** of it? Closes
with a strong/needs-correction verdict ledger and names the singular next research focus: **"What is a
conditional determination?"**, formalized as `(O,E,F_<t,C,R)→D_t(p)`.
