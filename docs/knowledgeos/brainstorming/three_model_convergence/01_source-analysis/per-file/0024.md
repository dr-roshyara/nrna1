## What Is This Document About?
Confirms 0020's own forward annotation exactly: **one claim falsified, two weakened, two survived,
two unchanged** — the model does not survive intact.

**W-1** (D-2's independence): `Round39-D6`'s own header shows the ARB participates in **both** D-1 and
D-2's authority — not a separate authority, a scoping device under a shared one — and the "own
decision series" ground is **falsified as evolution specifically**: the ADR series was "authored

independent *evolution over time*. **This is a precise, important distinction**: independence-of-
subject and independence-of-evolution are shown to be separable properties that a single piece of
evidence (a decision series) cannot establish together.

**W-2** (D-3's ACL): the strongest falsifier in the report. Three tests are run against artifacts, not
prose: no Capability Mapping *artifact* exists at all; exactly one source cites the layer (its own
origin document, plus eight documents the same author wrote the same day); and the "abstract"
vocabulary (`deny`/`request approval`/`allow`) is shown to be **nearly the identity function** against
the concrete runtime config (`deny`/`ask`/`allow`). The DDD consequence is stated precisely: *"an
Anti-Corruption Layer that performs an identity mapping is not an ACL. It is a restatement."*

**W-3** (PKS as projection): falsified for **every existing PKS artifact**, on documented fields
incompatible with a projection's lifecycle — human authorship, commission records, a 9.9/10 ARB
endorsement, an acceptance disposition. *"You do not endorse a projection at 9.9/10. You regenerate
it."* A mechanical grep corroborates: ten files use the `authority: derived|generated` vocabulary in
**prose mentions only**, none as an applied, validated frontmatter field.

Two findings **strengthen** the model on grounds the original had not used: **S-1** discovers that
D-2's authority body carries an explicit, deliberately maintained **mutual scope-exclusion rule**
("Governance discoveries... MUST NOT appear here"), argued as *stronger* evidence for two models than
the falsified "own decision series" ground — a shared authority that forbids itself from mixing
subjects is evidence *for* separation, not against it. **S-2** attempts to revive D-4 (Workflow) by
searching for independent ownership and fails: the EEP's own header names `Owner: Decision Authority`
— the same owner as D-1 — confirming the rejection.

§3 records six unresolved questions, the sharpest being **U-1**: whether `Round39-MC` was adopted by
sponsor authority *because* D-2 is independent, or *despite* being part of D-1 — stated as
undiscoverable from artifacts, because "only the sponsor and ARB know, and the model's validity turns
