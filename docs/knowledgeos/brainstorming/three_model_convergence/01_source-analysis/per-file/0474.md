## Tier 2
**TRIGGERED — LANDMARK: Conflict split, Reframe introduced as a new operation.** Part A defines Conflict
(Conflict≠Incoherence) with five types (Logical/Normative/Epistemic/Temporal/Contextual), each formalized
with resolution strategies.

Part B: **splits Conflict into two levels** — **Conflict_A(A1,A2)** (assertions cannot jointly be accepted)
vs. **Conflict_I(I1,I2|P,K,C)** (both assertions true, but derived implications compete) — worked via
Bhīṣma: Grandfather∧Opponent is satisfiable; the real tension is "protect grandfather" vs. "fight opponent."
**Demotes Temporal/Contextual conflict to Frame parameters**: Conflict=ContentConflict+ApplicableFrame,
F=(Scope,Context,Time,Purpose) — reducing to three genuine kinds (Logical/Normative/Epistemic). Distinguishes
EvidenceConflict≠AssertionConflict.

**Delivers the biggest correction: Resolution≠Elimination** — six outcomes (Eliminated/Decided/Reframed/
Accepted/Deferred/Unresolved), rejecting "one side wins." **Philosophically deepens the Gita reading**:
Krishna doesn't choose Family-vs-Kingdom, he **changes the conceptual frame** — states "some conflicts are
resolved by knowledge transformation, not merely evidence selection." Introduces a new first-class
operation: **Reframe(K,C)→K'**, dissolving conflicts without rejecting either original observation.

Revises the DDD object/operation separation (Conflict is an object; Detection/Classification/Resolution/
Reframing are operations). Revises the lifecycle into a branching model (Accepted≠Resolved). Formalizes a
complete transition system: K_t→[Detect]→C_t→[Classify]→C_t*→[Navigate]→e_t→[Apply]→K_{t+1}. **Identifies
three orthogonal uncertainty-reduction mechanisms**: Evidence acquisition, Dimension discovery, and
Reframing — "more evidence is not always the solution." Closes marking Question 9 "Accepted, not yet
frozen" and launches **Question 10 — What is a Gap, and how does it differ from a Conflict?**
