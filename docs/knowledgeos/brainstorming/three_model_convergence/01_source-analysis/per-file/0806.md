mistake would be to assume that every disagreement means that one side is simply 'wrong.'"** Boxed:
**"conflict is first an epistemic fact, not an error."** Rejects "latest wins" (boxed `Timestamp⇏Truth`)
and "highest confidence wins" (boxed "confidence alone does not resolve semantic conflict"), each with a
worked counterexample.

checked before declaring genuine contradiction, boxed **"version difference can explain apparent
contradiction."** Delivers an eight-category conflict classification, "much richer than `conflict=true`,"
including **different interpretation** as a DDD bounded-context-language phenomenon ("safe" meaning
different things in Security vs. Operations contexts).

**§181.11-181.16 delivers the claim tuple** `C=⟨Proposition,Context,Scope,Time,Source⟩` and the resolution

deletion."**

**§181.17-181.23 delivers the Bayesian lens with an explicit warning**, boxed: **"KnowledgeOS should not
become a fake Bayesian oracle."** **Delivers evidence independence extended to document corroboration**:
five documents copying one source are not five independent pieces of evidence
(`EvidenceCount=5⇏EvidenceStrength=5×`) — directly extending 0793's "three-agent problem." Boxed
**`Triangulation⇏Truth`**.

**§181.24-181.28 delivers "disagreement itself becomes knowledge"**: `Disputed(P)` as legitimate
organizational knowledge, worked through a **directly self-resonant architecture example**:

silently choosing whichever document was indexed last," echoing the arc's own 0787/0798 bounded-context
work. Reconfirms epistemic resolution vs. governance resolution, delivering `ResolutionAuthority=
f(ConflictType)`.

**§181.29-181.31 delivers the central AI-safety contribution**, boxed: `Conflict→Classification→
ResponsibleContext→ResolutionMethod`, explicitly not "AI picks winner." **Delivers "AI should surface
conflict, not conceal it"**, crystallized into **the AI epistemic contract**: Detect/Expose/Preserve/
Assess/RecommendInvestigation permitted; DeleteConflict/InventResolution forbidden.

**§181.32-181.35 delivers domain-rule-based deterministic resolution** (`Supersedes`, `SourcePriority`

**§181.36-181.38 delivers the complete conflict model** (`ConflictCandidate`, a nine-category
