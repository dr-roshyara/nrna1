## Tier 2
**TRIGGERED — landmark deepening file.** Engages Robert Brandom's *Making It Explicit*, explicitly cast as
filling the missing bridge `knowledge → concepts → language → inference → commitments → social assessment →
explicit representation`. Reframes the Kernel-discovery process around a **commitment-and-relation
substrate**: a discursive participant undertakes **Commitment**s (with content, support, **Entitlement**,
inferential consequences, challenges) rather than the Kernel resting on isolated Proposition/Assertion/
Knowledge objects. "Deontic scorekeeping" tracks who is committed/entitled to what across participants,
producing "a living epistemic state, not a static knowledge graph." Formally separates Proposition/Claim/
Commitment/Entitlement/Implication/Challenge — directly answering the corpus's long-standing fact-vs-claim
struggle. Introduces a **three-tier epistemic-participant typology** (information producer / reasoning
participant / normative authority) replacing the vague "Agent" term and sharpening 0285's Agent-vs-
Accountable-Actor split; a candidate **Commitment Ledger** derived structure; a tuple `K_t^A =
(P,C,E,I,N,R)` with a `Transition` operator (explicitly not-yet-adopted); and two new named principles —
**Inferential Content Principle** and **Explicitness Principle**. Substantially revises the tentative Kernel
candidate list to foreground Commitment and Entitlement as first-class items, demoting Knowledge/Concept/
Topic/Argument/Explanation/Answer/Report to derived structures. Two worked examples ("valid election"
inferential consequences; an Architecture Board approval as a governance score) directly connect the
abstract research to the project's own election/governance domain, alongside 0293's Searle connection.
Explicitly frames itself as **complementary, not competing**, with Williamson (0320): "Williamson: do not
reduce knowledge to simpler ingredients. Brandom: model what knowledge-bearing discourse DOES." Also
explicitly, pre-emptively warns against repeating the corpus's own 0282/0285 Fagin-overclaim mistake —
Brandom's scorekeeping theory should not become Kernel ontology wholesale.
