## Tier 2
**TRIGGERED — Moksha reframed from "nearly infinite knowledge" to an epistemic phase transition.**
Continues 0500's Knowledge Ātma thread. Rejects "Moksha = nearly infinite knowledge" as equivalent to
maximal information, "much narrower than the philosophical concept."

**Foundational correction**: **Knowledge Quantity≠Knowledge Completeness** — an infinite subset can still
have infinitely many things missing (worked via ℕ⊂ℝ: both infinite, but knowing every natural number does
not mean knowing every real number) — "exactly consistent with the Zero theory." Initially formulates
Moksha as `Δ(K_t,I_t)→0`, then refines: "Moksha represents an epistemic condition in which the Knowledge
Ātma has transcended the relevant epistemic boundaries of its journey... the discrepancy... approaches
zero" — `lim_{t→T_M}Δ_t→0`.

**Delivers the deepest reframing**: Moksha may represent not increasing knowledge quantity but a
fundamental **change of epistemic regime** — `EpistemicRegime_1→EpistemicRegime_M` — "much closer to a
mathematical notion of phase transition than merely 'more data.'" Confirms Knowledge Ātma (𝒜_K, 0500)
persists through this transition — Moksha transforms the Ātma's relationship to the Knowledge Space rather
than destroying it. **Reframes Zero's limiting role**: rather than all currently-detected gaps filled
(`G_t→∅`, potentially unreachable in an unbounded space), the stronger, "much more profound" concept is that
**the epistemic boundary itself is transcended**. **Blocks a naive Moksha=K_t=I_t identification**, since the
Ideal State itself evolves — Moksha=transcendence of the ordinary finite epistemic journey altogether, not
convergence on any single Ideal State snapshot.

**Formalizes a Moksha transition object**: `M=(K_{T_M},T_M,𝒜_K,𝒦,Γ_M)` — transition time, knowledge state at
that point, Knowledge Ātma, Knowledge Space, and defining epistemic regime — "K_{T_M} is not merely 'large';
it is epistemically transformed." Delivers a nine-row vocabulary table and a compact mathematical hierarchy.

**Closes with a crucial implementation discipline**: explicitly declines to implement "Moksha" as a literal
software state, modeling it as "a theoretical/metaphorical upper-bound concept unless our later examination
of the Gītā gives us a sufficiently precise reason to make it architectural" — restating 0494's
source-to-architecture discipline in case-specific form: Gītā concept→conceptual abstraction→mathematical
hypothesis→architecture only if validated — "the Gītā generates hypotheses for KnowledgeOS; mathematics
tests their coherence; DDD determines whether they deserve to become domain concepts."
