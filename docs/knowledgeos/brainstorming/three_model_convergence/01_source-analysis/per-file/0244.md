## Tier 2
**TRIGGERED (critical).** Significant methodological pivot: instead of "what is knowledge?", ask "what is
demonstrably **NOT** knowledge?" and statistically infer the residual that survives elimination. Formalizes
a generative model `X = G(K,N,C,ε)` (candidate-knowledge/non-knowledge/context/noise), inverted to
`P(K,N,C|X)` — PRML's forward-generative machinery run backward. Distinguishes `non-observation ≠ negative
evidence`. Produces a 14-item non-knowledge taxonomy that must not collapse into one category, and the
strongest strength criterion in this sub-arc: **invariance under transformation** — a candidate survives
paraphrase/language/style/domain changes, tested empirically (`z(T_i(X)) ≈ z(X)`), stronger than raw
frequency (`frequency != fundamentality`, formalized as `fundamentality ≈ stability × cross-context
invariance × explanatory power` with an Occam-factor complexity penalty). Produces a candidate, explicitly
non-final but **experimentally attackable** operational definition of knowledge: a statistically stable,
transferable, explanatory structure invariant under appropriate transformations after representational/
contextual/conventional/stochastic effects are accounted for — distinct from and complementary to the
corpus's philosophically-derived Kernel definitions (0216-0230 lineage).
