operational": Zero identifies discrepancy, Lord generates candidate remedies, Sārathi decides what happens
next. Delivers the corrective principle **`Best action ≠ action that merely reduces discrepancy most`**,
since real actions carry cost, risk, reversibility, authority requirements, and information value.

Reconfirms `Knowledge≠Action` and `Recommendation≠Execution`. **Formalizes Action as a structured domain
object** `a=(Intent,Preconditions,Effects,Cost,Risk,Reversibility,Authority,EvidenceRequirements,Context)`,
preconditions evaluated against `K_t` (`Executable(a)=False` if a critical precondition is unknown absent
explicit governance override). Delivers standard expected-utility reasoning `EU(a)=Σ_i P(o_i|a,K_t)U(o_i)`,
then the foundational insight that information itself has decision value (inspecting logs: zero direct
effect, high uncertainty-reduction value).

**Rigorously formalizes Value of Information**: `EVSI(q)=𝔼_r[max_a EU(a|K_{t+1}(r))]-max_a EU(a|K_t)`, plus
a simpler policy-level approximation `VOI(q)=ExpectedDecisionImprovement(q)-Cost(q)`. Redefines Lord's role
precisely (`Lord:(K_t,I_t,Δ_t)→𝒜_t`, candidates annotated by ExpectedBenefit/Cost/Risk/VOI/Reversibility).
**Formalizes Sārathi as a genuine decision function**, `Sārathi:(K_t,I_t,Δ_t,𝒜_t,ρ)→Decision`, six-value
output `{Execute,Investigate,Defer,Reject,Escalate,RequestApproval}`. Delivers a crucial authority boundary
(production firewall change → `RequestApproval(a)`, not `Execute(a)`) — **`Decision capability≠Execution
authority`**.

Formalizes decision feasibility (`Feasible=Preconditions∧Authority∧Constraints∧Safety`) and constrained
optimization `a*=argmax_{a∈𝒜^feasible}EU(a)` s.t. risk/cost ceilings. **Delivers `Utility is not
universal`** — policy/purpose dependent, "following the same principle established for thresholds." Delivers
multi-objective decision-making over a named objective set, and **the file's central methodological
recommendation — Pareto optimality**: present non-dominated candidates to a human "rather than pretending
the system knows the organization's exact utility weights." Worked five-action comparison table shows
neither naive cost-minimization nor naive discrepancy-elimination captures the correct choice
(inspect-logs, high VOI/low risk) — "precisely what we want Sārathi to capture."

Introduces exploration-vs-exploitation and formalizes sequential decision-making
(`(K_t,a_t,o_t)→K_{t+1}=Update(...)`). Delivers "the complete Sārathi loop" diagram. Reconfirms **action
outcomes are evidence**, and delivers **failed actions are also knowledge** (a failed firewall test
produces genuine evidence, "failure is an epistemic event," flagged important for engineering systems).
Formalizes Action provenance via `ActionEvent=(Actor,Action,Intent,KnowledgeState,Policy,Timestamp,
Outcome,Evidence)`.

**Delivers explicit first-class human-decision modeling**: a human overriding Sārathi's recommendation is
