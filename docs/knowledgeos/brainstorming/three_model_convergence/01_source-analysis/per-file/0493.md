NOT claiming the Gita is a formal specification — asks whether the model can faithfully represent Chapter

exposes several places where our formal model is still incomplete," forcing Purpose, Normative Authority,
Role/Duty, Agency, Motivation, Guidance, and Action Consequence into first-class concepts.

**Central discovery**: introduces **Normative State N_t** as a fifth fundamental state — IdealState≠
NormativeState (desired-state content vs. actor obligation). Extends the discrepancy model with Δ_N
(unknown duty, conflicting duties, insufficient authority). Reconfirms Q16's Conflict-as-relation, adding
**PerceivedConflict≠ActualConflict** (Arjuna sees contradiction; Krishna reveals actual relation) — a
four-way taxonomy {Apparent,Actual,Resolved,Unresolved}. Reconfirms Support⊥Resolution via MoreEvidence⇏
Resolution. **Introduces Synthesis as a sixth Resolution strategy** — Synthesis(A_1,A_2,Context)→A_3,
distinct from inference (Knowledge+Action reconstructed into Knowledge+RightlyOrientedAction=Karma-yoga,
not choose-A-or-B). Reconfirms the Clarification invariant via Arjuna's explicit request for determination.

**Introduces Actor State and AffectiveState** as objects distinct from Knowledge State:
ActorState=(Role,Capabilities,KnowledgeAccess,Authority,Purpose,Commitments,AffectiveContext) —
KnowledgeState≠ActorState. Validates the multidimensional epistemic state via the text's senses<mind<
intelligence<self hierarchy but explicitly **rejects turning it into a universal epistemic ladder**
(HierarchyOfFaculties≠EpistemicConfidence). **Introduces Agency**: ActionExecution≠AgencyAttribution,
decomposing Action into (Actor,Operation,Purpose,Authority,Context,Execution,Outcome,Attribution) — performer
≠authorizer≠causer≠responsible-party in general. **Makes Role→Duty mandatory and Duty≠Goal** a fourth
non-collapsing concept. Formalizes a **Normative Model**: N=(Roles,Duties,Obligations,Permissions,
Prohibitions,Principles,Authority); NormativeAssessment(A,N,C)→{Compliant,Violating,Undetermined,
Conflicting}. **Distinguishes Authority≠Evidence, Source≠Authority** — Source→Evidence, Authority→Norm,
Policy→DecisionRule kept as three non-synonymous primitives. **Corrects Sārathi's signature**:
Sārathi:(K,U,N,Δ,P,Role)→Guidance, not directly →Action — Guidance≠Decision≠Action.

Extends the central loop to Question→Intent→Context→Knowledge→Understanding→NormativeAssessment→
Discrepancy→Guidance→Decision→Action→Outcome→Observation, explicitly cyclic. Introduces **Trajectory** as
first-class, extends Epistemic Lineage (0492) with normative/guidance/decision/action/outcome stages.
Reconfirms ObservableAction≠SemanticAction and introduces **Intention≠Purpose**. Introduces
ExemplaryBehavior/NormPropagation (LeaderAction→FollowerBehavior), suggesting a Governance/Normative-
Influence bounded context. Introduces OptimalGuidance≠MaximumInformation and
Readiness(Actor,Knowledge,Context) as a relation (LearningReady). Reconfirms Knowledge≠Understanding via
CorrectFacts⇏CorrectDecision — "perhaps the most important validation of the entire KnowledgeOS concept."
Formalizes Understanding: U=(ConceptualModel,Relations,Implications,NormativeMeaning,DecisionConsequences,
Uncertainty).

Derives a **five-way Gap taxonomy**: Epistemic/Understanding/Normative/Decision/Domain. **Rejects the prior
"After Moral Resolution→Δ=∅" claim a SECOND, independent way** (after 0488's Discrepancy≠Deficiency route):
even after normative understanding, uncertainty/risk/emotional-difficulty may remain — Resolution≠Δ=∅;
"CompleteKnowledge is unnecessary for action." Proposes a full Chapter-3-derived architecture diagram and a
**ten-component complete operational state** 𝒳_t=(Q_t,K_t,U_t,N_t,X_t,A_t,D_t,G_t,Δ_t,H_t). Crystallizes two
master invariants: **"Knowledge does not determine Action by itself"** and **"Action does not prove
Understanding"** (Knowledge⇏Understanding⇏Decision⇏Action).

Closes with a 19-row validation matrix (5 rows flagged "Needs extension"/"Major missing concept": Action,
