## What Is This Document About?
An isolated session, blocked from the entire `docs/knowledgeos/**`/`docs/pks/**` conclusion corpus,

document scores the result against this track's own findings on four dispositions.

**Twelve conclusions were reproduced independently** — including the kernel-membership list (*"the
genuine kernel; surprisingly small and clean"*, near-identical to this track's own), the individual
named ownership of a governed context, and the rejection of *"PublicDigit is the laboratory"* against

methodological claim about what corroborates a finding in the absence of controlled replication.

**Three corrections are more consequential than the convergences.** D-1 **falsifies this track's own
most-repeated claim** — *"0 traversals"* of the evidence back-edge, asserted "in at least six
documents" — with the reconciliation stated precisely: the original narrow claim (no evidence flowed

generalization is wrong; the informal back-edge has run **at least three times** (three named
rulings). The consequence is stated exactly: *"the flywheel has never turned" must be retired;
"the flywheel turns rarely and informally" is what the evidence supports.* D-2 is a methodological
indictment: the blind reviewer consulted a **frozen six-context map that this track's own two full
rounds of domain-model discovery never opened** — named explicitly as *"occurrence #11"* of the
recurring proposing-before-searching pattern (ledger entry 9, now at n=11 rather than n=5). D-3 is
self-indicting in a different way: CAP-001's authorization does not appear in the rulings register at
all, which the document states as *"a material weakness for a system whose thesis is
traceability."* D-4 surfaces a standing falsification (the EKP's *consumption model*, not its

§3 records the strongest single methodological result: the blind reviewer, without knowing the MVK

correctly, as **method cross-validation** distinct from conclusion convergence.
