## Tier 2
**TRIGGERED** — delivers the Three-Layer Architecture, a systematic computability audit of the whole
theory, and the headline finding on judgment vs. determinism.

Reframes the research question: not conceptual attractiveness but boxed **`Is the definition an effective
object that can actually be computed, decided, persisted, replayed and implemented without hidden human
judgment?`** — a closure audit, forbidding "calling something computable merely because a program could
approximate it."

**§266.1** delivers boxed **`Computable≠Implementable in principle`**. **§266.2** delivers a three-class
taxonomy (directly computable / computable-after-parameters / not computable as currently defined —
`Authoritative`, `Relevant`, `Correct`, `Adequate`).

**§266.3-266.27 delivers a systematic per-construct audit of essentially every object this sub-arc has
formalized**, each with a genuinely new distinction: Entity (`membership=UNDER-SPECIFIED` without an
ontology boundary); Dimension (`Structural validity≠Semantic adequacy`); Value membership (tractability
depends on `V_D`'s representation); Assertion (`StructuralValidation` computable, `Truth(A)` not); Status
(boxed **`TransitionValidity≠StatusAssignment`**, "one of the most important hidden boundaries");
Equality (`StructuralEquality=COMPUTABLE`, `SemanticEquality=CONDITIONAL`); Identity
(`IdentityConstruction` computable, `IdentityResolution` may be human-governed); Provenance
(`ProvenanceLookup=COMPUTABLE` under stated assumptions, but `lookup≠validation` — extends 0894's
placement recommendation); **Replay** (`Conditionally Computable`; **delivers `Replayability⇒Immutable
or versioned transformation semantics`**; **delivers the genuinely new negative finding boxed `Replay≠
Reversal`** — many-to-one transformations lose information, so replay ≠ recoverability); Preconditions/
Postconditions (`FormalPostcondition≠NaturalLanguageExpectation`); **Minimality** (boxed **`Minimality=
NOT YET COMPUTABLY ESTABLISHED`**, explicitly "not a defect... a correct research status" — delivers
**`Empirical minimality evidence≠Mathematical minimality proof`**, retroactively contextualizing every
removal-test table from 0888-0893); Policy (`Governance decision=possibly non-computable input`);
Evidence ("computable in layers"); Statistics (boxed **`StatisticalFormula≠ComputableKnowledgeOSQuantity`**
— generalizes 0893's probability-distribution rejection); Probability (`UNDER-SPECIFIED` without an
explicit `(Ω,𝔉,P)` space); Uncertainty (`not computable until its semantics are fixed` — probability vs.
confidence vs. epistemic strength vs. error bound vs. entropy vs. subjective belief are mathematically
different).

**§266.28 delivers "perhaps the most valuable output of Step 266": a 23-row Hidden Human Judgment Map**,
a single consolidated color-coded computability status for the entire theory to date.

**§266.29-266.30 delivers the THREE-LAYER ARCHITECTURE**: Formal/Deterministic Core (typing, identity
references, canonicalization, equality, membership, transition validation, replay, hashing) /
External/Parametric Semantics (policy, evidence, time, provenance store, authority, measurement regime) /
Normative/Assessment Boundary (truth assessment, relevance, adequacy, authority, epistemic judgment,
statistical assumptions) — with an explicit DDD warning against hiding an unmodeled human judgment inside
a method named `isValid()`.

**§266.31 delivers "the most important finding"**: boxed **`KnowledgeOS can be computationally rigorous
without claiming that all knowledge judgment is computable`** — proposing an explicit `ComputableCore`
(same inputs → same result) vs. `JudgmentBoundary` (decision recorded, attributed, contextualized,
auditable) split.

**§266.32 delivers the candidate Computability Boundary Principle**: boxed **`Computability(T)⇒
Computability(all semantic dependencies of T)`** — explicitly "a derived candidate principle, not yet a
formal theorem." **§266.33-266.34 reframes the kernel problem** into two separate questions (what must be
in the deterministic state vs. what remains an explicit external/normative input), delivering boxed
**`Computability of candidate K≠Sufficiency of candidate K`** as two independent gates.

**§266.35** delivers a further 29-row audit matrix. **§266.36 delivers an honest self-classification**
using the six-level evidence vocabulary (from 0888-0889): this step's claims are mostly `DERIVED`/
`VERIFIED BY FORMAL ANALYSIS`, explicitly not `EXECUTED` or `IMPLEMENTED`. **§266.37 delivers the critical
negative result**: boxed **`The existence of formal notation does not imply computability`** — `Relevant`,
`Minimal`, `Truth` can all be syntactically valid yet undecidable. **§266.38 delivers a five-label
replacement vocabulary** for "valid": `DECIDABLE`/`COMPUTABLE`/`PARAMETRIC`/`HUMAN-ASSESSABLE`/
`UNDER-SPECIFIED`.

**Verdict**: boxed **`COMPUTABILITY: PARTIALLY ESTABLISHED — NOT CLOSED`**. Biggest result: "The
KnowledgeOS theory does not need to make human judgment computable. It needs to make the boundary between
deterministic computation and explicit judgment formally visible."
