## Tier 2
**TRIGGERED.** Possibly the most fundamental reframing since 0087 itself.

**⭐ H-KOS-Relation-001** ("probably one of the strongest kernel candidates discovered so far"):
*"KnowledgeOS SHALL treat epistemic relationships as first-class entities and SHALL NOT reduce them
into attributes of knowledge objects."* Sourced from **Sambandha** (Relation), described in Navya-Nyāya
as "ontologically primary, not merely grammatical." **Explicitly reinterprets every prior
forbidden-collapse pair** (`Evidence≠Authority`, `Observation≠Decision`, `Assessment≠Authority`,
`Expression≠Meaning`, `Representation≠Identity`) as a **relationship constraint**, not an object
property — a potential meta-level reframing of the whole approach: is the kernel about state
components, or about the relationships between them?

**H-KOS-Context-002** (Avacchedaka/delimiter): *"KnowledgeOS SHALL preserve the delimiting conditions
under which a proposition is valid."* Reframes Context — contested since 0088–0091 — away from a flat
state-dimension toward a **boundary/delimiter** concept: "context is not metadata, context is a
logical boundary." This is a third, still-incompatible treatment of Context (0089's flat dimension /
0091's merge-into-Identity / 0094's delimiter-boundary).

**16 Padārthas mapped onto an epistemic-inquiry lifecycle** (not kernel entities):
`Observation → Doubt → Investigation → Reasoning → Challenge → Determination → Knowledge State`
(`H-KOS-Lifecycle-Reasoning-001`). **Vāda/Jalpa/Vitaṇḍā** (truth-seeking/competitive/destructive
debate) → `H-KOS-Intent-001`: the *intent* behind reasoning matters and should be classified.
**Hetvābhāsa** (fallacy taxonomy, already named at 0092) → `H-KOS-Failure-001` — restated from 0093's
`H-KOS-Fallacy-001` under a slightly different name, the H-KOS-* scheme's **first internal
near-duplicate naming event**, flagged rather than silently merged.

**Genuinely sophisticated Navya-Nyāya vocabulary**: Pratiyogin/Anuyogin (the two terms in a relation),
**Abhāva** (*typed*, structured absence, not mere "nothing"), **Tādātmya** (non-extensional identity),
Paryāpti (a quantification concept), Viśeṣaṇa-viśeṣya (qualifier-qualificand), and "unbounded
relational depth" (paramparā-sambandha — relations of relations of relations). Grounded in real
history (Gaṅgeśa Upādhyāya's 14th-century *Tattvacintāmaṇi*; Daniel Ingalls' comparison to Frege) —
among the most mathematically sophisticated Indian-logic content in the corpus, the document itself
noting unconfirmed academic attempts to encode Navya-Nyāya in modern type theory.

**Updated Kernel Formula — a fifth non-identical reshaping.** "Preserves" 1–8: Identity, Evidence,
Authority, **Context**, Transformation, Time, Uncertainty, **Knower** (both kept as *separate* items,
unlike 0091's merge). "Through" 9–12: Relations, Reasoning chains, Validation processes, Revision
history — an explicit two-tier structure (preserved state vs. the mechanism preserving it).

**Explicit demotion of Mokṣa.** "Nyāya liberation goal" placed in "Research Only," not among
kernel-relevant candidates — the corpus's first explicit disciplinary ruling on the
Purification/Mokṣa correspondence row of the MD-015 correspondence table.

**Final Insight**, a five-lens summary: Vāṇī (meaning survives expression) + Zero (identity survives
transformation) + Gita/Vedanta (knower and known cannot be separated) + Tarka (reasoning must be
governed) + Navya-Nyāya (relationships are the primitive through which knowledge exists) → *"Knowledge
is not a thing. Knowledge is a justified network of relationships between a knower, an object,
evidence, reasoning, context, and conclusion."* Called "likely the final missing piece before P4
mapping."
