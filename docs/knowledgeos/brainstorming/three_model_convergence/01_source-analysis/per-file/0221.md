## Tier 2
**TRIGGERED (critical).** Ammerman & Singer's *Belief, Knowledge, and Truth* anthology (Prichard, Whately,
Peirce, Cook Wilson, Russell, Ryle, plus five competing truth theories), explicitly framed as extending
"our previous synthesis" (0216). Key findings: **belief is not low-confidence knowledge** (Prichard) —
sharpens `CONFIDENCE!=KNOWLEDGE` to `CONFIDENCE!=EPISTEMIC_KIND`; **doubt ≠ disbelief**, introducing
`NOT_PROVEN` distinct from `REJECTED` (Whately); **Peirce's Doubt→Inquiry→Settlement→Belief→Action chain**,
independently converging with the corpus's prior OpenInvestigation/KnowledgeInvestigation/Inquiry family —
motivates a proposed **Inquiry Context** as its own bounded context, explicitly not owning Knowledge;
**epistemic status ≠ practical decision** (Cook Wilson) — one may act on a `NOT_PROVEN` hypothesis without
contradiction, strengthening the Decision Boundary. Multiple incompatible truth theories deliberately
juxtaposed by the anthology directly motivate: **do not build a TruthService** — that would silently
choose one epistemological theory. Adds `EVIDENCE_STRENGTH!=TRUTH_FORCE`, refines `UNKNOWN` ("inquiry
has not established sufficient standing," not "nobody knows"), and — the most important DDD correction —
splits the prior single combinatorial epistemic state machine into **three orthogonal dimensions**:
EpistemicStanding, EvidenceCondition, OperationalDisposition. Adds `InquiryStoppingPolicy`, connecting
Peirce's doubt-settlement to the corpus's prior Value-of-Information work.
