## Tier 2
**TRIGGERED.** Second consecutive direct primary-source engagement (after 0264's Tractatus), this time
with Shannon & Weaver's *The Mathematical Theory of Communication*, read specifically for KnowledgeOS
architectural consequences. Establishes **Information ≠ Meaning ≠ Knowledge** as architectural doctrine,
directly grounded in Shannon's technical result that meaningful and nonsense messages can be
information-theoretically equivalent. **Grounds (rather than merely resembles) the corpus's prior
independently-proposed entropy-based knowledge-gain formulas** (0259's `H(K)`, 0264's `K(P)=1-H(P|E)`) in
Shannon's actual mathematics — equivocation `H(X|Y)` (called "perhaps the most useful concept in the entire
book") and mutual information `I(X;E)=H(X)-H(X|E)` — a genuine convergence event, not another independent
variant. Introduces two noise types (engineering vs. Weaver's own semantic noise/semantic receiver), a
proposed **Semantic Integrity Layer**, the distinction between duplication (weak) and independent redundancy
(strong — "an epistemic error-correcting code," do not aggressively deduplicate), channel capacity reframed
as an agent-knowledge-delivery constraint (`max useful knowledge delivered s.t. context_load ≤
agent_capacity`), a shift from document-centric to observation-centric KnowledgeOS (documents as one
evidence channel among many), and a proposed **KnowledgeOS Agent Knowledge Protocol**. Adds a fourteenth
Knowledge-vector-family variant (a multiplicative `Knowledge Quality ~ Meaning×EvidenceFidelity×
Consistency×ContextValidity×TemporalValidity` equation with paired uncertainty/resilience functions `U(K)`,
`R(K)`). Closes with two headline principles explicitly held as research-derived, pending comparison
against the existing frozen KnowledgeOS architecture before adoption — a disciplined non-adoption stance.
