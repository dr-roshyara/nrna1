## What Is This Document About?

The document is a **synthesis, explicitly not a discovery** — it claims every assertion traces to
an artifact already produced. Its central intellectual work is to answer "what are we actually
building?" for KnowledgeOS. It distinguishes four product-level entities: **KnowledgeOS** (a
reusable engineering platform whose output is *rules and methods*, never product knowledge), the
**PKS / Product Knowledge Space** (one product's knowledge; an instance, never reusable), the
**Business Product**, and **Running Software**, plus the **AI Runtime** which it demotes to *an
adapter, not a product*. It contributes one concept of its own: **Product Binding** — the
product-specific input contract that a reusable method requires.

Its sharpest move is §3, the **extraction boundary**. The document argues that the boundary
between platform and product is *not a file list but a decomposition*: every artifact splits into
**METHOD** (domain-free, binding-free, evidence-free → KnowledgeOS), **BINDING** (the
product-specific input contract → PKS) and **EVIDENCE / case law** (what happened in one project
→ PKS). It applies this to `Round47-OP` and shows the nine boundary criteria are METHOD while
`SD-1` is a BINDING and the worked election seams are EVIDENCE. It then generalises this to a
four-tier **Portability Ladder** and reports an instrument finding: *neither Tier-2 (binding-
coupled) nor Tier-3 (case-law-diluted) blockage is detectable by searching for domain vocabulary*
— a grep for election terms found neither, so a three-question test is required instead.

Three further principles are recorded: **P3** separates a *responsibility* (exists — an architect
reading documents) from a *component* (does not exist, n=0), with the honest formulation that the
PKS Generator today is "a Human-in-the-Loop Adapter — the port exists; the adapter is a person";
**P4** corrects the evidence loop from `Evidence → Decision → Change` to `Evidence → HARVEST →
Candidate → Promotion → Change`; **P5** synthesises the product architecture and refuses to assign
a DDD relationship pattern, noting that the proposed "Separate Ways with a Shared Kernel" reading
is internally tense in Evans' taxonomy (Separate Ways means *no* integration).

Methodologically the document is disciplined to an unusual degree, and this discipline — not its
content — is what makes it relevant to the convergence research. It (a) marks itself CANDIDATE,
not adopted, because "a baseline becomes a baseline by an adoption event, not by being written";
(b) applies an explicit evidence-strength rule (ES-006.1: *n=1 admits a CANDIDATE, never a rule*)
and therefore demotes **all five** of its own principles to candidates; (c) refuses to mint
identifiers after a capability check returned `INCONCLUSIVE`, logging that the tool's verdict
*changed the action*; and (d) refuses to propose any directory, citing a rule that "a directory
exists only when its first artifact arrives". It closes with a register of 13 open questions
(OQ-S1..S5, OQ-K1..K6, OQ-C1..C2) and 10 evidence gaps (G-1..G-10) that it deliberately leaves open.

## Main Argument / Purpose

**Argument:** KnowledgeOS's *method* has been demonstrated portable; its *boundary* has not.
Therefore the outstanding work is not extraction (moving files) but decomposition (separating
method from binding from evidence in each artifact), and that decomposition cannot be automated by
vocabulary search. **Purpose:** to give the Decision Authority a single falsifiable strategic
reference model, and to convert an accumulation of reports into one submitted candidate.

## TIER 2 — DEEP ANALYSIS
**Trigger status: TRIGGERED** — the file states a **minimality/membership criterion for what
belongs inside a "kernel"**, and installs the evidence-strength ordering that later governs the
formal status of every claim in the corpus. Both bear directly on §23 (Kernel state minimality) and
§3 (status vocabulary).

### Mathematical derivation
None. No derivation is performed anywhere in the file.

### Formal definitions
No *mathematical* definition. Three **quasi-formal** constructs are stated with enough precision to
be tested, and are recorded here at exactly that strength:

1. **Tier-1 (kernel) membership predicate.**
   `Tier1(a) ⟺ domainFree(a) ∧ bindingFree(a) ∧ evidenceFree(a)`
   A three-place conjunction over artifacts. `[DF]` in the engineering register; `[UN]` as
   mathematics — none of the three predicates has a decision procedure. The file itself supplies
   the negative result that makes this sharp: **the predicates are not decidable by vocabulary
   search.** A grep for domain terms returned false-negative on both binding-coupling and case-law
   dilution. So the predicate is *stated* but *not computable*, and the file knows it.

2. **Responsibility→Component promotion predicate.**
   `Component(r) ⟸ exercised(r) ≥ 1 ∧ ∃ actor ≠ originator ∧ patternExtracted(r, n ≥ 2)`

3. **Evidence-strength ordering.** `n=0 ⇒ ∅ · n=1 ⇒ CANDIDATE · n≥2 ⇒ promotable`.
   An **ordinal** grading on occurrence count. Not a probability, not a measure, not a statistic.

### Operator analysis
**No operators.** The file contains processes (HARVEST, PROMOTE, DECOMPOSE, EXTRACT) with no
input/output type, no precondition, no postcondition and no composition. Registering any of them in
the §21 operator register at this point would be an analogy→operator promotion, which §3 forbids.
Recorded as `[AN]`, and deliberately **not** carried to `07_formalization/operator-candidate-register.md`.

### Contradictions

Kernel"* is incoherent in Evans' taxonomy (Separate Ways means no integration; a Shared Kernel is
integration). The file refers it to authority rather than resolving it. `[CT]` — recorded against
