example (mandatory ADR review vs. standard review). Produces a four-type epistemic taxonomy (Descriptive/
Associational/Causal/Counterfactual) and a 21-primitive **Causal Reasoning & Assurance Layer**. Proposes two
constitutional principles: no associational→causal promotion without explicit identifiability/sensitivity,
and prediction ≠ causal inference must remain architecturally distinct. Directly operationalizes and extends
0205's `CAUSAL-INFERENCE-GATE`/Claim Maturity Ladder — logged as `unresolved_equivalence` against 0205's
