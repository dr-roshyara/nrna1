## What Is This Document About?
The document evaluates the architecture against one declared fitness function: *does today's
architecture support `knowledgeos init`?* Its answer is deliberately split. **Architecturally:
almost** — 9 of 11 responsibilities have a place, a method and an owner, and only one true
architectural gap exists. **Evidentially: no**, and `knowledgeos init` is judged *premature as a
build target*, because componentising a responsibility requires n ≥ 2 with an extracted repeatable
pattern and PKS Generation stands at n=0.

Its stated main contribution is the **separation of three kinds of gap** that the corpus had been
conflating: **ARCHITECTURAL** (a responsibility the architecture has no place for — resolved by
modelling; exactly one, GENESIS), **GOVERNANCE** (a clause coupling a reusable thing to one product
— resolved by a ruling; exactly one, SD-1), and **EVIDENCE** (the thing exists but has never been
exercised — resolvable *only by doing it*; four). This converts a vague "the gap is evidence, not
architecture" into an exact count: 1 / 1 / 4.

The assessment then runs an 11-responsibility matrix across four commissioned dimensions
(METHOD · BINDING · EVIDENCE · IMPLEMENTATION) and adds a fifth of its own: **ENFORCEMENT**. The
dimension totals are the shape of its answer — METHOD is nearly complete (8 of 11), EVIDENCE is thin
(4 at n=0), IMPLEMENTATION is mostly manual (6 of 11), and ENFORCEMENT was initially scored 0 of 11.

That last score is then **falsified inside the same document**, and the self-correction is the most
methodologically interesting event in the file. Applying a `Boundaries ≠ Triggers` vocabulary
immediately showed that the runtime enforces 41 controls (`deny`×19, `ask`×22) while 16 advisory
mechanisms enforce nothing. The corrected claim is sharper than the original: *no **capability**
enforces* — and **enforcement lives in the runtime adapter while capabilities live in the platform,
so enforcement exists exactly where governance does not, and is absent exactly where it does.**

Three further corrections are issued against the commissioning addendum: SD-1 was misattributed to
Product Discovery when it constrains Strategic DDD; a binding leak (`ES-005.1` naming a specific
product) sits **inside the governance layer itself**, the one place the model assumed clean; and *a
specification is not an implementation*, demoting the Runtime Adapter from L3 to L1. The document
also affirms a precise limit on extraction: decomposition is permitted, but **decomposition followed
by materialization remains barred** — *the output of decomposition is a classification, not a
document set*.
