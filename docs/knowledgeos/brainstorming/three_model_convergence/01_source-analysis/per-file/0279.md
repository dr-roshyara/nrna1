## Tier 2
**TRIGGERED — landmark methodological file.** Not a source extraction: explicitly refuses to freeze the
Knowledge Space Kernel on McGinn alone ("McGinn exposed the logical/metaphysical floor, but does not by
itself define what a Knowledge Space is"), and proposes a seven-book research program — Floridi
(*Philosophy of Information*), Williamson (*Knowledge and Its Limits*, treating knowledge as
**explanatorily fundamental** rather than reducible to truth+belief+justification — flagged as a potential
future reversal of the corpus's working assumption), Dretske (*Knowledge and the Flow of Information*),
Fagin/Halpern/Moses/Vardi (*Reasoning About Knowledge*, "probably the book that could most directly connect
the philosophical Kernel to the AI Engineering Platform"), Brachman & Levesque (*Knowledge Representation
and Reasoning*), Brandom (*Making It Explicit*), and Searle (*The Construction of Social Reality*), each
with a stated priority and Zero-Lens question. Institutes a **cross-book-intersection discipline**
(`Book A → Kernel Candidates A`, `Book B → Kernel Candidates B`, … → Cross-Book Intersection → Zero-Lens
Invariants → Knowledge Space Kernel) — "much stronger than taking one philosopher's ontology and declaring
it our architecture" — the corpus's own forward-looking corrective for exactly the single-source
literalization risk exhibited at 0276/0278, though not a retroactive resolution of that tension. Proposes a
standardized 19-question Zero-Lens Extraction template for future book engagements, and a
three-primitive-dimension hypothesis (Ontological / Semantic / Epistemic spaces, unified by a Modal/
Temporal structure) as a candidate alternative to a single unified ontology.
