## Tier 2
**TRIGGERED — landmark architectural proposal.** Extends 0429's Sañjaya mapping into a full **TWO-LAYER
KnowledgeOS architecture**: **Sañjaya Layer** (L_S=State Knowledge — "what is the state?", reconstructs
without judgment) and **Arjuna Layer** (L_A=Understanding/Decision Knowledge — "what does this mean for
me?", Compare(S_t,I_N)→Understanding_t). Explicitly declines to make Krishna a third layer — Krishna is a
lens/reasoning mechanism operating ON the Arjuna layer.

**Central formal contributions**: splits **Zero into Zero_S/Zero_A** (state-knowledge gaps vs.
understanding/decision gaps) and **Lord into Lord-Sañjaya/Lord-Arjuna** — the latter distinguishing
discovering new dimensions *of the observed state* from discovering that *the ideal state's own definition*
may be incomplete ("we didn't know Nexus had this dependency" vs. "we didn't realize this dependency was
part of what the ideal Nexus state needed to describe"). Produces a non-linear **recursive epistemic
feedback loop** (Lord→Sañjaya→Zero→Arjuna→Krishna→Human-Knower-learns→Refines-Ideal-State→loops back).
Proposed explicitly as a candidate architecture to test against Chapter 2, continuing 0427's
falsification-testing plan.
