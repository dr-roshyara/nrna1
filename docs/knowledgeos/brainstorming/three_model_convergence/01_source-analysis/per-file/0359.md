## Tier 2
**TRIGGERED — corpus's first direct engagement with Knowledge Space Theory's actual formalism**,
operationalizing Phase B of 0357's six-phase reading programme (the exact source whose existence was
discovered at 0351 and fully bibliographically identified at 0357: Doignon & Falmagne). Precisely
establishes the core formalism — `Q` (domain of items/questions), `K ⊆ Q` (a knowledge state), `(Q,𝒦)` (a
knowledge structure), and a true **knowledge space** additionally requiring closure under union — and uses
this to disambiguate the corpus's own long-informal phrase "Knowledge Space" into (at least) six distinct
senses that must not be equated. Extracts, via an intervening analysis it explicitly corrects rather than
adopts wholesale: **surmise systems** (prerequisite AND/OR clauses `σ(q)`, with an explicit warning against
conflating dependency-as-correlation/prerequisite/entailment/cause/inference); **base/atoms** as a
compact generative representation for combinatorially huge state spaces (up to 2^100 states for 100 items),
flagged as a scalability-relevant hypothesis, not a Kernel-storage mandate; the **entailment↔knowledge-space
one-to-one correspondence**, called "perhaps the strongest result," directly supporting the corpus's own
regime concept (multiple equivalent representations of one phenomenon); an **expert-elicitation** mechanism
raising a new provenance/authority question (when does an expert assertion become a structural rule rather
than an epistemic fact?); a **skill mapping** `τ:Q→2^S` showing an epistemic state can be derived from a
latent structure; a **competency-model representation theorem**, raising a question explicitly named
"central to our Kernel research" — if multiple internal models generate the same observable state, should
the Kernel preserve the *model* or only the *evidence*? — **probabilistic knowledge structures**
`(Q,𝒦,p)`, concrete evidence that Knowledge State ≠ Probability (probability layered *on top of* a
structural object); a **response function** `r(R,K)` explicitly modeling careless errors and lucky guesses,
producing a genuine, still-open research intersection with Pritchard's anti-luck condition (0358); a real
**temporal learning model** (maximal chains of states with stochastic transition times), explicitly scoped
to learning/acquisition within KST's own domain and NOT claimed to solve KnowledgeOS's broader
historical-truth/reconstruction problem; **adaptive Markov assessment procedures**, concrete computational
support for "extraction can be probabilistic while the state itself is not"; and **Galois connections**
among structures/relations/surmise-functions/entailments, again read as evidence for multiple
representations rather than a mandate that the Kernel itself be Galois-connected algebra.

Explicitly **rejects three claims** from the intervening analysis: (1) "model the Kernel as `Q` and the
immutable historical record" — rejected, `Q` is merely a domain of items, `Kernel=Q` is an invented mapping,
not a KST result; (2) "Knowledge State is a *perfect* candidate for Projection" — downgraded to "candidate,
yes; perfect, no," since KST's state has a specific solve-based interpretation needing further testing;
(3) "Knowledge Spaces provides a *complete framework* for KnowledgeOS" — rejected outright, with an
explicit list of what KST does not solve (truth, testimony, belief, justification, temporal truth,
institutional knowledge, provenance, understanding, observer-dependence, general epistemic states, AI
knowledge). Produces **ten disciplined FACT-KST-01..10 items** and **three new hypotheses** (H-KST-1:
Knowledge Space = admissible states, not "everything knowable"; H-KST-2: knowledge may be structural while
*extraction* is probabilistic; H-KST-3: state derivable from substrate via Evidence/Responses→Assessment→
KnowledgeState). Reaches the corpus's **sharpest non-collapse principle to date**: `𝒦 ≠ K_t^A ≠
P(K_t^A=K|O_≤t^A)` (Knowledge Space ≠ actual epistemic state ≠ belief/probability over states), which,
combined with Pritchard (0358), yields a four-way principle — **Knowledge ≠ Knowledge State ≠ Knowledge
Space ≠ Knowledge Extraction** — explicitly called "now one of the strongest conclusions in our research."
Closes with a sharpened next research question (`H_≤t,O_≤t^A →[R]→ K_t^A ∈ 𝒦`, followed by a *separate*
later Pritchard-style `K_t^A →[R_epistemology]→ Knows(A,p)?`) and an explicitly **non-final** layered
research-synthesis diagram: Historical Substrate → Observations → Epistemic State → [Knowledge Space,
Probability] → Epistemic Regime → Knowledge Claim → Understanding — recommending the next step attack this
distinction rather than add further components.
