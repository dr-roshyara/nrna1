Chapter 3, directly re-reading the primary text. Central thesis: "Chapter 3 does not directly prove our

**First correction**: Chapter 3 is reclassified as an **Action Theory with an epistemic dimension**, not an
Epistemic Theory — 0493 over-weighted the epistemic reading. Reconfirms Knowledge≠Understanding but narrows
Understanding to "interpretation of relationships among known propositions in context." Splits Purpose into
five concepts: Goal≠Duty≠Intention≠Result≠Orientation.

**Central methodological correction**: "Normative State is valid as an architectural abstraction — but NOT
as a direct Gītā fact" — 0493's "Chapter 3 proves we need Normative State" is rejected as too strong; the
correct form is that a NormativeModel is REQUIRED TO REPRESENT the domain (inference), never that the text
defines "NormativeState" (textual claim) — applied identically to Authority. **Central mathematical
correction**: rejects 0493's fixed Δ=(Δ_E,Δ_U,Δ_N,Δ_D,Δ_Decision,Δ_A) vector as conflating state/relation/
deficiency categories and assuming unjustified orthogonality (one finding can be simultaneously semantic+
normative) — replaced by **Discrepancy as a typed SET of findings**: Δ(S,C,P)=Diff(S,Expected(C,P)),
type(d_i)∈{Epistemic,Semantic,Normative,Domain,Decision,Operational,...}.

**Identifies the chapter's actually strongest insight**: same external action ⇏ same semantic action
(verses 25-26) — **ObservedAction≠MeaningfulAction≠ActionIntent**, worked via two engineers both executing
deploy() identically but one authorized/reviewed, one not — **ActionIdentity≠ActionObservation**.
Strengthens Role/Duty: Duty=f(Role,Context,NormativeFramework), not f(Actor). **Explicitly warns against
over-generalizing "sva-dharma"** — must not become "every software actor has a role and duty"; narrowed to
"role-context must participate in determining applicable duties." Corrects DecisionReady from a textual
claim to a design principle: DecisionReady(S,C) iff DecisionCriteria(S,C), never iff Δ=∅.

Revises Sārathi again: (State,Norms,Role,Context,Discrepancies)→Guidance→CandidateActions→Decision→Action.
**Corrects Lord from "reasoning engine" to AuthoritativeKnowledge+NormativeOrientation+
InterpretiveResolution**. Introduces Behavior→Example→SocialStandard (governance/exemplar concept). Corrects
"LearningReady" from a proposed state variable (0493) to a derived concept. **Second major corrective
finding**: explicitly rejects the text's senses<mind<intelligence<self hierarchy and the smoke/dust/embryo
analogy as ANY basis for a KnowledgeOS epistemic hierarchy or confidence scale — "Gītā hierarchy ≠
KnowledgeOS epistemic hierarchy," usable only as inspiration, reconfirming the corpus's anti-universal-ladder
principle from a new angle. Corrects "AffectiveState" (0493, too narrow) to a broader **AgentCondition**
(cognitive/motivational/affective/capability/role/authority/commitments).

Delivers a **cleaner separated mathematical model** rejecting 0493's ten-component 𝒳_t:
S_t=(World_t,Knowledge_t,Understanding_t,Norms_t,Agent_t,Context_t); E_t=Evaluate(S_t,Criteria_t);
Δ_t=Diff(S_t,Criteria_t); G_t=Guide(S_t,Δ_t); D_t=Decide(S_t,G_t); A_t=Execute(D_t);
S_{t+1}=T(S_t,A_t,O_{t+1}). **Delivers a crucial DDD distinction: Norm is not State** (four-way worked
example: Norm/State/Observation/Evaluation for one situation). **Rejects one giant "KnowledgeOS State"**,
proposing six bounded contexts (Knowledge, Understanding, Normative/Governance, Decision, Action,
Observation). Elevates the yajña cycle to a genuine feedback/cyclic **dynamical system**, while explicitly
declining the unsupported leap to "therefore Event Sourcing." Introduces **Authority≠Qualification** (a
production-deployment-approval example) and a **Permission-to-Act framework**:
CanAct=f(Role,Authority,Qualification,Context); Can≠Should≠Will; Actionability=(CanAct,ShouldAct,
ReadyToAct). Demotes DecisionGap to typed decision-engine reasons rather than a first-class dimension.

Freezes a final core (S=(W,K,U,N,A,C); E=Evaluate(S,P); Δ=Diff(E); G=Guide(S,Δ); D=Decide(S,G);
a=Execute(D); S'=T(S,a,o)) and reformalizes Zero/Lord/Sārathi as capabilities: Z(S,P)→Δ; L(K,N,U,C)→R;
S_a(S,R)→G. Delivers the deepest DDD conclusion: "the semantic center of the model is not Knowledge. It is
the relationship between an Actor, a Normative Context, a Situation, an Understanding, and an intended
Action." Closes with an **eight-item "what I would NOT freeze yet" list** walking back 0493's overreaches,
a thirteen-item "would freeze" list, a **ten-dimension letter-grade self-assessment of 0493** (A to C+),
diagnosing **"over-formalization too early"** as the root weakness, and introduces the required **four-stage
