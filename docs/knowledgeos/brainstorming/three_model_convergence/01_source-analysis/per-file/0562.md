## Tier 2
**TRIGGERED — CAPSTONE LANDMARK: Authority/Reliability/EvidentialSupport/KnowledgeCommitment fully
formalized.** Executes Step 19, explicitly made "a major formal layer" rather than a bare reliability
score. Poses the governing problem (incompatible propositions, how to decide accepted/uncertain/rejected/
escalated) and rejects "choose the most trustworthy source" as too simplistic.

Delivers the foundational four-way separation building on `Evidence≠Truth`/`Source≠Evidence`:
**`Authority≠Reliability≠EvidentialSupport≠KnowledgeCommitment`**. Worked three-source example
(constitution/engineer/LLM) explicitly rejects naive averaging — **"this is not a democracy"** —
governing principle: **`Knowledge commitment is governed epistemic synthesis, not source voting.`**

Formalizes `Source=(SourceID,Type,Origin,Owner,Context,AuthorityProfile,ReliabilityProfile,TemporalScope,
Provenance)` and `Source⊃Evidence`. Formalizes Evidence support as a structured, non-scalar profile
`Support(e,A)=(Direction,Strength,Specificity,Directness,Completeness)`. Formalizes Authority
(`Authority(S,A,Ctx)`) via a comparative table across seven source types, and **`Authority is scoped`**.

Formalizes Reliability `Rel(S,A,Ctx,t)` as empirically estimable but **`not an intrinsic metaphysical
property`**, worked via a 990/1000 accuracy example with explicit statistical cautions. **Introduces a
Bayesian reliability formalism** (`p~Beta(α,β)`, updated to `Beta(α+k,β+n-k)`), preserving reliability as
a distribution. Delivers **`Reliability(S)≠Reliability(S,A)`** — general reliability does not transfer to
a specific proposition type.

Formalizes Applicability `Applicable(S,A,Ctx,t)`, distinct from Authority∧Reliability (a superseded
authoritative decision no longer applies), and connects temporal validity to 0558:
`Commitment=f(Authority,Support,Reliability,Applicability,Time,...)`.

**Delivers the file's central statistical safeguard — Independence**: five websites copying one original
article are NOT five independent confirmations — `EffectiveEvidence<3`, formalized via
`Independence(e_i,e_j)` and an explicit evidence dependency graph (`DerivedFrom`), preventing "evidence
inflation." Delivers **Corroboration≠mere source count** (requires independent error mechanisms).

Delivers `EvidenceConflict(A,¬A)` handling (never collapse to `A=0.5`), a multi-dimensional evidence
comparison profile `W(e,A,C)=(Authority,Reliability,Directness,Specificity,Freshness,Independence,
Completeness)` — explicitly not one score — and **`Evidence comparison is purpose and proposition
dependent`**. **Delivers the file's key mathematical result — evidence need not be totally ordered**: a
Pareto-style partial order `e_1⪰e_2` permits genuine incomparability, `EvidenceComparison=Incomparable`
— an explicit alternative to inventing a winner. Delivers `AuthorityHierarchy is domain policy` and
proposition-specific authority (worked source-reliability matrix), preventing "global trust rankings from
becoming absurd."

**Delivers the file's central LLM-governance statement**: `LLMOutput` is "a generated informational
artifact, not a trusted source of organizational truth" — `Authority(LLM,GovernanceRule)=0` unless
explicitly assigned; outputs become only Candidate Evidence/Assertion/Inference —
`LLM→Candidate→EvidenceAssessment→GovernedKnowledge`, "one of the defining architectural properties of
KnowledgeOS."

**Delivers the file's central formal deliverable — Knowledge Commitment**: a non-linear lifecycle
(Candidate→Supported→Corroborated→Accepted→Committed, with branches). Formalizes
**`Committed_ρ(A,K,Ctx,t)`** — governance-permitted acceptance, explicitly superior to "KnowledgeOS knows
A." Delivers purpose-sensitive commitment `Commitment(A,P,Ctx,t)` (worked "Nexus is a production system"
example). Formalizes a four-way Knowledge State partition `K_t=K_C∪K_S∪K_U∪K_X`, and the crucial statement
**`Committed(A)⇏A is universally true`**. Formalizes the commitment function
`Commit_ρ(A,K,E)→{Commit,Reject,Defer,Escalate,RemainUncertain}` and a structured commitment predicate
preferred over a bare score threshold. Delivers `Thresholds are policy, not mathematics of truth`.

Delivers a careful Bayesian boundary: `P(A|E)=0.95` does not imply `Committed(A)` —
**`EpistemicProbability≠OperationalPermission`**, "extremely important." Delivers **the second major
statistical safeguard — no naive evidence multiplication** (must not multiply likelihoods for copied
evidence). Delivers reliability drift/temporality (`Trust is not necessarily stationary`, connects to
0558) and Authority's own temporal governance. Delivers **`Quality(Source)≠Quality(Evidence)`** (a
reliable source can still hold a stale record).

Formalizes the **Knowledge Assessment** conclusion object `KA=(Assertion,SupportingEvidence,
ContradictingEvidence,AuthorityAssessment,ReliabilityAssessment,Applicability,TemporalAssessment,
IndependenceAssessment,ConflictStatus,CommitmentStatus,Rationale)` — "the bridge between Evidence and
Knowledge State," explicitly contrasted against a bare scalar as "vastly more informative." Delivers a
full Commitment lifecycle with `Disputed⇏Rejected`, Commitment revocation
(`Committed(A)→UnderReview(A)→[possibly]Retracted(A)`), Commitment as a domain event
`KnowledgeCommitted=(AssertionID,Decision,PolicyVersion,DecisionMaker,EvidenceSet,Timestamp,Context)`, and
Human vs. Automated commitment (`CommitmentAuthority`, `AutomationPermission(A,Ctx)`).

**Delivers the file's central methodological rejection**: explicitly rejects naive linear weighted-sum
evidence scoring `W=w_1A+w_2R+w_3F` as the fundamental model, favoring a structured
`EvidenceProfile=(A,R,S,D,F,I,C,...)` interpreted by policy. Delivers the **general commitment function**
`Commit_ρ(A,E,S,Ctx,t,P)→𝒮` (𝒮 a seven-value result space, richer than 0557's six-value Sārathi decision

Delivers a consolidated six-stage evidence-to-knowledge pipeline governed by
Authority+Reliability+Applicability+TemporalValidity+Independence+Conflict. **Extends Zero significantly**
(InsufficientEvidence/AuthorityConflict/ReliabilityConcern/SourceDependence/TemporalExpiry/
UnresolvedConflict/UncommittedCriticalKnowledge). Extends Lord (independent-source, system-of-record,
authority-consultation, revalidation actions) and Sārathi
(`RequiredEvidenceLevel depends on action risk`, connecting Steps 15 and 19).

Delivers **Risk-adjusted epistemic requirement**: `RequiredConfidence=f(Risk,Impact,Reversibility,

**Delivers the file's central formal deliverable — fifteen canonical invariants (A1-A15)**: Source≠
Evidence; Authority≠Reliability; Reliability≠EvidentialSupport; EvidentialSupport≠KnowledgeCommitment;
