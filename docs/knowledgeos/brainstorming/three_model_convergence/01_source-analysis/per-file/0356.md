## Tier 2
**TRIGGERED — likely the decisive research-direction synthesis point for the entire 0347-0356
phase_measure_theory stretch.** Rejects both further tuple formalization and deeper measure theory in
favor of **Temporal Epistemic Reconstruction**: "what is required to reconstruct what a participant could
legitimately know at a particular point in time?" — chosen because it "simultaneously tests almost every
hypothesis we currently have" while deferring commitment to any mathematical regime. Deliberately
**suspends "What is KnowledgeOS?"** (too many coexisting candidate descriptions) in favor of one unifying
underlying problem: "given a changing world and incomplete observations, reconstruct the epistemic state
that was supportable at a particular time, for a particular participant, under a particular context and
evaluation regime." States a new independence-of-changes invariant, **`ΔD ≠ ΔF ≠ ΔE ≠ ΔK`**. Proposes two
concrete worked temporal episodes — a version-upgrade scenario testing "what was A's knowledge at 11:30?
Not what is true now" and an extended conflicting-monitoring-data episode generating genuinely hard
questions (does the system need to *resolve* contradictions or merely *preserve* them? is truth something
the Kernel knows, or something a regime evaluates?) — explicitly "more powerful than implementing ten Core
entities." Proposes a **four-regime "attack knowledge" test** (logical/probabilistic/institutional/
evidential) on the *same* substrate, asking whether these are different kinds of Knowledge or different
evaluations of one substrate — "much more important than deciding whether `Knows` is primitive." Precisely
re-sequences measure theory's entry point: only *after* a concrete episode exists should one ask whether
probabilistic extraction can be rigorously formulated, letting the case decide exactly which machinery is
needed — **"problem first, mathematical structure second,"** explicitly rejecting the reverse. Reformulates
the **strongest-yet Zero Lens**: "remove the candidate; what fundamentally becomes impossible?" — applied
to every element of the candidate ten-tuple — and proposes `S≤t →[A,C,R]→ E_t^{A,R}` with an explicit test
for whether `S≤t` can remain **regime-neutral**. Proposes determining Kernel membership **empirically**
via removal experiments rather than by assertion, and organizes the next research phase into **five
tracks** (A Temporal Epistemology, B Knowledge Space — explicitly citing 0351's KST discovery, C
Extraction, D Mathematical Regimes, E Kernel Falsification — sequenced *last*). Explicitly defers all
implementation (no tuple build, no `KnowledgeAttribution`, no `Knows` aggregate, no probabilistic regime,
no frozen API/invariants) in favor of first proving necessity. A second, fully endorsing layer rates this
**"the most methodologically mature proposal we have seen,"** confirms every point via a ten-row agreement
table, flags five unresolved items (historical-substrate definition, reconstruction-process formalization,
`ValidAt`/`CurrentAt`/`CompleteRelativeTo` testing, regime-comparison criteria, actually running the
Remove-X test), and produces a concrete six-item execution plan.
