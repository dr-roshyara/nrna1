a richer `Status(k,t,C)`), challenges the ten-criteria list for conflating different layers, and revises

**Section 3** (lines 1027-1706) introduces the **Ideal Knowledge State `K_t*`** — not "all Knowledge" but
what would answer all *legitimate* questions `Q_t^D` at time `t`, with `K_t*` explicitly **unobservable**
(otherwise not "ideal"), gap `Δ_t=K_t*-K̂_t`. Distinguishes an `E[K_t|I_≤t]` framing (needs `K_t` as a
random variable — not assumed) from a safer `Estimator(K_t*|I_≤t)`. Introduces **Deduction/Induction/
Abduction** as genuinely distinct extraction mechanisms (worked deductive and abductive examples),
requiring **Probability + Logic to coexist** (not OR). Introduces **Answerability** as an alternative
Knowledge-sufficiency criterion — "Unknown"/"insufficient evidence" are themselves valid Knowledge.

**Section 4** (lines 1707-2195, duplicated at 2198-2686) opens an **Epistemic-vs-Normative justification**
dichotomy (Evidence→Claim vs. Rule/Principle→Decision), distinguishing **Manifesto** (a principle set
justifying decisions within its own system, Manifesto≠Evidence) from **Documentation** (a carrier, not the
justification itself). Formalizes distinct justification graphs and a `J_0→J_1→...→J_4` authority chain,
tying to `K_t*` via "Answerability requires justification traceability." Disambiguates "natural laws of
humanity" into three explicit candidate meanings, refusing to adopt any. Closes with the pivotal open
question: one Knowledge State, or a **multi-regime epistemic model**?

**Section 5** (lines 2688-3206) concretizes justification into a **Fact-based comparison-and-closure**
model: `(F_t,R_t)⊢K`; Evidence as the `O_t→E→F` bridge; the pivotal **Comparison** mechanism
`(F_≤t,S_t)→[Comparison]→Δ_t→[Reasoning]→K_t`, showing Knowledge can be *derived*, not merely stored
(worked example: version-mismatch knowledge not directly observed). Reformulates `K_t* = Cn_{R_t}(S_t∪F_≤t)`
(logical closure), **relocates probability earlier** (`O_t→P(F_i|O_t)→F̂_t`), and re-attributes the
approximation gap to incomplete *facts*, not imperfect logic. Closes with a seven-stage research-chain
diagram and the corpus's new, sharper next question: **"What is a Fact?"** — proposed as the key to
Knowledge → Knowledge Unit → Kernel.
