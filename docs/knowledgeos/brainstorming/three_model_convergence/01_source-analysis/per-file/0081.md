## Tier 2
**TRIGGERED.** The most rigorous, evidence-graded cross-system kernel-candidate synthesis in the
corpus to date.

**Central finding.** DeepSeek did not find a hidden "KnowledgeOS kernel" — it found that EKS, PKS, and
AIP "already share a small set of epistemic/governance invariants, even though they do not share a
common implementation or explicitly define a common kernel." The report is explicit that it is not
authoritative and does not itself define the kernel.

**Ten architectural findings, each graded per system:**
1. **Authority deliberately outside knowledge** (CONFIDENCE ≠ AUTHORITY) — ESTABLISHED in EKS/PKS,
   PARTIALLY ESTABLISHED in AIP. `transitions[] ≠ grants[]` in EKS; promotion-as-authority-act in PKS.
2. **Not probabilistic** — an external CAPPI proposal (μ/τ/w Bayesian fusion → continuous quality
   score) tested against current architecture finds **zero support**: all three systems use closed,
   discrete epistemic vocabularies (PASS/FAIL/WARN/INCONCLUSIVE/UNKNOWN/AMBIGUOUS…) instead.
3. **UNKNOWN as first-class**, not an exception — distinct from FALSE, from NOT AUTHORIZED, from
   DENIED. Proposed as candidate **C-10**.
4. **Discrete epistemic states strongly preferred** across all three, independently converged on.
5. **State = fold** — current state is always reconstructed from an append-only, forward-only history,
   never independently mutable. Candidate **C-2**.
6. **No in-place rewriting / forward-only supersession** — strongest in PKS (AP-3), present but not
   mechanically enforced in AIP. Candidate **C-5**.
7. **Diagnostics must not mutate authoritative state** — ESTABLISHED (EKS resolver read-pure; PKS
   projections regenerable/non-authoritative; AIP weaker but still distinguishes produced-record from
   owned-authority).
8. **Invariant ownership, not events, produces consistency** — none of the three has an event bus, yet
   each achieves consistency via single-owner invariants (EKS: 11/11). Explicitly scoped: SUPPORTED for
   non-distributed systems, UNKNOWN for a distributed KnowledgeOS.
9. **Fail-closed philosophy** across all three — explicitly **CONTRADICTS** CAPPI's proposed
   fallback-version chain (v21→v20→v18→expert floor).
10. **Semantic non-collapse principle** — a recurring family of `X ≠ Y` boundaries (ACTIVE≠AUTHORIZED,
    REGISTRY≠AUTHORITY, ASSESSMENT≠AUTHORITY, RECOMMENDATION≠DECISION, DIAGNOSTIC≠AUTHORITATIVE STATE,
    UNKNOWN≠FALSE, PROJECTION≠SOURCE) — proposed as possibly the deeper pattern behind most of the
    other findings, including PKS's authority⊥status⊥maturity⊥adoption orthogonality.

**Negative finding, direct empirical test of 0080's hypothesis.** 0080 (same day, prior sequence)
proposed `Observation→Evidence→Claim→Inference→Belief→{confidence,authority}→Decision`. This file
reports DeepSeek's test of that shape against real architecture: **Observation, Evidence, Authority,
Decision are present; Claim and Confidence are NOT.** Explicit conclusion: "Claim and Confidence are
currently research questions, not architecture" — a corpus-internal same-day refinement of a
just-proposed pipeline by measured evidence, not by further speculation.

**Temporal knowledge — the biggest architectural gap, now quantified.** EKS/AIP: only **2 of 218**
transitions carry any date. PKS has supersession semantics but no timestamp dimension. The
architecture generally can answer "what is recorded now," not "what was authoritative at time T."
Explicitly **not** concluded that bitemporal storage is required — left as UNKNOWN.

**Provenance weaker than assumed** — partially established in each system (EKS: some unresolved refs;
PKS: reconstructed provenance; AIP: non-attestable process identity). Provenance and temporal state
named as the proposed kernel boundary's two weakest-supported elements.

**Final candidate list.** 8 kept as strong kernel candidates — **C-1** authority-as-recorded-reference-
to-a-human-act, **C-2** state-as-fold, **C-3** closed verdict vocabulary as published language, **C-4**
assessment-confers-no-authority, **C-5** forward-only supersession, **C-6** per-kind register-scoped
identity, **C-7** regenerable non-authoritative projection, **C-10** honest UNKNOWN as first-class.
2 explicitly rejected — **C-8** epistemic-class discipline (→ PKS/domain concern, not kernel), **C-9**
advisory-vs-blocking enforcement (→ platform capability, not kernel).
