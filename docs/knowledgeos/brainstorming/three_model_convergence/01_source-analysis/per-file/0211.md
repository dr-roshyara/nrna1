## Tier 2
**TRIGGERED (critical).** Direct continuation of 0210: Zero Lens + Chinese philosophical lens (Dao/
Yin-Yang/Confucianism/li). Zero Lens forces apart four objects that must never collapse: World event /
Observation / Interpretation / Knowledge claim. The Chinese lens adds **Situational Provenance**
(conditions/environment/relation/phase/actors/change/absence/exclusions), a **Yin-Yang polarity**
requirement (actively seek contradicting, not just supporting, observations), a Confucian **observer-role**
dimension (WHO as role/authority/purpose, explicitly not "trust the person"), and **li** as appropriate-
procedure. Introduces `EvidenceTrajectory` (change as evidence-bearing structure — a true snapshot can
mislead without trajectory), a six-way absence taxonomy (Observed/Not-observed/Expected-but-absent/
Unexpectedly-present/Unknown/Not-measurable, with "not observed" ≠ "does not exist"), and a "conceptual
hardening" warning against Observation→pattern→category→reality collapse. Core principle: **evidence is
not a thing stored — it is a status an observation may acquire** through a defensible relationship between
phenomenon, acquisition, situation, hypothesis and explanation. Reframes AI as an "Epistemic Inquiry
Assistant." Proposes a five-lens Evidence Acquisition Discovery Round as next step. Noted (not logged as
equivalence) as potentially relevant to the registry's still-OPEN/UNRECONCILED Context dimension.
