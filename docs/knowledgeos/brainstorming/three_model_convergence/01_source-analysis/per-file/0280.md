reframes the long-standing topic-boundary question as a boundary of abstraction/purpose/scope/observability

"knowledge can be infinite" intuition into "unbounded in extension, but every usable representation is

knowledge progression (data→meaningful→truthful→relevant→**accounted**→knowledge — truthful+relevant
information can still be epistemically lucky without an account), and formalizes the Question→Answer→
Account cycle with a Why-question taxonomy (historical/teleological/functional). Explicitly warns against
an all-containing Kernel via Floridi's non-reductionist "UTI challenge" stance. Produces an explicitly
versioned, explicitly provisional **"Knowledge Space Model v0.2"** — a seventh candidate in the corpus's
Kernel-structure-synthesis family — held pending testing against Williamson next, and closes with Floridi's
own suggestion that Knowledge Space may be a *family* of interrelated spaces (data/semantic/epistemic/
state/modal/inference) related via LoA as a coordinate system.
