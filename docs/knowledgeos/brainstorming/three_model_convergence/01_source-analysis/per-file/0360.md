**Layer 3 (lines 1898-2665, the genuinely new content)** is a fifteen-point analysis reviewing and
refining the extraction. Sharpens 0359's non-collapse principle from two levels to an explicit **three
levels**: `𝒦 ≠ K_t^A ≠ K̂_t^A` (possible states ≠ actual latent state ≠ **estimated** state), noting
`P(K_t^A|O_≤t)` is "an epistemic state of the *assessor* about the learner's state, not necessarily the
learner's state itself." Issues an explicit terminology caution — do NOT use "projection" for the
estimated-state concept until its specific mathematical sense (Hilbert-space projection / conditional
expectation / geometric projection) is disambiguated — directly extending the corpus's long-tracked
measure-theory Projection-vocabulary-collision finding (0324/0327/0333/0345/0351). Confirms surmise
systems should NOT yet be called "regimes" (no substrate/regime separation in KST itself). Calls the
entailment-is-not-logical-entailment finding "exactly the sort of terminology collision our DDD/
Ubiquitous-Language work needs to catch," and generalizes the Galois multiple-representations result into
an explicit open hypothesis requiring independent research beyond KST. Proposes a still-hypothesis research
model, `Historical/Empirical Substrate →[Regime]→ Epistemic State →[Evaluation]→ Knowledge Attribution`,
and recommends the next reading be **epistemic logic / dynamic epistemic logic**, not further KST, framed
explicitly as an attack on the substrate/regime/epistemic-state hypothesis rather than confirmation-seeking.

**Closes with the file's most consequential architectural recommendation**: **BAN "Belief" from the Kernel
vocabulary** at this stage — the word conflates at least eight distinct referents (psychological state,
propositional acceptance, confidence, expectation, disposition-to-act, probability assignment,
institutional position, internal model), illustrated by a worked five-way-ambiguous "the system believes
election E is valid" example. Proposes a four-stage alternative — **Observation → Information →
Representation → Epistemic Evaluation** — with a regime then deciding whether the resulting representation
counts as belief/knowledge/justified-belief/competence/expectation/confidence, plus a vague-term-to-
precise-term vocabulary table explicitly invoking the DDD principle "do not create a domain concept merely
because natural language uses a convenient word." Closes by proposing the Kernel might instead preserve
what-was-observed + what-was-accessible + what-was-asserted + what-was-derived + why + when + by-whom +
from-what, letting a regime assign the epistemic label — explicitly flagged throughout as **a research
hypothesis, not an architecture decision**.
