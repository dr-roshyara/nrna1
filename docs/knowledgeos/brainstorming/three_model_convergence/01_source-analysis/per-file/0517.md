**TRIGGERED — LANDMARK SELF-CORRECTION: "don't map chakra=knowledge layer."** Rejects an earlier direct
chakra-to-KnowledgeOS-layer mapping as too simplistic, joining the corpus's established discipline against
literal Sanskrit-architecture imports (0405, 0414, 0418, 0424). Corrected thesis: "the consciousness model
is not one architecture. It is a multi-lens model of a living system." First correction: the source presents
several genuinely DIFFERENT conceptual systems (tattvas, koshas, chakras, Yoga, epistemology) the source
connects but does NOT treat as interchangeable.

**Delivers a systematic ten-lens re-analysis**: Ontology (Reality is not knowledge about reality — "one of
the most important architectural principles"); Epistemology (Sankhya's three pramanas give a richer
pipeline Reality→{Observation,Inference,Testimony}→Interpretation→Claim→Evaluation→Knowledge State);
Cognitive architecture (Manas/Buddhi/Ahamkara reframed as three FUNCTIONS — observation/attention,
reasoning/assessment, standpoint/ownership/bias — "every knowledge system has a standpoint problem"); DDD
(seven candidate bounded contexts derived from consciousness faculties, explicitly not claimed by the
source itself); Cybernetics (KnowledgeOS as a feedback-loop organism, not a static repository); Governance/
sovereignty (Purusha≠Prakriti conceptually, not literally, grounds **Knowledge content≠Authority over
knowledge** — "exactly the sovereignty principle we have been protecting elsewhere"); Evidence/assurance
(perception-failure taxonomy reframed as an observation-reliability problem, reinforcing orthogonality);
Temporal/lineage (samskaras reinterpreted as `Interpretation_t=f(Evidence_t,Knowledge_{t-1})` — "Knowledge
is path-dependent"); Architecture (Structure/Process/Development explicitly disentangled); AI-agent
cognition (a required Meta-Cognition layer — "how do I know this? what assumptions? am I authorized to
act?" — connecting to verification/review/governance/deterministic-assurance mechanisms).

**Delivers a twelve-row multi-lens reference table** mapping each lens's consciousness-question to its
KnowledgeOS-question — "this is the model I would keep." **Demotes the chakra mapping to a diagnostic
lens**: NOT "KnowledgeOS has seven chakras," but a "diagnostic vocabulary for systemic maturity and
blockage" — a seven-item "Systemic Knowledge Health" table (Ground/Identity/Agency/Relationship/Expression/
Insight/Integration, each with a corresponding blockage failure mode) — "useful without pretending the
chakra system is an engineering taxonomy." Reframes the koshas as a maturity/depth lens
(Concrete→Operational→Interpretive→Explanatory→Integrated) and Yoga as the transformation lens
(Discipline→Stability→ControlOfInput→Focus→SustainedReasoning→Integration) — neither a component
architecture.

**Delivers the final proposed architecture**: Ontology/Epistemology/Governance feeding a shared Knowledge
State, then Cognition/Temporality/Cybernetics feeding Development (with Koshas/Chakras/Yoga as three
sub-lenses within Development), culminating in META-KNOWLEDGE — "the real prize." **Delivers the file's
deepest conclusion**: reframes KnowledgeOS as a **reflexive knowledge system** —
`K+provenance+uncertainty+authority+perspective+history+assumptions+limitations+transformations`,
additionally capable of reasoning about its own epistemic state — explicitly not to make KnowledgeOS
"mystical" but because ancient consciousness models ask a question "conventional information architecture
often ignores: what happens when the system that knows becomes capable of examining how it knows?"

**Bridge candidate**: the reflexive-knowledge-system formulation should be compared against 0410's
Object-Knowledge/Meta-Knowledge M(K) framework and 0492's Epistemic Lineage.
