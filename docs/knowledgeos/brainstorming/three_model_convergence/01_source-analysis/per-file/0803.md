**§178.19-178.22 delivers "facts and norms have different epistemic origins"** and **three distinct
meanings of "valid"** (epistemic/governance/operational — "not the same predicate"), warning against a
generic `isValid=true`.

**`Knowledge→ApplicablePolicies→Constraints→Alternatives→Evaluation→Decision→Authorization`**.

**"a legitimate decision must be explainable against its applicable governance context."** Delivers
`DecisionProvenance≠KnowledgeProvenance` and the complete eight-stage provenance chain, boxed:
**`Observation→Evidence→Claim→Determination→Decision→Authorization→Execution→Outcome`**.

**§178.31-178.33 delivers the file's central AI-safety invariant**, boxed: **"no semantic transformation
may occur implicitly across bounded-context boundaries."** **Explicitly names the precise AI-agent failure
mode**: "it might reason: evidence suggests the change is safe → therefore execute it. That is precisely
what the architecture must prevent" — the arc's most concrete, actionable AI-safety principle to date.
Re-delivers **`AIRecommendation≠GovernanceDecision`** — both can be simultaneously valid artifacts even
when they differ.

`Reject`), crystallized: not `Knowledge⇒Decision`, but `Knowledge→DecisionBasis`,
`Decision=GovernanceFunction(DecisionBasis,Norms,Constraints,Authority)`.

Constraints,Risk,Authority,TemporalContext)`** with `Determination=E(Evidence,Method,Context,
