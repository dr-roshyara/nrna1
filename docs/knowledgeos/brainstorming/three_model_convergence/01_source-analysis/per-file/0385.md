## Tier 2
**NOT TRIGGERED.** A concise, direct continuation of 0383/0384's Ideal-State research, clarifying "every
information" as **all possible information** about the thing, not merely some — `I_t` = the totality of
all possible information about the object/domain at time `t`, possibly infinite. Sharpens 0361's original
Knowledge-Space hypothesis into `𝒦` = the space of all possible information/knowledge elements, with
`I_t⊆𝒦`. Introduces **observer-indexed notation** into this lineage for the first time — `K_t^A⊆I_t` and
`O_t^A` — connecting the single-observer 0383/0384 formulation back to the corpus's participant-relative
epistemic-logic tradition (Fagin). Reaffirms `P(x∈I_t|O_≤t^A)` as observer-relative extraction uncertainty,
reconfirming Knowledge≠Probability while flagging that whether probability is *always* the right mechanism
remains untested.

Closes by naming **"the next critical question"**: a three-way disambiguation of `I_t` itself — (1) all
logically possible information; (2) all information actually **true** about the thing at `t`; (3) all
information that could **in principle be known** — explicitly not equivalent, and determinative of what the
infinite Knowledge Space and KnowledgeOS's extraction target actually mean.
