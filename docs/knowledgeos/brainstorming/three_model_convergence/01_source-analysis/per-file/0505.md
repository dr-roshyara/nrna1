**TRIGGERED — LANDMARK: Ātma split into two distinct concepts.** Resolves a latent ambiguity carried across
0503-0504, where "Ātma=the Knower" and "Ātma=the Kernel identity" had implicitly been treated as the same
referent. Explicitly splits Ātma into two concepts that must never be confused.

**Human/Knower Ātma (𝒜_H)**: the human Knower (Arjuna) — has intent, asks questions, observes, experiences
uncertainty, evaluates meaning, decides, acts; K^H_0→K^H_1→... while the Knower remains the persistent
subject. **KnowledgeOS Ātma (𝒜_K)**: explicitly NOT another human Knower — the persistent identity/principle
of the KnowledgeOS epistemic system/kernel, operating via observation representation, evidence/provenance,
dimension discovery, epistemic evaluation, Zero, coherence, state transition, investigation support.
Memorable summary: "Human Ātma asks: What do I want/need to know? Knowledge Ātma operates: Given what is
known, what can be established, what is missing, and what should be investigated?"

**Delivers the central epistemic-discipline safeguard**: explicitly declines to claim "KnowledgeOS Ātma is
literally a second Ātman" — "that would move from architectural analogy into a metaphysical claim." Both
defined purely as formal abstractions; the shared word "Ātma" expresses only the analogy of
persistence/invariant identity, never metaphysical identity between the human subject and the computational
system — extending the corpus's architectural-inference-vs-metaphysical-claim discipline (0494, 0500, 0501,
0504) one step further, to the relationship between the corpus's own two abstractions. Models the
Arjuna-Sārathi interaction as a pipeline: `𝒜_H--Intent-->𝒜_K--Investigation-->K_t--Zero-->Δ_t--Sārathi-->
next inquiry`, reworked through the "show me whom I have to fight" example.

**Delivers the central boundary, a four-way non-collapse chain**: **`Knower Ātma ≠ Knowledge Ātma ≠
Knowledge State ≠ Knowledge Space`**, formalized as `𝒜_H≠𝒜_K≠K_t⊆𝒦`. Frames the architecture as Human Knower
↔ KnowledgeOS Ātma ↔ Knowledge Space, with Zero/Lord/Sārathi operating between the current state and the
expanding horizon — Human Ātma as the epistemic subject; Knowledge Ātma as its epistemic computational
counterpart/interface. Closes proposing "a significant conceptual milestone" as the next foundational
question: "What is the exact relationship between the Human Knower Ātma and the KnowledgeOS Ātma?" —
preferred over continuing with "another isolated mathematical object."

**Bridge candidate**: 𝒜_H should be cross-checked against 0493's separately-defined Actor State, which may
