"the missing bridge we have been searching for" between Observation/Evidence and Fact, refining the chain

yielding a five-stage chain. Applies the corpus's Ten-Lens methodology (0369/0370) specifically to Reason

collection of facts. It is a structured body of determinations whose facts are connected to the reasons
that warranted them."

Probability, Bayesian inference, Dempster-Shafer, Many-valued logic, Intuitionistic logic) that would each
answer differently. Proposes the clearest Kernel-content candidate to date: preserve
`Assessment(p,context,time,basis)` uninterpreted, letting a **regime** interpret it as
`P(p)=0.8`/`T(p)=0.8`/`Support(p)=0.8` — restating "the Kernel preserves the epistemic substrate; regimes
provide mathematical interpretations." Declares "Is Knowledge probabilistic?" **already answered** (no),
replaced by the sharper question of what an epistemic value `v∈[0,1]` means and under what conditions it
should be interpreted which way — proposing the **common structure across regimes** as "one of the
strongest candidates for the eventual KnowledgeOS substrate." Two new MD-017 bridge_candidates recorded
