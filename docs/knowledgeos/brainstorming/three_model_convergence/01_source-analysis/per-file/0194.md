## Tier 2
**TRIGGERED (critical).** *Philosophy of Complex Systems* (Cliff Hooker et al.) extracted into 22 major
principles. Central: **knowledge is condition-structured** — all claims must be specified relative to
their validity conditions. Key new content: dynamical perspectivism/poly-ocularity (multiple perspectives
≠ multiple realities); the ontic/epistemic distinction (SYSTEM ≠ MODEL-OF-SYSTEM); process metaphysics
(change is default, stability requires explanation); boundaries-are-hypotheses (reinterpreting DDD bounded
contexts); emergence ≠ mere complexity; a non-monotonic epistemic ladder (pattern ≠ mechanism); the
"Russell Chicken" example motivating an EvidenceEvaluation object; open-vs-closed problems; problem-
solution co-evolution (SDAL); error-as-information; robustness vs. sensitivity; KISS vs. KIDS;
possibility-space over point-prediction; adaptive resilience substituting for prediction. Produces **ten
new candidate concepts** (ValidityContext, Perspective, EvidenceEvaluation, KnowledgeTransition,
OpenInvestigation, RobustnessProfile, AdaptiveOption, SystemBoundary, Model, ModelPurpose) opening a
dedicated "Condition-Structured Knowledge" research thread — a **third independent convergence** (after
0174/0176 and 0193) on preserving investigation process, not just final claims. Explicitly reaffirms the
architectural freeze, grounded partly in the source's own epistemic humility about its field's immaturity.
