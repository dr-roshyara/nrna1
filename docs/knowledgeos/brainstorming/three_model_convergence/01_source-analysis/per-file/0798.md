**§173.1-173.9 systematically merge-tests all seven adjacent concept pairs**: Observation+Evidence ("can
plausibly form one context"); Evidence+Knowledge ("should remain semantically distinct" —
`Lifecycle(E)≠Lifecycle(K)`); Knowledge+Determination (`PersistentKnowledge≠AssessmentInstance`);
Determination+Decision ("should not be collapsed" — `EpistemicReasoning≠GovernanceChoice`); Decision+

AuthorizedAction`); Execution+Outcome (strong candidate for one operational context despite

**§173.10-173.18 delivers the five formal DDD tests** (Language, Invariants, Lifecycle, Ownership, Change
pressure) applied to five candidate groupings, with the explicit warning that even **"KnowledgeOS itself
risks becoming a semantic dumping ground"** if the platform's own name erases the established
`Observation≠Evidence≠Knowledge≠Decision` distinctions. **Delivers the qualitative DDD scoring matrix**,
explicitly disclaimed as "not a quantitative statistical score... a structured DDD discovery instrument."

**§173.19-173.23 delivers the file's central discovery**: "the strongest boundaries appear not between
individual nouns but between **types of responsibility**." **Crystallizes the three-zone architecture**,
doubly boxed: **`Epistemic→Governance→Operational→Epistemic`** (with feedback) — Epistemic ("what do we
know, and what follows from it?"), Governance ("what shall we do, and who is permitted to cause it?"),
Operational ("what did we do, and what happened?") — refining 0797's two-worlds model into three,
explicitly "a much more meaningful architecture than eight independent services," still a hypothesis.

**§173.25-173.30 delivers the first context map** and **anti-corruption-layer discipline**: contexts must
not share internal models directly (Governance receives a translated `DeterminationSummary`, not the full
Knowledge model), boxed **"bounded contexts should communicate through domain contracts, not through
accidental sharing of internal models,"** delivering **three candidate semantic contracts**:
`DeterminationForDecision`, `AuthorizedAction`, `OutcomeObservation`.

**§173.31-173.35 delivers the forward/backward symmetry** (`ForwardIntent`/`BackwardEvidence`) unified
into the architectural loop `Knowledge→[reasoning]→Decision→[authority]→Action→[observation]→Knowledge'`,
and **"this is where AI naturally fits"**: AI participates at specific points but "does not become the
bounded context. AI is an actor/capability crossing contexts." Boxed **`AI capability≠Architecture`**:
"if replacing GPT/Claude/etc. destroys the domain model, the architecture is too tightly coupled to the
technology" — explicitly validating the corpus's own prior `Agent→Tools→KnowledgeOS` harness design.

**§173.36-173.39 falsifies both extremes**: a single giant `KnowledgeOS` context ("enormous semantic
coupling") and eight microservices-per-noun ("distributed complexity before proving independent
consistency boundaries") — both rejected, landing on **"a small number of semantically coherent bounded
contexts, connected through explicit domain contracts."** Delivers the Step 173 verdict: three strategic
domains, the feedback loop, three candidate semantic contracts, and five reaffirmed conceptual-inequality
invariants.

**Closes opening "Step 174 — Context Map and Domain-Contract Experiment,"** proposing to simulate
information exchange across the three zones via seven boundary questions per transition, with **the
immutability/versioning question** as the central upcoming test: `Determination_v1→Decision_v1` versus a

decision remain anchored to its historical determination? No actual repository implementation occurs
