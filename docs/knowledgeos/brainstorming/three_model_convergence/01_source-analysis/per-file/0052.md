## What Is This Document About?
The headline verdict: external evidence supports the **decision framework** behind the six-role model
more strongly than any specific ownership assignment — role, capability, bounded context, agent, and
service are consistently kept apart across NIST/ISO/DDD sources, and the document explicitly states
"the research does not support changing the adopted six-role operating model."

The document examines four specific capabilities (C-5 Separation Attestation, C-10 Knowledge
Distribution, C-14 Policy Enforcement, C-19 Communication Composition) against external patterns, and
its single most valuable methodological contribution is the **capability vs. bounded context vs.
stewardship** distinction, sourced to the Open Agile Architecture standard and Evans/Vernon/Fowler: a
capability belongs to the problem space (technology- and organization-independent — "an ability the
organization possesses"); a bounded context belongs to the solution space (bounded meaning, ubiquitous
language, explicit invariants, ownership, a reason to evolve independently); **stewardship** is the
intermediate case — a capability with an accountable owner and policy but no independent model,
invariants, or lifecycle state. A precise **ten-criterion promotion test** is given for when a
capability crosses into being a genuine bounded context (own ubiquitous language, stable invariants,
independent lifecycle, independently governed state, distinct decision rights, multiple consumers,
independent change pressure, cross-context relationships, an explicit provenance model, a clear
failure/escalation model).

Crucially, the document then states an **explicit anti-pattern** — role-to-context mapping — using
almost exactly the shape 0050 (read immediately prior, same conversational thread) proposed:
*"Governance Engineer → Governance bounded context; Communication Engineer → Communication bounded
context; Knowledge Engineer → Knowledge bounded context... this may be valid eventually, but it is not
evidence by itself."* This is a genuine external corrective delivered *into* the very track that
produced the pattern being corrected — the role could be a workflow responsibility, a stewardship
assignment, a cross-context concern, an operating persona, or an authority role, and none of these
follow automatically from naming a role.

The closing "Analytical conclusion" states nine `≠` distinctions as the document's summary of what
external research validates: role≠capability, capability≠bounded-context, responsibility≠authority,
publication≠distribution, verification≠self-declaration, policy-definition≠enforcement,
file-path≠evidence-identity, audit-record≠provenance-proof, agent-persona≠domain-boundary.
