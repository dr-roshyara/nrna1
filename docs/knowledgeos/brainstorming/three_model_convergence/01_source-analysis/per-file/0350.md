## Tier 2
**TRIGGERED — landmark governance charter establishing a third, deliberately isolated research track.**
Explicitly "independent of Claude Session 1 and Session 2... do not use their conclusions as premises" —
investigating Knowledge/KnowledgeOS/the Kernel from first principles under the framing "if KnowledgeOS is
intended to become the brain of a computer, what must Knowledge be, what must KnowledgeOS be, and what is
the smallest Kernel that can make such a brain possible?" **A striking corpus-internal structural parallel
to this reconstruction's own governing multi-model independence discipline.** Organizes **sixteen
questions (Q0-Q16) across twelve groups**: computer-brain properties (Q0, 23 candidates); Knowledge's
ontology across thirteen competing accounts (Q1), whether it requires a knower (Q2), and its relation to
Truth (Q3); mathematical representability across twenty candidate structures (Q4); measure theory's
applicability, with eleven sub-questions and the sharp "which aspects of Knowledge are measurable, and
which are not?" (Q5), and a rigorous test of five "knowledge=X" equations against a three-level non-
collapse chain Knowledge→properties→measurements (Q6); Knowledge Space existence across eleven structural
candidates, plus two sharp sub-questions about cross-computer/cross-projection divergence (Q7);
temporality, with an explicit **six-way non-collapse rule** (truth/belief/evidence/context/reliance/
representation changed "must not automatically be treated as the same event") closely paralleling this
reconstruction's own tracked non-collapse disciplines (Q8); identity-through-change, "fundamental to a
Kernel" (Q9); seventeen candidate Knowledge-Brain operations to be derived, not assumed (Q10); KnowledgeOS's
nature, to be *derived* rather than chosen by analogy (Q11); the smallest possible Kernel via a six-way
responsibility separation (Q12) and a per-candidate removal test — "if this is removed from the Kernel,
what fundamental capability... becomes impossible?" — structurally identical to this reconstruction's own
Zero-Lens/Merricks discipline (Q13); comparison against existing computer architecture (Q14); the complete
`Reality→Observation→...→New Observation` epistemic cycle (Q15); and **"the most important question" /
"the critical falsification question"** (Q16): given an already-assembled Database+LLM+VectorDB+
KnowledgeGraph+WorkflowEngine+EventLog stack, what capability is *still missing* that would justify calling
KnowledgeOS a brain — explicitly permitting "nothing" as a valid, reportable answer. Specifies an
eight-category evidence-classification discipline (FACT/MATHEMATICAL RESULT/.../IMPLEMENTATION
CONSEQUENCE, distinct from 0349's parallel E1-E7 taxonomy) and three non-equation guardrails (measurable ≠
fundamental; computable ≠ Knowledge; useful ≠ Kernel). Requires four independent final deliverables
(Knowledge/Knowledge Space/KnowledgeOS/Kernel) plus an implementation question, explicitly permitting
negative/deflationary conclusions ("measure theory is only a regime," "the brain analogy is inadequate")
and forbidding premature Kernel design.
