### Formal definitions
The reuse litmus is stated as a two-branch decision procedure over documents:
```
adoptableUnchanged(d, project') = true   ⟹  d ∈ platform
needsProjectContext(d) ∨ needsEvidence(d) ⟹  d ∈ product
```

Total and exclusive on its face. **But it is not decidable**, for the reason established at 0001 and
evidenced at 0008: portability is not a surface property and cannot be tested by vocabulary
inspection. The corpus therefore has, at seq 9, **a stated total decision procedure whose decision
method is known to be unavailable.** That is the sharpest single statement of the pattern so far.
