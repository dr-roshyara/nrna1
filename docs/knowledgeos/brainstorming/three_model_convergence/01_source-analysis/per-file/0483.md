**TRIGGERED — LANDMARK: Zero/Lord/Sārathi formally bounded to a Guidance layer, never the transition
mechanism itself (~30 combined corrections across two reviews).** Part A proposes Zero→Lord→Sārathi→
Commit→Transition as though mandatory for every operation, with Sārathi's vocabulary including Commit
directly and Zero's Gap/Conflict/Boundary as three co-equal outputs.

Part B (first review) delivers the **central correction**: the chain is NOT mandatory — three counter-
examples (direct evidence update; conflict→Sārathi with no Lord; scheduled staleness with none of the
three) show Zero/Lord/Sārathi form "a guidance cycle that may propose or influence transitions; they do
not constitute the transition mechanism itself." **Removes Commit from Sārathi's vocabulary** — commitment
is authorization/governance, not epistemic — replaced by a_t→[Authorize]→c_t→[Execute]→e_t, introducing
**Authorize(N_t,a_t,Policy_t)→c_t** with the Knower (N) as a first-class parameter for the first time.
Reconceives **Boundary as a superset** over Gap/Unresolvable/Unknown/Ambiguity/InsufficientBasis, not a
third sibling to Gap/Conflict. Reframes **Lord as genuine hypothesis-space expansion**
(Lord:(K,I,Z)→ℋ), not mere dimension suggestion — corrects the Arjuna example so Lord generates candidate
hypotheses, not asserted facts. Gives Sārathi an explicit Policy_t parameter ("a policy function, not
simply an AI function"). Delivers the deepest model yet: Ω(K,a)→O (observation), Γ(K,O)→e
(interpretation), δ(K,e)→K' (transition), with Zero/Lord/Sārathi confined to a separate Guidance layer —
**Guidance≠Observation≠Interpretation≠Transition**. Closes with the invariant **Zero,Lord,Sārathi∉State
Mutation Boundary; only validated domain events may mutate K**, flagging this as foundational for the next
question (who controls the Ideal State) since otherwise Sārathi/Lord could accidentally become the
authority defining what the Knower should know.

Part C (revised definition) states four theorems G1-G4 (guidance cycle; Guidance≠Authorization≠Execution≠
Transition; only validated events mutate K; the three lenses do not change K).

Part D (second review) adds ~15 further corrections: **Execute reformalized as non-deterministic**
(Execute:C→{Success(e),Failure(f),Partial(e,f)}), **Authorize returns a full AuthResult**
(Authorized/Rejected/Deferred/Modified, not merely a Command), **ExecutionResult≠DomainEvent** made
explicit. Introduces an **Evaluation Context E_t=(I_t,P_t,C_t)** to reduce parameter noise. Hardens
**Sārathi(...)→Recommendation, never→Command**. **Qualifies "Knower owns Ideal State"**:
IdealState=KnowerIntent+ApplicableNorms+Constraints+Purpose, never simply KnowerPreference — "exactly why
Q18 is important." Disambiguates **Constraint/Policy/Norm** as three distinct rule-kinds. Adds a
post-transition invariant-preservation requirement. States **Theorem G5 — Guidance is not a prerequisite
for transition**: a valid K_{t+1}=δ(K_t,e_t) can occur with no preceding Zero/Lord/Sārathi cycle,
preventing the lenses from becoming mandatory middleware.

Closes declining to rewrite Q17 from scratch — Guidance≠Authorization≠Execution≠Transition confirmed
"exactly right" — and launches **Question 18: who has the authority to define, modify, approve, constrain,
or reject the Ideal State?**, explicitly connecting epistemology, DDD, governance, and normative authority.
