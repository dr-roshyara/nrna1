# Non-Convergences and Open Questions

The mandatory "what does not converge" section (authorization §12). A successful Phase 3 is not
measured by how much unifies — it is equally important to establish what must remain separate. This
document is organized in four parts: model-specific structures with no counterpart; rejected or
low-confidence correspondence proposals from the matrix; methodological parallels that are explicitly
**not** domain correspondences; and any new cross-model observation, labeled per §14.

---

would need to anticipate the other's formal apparatus. A structure existing in one model and not the
other is exactly what independent reconstruction should be expected to produce.

## 3. Methodological parallels that are explicitly NOT domain correspondences

Two genuine parallels surfaced during adjudication that must be recorded as facts about **how the two
underlying corpora were produced**, never mistaken for facts about **what either model's theory
claims**:

**Production-time self-correction discipline.** Model A's 0808 retracts three earlier Gītā-derived
formulas within its own evidence base (§M, CT-3). Model B's M0045 refutes three of M0043's own
theorems (THM-1/THM-5/THM-11) the same research day it was produced (§D). Both corpora exhibit the
identical behavior — a rigorous internal audit catching and correcting overreach shortly after it was
first written — but this is a shared property of the **research discipline** that produced both
corpora (very likely reflecting a single underlying methodology applied consistently across the whole
`three_model_convergence`/`mathematical_ideas_that_can_be_implemented` programme), not a shared
property of Gītā-philosophy and mathematics/statistics as domains. Recorded here, explicitly excluded
from `02_correspondence-matrix.md`, so it is never later cited as evidence of a domain-level
correspondence.

**Independent construction of graded epistemic-status vocabularies.** Already recorded at
PARTIAL CORRESPONDENCE (matrix Row 10) at the methodological-need level — both corpora judged binary
true/false classification insufficient and built multi-valued schemes. Restated here because it
belongs conceptually with the self-correction-discipline parallel above: both are evidence that the
*reconstruction programme itself* has stable methodological habits, independent of the specific
content domain each model addresses.

## 4. New cross-model observations (labeled per authorization §14)

> **PROPOSED CROSS-MODEL HYPOTHESIS** (not part of either original model; not independently supported
> by either model's own source content; requires further, separately-authorized testing before any
> stronger status is considered):
>
> **Under-constrained attempts to formalize "epistemic state" tend to proliferate non-convergent
> tuple variants, independent of content domain.** Both models independently produced an internally
> unreconciled, comparably-sized (9+ variant) family of competing tuple formalizations for "state"
> (Model A's Knowledge Vector family, §G/UE-2; Model B's K_t tuple family, §G) — each flagged
> unresolved by its own corpus, using different vocabularies and non-overlapping component sets. One
> possible explanation is that this pattern is a general feature of any sufficiently ambitious attempt
> to formalize "epistemic state" without an early-committed minimal representation (echoing, within
> Model B alone, the representation-dependence finding that already governs its own kernel work,
> §P-2) — i.e., the same phenomenon Model B proved for kernels (multiple non-equivalent minimal
> structures depending on representation choice) may also hold for state-tuples, and may hold
> independent of whether the underlying domain is philosophical or mathematical. **This is a
> hypothesis about the reconstruction process, offered for future testing — it is not established by
> either model's evidence, does not modify either model's own artifacts, and must not be cited as a
> finding of Model A or Model B individually.**

No other candidate abstraction, framework, or possible universal kernel was found to have sufficient
evidentiary support (from either model, or from the correspondence matrix) to warrant even a
hypothesis-level label. In particular: **no evidence in this adjudication pass supports a "universal
kernel" spanning Model A and Model B** — `02_correspondence-matrix.md`'s Row 1 (Kernel) settled at
UNRESOLVED specifically because no such structure was demonstrated, and this document does not
smuggle one back in under a different name.

## Summary

**What does not converge, in one list**: eight model-specific structures with no counterpart on the
other side; six correspondence proposals that did not clear a weak evidentiary bar; two genuine
parallels correctly attributed to shared research methodology rather than shared theory; and one
labeled hypothesis about the reconstruction process, offered for future testing, not asserted as a
finding of either model. **This is the expected, correct shape of an honest adjudication between two
independently-reconstructed, differently-sourced models** — not a shortfall against some hoped-for
unification.
