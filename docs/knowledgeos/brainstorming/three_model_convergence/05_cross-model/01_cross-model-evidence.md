# Cross-Model Evidence — organized by the sixteen investigation targets

This document does **not** adjudicate. It organizes what each frozen model actually contains, target
by target, as the raw material `02_correspondence-matrix.md` adjudicates from. Every pointer below is
into the already-governed Model-A/Model-B registers (never re-derived, never re-interpreted). Where a
model contains **no defensible counterpart** for a target, that is recorded explicitly, per the
user's own mid-execution instruction not to let the candidate list become a hidden completeness
assumption.

---

## 1. Kernel

**Model A** carries kernel-related material across four distinct, non-collapsed sub-threads:
§F — a four-way `unresolved_equivalence` family of independently-proposed Kernel schemas (0219's
Proposition/Position/Claim vs. 0216's KnowledgeClaim/Evidence/Determination/Authority vs. "K-1"'s
KnowledgeAggregate+ConflictRecord+VerificationPort vs. Kernel Candidate v0.2/v0.3's eight-field
structure); §I–§L — the Sañjaya/Arjuna architecture, Krishna≠Sārathi, Decision Readiness, and
Second-Order Observation, all self-flagged `alternative_minimal_kernel: true` in their own per-file
records (ten files total, per CT-4); §N — the KR-SIM companion series' six-cycle "zero kernel
candidate" finding, scope-corrected at the Phase-1 audit to apply only to that series' own narrow
mathematical-primitive test, not to the ten architecturally-flagged proposals.

**Model B** carries: §B/§P — the executed kernel-reduction experiment (C0, 13 operators →
falsification of "13 is minimal" → four distinct cardinality-8 minimal kernels, representation-
dependent); §L — the four-way Knowledge-Space/Epistemic-State/Kernel/History conflation diagnosis
(M0085), explicitly disciplining against literal identification.

**Both models independently produce an internally *unresolved, multi-candidate* kernel picture** —
neither converges on one schema even within its own evidence. This shared *shape of unresolvedness*
is itself notable and is treated as its own finding in the matrix (not assumed to imply the two
unresolved pictures are the same picture).

## 2. Minimality

**Model A** has no formal minimality construct. The closest analogue is the P5 kernel-admission test
(§E) — three executed instances, each a binary "Add to Kernel? No" decision procedure, never
generalized into a minimality proof or a cardinality claim.

**Model B** has a rigorously tested, quantified minimality result: representation-dependent kernel
minimality (§B/§P-2), four distinct 8-operator minimal kernels under different admissible semantic
algebras.

**Finding: Model A has no demonstrated counterpart to Model B's minimality construct.** P5's binary
admission test and Model B's ablation-based minimality proof are different *kinds* of procedure (one
asks "is X in or out," the other asks "is this set irreducible").

## 3. Representation

**Model A**'s closest material is the 26-lens system (§D) — "lenses are instruments for discovering
and challenging architecture, not components of the Kernel." This is a governance/methodology
discipline about how examination tools relate to the object examined, not a formal representation-
theoretic construct with a projection map or an equivalence relation.

**Model B** has a fully formal apparatus: §F's induced equivalence `x₁ ∼_π x₂ ⟺ π(x₁)=π(x₂)`, the
Representation Adequacy Principle, and §I's tested-and-rejected Hilbert-space representation.

**Finding: largely a Model-B-only formal apparatus.** A weak functional analogy exists at the
meta-level — both models independently discipline themselves against mistaking an examination
instrument/projection for the object itself — but this is a shared *methodological stance*, not a
shared formal object, and is recorded as such in the matrix (expected ceiling: FUNCTIONAL ANALOGY).

## 4. Structure

Both models use "structure" diffusely (Model A: "architecture," of the Sañjaya/Arjuna kind; Model B:
formally, as equivalence classes under a projection). **This target is not given its own matrix row**
— its content is fully absorbed by the Kernel (target 1) and Representation (target 3) rows, and
opening a third, more diffuse row would duplicate evidence already adjudicated there rather than add
anything. Recorded here as a deliberate scoping decision, not a silently dropped target.

## 5. State

**Model A** §G: the Knowledge Vector/Knowledge State family — at least nine independently-motivated,
non-identical tuple formalizations (e.g. `K=(R,M,I,P,E,B)`, `KS=(P,E,M,T,U,D,R,Z,τ)`), explicitly
flagged `unresolved_equivalence` by the corpus's own records (UE-2); §I: the Sañjaya Layer (L_S,
"state knowledge") as one architectural component of the Sañjaya/Arjuna system.

**Model B** §A: K_t/Δ_t (knowledge state / knowledge gap), with Δ_t eventually frozen to a single
canonical definition; §G: a *separate*, likewise nine-plus-variant K_t tuple family, explicitly
flagged `unresolved_equivalence` by that corpus's own records — including an outlier 11-component
structure with twenty unreconciled "core invariants" (M0125/M0126).

**Finding: both models independently produce an unreconciled, proliferating tuple family for
"state," of comparable cardinality (9+ variants each), each flagged unresolved by its own corpus.**
This is the single strongest surface-level structural parallel found across the two evidence bases —
and precisely the kind the non-negotiable principle warns against over-reading. Carried into the
matrix for full six-level adjudication.

## 6. Transition

**Model A** has no formal transition function. The closest material is §M's capstone principle ("the
Gītā lens gives invariants and questions about transitions, not components") — a meta-level emphasis
on process over structure, not itself a defined transition object — and §L's Second-Order Observation
(`O^(2)`), which operates *on* a knower's state rather than *transitioning* it.

**Model B** has a formal transition apparatus: §K's Epistemic Closure Event (`K_t → K_{t+1}`,
confirmed executed, M0080) and §D's THM-1 ("at least one admissible transition changes state").

**Finding: Model A has no demonstrated formal counterpart.** A weaker, meta-level parallel exists —
both models independently privilege "what changes and why" over "what components exist" — recorded
as a shared methodological emphasis, not a shared formal transition object.

## 7. Operator

**Model A**'s closest material is the nine Sārathi Role Functions (§I/§J), applied to a DDD-style
decision-support role (Krishna as "lens/reasoning mechanism operating on the Arjuna layer") — never
formally enumerated as a minimal or ablation-tested set.

**Model B** §B: thirteen explicitly named kernel operators (Observe, Interpret, Represent, Relate,
Discriminate, Hypothesize, Infer, DetectGap, Challenge, Validate, Revise, Determine, Select), each
individually ablation-tested; a fourteenth, `Qualify`, discovered missing and added (§C).

**Finding: a candidate row exists, but at a low evidentiary ceiling** — Model A's role-functions are
domain-specific and DDD-typed (governance-oriented), Model B's operators are kernel-primitives
subjected to a formal irreducibility test; the two are different *kinds* of object playing loosely
similar organizing roles.

## 7a. Determine / Decision (a specific narrow sub-case, given its own matrix row)

**Model A** §K: Decision Readiness (nine criteria, two explicitly Knower-owned, not
system-computable) extending an earlier Decision Sufficiency concept, with the merge-or-parallel
question left explicitly open (OQ-4).

**Model B** §B: `Determine` and `Select` are two of the C0 kernel's thirteen ablation-tested
operators; `DetectGap` is a third, related operator.

**Finding**: a specific, narrow lexical overlap ("determine"/"decision" as words), worth its own
matrix row precisely because it is narrow enough to actually test, unlike the much larger "Kernel"
question — but the underlying objects differ sharply in formal status (a nine-criterion,
partly-human-owned readiness assessment vs. a single ablation-tested kernel primitive).

## 8. Invariant

**Model A** §J: a governance invariant (`KnowledgeOS =/=> Decision`, `KnowledgeOS =/=> Action`) — a
normative rule about system authority, not a mathematical structure preserved under a map; §H: the
EKI (Epistemic Knowledge Invariant) numbering catalog, itself carrying a documented ID collision
(CT-2).

**Model B** §F: `Custody(I) = {c : c responsible for preserving I}` — invariant custody as a formal
component of the Representation Adequacy Principle, about what must survive a projection.

**Finding**: both models use "invariant," but for materially different senses — Model A's is a
normative/governance rule (what the system must never do), Model B's is a structure-preservation
property (what a map must not destroy). Carried into the matrix specifically to record this
difference, not to assert a correspondence.

## 9. Equivalence

**Model A** uses `unresolved_equivalence` as MD-017's own default relationship type throughout its
register and contradictions document (§F/§G above; UE-1 through UE-5) — a **procedural convention of
the reconstruction protocol itself**, applied identically across every concept pairing this
reconstruction has ever logged.

**Model B** uses the identical MD-017 convention procedurally (§G's K_t family, for example) **and**
additionally has a formal, tested mathematical equivalence relation internal to its own theory: §F's
`∼_π` (induced equivalence, a genuine relation with defined properties) and §H's `∼_Λ` (tested and
proven **not transitive**, FR-001).

**Finding — an important trap the non-negotiable principle exists to catch**: both models' registers
are saturated with the word/relation "equivalence," but almost all of that saturation is the *shared
governing protocol* (both reconstructions were built under the same `protocol.md`/MD-017 discipline),
not a shared *domain* object. Only Model B's `∼_π`/`∼_Λ` are domain-internal formal relations; Model A
has no domain-internal formal equivalence relation of its own (its uses of `unresolved_equivalence`
are always the reconstruction's own annotation on Model-A material, never a construct Model A's
*source content* itself defines). Recorded explicitly so the matrix does not mistake shared
methodology for shared theory.

## 10. Reduction

**Model A** has no named "reduction" formalism anywhere in its evidence base.

**Model B** §B: the kernel-reduction experiment itself (13 operators tested down toward a minimal
set). Note also (recorded for completeness, not as Model-B evidence): the broader math-lane
"KR-REP-REDUCTION" / representation-reduction research programme referenced in this project's own
session history sits in the **M0133–M0282 range, confirmed by direct query of the per-file records to
carry `model.primary: KR-SIM`, not `b`, for every file in that range** — i.e. it is boundary material
under Phase 2's own evidence-population rule, not part of Model B's 151-record independent evidence
base, and is therefore not treated as Model-B evidence here.

**Finding: Model A has no demonstrated counterpart.**

## 11. Closure

**Model A** has no "closure" formalism. §M's capstone language ("invariants and questions... not
components") is not about closure in any technical sense.

**Model B** has *multiple, internally not-yet-disambiguated* senses of "closure": §E's Zero Closure
(an explicitly still-open evaluation predicate, distinct from Zero Lens); §K's Epistemic Closure
Event (a state transition); and §F's own record that M0100 catalogued **seven non-equivalent
candidate meanings of "closure"** without selecting one.

**Finding: Model A has no demonstrated counterpart, and Model B's own internal "closure" vocabulary
is not yet disambiguated even within its own evidence base** — any future cross-model claim about
"closure" would first need Model B's own governance to pick a sense, which is out of this phase's
scope and not attempted here.

## 12. History / Audit

**Model A** has no formally named "History" or "Audit" construct. 0808's retraction of three earlier
formulas (§M, CT-3) functions as a self-correction *event* but is never generalized into a named
History/Audit formalism.

**Model B** §K: OQ-3/P-6 explicitly asks whether ClosureEvent's irreversibility is a **kernel**
property or merely a property of **History/Audit** — and this question is itself recorded
**UNRESOLVED within Model B's own evidence base** (no execution record found for the decisive test
M0080 names).

**Finding: Model A has no demonstrated counterpart, and Model B's own construct has not risen above
UNRESOLVED internally** — this target cannot exceed UNRESOLVED in the matrix even in principle at
this time, independent of any cross-model question.

## 13. Composition

**Model A** has no composition-rule research of any kind.

**Model B** §N: the composition-rule research line (KR-COMP-SEP-2026-09) — three candidates excluded,
two survive testing without being shown necessary or sufficient.

**Finding: Model A has no demonstrated counterpart.**

## 14. Event

**Model A** has no formally named "Event" construct, though several moments function as events in an
informal sense (0808's retraction; Second-Order Observation's proposed reflexive check).

**Model B** §K: the Epistemic Closure Event is a formally named, tested transition-event
(`Orgasm_t: K_t → K_{t+1}`).

**Finding: Model A has no demonstrated formal counterpart.** A separate, genuinely interesting
observation belongs here but is **methodological, not domain-theoretic**, and is carried to
`04_non-convergences-and-open-questions.md` rather than the matrix: both corpora exhibit the *same
kind of production-time self-correction event* — Model A's 0808 retracts three earlier formulas
within its own evidence base; Model B's M0045 refutes three of M0043's own theorems the same research
day (§D). This is a parallel in **how both underlying corpora were produced and self-audited**, not a
parallel in either model's theory content, and must not be reported as if it were.

## 15. Observation

**Model A** §L: Second-Order Observation (`O^(2)`, `Observe(KnowerState)`) — a reflexive, higher-order
capability (observing the Knower's *own* epistemic state), self-labeled "the most significant
discovery," HYPOTHETICAL maturity (single-file proposal, never tested within this evidence base).

**Model B** §B: `Observe` is one of the thirteen first-order, ablation-tested kernel operators (C0).

**Finding**: both use "observation," but at different *orders* — Model A's is reflexive/second-order
and untested; Model B's is a primitive, first-order, tested operator. A candidate row, low
evidentiary ceiling expected.

## 16. Knowledge / Epistemic Status

**Model A** §A: the catuṣkoṭi four-valued classification (`EpistemicState(P) ∈ {T,F,B,N}`); §B: the
binary meta-principle classification (kernel-admission verdicts).

**Model B** §O: an eleven-value epistemic-status vocabulary (`ESTABLISHED/DERIVED/BOUNDED/
HYPOTHESIS/…`); §C: the Epistemic Standards/Assessment layer (`A_t=⟨attitude,strength,warrant,
status⟩`).

**Finding**: both models independently needed, and independently built, a graded epistemic-status
vocabulary — this is evidence of a **shared methodological need**, not a shared domain object (the
specific value sets do not overlap: four-valued logical classification vs. an eleven-value
governance-status taxonomy). Carried into the matrix at the methodological-parallel ceiling.
