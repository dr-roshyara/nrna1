## Cluster 1 — Classical Indian epistemology/logic mining (seq 0092–0132, 2026-08-22)

**21 files.** A dense, disciplined external-source-engagement programme: Nyāya/Vaiśeṣika pramāṇa
(valid-knowledge-source) theory, Navya-Nyāya's precision relational calculus, Patañjali's Yoga
Sūtra, Kashmir Śaivism (Prakāśa-Vimarśa), Śākta Tantra, Abhāva (structured absence) ontology, and
Sanskrit/Pāṇinian grammar (Kāraka case theory, "Semantic Normal Form," the "Artha Language"
proposal). **Contribution:** the corpus's first systematic pramāṇa taxonomy, the Hetvābhāsa fallacy
typology, a four-type Abhāva (absence) refinement of Unknown-preservation, and — critically — the
first appearance of a recurring, disciplined "meta-principle" status category (Zero at 0110, Mokṣa
at 0114, Śiva-Śakti at 0115, Gaṇeśa-derived wisdom at 0117) distinguishing non-kernel philosophical
principles from constitutional candidates. Also the origin of a still-unreconciled proliferation of
candidate-ID naming schemes (H-KOS-*, INV-KOS-*, ADR-KOS-EPI-*, KOS-*, KOS-ATOM-*) and the
long-running, never-resolved **Context dispute** (Context treated as a flat dimension / an
Avacchedaka delimiter / a semantic-boundary concept / "context-bounded meaning" — four
non-identical treatments). Three of the corpus's own explicitly-executed "P5" kernel-admission
decisions occur here (0108, 0110, 0127), each with a disciplined *negative* verdict.

## Cluster 2 — The 26-lens system and its Chinese-philosophy extension (seq 0161–0166, 2026-08-23)

**3 files.** 0161 is the single most comprehensive methodological consolidation in the Model-A
evidence base: a canonical, one-by-one definition of 26 named lenses used throughout the whole
research programme, classified into three authority tiers (Observation / Architectural-Adjudication
/ Mechanism-Candidate), with "Convergence of lenses does not make the lens itself architectural
authority" promoted to a named Meta-Lens. 0165/0166 propose ten Chinese philosophical lenses
(Yijing, Li, Yin-Yang, Ziran, Gongfu, Ming, Jing, Shi, He, Wu) as a cross-tradition extension —
**none adopted**, explicitly kept at Observation-layer status only.

## Cluster 4 — Ashtavakra Gītā / Upaniṣadic Lord-Zero lens arc (seq 0407–0421, 2026-08-26)

**5 files.** 0407 states, in its starkest and explicitly *unresolved* form, "the reality or
appearance" (Mithyā) challenge — whether KnowledgeOS's entire modeling enterprise rests on a
category error — self-labeled "a fundamental challenge to the entire KnowledgeOS enterprise" and
left open. 0409/0414/0417 develop the Lord/Omega lens (an infinite knowledge horizon, explicitly
**not** the union of all discoverable dimensions, never exhausted even in an infinite-time limit of

questions *without* the explicit non-collapse/rejection discipline every other file in this cluster
uses — occurring immediately after a prior file had explicitly warned against exactly this failure
mode.

## Cluster 5 — The Sanjaya/Arjuna/Krishna architecture (seq 0427–0451, 2026-08-26)

**16 files** (0428, 0437, 0442, 0444 are duplicates/near-duplicates, excluded from independent
counting). **The single most mature and internally coherent architectural synthesis in the entire
Model-A evidence base** — a disciplined, chapter-by-chapter falsification-testing programme applied
to the authoritative *Bhagavad-gītā As It Is* text (Prabhupāda's edition, with Sanskrit,
transliteration, and purports). Delivers, in order: the empirical finding "more information does
not imply more decision certainty" (0427); the KnowledgeOS-as-Sañjaya-like-epistemic-intermediary
analogy with an explicit non-identity discipline (0429); a full two-layer architecture — Sañjaya
Layer (state knowledge) / Arjuna Layer (understanding/decision knowledge), with Zero and Lord each
split into layer-specific instances (0430); execution and "full validation" of six pre-registered
falsification tests against the actual Chapter 2 text (0435); a DDD Entity/Role distinction, Krishna
(entity) ≠ Sārathi (role), with nine concrete Sārathi Role Functions (0436); the canonical,
self-declared formulation of the Krishna Lens with a "Decision Readiness" concept (nine criteria,
two Knower-owned) (0441); a formal two-Knower-position distinction, Dhṛtarāṣṭra (remote) vs. Arjuna
(actor) (0443); and eight further refinements to the Observation model culminating in **Second-Order
Observation** — KnowledgeOS observing the Knower's own epistemic state, not merely domain reality —
explicitly self-labeled "the most significant discovery" (0451).

## Cluster 6 — Gītā Chapter 4 validation arc (seq 0736–0808, 2026-08-28–29)

**12 files** (0754 is a duplicate, excluded). 0736 is explicitly flagged by its own source text as
**the first direct Bhagavad-gītā content in 735 prior primary files** — every file before it had
`gita_content=false`. Delivers a disciplined cross-model stress test with an explicit framing rule
quoted verbatim: *"The Gītā is an external conceptual lens for testing KnowledgeOS — not a new
'Gītā dimension' of the architecture."* 0752/0753 deliver two genuinely new architectural
principles (Principle IV-A: "do nothing" as a first-class governed outcome; Principle IV-B:
Continuity ≠ Accessible Memory). **0772 is preserved as a documented negative exemplar**: a
DeepSeek-sourced, verse-by-verse "mathematical derivation" converting Gītā passages directly into
boxed formulas with no justification shown, explicitly recorded by the corpus's own governance
apparatus as the exact failure mode its anti-analogy disciplines exist to catch. 0775/0776 recover
from this with a formal, reusable five-step interpretation method (Extract→Generalize→Compare→
Stress-test→Classify) and a five-category outcome taxonomy. 0800/0801 give the corpus's
"state-does-not-know-its-past" observation its first rigorous statistical-identifiability treatment,
with a worked numeric counterexample. **0808 is this cluster's capstone**: an unscheduled
self-critique checkpoint that *explicitly retracts three of the corpus's own earlier Gītā-derived
mathematical formulas* (a knowledge-decay exponential, a divine-intervention threshold function, a
binary epistemic-reward function) as "too literal," and states the governing rule this whole
reconstruction inherits: **"the Gītā lens gives invariants and questions about transitions, not
components"** — "Krishna should NOT become an architectural component."

## Cluster 7 — The KR-SIM Gītā companion series, formal closure (seq 2329–2376, 2026-09-02–04)

**6 files**, the corpus's final closure on Model-A kernel-candidacy. A structurally consistent
series of companion studies, each applying a strict question/lens/corroboration-only discipline
(never entity) to a different chapter of the Gītā against a distinct KR-SIM-lane experiment
(contradiction, elimination, representation-reduction, audit, bridge-confounding, and a final
declared-vs-effective-reads study). **Each of the six independently concludes the Gītā supplies zero
kernel candidates** — an explicit, incrementing running count (fourth, fifth, sixth, seventh
consecutive occurrence) — culminating at seq 2376, **the very last file in the entire main corpus
manifest**, with a formal five-cycle closure: *"The Gītā strand has now run five cycles... in none
of them has it produced a kernel primitive... stop testing it for kernel candidacy — the candidacy
question is answered."* This is the single most repeatedly-corroborated finding in the whole
Model-A evidence base.
