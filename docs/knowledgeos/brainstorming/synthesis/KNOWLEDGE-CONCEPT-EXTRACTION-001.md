## 1 · Executive Summary

**⟦C⟧ The corpus contains no single definition of Knowledge. It contains roughly 90 definitional
sentences and at least ten materially different conceptions.** The most frequent move is *negative*:
`Knowledge is not…` outnumbers `Knowledge is…` in Phase 1.

**⟦C⟧ Phase 1 converged on Knowledge as a RELATIONSHIP**, stated repeatedly and independently:

> *"Knowledge is not a thing. Knowledge is a justified network of relationships between a knower, an
> object, evidence, reasoning, context, and conclusion."* — `20260822-0225`
> *"Knowledge is not a collection of statements. Knowledge is a governed relationship between
> reality, observation, reasoning, context, and the knower."* — `20260822-0302`
> *"Knowledge is not only the object. Knowledge is **the relationship**."* — `20260822-0135`

**⟦C⟧ Phase 2 shifted to a factive, participant-relative, time-dependent relation directed at a
Knowledge Space**, and added two conceptions Phase 1 does not contain: **capacity** and
**measure over a space**.

**⟦I⟧ The single most important correction this extraction produces:** the *relationship* conception
is **Phase 1 original project thinking**, not later book-derived research. Any account that treats
"knowledge as relationship" as an import is wrong. Conversely **Knowledge Space (in its Phase 2
sense), capacity, and measure are not present in Phase 1** and must not be attributed to it.

## 5 · Conceptual Evolution — Phase 1 → Phase 2 (⟦C⟧ + ⟦I⟧)

```
P1  Knowledge is NOT stored content            (≈12 docs, negative epistemology)
        │
P1  Knowledge is a RELATIONSHIP                 0135 → 0225 → 0249 → 0302 → 1057
        │                                       (five independent arrivals, one day)
P1  Knowledge is JUSTIFIED STATE EVOLUTION      1016, 1018
        │
        ├─── [handover into kernel/, 2026-08-23] ───
        │
P2  relation/state DIRECTED AT a Knowledge Space 171008, 171853
P2  CAPACITY                                     155510, 180828
P2  MEASURE over a space                         181038 → attacked 181719
P2  PRIMITIVE / unanalysable                     162824
```

**⟦I⟧ What changed:** Phase 1 asked *what knowledge consists of*; Phase 2 asked *what knowledge is
relative to* — a space, a participant, a time. The relatum moved from an internal list
(reality/observer/evidence/reasoning) to an **external space**.

**⟦I⟧ What did NOT change:** the refusal of stored content. That is the corpus's most stable result,
holding across both phases and agreeing with ⟦L⟧ §17.

**⟦I⟧ Attribution discipline — a correction to my own earlier finding.** I previously reported that
"Knowledge Space" first appears at `kernel/20260825-103542`. **That was kernel-scoped and wrong
corpus-wide.** The phrase appears in Phase 1 at `20260822-0200` — three days earlier. But ⟦C⟧ that
occurrence is a **diagram label** introducing *Cognitive Worlds* to explain *why contradictions can
coexist* (contradiction as context separation, not corruption). **⟦I⟧ It is not the Phase 2 concept:
no unboundedness, no projection, no participant relativity, no time index.** So the earlier
conclusion survives in substance — the Phase 2 Knowledge-Space model is not recoverable from
Phase 1 — but the supporting claim must be restated as *"same term, different concept"*, not
*"term absent"*.

## 6 · Knowledge and Identity

**⟦C⟧** The corpus distinguishes similarity from identity explicitly (`⟨C-1⟩`-adjacent material,
`0113` expression-meaning invariance, 31 identity references). Competing answers:

| Position | Support | Phase |
|---|---|---|
| identity is **assigned, never derived** | ⟦L⟧ INV-KOS-IDENTITY-001; corpus echoes it | both |
| identity is **primitive, unitary, indefinable** | `kernel/…115235`, `120122` (McGinn) | P2 book |
| identity is **transformational and contextual** | `kernel/…094638` (Daoist *ziran*, 170 refs) | P2 book |
| identity is **relational** — knowledge *is* the relationship, so identity is the relation's | `0135`, `0225` | **P1 original** |

**⟦I⟧ Unresolved, and asymmetric:** the only K4-strength challenge (`094638`) is book-derived, while

identity** — P1 treats identity as needed, not as explained.

**Can knowledge exist without identity?** ⟦C⟧ Not addressed anywhere. ⟦L⟧ answers by construction:
remove identity and *"the system is a document store, an embedding database, or a similarity engine"*
(§4).

## 8 · Knowledge and Truth / Belief / Assertion / Reliance

**⟦C⟧ The corpus keeps these apart deliberately and repeatedly:**
*"Knowledge is not possession of truth"* (`1057`) · *"not created by belief"* (`1057`) ·
*"not simply whatever an agent believes"* · *"temporally defeasible without being merely belief"* ·
*"the system records something as knowledge" ≠ "it is true"* (implicit throughout the negative
epistemology document `105921`, the corpus's densest treatment at 17 hits).

**⟦I⟧ But "factive" (K-6) reintroduces truth by the back door.** A factive relation entails truth. A
system with no access to truth cannot verify factivity. **Unreconciled** — and it is the same defect
class I recorded against possible-worlds `π`.

**⟦L⟧** *Meaning ≠ Truth*, *Evidence ≠ Authority*, *Agent ≠ Truth* are all §15 non-collapse rows;
*Truth machine / oracle* is a §17 rejection. **⟦I⟧ Corpus and law agree strongly here** — the
strongest convergence found in this extraction.

## 12 · Zero-Lens Findings

**⟦C⟧ Applied as a test.** Stripping optional attributes, what survives across *both* phases is:
**a knower/participant · something known · a ground · and a record that the two were connected.**
Every phase and nearly every model retains those four; nothing else survives universally.

**⟦C⟧ Absence.** The corpus distinguishes *"nothing exists"* from *"we do not know"* — `0258`:
*"Knowledge is not only about what exists"*; ⟦L⟧ §9: *unknown ≠ absent ≠ false*.

**Is UNKNOWN the absence of Knowledge, or a state of an identified object?**
⟦L⟧ answers unambiguously: ⟨Z-1⟩ — *"`UNKNOWN` · `ABSENT` · `FALSE` · `REJECTED` are states **of an
identified epistemic object**"*, while ⟨C-5⟩ maps a mechanism's inability to determine → `UNKNOWN`.
**⟦C⟧ The corpus does not resolve this**, and `kernel/…125708` §17 pushes the other way (*"UNKNOWN
must not be a single epistemic state"*, eight readings).
**No new state is proposed here.**

## 14 · Important Negative Findings

**⟦C⟧ Explicitly rejected by the corpus:** knowledge-as-thing/stored-object/content/statement-set;
belief as sufficient; possession of truth.
**⟦C⟧ Considered then abandoned:** *Cognitive Worlds* (`0200`) — appears once, never developed.
**⟦C⟧ Appears only in Phase 2:** Knowledge Space (substantive sense) · projection (epistemic) ·

**⟦C⟧ Appears only in book-derived material:** primitive/unanalysable (Williamson) ·
institutional (Searle) · transformational identity (*ziran*) · infinite-belief/finite-representation
(Brachman & Levesque).
**⟦C⟧ Never found in either phase:** whether change creates a new identity · whether two projections
may differ without knowledge differing · whether absence from a projection carries meaning ·
whether history belongs to the object or describes it.

**⟦I⟧ Stated limit, per instruction:** these are keyword-and-reading absences over 250 documents, not
proofs. Each "never found" is a **candidate absence for Phase D confirmation**.

## 15 · Potential Falsification Questions (for use *after* formal adjudication)

1. If Knowledge is a **capacity** (K-7), what does an identity-bearing aggregate hold?
2. If Knowledge is **not one entity** (K-4), what bears `KnowledgeId`?
3. If Knowledge is **primitive** (K-11), is the whole justified-state model an over-specification?
4. If change is **continuous** (K-8), can forward-only governed transitions represent it without loss?
5. If Knowledge is **factive** (K-6), how does a system with no truth access assert it?
6. If identity is **contextual** (`094638`), what happens to identity continuity across contexts?
7. If Knowledge is **institutional** (K-10), is the *participant* an agent or an institution?

**⟦I⟧ Questions 1, 2 and 3 are the strongest** — each attacks the existence of the core object rather
than its attributes.

## 16 · Unresolved Questions & Conclusions — no model chosen

**Unresolved:** the relatum set of K-2 · state vs relation (K-2 vs K-3) · whether capacity and
measure conceptions are compatible with identity at all · factivity without truth access ·
whether Knowledge Space belongs anywhere near the Kernel · every item in §14's "never found".

**Conclusions, stated without selecting a model:**

1. **⟦C⟧ The corpus's most stable result is negative** — Knowledge is not stored content. It holds
   across both phases and agrees with formal law.
2. **⟦C⟧ Phase 1's positive contribution is the *relationship* conception**, reached five times
   independently in a single day. It is original project thinking.
3. **⟦C⟧ Phase 2's distinctive contribution is relativisation** — to a space, a participant, a time.
4. **⟦I⟧ Three Phase 2 conceptions (pipeline, capacity, measure) are incompatible with an
   identity-bearing core object.** They are the real falsification material.
5. **⟦I⟧ One conception (K-11, primitive) would invalidate the corpus's own method.** Unaddressed.
6. **⟦I⟧ Nothing in this extraction establishes that the formal core domain is wrong.** It
   establishes what would have to be true for it to be wrong — which was the assignment.
