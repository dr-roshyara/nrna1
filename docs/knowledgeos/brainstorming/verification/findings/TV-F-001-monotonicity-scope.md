## Claim under examination

## Test performed

## Result — **C-020 is MODIFIED (downgraded)**

1. **No two statements share both object and operation.** step-004 §6 quantifies over the *signed component* `S⁺` under *addition of a single admissible, independent, sign-matched* item (`e ⇒ P`) — and itself adds: *"This does **not** mean that the final conclusion must become more certain."* The non-monotonicity demonstrations concern the posterior scalar `P(H)` under **contradictory** input (025c-2), contract `Readiness` under negative resolution of an Unknown (025a-1), current-state inclusion under supersession (025k — with `History monotonic; CurrentState need not be`), and whole-state entailment under invalidating revision (031). **CONFIRMED as consistent set; the recorded axiom-vs-counterexample reading is REJECTED.**
2. **The disambiguation exists in-corpus, twice** — step-004 §6's closing disclaimer, and closure-04a §8 (`20260827-133711_closure-04a-mathematical-laws-of-evidence-assessment.md`), which explicitly rejects naive monotonicity `E⊆E' ⇒ Support(E)≤Support(E')` as "not universally valid" and adopts **signed monotonicity** with the caution "total epistemic status may become more uncertain". The prior register text "never stated" was **factually wrong**; corrected.
3. **Residual defects (the surviving finding):**
   - **D1 — retraction blind spot:** step-004's axiom quantifies only over additions; whether retracting an admissible supporting item may reduce `S⁺` is asked and answered nowhere (retract/revise/invalidate: zero hits in step-004). UNDER-SPECIFIED.
   - **D2 — unlinked clusters:** the evidence-component algebra (04-series: 003/004/04a/04b/evidence-algebra-01/060) and the knowledge-state algebra (025a/025c/025k/031) never cite each other; `S⁺` appears only in the former. The coherence is real but *uncoordinated* — an integration gap (class G12), not a contradiction.
   - **D3 — over-broad table row:** 025k §25K.48's "Universal monotonicity ❌" is unqualified as to object; §25K.49 scopes it to CurrentKnowledge, but the row read alone over-reaches and no file exempts `S⁺`.
