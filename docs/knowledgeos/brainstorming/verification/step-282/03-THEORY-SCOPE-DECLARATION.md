## The theory CLAIMS to solve
Knowledge state `K=(𝒜,ℛ)` · Assertion `(id,P,e,c,t,Π)` · Proposition `P=(E,D,V)` with scale-typed
Dimensions · Evidence as qualified observation, held by reference · Epistemic status `Σ=(dir,str)`,

## The theory explicitly DOES NOT claim to solve — scope exclusions

| Excluded | Why, with evidence |
|---|---|
| **Probability / statistical inference** | **F21: removing it breaks 0 of 13 mandatory constructs.** No random experiment exists. Every quantitative notion is **ordinal**, and 42.10 actively **forbids** the averaging a measure would license. **A Measurement bounded context may add it; the core does not need it.** |
| **Uncertainty quantification** | follows from the above |
| **Distributed / multi-node consistency** | no theory claim depends on it |
| **Production measurement execution** | the measurement **model** is a parameter; the **executor** is engineering |
| **Governance ratification** | the theory says authority is *recorded, never granted* — ratification is an external human act |
| **Real-system implementation** | `FC ⇏ CC ⇏ EC` |

> **A scope exclusion is not a gap.** It is a declared boundary, and each of the six above was tested
> rather than assumed. **Step 246's `KnowledgeOS = Probability Distribution` falls outside this scope and
> remains an unsupported SOURCE CLAIM.**
