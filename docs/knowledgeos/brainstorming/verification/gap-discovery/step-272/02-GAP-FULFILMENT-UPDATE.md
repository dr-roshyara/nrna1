## 4. One correction this update forces on the second-order package

`second-order/06` §1 retired D-1 on the ground that *"the answer does not vary across the open
choice."* `canonical-construction` sharpens this correctly: `K` is invariant across all **16**
resolutions of D-1 — which **confirms** the retirement for `K`.

But D-1 also governs `Minimality(K|𝒯)` and the congruence proof, and **those are not invariant**.
Step 277 says so: *"Minimality: OPEN."*

**Correction:** D-1 is retired **for the ontology of `K`** and **remains open for `K`'s minimality
claim**. The second-order retirement was correct in scope and overstated in reach.
