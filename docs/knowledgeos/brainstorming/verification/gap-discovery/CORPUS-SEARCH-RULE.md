# Mandatory Corpus Rule — binding on this verification lane

**Issued by the user, 2026-09-06. Supersedes every scoping practice previously used in this lane.**

## The search universe

$$\boxed{\texttt{docs/knowledgeos/brainstorming/}\ \textbf{is the complete corpus.}}$$

Absence from a ratified document, `governance/`, `reviews/`, the latest synthesis, or an expected
filename is **not** evidence that a thing is undefined.

## Before writing any of these words

`undefined` · `missing` · `absent` · `not specified` · `not derived` · `impossible to implement` ·
`unavailable` · `a blocker`

the **entire** `brainstorming/` tree must be searched — **conceptually and mathematically**, not by
one glyph, one lane, one filename or one terminology: exact terms, synonyms, abbreviations,
alternative terminology, mathematical notation, **LaTeX**, definitions, examples, derivations,
experiments, simulations, specifications, decision records, TODOs, rejected alternatives, competing
models.

## Multiplicity is expected and is not a gap

The corpus may hold competing, superseded, rejected, partial, incompatible and different-abstraction
definitions of the same thing, all at once.

$$\textbf{Multiple definitions} \neq \textbf{missing definition}\qquad\textbf{Multiple solutions} \neq \textbf{unresolved absence}$$

They are evidence that a **choice, reconciliation, precedence decision or further derivation** may be
required.

## Never silently choose

With two or more materially different definitions: identify **all** relevant alternatives · locate
their sources · state exactly how they differ · classify them (equivalent · semantically equivalent
but structurally different · complementary · different abstraction levels · superseded ·
contradictory · genuinely competing) · check the corpus for a precedence rule, later decision,
derivation, experiment or explicit selection · **if the corpus does not resolve it, STOP and ask.**

## The three states, distinguished on every finding

| | |
|---|---|
| **A · NOT FOUND** | a **search result**, never evidence of non-existence |
| **B · DEFINED MULTIPLE WAYS** | a **corpus finding**, reported as such |
| **C · CANONICALLY RESOLVED** | the corpus itself shows which has precedence — **only here may one be treated as canonical** |

## Conflict record (mandatory shape)

concept/question · definition A · sources for A · definition B · sources for B · further
alternatives · the exact difference · evidence of equivalence/complementarity/conflict · existing
precedence or decision evidence · what remains undecided · consequence for implementation ·
**decision required from user/Governance**.

**Never resolved by preference.**

## The correct outcome when alternatives compete

$$\boxed{\textbf{DECISION REQUIRED}\qquad\textbf{not}\qquad\text{BLOCKED because undefined}}$$

Options to offer: select A · select B · combine · derive a unifying definition · preserve both at
different abstraction levels · run an experiment · defer · escalate to Governance.

## Revised meaning of "gap"

A genuine gap exists **only after** the whole corpus was searched, alternative terminology and
mathematical representations were searched, proposed solutions were examined, competing approaches
were identified, no existing artifact closes the question, and the issue is genuinely unresolved.

$$\textbf{A failed search is not a theory gap.}\qquad\textbf{A multiplicity is not a theory gap.}$$

A disagreement between definitions is a **decision/reconciliation problem** unless the corpus proves
otherwise.

## Required language

> *"The concept is defined in the brainstorming corpus in multiple ways. I found A, B and C. I cannot
> legitimately select between them without an explicit precedence rule or decision. Here is the
> difference and the consequence of each. Please decide how we should proceed."*

**Not** *"the concept is undefined"* — unless a complete-corpus search establishes it.

## Core principle

**The brainstorming corpus is the search universe. Multiplicity must be discovered before absence is
claimed. When alternatives exist and the corpus does not resolve them, surface the alternatives and
ask — do not silently choose.**
