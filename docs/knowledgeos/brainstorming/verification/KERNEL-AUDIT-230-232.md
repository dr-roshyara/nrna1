# Kernel Audit — Step 230's candidate kernel

## 0. Verdict

> **`𝒦 = (K,C,T,E,A)` is `UNDER-SPECIFIED` and `NOT MINIMAL AS CLAIMED`.**
>
> It is **not closed** (its own definitions require `Policy` and `τ`, neither of which is a component),
> **not independent** (four of five components appear inside the fifth), and **minimality is asserted, never
> demonstrated** — which the corpus, to its credit, half-admits by calling it a *"candidate"*.

**The corpus's own hedge is accurate and is recorded as such:** §230.50 says *"this is still a **candidate
reconstruction**"* and *"The other properties appear **potentially** derived rather than primitive."* The
word "minimal" in §230.31's heading is not supported by anything in the file.

## 2. Is `L` derivable from `T`? (§4 Q2) — and is lineage *necessarily* `History(T)`? (§4 Q3)

**PARTIALLY. The reduction holds for internal lineage and FAILS AT THE BASE CASE.**

The reduction is defensible because `T` is rich. §230.10, verbatim:

```
T = (K_A, C_A, A_t, P_t, E_t, τ) → (K_B, C_B)
```

and §230.13's lineage edges carry `e = (T, A, E, C, τ)`. Since `T` transports `A`, `E`, `C` and `τ`,
`History(T)` does reconstruct §230.12's lineage entries `{(K_i, T_i, A_i, E_i, τ_i, C_i)}`.

**But `History(T)` is empty at `t = 0`, and `K_0`'s evidence still has an external source.**

> **COUNTEREXAMPLE (executed test §4).** At the initial state there are no transformations, so
> `History(T) = ∅`. Yet the evidence constituting `K_0` originated somewhere — a document, a measurement,
> an external system. That origin is **provenance**, it is lineage, and **it is not derivable from any
> transformation.**

**This matters because §230.13 boxes `KnowledgeOS lineage = typed provenance graph`.** A provenance graph
has **nodes and edges**. `History(T)` supplies the **edges**. The **external provenance of the nodes** —
where the initial artifacts came from — is not a transformation and is not recoverable.

**VERDICT on Q2:** `L = History(T)` is **VALID FOR INTERNAL LINEAGE ONLY**.
**VERDICT on Q3:** **NO — lineage is not necessarily `History(T)`.** The corpus conflates *internal
derivation history* with *external provenance*, which its own step-073 and step-022 kept apart.

## 3. Can lineage exist independently of transformation? (§4 Q4)

**YES — see §2 above.** The base case is a witness. **This is the precise reason `L` cannot be dropped
without loss**, and therefore the reduction from the 6-tuple to the 5-tuple **loses external provenance**
unless provenance is folded into `K` (which §230 does not do) or `E` (which §230.15 does not state).

## 4. **THE DECISIVE RESULT — the kernel is not closed** (§4 Q10, Q11)

**EXECUTED TEST:**

```
symbols referenced by the kernel's own definitions:
    ['A', 'C', 'E', 'K', 'Policy', 'T', 'tau']
NOT kernel components:  ['Policy', 'tau']
=> CLOSED?  NO
```

Two independent sources force this:

1. **§230.10's definition of `T`** requires `P_t` (**Policy**) and `τ` (**time**).
2. **§230.32's derivation of Assurance** is `f(E, T, Policy)` — **Policy again**.

**`Policy` and `τ` are not components of `(K,C,T,E,A)`.**

> **Therefore the kernel cannot define its own transformation component.** Either the kernel is
> `(K,C,T,E,A,P,τ)` — seven components — or `T` is not fully specified by the kernel that contains it.
>
> **The corpus never notices this.** §230.32 writes `Policy` into a derivation on the same page that
> §230.31 boxes the five-component kernel.

**VERDICT: `UNDER-SPECIFIED`. This is the single strongest finding of the audit.**

## 5. **The independence failure** (§4 Q12)

**EXECUTED TEST:**

```
T's signature (§230.10) contains: ['A', 'C', 'E', 'K', 'Policy', 'tau']
=> 4 of the other 4 kernel components appear INSIDE T: ['A', 'C', 'E', 'K']
```

**Every other component of the kernel appears inside `T`'s own signature.**

This makes the components **mutually definable**, and the direction of primitiveness undetermined:

- If **`T` is primitive** with the §230.10 signature, then `K`, `C`, `A`, `E` are **projections of `T`'s
  domain** — recoverable from `T`, hence not independent primitives.
- If **`K`, `C`, `A`, `E` are primitive**, then `T` is a **derived** construction over them, hence not a
  primitive.

**Both readings cannot hold. The corpus asserts both**, listing all five as kernel components while
defining one in terms of the other four.

**No independence argument appears anywhere in Step 230.**

## 6. Removal test (§4 Q12) — EXECUTED

Derived properties taken verbatim from §230.32.

```
remove K: breaks [SemanticIntegrity, EpistemicIntegrity]      <- recoverable as a projection of T
remove C: breaks [SemanticIntegrity, BoundaryPreservation]    <- recoverable as a projection of T
remove T: breaks [SemanticIntegrity, BoundaryPreservation,
                  GovernedAction, TemporalLineage,
                  Assurance, Lineage]                          <- NOT recoverable
remove E: breaks [EpistemicIntegrity, Assurance]              <- recoverable as a projection of T
remove A: breaks [GovernedAction]                             <- recoverable as a projection of T
```

**Reading this correctly:**

- **`T` is the only component whose removal is not repairable.** It breaks six of seven derived properties
  and nothing recovers it.
- **`K`, `C`, `E`, `A` each break something — but each is recoverable from `T`'s signature.** Under the
  "`T` is primitive" reading, **the minimal kernel may be `(T)` alone**, with the other four as projections.
- **`A`'s removal breaks exactly one property** (`GovernedAction`), making it the weakest claim to
  primitiveness on the corpus's own derivation table.

**VERDICT on Q11/Q12: minimality is NOT DEMONSTRATED, and the executed test gives positive reason to doubt
it.** `(K,C,T,E,A)` is very likely **redundant**, not minimal.

**VERDICT on Q13 (is there a formal minimality proof?): NO.** §230.32 ends *"This is precisely the
compression we were looking for"* — an assertion. The six derived properties are all `f(...)` with **no
function ever supplied**, so even the claimed derivations are names, not derivations.

## 7. Is evidence primitive or derived? (§4 Q5) — **`E` IS DOUBLE-BOUND**

**EXECUTED TEST:**

```
Model F (§230.51):  K = (Graph, Types, Propositions, Evidence, Probability, Time)
  Evidence appears INSIDE K and ALSO as kernel component E   -> DUPLICATED
  Time appears INSIDE K but tau is NOT a kernel component    -> ASYMMETRIC
```

**If `K` contains `Evidence` (Model F, which Step 230 itself proposes as the hybrid answer to "what is

And **`Time` sits inside `K` while `τ` — which `T` requires — is not a kernel component at all.** The
treatment of the two is asymmetric with no stated reason.

**VERDICT: `CONTRADICTED`.** `E` cannot be both a constituent of `K` and a sibling of `K` in the same tuple
without a stated distinction (e.g. *evidence-as-content* vs *evidence-as-warrant*). No such distinction is
made.

## 14. What survives

**Genuinely sound in Step 230, and worth carrying forward:**

- **§230.30's reduction `L = History(T)`** — a legitimate compression, transparently argued, correct for
  internal lineage.
- **§230.13's `KnowledgeOS lineage = typed provenance graph`** — the right structure (DAG with branching
  and merging), and stronger than version control, as the file says.
- **§230.11's `Command → Transformation → Event`** — the correct DDD triad, and it correctly separates a
  transformation (a domain operation) from an event (a record).
- **§230.17 `Observation ≠ PopulationTruth`** and §230.19's `Assurance = Deterministic ∪ Statistical ∪
  HumanJudgement` — both correct.
- **§230.42's `BoundedContext ≠ Microservice`, `Aggregate ≠ DatabaseTable`, `DomainEvent ≠ MessageBrokerMessage`** — correct DDD guards.
- **§230.45's `Gita is not a mathematical axiom` / `not a DDD bounded context`** — the metaphor discipline holds.
- **§230.49's admission that `K` is undefined** — the most valuable sentence in the file.

**What does not survive:** the claim of minimality; the closure of the kernel; the independence of its
components; the six `f(...)` derivations; and `L = History(T)` as a *total* account of lineage.
