## 2. Can `𝒪` be derived? — the test

`DERIVED` — **Derivation principle** (mine; stated so it can be attacked):

> An operation `o` is **mandatory** iff removing `o` makes some **corpus-stated non-collapse law**
> inexpressible — two states the corpus requires to be distinct become identical, or a required
> distinction becomes unreachable.

This is entailment, not preference: **the laws are `CORPUS`, the forcing relation is `DERIVED`,
and the result is whatever survives.** 26 distinct non-collapse laws were extracted by measurement
(with repetition counts as evidence weight); 17 carry a forcing consequence.
