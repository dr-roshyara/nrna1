## 2. The strongest locus this cycle — **kṣetra / kṣetrajña**, 13.1–2

> **idaṃ śarīraṃ kaunteya kṣetram ity abhidhīyate**
> **etad yo vetti taṃ prāhuḥ kṣetrajña iti tad-vidaḥ**
>
> "This body is called the *field*; one who knows it, the knowers call the *knower-of-the-field*."

The Gītā's basic epistemic move is a **split between what is observed and that which observes** —
and, crucially, the split is **declared**, not discovered. The field is *named as a field* before
anything is said about it.

**The structural correspondence to this cycle's central problem:**

$$
\underbrace{K = (d_1, d_2, \dots, d_n)}_{\text{the field — the declared dimensions}}
\qquad
\underbrace{\Pi_i : K \mapsto O_i}_{\text{the frame that observes it}}
$$

The corpus arrived independently at *"Zero is a **zeroing of selected dimensions** in order to
establish a particular observational frame"* (file `…042545_knowledge-zero-as-zeroing-selected-dimensions…`).
That is the kṣetra/kṣetrajña split with the dimensions written out.

`[CORPUS]` **And 13.5–6 does exactly what the corpus needs next: it *enumerates* the field's
constituents.** The Gītā does not say "the field has parts"; it lists them. The lens's
contribution is therefore a **discipline**, not a structure:

> **No frame may be defined without a declared enumeration of the dimensions it projects from.**

### Where this lands — and it is not new

That obligation **already exists** in the theory as `FR-003`: an observable's effective read-set
must be computed through the transformation's provenance and **verified empirically**. The lens
**names** `FR-003`; it does not add to it.

## 3. The three kinds of knowledge — 18.20–22 — a typology of **projection error**

> **sarva-bhūteṣu yenaikaṃ bhāvam avyayam īkṣate** … *sāttvika* (18.20)
> **pṛthaktvena tu yaj jñānaṃ nānā-bhāvān pṛthag-vidhān** … *rājasa* (18.21)
> **yat tu kṛtsna-vad ekasmin kārye saktam** … *tāmasa* (18.22)

Read structurally — one undivided seen across the many; only manifold separateness; **clinging to
one part as if it were the whole** — this is a **three-way typology of ways a projection can go
wrong**, and each has an exact counterpart in the experimental record:

| Gītā type | structural reading | what the experiments found |
|---|---|---|
| *sāttvika* — the one across the many | several $\Pi$ that **collapse** to the same predicate where the transformation makes them equivalent | **`[EXP]` Π-collapse is real**: after dedup, value multiset, value set and **arity** are the same observable (KR-BRIDGE-02 §5) |
| *rājasa* — only separateness | treating a Π-family's **cardinality** as its number of mechanisms | **`[EXP]` refuted**: 14 surviving cells were **3** mechanisms |
| *tāmasa* — one part taken for the whole | treating **one** $\Pi$ as *the* observable | **`[EXP]` the error is measurable**: Zero rates ran 0.000 → 1.000 across five $\Pi$ on identical data |

> `[CORPUS]` **This is the cleanest mapping the strand has produced in six cycles.** The typology
> is not vague: each type picks out a *distinct, already-measured* failure.

**And it is still naming.** Every row's right-hand column was established before the verse was
opened. The lens supplies a **vocabulary** for a finding, not the finding.

## 4. Neutralization that leaves a trace — where the Gītā is the *weaker* source

The corpus's Nīlakaṇṭha thread (a harmful contribution enters → is neutralized → does not
propagate → **but leaves a persistent trace**) is **Purāṇic, not Gītā.** The nearest Gītā locus:

> **jñānāgniḥ sarva-karmāṇi bhasmasāt kurute tathā** — 4.37
> "the fire of knowledge burns all actions to ash"

`[CORPUS]` **Note what survives the burning: ash.** Even the Gītā's strongest purification image
does not yield *nothing*; it yields a **residue of a different kind**. That is structurally the
same shape as the corpus's "neutralization leaves a trace" — and the same shape as the
programme's own **Remainder trichotomy**:

$$\mathbf{A} \equiv \mathbf{C} \ (1\,182/1\,182) \qquad \mathbf{B} \ \text{is a different object}$$

*"The residue of the operation is not the residue of the concern"* (theory doc 03a §4). Ash is
the residue of the operation. What the fire was **for** is a different question.

> ⚠️ **The honest reading:** the Purāṇic image is the better fit and the Gītā is being stretched
> to reach it. Recorded so the stretch is visible.

### `[OPEN]` The one thing that would overturn this

A Gītā-derived distinction that yields a **prediction the current theory does not already make**
— something the experiments could go and test, and could fail. Six cycles have produced
**re-descriptions**, never a prediction. Until one appears, the strand's status is settled.

**What it would have to look like:** a verse-derived structural claim of the form *"under
condition X, observable $\Pi$ and inquiry $Q$ must behave in way Y"*, where Y is not already
entailed by the definitions in theory docs 01–05. **No candidate in this cycle's 38 files has
that shape.**
