## 3. The locus that fits this cycle — **18.63**, the teaching that declines to decide

> *iti te jñānam ākhyātaṁ guhyād guhyataraṁ mayā*
> *vimṛśyaitad **aśeṣeṇa** yathecchasi tathā kuru*
> — "This knowledge, more secret than secret, has been declared to you by me.
> Having reflected on it **without remainder**, do as you wish."

The structural move is the point, not the piety. Seven hundred verses of exhaustive analysis
terminate by **explicitly returning the decision to the agent** rather than determining it. The
text does not run out of argument; it marks a boundary and stops on the far side of it.

### Where it lands

Today's computation ended in exactly that shape. `Closure(𝒦₉)` was computed *aśeṣeṇa* — the
closure is by definition without remainder — and its §10 recommendation reads:

> *"The smallest legitimate next act is a **governance** act, not a computation … no amount of
> further computation can settle it."*

`aśeṣeṇa` is the load-bearing word: **exhaustiveness of the analysis is compatible with
undetermination of the act.** That is precisely why closure size ranging 15…22 is not a defect in
the computation — completing the analysis was never going to produce the ruling.

> **`[CORPUS]` This NAMES a result the computation produced. It did not predict it.**
> The naming is good — it is the crispest available label for "analysis complete, decision
> undetermined, and the boundary is explicit rather than a gap." It is still a naming.

## 4. Sharpening an existing candidate — **18.22** and the §E failure

Cycle 6 recorded the three-fold `jñāna` typology (18.20–22) as the strand's strongest mapping.
This cycle **sharpens that existing candidate**; it does **not** open a new one.

> *yat tu **kṛtsnavad ekasmin kārye saktam** ahaitukam*
> *atattvārthavad alpaṁ ca tat tāmasam udāhṛtam* (18.22)
> — "that which clings to **one single effect as if it were the whole**, without reason, without
> regard for the truth of the matter, and trivial — that is called tāmasa."

`kṛtsnavat` — "as if entire". Today's §E failure has that exact form: `minimum_implementable.py`
concluded *"a kernel can be built without them"* from a closure over **one** stipulated basis, and
stated it **absolutely**. Four of its eight "justified exclusions" fail once the basis changes.
The defect is not the closure — it is treating a basis-relative result *kṛtsnavat*.

And 18.20–21 complete the fit: sāttvika sees *one imperishable being undivided among the divided*
(`avibhaktaṁ vibhakteṣu`); rājasa sees *manifold separate entities* (`pṛthaktvena … nānā-bhāvān`).
Two incomparable closures over one canon is the rājasa/sāttvika tension stated in dependency terms.

**Classification: SHARPENING of cycle-6 candidate #4. Not a new candidate. Not a kernel primitive.**

## 6. New corpus, new naming — **2.42–43** and Part XX's retrieval boundary

Part XX (query, retrieval, RAG) is new to this cycle. Its central discipline is that *search
relevance, embedding similarity, graph paths and generated text must not masquerade as evidence*.

> *yām imāṁ **puṣpitāṁ vācaṁ** pravadanty avipaścitaḥ*
> *veda-vāda-ratāḥ pārtha **nānyad astīti vādinaḥ*** (2.42)
> — "the **flowery speech** the unwise utter, delighting in the letter of the Veda, saying
> **'there is nothing else'**."

The named failure is **fluency mistaken for warrant**, and `nānyad asti` — "there is nothing else"
— is the retrieval-boundary failure exactly: treating what was returned as exhausting what is.

**`[CORPUS]` A naming of a boundary Part XX already draws. No mechanical content added.**

## 7. Where the Gītā is the weaker source — **fourth consecutive recording**

The single item robust across all 14 worlds *and* carrying a substantive defect is
**`InvariantReg` — the mandatory invariant register, never enumerated** (G-67).

The Gītā offers **nothing** on enumerating a register of binding injunctions. That is
**Mīmāṃsā** territory — the `vidhi` / `niṣedha` / `arthavāda` classification exists precisely to
decide which utterances are binding injunctions and which are commendation. It is a far better
fit, and **it is not the Gītā.**

> **`[NEG]` It must not be smuggled into this strand.** Recorded here for the same reason
> cycle 7 recorded Śūnya/Śaiva as out of scope: naming the better-fitting source is useful;
> relabelling it as Gītā would be a provenance violation of the kind `FR-003` exists to prevent.
