# 1. Method, and the constraint that governs it

## 1.1 The binding constraint

Theory v1.2 **bans** this derivation chain:

```
Gītā concept  →  epistemic primitive  →  DDD element  →  architecture      ❌ BANNED
```

A philosophical reading may **motivate a question**, supply a **lens**, or name a **candidate**. It may
**never licence a primitive**, and a primitive may never licence a DDD element by resemblance. Each
arrow must be earned separately, on its own evidence.

**Therefore this study produces exactly three kinds of output** — and the register in §6 is typed
accordingly:

| output | what it may do | what it may never do |
|---|---|---|
| **question** | direct an experiment | assert an answer |
| **lens** | make a distinction visible | become the thing it makes visible |
| **corroboration** | agree, from outside, with something already derived | serve as the evidence for it |

**A fourth kind — `entity` — is not produced.** Where a reading *looks* like it yields an entity, that
is the ban firing, and it is recorded as a refusal in §8.

## 1.2 Why the Gītā, for this experiment specifically

Not by analogy-hunting. **The Bhagavad Gītā opens with a case of exactly the phenomenon
`KR-CONTR-2026-09` is trying to represent**, and it is the text's explicit subject for its first two
chapters — chapter 1 is titled *Arjuna-viṣāda-yoga*.

Arjuna's state at 1.28–47 is:

- **not uncertainty** — he knows every relevant fact about the battlefield;
- **not absence** — both commitments are fully represented to him;
- **not insufficient evidence** — more information would not move him;
- **not unobservability** — nothing is hidden;
- **not non-assessment** — he has assessed, at length, and the assessment produced the problem.

> **That is the protocol's required-distinction list, arrived at independently and about 2 000 years
> earlier.** It is corroboration of the *distinctions*, not evidence for any representation of them.

# 2. The textual loci

Six loci carry the material. Verse references are to the standard recension.

## 2.1 `viṣāda` — the state itself · **1.28–47, 2.1**

`sīdanti mama gātrāṇi mukhaṃ ca pariśuṣyati` (1.29) — *"my limbs fail and my mouth dries."* Sañjaya
names the state at 2.1: `viṣīdantam` — one who has collapsed.

**Structurally:** two commitments, both active, both endorsed, mutually incompatible in application —
`kṣatriya-dharma` (the warrior must fight) against `kula-dharma` and non-injury to kin. **Neither is
retracted. Neither is superseded. Both are current.** The result is not a wrong answer but **no
answer**: paralysis.

> **Lens L-1 — "informed paralysis".** A state may be **complete in evidence and still
> non-actionable.** Non-actionability is therefore **not** a deficit of evidence and cannot be repaired
> by acquiring more.

## 2.2 `dharma-saṃmūḍha-cetāḥ` — the layer at which it lives · **2.7**

`kārpaṇya-doṣopahata-svabhāvaḥ pṛcchāmi tvāṃ dharma-saṃmūḍha-cetāḥ` — *"my mind confounded as to
dharma, I ask you."*

**He does not say his perception is confounded, or his evidence.** He locates the confusion precisely:
in **dharma** — the normative model. **This is contradiction at the model/commitment layer, not at the
observation or evidence layer.**

> **Corroboration C-1** of `KR-CONTR-2026-09` §12: **`EvidenceConflict ≠ ClaimContradiction ≠
> ModelConflict`.** The Gītā's protagonist diagnoses his own case as the third, and the text sustains
> that classification for eighteen chapters.

## 2.3 `na caitad vidmaḥ kataran no garīyaḥ` — the missing order · **2.6**

*"Nor do we know which of the two is the weightier."*

**This is not doubt about facts. It is the absence of a comparative ordering over options.** Arjuna
can state both alternatives completely and cannot rank them.

> **Corroboration C-2** — for the programme's open item **`⪰`**. The Gītā's formulation of Arjuna's
> problem is **precisely an undefined preference relation**, and the text treats this as the harder
> problem, not a preliminary one.
>
> **⚠️ And the discipline this demands:** the programme has already ruled that `N_eff` and `⪰` are
> **independent**. This locus is corroboration for `⪰` **only**. It says nothing about `N_eff`, and
> must not be recruited for it.

## 2.4 `dvandva` — the pairs of opposites · **2.45, 7.27, 15.5**

`nirdvandvo nitya-sattva-stho` (2.45) — *"be free of the pairs of opposites."* The prescription for a
`dvandva` is **`dvandva-atīta`** — *going beyond* the pair.

**Note what is not prescribed.** The text does not resolve a `dvandva` by (i) picking a side,
(ii) averaging, or (iii) **adding a third term to the pair**. It changes the **frame** in which the
pair was posed.

> **Lens L-2 — "the frame, not the value".** When two opposed commitments are both genuinely held,
> the available move may be a **change of frame** rather than an **extension of the value set**.
>
> **This is a lens on the fourth-value question, and it must not be read as an answer to it.**
> `KR-CONTR-2026-09` §9 reaches a structurally similar conclusion — *the obstruction is not the number
> of values* — **by proof, from the evaluation-outcome family.** The lens made the question askable;
> **the theorem is what settles it.** Reversing that order would be exactly the banned derivation.

## 2.5 `saṃśaya` — doubt, and why it is a *different* pathology · **4.40, 6.39**

`saṃśayātmā vinaśyati` (4.40) — *"the one whose self is doubt perishes."* At 6.39 Arjuna asks Kṛṣṇa to
**cut** his doubt — `chettum` — *"completely, without remainder."*

**The Gītā treats `saṃśaya` and `viṣāda` as two distinct pathologies with two distinct remedies.**
`saṃśaya` is cut by instruction; `viṣāda` is not — chapter 2's teaching does not remove Arjuna's
contradiction, and at 18.73 he reports it gone only after the whole text.

> **Corroboration C-3:** **doubt ≠ contradiction**, from an independent tradition. This is the
> distinction `KR-CONTR-2026-09` tests as `Contr ≠ Unknown`.
>
> **⚠️ Corpus-discipline note (ES-005.4 — consume or extend, never create a second).** The corpus
> **already holds `saṃśaya` as a Candidate**, extracted from **Nyāya**
> (`20260822-0218-tarka-shastra-nyaya-epistemology-research-extraction.md`: *"structured doubt that
> initiates inquiry, not mere uncertainty"*). **This study therefore does NOT introduce a second
> `saṃśaya` candidate.** It **extends the existing one** with a contrast the Nyāya extraction does not
> carry: `saṃśaya` initiates inquiry; **`viṣāda` halts it.** Same family, opposite dynamics.

## 2.6 `guṇa` — coexisting qualities, not exclusive values · **14.5–10, 18.40**

`sattva rajas tamas` are not three values one of which obtains. **They coexist in every state and
differ in proportion**; 14.10 describes each *prevailing by suppressing* the other two.

> **Lens L-3 — "proportion, not selection".** A representation may need to say **how much of each**
> rather than **which one**. This is a lens on `KR-CONTR-2026-09` §11's finding that contradiction has
> **internal structure** (10 subtypes → 10 classes under a structured representation, 2 under a flat
> one).
>
> **Explicitly NOT concluded:** that KnowledgeOS should adopt a three-component proportion vector.
> That would be the banned chain, and the experiment supplies no evidence for it.

## 2.7 `yathecchasi tathā kuru` — the resolution is a decision · **18.63**

`vimṛśyaitad aśeṣeṇa yathecchasi tathā kuru` — *"having reflected on this fully, do as you wish."*

**This is the single most important locus in the study, and it is easy to miss.** After seventeen
chapters, the text does **not** resolve Arjuna's contradiction by deriving the answer. It supplies
everything it can and then **hands the resolution back as a decision.**

> **Corroboration C-4 — the strongest in this study.** The Gītā's own treatment of an irresolvable
> pair of commitments is: **exhaust the evidence, then decide.** Not: *keep gathering evidence until
> the answer appears.*
>
> This corroborates, from outside, the distinction the programme arrived at independently and has now
> applied four times — **`evidence ≠ decision`**: factivity (`R1`), `FR-001`'s freeze, adopting
> `M3≅M4`, adopting `𝓑`. **`KR-CONTR-2026-09` §21 adds two more**: `ℛ_req` and the frame qualifier `φ`.
>
> **It is corroboration, not evidence.** The programme derived this from its own failures; the Gītā
> agrees with it. Agreement from outside is worth recording and is worth nothing as proof.

# 6. Kernel candidates — the honest verdict

> ## **The Gītā supplies NO kernel candidate. Not one.**

**Why, stated as an argument rather than a preference:**

1. **`KR-CONTR-2026-09` §13 shows contradiction is `K2`** — derivable from the existing candidate
   powers, requiring a new *representation*, not a new *capability*. A philosophical source cannot
   overturn a derivability result.
2. **`C-1` is binding**: *do not put a mechanism into the kernel merely because KnowledgeOS can use
   it.* **Textual resonance is a much weaker warrant than usability, and usability already fails.**
3. **The ban** forbids the chain that would be required to get from a Gītā concept to a kernel power.
4. **The programme's record is consistent**: across the whole corpus the Gītā's productive
   contribution has been **questions and lenses, never entities** — and the one derivation it did
   produce, the `Zero`/Śūnya chain, was earned by 1 620 000 assignments, **not by the reading**.

> **The kernel remains NOT SELECTABLE, and nothing in this study bears on it.**

# 7. What must not be concluded from this document

- **Not** that the Gītā supports a four-valued logic. It bears on the *question*, not the answer, and
  `KR-CONTR` answered it by proof.
- **Not** that `viṣāda` should become a state, a value, or a type.
- **Not** that `dvandva-atīta` is an operator, or that `φ` should be derived from it.
- **Not** that corroboration from outside strengthens a KnowledgeOS result. **It does not.** A result
  derived from 20 000 trials and a deterministic witness suite is exactly as strong after this
  document as before it.
- **Not** that the absence of kernel candidates is a failure of the study. **It is the study's most
  disciplined output.**
