# 1. Why this text, for this experiment

`KR-BRIDGE-01` looked for a connection between two things and **found that the apparent connection was
a third thing acting on both.** Pooled `RD = −0.363`; stratified, `0.000` exactly. **Redundancy was
causing both Zero and inadequacy.**

**That is a causal-attribution problem, and the Gītā's central philosophical move is a
causal-attribution correction.**

# 2. The loci

## 2.1 `prakṛteḥ kriyamāṇāni guṇaiḥ karmāṇi sarvaśaḥ` · 3.27

> *actions are performed entirely by the qualities of nature; one whose self is deluded by
> ego-sense thinks "I am the doer"*

**The verse's structure is precisely a confounding correction.** An agent observes a correlation
between *self* and *action* and infers agency. The text says the correlation is real and **the
attribution is wrong** — a **third factor** (`guṇa`) is producing both.

> **Corroboration C-1** — for what stratification did. **The pooled association was real** (it is in
> the data, on both splits). **The attribution to Zero was wrong.** `redundancy → Zero` and
> `redundancy → inadequacy` — a common cause, exactly the shape 3.27 describes.
>
> ⚠️ **This corroborates the SHAPE of the error, not the method of detecting it.** The verse offers no
> stratification, no contingency table, no test. **The checklist supplied that** — specifically §12,
> which mandated stratification *before* any association could be reported.

## 2.2 `nānyaṁ guṇebhyaḥ kartāraṁ yadā draṣṭānupaśyati` · 14.19

> *when the seer perceives no agent other than the qualities*

**A sharper form of the same move: the correction is not to find a better agent, but to stop looking
for one at that level.**

> **Lens L-1 — "no relationship" is a finding, not a failure.** The experiment's `OUTCOME A` is
> exactly a refusal to name an agent where the evidence names none. **§27 of the protocol says the
> same thing in its own vocabulary: *a clean negative result is equally valuable*.**
>
> **Recorded as a lens because it frames the outcome; it does not license it.** The licence came from
> the four-way table and the stratification.

## 2.3 `samatvaṁ yoga ucyate` · 2.48

> *evenness is called yoga*

**The experiment was required to seek all four cells and to privilege none** — `H0`, `H1`, `H2`, `H3`
weighted equally, with §2's explicit instruction *"Do NOT privilege H1."*

> **Corroboration C-2, and a weak one.** The verse concerns equanimity toward outcomes; the protocol
> concerns non-privileging of hypotheses. **These rhyme; they are not the same claim**, and I record
> the difference rather than letting the rhyme do argumentative work.

## 2.4 The refusal — `yoga-sthaḥ kuru karmāṇi saṅgaṁ tyaktvā` · 2.48a

> *established in yoga, perform actions, having abandoned attachment*

**A tempting reading:** having found no bridge, abandon attachment to the result and move on.

> ## ⚠️ REFUSED — and for a reason specific to this experiment.
> **`OUTCOME A` is not a null to be shrugged off. It is a constraint with content**: it says a formal
> bridge hypothesis **may not** be proposed (§22 permits that only under `OUTCOME C`). **A reading of
> 2.48a as "release the result" would license exactly the drift the governance freeze forbids** —
> from *no relationship established* toward *the question is settled and we may proceed*.
>
> **Third refusal in three studies.** 2.50 (`skill in action`), 18.66 (`abandon all duties`), and now
> 2.48a. **All three are the text's most quotable lines, and all three would license a shortcut.**

# 6. What must not be concluded

- **Not** that the Gītā anticipated confounding analysis. **It supplies the shape of the error and
  none of the machinery for finding it.**
- **Not** that `OUTCOME A` is philosophically endorsed. **The verse and the result agree in form and
  were reached by unrelated means.**
- **Not** that three refusals in three studies means the text is unproductive. **The refusals are the
  most reliable output this strand produces** — each names a shortcut the programme would otherwise
  be tempted by.

> ## The strongest statement made must never exceed the strength of the available evidence.
>
> Yield: **two lenses, one weak corroboration, one refusal.** The corroboration is weak and is
> labelled weak. **The refusal is again the most useful part.**
