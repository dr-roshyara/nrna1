> **A decision is not a higher grade of claim — it is a different kind of record.**
>
> Governance is **orthogonal** to epistemic status (§C4.4). A decision attaches to a question; it does
> not promote the claim that informed it. A finding does not become "more true" by being adopted, and
> an adopted decision is not thereby correct. **Governance ≠ truth. Not ratified ≠ false.**
>
> For the same reason this record carries `claim_layer: N/A`. Recording a decision as the top rung of
> an epistemic ladder would make the ladder do two unrelated jobs at once.
