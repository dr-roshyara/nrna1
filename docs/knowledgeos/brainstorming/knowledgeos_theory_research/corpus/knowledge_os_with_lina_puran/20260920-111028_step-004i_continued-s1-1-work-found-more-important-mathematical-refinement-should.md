# 2. The deeper mathematical solution

The graph experiment used only:

$$
C^0\xrightarrow{d^0}C^1.
$$

We need one more level:

$$
\boxed{
C^0
\xrightarrow{d^0}
C^1
\xrightarrow{d^1}
C^2
}
$$

where

$$
d^1d^0=0.
$$

This is the beginning of a **cellular cochain complex**.

### Cochain

A cochain is an assignment of algebraic values to cells of a particular dimension.

For example:

* \(0\)-cochain → values on vertices,
* \(1\)-cochain → values on edges,
* \(2\)-cochain → values on faces.

---

# 3. Why this solves our problem

Take a triangle:

```text
        A
       / \
      /   \
     B-----C
```

Now treat the triangle as a **filled 2-simplex**, not merely three graph edges.

We have:

$$
C^0
=
\{x_A,x_B,x_C\}
$$

and:

$$
C^1
=
\{b_{AB},b_{BC},b_{CA}\}.
$$

The 2-cell gives:

$$
C^2=\{q\}.
$$

The first differential is:

$$
d^0(x)_ {AB}=x_A+x_B
$$

and similarly for the other edges.

The second differential is:

$$
d^1(b)
=
b_{AB}+b_{BC}+b_{CA}.
$$

Therefore:

$$
d^1d^0=0.
$$

That is an exact algebraic invariant.

# 7. This gives us the right meaning of obstruction

Define:

$$
Z^1=\ker d^1
$$

as the set of locally compatible constraint assignments.

Define:

$$
B^1=\operatorname{im}d^0
$$

as the constraint assignments generated by a genuine global state.

Then:

$$
H^1=\frac{Z^1}{B^1}.
$$

This gives three possibilities:

### Case A

$$
b\notin Z^1.
$$

Local compatibility itself fails.

### Case B

$$
b\in Z^1,\qquad b\notin B^1.
$$

Local compatibility holds, but there is no global realization.

This is the genuine **cohomological obstruction**.

### Case C

$$
b\in B^1.
$$

A global state exists.

This is much stronger than our previous model.

# 10. New formal KnowledgeOS definitions

## 10.1 Local state

A state valid within a bounded context:

$$
s_U\in\mathscr S(U).
$$

---

## 10.2 Constraint

A condition that must hold between local states.

Example:

$$
x_A+x_B=1.
$$

---

## 10.3 Cochain

A mathematical representation of local constraints.

$$
b\in C^1.
$$

---

## 10.4 Differential

A mapping that computes compatibility conditions:

$$
d^1:C^1\rightarrow C^2.
$$

---

## 10.5 Cocycle

A constraint assignment satisfying:

$$
d^1b=0.
$$

---

## 10.6 Coboundary

A constraint assignment generated by a lower-level global state:

$$
b=d^0x.
$$

---

## 10.7 Cohomology

The quotient:

$$
H^1=Z^1/B^1.
$$

It identifies compatible structures that cannot be explained by the chosen global-generation mechanism.

---

## 10.8 Obstruction class

For a cocycle \(b\):

$$
[b]\in H^1.
$$

If:

$$
[b]\neq0,
$$

the local structure is compatible but cannot be generated by a global state under the declared model.
