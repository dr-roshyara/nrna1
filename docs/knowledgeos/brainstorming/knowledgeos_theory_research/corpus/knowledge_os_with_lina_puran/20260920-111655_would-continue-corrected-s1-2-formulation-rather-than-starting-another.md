# 3. KnowledgeOS interpretation

We now define the objects precisely.

### 0-cell

A **knowledge node**.

Example:

```text
E1 = Evidence 1
E2 = Evidence 2
M  = Model
D  = Determination
```

### 1-cell

A **local compatibility constraint** between knowledge nodes.

Example:

$$
E1\rightarrow M
$$

may impose:

$$
x_{E1}\oplus x_M=b_{E1,M}.
$$

### 2-cell

A **higher-order compatibility relation** between several constraints.

For a triangle:

```text
       A
      / \
     /   \
    B-----C
```

the 2-cell says that the three edge constraints must themselves be compatible.

### Cochain

A mathematical assignment to cells.

For example:

$$
x\in C^0
$$

is an assignment of values to knowledge nodes.

### Differential

A transformation between dimensions:

$$
d^0:C^0\rightarrow C^1
$$

and

$$
d^1:C^1\rightarrow C^2.
$$

### Fundamental invariant

We require:

$$
\boxed{d^1d^0=0}.
$$

This is not an interpretation. It is an exact algebraic property that the implementation must verify.

# 4. First computational proof

For the triangle,

$$
d^0=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&1&0\\
0&1&1\\
1&0&1
\end{pmatrix}
$$

and

$$
d^1=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&1&1
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The computation gives:

$$
d^1d^0=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&0&0
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

So:

$$
\boxed{d^1d^0=0}
$$

is verified exactly.

This gives us our first machine-checkable KnowledgeOS mathematical invariant.

This is the genuine phenomenon we were looking for:

# 9. Important DDD correction

We now have **three different domain concepts**:

### Dependency

$$
A\rightarrow B
$$

means:

> B depends on A.

### Compatibility

$$
A\sim_C B
$$

means:

> A and B satisfy the specified compatibility contract.

### Obstruction

$$
[A]\neq0
$$

means:

> The chosen local compatibility information cannot be globally realized under the current mathematical model.

These must not be one aggregate or one relation type.

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{
Dependency\neq Compatibility\neq Obstruction
}
$$

This should become an architectural invariant.
