# 1. First: what has actually been mathematically derived?

The strongest genuine mathematical derivation is **MD-058**.

Its central result was:

$$
\boxed{
K_1\approx_{Q,\mathcal O}K_2
\iff
Obs_{Q,\mathcal O}(K_1)=Obs_{Q,\mathcal O}(K_2)
}
$$

This was derived as the weakest relation satisfying the then-established requirements, not simply chosen by preference. 

And because it is based on equality of observations/functions:

$$
K\approx K
$$

$$
K_1\approx K_2\Rightarrow K_2\approx K_1
$$

$$
K_1\approx K_2\land K_2\approx K_3
\Rightarrow K_1\approx K_3
$$

so the relation is an actual equivalence relation by construction. 

That is a **real mathematical derivation**.

# 2. We also derived a genuine representation-independence result

MD-058 tested the relation against the one candidate that actually had executable semantics: F3.

The existing F3 construction was:

$$
Beh_{\mathfrak c}(K)=Reach(Ops(K))
$$

and the derivation established:

> If two operators are merged without changing their net atom production, the reachable atom closure remains unchanged.

Formally:

$$
Reach(Ops(K))
=
Reach(Ops(K'))
$$

and consequently:

$$
Obs_{\mathcal O}(K)=Obs_{\mathcal O}(K')
$$

under the stated conditions. 

That is our **first concrete mathematical theorem about representation independence**.

# 3. We also derived a counterexample

This is actually just as important.

A split representation can expose an intermediate atom that the original representation does not expose.

Then:

$$
Obs_{\mathcal O}(K)\neq Obs_{\mathcal O}(K')
$$

even though the two representations perform the same net transformation.

That led to the newly derived requirement:

$$
\boxed{R1a:\mathcal O\text{ must itself be representation-neutral}}
$$

This was not present beforehand; it was generated by the mathematical counterexample. 

So we have:

**Theorem → Counterexample → New necessary condition.**

That is genuine theory construction.

# 4. We derived something even more important for Kernel minimality

We discovered that **minimality cannot safely be calculated over raw representations**.

If two representations are semantically equivalent but have different numbers of components, then:

$$
|K_1|\neq |K_2|

does not imply different semantic size.

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{

\rightarrow
\text{quotient}
\rightarrow
\text{minimality}

rather than:

$$
\text{representation}
\rightarrow
\text{size}

\text{minimality}.

MD-058 therefore derived that `MinKer` must operate over equivalence classes:

$$
\mathfrak K_{\mathrm{adm}}/\approx_{Q,\mathcal O}

before minimality is meaningful. 

This is a very important result for our current Kernel question.
