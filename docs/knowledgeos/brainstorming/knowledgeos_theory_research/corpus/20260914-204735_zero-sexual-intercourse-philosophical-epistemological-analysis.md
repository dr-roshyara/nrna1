## Part 1: The Book's Own Connection

### 1.1 Jacob Meskin's Interpretation

In *Finding Zero*, Jacob Meskin (Aczel's philosopher friend) makes a direct connection:

> "Does one dare to hazard the (by now obvious) surmise that zero is the (in a sense!) principle of the womb, the vagina, and that the numbers, that is to say numerical quantities as opposed to zero, are the principle of the phallus? Are enumeration, measurement, even the ticking off of a Geiger counter or digital display perhaps, an echo of . . . sexual intercourse, where numbers move back and forth in a field opened up to their waxing and waning only by the blessing of a receptive, enveloping vacuity ready to receive them?"

**Key Idea:** Zero = the receptive void (womb/vagina) that allows numbers (the phallus) to move and create meaning. Without the zero, there is no place for numbers to occupy.

### 1.2 Nagarjuna's Shunyata as Womb

Meskin continues:
> "Shunyata would seem to be the fundamental openness of reality, its receptivity, the yielding framework through which and within which change and fluctuation and movement become possible. It is as if we are saying, à la Nagarjuna, that it is only because of zero (Shunyata) that there can be variation in intensity (number). Without emptiness there could be no movement; without zero, there could be no numbers."
