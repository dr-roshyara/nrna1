## Part III — Q-ZOOM-DET: Categorical Type of Determine

### III.1 Precise statement

**D40 (Determine contract, frozen).** For an inquiry \(Q\), knowledge \(K\), and evidence \(E\):
\[
\text{Determine}(Q \mid K, E) \iff \text{Supported}(h \mid K, E) \wedge \text{CompetitorsExcluded}(h \mid K, E).
\]

**Q-ZOOM-DET.** What is the categorical type of Determine, and how does it interact with the corpus's architecture (L1–L3.0′)?

### III.2 The three candidate typings

**(T-a) Determine is a limit in a category of predicates.**

Define \(\mathbf{Pred}(K, E)\) as the category whose objects are predicates \(P: \mathcal{H}_Q \to \{\text{True}, \text{False}\}\) over the hypothesis space \(\mathcal{H}_Q\) at \((K, E)\), and whose morphisms are entailments \(P \Rightarrow P'\). This is a *poset category*.

Then:
- \(\text{Supported}\) is an object of \(\mathbf{Pred}(K, E)\).
- \(\text{CompetitorsExcluded}\) is an object of \(\mathbf{Pred}(K, E)\).
- \(\text{Determine} = \text{Supported} \wedge \text{CompetitorsExcluded}\) is the *product* (meet) in \(\mathbf{Pred}(K, E)\).

**Consequence.** Determine is a *limit* (a binary product).

**(T-b) Determine is a functor from \((K, E)\)-space to predicates.**

Define \(\mathbf{KE}\) as the category of \((K, E)\) pairs with evidence-refinement morphisms. Then:
\[
\text{Determine}: \mathbf{KE} \to \mathbf{Pred},
\]
sending \((K, E)\) to the predicate \(\text{Determine}(- \mid K, E)\).

**Consequence.** Determine is a *functor*.

**(T-c) Determine is a natural transformation.**

By D36 (Iteration 13), Determine is already typed as a natural transformation. This typing is *not new*; it is *prior* to Q-ZOOM-DET.

**Consequence.** Determine is a natural transformation (already derived).

### III.3 Which typing is correct?

**(T-a) and (T-b) are not competing with (T-c); they are refinements at different levels:**

- **(T-c) is the *type* of Determine as a *transformation between functors*.** It says Determine is a natural transformation.
- **(T-b) is the *type* of Determine as a *functor* in the evidence variable.** It says Determine is a functor from evidence-contexts to predicates.
- **(T-a) is the *type* of Determine as a *predicate*.** It says Determine is a specific predicate (a meet) in the predicate lattice.

**These are compatible.** The full typing is:

\[
\text{Determine}: \mathbf{KE} \to \mathbf{Pred}, \quad (K, E) \mapsto (\text{Supported} \wedge \text{CompetitorsExcluded})(-\mid K, E),
\]
which is a *functor* that is *natural* in the evidence variable.

**Both (T-a) and (T-b) are thus correct, and refine (T-c).**

### III.4 Testing the typing against falsification

**Attempt 1 — Does Determine as product (T-a) require the predicate lattice to be *complete*?**

For Determine to be a product, the meet must *exist* in \(\mathbf{Pred}(K, E)\). Predicates over a finite hypothesis space form a *finite* lattice, which is complete. ✓

**Attempt 2 — Does Determine as functor (T-b) require \(\mathbf{KE}\) to have a specific structure?**

\(\mathbf{KE}\) requires evidence-refinement morphisms. By D27 (Explore produces evidence), evidence refines. ✓

**Attempt 3 — Do the typings collapse?**

If the predicate lattice is trivially Boolean, \(\text{Supported} \wedge \text{CompetitorsExcluded}\) is a Boolean conjunction. If the lattice is *non-Boolean* (e.g., a Heyting algebra for intuitionistic evidence), the meet is a Heyting meet. The corpus does *not* specify the lattice type.

**Gap.** The *type of the predicate lattice* is not derived. Is it Boolean, Heyting, or something else?

**Consequence.** The typing (T-a) is *conditional* on the predicate lattice's type.

### III.5 The predicate lattice gap

**Q-ZOOM-DET-pre.** What is the *type* of the predicate lattice \(\mathbf{Pred}(K, E)\)?

Three candidates:

**(L-a) Boolean.** Predicates form a Boolean algebra; classical conjunction. Determine is classical AND.

**(L-b) Heyting.** Predicates form a Heyting algebra; intuitionistic conjunction. Determine is Heyting meet; *excluded middle fails*.

**(L-c) Probabilistic/Continuous.** Predicates take values in \([0, 1]\); Determine is a threshold on a probability.

**Which is correct?**

The corpus's Determine contract (D40) *defines* Determine as a *conjunction* of two predicates, but does not specify whether the conjunction is classical or intuitionistic.

**Test via the decoy precondition (D42).** D42 says meaningful competitors must be *generated* and *excluded*. This implies:
- "CompetitorsExcluded" is not a *static* fact about the hypothesis space; it is a *dynamic* fact about whether the *generated* competitors were ruled out.
- If new competitors can be generated after Determine is reached, then Determine is *defeasible* — a characteristically *intuitionistic* property.

**Consequence.** The predicate lattice is *at least Heyting* (L-b), not Boolean.

**Test via the effect floor (D41).** D41 defines a *continuous* threshold \(\Delta \ge 0.10\). This implies:
- Predicates are not *binary*; they are *thresholded*.
- Determine is a *thresholded* conjunction.

**Consequence.** The predicate lattice is *continuous* (L-c), refining (L-b).

**Synthesis.** The predicate lattice is a *Heyting-valued continuous structure* — a *Heyting algebra over \([0, 1]\) with a threshold*.

**Formalisation.** Define:
\[
\text{Pred}(K, E) := \left\{ P : \mathcal{H}_Q \to [0, 1] \right\} / \sim_\tau,
\]
where \(\sim_\tau\) is the equivalence relation \(P \sim_\tau P'\) iff \(P \ge \tau \iff P' \ge \tau\) for the frozen threshold \(\tau\) (0.10 primary, 0.05 borderline).

Then Determine is the *thresholded meet*:
\[
\text{Determine}(h \mid K, E) := \left[ \min\{\text{Supported}(h \mid K, E), \text{CompetitorsExcluded}(h \mid K, E)\} \ge \tau \right].
\]

**This is a *complete* typing of Determine.** ✓

### III.6 Interaction with the corpus's architecture

**(a) With L3.0 (Zoom-in).** Determine is the *terminal* operation of the Zoom-in cycle. Its typing as a thresholded meet is *consistent* with Zoom-in's functorial structure (D36).

**(b) With L3.0′ (Zoom-out).** Determine yields \(D_Q\), which is *integrated* into \(K_{t+1}\). The threshold \(\tau\) *propagates* through \(\sqcup\) — \(D_Q\) carries the threshold-value of Determine. ✓

**(c) With L2.5 (Governance).** Determine is a *predicate over the hypothesis space*, not over the carrier \(E\). It does *not* interact with the Calkin structure. **Orthogonal to L2.5.** ✓

**(d) With L2.75 (Fibre-internal).** Same — orthogonal. ✓

**Verdict.** Determine's typing is *consistent* with L3.0/L3.0′ and *orthogonal* to L2.5/L2.75.

### III.7 Nexus Repository instantiation

Nexus 70 GB/day → GitLab Runner:

- **Hypothesis space.** \(\mathcal{H}_Q = \{\text{backup}, \text{repo}, \text{CICD}, \text{config}, \text{external}, \ldots\}\).
- **Supported.** Each hypothesis \(h\) has a support-value \(s_h \in [0, 1]\).
- **CompetitorsExcluded.** For a candidate \(h\), the exclusion-value \(e_h \in [0, 1]\) measures how well the *other* hypotheses are ruled out.
- **Determine.** \(h\) is determined iff \(\min(s_h, e_h) \ge 0.10\) (primary) — i.e., \(h\) is supported AND competitors are excluded.

**Threshold semantics.** If \(s_{CICD} = 0.85\) but \(e_{CICD} = 0.05\) (competitors not excluded), then Determine *fails*. The investigation must continue. This is the correct epistemic discipline: support alone is insufficient.

**DDD application (only now, after the math is clear).**
A Nexus bounded context that has *determined* a cause has \(\min(s_h, e_h) \ge \tau\). A context that has *only* strong support but weak exclusion is *not a determined context* — it is an *investigation context*. The DDD boundary algebra thus distinguishes:
- **Investigation contexts** (Determine not achieved).
- **Determination contexts** (Determine achieved with \(h\)).
- **Integrated contexts** (Determine achieved and \(D_Q\) integrated into \(K_{t+1}\)).

### III.8 What this establishes

- **Determine is *fully typed*.** As a functor \(\mathbf{KE} \to \mathbf{Pred}\) with a thresholded-meet predicate.
- **The predicate lattice is Heyting-valued over \([0, 1]\) with threshold \(\tau\).** Derived from D40–D42.
- **Determine is orthogonal to L2.5/L2.75.** It lives strictly on L3.0.
- **The DDD boundary algebra (Q-L4-1) now has *typed* objects** — integrated determinations carry their threshold-value.
- **The decoy precondition (D42) is captured** by the exclusion-value \(e_h\) being *dynamic*, not static.
