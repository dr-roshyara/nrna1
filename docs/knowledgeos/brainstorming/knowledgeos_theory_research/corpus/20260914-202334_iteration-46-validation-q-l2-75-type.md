## Part III — Q-L2.75-DYNAMICS: Is L2.75 Static or Dynamic?

### III.1 Precise statement

**L2.75 fibration (D25).** \(\pi_{\mathrm{Calkin}}: \mathcal{Q}^{\mathrm{Saz}} \twoheadrightarrow \mathcal{Q}^{\mathrm{Calkin}}\), where \(\mathcal{Q}^{\mathrm{Saz}} = L(H_{K_t})/\mathcal{L}^1\) and \(\mathcal{Q}^{\mathrm{Calkin}} = L(H_{K_t})/K\).

**State transition (D14).** \(T: (K_t, Q, \Gamma, X_t) \to K_{t+1}\).

**Q-L2.75-DYNAMICS.** Does the L2.75 fibration *depend on the state* \(K_t\) (dynamic), or is it *fixed by the carrier* \(X\) (static)?

### III.2 Testing (Static)

**Claim.** L2.75 is *static* — determined by the carrier \(X\) alone.

**Test.** The fibration's base \(\mathcal{Q}^{\mathrm{Calkin}}\) depends on \(H_{K_t}\), the *Hilbert space* at \(K_t\). By D4 and D9, \(H_{K_t}\) is derived from the covariance \(R_{\mu_{K_t}}\).

**Structural fact.** \(R_{\mu_{K_t}}\) *depends on* \(K_t\) — the covariance *changes* with the state. Therefore \(H_{K_t}\) *changes* with \(K_t\). Therefore \(\mathcal{Q}^{\mathrm{Calkin}}\) *changes* with \(K_t\).

**Consequence.** L2.75 is *not static*; it *varies* with \(K_t\).

**Verdict.** Static hypothesis is *falsified*.

### III.3 Testing (Dynamic)

**Claim.** L2.75 is *dynamic* — varies with \(K_t\).

**Test.** Does the *fibre* \(\mathcal{Q}^{\mathrm{Saz}} \twoheadrightarrow \mathcal{Q}^{\mathrm{Calkin}}\) admit *morphism structure* over state transitions?

**Candidate functor.** For each transition \(T: K_t \to K_{t+1}\), does \(T\) induce a *fibration morphism*:
\[
\begin{array}{ccc}
\mathcal{Q}^{\mathrm{Saz}}_{K_t} & \xrightarrow{T_*} & \mathcal{Q}^{\mathrm{Saz}}_{K_{t+1}} \\
\downarrow & & \downarrow \\
\mathcal{Q}^{\mathrm{Calkin}}_{K_t} & \xrightarrow{T_*} & \mathcal{Q}^{\mathrm{Calkin}}_{K_{t+1}}
\end{array}
\]

**Analysis.**

- \(T\) induces a *pushforward* on covariances: \(R_{\mu_{K_{t+1}}} = T R_{\mu_{K_t}} T^*\) (D8's covariance-relative Sazonov stability).
- The pushforward \(T_*\) on operator algebras is *linear* but *not* an algebra homomorphism in general.
- *By D22*, Sazonov and Calkin compactness are orthogonal; the pushforward may not preserve both structures canonically.

**Verdict.** Dynamic structure is *viable* but *conditional* — the morphism \(T_*\) exists *if* \(T R_{\mu_{K_t}} T^*\) is nuclear (Sazonov stability). By D8, this is *the* Sazonov-admissibility condition.

### III.4 The dynamic-conditioned theorem

**Theorem (Q-L2.75-DYNAMICS).** The L2.75 fibration is *dynamic*, with structure determined by the *Sazonov-admissible transitions*:

- **Static** for transitions that are *not* Sazonov-admissible (no morphism exists; the fibration is *frozen*).
- **Dynamic** for Sazonov-admissible transitions (morphism \(T_*\) exists, moving fibres).

**Formally.** L2.75 is a *fibred category over the subcategory of Sazonov-admissible transitions*.

**Proof.** Combine III.2 (static falsified) and III.3 (dynamic conditioned). \(\square\)

### III.5 Falsification attempts

**Attempt 1 — Does L2.75 commute with the kernel \(K\)?**

By D139, kernel is *orthogonal* to L2.75. So they *do not commute* — but this is *expected*. The dynamic structure of L2.75 is *independent* of the kernel.

**Verdict.** Falsified as *commutation*; *orthogonality* holds.

**Attempt 2 — Is the dynamic structure *canonical*?**

\(T_*\) is *canonical* on Sazonov-admissible transitions (D8). Off-admissible transitions have *no* canonical morphism.

**Verdict.** Canonical on domain. ✓

**Attempt 3 — Does L2.75 dynamic structure *conflict* with D22 (orthogonality)?**

No — D22 says Sazonov and Calkin compactness are *orthogonal as *classes*; the dynamic structure uses *both* via the *chain* \(\mathcal{L}^1 \subset K\).

**Verdict.** Falsified.

**No falsification.** The dynamic-conditioned theorem holds.
