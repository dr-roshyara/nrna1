# 240.20 Proposed contradiction classification

We should therefore classify every contradiction into one of five categories:

### C1 — Genuine logical contradiction

$$
A\land\neg A.
$$

### C2 — Competing models

$$
M_1\neq M_2
$$

with no proof that both are equivalent.

### C3 — Refinement

$$
M_2=M_1+\Delta.
$$

### C4 — Projection/view difference

$$
M_2=\pi(M_1).
$$

### C5 — Terminological/type collision

Same symbol, different semantic type.

This classification will prevent us from incorrectly "resolving" harmless historical variation.

# 240.23 The critical mathematical insight

The contradiction registry suggests that we should **stop trying to find the one correct tuple first**.

Instead, construct a typed meta-model:

$$
\mathcal M=
(\mathcal O,\mathcal T,\mathcal R,\mathcal F,\mathcal P)
$$

where:

* \(\mathcal O\) = object types;
* \(\mathcal T\) = state/time types;
* \(\mathcal R\) = relations;
* \(\mathcal F\) = transformations/functions;
* \(\mathcal P\) = predicates/invariants.

Then the competing formulations become mappings into the meta-model.

This is much more mathematically robust than selecting one historical tuple and declaring everything else obsolete.
