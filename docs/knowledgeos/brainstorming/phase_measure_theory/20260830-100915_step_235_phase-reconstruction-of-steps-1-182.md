$$

(Q_j,C_j,D_j,S_j,M_j,G_j,H_j,\Delta_j)

where:

* \(Q_j\) — dominant architectural question;
* \(C_j\) — concepts introduced;

* \(M_j\) — mathematical development;

### Phase transition criterion

We should define a genuine architectural phase change as:

$$
\boxed{
P_i\rightarrow P_{i+1}
\iff
\text{the dominant architectural model materially changes}.
}
$$

A new formula or a new term alone does **not** constitute a new phase.

A phase changes when, for example:

$$
Ontology_{new}\neq Ontology_{old}
$$

or:

$$
Governance_{new}\neq Governance_{old}
$$

or:

$$
SystemBoundary_{new}\neq SystemBoundary_{old}.
$$
