# Research Synthesis: The 24 Undefined Questions — The Path Forward

## Executive Summary

After extensive analysis through multiple lenses (KST, epistemology, social construction, Zero, Lord, Vāṇī, Tripuṭī, Nyāya, and Vedic cosmology), we have reached a critical juncture. We have solved many **distinctions** but have not yet defined the **mathematical/domain semantics** behind them. This document catalogs the 24 remaining undefined questions and proposes a path forward.

---

> We have defined a powerful theory of a knowledge state, but we have not yet formally defined the boundary between the observed reality, the ideal state of that reality, and the system's current representation of that reality.
