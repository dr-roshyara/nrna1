## 1. The Core Architecture: Lord → Krishna → KnowledgeOS → Human Knower

The architecture is now a clean, layered flow from the ultimate source to the human actor.

$$
\boxed{
\text{Lord} \rightarrow \text{Krishna} \rightarrow \text{KnowledgeOS} \rightarrow \text{Human Knower}
}
$$

**Where:**

- **Lord (Ω):** The infinite, unbounded knowledge horizon. The source of all potential knowledge.
- **Krishna:** Contextualized, accessible knowledge that can guide the Knower. It is knowledge *made available*.
- **KnowledgeOS:** The epistemic system that operationalizes Krishna's knowledge. It performs the roles of **Observer (Sañjaya)** and **Guide (Sārathi)**.
- **Human Knower:** The recipient and owner of understanding, decision, and action.

**The Key Invariant:**

$$
\boxed{
\text{Krishna} \neq \text{KnowledgeOS}
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
\text{KnowledgeOS} = \text{The system that makes Krishna-like knowledge operational as guidance.}
}
$$

## 2. The Two Roles of KnowledgeOS: Sañjaya + Sārathi

| Role | Function | Formalization | Primary Knower Context |

| **Sañjaya** | **Observer:** Reconstructs the state for the Knower. | `Reality → Observation → Evidence → StateKnowledge` | Dhṛtarāṣṭra (remote, limited access). |
| **Sārathi** | **Guide:** Guides the Knower through the knowledge journey. | `StateKnowledge → Understanding → Guidance → DecisionReadiness` | Arjuna (directly involved, uncertain about action). |

**The Formal Statement:**

$$
\boxed{
\text{KnowledgeOS} = \text{Sañjaya} + \text{Sārathi}

## 3. The Two Knower Positions: Dhṛtarāṣṭra and Arjuna

| Knower | Role | Relationship to KnowledgeOS | Primary Need |

| Concept | Definition | Role in the Architecture |

$$
\boxed{
\text{Krishna is the knowledge; KnowledgeOS is the epistemic system through which that knowledge can be observed, structured, exposed, and used to guide the Knower.}

\boxed{
\text{KnowledgeOS} \neq \text{Krishna}
