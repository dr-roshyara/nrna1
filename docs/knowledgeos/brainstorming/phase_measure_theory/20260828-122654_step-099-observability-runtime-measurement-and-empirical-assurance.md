# Step 99 — Observability, Runtime Measurement and Empirical Assurance

We continue exactly from Step 98.

Step 98 established:

$$
\boxed{
Optimize\ negotiable\ properties\
subject\ to\ non\text{-}negotiable\
invariants.
}
$$

But we have accumulated a large set of invariants.

That creates a new problem:

> **How do we know that the running KnowledgeOS actually satisfies them?**

An architecture document can say:

$$
I_{Security}=True
$$

but the production system may behave differently.

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{
Declared\ Architecture
\neq
Observed\ Architecture
}
$$

unless we continuously establish the correspondence.

---

# 99.1 — Architecture must become observable

Let:

$$
A_{design}
$$

be the intended architecture.

Let:

$$
A_{runtime}(t)
$$

be what actually exists at runtime.

Our requirement is:

$$
\boxed{
A_{runtime}(t)
\models
A_{design}
}
$$

for the properties that are supposed to be enforced operationally.

# 99.80 — New invariants

### Runtime conformance

$$
\boxed{
I_{RuntimeConformance}:
Where\ runtime\ conformance\
is\ required,\ observed\
system\ behavior\ must\
remain\ consistent\ with\
the\ applicable\ approved\
architecture\ specification.
}
$$

### Observability integrity

$$
\boxed{
I_{ObservabilityIntegrity}:
Material\ assurance\ claims\
must\ account\ for\ the\
integrity,\ completeness,\
and\ availability\ of\
the\ telemetry\ supporting\
them.
}
$$

### Measurement honesty

$$
\boxed{
I_{MeasurementHonesty}:
Measurements\ must\ retain\
their\ applicable\
sampling,\ uncertainty,\
coverage,\ and\ freshness\
characteristics.
}
$$

### Silence semantics

$$
\boxed{
I_{SilenceSemantics}:
Absence\ of\ telemetry\
must\ not\ be\ interpreted\
as\ evidence\ that\ the\
corresponding\ event\
did\ not\ occur.
}
$$

### Assurance provenance

$$
\boxed{
I_{AssuranceProvenance}:
Every\ material\
assurance\ claim\ must\
be\ traceable\ to\ the\
evidence,\ verification,\
version,\ and\ conditions\
that\ support\ it.
}
$$

### Monitoring meta-assurance

$$
\boxed{
I_{MonitoringAssurance}:
The\ system\ must\ detect\
or\ expose\ material\
failures\ in\ the\
observability\ mechanisms\
upon\ which\ critical\
assurance\ depends.
}
$$

### Drift detection

$$
\boxed{
I_{DriftDetection}:
Material\ divergence\
between\ approved\
architecture/configuration\
and\ observed\ runtime\
must\ become\ detectable\
knowledge.
}
$$

### Assurance freshness

$$
\boxed{
I_{AssuranceFreshness}:
An\ assurance\ claim\ must\
have\ a\ validity\ interval\
appropriate\ to\ the\
rate\ and\ significance\
of\ system\ change.
}
$$

### Invariant monitoring

$$
\boxed{
I_{InvariantMonitoring}:
Material\ runtime\
invariants\ must\ have\
an\ appropriate\
observation\ or\
verification\ mechanism\
where\ operational\
assurance\ requires\ it.
}
$$

### Evidence separation

$$
\boxed{
I_{EvidenceSeparation}:
Runtime\ observations,\
interpretations,\ and\
assurance\ claims\ must\
remain\ distinguishable.
}
$$
