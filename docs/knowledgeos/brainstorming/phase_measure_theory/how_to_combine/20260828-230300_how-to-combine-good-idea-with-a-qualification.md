# The important change I would make

I would treat the current work as:

> **Architecture synthesis → architecture stabilization → book production**

rather than:

> Research → write book → discover architecture while writing.

Your Edition 1 was essentially the first synthesis.

Your Edition 2 is now becoming a **deep architecture exposition and architectural consistency test**.

That is healthy.

But there is one danger.

### The book must not become the architecture-authority mechanism

The hierarchy should remain:

```text
Evidence / corpus
       ↓
Research & reconstruction
       ↓
Formal model
       ↓
Architecture
       ↓
Governance / ratification
       ↓
Book
```

The book explains the architecture.

It does **not** decide the architecture.

That distinction in your current governance model is extremely important.

Otherwise, every interesting discovery made while writing risks silently becoming an architectural change.

Your GN-42 rule is therefore excellent:

> **Edition 2 may explain the architecture more deeply, but may not expand the architecture.**

I would keep that principle very firmly.

That is unusually valuable for an architecture of **knowledge itself**.
