## Executive Summary

**Chapter 4 (Jnana Yoga — The Yoga of Knowledge) provides the complete epistemological foundation for KnowledgeOS.** It is the most directly applicable chapter to the **theory of knowledge itself** — its origin, transmission, preservation, and transformative power.

**The central insights for KnowledgeOS:**

1. **Knowledge has lineage** — provenance is essential to epistemic identity
2. **Knowledge decays over time** — without transmission, it is lost
3. **Knowledge must be restored** — intervention is necessary when lost
4. **Knowledge transforms action** — wisdom changes the meaning of action
5. **Knowledge purifies** — wisdom burns away attachment
6. **Knowledge is the supreme sacrifice** — the highest goal is epistemic excellence
7. **Knowledge requires a teacher** — source trust is foundational
8. **Knowledge cuts doubt** — wisdom enables decision
