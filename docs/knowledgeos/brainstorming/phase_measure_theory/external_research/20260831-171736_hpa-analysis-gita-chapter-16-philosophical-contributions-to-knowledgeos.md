## Executive Summary

**Chapter 16 (Daivasura Sampad Vibhag Yoga — The Yoga of Divine and Demonic Qualities) provides the missing theory of epistemic character, bias, and system integrity.**

While previous chapters gave us knowledge theory (Ch 4), action theory (Ch 5), discipline (Ch 6), epistemology (Ch 7), persistence (Ch 8), and transformation (Ch 18), Chapter 16 gives us the **theory of epistemic virtue and vice** — what makes a knowledge system trustworthy or corrupt.

**The core insight:**

> **A knowledge system is not merely a collection of facts. It has character. It can be divine (Daivi) or demonic (Asuri). Its character determines whether it produces truth or delusion, liberation or bondage.**

This is the **missing ethics and integrity layer** of KnowledgeOS.
