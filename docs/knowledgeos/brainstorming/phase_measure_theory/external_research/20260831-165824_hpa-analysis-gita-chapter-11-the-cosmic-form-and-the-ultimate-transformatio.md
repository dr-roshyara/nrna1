### 1.4 Arjuna's Description (Verses 11.14-31)

**Text:**
```
Arjuna said:
"I can see within your body all the demigods
and the hosts of different creatures.
I can see Brahma seated on a lotus,
Shiva, and all the sages and celestial serpents."

"I can see your infinite form with countless arms,
bellies, faces, and eyes.
I don't see any beginning, middle, or end of your universal form."

"I can see your form, decorated with a crown,
armed with club and discus.
A mass of blazing lights is illuminating all around you,
similar to the lights of the sun."

"You're the eternal absolute truth known by the Vedas.
You're the ultimate abode of all the universe.
You support all the creations and creatures of this universe.
You're the ultimate protector of Dharma."

"You're without any beginning, middle, and end;
Your unlimited power has no limits.
You have infinitely long arms; your eyes are like the sun and the moon.
The entire universe is warmed by the radiance of your emanating rays of light."

"O greatest of all beings, the whole universe is penetrated by you and you alone."

"All the demigods take shelter in you and offer prayer with their folded hands."

"The Rudras, Adityas, Vasus, Vishwadevas, Sadhyas, Ashwini Kumars,
Maruts, Gandharvas, Ancestors, Asuras, Yakshas, and Siddhas
are beholding you and are struck in wonder."

"Oh supreme lord, seeing your immense, magnificent universal form
with countless mouths, eyes, arms, thighs, feet, stomach, and teeth,
all the world and its creatures are terrified, along with me."

"Oh my lord Vishnu, looking upon your brilliant form of countless colors
that are touching the sky, with massive open mouth and blazing eyes,
I tremble from within with fear and can't maintain my peace of mind."

"After seeing your countless mouths with countless teeth that terrifies me,
it resembles the raging fire of the annihilation of the universe."

"I can see that all the sons of Dhritrashtra, along with their royal allies,
including Bhima, Karna, Dronacharya, and also the warriors from our side,
```

### 1.6 Arjuna's Prayer (Verses 11.35-45)

**Text:**
```
Arjuna said:
"Oh, Master of all the senses, It's appropriate that the whole universe praises you.
Demons struck with terror and fear whenever they see you
and fly away in all directions."

"O great one, you're greater than Lord Brahma — who's the creator of this universe,
why should they not bow down before you."

"You're the supreme original divine personality;
You're the sole resting refuge for the entire universe and its beings."

"You're Vayu (the deity of wind), Yamaraj (the deity of death),
Agni (the deity of fire), Varun (deity of water) and Chandra (the moon god)."

"I was not aware of your greatness, my lord.
I addressed you as 'O Krishna,' 'O Yadava,' and 'O Dear Friend.'
I disrespected you while eating, playing, relaxing, and sitting together.
For all of that, I beg forgiveness."

"You're the father of this entire universe and its moving and non-moving creatures.
You deserve worship and are a supreme spiritual teacher.
There's no one equal to you in this whole world."

"Therefore, O Lord, I want to bow down and prostrate in front of you;
I pray for your grace unto me. O Lord, please forgive me."

"I am glad to see your universal form that I have never seen before.
I feel great pleasure, and still, my mind shakes with fear."

"With thousands of arms, O Lord reveals to me your pleasing form
with four arms, carrying the club and Sudarshana Chakra in your hands."
```

### 1.7 The Return to the Pleasing Form (Verses 11.46-55)

**Text:**
```
Krishna said:
"Being pleased with you, I gave you the divine vision,
through which you can see my radiant, infinite, and cosmic form.
Before you, no one has ever seen this universal divine form."

"O best of Kurus, no mortal in this entire world has ever seen this universal form.
Neither by practicing Vedic austerities, nor by sacrifice,
nor by chanting mantras, doing Vedic rituals, or doing any charity work."

"Don't get puzzled or fearful by looking at this enormous, terrifying form of mine.
Stay calm and fearless. Behold once again to see the pleasing form of mine."

"After speaking this, Krishna displayed his pleasing four-armed form.
After that, he resumed back to his gentle two-armed human form,
consoling the fearful Arjuna."

Arjuna said:
"O Lord Krishna, after looking at your pleasing human form,
my mind is peaceful once again, and I have regained my calm."

Krishna said:
"This universal cosmic form is difficult to see.
Even the demigods are eager to catch a glimpse of this form.
Neither by the study of Vedas, nor by penance,
nor by sacrifices and rituals, nor by charity,
can this universal form of mine be revealed."

"Only with pure devotion and Bhakti-Yoga,
can I be entirely known as I am.
Only by pure devotion can one attain the divine vision
and indeed be in union with me."

"My devotees who perform their Karma for my sake,
setting themselves free from all worldly attachments,
who live without any hatred with other creatures and beings,
such devotees surely attain me."
```

The Knower is transformed by the vision, then **returns to the ordinary state** but **transformed**. This is the **complete epistemic cycle**.
