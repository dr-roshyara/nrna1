## 1. Where the earlier KnowledgeOS theory had reached

Before the current v0.2 synthesis, the work had already developed a fairly rich conceptual architecture.

### KnowledgeOS had become an epistemic guidance system

The strongest formulation was:

$$
\boxed{\text{KnowledgeOS = an Epistemic Sārathi guiding the Knower through an evolving knowledge journey}}
$$

rather than an answer engine. 

The associated principle was:

$$
\boxed{\text{See} \neq \text{Guide} \neq \text{Decide}}
$$

with:

* observation/reconstruction;
* KnowledgeOS/Sārathi guidance;
* human Knower decision and action.



That distinction is extremely important because it later became compatible with the formal chain:

$$
Evidence
\neq
Determination
\neq
Proposal
\neq
Decision
\neq
Authorization
\neq
Action.
$$

The earlier Step-era formalization had:

$$

where approximately:

* \(\mathcal A_t\) = assertions;
* \(\mathcal R_t\) = relationships;
* \(\mathcal E_t\) = evidence;
* \(\mathcal H_t\) = history;
* \(\mathcal Z_t\) = Zero findings;
* \(\mathcal L_t\) = candidate dimensions/Lord outputs.



That is historically important because today's formal \(K_t\) did **not** appear from nowhere in Step 049. There was already an earlier conceptual state model.

But the later synthesis correctly discovered that some of those old structures had been renamed, compressed, or replaced. That is why archaeology is important.

\boxed{\text{Zero} \neq \text{Lord} \neq \text{Sārathi} \neq \text{Transition}}

\text{Sārathi}(K_t,\mathcal Z_t,\mathcal L_t,I_t,Q_t,C_t)

That is already remarkably close to what we are now calling **action selection / guidance**, even though the modern formal model deliberately avoids promoting the old lens vocabulary into architectural objects.
