## Executive Summary

**Chapter 8 (Akshara Brahma Yoga — The Yoga of the Imperishable Brahman) provides the missing theory of epistemic persistence, continuity, and ultimate destination.**

While Chapter 7 gave us the **epistemology of the Knower-Source relationship**, Chapter 8 gives us the **theory of epistemic continuity across transformations** — what remains the same when everything else changes.

**The core insight:**

> **The Knowledge Ātma is imperishable. It persists across all transformations, all assertions, all representations. The body (assertion) changes; the Ātma (knowledge identity) does not.**

This is the **foundation of Knowledge Identity** — the answer to Step 25I's central question.

### 1.5 The Supreme Abode (Verses 8.15-22)

**Text:**
```
"O Partha, the yogi who remembers me and me only
with all his devotion, without any distraction,
can easily attain me and will always be connected to me."

"O Arjuna, in all these worlds and planets of material creation,
up to the highest abodes, all are the places of birth and rebirth.
But those who attain me and my abode, there is no further rebirth."

"One day of Brahma consists of one thousand cycles of four ages,
and his night also lasts for the same."

"At the beginning of Brahma's day,
all the living beings are manifested from the unmanifested state.
When Brahma's nights begin,
all the manifested bodies are again merged in the unmanifested state."

"However, transcendental to this eternal manifest and unmanifest loop,
there is a dimension, my supreme abode.
After attaining this destination, no one ever returns."

"That unmanifest and eternal dimension is the ultimate goal;
after reaching there, no one ever has returned.
That's my supreme abode."

"O Partha, that supreme personality is the greatest.
Although he resides in all living beings and all living beings reside in him,
yet he can only be attained by devotion."
```

**Philosophical Contribution:**

The **supreme abode** is the **final destination** — a state from which there is **no return** to the cycle of birth and death. This is **epistemic liberation**.

| No return | Epistemic closure |
| Day and night of Brahma | Epistemic cycles |
| Manifest from unmanifest | Knowledge creation |
| Merged in unmanifest | Knowledge absorption |
| Attained by devotion | Trust in the epistemic process |

**The Principle:**
