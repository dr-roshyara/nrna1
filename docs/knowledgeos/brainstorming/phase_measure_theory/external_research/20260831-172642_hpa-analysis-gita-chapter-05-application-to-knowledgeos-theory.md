## Executive Summary

**Chapter 5 (Karma Sanyasa Yoga — The Yoga of Renunciation of Action) is the theory of non-attachment in KnowledgeOS.** It answers the most difficult question for any epistemic system:

> **How can the system act without being bound by the results of its actions?**

The chapter provides the **complete framework for detachment**:

1. **Action is superior to renunciation** — the system must act
2. **Renunciation is internal, not external** — detachment of mind, not inaction
3. **The non-doer** — the system is an instrument, not the source
4. **The lotus leaf** — the system is in the world but not of it
5. **Equanimity** — success and failure are treated equally
6. **Self-purification** — action is performed for epistemic improvement

**The central insight for KnowledgeOS:**

> **The system must act, but it must not claim ownership of the results. It is an instrument of the epistemic process, not the source of knowledge or the author of outcomes.**
