## Executive Summary

**Chapter 17 (Shraddha Traya Vibhag Yoga — The Yoga of the Threefold Faith) provides the missing theory of epistemic trust, quality classification, and system integrity verification.**

While Chapter 16 gave us the **theory of epistemic character** (Daivi vs Asuri), Chapter 17 gives us the **theory of epistemic trust and quality** — how to classify, evaluate, and verify knowledge based on its source, quality, and intention.

**The core insight:**

> **Faith (Shraddha) is not blind belief. It is epistemic trust that varies by quality (Sattva, Rajas, Tamas). The quality of faith determines the quality of knowledge, action, and outcome. A knowledge system must classify, evaluate, and verify based on these qualities.**

This is the **missing quality assurance and trust verification layer** of KnowledgeOS.

### 1.2 The Threefold Faith (Verses 17.2-4)

**Text:**
```

"Every human being takes birth with faith;

Sattvic, Rajasic, or Tamasic. Now please hear about this."

"Based on their nature of consciousness of mind,
a particular type of faith develops over time.
The nature of faith hence determines what the person is."

"Those in the mode of goodness and have developed the faith
will worship the celestial deities.
Those who have the mode of passion and have faith
will worship their ancestors' spirits.

| **Tamas** | Ignorance | Ghosts, demons | Unverified, suspect sources |

**The Principle:**

$$
\boxed{
\text{Faith}(K_t) \in \{\text{Sattva}, \text{Rajas}, \text{Tamas}\}
}

### 1.5 The Threefold Austerity (Verses 17.14-19)

**Text:**
```
"Worship the supreme consciousness, the Brahmins,
the wise, the spiritual teachers, and the elders —
Do this with purity, cleanliness, celibacy,
sincerity, and non-violence."

"Words that don't cause pain, sorrow and are truthful,
pleasing, and beneficial for others;
Along with the regular recitation of Vedas —
All of this is considered austerity of speech."

"The austerity of mind is silence, gentleness,
self-control, and purity of goals and ambitions."

"When an individual takes up these three kinds of austerity
with firm determination and without any selfish motives,
they can be considered Goodness mode."

"When an individual performs Austerities with the intention of

### 1.7 The Three Words (Verses 17.23-27)

"Those who perform sacrifices, give charities, perform austerity
should always begin it by chanting the syllable 'Om' first."

"Those who don't seek any rewards and have no expectations

sacrifices, austerities, rituals, and charities."

"The word 'Sat' represents the nature of Absolute and the Goodness.
