## Executive Summary

**Chapter 7 (Jnana Vijñana Yoga — The Yoga of Knowledge and Wisdom) provides the missing epistemological framework for KnowledgeOS.**

While Chapter 4 gave us **knowledge theory** (lineage, transmission, purification), Chapter 5 gave us **action theory** (Karma Yoga, non-attachment), and Chapter 6 gave us **discipline theory** (mind control, practice, detachment), Chapter 7 gives us **the theory of the knower's relationship to knowledge itself**.

**Text:**
```
