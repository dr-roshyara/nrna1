## Executive Summary

**YES — the Gītā contains a complete, sophisticated theory of transformation.** It is not merely about "action" (karma) but about **how knowledge transforms the Knower, the action, and the outcome.**

The Gītā's transformation theory is distributed across multiple chapters:

| Chapter | Transformation Type | KnowledgeOS Parallel |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Ch 2 | Self-knowledge transforms the Knower | \( A_t \rightarrow A_{t+1} \) |
| Ch 3 | Action transforms the world | \( T: K \rightarrow K' \) |
| Ch 4 | Knowledge transforms action | \( K + T \rightarrow T^* \) |
| Ch 5 | Renunciation transforms attachment | \( \text{Attachment} \rightarrow \text{Detachment} \) |
| Ch 6 | Discipline transforms the mind | \( \text{Mind} \rightarrow \text{Controlled Mind} \) |
| Ch 7-8 | Knowledge transforms the Knower's identity | \( A_t \rightarrow \mathcal{K}_{\text{ātma}} \) |
| Ch 18 | Complete transformation | \( \text{Confusion} \rightarrow \text{Decision} \rightarrow \text{Action} \) |

**The deepest insight:**

> **Transformation is not merely a change of state. It is a change of identity, understanding, and relationship to knowledge.**
