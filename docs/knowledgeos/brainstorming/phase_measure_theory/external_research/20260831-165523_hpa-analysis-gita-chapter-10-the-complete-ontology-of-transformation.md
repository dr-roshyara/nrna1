## Part 1: The Philosophical Structure of Chapter 10

### 1.1 Arjuna's Confession (Verses 10.1-18)

**Text:**
```
"Listen to these divine teachings, O mighty Arjuna.
I shall speak this to you because I desire your welfare
because you're my dear friend."

"Neither the demigods nor the sages are aware of my origin.
Indeed, I am the source from which those demigods and sages originated."

"The one who knows me as birthless, beginningless,
and the Supreme Lord of the universe,
that person is free from illusion and is free from Karma and all evils."

"From me and me only, all the qualities of humans arise —
intelligence, knowledge, clarity of thought, freedom from delusion,
forgiveness, truthfulness, control over senses, happiness and sadness,
fear and fearlessness, birth and death,
non-violence, equanimity, satisfaction, austerity,
charity, fame and even infamy."

"The seven great sages, the four Kumaras, and the Manus,
from whom all the people of this world have descended,
are a manifestation of my mind."

"The one who knows the truth of my glories and process of yoga
becomes united with me by the process of Bhakti Yoga.
There is no doubt of that."

"I am the source of everything; everything originates from me.
The wise ones know about this, and they worship me
with great faith and devotion."

"Those who have dedicated their life to me
and fixed their minds on me,
those people derive contentment and bliss
in always enlightening others about me."

"Those who worship me with all their love and devotion,
I always bestow supreme knowledge and inspiration upon them
so they can attain me."

"Out of compassion for my devotees,
I, who is there in hearts,
appear and destroy all the darkness that has been born there,
with the light of supreme knowledge."
```
