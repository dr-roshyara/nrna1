# R1 · Source Evidence Register

**Discipline (Step 286 §2):** a `[P]` or `[R]` statement must never silently become `[S]`.
**Constraint honoured throughout:** the supplied edition presents Kṛṣṇa as the Supreme Personality of
Godhead, **not** as an abstract knowledge space. Therefore `Kṛṣṇa → Ω` can only ever be a KnowledgeOS
**research abstraction**, and it is marked as such wherever it appears.

| ID | Locus | `[S]` what the text says | `[C]` what the edition's commentary adds | `[P]` interpretation used here |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **E-01** | Ch. 13 | distinguishes `kṣetra` (field) from `kṣetra-jña` (knower of the field) | the body changes while the knower is distinguished from those changes | a changing field and an agent that knows it are **different categories** |
| **E-02** | **13.3** | **Kṛṣṇa is the knower in *all* fields** | the Supreme knower, distinct from the individual | **the source has TWO knower levels, not one** |
| **E-03** | 2.47 | one's right is to prescribed duty; the fruits/results are distinguished from it | duty is to be performed without proprietorship of results | action and result are **separately identifiable** |
| **E-04** | 2.48 | act while abandoning attachment to success and failure | equanimity in both outcomes | **outcome valence ≠ validity of the act** |
| **E-05** | 6.35 | control of the mind is difficult but achievable through practice and detachment | disciplined repetition | **iteration is a method**, not a single act |
| **E-06** | 6.40 | one engaged in auspicious activity does not meet destruction/degradation | progress is not simply lost | **effort is not annihilated by failure** |
| **E-07** | Ch. 2–18 | `buddhi` / `buddhi-yoga` — intelligence oriented to its stated goal | discriminative intelligence, distinct from raw sense-data | **discrimination is a stage distinct from observation** |
| **E-08** | Ch. 1–18 | Kṛṣṇa acts as charioteer while Arjuna must deliberate and act | the guide does not usurp the warrior's act | **guidance and decision are held by different parties** |
| **E-09** | Ch. 2–18 | `dharma` — role-bound obligation | duty as situated in role and station | **obligation is indexed to role and context** |
| **E-10** | Ch. 7–18 | Kṛṣṇa as Supreme Personality of Godhead | a person, explicitly not an abstraction | 🔴 **blocks any `Kṛṣṇa = Ω` identity claim** |

## Two things the register deliberately does NOT contain

1. **No Sanskrit term is treated as a KnowledgeOS type.** `Kṣetrajña` appears in ratified FA-4, but
   it got there through the architecture's own lineage — R1 records the source, not the ratification.
2. **No verse is cited as authority for a mathematical proposition.** Where a formal result exists,
   it is cited from the corpus (`31.19`, `§256.21`, `I-1`), never from the text.
