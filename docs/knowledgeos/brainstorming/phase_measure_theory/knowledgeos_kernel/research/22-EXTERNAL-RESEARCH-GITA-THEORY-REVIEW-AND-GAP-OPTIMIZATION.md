### What this can and cannot fill

**Recorded as `O-Q1`. Not promoted.**

## 7. Why no gap could be discharged — the structural reason

`18 §10` established: *the Gītā is rich in **distinctions** and empty of **carriers**; a kernel is made
of carriers.* **This review confirms it from the opposite direction.**

**Every gap in the register is a MEMBERSHIP, PROCEDURE or CARRIER question:**
*which operations are mandatory · what procedure decides `≡` · what body does `Qualify` have · what is
`δ`'s commit case · what measure is on `Ω`.*

*transformations act on different carriers · operations may be higher-order · actions have five slots.*

$$\boxed{\textbf{Distinctions sharpen questions. Only carriers close them.}}$$

*having drawn the distinction* for *having supplied the carrier*. **`291 Audit H` names the move —
a status change without a derivation.**
