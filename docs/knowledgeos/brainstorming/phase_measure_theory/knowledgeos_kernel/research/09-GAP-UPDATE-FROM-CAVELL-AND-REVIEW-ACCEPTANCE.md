## 4. ⭐ Cavell reframes `Qualify` — and it may not be `G1` after all

`Qualify`'s status has been **`G1` irreducible** because *"no corpus passage states necessary-and-sufficient qualification criteria."* Cavell suggests the search itself may be malformed:

> `[S]` *"Reasons — at definite points, for definite reasons, in different circumstances — **come to an end**."*
> `[P]` *"The end of reasons is not a failure but a **necessary stopping point**."*
> **Architectural question, verbatim:** *"How does the Kernel recognize and respect the end of justification? Does it treat this as a **gap to be filled**, or as a **fact to be acknowledged**?"*

`DERIVED` — **The corpus has been treating `Qualify`'s missing body as a gap to be filled.** If
justification terminates by construction, then a *total* `Qualify` **cannot exist**, and the correct
object is not a body but a **declared terminus** — structurally the same move the programme already
made for the authority regress (*"the mechanism records authority; it does not grant authority"*).

> **This does not close `Qualify`.** It reclassifies the *question*:
> from *"what are the necessary-and-sufficient criteria?"* (which may have no answer)
> to *"where does qualification legitimately stop, and what records that stopping?"* (which has a
> precedent in this very estate).
>
> **Recorded as a candidate reframing, `[H]`. `Qualify` remains `G1` on the register until tested.**
