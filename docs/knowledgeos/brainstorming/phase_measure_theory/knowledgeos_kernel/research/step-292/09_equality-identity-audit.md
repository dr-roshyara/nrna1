# 09 — Equality and identity — **boundary preserved**

## What Reiter supplies

**Unique-names axioms** for actions and situations:

```
do(a₁,s₁) = do(a₂,s₂)  ⊃  a₁ = a₂ ∧ s₁ = s₂
```

This is **syntactic identity of constructed terms**. It is complete, decidable at the term level, and
**exactly what a history needs**.

## What KnowledgeOS asks

`≡_sem` asks whether two knowledge contents are **semantically equivalent** — a different question at
a different level.

> `[NEG]` **`P5` REFUTED. Reiter provides no bridge from syntactic term identity to semantic
> equivalence, and does not claim to.**

## What must not be inferred

> **`≡_sem` is `OPEN` and NOT refuted.** The standing instruction holds: **it must not be asked a
> question it was not designed for.** Reiter's silence on it is not evidence against it.

## One genuine contribution

`A5` shows **observational equivalence does not imply situation identity** — two situations with
identical fluent values remain distinct.

> `[EXP]` **NEW SYNTHESIS:** this gives the programme a **third** equivalence level to keep apart,
> where it previously tracked two:
>
> ```
> syntactic identity   ≠   observational equivalence   ≠   semantic equivalence
>   (unique names)          (same fluent values)            (≡_sem, OPEN)
> ```
>
> **The middle level was not previously named in the corpus.** Naming it is useful; **it resolves
> nothing**, and `≡_sem` remains exactly as open as before.
