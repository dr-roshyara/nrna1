> ## 🔴 CORRECTED — two mathematical overstatements in this section
> *(Reviewer, 2026-09-01 11:52. Both objections are right, and both are in my own area.)*

> ✅ **This IMPROVES the position: `G-22`'s typing question is LESS blocked than I claimed.**

> **2. I wrote `C19`: *"a knowledge element must be typed as ONE of outcome | random variable | event |
