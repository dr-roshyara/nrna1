### The most important methodological correction

I would regard the equality finding as the central result of these passes.

The corpus does **not** lack equality. It already names:

`=` structural
`≡` semantic
`≈` observational
`≅_λ` provenance-sensitive

The problem is that **three lack sufficient decision machinery**.

That means the next question should *not* be:

> “What other equality should KnowledgeOS invent?”

It should be:

> **“What does each already-ratified equality relation mean operationally, and under what conditions can it be decided?”**

That is exactly analogous to `Qualify`: the vocabulary exists, but the executable/formal body is missing.

### The `D285-5` result should now be quoted narrowly

The defensible statement is:

> There exist distinct operations `o₁ ≠ o₂` and a state `K₀` such that their resulting states are equivalent **under a semantic projection that discards `Π`**.

It should **not** be promoted to:

> `δ` is non-injective.

Because under `≅_λ`, the same witness is injective.

That distinction is important architecturally: **the behaviour of the transformation depends on what information the chosen equality relation preserves.**
