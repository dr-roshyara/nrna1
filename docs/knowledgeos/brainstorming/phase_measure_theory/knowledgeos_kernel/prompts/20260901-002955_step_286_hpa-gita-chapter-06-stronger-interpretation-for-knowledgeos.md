## The really interesting consequence

An AI/knowledge system can be dangerous epistemically **without doing anything malicious**.

It can become its own enemy simply by:

1. receiving an observation,
2. misinterpreting it,
3. transforming its own state,
4. treating that transformation as valid,
5. using the resulting state as the basis for the next transformation.

Then:

$$
K_t
\xrightarrow{\delta_{\text{bad}}}
K_{t+1}
\xrightarrow{\delta_{\text{bad}}}
K_{t+2}
\xrightarrow{\delta_{\text{bad}}}
\cdots
$$

The system can **amplify its own epistemic error**.

That is much more profound than ordinary "bad input."

It means:

$$
\boxed{
\text{Epistemic self-corruption is a property of the knowing mechanism itself.}
}
$$

And Chapter 6 gives us a philosophical lens for precisely that phenomenon.
