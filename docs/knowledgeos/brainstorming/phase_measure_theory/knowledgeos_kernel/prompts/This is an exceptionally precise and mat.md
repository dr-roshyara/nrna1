### 2. Operational Congruence Verification Protocol

Let $\equiv_{\mathcal{K}}$ be the equivalence relation induced on $\mathcal{S}$ by the representation mapping $\rho_{\mathcal{K}_{\min}}(s) = (A(s), R(s), E(s))$:

$$s_1 \equiv_{\mathcal{K}} s_2 \iff \rho_{\mathcal{K}_{\min}}(s_1) = \rho_{\mathcal{K}_{\min}}(s_2)$$

To verify that $\equiv_{\mathcal{K}}$ is an operational congruence for $\Omega$, we prove that for every $o \in \Omega$ and any two states $s_1 \equiv_{\mathcal{K}} s_2$:

1. **Definedness Compatibility:** $o(s_1)$ is defined $\iff o(s_2)$ is defined.
2. **Output Compatibility:** If defined, $o(s_1) \equiv_{\mathcal{K}} o(s_2)$, meaning $\rho_{\mathcal{K}_{\min}}(o(s_1)) = \rho_{\mathcal{K}_{\min}}(o(s_2))$.

### 3. Verification Proofs for Each Operation $o \in \Omega$

#### Theorem D5.1-T1 — Congruence of $\mathrm{Assert}$

* **Definedness:** Precondition is $v \neq \bot$, which depends strictly on operation arguments and is independent of state $s$. Thus, defined for $s_1 \iff$ defined for $s_2$.
* **Output:** Since $A(s_1)=A(s_2)$, $R(s_1)=R(s_2)$, and $E(s_1)=E(s_2)$:

$$\rho_{\mathcal{K}_{\min}}(\mathrm{Assert}(a, v)(s_1)) = (A(s_1) \cup \{a\}, R(s_1), E(s_1)[a \mapsto v]) = \rho_{\mathcal{K}_{\min}}(\mathrm{Assert}(a, v)(s_2))$$


* **Verdict:** $\mathbf{\mathrm{Assert} \text{ IS A CONGRUENCE}}$.

#### Theorem D5.1-T2 — Congruence of $\mathrm{Link}$

* **Definedness:** Precondition requires $a_1, a_2 \in A(s)$ and $v \neq \bot$. Since $A(s_1) = A(s_2)$, $a_1, a_2 \in A(s_1) \iff a_1, a_2 \in A(s_2)$.
* **Output:** Let $e = (a_1, r, a_2)$.
$$\rho_{\mathcal{K}_{\min}}(\mathrm{Link}(s_1)) = (A(s_1), R(s_1) \cup \{e\}, E(s_1)[e \mapsto v]) = \rho_{\mathcal{K}_{\min}}(\mathrm{Link}(s_2))$$


* **Verdict:** $\mathbf{\mathrm{Link} \text{ IS A CONGRUENCE}}$.

#### Theorem D5.1-T3 — Congruence of $\mathrm{Merge}$

* **Definedness:** Precondition requires $a_1, a_2 \in A(s)$ and $a_1 \neq a_2$. Since $A(s_1) = A(s_2)$, precondition holds for $s_1 \iff$ holds for $s_2$.
* **Output:** The transformation constructs $A', R', E'$ strictly as deterministic functions of $A(s), R(s), E(s)$ and the parameters $a_1, a_2, f_E$. Since $\rho_{\mathcal{K}_{\min}}(s_1) = \rho_{\mathcal{K}_{\min}}(s_2)$, the resulting tuple $\rho_{\mathcal{K}_{\min}}(\mathrm{Merge}(s_1))$ is identical to $\rho_{\mathcal{K}_{\min}}(\mathrm{Merge}(s_2))$.
* **Verdict:** $\mathbf{\mathrm{Merge} \text{ IS A CONGRUENCE}}$.

#### Theorem D5.1-T4 — Congruence of $\mathrm{Retract}$

* **Definedness:** Precondition requires $x \in A(s) \cup R(s)$. Since $A(s_1) = A(s_2)$ and $R(s_1) = R(s_2)$, $x \in A(s_1) \cup R(s_1) \iff x \in A(s_2) \cup R(s_2)$.
* **Output:** $R_x$ is derived strictly from $R(s)$, and domain restrictions on $E(s)$ depend strictly on $A(s)$ and $R(s)$. Thus, output representations match identically.
* **Verdict:** $\mathbf{\mathrm{Retract} \text{ IS A CONGRUENCE}}$.
