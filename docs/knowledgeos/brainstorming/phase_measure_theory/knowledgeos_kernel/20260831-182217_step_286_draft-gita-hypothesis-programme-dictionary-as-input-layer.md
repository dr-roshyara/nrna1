Yes. Then I would **change the role of the dictionary substantially**.

The objective of Step 286 should not be to produce a beautiful Gītā–KnowledgeOS dictionary. The dictionary is only the **input layer**. The actual research programme should determine whether any of those proposed correspondences survive scrutiny.

The supplied Gītā is especially important here because it gives us source-grounded distinctions that are stronger than our earlier analogies. # 5. HYPOTHESIS H-K03 — KṢETRA / FIELD

This should be one of the **first serious investigations**.

The source gives us a strong starting point:

```text
kṣetra = field
kṣetra-jña = knower of field
```

The supplied edition explicitly explains the field/knower distinction and notes that the body changes while the knower is distinguished from those changes. 

And 13.3 makes the distinction even more significant by identifying Kṛṣṇa as the knower in all fields. 

### Research hypothesis

> **KnowledgeOS may need an explicit distinction between a changing knowledge field/state and the agent that knows/observes that field.**

Candidate:

$$
K_t \neq A_t
$$

where:

```text
Kₜ = state/field
Aₜ = situated Knower/Actor
```

But do not accept this yet.

### Test

Determine whether:

```text
Kₜ
```

can be defined independently of:

```text
Knower
```

and whether:

```text
Knower
```

can persist while:

```text
Kₜ → Kₜ₊₁
```

changes.

### Important complication

The Gītā itself introduces **more than one level of knower**.

Therefore investigate:

```text
individual kṣetra-jña
        versus
Kṛṣṇa / Supreme knower
```

Do **not** map both automatically to one KnowledgeOS `A`.

This may be one of the most important discoveries of the entire programme.

# 23. THE CENTRAL RESEARCH PRINCIPLE

I would put this at the very beginning of the actual Step 286 prompt:

> **The objective is not to prove that the Bhagavad Gītā explains KnowledgeOS. The objective is to determine whether any Gītā-derived conceptual correspondence survives independent philosophical, mathematical, epistemic, DDD, architectural, and falsification tests.**

And the second principle:

> **A correspondence becomes interesting precisely when it survives without requiring us to distort either the Gītā or KnowledgeOS.**

That is the real research programme.

The supplied text already gives us several unusually strong starting points—especially **field/knower**, **action/result**, **success/failure**, and **knowledge/intelligence/action**. So I would now make **Step 286 a genuine hypothesis-testing programme**, with the dictionary serving only as its **translation/evidence layer**.
