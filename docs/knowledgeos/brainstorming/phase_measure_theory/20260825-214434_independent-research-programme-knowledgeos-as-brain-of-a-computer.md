The research should ultimately produce four independent answers:

### 1. Knowledge

> **What is Knowledge?**

### 2. Knowledge Space

> **What mathematical/semantic structure represents Knowledge and its evolution?**

> **What must a computer system be if it is to operate as a brain over that Knowledge?**
