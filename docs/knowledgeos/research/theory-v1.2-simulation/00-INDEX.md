> predicates, and they disagree on the best case** — a determined, corroborated state is
> `strict=false`, `weak=true`, Kleene `=U`. Theory status: **B. PARTIALLY EXECUTABLE**, for better
> reasons than v1.1.

The three undetermined requirements are `governance`, `temporal`, `operational` — **exactly the
classes v1.2 marks open.** `Zero`'s evaluability is gated by the theory's own open items.

* Of `Zero`'s seven candidate readings: **state** and **missingness representation** are **REFUTED**
  (`Zero` is coarser than the four-way unknown taxonomy); predicate, derived view, boundary
  (three-valued only) and metaphor survive.

  `= ⟹ ≅_λ ⟹ ≡ ⟹ ≈` — and **that order is λ-relative**.
* **Both v1.1 failures persist.** CE-1 factivity is untouched and its premise *weakened*; CE-3
  revision-without-retraction is untouched but better diagnosed.
* Dropping the **source** component of the OPEN observation concept flips determination, attribution
  and `Zero`.
