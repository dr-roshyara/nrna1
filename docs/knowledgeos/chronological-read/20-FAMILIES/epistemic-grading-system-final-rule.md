## Rationale
- **[ANALYSIS/CORRECTION]** [S1194]: Absence-claims ('no document says X') are treated as the weakest evidence class; the programme downgraded two such absence-claims after fuller reading recovered material that sampling had missed. The single absence-claim promoted to a genuine finding (the corpus's lack of any ratified decision) earned that status specifically because it rested on systematic search rather than sampling.
- **[ARGUMENT]** [S1194]: Justifies the whole grading apparatus by an empirical pattern found in the corpus itself: the most confident-sounding documents proved least reliable, while the most reliable results (EXP-01, the Step-050 audit) were exactly the ones that stated their own limits -- so the grading system institutionalizes the corpus's own hardest-won lesson.
