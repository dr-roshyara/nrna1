## Rationale
This object addresses the meta-question of how a set of twelve ratified invariants earned their authority, rather than being asserted for elegance. It traces seven distinct repair genealogies, each pairing a finding (F-n) with a ruling (R-n) or an experimental result to an invariant (I-n): F-1→R-1→I-11 (a self-referential admission finding repaired into the stratification invariant by ruling), F-4→R-4→I-12 (a practiced-but-unstated order named by falsification and then ruled into an axiom), F-3→R-3→the boundary reading of I-4 (Committed demoted from a fourth epistemic rung), EXP-01→I-5/I-6 (the only experimentally grounded rows: a designed test, a recorded verdict, ratification, and an independent GN-46 re-derivation), Omega's dismantling→I-7, Step 109→I-8, and 025d→I-9 (whose own compression finding is left standing in the open, unresolved) [S1386]. The stated pattern across all seven genealogies is that "not one invariant was invented to make the model elegant" — each was read from sources, forced by falsification, or tested [S1386].
