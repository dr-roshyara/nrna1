## Rationale

Shows a single arrow notation A⇒B could denote eight fundamentally different things (logical implication, mathematical theorem, business rule, architectural constraint, causal claim, statistical relationship, heuristic, LLM hypothesis), requiring KnowledgeOS to adopt typed inference semantics. [S0875] States Probability ≠ Logical Implication as one of the most important safeguards against bad AI reasoning, illustrated by conditional failure probabilities not implying a deterministic rule. [S0875] States DerivedKnowledge ≠ DirectObservation and that a derived assertion's epistemic state must not automatically inherit its source's — Support(DerivedConclusion) depends on both Support(Premises) and Trustworthiness(ApplicableRule). [S0875] Defines an inference closure fixpoint Cl_R(K) for finite monotonic rule sets, then argues KnowledgeOS is fundamentally non-monotonic, requiring a defeasible entailment ⊢_def distinct from ordinary ⊢, where a defeasible conclusion may later cease to hold. [S0875] Draws a compiler analogy for the reasoning pipeline (NaturalLanguage→SemanticRepresentation→FormalRepresentation→Reasoner→DerivedKnowledge), argued to be much safer than one opaque end-to-end LLM reasoning step.
