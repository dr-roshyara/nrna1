## Rationale
Asserts this Navya-Nyaya material is more architecturally important than the prior Tarka extraction because it addresses the structure of reasoning (relations) rather than reasoning steps, singling out sambandha (relation) as ontologically primary [S0221]. Argues KnowledgeOS probably requires neither a relational-database-only nor vector-database-only model but a semantic relationship model (Knowledge Graph/Hypergraph with supported-by/bounded-by/derived-through edges), citing Navya-Nyaya's self-presenting representational structure as the architectural precedent [S0221]. Appended full reference table of all 16 padarthas with Sanskrit terms and descriptions, plus the full 12-item Prameya (objects of knowledge) enumeration, presented as background explanation rather than a KnowledgeOS-specific extraction [S0221]. Gives Navya-Nyaya's historical development (Gangesa Upadhyaya, then Raghunatha Siromani/Mathuranatha Tarkavagisa/Jagadisa Tarkalamkara in Navadvipa), its technical vocabulary table (sambandha, avacchedaka, pratiyogin/anuyogin, vyapti, abhava, tadatmya, paryapti, visesana-visesya), a canonical example compound sentence, and its 'unbounded relational depth' (parampara-sambandha) capability [S0221].
