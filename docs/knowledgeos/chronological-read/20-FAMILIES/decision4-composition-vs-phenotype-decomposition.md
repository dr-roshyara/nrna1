## Rationale

Corrects the interpretation of the second-epistemic-standard (S vs S') comparison: since a stricter determination standard changes which cases belong to the determined population P_det, a naive comparison across S and S' would confound population-composition change with genuine phenotype change; decomposes the prediction in advance into (i) the total shift across the full P_det,S vs P_det,S' populations, and (ii) the phenotype-only shift measured on their intersection (holding membership constant), both pre-declared so neither can be selectively reported after seeing which one looks more interesting -- reframing the whole comparison as a contract-relative stratified analysis rather than a pure intervention on a fixed population [S2811].
