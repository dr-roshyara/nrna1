## Sources (how this label entered the ledger)
- **OBJECT-INDEX** batch `B0033`, scope `THEORY-LEVEL`: Step 181's founding principle: when two apparently-valid claims genuinely conflict, the system first records ConflictDetected(C1,C2) rather than declaring either side Wrong -- 'conflict is first an epistemic fact, not an error', the existence of disagreement being itself knowledge about the knowledge state. Rejects two naive resolution rules: 'latest wins' (Timestamp does not imply Truth -- a newer claim from a stale document can be weaker than an older claim from a production test) and 'highest confidence wins' (confidence numbers without semantics -- comparability, calibration, independence, subjectivity, cross-agent provenance -- create false precision; 'confidence alone does not resolve semantic conflict').
