## Rationale
A systematic mathematical audit of S1321 rejects its central framing claim (that Chapter 4 provides a 'complete mathematical foundation' culminating in a formula for KnowledgeOS) and works through specific defects: exponential knowledge decay K_t^decayed = K_0*e^(-lambda*t) has no basis in the verses and lambda is never defined from observations, so 'lineage = useful architectural lens' but 'exponential decay = invented hypothesis'; the memory integral M(t) = integral_0^t K(tau) dtau is unjustified unless K(tau) is defined as measurable and the measure and meaning of integration are specified, and is replaced by a cleaner subset relation AccessibleHistory_t subset-or-equal HistoricalState_t; the intervention threshold I(t)=1 iff Delta(K_t,I_t)>theta invents an arbitrary scalar threshold with no textual basis, so 'condition-triggered intervention' is retained as a research lens while 'the specific threshold equation' is rejected as a derivation; the multiple-evidence-paths summation (sum W_i = Total Evidence) is judged particularly problematic because the corpus's own EXP-01 work already established that dependency and evidence relationships matter before aggregation and a simple sum can double-count dependent evidence, so the formula is 'not acceptable as an evidence calculus without a dependence model'; the Faith(K_t)=Confidence(K_t)/Uncertainty(K_t) ratio is rejected because it requires undefined semantics for both quantities and becomes undefined when Uncertainty=0 (the code's return of infinity is called 'a programming convention, not a mathematical derivation'); the wise-state vector W=(0,0,C,infinity) is rejected as not a valid mathematical structure until the vector space, coordinates, codomain, and whether infinity is legitimate in that space are all defined, and should be treated as metaphorical notation; and the final closing equation KnowledgeOS = Wisdom*Action/(Doubt+Attachment) is rejected outright: no defined units or domains, no derivation, no normalization, no reason KnowledgeOS should be a scalar, a possibly-zero denominator, and not following from any prior equation -- called 'a metaphorical synthesis presented in equation form,' exactly what the mathematical verification protocol must prevent.
