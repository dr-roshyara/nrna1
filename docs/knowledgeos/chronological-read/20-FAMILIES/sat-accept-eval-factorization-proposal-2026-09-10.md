[S2921] (DISTINCTION/ALTERNATIVE): Argues Eval(K,r)=Unknown must NOT automatically imply Sat(K,r)=0, invoking the boxed invariant Unknown != False != ProbabilityZero as already protected by the historical corpus. Presents two undecided candidate architectures for the Eval-to-Sat relation: Architecture A (binary Sat:KxR->{0,1} that is merely PARTIAL/undefined when acceptance isn't determinable) versus Architecture B (three-valued Sat:KxR->{top,bot,U}, noted to already exist as a later historical candidate formulation in the corpus). States the document's central insight: Sat is NOT the first missing object -- the real missing semantic boundary is the triad Eval + AcceptanceCondition + Accept, surrounded by the still-unresolved identities of r, Gamma, and EC.
