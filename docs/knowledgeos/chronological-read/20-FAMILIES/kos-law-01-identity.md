## Rationale
Special Area C ruling: Similarity != Identity is the identity law's limit case; removing it lets extension->identity at any degree of match, up to perfect coextension, collapsing KnowledgeOS into exactly the similarity engine it refuses to be; 'there is no degree of observable match at which the corruption is absent' (a decisive failure test). Consequence: one of the strongest KERNEL rows -- its deep form (Representation != Identity) carried by law 1; the typed encoding is representation, deferred [S0239]. LLM-comparison validation (5 candidates, validated not assumed): persistent epistemic identity -- FUNDAMENTAL ABSENCE (a stateless generative model cannot possess it by construction; scaffolding like RAG cannot grant identity, only look up stored identities); historical belief evolution -- FUNDAMENTAL ABSENCE (generation is stateless, no belief to evolve); provenance preservation, contradiction management, justification accountability -- each a DEGRADATION, not an absence (an LLM can be scaffolded to approximate each, weakly). Conclusion: the constitutional justification rests on exactly the two fundamental absences (mapping to laws 1 and 11), not a general 'LLMs are bad at X' claim [S0239]. Core challenge: the Kernel identity invariant assumes identity is fixed (immutable Value Object), intrinsic (property of the entity), and assigned/forced (mechanism-imposed); Daoist ziran ('self-so', opposite of wei/forcing) suggests identity is instead transformational, contextual, and natural, drawn from Daodejing ch.25/37/51 and Zhuangzi ch.2/13 plus Ames & Hall, Moeller, and Ziporyn scholarly interpretation [S0455].
