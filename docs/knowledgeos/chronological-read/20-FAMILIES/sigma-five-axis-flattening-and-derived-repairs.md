## Rationale
The central problem this label addresses is a long-unexplained conflict between an older 10-value Σ₀ epistemic-status vocabulary and a newer five-axis product structure Σ=(A,S,R,V,C). It reports that this apparent 4-vs-10 (or 7-vs-10) conflict "dissolves": the 10-value set is simply a flattening of the five-axis product space, with 7 of its 10 values mapping cleanly onto the product (Satisfied→S=Strong/VeryStrong, Conflicted→C=Active, Stale→V=Stale, etc.) and the remaining 3 (NotApplicable, Prohibited, Missing) turning out not to be epistemic-status values at all — a scope predicate, a policy verdict, and an inquiry-layer concept respectively [S2113, S2114, S2128]. This factorization is explicitly credited to a source document ("Q4A") dated 2026-08-26, which the rationale notes predates the entire later verification programme that had rediscovered the corresponding gaps [S2128]. A second thread addresses a genuine expressive defect: the single-polarity Support axis cannot state "the evidence refutes this" (verified by corpus grep showing Refut*/Disconfirm* never appear as Support values) [S2128], and a proposed repair — composing Q4A's five-axis structure with a separately-corpus-native (Step 272B) polarity powerset P({Support,Refute}) — resolves this, but the composition itself is explicitly graded DERIVED, not corpus-native or ratified, and the label repeatedly warns this repair "should not be promoted to architecture" [S2113, S2114]. A third thread works out an observational-equality parameterization Σ₁ ≈_X Σ₂ (agreement on axis-subset X), reducing the governance question from "invent an observational equality" to "choose among 32 candidate axis-subsets" — again explicitly not itself a governance decision [S2113, S2114]. Later review rows in the same label progressively tighten and qualify these findings: distinguishing label-projection equivalence from behavioural observational equivalence [S2122], correcting "the axes ARE the observations" to a weaker bounded-candidate-space claim [S2121], and correcting "Σ is never stored" from a universal claim to a constraint on one specific storage representation [S2117, S2123].
