- [S0847] (INVARIANT/ARGUMENT) States Conclusion≠Truth as fundamental: even Γ⊨P only means P follows from Γ under the specified formal system, not that the premises describe reality correctly, giving LogicalValidity≠EmpiricalTruth — 'one of the most important mathematical safeguards in the architecture'. - [S1646] (ALTERNATIVE) Two competing, explicitly undecided models for relationships: Model A (Relationship is reducible to/a subset of Proposition, so P=(x,d,v) suffices) versus Model B (Relationship is a separate primitive, requiring the proposition universe to split into attribute-propositions and relation-propositions); the choice is explicitly deferred pending counterexamples rather than made by preference.
- [S1646] (ARGUMENT/CONSTRAINT) Context must not leak into the Proposition type: although context c is independently necessary at the Assertion level (an executed removal test found it irrecoverable), inserting it into P would collapse the proposition/assertion distinction — P remains the semantic proposition, c remains the situational assertion context.
- [S1646] (ARGUMENT/CONSTRAINT) Evidence must not leak into the Proposition type: an executed removal test found evidence cannot be derived from proposition content, so P is not (P,e) — evidence attaches to the assertion, preserving that the same proposition can appear with different evidence across different assertions.
- [S1646] (ARGUMENT/CONSTRAINT) Major result: temporal validity t must not leak into P, because an executed counterexample found a single proposition can have two distinct validity intervals (requiring the system to wrap propositions in separate assertions to represent both) — temporal multiplicity is an assertion-level phenomenon, not a proposition-identity phenomenon.
- [S1649] (COUNTEREXAMPLE/ALTERNATIVE) Concrete relationship-representation counterexample: the corpus validly represents a relationship both as a dimension/value pair inside a proposition (P=(Bhishma,RelationshipToArjuna,Grandfather), 'Representation A') and as the separate first-class eight-field Relationship construct r=(E1,E2,T,R,Q,E,Sigma,tau) ('Representation B'), which can additionally carry relationship-specific evidence, epistemic status, validity, and qualifiers that the compact triple cannot; conclusion is that a relationship may need an Assertion-level wrapper even when its semantic core reduces to a proposition.
- [S1649] (ANALYSIS) DDD-flavored interpretation assigning each component of P=(E,D,V) a distinct responsibility/question: Entity=subject ('what is being described'), Dimension=semantic predicate/axis ('along which axis'), Value=typed object of the predicate ('what position') — analogous to, but explicitly distinct from, RDF or classical subject-predicate-object logical syntax.
- [S1664] (ANALYSIS/LIMITATION) Per-object computability audit for Entity, Dimension, Value and Proposition: Entity's semantic definition ('subject of knowledge') is conceptually clear but membership is not universally decidable without an explicit ontology/registration boundary; Dimension's structural validity is computable but semantic adequacy is not; Value membership is computable only where its value space V_D has an effective representation, ranging from trivially decidable (finite sets) through grammar-parseable (formal version strings) to fundamentally undecidable (open-ended conceptual domains); Proposition well-formedness is therefore CONDITIONALLY COMPUTABLE, contingent on all three membership predicates being computable.
