## Rationale
Reaffirms that differing contexts (production vs staging) can make apparently conflicting values non-contradictory, restating that many apparent contradictions are missing-dimension problems. [S0864] Argues classical AGM-style (consistency-preserving) belief revision is insufficient alone, since KnowledgeOS may intentionally retain inconsistent information; needs a more general evidence-based, contradiction-tolerant revision model. [S0864] Shows an apparent architecture-decision contradiction (feasible vs unacceptable risk) resolves into a multi-dimensional decision problem once separated into distinct dimensions (TechnicalFeasibility, OperationalRisk) — conflict detection can reveal a modeling deficiency. [S0864] Requires temporal context be part of conflict detection, since two differently-timed version assertions are not contradictory — reaffirming the earlier SourceTime/AcquisitionTime/ValidityTime separation. [S0864] Argues retraction can propagate through dependency (Retract(A)→Reassess(B) when B was inferred from A), making the provenance graph part of the semantic dependency model, not merely an audit feature. [S0864] Argues revision is computationally tractable for finite knowledge graphs by recomputing only the reachable affected subgraph Reachable(e,G_K), conditional on the inference/semantic-comparison policies themselves being computationally bounded.
