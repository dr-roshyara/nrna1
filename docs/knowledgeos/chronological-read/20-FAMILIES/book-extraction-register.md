## Rationale
- [S0704] (ANALYSIS/GOVERNANCE) Across all book-extraction sources the recurring disposition is uniform: extract the distinction the corpus can use, and refuse the source's ontology as Kernel content -- nothing in the register proposes a Kernel member, several explicitly forbid one. This uniformity is itself evidence that the book-extraction block is not where the corpus's competing Kernel models originate; every competing Kernel formulation recorded elsewhere came from P4 reasoning rounds or P1/P7 project documents, never from book extraction.
- [S0704] (ARGUMENT) Williamson (Knowledge and Its Limits): Knowledge is a first-class architectural category, not merely a computed combination of lower-level metadata -- opposing every 'derived' treatment in the corpus; and 'all knowledge is evidence', a stronger claim than anything else in the corpus, which -- if true -- makes the evidence member and the knowledge object partly collapse, cutting against the corpus's Entity/VO framing by making that distinction gradual rather than sharp. Recorded as a source claim, not adopted.
- [S0704] (ANALYSIS) Wittgenstein's Tractatus (as extracted in this register) contains no Kernel disposition sentence, verified by pattern search; its distinctions (fact != thing, sense != truth, saying != showing, name != proposition) were already carried elsewhere in the corpus's review of the same material -- recorded as no material new finding.
- [S0704] (ANALYSIS) Four further second-pass-audit sources add arrivals without new findings: Shannon & Weaver is placed as an eighth formalism-as-regime (S1-F032), its INFORMATION != MEANING != KNOWLEDGE chain already recorded (S1-F026); PRML is a third arrival at the already-recorded 'latent structure inferred from observation' pattern (S1-F014, S1-F032); a statistical-learning companion document pairs a qualitative vocabulary of problem-solving operations with formal pattern-discovery machinery, consistent with 'store the substrate, compute the measure' (S1-F038); and the Swar Gyan/dhatu source contributes only an orthographic-decomposition linguistic pipeline -- mechanism only, with no Kernel disposition, and the document itself notes it is not classical dhatu extraction.
