## Rationale
Final strategic assessment: an Epistemic OS could simplify KnowledgeOS (formal belief-revision foundations, consistency enforcement, automated contradiction detection, justification transparency) but could also complicate it (formal-logic overhead, mandatory justification graphs, unnecessary multi-agent complexity); recommendation is context-dependent adoption as an AI-reasoning-component lens only. [S0215] Reviewing the S0215 external research document, concludes KnowledgeOS should not itself become an Epistemic Operating System, but may contain an Epistemic OS as a capability -- avoiding the architectural mistake of collapsing KnowledgeOS into a belief engine. [S0216] Refines an earlier 'KnowledgeOS as logical brain' framing: it should not be the brain that believes, it should be the brain that controls reasoning integrity, mapping the three required epistemic subsystems (belief management, contradiction detection, truth constraint enforcement) onto an Epistemic Control Layer beneath the KnowledgeOS Constitutional Layer.
