The evidence base attributes the following rationale to this object (as recorded, cited to its source):

- **[ANALYSIS/EXTENSION]** [S0186]: Traces four successive historical architecture 'generations' of PKS/EKS/KnowledgeOS naming and positioning, showing a reversal from a certified repository-as-system-of-record position through a PostgreSQL-centric proposal and back to a repository-centric reconciliation.
- **[ANALYSIS/DEFINITION]** [S0206]: Single most important structural finding of the baseline: EKS has no runtime, only mechanisms -- no service, daemon, scheduler, queue, database, or deployment unit exists; every EKS behaviour is a CLI program invoked by a human or AI process plus a set of repository files, and nothing in EKS runs unless something outside it runs it.
- **[ANALYSIS/WARNING]** [S0206]: Third major finding, first-hand and not stated in any prior document: the Governance/Workflow subsystem (last active 2026-08-21, gitignored, 29 tests, no CI gate) and the Observation/Assurance subsystem (last active 2026-08-04, git-tracked, 43+252 tests, one non-blocking CI gate) have inverse operational vitality -- the subsystem carrying authority/ownership/lineage is untracked, while the subsystem carrying advisory metrics is durably persisted.
- **[ANALYSIS/RESTATEMENT]** [S0206]: Summary verdict of the whole baseline: a joining Principal Architect could understand EKS's mechanisms (sections 8-11, 15-16) precisely enough to run and extend them, but not its domain (sections 6-7, 9 show unmodelled language, unassigned invariant ownership, and only-candidate boundaries) -- 'EKS's conceptual model is more mature than its executable boundaries, and its executable mechanisms are more mature than its domain model,' both true simultaneously.
- **[ANALYSIS/EXTENSION]** [S0206]: An inferred (class B, implemented-but-implicit) purpose is proposed for EKS, stated by no document itself: to make multi-actor (human+AI) engineering work adjudicable after the fact, so any past act can be traced to the authority that permitted it and the evidence that justified it -- explicitly labelled as an inference, not a finding.
- **[EXPERIMENTAL-RESULT/ANALYSIS]** [S0206]: Measured operational state: six observation/assurance streams (lcom4, recommendations, outcomes, assessments, watch-usage, test-presence.jsonl) last recorded activity 2026-08-03/04, dormant 17-18 days; the governance/workflow runtime and session logs were active the same day (2026-08-21). 'The subsystem that is durably persisted is the one that is dormant; the subsystem that is untracked is the one that is alive' (proposition P-6).

- **[ANALYSIS/PRINCIPLE]** [S0206]: The 15 recorded Architectural Strengths (S-1..S-15, e.g. refusal/ambiguity/UNKNOWN as first-class tested outcomes; state always the fold of an append-only log; 324 green tests; contract tests asserting behaviour never implementation shape; structural rather than promissory guarantees; single interpretation authority with honest refusal; independent re-implementation as specification assurance; authority separated from lifecycle by construction; recommendation≠decision≠outcome≠assessment; repeated self-disclosure against interest; absence never implies permission; rules-as-data; genuine hexagonal discipline; self-measured non-use) share one shape: 'every one of them is a place where EKS made a guarantee structural instead of stated... where EKS is strong, it is because a rule was made impossible to break rather than important to follow. Where EKS is weak, the rule is stated and unenforced.'
- **[RESTATEMENT/ANALYSIS]** [S0206]: Closing maturity-by-layer assessment: contract discipline, deterministic-assurance implementation, and honesty of self-description all rate 5/5 stars; governance conceptual model (documented) rates 4/5 but unratified; governance tactical model, persistence/durability, temporal model, and separability/reuse all rate 1/5. The three sentences a joining Principal Architect most needs: (1) everything EKS knows is in files, everything it decides is recomputed from those files, and the most important files are not in git; (2) EKS refuses illegal writes to its own record and prevents nothing else -- its authority is evidentiary, not operative, by recorded design; (3) where EKS has invariants it has no types, and where it has types it has no authority -- so folder-structure boundaries are not the boundaries the invariants imply.
