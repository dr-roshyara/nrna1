## Rationale

Candidate C is refined into two sub-forms: C1 (collapsing Asked+Absent into Unknown, rejected because E4's own failure is evidence against that assumption) and C2 (a typed epistemic state Sigma*=IxE), with an explicit warning that elegance alone cannot justify accepting C — later executed and found isomorphic to, but less minimal than, Repair B [S1856]. A new equality consequence is derived: because inquiry state becomes observable under the repair, a difference in inquiry state between two knowledge states is sufficient by itself to establish observational non-equivalence, connecting the missingness repair directly to the pre-existing observational-equality theory [S1856]. Five falsification tests (F1, F3, F5, F6, F10) are identified as affected because they interact with absence, policy, or historical semantics, and must be re-run under the chosen repair — later executed 5/5 PASS [S1856]. A mathematician's-lens review reframes the seven mandatory states as not a genuinely new Sigma space but Sigma0=P({Support,Refute}) with its zero element (0,0) resolved into three refinements (M1-M3) plus a structural condition (M7) sitting outside Sigma entirely; the repair belongs specifically at the (0,0) collapse point, not spread across all of Sigma [S1857]. An executed isomorphism proof then shows Repair B's inquiry-register content and Repair C2's typed-epistemic-state content are the same object in different notation, differing only in placement — C2 folds the inquiry bit into Sigma, B keeps Sigma untouched [S1862]. The Step-281 exec/ run's environment is recorded as a fixture literal (executed 2026-08-30T23:49:31+02:00, Python 3.13.2), consistent with the corpus-wide reproducibility discipline of recording rather than consuming wall-clock time [S1873]. The reason the missingness defect reached into the policy/authority layer at all is explained: before the repair, "no applicable policy" and "never enquired" produced the identical observation, exactly why F1 and F5 needed re-running [S1875]. Because Repair B places Q_t outside K rather than mutating a K-component, invariant preservation becomes a typing fact rather than a proof obligation — a structural argument favoring Repair B over Candidates A and C2 [S1879]. The repair closed exactly the two gaps it targeted (T-1, T-2) but slightly worsened the empirical evidence picture, since the real EKP has no inquiry register, so Q_t is verified only at Level 4, adding a 17th Level-4-only result to the existing 16 [S1881].
