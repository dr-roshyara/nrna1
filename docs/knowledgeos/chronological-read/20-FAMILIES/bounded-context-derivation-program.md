## Rationale
Derives an architectural distinction from a Gita-lens observation about continuous identity across changing embodied states: EntityId != CurrentState, since an entity can pass through S1->S2->S3 without becoming a different entity -- explicitly framed as a conceptual lens reinforcing a DDD distinction, not a metaphysical claim ('not a proof of reincarnation'). Extends this with a second observation (a new state does not necessarily possess the knowledge of previous states): CurrentState_t = pi(History_{<=t}), where the projection pi may intentionally forget information, making 'forgetting' a first-class architectural phenomenon; derives the three-level epistemic hierarchy Actor-knowledge subset-or-equal System-memory subset-or-equal Reality, proposed as a recurring architectural principle. Explicitly guards against collapsing the analogy into identity, boxing the rule chain 'philosophical analogy -> architectural question -> formal model' and stating 'we should never write KnowledgeOS is Krishna' [S1503]. Delivers a graded verdict over the seven candidate contexts: Evidence, Assessment, Governance, and Execution classified Strong; Decision and Observation classified Strong-but-requires-further-analysis; Identity/Lineage/Versioning/Correlation reclassified as cross-cutting concerns rather than contexts in their own right; and Model classified not yet justified as a separate context pending examination of the actual model registry and lifecycle. Adds the explicit architectural caution that a bounded context is not required to become a microservice -- it may be implemented as a module, package, component, service, or deployment unit -- boxing BoundedContext != Microservice as the DDD boundary and the deployment boundary are separate decisions [S1503].
