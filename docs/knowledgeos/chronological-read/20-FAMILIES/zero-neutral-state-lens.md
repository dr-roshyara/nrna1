## Rationale

Zero-Lens result on Williamson: after removing beliefs, documents, language, databases, probabilities, possible worlds, embeddings, topics, LLMs, what survives is minimal: 'A participant is related to a proposition/content in a world/context/time such that the proposition is true and the participant is in a factive state of knowing.' Knowledge itself should not be reduced to its representations [S0561]. Zero-Lens extraction on Brandom (removing language/English/sentences/speech/Frege/Wittgenstein/philosophy) yields ten survivor claims: participants make commitments; commitments have inferential consequences; some commitments are supported/entitled; commitments can conflict; participants assess one another's commitments; new actions alter the state; implicit relations can become explicit; concepts are characterized partly by inferential roles; objectivity can emerge within social assessment; a claim is not merely a string but a position in a normative/inferential structure [S0562]. Zero-Lens extraction on Brachman & Levesque (removing FOL/Prolog/frames/description logic/SAT/default logic/Bayesian networks/STRIPS/GOLOG) yields twelve survivor claims: an underlying world/domain exists; a participant has beliefs/knowledge about it; only part is explicitly represented; representation is a mapping between domains; reasoning derives implicit content from explicit content; meaning depends on interpretation/context; different representation languages have different expressive power; reasoning capability is resource-limited; knowledge changes and can be revised; uncertainty/vagueness require richer representations; the same knowledge may have different representations; action depends on knowledge — described as extraordinarily close to the current model [S0566]. Zero-Lens applied across both phases: after stripping optional attributes, what survives universally is a knower/participant, something known, a ground, and a record that the two were connected — every phase and nearly every model retains those four; nothing else survives universally. The corpus distinguishes 'nothing exists' from 'we do not know' (0258: 'Knowledge is not only about what exists'; formal law §9: unknown≠absent≠false). Whether UNKNOWN is the absence of Knowledge or a state of an identified object is NOT resolved by the corpus: formal law (⟨Z-1⟩) says UNKNOWN/ABSENT/FALSE/REJECTED are states of an identified epistemic object, while kernel document 125708 §17 pushes the other way ('UNKNOWN must not be a single epistemic state', eight readings) — no new state proposed here [S0577]. Zero as the epistemology of absence (Vedic-zero lens era, 20260822-103624) was, inside the formal track, transformed into the goal-gap function (CON-01); on the repository side the sibling sense was instead RATIFIED as meta-principle Z-KOS-001 — resulting in the three-sense Zero naming collision, a live naming hazard for Final Architecture [S1113].
