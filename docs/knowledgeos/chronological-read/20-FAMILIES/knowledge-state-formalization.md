- [S1684] (ANALYSIS/LIMITATION) A table of eleven operations over K (member, add, merge, supersede, contradicts, dedup, Sigma, Gamma, delta, History, Lineage) with signatures, preconditions, postconditions, determinism and failure modes shows three have no defined body: dedup does not exist as an operation, Sigma's strength rule is absent, and delta's body is undefined for the commit case.
