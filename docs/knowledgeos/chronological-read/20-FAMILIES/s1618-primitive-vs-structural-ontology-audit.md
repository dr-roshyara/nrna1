## Rationale
- **[ARGUMENT/ANALYSIS]** [S1618]: Identifies the historical 8-primitive candidate set K8=(E,S,V,O,P,R,Pi,A) as heterogeneous by category (mixing objects, states, occurrences, relations, normative constraints, and executable activities), not yet a homogeneous primitive type set.
- **[ANALYSIS]** [S1618]: Rates Event and Observation as the strongest primitive candidates in the set: Event is distinct from State and stable (with the caveat domain events must not be conflated with technical event messages); Observation's removal would destroy the already-established Observation!=Evidence distinction, giving it strong (though not mathematically proven) primitive status.
- **[CORRECTION/ALTERNATIVE]** [S1618]: Sharpens the open Claim-vs-Proposition question (S1616) into a specific alternative: rather than Claim=Proposition, propose Claim=Role(Proposition) via a Claims(actor,proposition) relation, since a proposition can exist unasserted -- judged more DDD-consistent than plain identification.
- **[ARGUMENT/PRINCIPLE]** [S1618]: States a critical mathematical/DDD principle: a universal relation encoding can technically represent many proposed primitives (Identity, Supports, DerivedFrom, Supersedes, Causes) without preserving their specific semantics (occurrence, temporal identity, causal interpretation, replay semantics) -- representability is not proof of ontological reducibility, so Relation is reclassified as a possible mathematical construction mechanism rather than a domain primitive in its own right.
- **[ARGUMENT/CORRECTION]** [S1618]: Reclassifies Policy and Action as unlikely K-primitives: Policy is better modeled as a constraint on operations rather than a knowledge object; Action is likely outside the epistemic substrate entirely (knowledge informs action but does not execute it), though this does not diminish Action's importance elsewhere in the architecture.
- **[ANALYSIS/OPEN-QUESTION]** [S1618]: Downgrades three historically proposed derived-concept equations to conditional/unresolved status: Evidence=QualifiedObservation is conditional pending a defined qualification function; Identity=EntityIdentityRelation risks representing identity without defining it (a relation is not automatically the same as semantic identity, unless equivalence properties are proven); Provenance=TypedDependencyRelation remains unresolved since Step 246's (S1601) open provenance-minimality question is unresolved.
- **[ALTERNATIVE/FORMALIZATION]** [S1618]: Poses 4 mathematically coherent but mutually exclusive candidate ontologies for the carrier of K (Knowledge-as-primitive-object, risking circularity; Knowledge-as-structured-configuration over (objects,relations,qualifications,history), risking unproven reinvention of an earlier kernel; Knowledge-as-typed-graph, conflating representability with ontological necessity; Knowledge-as-opaque-algebraic-state, elegant for transitions but silent on the primitive-object question), none yet justified as canonical by the corpus.
- **[ARGUMENT/CORRECTION]** [S1618]: Explains why equality and transition could not be finalized in earlier steps: both depend fundamentally on which of the 4 candidate ontologies (set/graph/provenance-sensitive/observational) is chosen for K -- set equality, graph isomorphism, provenance-equivalence, and behavioral equivalence are each appropriate under a different ontology, so neither equality nor transition can be specified independently of that prior choice, a genuine mathematical dependency rather than a glossary gap.
