## Rationale
Central closure-taxonomy finding: testing four candidate closure mechanisms (finite enumeration, closed type/schema, declared admissibility predicate, governance boundary) against the primary corpus shows a typed five-kind schema already exists and 277 explicitly calls the operation-kind classification CLOSED (with minimality left OPEN); the actually-missing item is a rule determining which operations are mandatory, not an enumeration of possible operations. [S2179] Separates six properties that closure of O/T might bear on for the candidate relation equiv_K: definability (does NOT require O/T closure - the formula is well-formed for any O_K), while totality, decidability, canonicality, computability and operational completeness all DO require it; concludes 'equiv is blocked on O_K' as previously stated was imprecise - the definition is not blocked, but five of six other properties are. [S2179] States the artifact's central corrective finding: prior steps' claim that 'O is not closed' materially narrows to 'O's operation-KIND classification is closed (277); its MANDATORY MEMBERSHIP is not' - the blocker is a membership rule, not an enumeration. [S2182] Classifies eight identified O/T/O_K-related blockers by type: O's mandatory-membership rule, O_K's extension, and T's membership are NORMATIVE; the O glyph overload and 261.21's self-reference are DOCUMENTARY (repairable without any decision); the missing kind-5 (observation) schema is TYPE-THEORETIC; Admissible(o,K) being named-but-unevaluable is FORMAL/DERIVATIONAL (routed to gap G1); delta's commit case is also FORMAL/DERIVATIONAL. [S2182] Tests eight candidate derivation routes for O/T mandatory membership (from K - circular; from invariants - circular; from a minimality proof - blocked, 259 forbids it pre-congruence; from the ratified 8 primitives - under-specified, they are primitives not operations; from the forced lower bound - only partially derivable, a candidate set not a membership rule; from 259.7's five-kind schema - partially derivable, the schema itself is derived and 277 calls classification closed; from Admissible(o,K) - blocked, routed to gap G1; declaring mandatory membership directly - the only route with no unmet precondition, classified NORMATIVE), concluding no derivation route reaches operation membership from already-ratified material. [S2188] Classifies the overall O/T governance-vs-derivation boundary as Case E (MIXED) and splits the result into three categories: derivable (the five-kind schema, 8 typed signatures, T subset O, equiv_K's definability, the 258-A congruence criterion), engineering/documentary TODOs needing no decision (the O glyph overload, 261.21's self-reference, registering TraceOrigin/ExplainRevision, a kind-5 type), and genuine decisions (N-4 mandatory membership, N-3 O_K's extension, N-2 Decision-3's residue, N-1' ratify the 261.21 candidate).
