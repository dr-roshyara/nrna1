## Rationale
Step 215's own stated purpose is to shift from generating new theory to reconstructing the evidentiary basis of an existing 182-step corpus, motivated by the observation that a conversational "step" is not necessarily an architectural contribution (StepCount ≠ KnowledgeCount) [S1527]. Its deeper argument identifies a recurring pattern across ten candidate distinctions (Identity≠State, Knowledge≠Authority, etc.) as instances of one common structural mistake — collapsing A≈B where a genuine distinction A≠B matters — proposed as possibly "one of the deepest architectural themes in the entire book" [S1527]. However, an independent verification audit (S1541) found this rationale itself under-evidenced: the recurrence claim is asserted in the indicative without a single cited instance, and the exact metric the file itself proposes to substantiate it (Convergence(I)) is computed for zero of the ten principles — "DEFINED but not INPUTS_KNOWN, not CONSTRUCTIBLE, not COMPUTED" [S1541]. The audit's own rationale for flagging this so heavily is that Step 215 explicitly exempts itself (and seven predecessor documents) from the evidence-labeling discipline ([F]/[D]/[P]/[U]) that Step 213 declares mandatory for exactly these reconstruction documents — a "performative contradiction" the audit rates HIGH severity [S1541]. A later document (S1543) contextualizes the file's most serious formal defect (the ill-typed SemanticIntegrity∝DistinctionPreservation proportionality) as the fourth independently-committed instance of the same measurement-theoretic error across four unrelated bands of the corpus, "none aware of the others" [S1543], while a still-later document (S1551) documents an uncited self-repair of that same defect four steps later, in Step 219 [S1551].
