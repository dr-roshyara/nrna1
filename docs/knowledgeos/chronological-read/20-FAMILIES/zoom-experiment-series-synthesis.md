## Rationale
Synthesizes the whole experiment series: Zoom-in's two candidate meanings (restriction/descent vs inquiry-zoom) are shown to be genuinely different operations, not two implementations of one Zoom-in, since restriction plateaus/loses information while inquiry-zoom drives determination toward ~100% at tested budgets; Zoom-out, in contrast, was never established across three experiments -- KR-ZOOM-OUT-01 failed its calibration gate, KR-ZOOM-OUT-02 diagnosed the cause as generator-parameter insensitivity, and KR-ZOOM-OUT-03 exposed a deeper degeneracy (the primary estimand A5=0 was structurally forced, later successfully caught by the O-F* control) plus further negative results (M1's dominance was a population-composition artifact, M0 replicated descriptively but cannot be interpreted causally, FR-004's predicted seventh convergent instance failed, the S/S' intersection component was structurally invariant) -- concluding Zoom-out remains genuinely undefined.
