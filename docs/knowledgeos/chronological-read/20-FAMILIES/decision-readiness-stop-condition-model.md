## Rationale
The weighted-average Readiness score assumes all dimensions are compensable: a hypothetical case with 95% knowledge/understanding/conflict-resolution but an unresolved critical safety conflict could still average to Readiness=0.9 and incorrectly declare the state decision-ready. Presented as the counterexample motivating replacement of the scalar formula [S0804]. Overall assessment gives the original Q23 scores Conceptual 8/10, Mathematical 6.5/10, DDD 7.5/10, expecting 9.5/9/9.5 after corrections. States the central architectural insight: KnowledgeOS does not decide when 'enough knowledge' exists in the abstract, but whether enough decision-relevant knowledge exists for this Knower, this purpose, this context, and this decision — giving a progression chain from Discrepancy through Findings, Resolution Opportunities, Prioritization, Investigation, Decision Sufficiency, to Decision Posture (Q19→Q22→Q23) [S0804].
