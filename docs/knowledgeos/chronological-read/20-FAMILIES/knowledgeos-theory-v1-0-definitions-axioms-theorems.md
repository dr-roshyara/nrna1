### 1. Core ontology, the factive Knowledge relation, and the six founding non-collapses (S2373 — 9 rows)
[DEF-1] defines Knowledge as a factive epistemic relation Knows(s,p,c,t) between participant, proposition, context and time, with Knows(s,p,c,t) => True(p,c,t) when the epistemic contract is factive [S2373]. [AX-1] states the category non-collapse axiom with six named non-collapses: Observation≠Knowledge, Evidence≠Knowledge, Claim≠Truth, Probability≠Knowledge, Proposal≠Decision, Representation≠Identity [S2373]. [DEF-2]-[DEF-3] define World state W_t (not requiring complete access) and Observation as a method-mediated relation, giving Observation≠Reality and Observation≠Knowledge [S2373]. [DEF-4]/[AX-2] define the Knower as a participant with an epistemic frame F_s=(G_s,C_s,Q_s,EC_s,M_s), with frame ownership belonging to the knower/authority — KnowledgeOS may never silently redefine it [S2373]. [DEF-5]-[DEF-6] separate Proposition from Claim=(p,c,t,s) (seven possible statuses), giving Proposition≠Claim and Claim≠Truth [S2373]. [DEF-7] defines Evidence as a directional support relation Supports(e,p) with provenance prov(e)=(source,method,agent,time,context,integrity), giving the dependency chain Knowledge <- Evidence <- Provenance [S2373]. [DEF-8]-[DEF-9]/[AX-3] separate Extraction (Extract:E->X) from Determination (Determine(X,EC)->Status), so an extraction mechanism cannot silently become epistemic authority [S2373].
