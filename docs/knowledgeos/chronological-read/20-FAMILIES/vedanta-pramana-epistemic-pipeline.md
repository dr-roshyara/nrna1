## Rationale
The Veda->Upanishad->Vedanta structure is abstracted as Source->Essence extraction->Interpretation/validation method; warns against the common AI-system mistake of equating Document with Knowledge (better model: Expression->Meaning extraction->Understanding->Validated knowledge). [S0213] Reads Para Vidya vs Apara Vidya (higher/lower knowledge) not as a ranking to import into KnowledgeOS, but as evidence that a knowledge space can hold fundamentally different MODES of knowing (e.g. knowing phenomena vs knowing substrate/principle) — a change in kind, not merely 'Knowledge = dimensions × values'. [S0719]
