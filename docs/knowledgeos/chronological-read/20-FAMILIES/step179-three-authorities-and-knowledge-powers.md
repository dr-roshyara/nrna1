## Sources (how this label entered the ledger)
- **OBJECT-INDEX** (batch B0033, scope THEORY-LEVEL): Step 179 distinguishes EpistemicAuthority (authority to make a recognized determination in a domain), GovernanceAuthority (authority to make a binding organizational decision), and OperationalAuthority (authority to execute an action) as non-overlapping layers (a security specialist has epistemic authority over an assessment, the Architecture Board has governance authority over the decision, Operations has operational authority over deployment -- no contradiction). Distinguishes five distinct knowledge powers -- Read(K), Create(K), Modify(K), Supersede(K), Invalidate(K) -- noting a developer able to read an ADR cannot thereby invalidate it. Separates StatementAuthority (an actor can always assert a belief, creating a claim) from EpistemicAuthority (actually establishing a determination) from GovernanceAuthority -- asserting 'I believe migration is safe' does not make MigrationSafe an established determination merely by assertion.
