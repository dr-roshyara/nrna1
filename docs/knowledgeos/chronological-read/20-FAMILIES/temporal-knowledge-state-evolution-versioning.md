## Rationale
Refines conflict detection to require temporally overlapping validity intervals (non-overlapping successive intervals are not contradictory), introducing a specific TemporalConflict classification identifying the overlapping sub-interval rather than a bare Conflict=True [S0872]. Defines Bitemporal knowledge (valid time × knowledge time), required so a question like 'what did KnowledgeOS believe on a past date' is answered from the historical knowledge state, not today's [S0872]. Argues temporal reasoning (nine listed computations) is deterministically computable for finite explicit intervals, while natural-language temporal extraction remains AI-assisted and only Candidate status until normalized — TemporalReasoning can be deterministic even when TemporalExtraction is AI-assisted [S0872].
