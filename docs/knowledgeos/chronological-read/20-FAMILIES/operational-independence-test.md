## Rationale
States a deterministic rule requiring no LLM: if e_i leads to e_j in the provenance graph (e.g. Database → LLM extraction), then Ind(e_i,e_j)=Dependent. [S0855]

For two evidence items with no direct edge but a common ancestor (e.g. two LLMs, A and B, both reading Document D), states CommonAncestor(e_i,e_j) ⇒ Independent(e_i,e_j)≠Confirmed, with the default result Unknown unless the policy justifies conditional independence. [S0855]

Warns that different apparent sources (e.g. two documents both ultimately from Reuters) do not prove independence, since both may originate from the same underlying report: DifferentSource ⇏ Independent. [S0855]

Shows that independent acquisition mechanisms/provenance (e.g. two separate sensors reporting the same 20°C reading with no shared upstream observation) can establish OperationallyIndependent(e1,e2) under a declared policy, stressing the qualifier 'operationally'. [S0855]

Argues against collapsing independence into a single score like IndependenceScore=0.87, since a scalar obscures why evidence is considered independent, in favor of an itemized per-dimension Confirmed/Rejected breakdown that is auditable. [S0855]
