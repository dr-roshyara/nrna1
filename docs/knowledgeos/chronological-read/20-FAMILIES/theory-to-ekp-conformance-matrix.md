Relatedly, Second final-gate verdict, on empirical conformance: PARTIALLY ENGINEERINGALLY CONFORMANT; the mathematical-completeness verdict (FORMALLY INCOMPLETE) and the engineering-conformance verdict are deliberately kept separate and not collapsed into one score, since the theory is mathematically more complete than the EKP implements, while the EKP enforces more governance invariants than the theory currently has — neither representation dominates the other [S1647].
