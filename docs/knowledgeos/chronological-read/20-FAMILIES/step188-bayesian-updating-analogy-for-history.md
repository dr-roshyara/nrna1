## Sources (how this label entered the ledger)
- **OBJECT-INDEX** batch `B0033`, scope `THEORY-LEVEL`: Step 188 draws a precise Bayesian-updating analogy for why new evidence must never retroactively rewrite a historical decision: P_{t+1}(H) is proportional to P(E_{t+1}|H)*P_t(H) -- new evidence updates rather than overwrites the prior, giving the general principle 'new knowledge extends epistemic state; it does not rewrite the previous epistemic state.' Formalizes the decision function D_t=f(K_t,R_t,A_t) (knowledge/rules/authority available at t), stating a later K_{t+1}!=K_t does not retroactively change D_t; worked example: a decision-maker who estimated P(H|E_t)=0.60 and proceeded should never be judged as though they 'should have known' a later-revealed P=0.05.
