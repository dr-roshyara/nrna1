## Rationale
Argues matching classification alone (Internal knowledge, Internal actor) does not necessarily imply access; Purpose, Domain, Role, and NeedToKnow may also constrain it. [S0994] Argues AI necessitates OutputControl in addition to input AccessControl, since output may leak information from restricted inputs. [S0994] Argues sensitivity can apply not only to raw facts but to probabilities, models, correlations, predictions, and conclusions. [S0994] Notes aggregation (e.g. counts/averages) can safely obscure individuals, but small-group aggregation can still leak information. [S0994] Raises the tension between right-to-deletion and provenance-preservation: deleting K while another artifact retains Provenance(K) requires explicit data-lifecycle semantics. [S0994] States the genuine tension between ImmutableHistory (auditability) and Deletion (privacy), which cannot always be naively both satisfied and must not be hidden. [S0994] Proposes separating EventID/EventSemantics from identifying attributes as an architectural technique to reduce unnecessary exposure while preserving audit needs. [S0994] States the key architectural principle that authorization should preferably constrain retrieval itself, since post-retrieval filtering still exposes restricted information to the processing environment. [S0994] States embeddings may themselves encode sensitive information and cannot be treated as non-sensitive merely for being vectors. [S0994]
