## Rationale

The proposal's purpose is to give theory verification a single, explicit target: the Candidate KnowledgeOS Mathematical Theory (CKT), which must be reconstructed from the entire relevant corpus (book, mathematical artifacts, architecture artifacts, historical/research evidence) rather than assuming the book is automatically authoritative — the risk named is "we could end up proving that the book is internally consistent while the underlying KnowledgeOS theory is wrong" [S1413]. This closes a methodological gap: without a Theory Corpus Map, a verification pass has no inventory of where the theory even lives, nor which artifact carries what authority. Once produced, the map discloses its own coverage limitation up front: it is built from listing plus grep-based scanning with selective reading, not full-text reading of the roughly 1,350-file corpus, so absence claims (no axioms, no LaTeX, a single sigma-algebra hit) are grep-verified and strong, while claims about individual sampled files hold only at the level actually sampled [S1426]. The map also records a process-safety observation in passing: one sweep found an instruction-like text block appended inside a source file's content, correctly treated as untrusted data and ignored rather than acted on [S1426]. A related register (S1430) separately inventories the corpus's several independent "freeze list" (assumption-by-fiat) mechanisms scattered across different files — including this batch's own Step 201 and Step 203 freezes — that were never reconciled with two unrelated Q-series freeze lists, illustrating exactly the kind of fragmentation the Theory Corpus Map was proposed to surface [S1430].

**S1413 — defining the verification target (5 rows)**

Defines the Candidate KnowledgeOS Mathematical Theory (CKT) as the single object to be reconstructed from the whole corpus before verification begins; warns that the book (Edition 2) may silently strengthen or misstate what an underlying mathematical artifact actually establishes (e.g. claiming A<=>B where the original derivation supports only A=>B); establishes that architecture/implementation artifacts are evidence for conformance but never mathematical authority for the theory itself, so verification must run Theory->Architecture->Implementation and never the reverse; keeps historical truth (what was proposed/recorded) strictly separate from mathematical truth (whether a proposition is actually valid); and proposes Phase V0 (Theory Inventory) as the Verify Session's first task, producing a Theory Corpus Map table of artifact/contents/authority role.
