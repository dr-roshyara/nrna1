## Rationale
Reports seven unreconciled 'Knowledge' model genera surfaced across the corpus (understanding, relationship, product-with-members, justified state evolution, capacity, ratified domain commitment, inter-level phenomenon, plus factive/measurable-construct/continuously-changing variants), noting the programme explicitly closes negatively (rejecting knowledge=information+truth+belief+evidence+justification) while still asking 'what is knowledge itself?' on its final recorded day [S0751]. Catalogs eight Kernel candidate formulations (admission boundary, consistency boundary, decision boundary, transformation boundary, epistemic accountability core, constitutional distinction-preservation, epistemic coherence boundary, traceable relationship system) plus three ontological positions (boundary / enforcement-mechanism-of-a-boundary / domain space), with an explicit provenance warning that six of the eight arrived within a ~13-minute window sharing one prompt lineage — not eight independent arrivals, and should not be weighted as such [S0751]. Ranks the corpus's strongest results by independent-evidence weight rather than elegance: atomicity (not relatedness) determines aggregate membership (6 independent arrivals across both phases); formal apparatus is always a regime, never the ontology itself (7 different formalisms converge on one verdict); the Kernel preserves structures while reasoning regimes perform (4 independent sources); an anti-reasoner constraint plus a decidability test (mechanism-independent, testable); store-the-substrate/compute-the-measure (with Roberts' homomorphism justification); a minimum preservation unit (identity-bearing, contextualized assertion record) that survives the atomicity falsification untouched; and the extent-vs-contents reframing that dissolves much of the corpus's disagreement [S0751]. Preserves eight vocabulary collisions found across the corpus without resolving them: 'Kernel' used in 4 distinct senses, 'projection' in 7 senses, 'agent', 'UNKNOWN' at 2 altitudes, 'C-1' as 2 separate namespaces, 'boundary' meaning both invariant-determined and observer-relative, an altitude-vs-boundary confusion, and 'Zero lens' carrying two incompatible definitions 90 minutes apart (statistical residual vs subtractive-structural) — the last discovered only by the depth audit [S0751].
