## Rationale
Argues, via three worked removals (Claim minus Question, minus Evidence, minus Context), that Sunya occurs not in the object itself but in a missing relation: removing the question can erase epistemic determination, removing evidence can erase justification, and removing context can erase applicability, even though the claim syntactically persists [S2691].
