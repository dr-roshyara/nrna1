## Rationale
The constitutional layer arrived at the same prohibition from a different direction: a 'knowledge quality' scalar is a forbidden surrogate (Article 2's failure signature is literally 'Knowledge Quality = 0.87'). One prohibition, two independent derivations -- treated as evidence, while still refusing to call it proof. [S1150]

Omega enters on 2026-08-24 -- not in the measure-theory crisis where a reader of Part III might assume it was born, but in the typed-model and Bayesian-mind readings, as the ambient space a mathematical epistemic model quietly presupposes; the crisis, when it came a day later, inherited Omega and then had to dismantle it. The aggregation question -- how pieces of evidence combine, what duplicates and corroboration should do -- arrives here as reading-derived intuition from the statistics lenses, three days before the experiment (EXP-01) that would make two of those intuitions the only TESTED invariants in the architecture. [S1177]

The chapter's evidence base spans: Evidence = Observation+Relevance+Context (049 sec49.7/76); the e=(P,pi,rho,kappa,tau,delta,...) representation and three-layer design (Aggregation != Inference; policy-indexed); the formal criteria A-J; the calibration warning ('number pretending to be probability'); the conditional-independence caution; the executed seven-column verdict matrix; the four operators A1-A4; the dependency-first argument and duplicate-evidence argument (boxed laws); the (S+,S-) representation; I-5/I-6 as ratified TESTED invariants (v0.2 sec4); the CSV adjudication (GN-27); and the Evidence Record/Gate/Registry realization in RA v1.0. [S1248]

Reproduces the dependency argument establishing invariant I-6: given one API observation read by two LLMs, a naive count sees three pieces of evidence while a dependency-aware system sees one underlying observation plus two derived representations (EvidenceCount != IndependentEvidenceCount); no aggregation operator, however well-behaved, can see this distinction on its own, since resolving the dependency graph is work done before the operator runs -- therefore dependency resolution must architecturally precede aggregation, not merely be a nice-to-have, ratified as invariant I-6 at grade TESTED. [S1295]
