## Rationale
- [S0577] (ANALYSIS, RESTATEMENT): The corpus contains roughly 90 definitional sentences and at least ten materially different conceptions of knowledge, with 'Knowledge is not...' outnumbering 'Knowledge is...' in Phase 1. Phase 1 (root, 99 docs) converged independently on Knowledge as a RELATIONSHIP, stated five times in one day across distinct documents (0135, 0225, 0249, 0302, 1057). Phase 2 (kernel, 151 docs) shifted to a factive, participant-relative, time-dependent relation directed at a Knowledge Space, adding two conceptions absent from Phase 1: capacity and measure-over-a-space. Correction: the relationship conception is Phase 1 original project thinking, not an import from later book-derived research; conversely Knowledge Space (Phase 2 sense), capacity, and measure must not be attributed to Phase 1.
- [S0577] (ANALYSIS, DISTINCTION): Eleven competing conceptions of knowledge, all preserved and none chosen: K-1 stored content/a thing (rejected by both corpus and formal law); K-2 a justified/governed relationship among knower/object/evidence/reasoning/context/conclusion (Phase 1 original, dominant P1 position); K-3 justified state evolution (Phase 1 original); K-4 not one entity — an epistemic pipeline (Phase 1 original); K-5 Cognitive Worlds space partition (Phase 1, book-derived, never developed further); K-6 factive/participant-relative/time-dependent relation to a Knowledge Space (Phase 2, Gelfond & Kahl-derived); K-7 a capacity to project/discriminate/select/decide (Phase 2, response-mode); K-8 continuously changing body/dynamic flow (Phase 2, response-mode); K-9 structured state in a measurable space, projection as measure (Phase 2 architectural proposal, directly attacked by 181719=S0569); K-10 institutional/social, not individual (Phase 2, Searle-derived); K-11 primitive and unanalysable (Phase 2, Williamson-derived) — the deepest tension, since it denies the whole definitional field rather than competing within it; no document reconciles K-11 with K-2..K-9.
- [S0577] (ANALYSIS, CORRECTION): Conceptual evolution: Phase 1 (NOT stored content -> RELATIONSHIP -> JUSTIFIED STATE EVOLUTION) hands over into kernel/ (2026-08-23), then Phase 2 (relation/state directed at a Knowledge Space -> CAPACITY -> MEASURE over a space -> PRIMITIVE/unanalysable). What changed: the relatum moved from an internal list (reality/observer/evidence/reasoning) to an external space. What did not change: the refusal of stored content, the corpus's most stable result across both phases, agreeing with formal law §17. Self-correction: an earlier claim that 'Knowledge Space' first appears at kernel/20260825-103542 was kernel-scoped and wrong corpus-wide — the phrase appears three days earlier at 20260822-0200 (Phase 1) as a diagram label for 'Cognitive Worlds' explaining why contradictions can coexist, but it is a different concept (no unboundedness, no projection, no participant relativity, no time index) — restated as 'same term, different concept', not 'term absent.'
- [S0577] (ANALYSIS, OPEN-QUESTION): Four competing identity positions found: identity assigned-never-derived (formal law INV-KOS-IDENTITY-001, echoed by corpus, both phases); identity primitive/unitary/indefinable (McGinn, kernel 115235/120122, Phase 2 book); identity transformational/contextual (Daoist ziran, kernel 094638, Phase 2 book, 170 references, the only K4-strength challenge); identity relational — since knowledge IS the relationship, identity is the relation's (0135, 0225, Phase 1 original). Asymmetry noted: the only K4-strength challenge to identity is book-derived while the position it attacks is constitutional; no Phase 1 document takes a position on the nature of identity (P1 treats identity as needed, not explained). 'Can knowledge exist without identity?' is not addressed anywhere in the corpus; formal law answers by construction (removing identity makes the system 'a document store, an embedding database, or a similarity engine').
- [S0577] (ANALYSIS, OPEN-QUESTION): 42 Phase 2 documents carry temporal material versus almost none in Phase 1 ('at time t' appears once in the 13 earliest kernel documents and is rare at root). Positions: knowledge inherently knowledge-at-a-time (Phase 2 only, 171853/171008); knowledge revisable but not intrinsically temporal (Phase 1, 0302); knowledge evolves through governed transitions (Phase 1, 1016/1018); change is continuous (Phase 2, 160637/135642). 'Does change create a new identity?' is asked and answered by no document — the sharpest unresolved temporal question, exactly where K-8 (continuous change) and formal law (forward-only governed transitions, nothing overwritten) would collide; formal law's 'a revision creates a new state and retains the prior one' answers state succession, not identity succession.
- [S0577] (ANALYSIS, CONTRADICTION): The corpus deliberately and repeatedly separates knowledge from truth/belief/assertion/reliance ('knowledge is not possession of truth', 'not created by belief', 'not simply whatever an agent believes', 'the system records something as knowledge ≠ it is true' — densest treatment in document 105921 at 17 hits), strongly agreeing with formal law's Meaning≠Truth/Evidence≠Authority/Agent≠Truth non-collapse rules and its rejection of a Truth machine/oracle — the strongest convergence found in this extraction. But 'factive' (K-6, from Williamson via Gelfond & Kahl chain) reintroduces truth by the back door: a factive relation entails truth, yet a system with no access to truth cannot verify factivity — this tension is unreconciled, and is flagged as the same defect class previously recorded against possible-worlds π.
- [S0577] (ANALYSIS, LIMITATION): DDD boundary implications table across six conceptions: K-2 relationship -> identity belongs to the relation, admission=establishing a relatum set; K-3 state evolution -> identity assigned/persistent, admission=a governed transition; K-4 pipeline -> nothing single bears identity, identity undefined; K-6 factive relation to a space -> participant-indexed identity, admission requires truth access; K-7 capacity -> not storable, a capacity has no record; K-9 measure -> a measure/distribution, measures lack identity. Sharpest observation: K-4 (pipeline), K-7 (capacity), and K-9 (measure) cannot support an identity-bearing aggregate at all — if any were true, the current formal core domain (Knowledge Identity) would have no object, making them the corpus's most consequential falsification candidates; none of the three is Phase 1 (original project thinking).
- [S0577] (ANALYSIS, RESTATEMENT): Zero-Lens applied across both phases: after stripping optional attributes, what survives universally is a knower/participant, something known, a ground, and a record that the two were connected — every phase and nearly every model retains those four; nothing else survives universally. The corpus distinguishes 'nothing exists' from 'we do not know' (0258: 'Knowledge is not only about what exists'; formal law §9: unknown≠absent≠false). Whether UNKNOWN is the absence of Knowledge or a state of an identified object is NOT resolved by the corpus: formal law (⟨Z-1⟩) says UNKNOWN/ABSENT/FALSE/REJECTED are states of an identified epistemic object, while kernel document 125708 §17 pushes the other way ('UNKNOWN must not be a single epistemic state', eight readings) — no new state proposed here.
