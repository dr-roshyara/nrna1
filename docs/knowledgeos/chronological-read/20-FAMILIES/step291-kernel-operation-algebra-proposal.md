## Rationale
The philosophically-proposed minimal kernel algebra Omega_K={Discriminate,Qualify,Transition} (from S2219, the kernel-definition-from-evidence-maths-DDD file) maps exactly onto the formal lane's independently-established blockers: Discriminate = the equality/distinguishability relation equiv (the unique minimal cycle cut per Steps 289/291, unique over both graph universes); Qualify = the corpus's own Qualify:Observation x Policy -> Evidence, i.e. G1, named/typed/no body, irreducible; Transition = K_t --delta--> K_{t+1}, i.e. delta, commit case unspecifiable, executed as K1 is K0. This is a genuine independent arrival (derived from 'what must a kernel be able to do', not from the dependency graph, 261.23, or the G1 register) but a NEGATIVE convergence: it solves none of the three, and is a restatement of the blocker set, not a kernel. Registered as new gap G-90 (derived, raises confidence, closes nothing). Sharp consequence stated: any minimal kernel algebra containing Qualify inherits G1 -- since Qualify is the one irreducible gap, a genuinely minimal kernel cannot be closed without closing G1. [S2227] Tests the proposed kernel shape K=(K_t,Omega_K,I) slot by slot against the formal lane: K_t (the state) is OPEN (per Step 285; 258.35 says it may not be a tuple at all); Omega_K (permitted operations) is OPEN (N-4 mandatory membership open, 0 of 22 operations has a body/identity/ratification per 291); I (invariants) has 0 of 7 established (per REFINED-STEP-287-INVARIANTS), and I is downstream of equiv in 261.30's chain. Conclusion: a correctly-shaped triple whose three components are exactly the three open items -- a well-posed problem statement, not progress toward closure; must not be mistaken for populating the kernel (per 291's 'shape -> content requires a derivation, and none exists'). [S2227] Refines purification: P:Sigma_t->Sigma_{t+1} must not assume P(Sigma_t)>Sigma_t (per Step 287's finding that axes lack fully declared orders); instead a desired property Q(Sigma_{t+1})>=Q(Sigma_t) is wanted where Q is a future epistemic-quality functional that does not yet exist (an important research gap). Illustrates via example that K_1={1000 unsupported claims} could contain more information than K_2={100 well-supported claims} yet K_2 may be epistemically superior -- so Knowledge quantity != Knowledge quality, and Purification != accumulation; the kernel should maximize epistemic adequacy, not raw knowledge. [S2239] Ch1 is identified as the only Gita chapter addressing failure/uncertainty semantics -- one of the kernel proposal's own declared-open slots (primitive operators, types, pre/postconditions, composition rules, identity elements, invariants, failure/uncertainty semantics, minimality).
