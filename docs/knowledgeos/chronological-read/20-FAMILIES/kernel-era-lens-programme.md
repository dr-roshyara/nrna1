## Rationale
Explains that The kernel-era corpus (2026-08-22 to 2026-08-25, 170 documents) is a reading programme: take the still-soft idea of a knowledge kernel and pass it through as many disciplined lenses as three days allow -- Navya-Nyaya relation logic, the Bhagavad Gita's knower of the field, statistics (seven pillars, uncertainty and belief revision), Bayesian models of mind, typed mathematical modelling. Each lens was mined for properties a kernel must have, not for doctrine; the repository side's closure ruling would later call this the era's achievement: completely different traditions converged on the same structural problems and did not produce new kernel laws. [S1177] Explains that Omega enters on 2026-08-24 -- not in the measure-theory crisis where a reader of Part III might assume it was born, but in the typed-model and Bayesian-mind readings, as the ambient space a mathematical epistemic model quietly presupposes; the crisis, when it came a day later, inherited Omega and then had to dismantle it. The aggregation question -- how pieces of evidence combine, what duplicates and corroboration should do -- arrives here as reading-derived intuition from the statistics lenses, three days before the experiment (EXP-01) that would make two of those intuitions the only TESTED invariants in the architecture.
