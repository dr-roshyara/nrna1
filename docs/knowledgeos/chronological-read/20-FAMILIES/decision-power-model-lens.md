- (ARGUMENT/FORMALIZATION) DecisionPower is proposed as a derived concept, not a persisted primitive: DecisionPower(actor, context, time) computed from DecisionRights + Mandates + Constraints + Delegations + CurrentState, fitting the deterministic architecture direction. [S0406]
- (ARGUMENT/LIMITATION/EXTENSION) A 1998 source claim that computers can shape aims, provide/evaluate information and present options but do not possess the value judgment required for a 'true decision' is explicitly NOT imported as a KnowledgeOS truth (flagged as a dated philosophical/managerial position), but the underlying architectural insight is kept: decision support capability does not automatically imply decision authority; an AI Agent may discover/analyse evidence, generate/evaluate alternatives, recommend, and simulate consequences, without exercising a DecisionRight unless governance explicitly grants it. [S0406]
