Additionally, Target 3 (≅_λ provenance-sensitive equality, corpus 246 §D: 'iff content and relevant provenance are equivalent') is blocked because 'relevant' is undefined; the nearest corpus material (step-105) supplies only a relevance criterion ('decision-relevant provenance, not private chain-of-thought'), not a definition. Turning it into a predicate needs the decision context named. Flagged as mattering most because ≅_λ is exactly the relation under which δ is injective on D285-5's witness, so defining it would change an existing published result [S2094]. Further, Draws a design implication from the D285-5 quotient-relative non-injectivity finding: since multiple distinct operations may produce equivalent results under the relevant quotient, the Qualify function (once formalized) must be designed to handle non-injective operations rather than assuming a one-to-one operation-to-result mapping [S2100]. Relatedly, Summarizes the D285-5 witness's behavior across all four equality relations as evidence that equality cannot be hand-waved: structural equality makes the antecedent false; semantic equality makes the witness substantive only after choosing what semantics preserve; observational equality's result depends on the permitted observation set; provenance-sensitive equality can make the same witness cease to establish non-injectivity — concluding 'equality' is not one mathematical operation in this programme but a family whose members must each be operationally decided [S2106].
