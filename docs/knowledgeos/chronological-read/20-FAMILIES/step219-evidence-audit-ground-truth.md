## Rationale
Step 219 argues AI summarization is fundamentally a semantic projection (removing caveats, provenance, disagreement, temporal context, alternative interpretations) and should be conceptually modeled as SemanticTransformation, not mere text generation [S1545]. Step 219 argues that mathematics (preserve structure), statistics (preserve assumptions/uncertainty), DDD (preserve meaning within bounded contexts), AI (prevent semantic loss), governance (preserve authority/accountability), and history (preserve provenance/temporal validity) are distinct disciplines that converge around a single concept: semantic integrity [S1545].
