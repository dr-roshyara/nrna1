## Rationale
AMD6's independent review finds a genuine safety gap (DV-1) in the durability migration plan: the durable authoritative copy is written after its last all-or-nothing verification and never re-verified before the source is removed, so Phase 7's gate could report success while the durable copy is silently incomplete. [S0164] Finds that the two seemingly separate work items (cost-optimization/deterministic-assurance, and the durability migration) already share the same evidence base and back-test corpus, so integration is a sequencing/authority problem rather than a new architecture problem; the checker must remain warn-only through Phase 1 to avoid itself becoming a governance object requiring authority. [S0164] A self-referential observation: the ongoing governance work authorizing this very amendment is itself appended to the corpus the migration plan proposes to freeze, meaning a literal freeze would have had to prohibit the act that authorized the amendment. [S0183] Observes a structural risk pattern: repeatedly declaring and abandoning candidate reviewers without recording a START permanently shrinks the pool of processes still eligible to review, since each attempt itself becomes a disqualifying prior-participation fact. [S0185] Offers two lawful routes out of the identity-mismatch deadlock (resume the already-named process, or have Governance register a fresh lane naming the true performer) without recommending either as an authority act. [S0190] Observes that the eligibility-declaration and reviewer-lane-registration records produced by this very process are themselves untracked/gitignored, i.e. live examples of the exact durability gap the KOS-AIP-GOV-STATE-DURABILITY work item exists to fix. [S0190] Diagnoses across three refusal attempts (S0184, S0192, S0193) that the recurring blockage is a single mechanical process-vs-lane mismatch, not repeated genuine independence failures. [S0193] Concludes that the recurring blockage is best resolved by recording the missing human START on the already-registered lane, rather than by producing yet another candidate reviewer. [S0193]
