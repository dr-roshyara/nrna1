## Rationale
The Engineering Decision Model was created because standards say what the rules are, while the model says what decisions an engineer resolves with them: rules existed but the decisions they answer were unindexed. Its anti-problem: it is a decision INDEX, never a second rulebook (ARB, 2026-07-11) — created to prevent duplicated authority, not to add a layer. Its birth act included a vocabulary correction (2026-07-11): a DDD Domain Service is executable, a decision is a specification. Its adoption bar: one real engineering cycle must show consistent resolution through the model; it is DRAFT until earned [S0006]. Eight decisions are explicitly modeled, all of one family (the flow of knowledge and artifacts through governed work), grouped by question: 'Where does it belong?' (DetermineConcern, DeterminePlacement - mechanized by doc-placement.php); 'What is it/does it live?' (DetermineArtifactLifecycle - deletion litmus, DetermineArtifactType); 'Is it worth keeping/raising?' (DetermineReusePotential - Harvest, DeterminePromotionPath - ladder entry); 'What governs/verifies it?' (DetermineApplicableStandards, DetermineQualificationMethod). Deliberately excluded: ADR-making, capability approval, authority acts, promotion rulings — 'promotion is a human governance act and is never automated'; 'the AI evaluates and recommends — authority remains with governance.' The absence of governance decisions from the catalog is design, not gap [S0006]. Answered in a prior Discovery pass and carried forward unchanged: FREEZE (n>=3, undeclared authority) and RETIRE (PM-6) extend nothing in the catalog, submitted as insufficiency evidence per the model's own stopping rule. Implicit but exercised: the challenge-commissioning trigger and terminology adoption. Nothing further found in this pass, which per the stopping rule is the correct amount [S0006]. The model governs zero architecture decisions (no DetermineArchitectureDecision exists; ADRs are governance acts, outside the catalog by design). What it governs on the ladder Observation->Hypothesis->Evidence->Principle->Architecture is the entry points, not the rungs: DetermineReusePotential opens the ladder (observation->candidate); DeterminePromotionPath routes onto it (candidate->pilot->qualification->ARB decides). The rungs belong to ES-006.1, and the model correctly refuses to own them since it is an index, never a second rulebook [S0006]. The model's own Decision Catalogs table states Engineering Standards answer the catalog's Engineering Decisions, while Project-Knowledge-class Knowledge Decisions (DetermineKnowledgeNeed, Source, Representation, Qualification, Promotion) are 'pilot-gated candidates — arrive only with pilot evidence; same pattern, different domain.' Engineering Governance is present now; Knowledge Governance is anticipated with the arrival condition already stated, and the entire ontology/meta-model corpus of this programme is exactly the kind of pilot evidence the anticipated catalog says it is waiting for [S0006]. Ontology/meta-model elements map onto the existing decision model rather than extending it: archetype classification extends DetermineArtifactType (same question, richer vocabulary); KNOWLEDGE != ARTIFACT (I-1) is presupposed by DetermineArtifactLifecycle's deletion litmus; AUTHORITY-SCOPE/CONTAINER boundary maps to DetermineConcern/DeterminePlacement; GOVERNANCE-STATUS/EVIDENCE-STATUS maps to DeterminePromotionPath; NATURE (normative force) maps to DetermineApplicableStandards. Nothing in the ontology requires a new decision; the only extension pressure (FREEZE/RETIRE) is not ontology-driven, but comes from the enacted governance record. Verdict: the ontology supplies the vocabulary the catalog's procedures presuppose; the catalog supplies the purpose the ontology elements must serve — they interlock, neither extends the other [S0006]. A second decision model would violate ubiquitous language three times over: (1) the model's charter states it is 'a decision INDEX, never a second rulebook' — a v2 is exactly what it exists to prevent; (2) I-5, lint-enforced, requires one authoritative artifact per topic+context, so a second model is a literal violation; (3) ES-005.4 requires one rule -> one home. A competing 'KnowledgeOS Decision Model' would have been the UL violation and was not created; the Discovery and Validation passes are readings of the one model, not rivals to it [S0006]. D-2 survives on a new ground: Round39-D6's verbatim rule 'Governance discoveries (constitutional/capability) MUST NOT appear here. No DDD concepts here' is a deliberately maintained boundary (not an accident of filing), bidirectional in effect (ADR-M excludes governance, ADR-AIP carries no methodology rules), and survives the shared-authority finding since a shared authority that forbids itself from mixing two subjects is evidence FOR two models, not against. D-2 remains a boundary, but on subject exclusion, not on decision-series separation or independent evolution [S0011]. Per-domain confidence after falsification (R16 Strong/Medium/Weak scale): D-1 Strong->STRONG; D-2 Strong->MEDIUM; D-3 Strong->WEAK; D-6a (Evidence Protocol) new->MEDIUM; D-6b (Product Evidence) new->STRONG; D-5 (Engineering Capability, not a context) Medium->MEDIUM (unchanged, H-CAT-1 stands); D-4 (Workflow, rejected) ->STRONG as a rejection; D-7 (PKS, a context type) new->WEAK. Distinction drawn: falsified is worse than unevidenced — D-3 and D-7 carry contradicting evidence (falsified); D-6a carries no evidence (unevidenced) [S0011]. Engineer decisions (resolved by applying a governed procedure) are indexed as a catalog (the eight decisions of the Decision Model); governance decisions (resolved by an authority act, by DA/ARB/sponsor) are recorded richly (R-1..R-77 rulings, DR-1..DR-8, the ADR series, the Decision Docket D-1..D-9b) but cataloged nowhere. Whether that asymmetry is a defect or the correct shape is recorded as U-DM-1, not resolved [S0017]. Every recurring decision found in the enacted record was tested against the stopping rule's own question ('which existing decision does this extend?'). All but two map onto existing catalog entries. FREEZE/UNFREEZE extends nothing found, exercised n>=3 (methodology freeze 2026-08-01, architecture freeze 2026-08-03, corpus freeze Phase A), each time by declaration under no named authority, procedure, or reversal condition. RETIRE/PRESERVE knowledge extends nothing (PM-6 asks exactly this, still open); retirement meanwhile happens informally by supersession. Both are submitted as insufficiency evidence for the Decision Model's own ladder, not as new catalog entries, which only the ARB may mint [S0017]. Nine ontology elements are each bound to the specific decision they serve, applying Evans' test: KNOWLEDGE!=ARTIFACT (I-1) underwrites the deletion litmus of DetermineArtifactLifecycle (unaskable unless knowledge and file differ); AUTHORITY-SCOPE serves DetermineConcern/DeterminePlacement; GOVERNANCE-STATUS serves DeterminePromotionPath; NATURE:normative-force serves DetermineApplicableStandards; DP-n anchoring serves the approve-capability-change decision; CONTAINER boundary would serve the extraction/adoption decision (the MVK FAIL is this decision made without the model); PROJECTION+attestation (corrected I-4) serves the trust-or-rebuild decision; EVIDENCE-STATUS serves the earns/grants promotion gate; the four partial orders serve the direction-of-change-propagation decision [S0017]. Testing the reviewer's proposed chain Mission->Strategy->Principles->Capability: ENGINEERING STRATEGY is admitted (12+ charter artifacts); ENGINEERING PRINCIPLE is admitted (six registers with own identifier series: AIP-01..14, AP-1..10, PGP-01..05, MC-01..08, SD-1..7, DR-1..8); DESIGN POLICY (DP-n) is discovered as a new anchor, since the Capability Catalog binds every capability to a DP (CAP-001->DP-1, etc.) and neither the reviewer nor the author had named it; 'Engineering Domain owns Capability' is rejected — no artifact links any CAP-n to a PD-n; the reviewer's concern that capabilities must not float is correct, but the repository already anchors them to DP-n, not to a domain. Chain: MISSION -> STRATEGY -> PRINCIPLE -> DESIGN POLICY -> CAPABILITY -> KNOWLEDGE -> ARTIFACT [S0021]. 14 out-of-boundary reaches recorded, of which 3 are contamination (2 recognized and refused: doc-placement.php + the 14-phase Operating Loop; 1 disclosed: the bootstrap sequence itself, supplied by the experiment brief rather than the MVK). Reach #13 is flagged as the most important line: left to the MVK alone, the session would have begun at 'Idea -> Implementation Plan' without modeling a context first — the record is therefore more favorable to the MVK than reality warrants, and the true state is worse than FAIL suggests [S0025]. Three structural failures: F-1 the EEP cannot be satisfied at bootstrap (forbids one party holding both Engineer and Independent Reviewer, forbids skipping stages, forbids implementing before approval, and the light gate EP-01-Light is the source project's property — the MVK forbids proceeding and supplies no lawful way to proceed); F-2 there is no genesis path (the sole sanctioned route to an architecture requires operational evidence absent on day one and explicitly forbids idea->ADR->implementation); F-3, originally 'there is no strategic-DDD method', is refuted and restated: the method exists (Round47-00 Strategic DDD Constitution, Round47-OP Operating Protocol, with nine product-neutral boundary criteria and a rejection protocol) but is product-coupled via SD-1, which admits only PublicDigit's Certified Domain Knowledge Release — the bootstrap session could not see them because they were not included in the MVK file list [S0025].
