## Rationale
Concludes KnowledgeOS should not primarily ask 'what evidence do we have?' but rather 'given what we are trying to know or decide, what evidence do we need, how should we obtain it, how do we know the acquisition is trustworthy, and when is the resulting observation entitled to count as evidence?' -- reordering the architecture to start from EvidenceNeed rather than an Evidence Repository, grounded in the claim that evidence claims are themselves empirical (Thomson's discovery of the Hertz flaw as the clearest demonstration).
