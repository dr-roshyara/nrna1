## Rationale
Analysis: Headline finding: of a 20-capability inventory (C-1..C-20) derived from evidence of what the platform actually does, four capabilities (C-5 separation attestation, C-10 knowledge distribution, C-14 policy enforcement, C-19 communication composition) have no declared owner -- proposed as the real substance of ADR-AIP-04, in contrast to the six proposed roles. [S0091] Finding F-9/F-9a: the drafting process itself, first-hand, occupied verification, architecture-review, architecture-drafting and domain roles in sequence within one process, self-recording its own STARTs each time -- evidence that 'Governance' as practised is a cross-cutting capability set with only ONE strictly-separated duty (acceptance/closure via COMPLETE, mechanically refused to non-governance/human writers), not a distinct actor. [S0091] Analysis: An independence gate is extended beyond mere non-authorship to material participation: a verifier whose own prior findings materially shaped a later correction chain is disqualified from verifying that chain's output, even without direct authorship of the artifact under review. [S0100] Analysis: Structural finding: C-5, C-10, C-14, C-19 are not four independent candidate bounded contexts but four capabilities within one control loop -- BC-1's policy/knowledge is delivered by C-10 to a gated session, C-14 gates the act, C-19 expresses the outcome, and C-5 attests who acted and how separately -- while still having four distinct reasons to change, so the relationship, not a merged boundary, is what explains them. [S0102] A unifying control-plane invariant candidate (proposed, not adopted): a gate's verdict may not be issued by the party the gate constrains; observed support: G-1 already enforces this for closure, and CAP-09's write-paths are deliberately closed, so the invariant is already implemented twice but never stated as a general rule. [S0102] Analysis: Finding I-2, an explicit analogy: the estate's identity-attribution mechanism has produced both a false negative and a false positive on the same question within one day, on the exact work item studying separation attestation (C-5) -- framed as C-5 failing in production, recorded as evidence, with no ownership or OQ-A conclusion drawn. [S0104] Analysis: A self-referential separation argument: Governance for the work item refuses to author a C-5 analysis because doing so would make it a producer on the item it governs -- the exact shape of defect §4 of the provenance reconciliation had just recorded -- and the refusal is offered as a third, live instance of C-5 evidence on the very work item studying C-5. [S0106] Analysis: The sharpest finding in the D3 prep: a C-10 receipt's binding to a specific session execution can never be stronger than what INV-ATTR-1/INV-ATTR-2 permit (self-declared, not attested), so identity binding cannot be placed inside C-10's authoritative state -- it is C-5-shaped, and C-5 owns no attestation record either. [S0112] Analysis: The earlier reclassification of the AMD2 verification artifact (ca6039a8) as historical-but-not-independent is offered as a real, worked instance of what 'receipt invalidation' should look like: invalidation is purpose-scoped (it removes assurance standing while leaving the content usable as evidence), not a global deletion. [S0114] Analysis: A candidate INDEPENDENT evidence dimension is refused for C-10's establishment model because it is C-5's own subject matter (whose organizational independence is itself NOT_ESTABLISHED and cannot be currently supplied), which would make C-10 unestablishable for reasons unrelated to C-10; REPRODUCIBLE (can anyone re-derive the observation?) is adopted instead as doing the same protective work without importing an unresolved capability's unresolved state. [S0117] Analysis: A structural result from the KOS-AIP04-DISCOVERY-001 role-model discovery: the estate's assurance is strong exactly where it is executable (BC-7, the workflow engine) and weak exactly where it is prose (everywhere else); since separation itself is Declared, not attestable (INV-ATTR-2), naming more precise roles buys no additional assurance. [S0164] Analysis: Records four capabilities with no bounded-context owner as one control loop (policy/knowledge DEFINED in BC-1 -> C-10 DELIVERS -> C-14 GATES -> C-19 EXPRESSES <- C-5 ATTESTS): C-5 Separation attestation (existence YES, cross-context control-plane assurance capability, NOT a bounded context); C-10 Knowledge distribution (existence NOT YET ESTABLISHED, deferred); C-14 Policy enforcement (existence CONTESTED, deferred); C-19 Communication composition (existence YES, stewardship/cross-cutting expression concern, NOT a bounded context). Flagged as a proposal finding, not adopted architecture; C-10/C-14 existence remain UNDECIDED. [S0234]
