## Sources (how this label entered the ledger)
- **OBJECT-INDEX** (batch B0033, scope THEORY-LEVEL): Step 171 refines the earlier categorical claim 'AI cannot create knowledge' (called unnecessarily absolute) into a general PromotionRule(S_i,S_{i+1}) mechanism -- e.g. PromotionRule(Assessment,EstablishedKnowledge) might require EvidenceSufficient AND MethodValid AND RequiredReviewComplete -- so whether AI output can become established knowledge is a domain-specific promotion-rule question, not an absolute prohibition. Also states that Determine/Decide/Authorize/Execute must remain distinct verbs in the domain's ubiquitous language; collapsing them into one word like 'Approve' is flagged as a semantic ambiguity that will eventually become an architectural defect. Restates Determination not-implies-unique-Decision (the same determination can legitimately lead to different decisions, e.g. Remediate vs AcceptRisk, depending on governance context).
