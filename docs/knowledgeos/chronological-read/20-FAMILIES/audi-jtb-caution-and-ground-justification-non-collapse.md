## Sources (how this label entered the ledger)
- **OBJECT-INDEX**, batch B0056, scope THEORY-LEVEL: Extraction from Robert Audi's 'Epistemology: A Contemporary Introduction to the Theory of Knowledge' (3rd ed.), the designated first book of a new epistemology research set. Reports Audi's deliberately cautious general conception -- knowledge is true belief based in the right way on the right kind of ground -- explicitly NOT offered as a complete final analysis (the right ground could involve causation, justification, reliability, consciousness-accessibility, epistemic virtue, direct/non-inferential grounding, or a combination), with a warning against treating this as the settled theorem Knowledge=True+Belief+Justification, since a justified true belief can still fail to be knowledge (truth obtained accidentally/via a misleading situation) and knowledge might exist without ordinary accessible justification. Distinguishes causal grounding, justificational grounding, and epistemic grounding as non-interchangeable relations, refining Information->Knowledge into Source->Evidence->Belief/Representation->Grounding->Truth->Knowledge (mapping still unresolved). Reports Audi's inference-transmission model (inference transmits knowledge/justification, it is not itself a basic source of them) with necessary-vs-sufficient transmission conditions, and his description of a continuously changing belief structure (experience/thought/action/learning/forgetting/inference/acceptance/revision/rejection/speaking/listening) as supporting evidence (not proof) for the corpus's own K_t!=K_{t+1} non-monotonicity hypothesis. Reports Audi's lottery example (~0.999999 probability of losing yet not knowing one will lose) as a strong confirmation that high probability != knowledge, reinforcing the corpus's rejection of p_i as a knowledge-quantity measure. States the biggest conceptual contribution as a five-way non-collapse Ground != Justification != Belief != Truth != Knowledge (and probably Information != Evidence != Justification), grounded in Audi's situational/propositional-justification vs belief/doxastic-justification distinction (one can have justification available without actually believing the proposition) -- flagged as a potentially major missing KnowledgeOS layer. Produces a 15-row preliminary Audi->KnowledgeOS research map (Reality/Perception/Testimony/Memory/Reason/Belief/Ground/Justification/Truth/Inference/Revision/Knowledge/Probability/Coherence/Skepticism), explicitly marking Knowledge<->K_t as 'not yet equivalent.' Proposes the research hypothesis KnowledgeFormation:(Evidence,Ground,Belief,Justification,Truth,Context,Knower)->Knowledge as the target of a still-missing 'Knowledge Formation Theory', explicitly NOT modifying the KnowledgeOS kernel on Audi's authority, and raises the deeper open question 'is KnowledgeOS actually modelling knowledge, or is it currently modelling justified epistemic state?' as requiring investigation before finalizing K_t.
