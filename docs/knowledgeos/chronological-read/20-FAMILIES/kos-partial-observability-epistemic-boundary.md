## Rationale
Formalizes the observation boundary as a map O: X -> O; if O(x1)=O(x2) the states are indistinguishable through that function. States that if O is injective over the relevant state space, observations can in principle uniquely identify the state; if not, state identification is inherently limited. Concludes KnowledgeOS cannot solve ObservationArchitecture problems merely by adding more AI: if the necessary signal is not captured, AI cannot reliably reconstruct it. Experiment 11: AI attempting to infer an unobserved X purely from unrelated variables should yield InferenceStatus=ModelDependent, not Observed -- PASS. States sensor/observation design is itself part of knowledge architecture: 'the quality of the resulting knowledge depends on what can actually be observed'. [S0962]
