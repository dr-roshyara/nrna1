## Rationale

Applied reflexively: the entire 257-document brainstorming corpus this programme reconstructs is itself only priority-7 evidence by the project's own oldest stated rule, meaning the reconstruction's output can only ever be candidate pressure on the formal model, never a competing authority. DOC-1 §61 also excludes 'future KnowledgeOS kernel concepts' from current EKS, and §44 states historical EKS architecture and the later KnowledgeOS target architecture are not interchangeable — the corpus's oldest document already forbids the retrospective attribution this programme guards against.
