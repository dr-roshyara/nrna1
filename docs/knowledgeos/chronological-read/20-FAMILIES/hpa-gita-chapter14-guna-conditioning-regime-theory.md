## Rationale
Interprets 'without governance mind can become its own enemy' via a self-reinforcing epistemic degradation feedback loop: G_t -> B_t (Buddhi) -> o_t (chosen operation) -> K_{t+1}=delta(K_t,o_t) -> G_{t+1}, with worked examples Tamas->poor discrimination->bad operation->worse knowledge state->more Tamas, and Rajas->excessive activity->attachment to outcomes->more activity->more Rajas. Proposed as a possible mathematical model of self-reinforcing epistemic degradation, a candidate KnowledgeOS research direction [S2231].
