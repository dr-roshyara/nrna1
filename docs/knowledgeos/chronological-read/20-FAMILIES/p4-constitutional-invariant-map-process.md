## Rationale
Two rows are classified rationale-bearing. The purpose of this whole discipline: to prevent philosophical/metaphorical/mathematical research lenses from being treated as architecture before being filtered against actual engineering evidence. S0213 recommends declaring the five research lenses (Topology/Vani/Zero/Gita-Tripuisi/Vedanta) "saturated" — each contributed one missing boundary, and no more philosophical/metaphorical sources should be added unless they challenge an existing invariant [S0213]. The alternative this replaces is open-ended research collection; the proposed next step is the "P4: Constitutional Invariant Map" with a Filter/Separate/Test methodology (Filter out beautiful-but-untestable/metaphorical-only/duplicate/implementation-specific items; Separate into Established/Candidate/Hypothesis/Rejected; Test each candidate via Dimension→Forbidden collapse→Evidence→Validation target) [S0213]. This closes the gap of not having a disciplined boundary between "interesting philosophical parallel" and "constitutional invariant." The self-contradiction captured in S0217 (one turn calling the framework "architecturally complete," the next turn explicitly rejecting that) is itself evidence *for* the discipline's necessity — it shows the temptation this process exists to guard against [S0217].
