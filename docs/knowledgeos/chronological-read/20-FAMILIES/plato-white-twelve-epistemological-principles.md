Central facts: Plato holds facts about the world are independent of human belief/agreement (Knowledge is not created merely by agreement); Plato's Theaetetus proposes and REJECTS knowledge=true-belief (jury/eyewitness example: true belief from testimony != the eyewitness's knowledge) and later also rejects knowledge=true-belief+logos, so Plato 'does not find an answer' to what is knowledge -- White explicitly warns against summarizing 'Plato defined knowledge as X.' Plato distrusts hearsay/majority/authority as insufficient grounds (unanimity does not create authority), preferring firsthand examination; develops the elenchus method (claim->examination->counterexample->revision/rejection->stronger claim) and a method of hypothesis (hypothesis->consequences->test->problem?->{higher hypothesis | provisional acceptance}) culminating in the Republic's search for an 'unhypothesized beginning' as a foundation from which conclusions can be derived downward, giving knowledge a dependency/foundation structure rather than being a flat fact collection. The Theaetetus wax-tablet analogy shows perception/memory can be mismatched, producing false belief through incorrect ASSOCIATION, not only incorrect observation. The Phaedrus/Cratylus material shows Plato increasingly distrusts language (words may deceive; a name is not the object; collection and division combat conflating distinct Forms), and the Seventh Letter holds reality can be known even where language cannot adequately describe it, giving Representation != Knowledge. Distills twelve candidate KnowledgeOS Epistemological Principles P1-P12: P1 Reality Independence (a fact is not made true by belief/agreement); P2 Belief != Knowledge; P3 Authority != Truth; P4 claims must be examinable; P5 definitions matter (identifying what something really is, not just examples); P6 Representation != Reality; P7 perception can be wrong; P8 inquiry requires a target; P9 hypotheses are provisional; P10 knowledge has dependencies/foundations; P11 distinction prevents knowledge errors (confusing concepts is a knowledge error); P12 knowledge cannot be reduced to a simple formula (Plato's own investigation leaves it unresolved). Closes by combining with the file's earlier Vedic extraction into a four-layer architecture (Wisdom: judgment/action/use -> Knowledge: validated understanding of reality -> Belief: accepted interpretation -> Information: representation/signal, over Reality), with the summary warning 'never mistake the representation for the reality, the belief for the knowledge, or the agreement for the truth.'
