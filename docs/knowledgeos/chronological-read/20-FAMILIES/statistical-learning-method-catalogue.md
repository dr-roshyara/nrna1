## Rationale

Bottom-line synthesis statement listing the eleven transferable facts as the actual value of the book for KnowledgeOS, explicitly contrasted with importing specific algorithms (Random Forest, Lasso, PCA, SVM) [S0469]. Additionally, Bias-variance tradeoff (more complex models generally reduce bias but increase variance) is adopted as an architectural principle for model/reasoning-method routing: ModelSelection=f(Bias,Variance,Loss,Context,Cost), giving BiggerModel ⇏ BetterKnowledge. Notes that squared bias can be substantial while classification error remains zero if the estimate stays on the correct side of a decision boundary, so ModelError ≠ DecisionError and SmallModelError ⇏ CorrectDecision — motivating a distinction between Prediction Quality, Decision Quality, and Knowledge Quality as three non-collapsible things (Prediction -> Decision -> Knowledge claim) [S0833]. Further, Final assessment: the book should not be adopted as an 'ML methods' reference but as a candidate Inference Theory layer — a formal bridge from epistemically-admissible-context to decision-loss to model-selection to generalization to uncertainty to assurance; the underlying discipline (assumptions->representation->model->loss->fitting->generalization->validation->uncertainty->selection) maps to KnowledgeOS even though specific techniques (Lasso, SVM, Random Forest, boosting, neural networks, PCA, k-means) are not themselves adopted [S0833].
