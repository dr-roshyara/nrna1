## Rationale
The sharpest finding in the D3 prep: a C-10 receipt's binding to a specific session execution can never be stronger than what INV-ATTR-1/INV-ATTR-2 permit (self-declared, not attested), so identity binding cannot be placed inside C-10's authoritative state -- it is C-5-shaped, and C-5 owns no attestation record either [S0112].

Analyzes the O-5 instability as producing two asymmetric failure directions: (A) false independence -- a restarted process presenting a clean UUID could be wrongly admitted as independent while carrying the complete prior context of the very work it should be excluded from, destroying the independence guarantee retrospectively with a record that looks clean; (B) lost continuity -- a legitimately continuing process cannot prove it is the registered lane holder and must be re-established, a merely tolerable cost. States the governing principle that any remedy must err toward B, since a wrongly-barred process is one governed act away from repair while a wrongly-admitted one can never be repaired because its check was never actually independent [S0400].
