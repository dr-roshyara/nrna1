- **[RETRACTION/ARGUMENT]** [S2171]: Formally withdraws a seventh ground that Steps 288-289 had added to the Step-261 stop-gate ('the equiv/approx register is self-contradictory, so selecting a kernel would mean selecting over an undefined term'), replacing it with N-1A's finding that the register is a well-formed (if incomplete) typed family, not a contradiction. - **[ARGUMENT/EXPLANATION]** [S2173]: The corpus's requirement that operations respect the semantic equality relation (congruence) is stated over ≡_K and the transformation family alone, with no mention of the observational relation approx, giving congruence an independent evidentiary basis separate from equality-vs-observational-equivalence questions.
- **[DEFINITION/EXPLANATION]** [S2176]: New documentary gap G-76: Step 261 states the same six-relation register in two different notations (261.1 and 261.20/261.25), with the second notation overloading the equiv/congruence glyph across four different relation roles, plausibly explaining why the equiv/approx 'contradiction' was mis-detected.
- **[ANALYSIS]** [S2187]: Reconfirms, for a third time across the programme, that 261.23's six conditions split into an equality block (1,2,3,6) and a representation block (4,5); the fact that five consecutive steps of intensive equality research have produced zero movement on conditions 4-5 is treated as itself the evidence that they are structurally separate and will not be released by resolving equality.
- **[EXPERIMENTAL-RESULT/ARGUMENT]** [S2203]: Formally tests and disproves the proposition that resolving N-4 (O/T mandatory membership) reduces the 261.23 stop-gate condition count: N-4 unblocks further analysis for conditions 1, 3 and 6, and constitutes only the membership half of condition 2 (which would move from FAILED to PARTIAL, never to RESOLVED, since bodies/identity/ratification remain open); conditions 4 and 5 (the representation block) are entirely untouched by N-4.
