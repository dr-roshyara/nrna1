## Rationale
Challenges the prior formula Knowledge approx Difference(ObservedState,IdealState): since knowledge is factive and involves belief, ability, and anti-luck/safety, a correct determination (e.g. 'Nexus is behind reference version 3.85') is not automatically Knowledge under the philosophical account -- this is a significant challenge requiring the model to distinguish correctness from knowledge-hood. [S0707] Millar's recognitional-ability account challenges the assumption that knowledge extraction is always probabilistic extraction: a trained engineer recognizing 'this configuration is abnormal' is a way of coming to know that need not be a probability calculation; different sources (AI model, database query, sensor, human expert, trusted document) may represent genuinely different ways of coming to know, supporting the idea of multiple extraction regimes -- though it does not specify how to implement them. [S0707]
