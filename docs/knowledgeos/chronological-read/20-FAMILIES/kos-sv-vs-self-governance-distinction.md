## Rationale
Argues the Finding->Action boundary is necessary because a checker finding a violation cannot on its own know whether the deviation is intentional, covered by an exception, safe to change now, in conflict with other in-flight changes, or who holds approval authority — hence Detection ≠ Authorization. [S1023] States the 'bidirectional truth problem': given Architecture=A and Runtime=B, four possibilities exist (runtime is drift and architecture is correct; architecture is historical/outdated and runtime is correct; B is an approved exception; both are incomplete) — Difference ≠ Error, requiring Difference -> Assessment -> Disposition rather than automatic inference, and often human/domain authority. [S1023]
