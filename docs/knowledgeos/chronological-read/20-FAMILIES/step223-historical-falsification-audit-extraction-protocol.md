## Rationale
Formula provenance requirement: e.g. the empirical CDF estimator hat F_n(x) must not be claimed as 'the architecture's formula' without establishing WHY it was introduced -- an actual model of system behavior, an analogy, a statistical experiment, a theoretical framework, or a later synthesis are completely different claims and must not be conflated. [S1552] Divergence analysis makes DDD empirically testable: instead of declaring bounded contexts from intuition, identify SemanticClusters where a concept (e.g. Evidence_Engineering vs Evidence_Governance) repeatedly clusters around different meanings, then test whether the clusters share identity/lifecycle/invariants/ownership/language -- if not, BoundedContext becomes a stronger hypothesis. [S1552] The book can distinguish six epistemic layers of claim ('what we observed' / 'inferred' / 'formalized' / 'built' / 'verified' / 'later understood philosophically'); this distinction is proposed as potentially one of the strongest methodological contributions of the work. [S1552]
