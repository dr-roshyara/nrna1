## Rationale
- [S2741] (ARGUMENT/DISTINCTION) Eliminability-does-not-give-Realization is not a corollary of the other two separations: Zero is defined via Π, which is read-disjoint from Q by construction, so it carries no information about Q's recoverability "by design" — verified rather than assumed.
- [S2742] (DEFINITION/EXPLANATION) Explains why a stratum must have both Zero/non-Zero and Adequate/Inadequate occurring to be informative — strata at deterministic extremes contribute nothing, which is why flattening the redundancy distribution failed (balance is not power) and why a finer stratification variable gave 13x the informative strata.
- [S2743] (WARNING/EXPLANATION) The elimination operator E_S must be typed to the level it acts at and must recompute relative fields after removing S — naive set subtraction leaves stale relative values.
- [S2743] (ARGUMENT/EXTENSION) Proposes candidate FR-003 (anti-circularity): read-disjointness of Π and Q must be verified on the triple (Π,Q,T), because T can derive an output field Π reads from sources Π never declared — "declaration is not verification," evidenced by two concrete KR-BRIDGE-02 failures.
