## Rationale
Analysis: Statistical governing finding: model uncertainty dominates sampling uncertainty by a wide margin and the programme's instruments were calibrated the wrong way round -- across 150,000 randomized trials the suite produced exactly one non-vacuous result (the central factivity theorem, established by just two worlds), while seven of eleven experiments used no randomization at all and produced more. [S2436] States as a principle: Randomized testing cannot tell whether two epistemic conditions are the same condition; it measures how often a chosen generator produces a situation. The three instruments that actually produced results across the eleven experiments were adversarial deterministic cases (2 worlds -> the factivity theorem), exhaustive enumeration over small structured spaces (1,620,000 exhaustive assignments -> the Zero chain), and witnesses (2^11 exhaustive facet subsets -> the two minimal typing sets). [S2436] Argues that I-2: three specification defects (Sat_content not a function on contradictory states, the reason vocabulary incomplete in 2 of 32 cells, operational evaluation not well-founded) were invisible until a case was built to adversarially break them and would have been concealed by implementation -- specification-before-implementation is the only reason they were found here. [S2436] Argues that I-3: over-specification is as dangerous as under-specification -- the typed facet vocabulary has 37 of 41 distinctions doing no work on the motivating suite; each has a provenance but none has evidence; a vocabulary large enough to separate anything separates nothing. [S2436]
