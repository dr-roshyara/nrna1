- **[FORMALIZATION/ARGUMENT]** [S1291]: States and reproduces the source's admissibility law as a strict logical conjunction (Admissible(d,K,t) = Pre AND Invariant AND Assurance AND Authorization) with its own worked counterexample proof that no averaging is permitted: even with three of four conditions true, one false condition (Invariant=False) makes the whole decision inadmissible -- a decision 95% admissible is inadmissible; the safety gate is the invariant clause with teeth (for critical invariants, False implies Block), running before side effects, never as a post-hoc audit.
- **[VALIDATION/ANALYSIS]** [S1291]: Identifies the session-bootstrap mechanism as the chapter's single piece of running code, demonstrating the no-averaging conjunction law and the Unknown-safety default in implementation: its six-way non-collapse (identity != role != eligibility != authorization != ownership != continuation) resolves fail-closed, and any Unknown in the chain yields NOT AUTHORIZED, never inventing identity or authorization; observes that of everything the chapter teaches, the refusal mechanism was built first, before the selector, contract evaluator, or action loop -- read as the architecture's priority statement made in code.
