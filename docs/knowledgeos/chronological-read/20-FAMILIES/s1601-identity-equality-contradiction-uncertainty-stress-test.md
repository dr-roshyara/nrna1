## Rationale
- [S1601] (ARGUMENT/EXAMPLE) Gives a worked counterexample (two states differing only in which distinct evidence item supports the same proposition) showing equality is undecidable until it is settled whether provenance is semantically constitutive of knowledge identity or merely metadata -- concluding equality is ontology/semantics-dependent, not free-standing.
- [S1601] (ARGUMENT/EXAMPLE) Shows via a temporal-interval example (HasVersion(S,v1) valid on [t1,t2), HasVersion(S,v2) valid on [t2,t3)) that two propositions with conflicting content are not automatically contradictory, since contradiction depends on context c as well as content; concludes contradiction is a derived semantic relation Contradicts(p1,p2,c), not a primitive Knowledge-State field.
- [S1601] (ARGUMENT/WARNING) Argues a bare numeric uncertainty value (e.g. P(p)=0.7) has no epistemic semantics on its own, since it could denote any of at least 7 mathematically distinct quantities (probability of truth, observation confidence, source reliability, posterior probability, population frequency, degree of belief, or probability of an answer) -- reinforcing an earlier corpus warning against identifying measurement/probability with knowledge itself.
- [S1609] (CORRECTION/ANALYSIS) Reinterprets the corpus's earlier (Omega,F,mu) measure-theoretic proposal as a probabilistic regime layered over the state space (e.g. Omega=K, the space of possible knowledge states) rather than as part of K itself -- consistent with and reinforcing the parallel Step 246/248 relocation of probability out of the kernel.
- [S1618] (ANALYSIS/OPEN-QUESTION) Downgrades three historically proposed derived-concept equations to conditional/unresolved status: Evidence=QualifiedObservation is conditional pending a defined qualification function; Identity=EntityIdentityRelation risks representing identity without defining it (a relation is not automatically the same as semantic identity, unless equivalence properties are proven); Provenance=TypedDependencyRelation remains unresolved since Step 246's (S1601) open provenance-minimality question is unresolved.
- [S1618] (ARGUMENT/CORRECTION) Explains why equality and transition could not be finalized in earlier steps: both depend fundamentally on which of the 4 candidate ontologies (set/graph/provenance-sensitive/observational) is chosen for K -- set equality, graph isomorphism, provenance-equivalence, and behavioral equivalence are each appropriate under a different ontology, so neither equality nor transition can be specified independently of that prior choice, a genuine mathematical dependency rather than a glossary gap.
- [S1631] (ANALYSIS) Compares the two streams' K definitions directly: Claude's K=(A,R) (S1612) versus ChatGPT's 4-layer Structure(X,R,Q,H,...) (S1601's substrate/relations/qualifications/regime-projections decomposition), rating them only PARTLY the same -- Claude's exclusion of History (H) from K stands on executed evidence, while the inclusion of epistemic Qualification (Q) remains a genuinely open point of disagreement (U-1).
