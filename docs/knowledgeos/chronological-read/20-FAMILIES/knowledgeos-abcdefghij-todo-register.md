## Rationale
Restates the v1.1 witness finding motivating TODO Group A: `K_t=Γ(E_t,Q,C,EC)` cannot be simultaneously an attributive construction and factive when truth is absent from Γ's domain, since two possible worlds with identical epistemic input but different truth values would produce the same epistemic state [S2478]. Offers three factivity-resolution candidates — Rename, Externalize (with an 88.25% survival rate flagged as an experimental result, not an architectural recommendation), and Hybrid [S2478] — and separately argues that `Sat_content` is not well-defined on contradictory states, motivating four candidate resolutions to the contradiction/evaluation-domain problem [S2478]. The reviewing pass (S2479) agrees with the register's overall structure and intent but refuses unchanged approval, objecting specifically to the linearity of its dependency graph: it argues, citing already-run experiments, that Factivity→Contr is not a justified dependency (contradiction semantics can be investigated without deciding whether p is true in the world) [S2479], that the progress ordering ⪰ need not wait for Factivity/Contr closure [S2479], and that semantic equivalence (≡sem) should be downgraded from "foundation of kernel reduction" to "candidate prerequisite" because projection research has not shown equivalence alone suffices — equivalence, invariants, observability, adequacy, and operational preservation are jointly needed [S2479]. This closes the gap of an initially linear, monolithic TODO list by replacing it with an explicit research dependency graph and a six-value TODO-type taxonomy (DECISION/EXPERIMENT/DEFINITION/REDUCTION/GOVERNANCE/RESEARCH) distinguishing semantic questions, research questions, and architectural decisions that had previously been conflated under one heading [S2478, S2479].
