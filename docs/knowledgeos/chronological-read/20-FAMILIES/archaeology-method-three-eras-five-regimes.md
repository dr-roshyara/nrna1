## Rationale
Evidence-map table for II.1: the 688-document/three-era/five-regime count from the archaeology inventory, reasoning-timeline, and corpus-inventory [E]; a governing sentence from GN-12/18 rulings [R]; three structural session-control rules from the GN ledger/coverage report [M]; an instruments list from extant analysis registries [E]; the classification vocabulary including PROCESS/PROMPT from GN-25/GN-26 [R]; and AF-009's characterization of a pre-governance corpus from the archaeology decisions artifact [H]/[E]. [S1201]

The reconstruction sorted 688 documents into three sequential eras (root, kernel, formal) and five methodological regimes inside the formal era (measure-theory crisis, question series, Gita/Sarathi, computational closures, step series), measuring each regime's vocabulary, evidence standard and turnover before extracting any concept; the governing question was 'what happened in here?' not 'what is true in here?'. [S1228]

The archaeology pass built five instruments: a claim registry, decision registry, contradiction registry (tensions mapped before resolution), experiment registry, and a semantic lineage map tracking contested terms (Zero, Omega, Sarathi, etc.) across regime boundaries, because in this corpus the same word rarely keeps the same meaning. [S1228]

The book was produced in BA-6 order: Part III first (the authority surface: III.1-III.10 architecture), then Part I (I.1-I.6 discovery), Part II (II.1-II.4 reconstruction method), Part IV (IV.1-IV.5 implications); each chapter folder holds chapter.md, evidence-map.md, claims.md, unresolved.md per GN-15/16. [S1235]

Catalogs the method's own preserved failures as part of its record (never scrubbed): a coverage overstatement, an unwritten claimed artifact, a governance error where a recommendation was executed as a ruling, a stale instruction caught by verification rather than trust, and (self-referentially) a post-Edition-2 chapter edited after its own conformance check, which is exactly the failure that produced the post-edit re-verification rule (BA-ED2-12) now binding this chapter itself. [S1405]
