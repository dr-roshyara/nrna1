## Rationale

Argues a simple Linga x Yoni model is insufficient for the Gita because it omits Ksetrajna (the knower), proposing instead a three-part model Knower(J) | ActivePrinciple(A) | Field(F) as potentially more interesting than importing a two-pole Linga-Yoni into KnowledgeOS [S2458]. Connects the S2453/S2457 empirical finding (Generation+Examination does not imply Progress) to the Gita's concept of Viveka (discrimination/discernment) as a candidate missing third operation: Generation -> Field interaction -> Discrimination -> Transformation, rather than the tested-and-failed Generation -> Examination -> Knowledge pipeline [S2458].
