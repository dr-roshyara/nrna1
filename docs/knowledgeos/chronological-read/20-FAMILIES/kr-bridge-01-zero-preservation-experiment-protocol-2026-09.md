## Rationale

[S2657] Explains the rationale for the four-cell falsification design: it prevents the experiment from quietly assuming either implication (Zero=>Adequacy would require the Zero+Inadequate cell to vanish; Adequacy=>Zero would require the Non-Zero+Adequate cell to vanish), and neither disappearance should be expected in advance -- the experiment's job is to find out -- with the independence of Zero's and Q's definitions as the other major safeguard against circularity, positioned as the experiment to run before X3 is interpreted as evidence for a general algebra. [S2657] Argues that KR-BRIDGE-01 strengthens (as a governance/research conclusion supported by current evidence, not a mathematical theorem) the separation KR-ZERO _|_ KR-REP-REDUCTION, since before the bridge experiment the possibility that Zero is somehow a preservation criterion remained live, and approves that the experiment did not respond to its negative result by inventing another bridge, correctly following the protocol's prohibition on advancing a bridge hypothesis under Outcome A. **Theme 1 — The KR-BRIDGE-01 experimental protocol (23 rows: S2657 ×23).** Commissions a bridge/falsification experiment to determine whether elimination/Transformation-relative Zero has any reproducible relationship to representation-preservation adequacy, explicitly without assuming Zero is a preservation criterion and forbidden from constructing an algebra, selecting a carrier, or modifying Theory v1.2. Specifies four competing hypotheses (H0 no relationship, H1 conditional association, H2 configuration-specific prediction, H3 confounded apparent relationship); mandates a parallel (non-sequential) representation family to avoid a data-processing-inequality artifact; prohibits pre-declaring a mathematical carrier; requires the preservation target `Q` be defined independently of Zero's own observable `Pi` (explicitly prohibiting defining Zero in terms of `Q`, which would make the experiment circular); requires a four-cell crossing design (Zero×Adequate, Zero×Inadequate, Non-Zero×Adequate, Non-Zero×Inadequate) with pre-declared parameter sweeps across ten parameters; mandates confounding analysis stratified by transformation/observable/contract/representation-family/source-structure/elimination-size, forbidding reporting an association that disappears after stratification as a general finding; requires testing the transformation×observable interaction; retains but does not presuppose conclusions from the KR-ZERO determination-order analysis; requires six mandatory pre-interpretation controls and independent train/test populations with pre-declared seeds; mandates ten falsification questions (Q1-Q10); defines four mutually exclusive outcome classes (A: no relationship, B: conditional, C: robust — the only case licensing a new bridge hypothesis, D: design failure); specifies two exact-filename deliverable artifacts; mandates six epistemic labels ([THEORY]/[EXP]/[NEG]/[PROP]/[OPEN]/[DESIGN]) with explicit anti-inflation prohibitions and immutability of Theory v1.2 during the experiment; defines success as a clear established/not-established/next-test verdict rather than finding an association; and explains the four-cell design's rationale (it prevents quietly assuming either implication direction).
