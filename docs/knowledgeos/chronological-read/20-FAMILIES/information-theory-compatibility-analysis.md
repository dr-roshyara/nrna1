## Rationale
Finds Zero(K,G,EC) (evaluated over a finite requirement set with sufficiency rules Gamma, returning one of ten statuses per requirement, boxed 'Zero != simple subtraction') fits Shannon's measure-free structural layer: requirements generate a finite meet-semilattice under partition refinement, and Zero is the set of requirements not yet in the satisfied down-set -- an antichain in a lattice, an ORDER rather than a NUMBER; the corpus's own boxed rejection of simple subtraction is exactly the statement that this lattice is not a measure, stated without the vocabulary. [S2315] Decisive incompatibility: Shannon entropy, mutual information and KL-divergence are all functionals of a declared probability distribution (Omega,F,P), which has zero occurrences in the corpus and was explicitly ruled unnecessary by Step 282's T-3; therefore the entire Shannon quantitative layer is unavailable to KnowledgeOS by the same ruling that declined probability, leaving only the measure-free lattice layer (which supports refinement/coverage/gap structure but no number). [S2315] Decisive incompatibility: Floridi's veridicality thesis holds semantic information is necessarily truthful (false 'information' is not information), but the corpus's Closure-03 result 'Admission is not a truth function' deliberately retains Refuted assertions as first-class members of A; adopting Floridi's thesis would make Refuted assertions inexpressible -- classified as the same shape of error as adopting S5 veridicality for Sigma in the DEL comparison document. [S2315] Finds Dretske's information-carrying definition (signal r carries the information that s is F iff the conditional probability of s-is-F given r and receiver prior knowledge k equals exactly 1) strictly stronger than what KnowledgeOS's Evidence(O,P,C,R) (a relevance relation under a rule) can supply, especially since the verification-lane Qualify function has no implemented body (TG-14); concludes Evidence is not Dretskean information-carrying and cannot become so without reversing the T-3 no-probability ruling. [S2315] Final verdict: information theory is the weakest fit of the five external theories compared in this series -- no entropy/mutual-information/KL is available (all require the declined distribution), Floridi's veridicality thesis directly contradicts the corpus's Admission-is-not-a-truth-function result, and Dretske needs a channel and independence/prior structure the corpus lacks -- but the measure-free partition/coverage lattice does exactly fit Zero, and the corpus's own 2026-08-25 conclusion ('information is not meaning is not knowledge') is endorsed as correct; the analysis's own addition is identifying that the surviving residue is specifically a LATTICE, and that Zero already sits in it. [S2315]
