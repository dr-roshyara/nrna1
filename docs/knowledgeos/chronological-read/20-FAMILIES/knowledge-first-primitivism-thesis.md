## Rationale
States the knowledge-first primitivism thesis (knowledge is not analysable into belief+truth+justification, via failed-Gettier-analysis and a coloured/red analogy) and formally defines the Factive Mental State Operator (FMSO: factive, stative, propositional, unanalysable) with the inference rule 'if Phi is an FMSO, from S-Phis-that-A one may infer S-knows-that-A'; translated as K_t should be represented as a mental state of the knower, not merely stored propositions, and treated as a primitive status. [S2530] Regress-of-justification argument (justification chains must terminate somewhere; they terminate at knowledge) translated as: K_t (knowledge state) is the foundation of the justification chain, and Determination should be grounded in K_t rather than an infinite regress.
