## Rationale
Produces a preliminary classification of ~29 corpus terms into domain-concept/mechanism/governance/ambiguous categories with a strength verdict per term, flagging State, Context, and Transformation as the most ambiguous (red) and Observation, Evidence, Assessment, Determination, Validation, Truth, Belief, Event, Action, Command, Authority, Governance, Conflict, Unknown, Identity, Invariant as comparatively strong (green) -- explicitly an audit finding, not a frozen vocabulary [S1616]. Poses 3 explicit unresolved models for the Claim-vs-Assertion relationship (synonyms; Claim as proposed vs Assertion as admitted/represented; or genuinely different bounded contexts), concluding none is currently established -- this question remains open [S1616]. States the step's most consequential general finding: a mathematically elegant kernel tuple like K=(K,C,T,E,A) can be semantically ill-typed BEFORE it is mathematically ill-typed, since single-letter symbols like C (Context, in >=4 senses) and A (Authority/Assessment/Action/Assertion) are each linguistically overloaded; proposes a semantic-type-discipline rule requiring every mathematical symbol to have exactly one declared semantic type per scope (e.g. E_obs:Observation vs E_ev:Evidence) as a genuine prerequisite for proving any transition function well-typed, not mere notational cosmetics [S1616]. Runs a corpus-wide (1467-file) ubiquitous-language census with file/step counts per term, confirming and quantifying several terms flagged elsewhere as overloaded (Evidence: reference-vs-object; Context: two meanings; Authority: actor-vs-trust-rank; Provenance: three distinct objects under one word; Validation/Validity/Verification: three words with unclear boundaries; Validity: five sub-predicates), and identifying two corpus concepts (Missingness, Non-identifiability) as effectively abandoned/dead after specific step numbers rather than reconciled [S1617]. Sharpens the open Claim-vs-Proposition question (S1616) into a specific alternative: rather than Claim=Proposition, propose Claim=Role(Proposition) via a Claims(actor,proposition) relation, since a proposition can exist unasserted -- judged more DDD-consistent than plain identification [S1618].
