Warns against AI language that collapses Inference into apparent HistoricalFact (e.g. 'the decision was probably made because...'), worked through a concrete example (a 2026-08-10 migration approval with evidence but no recorded rationale: an AI-generated explanation must be represented as Hypothesis, not HistoricalReason, with Rationale=Unknown unless explicit evidence exists) -- Hypothesis != HistoricalReason.
