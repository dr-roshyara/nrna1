The unhedged Evidence=Knowledge thesis (a false, even justifiedly-believed, proposition cannot be evidence) translated directly: Sat(K_t,r) requires knowledge not true belief, Boundary should distinguish known from merely-believed, Provenance must track known-vs-believed.
