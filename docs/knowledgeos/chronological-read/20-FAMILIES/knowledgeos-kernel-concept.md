## Rationale
The corpus's own explicit rationale-bearing rows (family.rationale_evidence had 15 entries visible; family.rationale_truncated_count = 57, so 57 further rationale-bearing rows exist beyond what is synthesized here — full text in `03-CONTRIBUTIONS.jsonl`) converge on a commercial-then-architectural arc:

The earliest rationale (B0005) is commercial, not architectural: [S0163] argues KnowledgeOS's defensible moat is "the kernel plus governance plus evidence model," using the Linux/Red-Hat precedent to justify an open-core business model; [S0167] explicitly warns against starting from an AI-driven "Digitalization Robot" ambition and instead recommends starting from "the smallest trusted kernel," recommending an open-source Kernel plus commercial Enterprise Platform, and a specific multi-language stack; [S0169] argues commercial positioning category determines addressable market. [S0176] is the corpus's own self-noted tension (T1): a minimal AI-free kernel proposal versus an AI-driven robot as the platform's top layer, "resolved by the corpus's own framing... but not formally decided." [S0201] (H-10) is the first attempt at a testable kernel/higher-layer split by concern (identity, evidence, provenance, lifecycle, authority, invariants, temporal state vs. assurance/analytics/inference/etc.), rating some concerns "supported" and others "weak."

The B0010 phase (the bulk of the corpus, S0363 onward) is explicitly architectural DDD discovery, and its own stated rationale is that a "small kernel" hypothesis must be tested against two named risks: being too small (letting essential responsibility leak into mechanisms) and too large (becoming a "God Aggregate") [S0363]. The recurring self-correction pattern throughout B0010 is that a reviewing author repeatedly refuses to accept a first-pass DDD conclusion (e.g. "Kernel = Admission Boundary") as decided, insisting instead on testing via "which invariant cannot be protected if this concept is outside the aggregate?" [S0363]. This produced, and then falsified, a "six-part aggregate" hypothesis (Identity+Evidence+Justification+EpistemicState+Confidence+History): initially proposed as necessary because the six parts are "mutually defining" [S0363, S0365, S0366, S0368, S0369], then explicitly FALSIFIED in [S0370] on the grounds that "semantic relatedness and traceability do NOT imply transactional atomicity" — the corpus's single most consequential internal correction. The stated purpose of the subsequent, narrower "Identity + Evidence References + Justification History" replacement boundary [S0370] is likewise flagged as needing the same rigor rather than being accepted outright [S0370, S0372].

The rationale for the extensive philosophical-lens apparatus (Nyaya, Navya-Nyaya, Shiva-Shakti, Ganesha, Zero, Gödel, Topology, ten Chinese lenses, Tarka/Nyaya logic, Williamson, Audi — S0383, S0384, S0393-S0399, S0402) is stated explicitly in [S0383]: lenses are "observation instruments," never "sources of architectural authority in themselves," with DDD (and a small set of "architectural-adjudication" lenses: Contextual Completeness, Negative Epistemology, the Gödel boundary, Zero) reserved as the only lenses that can actually decide what belongs in the Kernel. The corpus's own retrospective verdict on this entire apparatus [S0388] is that its value lies not in any design it produced (all designs were rejected) but in the convergence of independent rejections onto the same small boundary — explicitly, "the discussion's value is not in the designs it produced... its value is that the rejections converged, from many independent directions, onto the same small boundary."

A distinct meta-review thread (B0015, S0593-S0632) supplies a second-order rationale: it exists specifically to test whether the many independent Kernel-boundary proposals in B0010 are actually independent, finding several share prompt lineage and are not independent arrivals [S0611, S0614, S0616, S0618], and that a much-repeated "intersection = {Evidence, Provenance}" finding is "arithmetically correct but near-uninformative" [S0606] since a third list can only shrink or preserve an existing intersection.

A later Gita/Vedic-lens thread (B0020, B0054) gives a different rationale register: not DDD justification but a philosophical framing exercise testing whether "Kernel = discriminative epistemic mechanism operating on a changing state" (Manas/Buddhi analogy) is a better lens than "Kernel = storage," explicitly kept as "candidate research... not canonical" [S0818, S2219, S2235, S2252].
