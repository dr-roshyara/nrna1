## Sources (how this label entered the ledger)
- OBJECT-INDEX · batch B0033 · scope THEORY-LEVEL: Step 182's central proposition: 'authority is bounded' by Domain, Proposition/DecisionType, Scope, Time, Context -- no actor or system possesses universal organizational authority, and no single AuthorityLevel number is meaningful (Authority=10 is meaningless without 'authority for what'; must be Authority(a,Action,Scope,Time)). Applies this reflexively to KnowledgeOS itself: it may be authoritative as RecordOfDecision but not as CurrentOperationalReality, and may store an ArchitectureDecision without possessing AuthorityToChangeProduction -- 'the knowledge repository records authority; it does not automatically possess authority', preventing the system from becoming a hidden governance mechanism. Applies this to AI specifically: retrieving Decision=Approved does not let an AI infer 'I am authorized to execute' -- AuthorizationValid must be separately established, an agent-operating-contract requirement. States the platform's proper structural-enforcement role via a type-system analogy: 'KnowledgeOS enforces the integrity of governance artifacts; domain governance defines what those artifacts mean' -- the platform can enforce 'this object must have valid structure' (e.g. EveryDecisionHasAuthorityBasis, EveryDeterminationHasEvidenceBasis, EveryAuthorizationHasScope, EveryHistoricalArtifactHasTemporalContext) without inventing the domain rules themselves.
