## Rationale
Systematic elimination of single-structure candidate models for K: Set model (K subseteq X) captures membership but loses ordering/provenance/contradiction/uncertainty/history; Graph model (G_K=(V,E)) captures relationships naturally but not uncertainty/epistemic-status/temporal validity/probability; Probabilistic model (K~P_theta) is useful for uncertain phenomena but most architecture statements (e.g. 'Class X extends Y') are deterministic structural facts, not distributions -- Knowledge != ProbabilityDistribution; probability is a property of some knowledge, not its universal representation [S1563]. DDD-mathematics alignment table: bounded context protects M_C, context mapping defines phi_AB, transformation executes T, governance authorizes A, evidence supports E, lineage records lambda -- diagrammed as a candidate KnowledgeOS meta-model with Context above a Transformation node fed by Evidence and Authority and producing Knowledge K (Graph/Epistemics/Time/Provenance/Uncertainty) [S1563].

Principal result: four capabilities (authority, validation, governance, replay) are natively representable (Y) by NO model at all, including the hybrid Model F which scores only P on all four -- interpreted not as a model deficiency but as evidence these four are not properties of a knowledge state at all: Authority is a relation between actor and action (a property of a transformation, not a state), Validation is an external assessment of a state (a state cannot validate itself without circularity), Governance is a relation between policy and transformation, and Replay is a property of a history of transformations, not any single state [S1568]. The four-capability result vindicates Step 230's kernel decision to keep Authority A separate from K while directly refuting Step 231's own preferred hybrid Model F for trying to absorb authority (among other things) into K -- Steps 230 and 231's proposals are shown to be in unnoticed tension, and the matrix independently confirms the mandate's own hint that the correct answer may be a typed family of related structures rather than one tuple [S1568].
