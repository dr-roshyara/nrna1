## Rationale
Step 220 frames divergent interpretation (the same observation producing two different, later-discovered-incompatible principles I1 and I2) not necessarily as an error but as potentially revealing a previously hidden bounded context. [S1547] Step 220 argues that when the same term (e.g. Evidence) carries different meanings across contexts, the correct DDD response is not necessarily to force one universal definition but to recognize distinct bounded contexts -- concept divergence is framed as a boundary-discovery mechanism.
