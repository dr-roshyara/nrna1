## Rationale
The evidence base attributes the following rationale to this object (as recorded, cited to its source):

- **[EXPLANATION/EXPERIMENTAL-RESULT]** [S1176]: On the evening of 2026-08-25 a new corpus opens with a confident question: where should the work concentrate -- measure theory, or the definition of KnowledgeOS itself? The first documents choose mathematics: Knowledge would be a measurable structure, sigma-algebras would carry epistemic states. It lasted roughly a day. The crisis documents of the same phase record the failure honestly: the spaces in question refuse the machinery -- what exactly is measurable when the 'space' is everything an unbounded world could present, what does an observer integrate over when no observer stands outside. The foundation was abandoned -- graded ABANDONED by the archaeology, the first of the programme's great discarded alternatives (ALT-01) -- and the vocabulary turnover that followed was nearly total: the corpus stops speaking measure theory within days and never resumes.
- **[ANALYSIS/EXTENSION]** [S1176]: The crisis forced the dismantling of Omega, the inherited ambient space, productively: three different fates for three different roles -- the ideal-reference role was absorbed (eventually into the IdealState-and-contract machinery Part III.2 describes); the unboundedness was dropped, bounded away as a modelling decision; and the whole-space-observation role was inverted into the complement principle X_t != Observed(X_t) that survives as invariant I-7. An assumption of total view became a prohibition of assuming one -- arguably the crisis's finest product.
- **[ANALYSIS]** [S1176]: The crisis also set the method for everything after: when the mathematics failed, the corpus did not lower its standards -- it changed instruments, turning from foundations to questions (the Question series Part I.5 enters), and kept the demand for rigor that measure theory had been recruited for. The instrument was wrong; the appetite was right.
- **[RESTATEMENT/ANALYSIS]** [S1387]: Retraces the three-regime genesis of frame-ownership: the measure-theory regime had no Knower (an ungoverned Omega); the Gita/question regime re-homed ownership via the charioteer image and fixed Knower-authorized IdealState; the step regime formalized without re-deciding, then hardened the wiring via falsification and ruling. States the corpus's retrospective 'quiet selection rule' visible only in hindsight: from every lens, invariants were kept and identities were dropped -- what the charioteer OWNS survived as I-1; who the charioteer IS did not (the sovereign-named figure's readings dissolved, and the Atma-Kernel hypothesis was left explicitly unresolved as OQ-8).
