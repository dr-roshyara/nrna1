## Rationale
Argues that defining a transformation delta(K,o) presupposes knowing what o actually is, enumerating thirteen prior questions that must be answered before delta can even be well-defined (what counts as an operation; input state/object; output meaning; determinism; partiality; preserved invariants; identity used; provenance created; reversibility; monotonicity; governance; whether rejection is an operation or an outcome) [S2382]. Argues Step 288's finding that equality remains open makes Step 290 MORE constrained, not less: Step 290 cannot treat semantic equality, observational approximate-equality, and structural equality as interchangeable, and any claim that an operation 'preserves knowledge' is meaningless until the specific equality relation intended is named [S2382].
