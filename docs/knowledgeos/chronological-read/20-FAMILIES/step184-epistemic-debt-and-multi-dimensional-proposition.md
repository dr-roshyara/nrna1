- OBJECT-INDEX, batch B0033, scope THEORY-LEVEL: "Step 184 introduces 'epistemic debt' as a hypothesis analogous to technical debt: a decision with Rationale=Unknown may represent organizational epistemic debt, but only if the information was NotRecorded and important -- genuinely Indeterminate information incurs no debt. Formalizes (explicitly as a research model, not a production metric) EpistemicDebt = Importance x Recoverability x Missingness. States KnowledgeOS could proactively surface epistemic gaps as governance information (e.g. 'this architecture decision is binding, but its evidence chain is incomplete') without automatically invalidating the decision -- extends the Step-182 GovernanceAuthority!=EpistemicAuthority distinction into GovernanceValidity != EpistemicCompleteness (a decision can be Valid AND PoorlyDocumented). Proposes each proposition/decision carry multiple orthogonal dimensions rather than one truth score: P = <Content,Context,Time,Provenance,EpistemicStatus,GovernanceStatus,Authority>, sketched as a KnowledgeAssertion conceptual model (proposition/boundedContext/temporalScope/provenance/epistemicStatus/authorityContext/governanceStatus/relations), explicitly not yet a database schema."
