## Rationale

Maps the Vedanta Tripuṭī (Jñātā/Jñāna/Jñeya) onto KnowledgeOS interpretation (Epistemic Agent / Reasoning-transformation process / Knowledge object-proposition); the engineering extraction is not metaphysical truth but that knowledge without epistemic origin loses accountability. [S0218] Knower is traced in the prior R3->R5 PRESERVED lineage map (Sarathi invariant 'Knower owns the frame'); archaeological additions show it entering via the Gita-lens era (20260826-143231 and 130 further files carrying Sarathi), and Ksetrajna (Knower of the Field) surfacing on the repository side (Character_Definition section 15, H-KOS-Agent-001) — the only concept found in both the formal corpus and the repository constitution under the same Sanskrit lineage. [S1114] Knower (I-1): PARTIAL [E]: Art. 10 + H-KOS-Agent-001 (Ksetrajna); goal-ownership is L2-only. [S1118] States the Knower is the one concept found on both sides of the programme under the same lineage — the formal model's Knower (I-1) and the repository constitution's protected first-class epistemic agent (H-KOS-Agent-001, 'the Knower of the Field') — with the convergence PARTIAL: both make the Knower constitutive and inseparable, but ownership of goal and IdealState is established only on the formal side. [S1134] After the crisis, the corpus reorganized itself around numbered questions -- how does knowledge change state, who owns the goal, what makes evidence sufficient -- and, in parallel, returned to the Gita lens with new seriousness. The era's great gift to the final architecture is the Sarathi (charioteer) reading: the Knower owns the frame; everything else serves it. Of all the era's images, this is the one that hardened into an invariant (I-1) and survived every later regime unchanged. When Part III.2 says the Knower owns problem, purpose, IdealState, and final authority, it is speaking the formalized residue of this reading. [S1185]
