## Rationale

Identifies the Operation Registry (section 6.5) and Transformation Algebra (section 6.6) as the 'blocking pair': both are rated NOT ESTABLISHED, and several other sections' fields (invariant-obligations in 6.4, replay semantics in 6.9, failure semantics in 6.6 itself) are empty specifically BECAUSE 6.5 is empty, making this pair the root cause of the wider implementation-contract emptiness rather than one gap among many; states the governing rule that a section is filled only by a governance act (ratification), never merely by a sound derivation, and that the book documents this contract but never authors it. [S1961] Central finding: the ratified canon defines two legality CONSTRAINTS on state transitions (I-12, the ladder's covering relation excluding skipped moves; A6, that only an authority act, never evidence volume, crosses the Accepted->Committed boundary) without ever defining what a transition itself IS (no signature, no legality condition, no pre/post-conditions); a law quantifying over an undefined relation is not executable, so 'legal transition rules' reach 'ratified' status only as constraints, never as executable semantics. [S1962] Formally declares Transformations = BLOCKED/NOT CANONICAL, and identifies three specific dependency reasons this cannot be filled in isolation: (1) primary -- a transformation contract is written per-operation, and with zero ratified operations there is nothing to write contracts for, so Transformations is blocked BEHIND Operations, not merely alongside it; (2) secondary -- a postcondition is a predicate over the resulting state, which cannot be checked until K1=K2 is decidable, and canon defines neither identity nor equality; (3) secondary -- 'preserves invariant I-n' presupposes the set of I-n is closed, but canon asserts twelve invariants without asserting that is all of them. [S1962] Identifies nine capabilities (C-1..C-9) that the ratified canon logically requires to exist, each traced to a specific ratified forcing element, for none of which is an actual operation defined: C-1 move a ladder rung up (I-12); C-2 attach Committed to an Accepted item by authority act (A6, I-4); C-3 make a Determination Supported->Accepted under AcceptancePolicy (v0.1 concept row, grade READ); C-4 change an in-force policy version via governed decision (I-11, R-1 stratification loop -- GC-1 open); C-5 compose evidence so duplicates don't amplify and corroboration does (I-5, I-6, aggregation operator open by ruling OQ-3); C-6 compute the gap between state and requirement (Zero(K,EC), EC=eta(G,IdealState), R-2); C-7 turn an observation into evidence (the qualification predicate, undefined in canon); C-8 evaluate a decision contract for admissibility (DC 6-tuple, no ratified conjunction over exactly those six); C-9 act and observe the result (the decision interlock, far side open by ruling OQ-4). For every one of the nine, an unratified layer-2 candidate exists outside canon, but none is defined as canon. [S1963]
