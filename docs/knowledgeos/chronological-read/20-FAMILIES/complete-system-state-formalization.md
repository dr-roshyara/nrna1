Note: Introduces Inquiry as a first-class KnowledgeOS object distinct from a linguistic question (Question subset-of Inquiry): Q=(T,P,C,R,Gamma) -- target, purpose, context, requirements, adequacy rules -- specifying an epistemic demand rather than a sentence. Reframes the whole system around a duality of bottom-up (World->Information->Knowledge) and top-down (Inquiry->Requirements->Investigation) directions, closing the loop Inquiry->RequiredKnowledge->Investigation->Knowledge->Answer. Makes the Ideal State INQUIRY-RELATIVE (Q->I_Q=IdealState(Q), not a free-floating construct) and refines Zero to Zero(K_t,I_Q,EC) meaning 'we know enough for THIS inquiry', not 'we know everything.' Defines a Smallest Adequate Answer A_Q*=argmin_{A in candidate-answers(Q,K_t)} Complexity(A) subject to Adequate(A,Q)=true, where Adequate is a conjunction of target/evidence/certainty/context/provenance/decision-condition satisfaction (predicate left as research work); explicitly separates two distinct minimization problems -- knowledge-representation minimization (K_t*) versus answer minimization (A_Q*), which can diverge arbitrarily (large K_t, tiny A_Q*). Establishes that the smallest adequate answer may legitimately be an epistemic negative (Determined/PartiallyDetermined/Undetermined/Conflicting/NotApplicable/Unknown), explicitly preferable to hallucinating a completion (worked example: a migration-feasibility inquiry with only firewall configuration missing should answer 'migration cannot yet be determined; missing firewall configuration', not 'yes'). Uses this to give Inquiry an investigation-control role (search only for gaps that affect THIS inquiry's answer, an inquiry-relative dimension-discovery objective d_Q*=argmax_d [adequacy-improvement-for-Q | investigate(d)]/Cost(d)) and a principled stopping condition (Stop iff Zero(K_t,I_Q,EC)=0), addressing the 'when does the system stop learning' problem. Distinguishes answer minimality as semantic (inquiry-determined relevance), not merely linguistic compression (a 500-word answer can be more minimal than a 20-word one that omits a required qualification). Connects to Dretske's semantic-selection chain (Information->SemanticContent->InquiryRelevance->Knowledge->Answer) and to Cover-Thomas's sufficiency/distortion apparatus (Inquiry->relevance->sufficiency->minimal representation) as the mathematical foundation for the compression step. Concludes with a five-line summary framing: Inquiry defines the destination; Knowledge State defines what has been established; Zero identifies what is missing; Investigation reduces the relevant gap; Answer is the minimal adequate projection of knowledge onto the inquiry. Possibly a re-derivation of the corpus's much earlier Inquiry-as-first-class-object proposals (inquiry-question-layer-refinement B0011, inquiry-context-doubt-to-settlement B0012, the Q_t component of complete-system-state-formalization B0019) rather than a genuinely new discovery -- not reconciled against those within this batch.
