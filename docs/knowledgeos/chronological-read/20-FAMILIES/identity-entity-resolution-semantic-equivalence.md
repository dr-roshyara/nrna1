## Rationale
Connects entity resolution to Step 10's inference machinery: identity claims are themselves inferences, not direct observations, and must be represented as an IdentityAssertion carrying evidence and provenance. [S0866] Reaffirms semantic equivalence alone is insufficient for evidence counting; EvidenceDependency and SourceIndependence must also be checked (connecting to Step 11), so copied duplicate documents are not double-counted. [S0866] Worked example showing entity/environment/temporal identity must all be established before a version discrepancy can be declared a conflict — identity resolution is upstream of contradiction detection. [S0866] Connects identity gaps to the Ideal State: missing identity attributes (e.g. unknown environment) become a legitimate IdentityGap discrepancy, feeding the overall discrepancy Δ_E. [S0866] Shows deterministic infrastructure evidence (DNS + host inventory chaining) can combine with LLM semantic candidates to give a much stronger basis for entity resolution. [S0866] Argues entity-identity claims are supported by evidence via the same general evidence/assessment machinery — identity resolution needs no separate epistemology, a major architectural simplification. [S0866] Shows semantic equivalence can be established deterministically via ontology rules or proposed by an LLM as Candidate — SemanticEquivalence can be rule-based or AI-proposed. [S0866] Warns entity-resolution errors propagate into epistemic errors in both directions: incorrect merging creates false conflicts, incorrect separation hides real ones. [S0866]
