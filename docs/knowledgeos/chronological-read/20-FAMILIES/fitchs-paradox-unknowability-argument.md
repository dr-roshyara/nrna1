## Rationale
Full five-step formal Fitch's-paradox derivation (knowledge distributes over conjunction; factivity; no one can know an unknown truth; weak verificationism that all truths are knowable; therefore not all truths are knowable, since not all truths are known) plus the Moorean-twist corollary (impossible to know p-is-true-and-unknown); translated as: a Gap cannot be known AS a gap, Zero cannot be known AS Zero, Boundaries cannot be fully known -- a structural limit on KnowledgeOS's own self-representation of its unknowns.
