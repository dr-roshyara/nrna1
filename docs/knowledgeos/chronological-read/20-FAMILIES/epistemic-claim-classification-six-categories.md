## Sources (how this label entered the ledger)
- **OBJECT-INDEX** (batch B0056, scope METHODOLOGICAL): Proposed discipline for classifying every candidate architectural claim (e.g. from a 'Knowledge Kernel Architecture - Integrative Summary' source document with twelve claimed principles) into exactly one of six levels before treating it as architecture: (1) Definition -- what a concept means; (2) Invariant -- what must remain true across representations/regimes; (3) Empirical property -- demonstrated by observation/experiment; (4) Mathematical law -- formally proven; (5) Philosophical analogy -- useful inspiration, not architecture; (6) Implementation choice -- one possible realization. Critiques the source document for treating philosophical observations (e.g. 'Knowledge is not a possession -- it is a relationship') as though they directly license architectural requirements without this classification step, and for giving all twelve of its principles equal apparent status despite being different kinds of claims (e.g. 'Identity is relational' vs 'Residuals are signal' vs 'Avoidance is tracked as a state').
