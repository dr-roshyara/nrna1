## Rationale
Central thesis: KnowledgeOS cannot merely observe — Transformation T is mandatory — but T must be performed without attachment to whether it achieves EC=TRUE (Decision Readiness) in order to reach liberation. [S2051] Argues, from 'no one can abstain from action even for a single moment' (Gita 3.3-5), that the KnowledgeOS state-transition function δ is mandatory — i.e. the system architecture cannot have a pure-observation-only mode. [S2051] Derives, from Krishna's argument that leaders must act by example even with nothing to gain (3.20-24, King Janak), a KnowledgeOS governance claim that the system should continue acting/transforming even after reaching Decision Readiness (EC=TRUE), because other dependent users/processes follow its example — an argument for continued action grounded in social/systemic responsibility rather than the system's own evidentiary need. [S2051] Correction of a prior KnowledgeOS wording: the Gita does not literally say 'the mind is always busy determining what is right and wrong'; rather, in Gita 3.40-43 the philosophical structure is that Buddhi (intelligence) must discriminate and govern the lower mental/sensory tendencies (Senses < Mind < Intelligence/Buddhi < Self, concluding at 3.43 with instruction to use the higher faculty to control the lower forces).
