## Rationale
This object addresses a specific, well-posed problem left open by the corpus's admissibility/multiplicity work: raw candidate count |H| is used as a proxy for how much evidence a hypothesis space demands, but the KR-NEFF experiment shows this proxy is not adequate — holding |H|=1000 fixed while varying only the dependence structure among candidates yields effective multiplicities (N_eff) ranging from about 9 to about 941, and a 1000-candidate space with 50 independent groups is empirically indistinguishable from a genuinely independent 50-candidate space [S2467]. The rationale for treating this as more than a curiosity is that the effect is shown to propagate all the way through the pipeline — Complexity→Admissibility→Validation→Determination — not merely a screening-stage artifact, since admission, validation, and unique-determination rates all shift substantially between highly-dependent and highly-independent configurations at the same nominal |H| [S2467][S2468]. The object then documents, with unusual explicitness, its own successive attempts to formalize N_eff and their failures, using each failure to motivate the next hypothesis rather than treating the search as wasted effort. A closed-form fit N_eff≈n(1-ρ)² initially matched a narrow six-point grid well, but widening the domain produced ratio errors from 0.34 to 3.10 (a factor of 9), leading to explicit retraction and the stated lesson that a locally good empirical approximation is not a general law [S2467][S2468]. A second attempt reframes effective complexity via an observational-indistinguishability relation H_i ~_Λ H_j and proposes the quotient size |H/~_Λ| as a candidate [S2467][S2473], but this is refuted by a constructed 12-hypothesis sorites chain in which every adjacent pair is indistinguishable yet the endpoints are not — proving ~_Λ is a tolerance relation (reflexive, symmetric) but not transitive, so the quotient does not exist and cannot define N_eff [S2470][S2471]. A third attempt, delta-packing (the largest pairwise-distinguishable subset), matches measured N_eff almost exactly under duplication/grouping but fails catastrophically under correlation — off by 111x at ρ=0.9 and 222x at ρ=0.95 — because every pair remains individually distinguishable even though the family's maximum behaves like far fewer independent draws [S2470][S2471]. This failure pattern is used to argue for a specific theoretical conclusion: multiplicity burden is governed by the joint extreme-value behavior of candidate scores, a property of the whole family, whereas pairwise distinguishability is a property of pairs — the wrong invariant for this purpose — which is why effective complexity is argued to have at least two distinct components, Redundancy (a pairwise/semantic-equivalence-shaped notion, captured well by packing) and Coupling (a joint/extremal property that packing and semantic equivalence do not reach) [S2471]. A fourth and final attempt, spectral functionals of the candidate covariance structure (participation ratio, spectral entropy, trace/λ_max, rank), is shown to be exact on block/grouped structure but to fail dramatically under equicorrelation, and — compared against delta-packing — is shown to bracket the true measured burden from the opposite direction (packing overestimates, spectral underestimates), with neither capturing it, because the two functionals measure genuinely different things: effective covariance dimension versus a tail-of-the-maximum extreme-value property [S2476][S2477]. The object's closing rationale is explicitly about research discipline rather than a further formula: having exhausted four independent mathematical routes (closed form, quotient, packing, spectral) without success, it argues the correct conclusion is that pairwise/local constructions cannot by themselves represent a joint/family-level property, and that N_eff should be frozen as a known-open, well-posed problem outside the kernel rather than pursued with a fifth formula, on the general kernel-discipline principle against absorbing useful-but-unresolved statistical mechanisms into the kernel merely because they are useful [S2470][S2471].
