[S2374] Argues that Extracts Good's expected-factor-vs-expected-weight lesson (the expected likelihood-ratio factor for a wrong hypothesis can equal 1, making raw factors misleading as an accumulation measure, whereas weights are chosen specifically because they add), generalized into the principle 'choose a measure because its algebra matches the process' / 'metric choice must follow semantic operation, not precede it', aligned with the existing rule to canonicalize Delta before measuring it.
