## Rationale

Uses the infinite-fair-coin-toss fact (every individual infinite sequence has probability zero, yet meaningful events like 'first two tosses heads' have probability 1/4) to warn that measure-zero must never be read as a semantic claim ('does not exist' or 'is unknown'), directly protecting the existing Zero(K_t,I_t,EC) != P(A)=0 non-collapse as two genuinely different mathematical objects. [S2367] Uses the trajectory/coordinate-projection construction to give mathematical precision to History != Current State: the full trajectory omega=(K_0,...,K_t,...) contains the whole history while K_t is only one coordinate projection, while still allowing history to determine the current state under a sufficient-statistic-style model. [S2367] Draws a DDD consequence from the whole measure-theoretic apparatus: at least twelve distinct bounded concepts should be modelled separately (Knowledge Space, Epistemic State, Information Sigma-Field, Evidence, Probability/Uncertainty, Semantic Event, Conditional Distribution, Epistemic Transition, Inquiry, Determination, Validation, Decision) rather than one gigantic Knowledge aggregate, consistent with the DDD direction already reached from Davidson, Freedman, Titelbaum and Brown-Hwang. [S2367] Delivers the final joint verdict on both parts: the reviewed document discovered but did not solve the 'Omega problem' (that there is always a larger space beyond the currently represented state, termed the Lord-lens hypothesis); the measure-theory apparatus reframes this as the sharper question of what mathematical structure that larger space must possess, converging the Gita/Lord-Zero research with Davidson, Dretske, Titelbaum, Brown-Hwang and measure theory structurally without conflating them, and recommends the next research target be formalizing X, A, F_t and K_t before attempting to define any Knowledge probability measure.
