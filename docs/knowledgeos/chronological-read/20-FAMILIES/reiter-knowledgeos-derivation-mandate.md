## Rationale
Frames Reiter's successor-state axiom as a candidate compositional construction for delta (what an action adds, removes, and what persists because it was not removed), explicitly cautioning that this is a candidate formal foundation, not an automatic definition of KnowledgeOS's delta -- motivating the mandate to follow [S2538]. Whether Observation is an action remains OPEN: Reiter's sensing actions are actions whose effect is on knowledge, so the shape exists in Reiter, but the corpus reopened Observation as OPEN in v1.2 and has not closed it [S2561]. Whether Qualify is an operation or an epistemic boundary (rather than a computable function) is not decided; KR-CONTR-EVAL is cited as precedent making the 'boundary' option live, since that programme already found one case (reason) where an apparent function was really a boundary [S2561]. Sensing does not transfer cleanly from Reiter to KnowledgeOS: Reiter's sensing relies on knowledge being modelled by a factive accessibility relation, but R1 decided KnowledgeOS's object is A_t (an attribution), and no KnowledgeOS component may assert Knows [S2561]. [PROP] On corpus evidence (not Reiter): Authority sits OUTSIDE the transition mechanism, because R1 placed verification outside and governance acts are not state transitions [S2562]. [PROP] Provenance sits INSIDE the state but OUTSIDE the transition rule: it must survive the transition (citing KR-CONTR-EVAL's finding that the boundary/reason component is indispensable) but does not gate the transition itself [S2562]. Meta-level interpretation of the 9-of-10 negative-test refutation: no test fails because Reiter's formalism is wrong: each fails because the relevant Reiter construct is well-defined only relative to assumptions KnowledgeOS does not (yet) satisfy -- causal completeness, consistent effect axioms, factive knowledge, and an existing action theory. Reiter's machinery is therefore available exactly to the extent KnowledgeOS supplies the assumptions it requires, and those assumptions are precisely the programme's remaining open items [S2566].
