- OBJECT-INDEX, batch B0033, scope THEORY-LEVEL: "Step 183 proposes a candidate KnowledgeOS invariant: 'every governance-significant determination or decision must be reconstructable from its preserved context, evidence, provenance, authority and temporal state, TO THE EXTENT THOSE ELEMENTS WERE ACTUALLY RECORDED' -- the final clause is essential, since KnowledgeOS must never fabricate missing history; distinguishes Reconstructable from Inferable from Unknown, stating a system that honestly says 'the available evidence establishes X, but the reason Y cannot be reconstructed' has succeeded, not failed. Distinguishes four related-but-different capabilities: Auditability (can we prove what happened?), Traceability (can we follow where it came from?), Explainability (can we explain why it was considered reasonable?), Reconstructability (can an independent future actor rebuild the reasoning state?) -- the architecture is said to be moving toward the fourth. Proposes an experimental (explicitly not-yet-production) reconstruction-quality metric RQ(D) = relevant-justified-elements-reconstructable / relevant-elements-required, and frames the reconstruction problem information-theoretically (does the preserved representation X-hat contain sufficient information to explain D, i.e. P(D|X-hat) should be highly concentrated for the historical decision) as a way to ask the right question without pretending to have empirical parameters."
