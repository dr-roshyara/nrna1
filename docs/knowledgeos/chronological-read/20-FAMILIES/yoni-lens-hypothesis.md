## Rationale
The evidence base attributes the following rationale to this object (as recorded, cited to its source):

- **[ARGUMENT/LIMITATION]** [S2449]: Offers the Yoni Lens as a philosophical interpretation (not a mathematical derivation) of the existing transition equation K_{t+1}=Theta(K_t,Q_t,E_t,A_t,S_t,M_t,C_t): the emergence of a new epistemic configuration from interaction within the field.
- **[ARGUMENT]** [S2451]: Argues the two lenses are individually incomplete: Yoni without Zero produces blind generation (no self-critique), Zero without Yoni produces sterile examination (nothing to examine or renew), while their combination is generative examination.
- **[HYPOTHESIS/ARGUMENT]** [S2451]: Proposes the cycle is a spiral rather than a circle (each iteration deepens understanding rather than repeating identically) and a non-termination principle: the Yoni-Zero cycle never terminates because each new state creates new boundaries, which create new inquiry, which creates new generation.
- **[ANALYSIS]** [S2466]: Gives a claim-by-claim verdict table on the Yoni-Zero document: rejects Yoni/Zero as domain objects, leaves Zero=closure(B) open, judges K_{t+2}=Zero(Yoni(...)) premature, rejects 'KnowledgeOS = Yoni x Zero x Sarathi' and 'complete epistemic engine' framing for now, rejects the non-terminating-cycle claim as stated, and judges architectural integration not yet warranted -- independently converging with several findings later confirmed experimentally in the N-experiment (S2453).
