## Rationale
Adds a governance/institutional-legitimacy step after determination: 'Nexus is not compliant' as organizational knowledge is not merely a mathematical calculation from a determination -- it depends on institutionalized rules and socially established meanings (legitimation giving cognitive validity and normative legitimacy to institutional meanings), directly relevant to governance/architecture work. [S0708] Extends the Nexus example into a ten-layer table (observation/dimension/value/evidence/reason/rule/determination/conflict/owner-decision/social-institutional-fact), arguing KnowledgeOS cannot safely collapse these into one 'fact'. Adds that knowledge is distributed across organizational roles (security architect/infrastructure engineer/domain architect/owner each access different sectors of the total knowledge stock) -- there is not necessarily one person who possesses the entire knowledge state, fitting the infinite-dimension model extremely well.
