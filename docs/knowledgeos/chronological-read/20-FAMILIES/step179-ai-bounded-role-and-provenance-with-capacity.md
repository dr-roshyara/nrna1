- **OBJECT-INDEX** (batch B0033, scope THEORY-LEVEL): Step 179 warns against treating an AI agent as equivalent to a human role (AIArchitect != HumanArchitect): an AI performing architecture-analysis activities lacks organizational accountability, legal responsibility, governance authority, and final approval authority -- its role is a 'bounded role', not a replacement identity, requiring an explicit Role+CapabilitySet+AuthorizationSet definition for any AI agent. Requires agent output to declare its epistemic position explicitly (Actor: ArchitectureAgent / Activity: Assessment / Result: Candidate Determination / Authority: Advisory) rather than presenting a bare Result: Approved unless genuine authority exists. Extends provenance beyond bare identity to Provenance = Actor+Role+Activity+Time+Context (not CreatedBy=John), since the same actor's Role does not automatically imply Authority in every context (e.g. John acting as Observer rather than Architect in a given process) -- Authority is contextual, not role-inferred.
