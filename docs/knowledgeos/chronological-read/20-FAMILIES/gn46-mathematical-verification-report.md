## Rationale
Applying a mandated 6-step compression-family test to each PF member individually (source form, ratified form, what was removed, whether the removal was explained, what ratified property is affected, and the resulting classification), the governing question of whether the source-to-ratified-synthesis compression was deliberate abstraction or loss is answered BOTH: the synthesis mostly performed deliberate, defensible abstraction (8+ members judged defensible with no affected ratified property identified, e.g. PF-5b's operational Zero intentionally held open by OQ-4, PF-6's branches re-homed by a ratified decision D-FA-1, AF-F-11's dropped selector context argument), AND it demonstrably discarded five load-bearing pieces whose absence leaves ratified transitions under-typed: the PF-1 triple (Insufficient/Stale/Prohibited), Gamma (sufficiency rules, PF-5a), Q (epistemic sufficiency, PF-7), the decision-producing 'd-producer' (PF-8's MV-F-6 sliver), and the governance algebra's uniqueness/escalation core (PF-9). Not one of the five breaks a ratified invariant today; each limits the evaluability or expressibility of a ratified element [S1334]. All 27 underlying findings are consolidated into five systemic themes, each with a diagnosed root cause: (1) source-to-synthesis compression -- root: ratification recorded selections without loss-ledgers, part architectural (5 load-bearing members) and part merely explanatory; (2) transformation semantics under-specified (eta, ladder transition calculus, policy dynamics plus genesis, action semantics, adjudication, Learn/F) -- one root: the corpus formalized states/structures/boundaries but never the transformations between them, classified research; (3) identity/equality semantics (evidence equivalence, frame equivalence, requirement identity, K_t equality) -- one root: no identity calculus exists, classified research, with I-5 and replay conditionally defined until it does; (4) authorization/governance resolution semantics (the PF-9 governance-algebra core plus un-ratified escalation/AI-non-override plus AF-F-15's adjudication gap) -- one root: L5 (practice-layer) governance is richer than L2's (ratified-layer) governance surface, classified architectural-candidate; (5) the L2-to-L3/L4 realization gap -- standing and honest, no new members, characterized as a conformance profile rather than a defect [S1334].

Documents that the earlier historical KnowledgeOS work explored a much broader range of mathematical frameworks than the current v0.2 architecture retains: measure theory, probability, entropy, KL divergence, Wasserstein/epistemic distance, topology, knowledge-density/flux, a knowledge-space Omega, and continuous evolution equations -- for example a proposed continuity-style PDE, partial(k)/partial(t) + div(J) = S - D, with quantities like coverage, uncertainty, information gain, stability, and epistemic distance. States that later falsification/archaeology correctly concluded these should not automatically become the KnowledgeOS mathematical foundation, and explicitly frames the current mathematical audit (GN-46, S1326) as doing exactly this same separation work: distinguishing interesting mathematical frameworks from mathematics actually established by the ratified architecture -- consistent with GN-46's own finding that no metric/measure/entropy/topology claim appears anywhere in the ratified material [S1347]. The earlier Step-era handover to the executable phase stated, in substance, 'the model is ready for experiment, not proven theory', and explicitly marked as unresolved: the exact KnowledgeState algebra, the exact Evidence algebra, exact statistical inference, an exact causal framework, exact ontology, aggregate boundaries, persistence, and production technology. This document argues that the current architecture findings -- transformation semantics, K_t equality, evidence aggregation, policy dynamics, the Proposal-to-Decision lift, and computability (all findings registered in this batch's S1326/S1329/S1334) -- are therefore not completely new problems, but later, more rigorous manifestations of questions the earlier Step-era work had already discovered were open [S1347]. Sketches a twelve-stage historical evolution pipeline: early conceptual model -> KnowledgeState/lenses/journey -> Sarathi+Zero+Lord separation -> 'before we continue'/objective revision -> formal Questions -> the 25-series formalization -> Step 049 kernel reduction -> Step 050 adversarial audit -> falsification -> v0.2 canonical synthesis -> Final Architecture -> the current mathematical re-audit (GN-46, S1326). Characterizes v0.2 as a compression and purification of a much larger body of theory, explaining why current Edition-2 production is finding exactly the gaps it is finding. Recommends, rather than reconstructing artificial Step numbers beyond 156, building a four-way crosswalk: Step-era theory <-> v0.2 formal model <-> mathematical research <-> Gita Chapters 1-4, which would show which ideas were already discovered, formally retained, compressed, left open, rediscovered philosophically, and are now testable mathematically -- framed as a much stronger intellectual history of KnowledgeOS than simply claiming the Gita inspired the architecture. Poses an unresolved open research question: what mathematical object is actually produced by epistemic discernment -- a single action a_t in A, a three-valued classification D(K_t,G,Policy) -> {Permitted, Forbidden, Undetermined} over candidate actions, or a map D: A -> {epistemically justified classes}? Explicitly states this is not yet known [S1347].
