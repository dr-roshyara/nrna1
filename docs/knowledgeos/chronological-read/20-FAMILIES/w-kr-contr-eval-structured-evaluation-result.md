## Rationale
Establishes I7 (Contr≠Zero) holds, but for an uncomfortable reason -- they differ because contradiction produces closure rather than a gap, not because Zero adequately captures contradiction; Zero remains a lens and must not become an evaluation value, and nothing in this experiment supports promoting it. [S2495]

States the theorem that makes the structural (not cardinal) obstruction rigorous: since under the strongest candidate A all nine no-determinate-value conditions evaluate to the identical outcome U, no function of the outcome alone can separate any pair among them -- hence no flat domain of any cardinality indexed by evaluation outcome is adequate; of ten candidate fields, exactly reason separates all nine on its own. [S2495]

Kernel derivability analysis: every step (detecting Pos∧Neg via Represent/Relate/Discriminate, classifying subtype via Discriminate/Qualify, deciding resolution via Challenge/Determine, resolving via Revise/Select) is derivable from the existing candidate power set with no step requiring a new power -- contradiction is a semantic state, not a kernel operator (K2, not K1 since current representation cannot carry the needed distinctions, and not K3 since importance is not irreducibility); explicitly this is a protocol-scale designation, not a KnowledgeOS kernel selection. [S2495]

Retrospectively explains why Zero resisted definition: the old Zero(K,I,EC)⟺Δ=∅ was underspecified because Sat itself was -- when Sat(r)=U one must ask 'U because what?' since U_unobservable is epistemically different from U_not-assessed; the dependency is Sat->Zero, never the reverse, confirmed from two independent directions (this experiment's §8, and KR-CONTR-MODELS §6's saturation finding). [S2495]

Cites the E12 sub-result (reason has 10 distinct values over 21 conditions, does not encode the complete state, and alone fails to distinguish Satisfied/Unsatisfied) as justification for testing Reason-only, Standing-only, and Standing+Reason adequacy separately, rather than merely asserting reason is important -- this matches the later formal result in S2495 §9.1's three-guard test. [S2497]

Records that KR-COMP-2026-09 was commissioned as a direct test of a coupling (composition and frame qualification) already established by a predecessor experiment KR-CONTR-FDE-2026-09's result E-FDE-5 -- confirming the FDE-inspired implementation proposed in S2496/S2497 was actually executed, though its own write-up is not part of this batch.
