## Rationale
Produces a full reconciliation matrix across all mandate-required columns (level, corpus definition, source, decision procedure, computability, equivalence properties, congruence, provenance/governance inclusion, identity involvement, dependencies, contradictions, normative decisions) for eight named relations (=, ≡, ≈, ≅_λ/≅_P, ≅_I, ≅_H/∼_H/≡_𝒯, Continuity, ≡_D). Key entries: '=' is computable only relative to canonicalization and is NOT a congruence (executed); '≡' is not fully decidable and has transitivity unestablished; '≈' has no decision procedure since its observation set 𝒪_K is not closed; ≅_I is an equivalence relation only within a context (I_48); ≡_D is the only relation fully specified but sits at the wrong (value) level for K. [S2139] Pins every named equality-family relation to exactly one of ten mandate-required levels: L-value (≡_D); L-assertion (≡_exact, ≡_struct, ≡_sem,C, refinement ⪯, the seven 25J.26 edges); L-entity/object (=_I,=_V,=_S,=_E, ≅_I, EntityID/KAID/RecordID); L-state (=,≡,≈,≅_λ/≅_P); L-history (∼_H,≅_H,≡_𝒯,∼_F); L-provenance (Pi identity, ≅_P); L-system/behaviour (∼_H under all sigma, Continuity); while L-operation, L-event, and L-policy have NO relation defined at all (operation registry 𝒪 unratified; no event schema; nothing at the policy level), and L-observation's 𝒪_K is explicitly not closed. [S2141] Audits fifteen specific candidate level-promotions, finding ten INVALID (e.g. value-level equiv_D to state-level equiv; Sigma structural equality to K structural equality; Sigma order to K order -- the 'F5 error'; approx_X to corpus's approx; finite-sample agreement to equality, executed; hash equality to semantic equality, executed; decidability to congruence, executed; fold equality to behavioural equality; context-scoped cong_I transitivity to global transitivity; similarity threshold to identity), two VALID as proven theorems (X subset Y implies approx_Y refines approx_X, executed; approx_X is an equivalence relation for every X, executed), and three UNPROVEN (corpus's approx is an equivalence relation, blocked because 𝒪_K is not closed so there is no function to take the kernel of; equiv_sem is transitive; any equiv-implies-approx hierarchy, which is additionally forbidden as a chain per 261.9/258.19). Concludes zero unconditional Level-5 state-level relations, zero proven congruences, and empty implementable/governance-authorized columns across the whole family. [S2141] Restates the four rival result-domain codomains (25S.3, 38.10, 012 §35, 012 §36/46) and the Relation-vs-Status sorting principle (012 §36), applying the mandate's own distinction between 'decidable relation' (a total procedure that returns TRUE/FALSE correctly) and 'procedure that always returns a decision' (a total procedure may legitimately answer Unknown without deciding); boxes the resulting principle that an Unknown-returning procedure is NOT a complete equality decision procedure, and reiterates 012 §35's theorem-shaped result that semantic equality can never reach a total Boolean procedure, agreeing with three other independent loci (224.11, 25S.4, 38.22) that this is a feature, not a failure. [S2141] Produces a ten-relation completeness matrix (=, ≡, ≈, ≅_P, ≅_I, ≅_H/≡_𝒯, ∼_F fold, Continuity, ≡_D, and the author's own ≈_X) across eight columns (Named/Defined/Typed/Parameterized/Decidable/Implementable/Governance-authorized/Congruent), explicitly separating procedure strength from semantic soundness (congruence) as two orthogonal axes -- observing a relation can be maximally decidable and semantically useless, and '=' is exactly that example (fully named/defined/typed but only conditionally decidable and proven NOT a congruence). Result: implementable and governance-authorized columns are empty across all ten relations; zero proven congruences anywhere in the matrix. [S2141]
