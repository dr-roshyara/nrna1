## F. Remaining methodological defects (recorded; none promoted; none changes the frozen core)

1. **Timeline points dated NOT-CONFIRMED are written two ways.** PB02 writes `order: null` with an escalation (7 points). PB03, PB04 and PB05 write ORDERED with `date_applies_to_file: NOT-CONFIRMED` (44 points). Neither violates the protocol, but a ruling is needed before S5.
2. **Hits naming the object's own file in an index or listing** (S1890, S2615, S1426) are ruled FALSE-HIT or UNSUPPLIED-DIMENSION inconsistently. It makes no difference to the result, but a one-line ruling is recommended.
3. **Stage 2B at scale** (hundreds of occurrences) was done with templated per-file category maps. The outcomes are sound, but per-occurrence review is evidenced only at file level. How Stage 2B review is evidenced should be settled before S5 workloads.
4. **MTIME-based selection of a birth file** (PB05) shows that rule 3 of v1.6.6 does not say a candidate cannot be replaced on MTIME grounds. Only one agent did this.
5. **Fetched vs read.** The reader log cannot tell a whole-file read from a fetch. The audit is the only control (PB05).
6. **Pair-linked label files** (PB05:1): FALSE-HIT vs ESCALATED under §12.4 is a judgment call.
7. **Carried over from R2-001 and recorded after S4:**
   - A.4 variant gaps: ASCII→Unicode/LaTeX forms, accented transliterations (abhāva), and placeholder terms.
   - §14.2 rule-4 wording.
   - P1 mtime defects (66 MTIME-basis dates).
   - UTC vs local dates.
   - The P0 cited-date error found again by this audit (S1538: 02-FILES 2026-08-25, frontmatter 2026-08-30).
8. **Stochastic run-to-run variation** in research records and lexical births (§D) is a measured property of the instrument, not a defect to fix in S4. S5 must treat single-run candidates accordingly.
