## 4. Independence assumptions and limitations

**Assumed:** the extractors, M1 and M2 do not read each other's material. This is **mechanically audited** by the tool-call allowlist (§15), not only by prompt.

**Blinding of source identity:**
- **(a) Removed:** the deterministic letter-map shortcut. The map is derived from a runtime secret nonce (§11).
- **(b) Not removed:** source identity may still be **inferred from list structure**, because SEG propositions are atomic and numerous and the E2 output is shorter. **No semantic blinding is claimed.**

**Canaries:**
- They detect verbatim copying of another run's header string only.
- They also confirm that each output was produced under its own prompt.
- They are **not** the main leakage control. The tool-call audit is.

**Limitations kept:**
- single vendor;
- M2 = the E1 model (possible self-preference);
- E2 a smaller model;
- κ conditional on M1 clustering.

**Assumption of the tool-call audit:**
- the harness transcript records every tool call faithfully;
- an agent cannot alter its transcript without a tool call. Such a call would itself be outside the allowlist, and the transcript hash is also re-verified at `result`.

## 9. Quote verification and bounded repair (R4)

**Quote verification (unchanged mechanics; X1 changes the attribution for E runs, §17):**
- EXACT, or WHITESPACE after collapsing whitespace;
- for SEG, the quote must lie inside its segment, else OUT-OF-RANGE;
- MISS = not in the file.
- **Hard requirement (SEG):** zero MISS and zero OUT-OF-RANGE after at most one repair round.
- **Reference quality (E1/E2, X1):** a quote miss after repair blocks VALID as INSTRUMENT-UNDETERMINED and never counts as instrument failure.
- **m5:** no minimum quote length is introduced (that would be a new threshold). The quote-length distribution is reported as a diagnostic.

**Bounded repair sequence:**
1. **`check --pass 1`** on the original outputs writes:
   - `CHECK-1.json`;
   - `FAILURES-<run>.json` per extractor run: `{run, original_sha256, failures: [{key, proposition_id|null, class}]}`. The classes are MISSING-SEGMENT, UNKNOWN-SEGMENT, DUPLICATE-SEGMENT, SCHEMA:<field>, QUOTE-MISS and QUOTE-OUT-OF-SEGMENT.
2. **`freeze --stage check-1`** hashes CHECK-1, `V1-DISPATCH.json` (with per-run entry hashes), every FAILURES file and every original `OUT-<run>.jsonl`, and is committed. The failure list and the originals are now part of provenance, and `result` re-verifies them (X7).
3. **Repair prompt** (§8), once per run that has failures. There is no repair for runs without failures, and no second round.
4. **`check --pass 2`** applies `apply_repair` deterministically and writes `CHECK-2.json` and `REPAIR-DIFF.json`, with ACCEPTED/REJECTED and a reason for each repair record. It **rejects**:
   - UNFLAGGED-KEY;
   - DUPLICATE-REPAIR;
   - ADDED-PROPOSITION;
   - CHANGED-UNFLAGGED (an unflagged proposition in a repaired segment differs);
   - CHANGED-RESULT;
   - SEGMENT-WITHDRAWAL-NOT-FLAGGED.

   Rejected records are ignored, and the original record stands.
5. **Original protection:**
   - if an original output's sha256 differs from its FAILURES record, the output is treated as modified (RUN-INVALID);
   - a repair file written before the check-1 freeze is RUN-INVALID (from the transcript timestamps).
6. **`freeze --stage check-2`**, committed.

Repair can therefore only replace or withdraw flagged records and propositions, or add missing segments. It cannot become a second extraction pass.

## 11. Matching protocol and sealing (R5)

1. **`equalize`** (after the check-2 freeze):
   - generates a **secret nonce** (`secrets.token_hex(16)`);
   - derives the X/Y/Z permutation from sha256(nonce) and the item ids from (nonce, source, original id);
   - writes `MATCH-LISTS.json` and `SOURCE-KEY.json` (nonce, map, item key, `generated_utc`).
2. **`freeze --stage lists`** records MATCH-LISTS and the **hash only** of SOURCE-KEY. MATCH-LISTS is committed. **SOURCE-KEY is not committed** before the unseal stage.
3. **M1** (A01) runs. **`freeze --stage m1`** records CLUSTERS-A01 (committed), `V1-DISPATCH.json` and the **hash only** of DECISIONS-A01, which is not committed before unsealing.
4. **`sample`** runs, then `freeze --stage sample`, committed.
5. **M2** (A02) runs, then **`freeze --stage m2`** (DECISIONS-A02 and `V1-DISPATCH.json`, committed).
6. **`freeze --stage unseal`**: SOURCE-KEY, DECISIONS-A01 and the final `V1-DISPATCH.json` are committed. Their hashes must equal the sealed hashes.
7. **`ingest`** for all eight runs, committed. Then **`result`**, committed.

**Reproducibility after unsealing:** `result` **re-derives** MATCH-LISTS from the unsealed nonce and the frozen (bounded-repaired) outputs, and any difference is RUN-INVALID (X7). `result` is deterministic given the committed files.

**Blinding claim:** the letter map is not derivable before unsealing. Structural inference remains possible (§4).

## 12. Second-matcher sampling rule

**Unchanged:**
- seed = `int(first 8 hex digits of this document's commit, 16)`;
- strata (source_id, list);
- n = min(N, max(5, ⌈0.30·N⌉)) (5 and 30% ARBITRARY);

## 13. κ calculation

- **The κ_target definition is unchanged.** The label is NONE or STATUS:target. Cohen's κ = (p_o − p_e)/(1 − p_e).
- **Expected units (m2):** every sampled cluster × each of its two other lists.
- **NOT-COMPUTABLE** if:
  - M1 or M2 lacks any expected unit;
  - any decision is off-scale, including a **duplicate unit** or a MATCH/PARTIAL **target outside the named other list or of a different source_id** (minor, V1.1 audit);
  - p_e = 1 (m3, degenerate; previously this was reported as 1.0).
- **m1:** the primary unit is unchanged. The two directions of a cluster pair are **not independent**, so κ per ordered list pair is reported as a diagnostic. κ_status and raw agreement are also diagnostics.
- **What κ is:** agreement between two blinded matchers, conditional on M1's clustering.
- **What κ is not:** a validation of the clustering; evidence that matches are **correct** (m4: **agreement ≠ correctness**, since two matchers can agree wrongly); a measure of extraction completeness.

## 14. Structural coverage

**Unchanged:**
- coverage = segments with exactly one SEG record / segments;
- it measures **mechanical accounting of every segment**, not semantic completeness;
- it can be satisfied vacuously (every segment NO-SUBSTANTIVE), which is why capture stays a gate that can block VALID (§16).
- **Hard requirement:** coverage = 1.0.

## 15. Integrity: computed in `result`, after all eight runs (R2)

**RUN-INVALID if any of the following holds:**

1. **Access (read logs, all eight runs):**
   - any refused read;
   - any read outside the run's authorized files (label files for extractors; the six files for matchers);
   - any non-paged read;
   - any page hash that does not recompute;
   - **extractors:** every page of every label file must be read;
   - **matchers:** no pages are required, since reading is optional.
2. **Tool-call audit (harness transcript, all eight runs):**
   - every tool call must satisfy the allowlist (§8 TOOL RULES; `audit_calls`);
   - **Bash:** only `cd`, the paged reader for that run and an authorized file, and the validator. The script-path prefix may contain only `[A-Za-z0-9_./@-]`, so no shell metacharacters or substitutions are possible (X2);
   - **Read:** only the run's inputs, its own outputs, and persisted outputs of its **own Bash calls** that carry the harness `<persisted-output>` marker and lie under the session `tool-results/` directory (X4);
   - **Write/Edit:** only its own outputs;
   - **other tools:** only SubagentHandback, ToolSearch and TodoWrite;
   - **the transcript must be available at `result`, and its sha256 must still match its ingest record** (X5). TOOL-CALLS must match its ingest hash.

   So M2 reading `DECISIONS-A01.jsonl` requires a tool call naming or reaching it. That call is outside the allowlist and is detected, whether made by Read, `cat`, `git show`, `python3 -c`, Grep, Glob, or shell substitution inside an allowed command (X2). Likewise, M1 reading anything of M2's is detected, and M1 is additionally finished before M2's inputs exist (point 5). **The guarantee is relative to the harness transcript being complete** (§21).
3. **Canaries:** every agent-authored file's header carries its author's canary, and no agent-authored file contains another run's canary.
4. **Model identity:** as in §3.
5. **Freeze ordering (transcript and PROVENANCE times):**
   - all nine stages are present and in order;
   - extractors start after `segment-map` and end before `check-2`;
   - M1 starts after `lists` and ends before `m1`;
   - M2 starts after `sample` and ends before `m2`;
   - the unseal happens after `m2`;
   - the sealed hashes equal the unsealed ones;
   - the nonce was generated after `check-2`.
6. **Original output modified after its failure list,** or a repair file written before the check-1 freeze.
7. **Provenance (X7):**
   - any staged file differs from its latest freeze hash;
   - any dispatch entry differs from its first frozen form;
   - MATCH-LISTS is not reproduced from the unsealed nonce.
8. **Linkage (X5):** see §3. A missing or truncated transcript is RUN-INVALID.
9. **A §18 stop condition.**

## 16. Diagnostic metrics, and capture (R1)

**Capture is a relative recall measure against the E1∩E2-agreed set, not a capture–recapture estimate and not evidence of semantic completeness.**

- **Agreed set:** E1 clusters that M1 matched (MATCH) to an E2 cluster.
- **capture_strict:** the share of the agreed set that M1 matched (MATCH) to a SEG cluster.
- **capture_lenient:** the same share, counting MATCH or PARTIAL.
- **NOT-COMPUTABLE** if:
  - the agreed set has **fewer than 20** clusters (**ARBITRARY engineering rule**); the observed value is still reported as a diagnostic;
  - **any extractor run (SEG, E1, E2) did not complete** (X6): no capture is computed from partial output.
- **A low capture is reported with its possible causes:**
  - SEG omission (instrument);
  - matching or granularity effects;
  - M1 judgment bias;
  - a residual scope difference between SEG and the open-ended prompts.

**Other diagnostics (never gates):**
- the Chapman E1–E2 estimate (independence not established);
- clusters per source and file;
- overlap tables;
- κ_status, κ per direction, raw agreement;
- M2 clustering objections;
- the SEG NO-SUBSTANTIVE rate;
- type, status and decision distributions;
- the quote-length distribution;
- repair diffs;
- pages and bytes read per run;
- wall time.

## 17. Decision rules (R1, R3, X1, X6); every number ARBITRARY except the hard requirements

**Outcomes:** INSTRUMENTS-VALID · NEEDS-REVISION with qualifier ∈ {INSTRUMENT, INSTRUMENT-UNDETERMINED, RUN-INVALID} · NOT-USABLE. The rules are applied in order (`decide`):

1. **RUN-INVALID:** any §15 condition → **NEEDS-REVISION / RUN-INVALID**. **This is never evidence about the instrument.**
2. **NOT-USABLE (instrument evidence only):**
   - a **completed** SEG run with coverage < 0.95 after repair;
   - a **completed SEG** run with a quote-miss rate > 5% after repair (X1: E1/E2 runs never trigger this);
   - κ_target **computed** and < 0.40.

   **Capture never produces NOT-USABLE.**
3. **INSTRUMENTS-VALID**, iff all of:
   - all runs COMPLETED;
   - schema clean;
   - coverage = 1.0 (hard);
   - zero SEG quote misses (hard);
   - zero E1/E2 quote misses (reference quality, X1);
   - κ_target computed and ≥ 0.60;
   - capture_lenient computed and ≥ 0.80.
4. **NEEDS-REVISION / INSTRUMENT:** the run is valid, but a predefined engineering requirement is failed. This covers:
   - a SEG run that failed with CONTEXT-EXHAUSTED (an instrument feasibility property);
   - schema violations;
   - coverage < 1.0;
   - SEG quote misses > 0;
   - κ_target computed but < 0.60;
   - capture computed but < 0.80 (with its possible causes listed).
5. **NEEDS-REVISION / INSTRUMENT-UNDETERMINED:** the run is valid, but the evidence is insufficient. This covers:
   - κ NOT-COMPUTABLE (missing or off-scale units, or p_e = 1);
   - capture NOT-COMPUTABLE (agreed set < 20, or an extractor run incomplete, X6);
   - **E1/E2 quote misses after repair** (reference-extractor quality, X1);
   - a non-SEG run that failed, or a SEG run that failed for INFRASTRUCTURE or OTHER reasons.

**Classification of the named cases:**

| Case | Qualifier | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| κ NOT-COMPUTABLE | INSTRUMENT-UNDETERMINED | insufficient measurement |
| capture NOT-COMPUTABLE | INSTRUMENT-UNDETERMINED | insufficient measurement |
| context exhaustion, SEG | INSTRUMENT | feasibility property |
| context exhaustion, E / M | INSTRUMENT-UNDETERMINED | not a property of the instrument under test |
| access failure | RUN-INVALID | integrity |
| model mismatch | RUN-INVALID | integrity |
| E1/E2 quote misses | INSTRUMENT-UNDETERMINED | reference quality, not the instrument (X1) |
| missing or truncated transcript | RUN-INVALID | integrity cannot be verified (X5); this also applies to a FAILED run that left no transcript |

**Flagged, not changed (outside the authorized scope):** E1/E2 **schema** violations still count under "schema violations" → INSTRUMENT. That is the same attribution class as X1, and it is left for a human ruling.

**The §17/§18 contradiction is resolved:** an incomplete agent is classified by the table above, never by the coverage figure of an incomplete run.

## 18. Stop conditions

**Stop immediately, record and report; no further agent is dispatched.** The outcome is **NEEDS-REVISION / RUN-INVALID** for each of:
- a hold-out refusal;
- a content-hash mismatch;
- a read outside the authorized files;
- canary leakage;
- a model-identity mismatch;
- a tool call outside the allowlist.

**An agent that cannot finish** is recorded as FAILED with its `failure_reason` and harness evidence. The run then continues to the remaining steps where possible, and the outcome is classified per §17. **No re-dispatch** of any run (m6). There is no automatic continuation to the v2 pilot.

## 21. Explicit limitations

- **Scale:** six previously exposed files, two labels.
- **Single vendor:** the different-vendor model is not demonstrated as selectable (§3 observation).
- **Model overlap:** M2 = the E1 model; E2 is smaller.
- **κ scope:** conditional on M1 clustering, and agreement ≠ correctness.
- **Capture scope:** blind to propositions all sources miss, and dependent on M1 alone.
- **Structural source inference:** still possible.
- **Tool-call audit:** it relies on the harness transcript's completeness. Linkage, truncation and tampering after ingest are checked (X5), but completeness itself cannot be proved.
- **Arbitrary values:** the segment size, sample fraction, agreed-set minimum and every gate.
- **File selection:** chosen after sizes were visible.
- **E1/E2 schema violations:** still attributed as INSTRUMENT (flagged for a ruling; §17).
