## A.2 Semantics (proposed text for addendum §2)

1. **`absences.<dim>.reason` and `stage2_dispositions[*].reason`.** Agent justification text. It is META whatever the parent's type: the evidentiary content of an absence or a disposition lives in its structural fields (`resolution`, `supplied_by`, `by_dimension`, the source key). A reason creates no claim and needs no anchor.
2. **`timeline[*].historical_position`.** The agent's narrative of a point's position. It is META and **explicitly not a precedence input**. Precedence is A.10-only (§4.2, FD-1′c). An ordering asserted in this text is an unverified statement, and no predicate may consume it.
3. **`escalations[*].field`.** A structural address inside the same object. When it carries an S-id, the value must be exactly `timeline[<S-id>].<order|date_applies_to_file|change_vs_previous>`, where `<S-id>` is one of this object's `timeline[*].source_id`; otherwise R7-U FAIL. It is never a source reference. Its only effect is the historical rule-(b) null exemption (G-LOG-0023), which v2.3 already applies.

## A.3 The non-interference invariant (proposed addendum §3.0, INV-LEX)

> **Lexical source-id occurrence ≠ evidentiary support.** An S-id in a value supports nothing unless the value's location is registry-typed EVIDENTIARY (or QUOTE of an EVIDENTIARY element). A META or NONE occurrence is a mention.
>
> A mention creates no Evidence, Claim, Reconstruction input, precedence, lifecycle, statistical membership or canonical identity. It may only:
> - be typed;
> - be constrained by the whole-record lexical gates (G-01, AUDIT, QUARANTINE, S3) and by declared META rules;
> - change byte digests.
>
> Constraints on mentions are restrictive only: they add failures, never claims and never PASS.
>
> **Test obligation:** for every object O and META-only mention X at a registered path, the claim set, evidence obligations, anchors, reconstruction relations, precedence, lifecycle, statistics frame and canonical claim ids of O+X equal those of O, and failures_v2.4(O+X) = failures_v2.3(O+X) − {R7-U untyped failures at the registered paths}.

Why it holds by construction (B §2.2):
- `U.claims()` reads fixed structural paths and ignores the registry;
- Reconstruction reads 02-FILES and structural fields;
- the statistics frame takes no object;
- F2 bars META paths from every consumer.
