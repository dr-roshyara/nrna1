## 2. H-17

- **Null hypothesis (per cell):** conditional on the matching-band profile, exact arity and eligibility, the candidate role is exchangeable within each matched set, and the sets are independent. This is valid only under:
  - (A1) conditional exchangeability given the bands, which is untestable (no unmeasured confounding);
  - (A2) independence across sets.

  It is a model-based conditional test, not a randomization test.
- **Specification gaps:**
  - the weighting under variable r_s;
  - missing or undetermined outcomes. RC-13's "not scored" is **role-asymmetric**: it hits candidates only, because G-SHARED-GROUP controls are never P3a-judged;
  - partial control availability;
  - set-level band definitions;
  - the relaxation position of has-formal-rows;
  - the p formula and seed streams (exact convolution is available: the p-value is Poisson-binomial, with no Monte-Carlo error);
  - the BH input and its dependence assumption (PRDS, or Benjamini–Yekutieli as a sensitivity analysis).
- **Control reuse, measured:** for G-SHARED-GROUP, exact pair_count matching forces controls onto other sets' candidate labels, and 1,180 control label-slots compete for at most 896 labels. Simulated type-I error:
  - with controls from the shared label pool (the G-SHARED-GROUP structure): 0.021–0.043 at nominal 0.05, i.e. conservative;
  - with small reused pools: 0.0092 at nominal q/K for a pool of 40 (≈ 6×), and 0.10 for a pool of 10.

  Also, "the pre-registered alternative of §9E.2 item 6" is mislabelled: the frozen alternative is a *label-cluster* permutation test.
- **BH vs q/K:** q/K is the Bonferroni (single-rejection BH) threshold, not the BH detectable difference in general. Simulated per-cell BH power at m = 40, 0.2 → 0.4: 0.165 with 1 true alternative, 0.338 with 10, 0.584 with 30.
  - Recommendation: **(A)** rename it "Bonferroni-calibrated (single-rejection BH) detectable difference", with an optional pre-registered BH sensitivity table.
- **Power:** ≈ 0.38 is Fisher power; the exact permutation power is 0.382. At q/K: 0.050 (permutation) vs 0.057 (Fisher).

  80%-power detectable difference from p0 = 0.2 (permutation test, r = 2):

  | m | at q/K | at α = 0.05 |
  |---|---|---|
  | 20 | 0.55 | 0.40 |
  | 40 | 0.40 | 0.25 |
  | 100 | 0.25 | 0.15 |
