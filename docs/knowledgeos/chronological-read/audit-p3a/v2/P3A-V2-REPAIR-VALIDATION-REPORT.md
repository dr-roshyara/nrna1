## 0. Sampling method — reuse of already-collected, independently-validated
ground truth, explicitly justified

**This report does not commission a new blind review.** Two ground-truth
datasets already exist from this session's earlier work, both fully
independent of V2's design (collected before V2 was conceived), both already
satisfying every requirement §14 lists (reproducible, documented population,
frame, seed, strata, size, method):

| Dataset | Population | Frame | Seed | Strata | n | Method |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **PRIMARY Evidence Validation** (`primary_evidence_validation_comparison.json`) | 1,104 PRIMARY-provenance candidate pairs Stage 1 found but V1 never generated | `P3A-UNDEFINED-RELATIONSHIP-PROVENANCE.jsonl` | 20260921 | DEP_ONLY, LINEAGE_SUBSTRING_ONLY, BOTH_DEP_AND_LINEAGE, LINEAGE_SOURCEID_ONLY | 100 | Stratified random, proportional allocation, 5-per-stratum floor, blind independent review against raw source text |
| **High-Stakes Independent Review** (`highstakes_agreement_report.json`) | 158 SAME/REPLACEMENT/DERIVED-FROM pairs already in V1's 1,793 | `31-RECONCILIATION-PAIRS.jsonl` | round-robin, deterministic by pair_id | SAME, REPLACEMENT, DERIVED-FROM | 158 (full census of this population) | Blind independent re-review, 4 batches, zero exposure to original verdict |

**Why reuse is the correct methodological choice, not a shortcut**: both
datasets were produced by fresh, independent, blind reviewers reading raw
source text, exactly the standard §14 asks for. Re-running a third blind review
of an overlapping population would not improve statistical power — it would
duplicate collection cost without adding information V2's candidate-generation
question actually needs answered, which is **"does V2 correctly surface the
evidence a human reviewer already independently confirmed exists?"** — a
question these two datasets answer directly and exactly, mechanically, with no
new agent dispatch required.

**One genuinely new, small measurement was added** (§B below): an 8-pair
manual spot-check of mechanism F's post-fix output, since F is entirely novel
(no prior dataset could speak to it).

## E. Mechanism F (prose-trigger) — the one genuinely new measurement

**Before fix**: 538 candidate pairs; a random sample of 6 showed the word
"provenance" or "KnowledgeOS" involved in the large majority. **Quantified
exactly** (not sampled): 370 of 499 prose-only pairs (74.1%) involved just
these 2 of 2,497 labels.

**Fix applied**: exclude the 8 single-word (no-hyphen) labels from serving as
mechanism F's target. **After fix**: 102 candidate pairs (81% reduction). A
fresh, independent 8-pair random sample (seed 7) of the post-fix output was
manually read: **6 of 8 (75%) showed a plausible, genuinely specific candidate
relationship worth adjudicating** (e.g., `adr-aip-04` ↔
`capability-c5-separation-attestation`, driven by "derived from" language
naming a specific capability); **2 of 8 (25%) were weaker/more generic**
(mentioning a broad architectural term like `bounded-context` or
`knowledge-space` alongside a verb, without the surrounding text clearly being
*about* a relationship to that specific label). **This is a small sample
(n=8) — not a population-level precision estimate.** It supports the
qualitative conclusion "meaningfully improved, still noisier than mechanisms
B-E, correctly tagged LOW confidence" but does not support a precise
percentage claim at scale.

## F. Do not overinterpret (§16, direct compliance)

The prior "95.3% Potential P3a Miss Rate" remains scoped exactly as stated in
the PRIMARY Evidence Validation Report: it describes the population of
already-identified undefined signals, not the 1,793-pair V1 corpus as a whole,
and not "95% of KnowledgeOS relationships." **This validation report adds one
more precise number to replace speculation with measurement**: of that same
scoped population, **V2's candidate-generation mechanism now closes 99.93% of
the gap at the "can this even be adjudicated" level** — the remaining question
(would adjudication actually confirm a positive relationship for each) is
answered, for the subset already checked, at 86/86 = 100% in dataset B above,
and remains formally open for the roughly 1,200 recovered-but-not-yet-
individually-validated pairs.

## H. Known limitations (§13.11) and remaining risks (§13.12)

- Mechanism A's provenance is not computed (group-spanning, disclosed).
- `label_confidence` is not surfaced in the evidence bundle (disclosed gap,
  Field Preservation Matrix).
- Mechanism F's precision is measured only qualitatively on n=8; a proper
  precision study (stratified sample, blind review) would be needed before any
  future decision to adjudicate F-only candidates at scale.
- The 1 residual unrecovered pair (compound multi-label target string) reflects
  a genuine, disclosed edge case in deterministic substring resolution, not
  fixed further in this repair.
- This report validates 258 of V2's 3,089 candidates against real, independent
  ground truth (100 + 158, with 257 pairs of overlap already accounted for
  between "already-judged" categories) — the remaining ~2,830 candidates
  (mostly A_WITHIN_GROUP, unchanged from V1, and the new B/E recoveries not
  individually re-validated) are validated only at the mechanism level (via the
  15 synthetic fixtures), not the individual-pair level.
