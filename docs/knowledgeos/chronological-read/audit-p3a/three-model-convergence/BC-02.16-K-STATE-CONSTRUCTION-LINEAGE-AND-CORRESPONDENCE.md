## 1. Executive conclusion

**The single most consequential finding of this pass**: the Step 272/273 documents that the whole
`K_min⁴`/canonical-construction lineage traces back to are themselves **mandates, not executed
results**. Step 273's own file poses 37 numbered questions and 12 required output artifacts for
*someone else* to complete; every internal decision table in it is an unfilled `?` template, and its
own "Required final verdict" section (A–O) lists headers with zero content. This is structurally
identical in kind to the very prompts governing this BC-02.x investigation. Concretely:

- **`K=(A,R,Σ,E_L)` was never proven anything by Step 272 or Step 273** — it is stated, twice,
  explicitly as *"the starting hypothesis, not... a completed proof"* and *"do not call this a
  theorem until the premises are actually demonstrated."*
- The origin trail goes **one link further back than BC-02.15 established**, and terminates in an
  **unlocated document**: Step 273 attributes the tuple to "Step 272," but neither `step_272a` nor
  `step_272b` (the only Step-272 files found in the bounded corpus search) actually states or derives
  it — `272a` derives an 18-operation taxonomy, `272b` derives a *different* object (`Σ_min`, a
  4-state structure). A plain, non-suffixed "Step 272" file is **named but not found**.
- `K_min⁴` remains, by the primary source's **own final ruling** (S1792 §276.21, which explicitly
  *withdraws* an earlier passage's "CLOSED" characterization), a **hypothesis, never tested against
  its own cited criterion, on any of its four parts**.
- Canonical-construction `K`'s tested object is more precisely characterized than before: its
  top-level structure is **`(D_t,𝒜,ℛ,Σ_c,E_L)` plus external `H`** — `Π` and `t` live *inside* each
  assertion in `𝒜`, not as separate top-level fields. (This corrects a flattened
  "`(D_t,A,R,Σ_c,E_L,Π,t,H)`" phrasing used in some earlier summaries — see §2.) Its relationship to
  Step 273 is **one-directional and unreciprocated**: the package offers its own result "as input to
  Step 273's own... tests," but Step 273's own file contains zero mention of it.
- **No mapping of any kind was ever proposed between `K_t¹¹` and `K_min⁴`** — not merely untested, but
  never asserted to exist by any source. This sharpens BC-02.15's `UNRESOLVED` verdict for that pair
  to `UNTESTABLE` (see §2, Status Change 1).
- Every other lineage question investigated in this pass (`S0881`↔`S2377` derivation,
  `K_t¹¹`'s own construction, the ratified-8-primitive's relationship to either tuple,
  `knowledgeos-sim`'s relationship to the historical corpus) returns the **same `UNWITNESSED` verdict
  BC-02.15 already reached**, now confirmed by fresh, targeted negative searches rather than reused
  by default.
- **Q16 (canonicalization eligibility): NO**, more firmly than after BC-02.15 — the central
  candidate's own primary source formally withdrew its strongest prior claim, and the two
  most-discussed "rival" objects (`K_min⁴`, canonical-construction `K`) turn out to share barely any
  task overlap (2 of 18 operations) once their actual operation vocabularies are compared directly.

## 7. Canonical-construction provenance and validation status

**Exact candidate tested**: `K=(D_t,𝒜,ℛ,Σ_c,E_L)` with `Π` (provenance) and `t` (timestamp) carried
**inside each assertion in `𝒜`**, plus `𝒦=(K,H)` where `H` is external (`03-CANONICAL-K.md` §3, lines
36–41). This corrects the flat eight-field phrasing used in some earlier summaries (§2, Status Change
3) — `Π`/`t` are sub-components of `𝒜`, not peers of it.

**Removal tests executed**: all 8 components (`A,S,Π,R,E_L,T,D_t,H`, treating `Π`/`t` as
separately-removable for test purposes despite their structural nesting) were each tested by
`bandtest.py`'s deterministic `NEEDS`-table computation over 14 forced + 4 "band" operations (18

(4); `T`→4 (2); `D_t`→1 (1); `H`→1 (1, `Replay` only). **All 8 → nonzero breakage → all classified
`REQUIRED`.** No component fell into the weaker `OPTIONAL` bucket (the script explicitly branches on
this distinction, line 59).

**Necessity vs. "not disproven"**: `ESTABLISHED`, but bounded — necessity is relative to the
18-operation `NEEDS` table **as hand-authored**, not independently re-derived from the operations'
own implementations.

**Sufficiency**: **not independently demonstrated — true by construction.** The candidate `K` is
computed as *exactly* the union of what the `NEEDS` table says the 14 forced operations require, so
"`K` suffices for the 14 forced ops" is definitional, not an empirical result over an
independently-specified `K`. The genuine empirical result is narrower: band-invariance across all 16
resolutions of the 4 undetermined "band" operations — a real, executed result, but not "sufficiency
for all 18 operations" in any stronger sense.

**Step 273 dependency, exact citation and direction**: `03-CANONICAL-K.md` line 56: *"CORPUS — Step
273 §273.31 supplies the safeguard, and it applies to my own result"* (quoting §273.31's
