## HC-01 — Governance-Discipline-over-Formal-Machinery hybrid

**Components:** Model A's 26-lens epistemic-authority discipline ("lenses are instruments for
discovering and challenging architecture, not components of the Kernel") + Model B's tested
kernel-reduction machinery (C0 → falsified → four cardinality-8 minimal kernels).

**Relationship type:** Complementarity (H3-weakened) — A supplies a *process discipline for what
NOT to admit*; B supplies a *tested procedure for what a minimal admitted set could be*, under a
declared representation.

**Assumptions required for this hybrid to be coherent (stated, not silently adopted):**
1. A's admission-test discipline can be applied *to* B's operator-set candidates without first
   resolving whether operators are even the right kind of object for A's lens methodology to
   evaluate (CT-A4/GA-001's categorical mismatch is directly relevant and **unresolved** — this
   assumption is currently unsupported, not merely unproven).
2. B's "representation-dependent" qualifier (M0037) is compatible with A's own insistence that
   Kernel-admission verdicts be representation-independent, philosophically-motivated judgments —
   **not yet checked**.

**Dependency map:** Dep(A's lens discipline) ∩ Dep(B's kernel-reduction) — **not computed**, no
shared premise identified or ruled out in this pass.

**Unresolved issues:** the categorical noun/verb mismatch (GA-001) is a **live blocker** to
combining these two components at the Kernel-object level specifically; the hybrid may still be
coherent at the *process* level (apply A's discipline to B's *methodology*, not to B's *specific
kernel candidates*) — this narrower framing is untested.

**Validation readiness (P5, not run):** would require a concrete worked example applying A's
admission test to one of B's four cardinality-8 kernels, with an explicit representation choice
stated up front.
