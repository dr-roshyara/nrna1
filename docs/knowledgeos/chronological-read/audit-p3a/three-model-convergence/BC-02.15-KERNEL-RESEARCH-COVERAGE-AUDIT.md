## 1. Executive summary

**Central question answer**: the two folders contain a very large amount of genuine, often
rigorous, sometimes self-correcting later mathematical research — but **no discovery in either folder
changes the validated status of `K_t¹¹`, `K_min⁴`, canonical-construction `K`, or the ratified
8-primitive set**, all of which remain exactly where BC-02.14–16 left them (hypothesis, untested,
mutually `UNWITNESSED`). What the folders *do* contain is:

- **At least 4 genuinely new K/Kernel-adjacent objects** not in the BC-02.14 registry (`KO-K01`–`KO-K09`
  below), none of which is shown to be the same as any existing `KO-0XX` object.
- **A newly-discovered architectural rule pair (`258.35`/`258.21`)** that, if admissible, directly bears
  on the legitimacy of `K_t¹¹` and `K_min⁴` as tuple-shaped constructions at all — this *sharpens*
  BC-02.16's existing skepticism, it does not contradict it.
- **A rich body of negative/falsification results** (equality `=` proven not a congruence; a claimed
  join-semilattice refuted; a claimed matroid structure refuted; `{𝒪,𝒯}` refuted as a bootstrap cut;
  "Qualify is the unique irreducible blocker" downgraded to "one of at least five structurally
  identical gaps") — all of which *reinforce*, never weaken, the standing "hypothesis not theorem"
  and "UNWITNESSED, not proven different" discipline this whole investigation has followed.
- **One rigorous external-framework crosswalk** (Reiter's situation calculus) that establishes no
  closure/lattice/matroid structure for `K_t`/Kernel, and is itself best read as evidence *against*
  premature structural claims.
- **Two unresolved internal corpus discrepancies** (a "two 13-operator minima" narrative that conflicts
  with the already-established "four cardinality-8 covering sets" result; a "rank-invertibility"
  headline result that a separate, earlier document in the same folder lists as refuted) — reported
  honestly as contested, not adjudicated by this audit.
- **A real governance-history fact** (`K_t` was renamed `A_t` at some point, "post-`R1`," reframing the
  state object as an *attribution*) that plausibly explains, but does not prove, why `knowledgeos-sim`'s
  `Γ` function is itself named `KnowledgeAttribution`.

**Impact classification (§17 of the commissioning): Impact 2** — new evidence modifies the confidence
and precision of several existing findings (see §16) — **not Impact 3 or 4**. No BC-02.16 conclusion is
invalidated. BC-02.16 remains the correct prior step; no re-execution is required, only the two
addenda recorded in §16.

## 8. `Reach` / closure analysis (Phase D4)

**`Reach` itself** (the `kernel-reduction/` least-fixpoint coverage mechanism) was **not re-investigated
this pass** — it was already fully characterized in BC-02.14/Pre-BC-02.13 and no fork in this pass
found new evidence bearing on it directly. What this pass *did* find, materially relevant to "closure"
as a concept in the neighboring `knowledgeos_kernel/` research:

- **`10-closure-contract.md` (step-289) uses "closure" in a completely different, non-mathematical
  sense**: a 13-row governance/technical-readiness contract (0 of 13 satisfied), explicitly **not** an
  extensive/monotone/idempotent closure operator. This must not be conflated with `Reach`'s own
  closure-operator character.
- **Step-291/04's "closure-analysis"** tests whether the operation universe `𝒪`/`𝒪_K` forms a closed
  set, in the genuine mathematical sense, and finds: **operation-KIND classification is closed**
  (a 5-kind schema), but **membership, semantic bodies (0/22), operation identity (0/22), and
  ratification (0/22) are all open**. **No closure system is established for the operation universe.**
- **A claimed join-semilattice for the knowledge-space `𝕂`** (asserted in separate "verification-lane"
  documents outside this exact file set) is **explicitly refuted** here — by scope-restoration (the
  underlying result held only for `PureClaimSetUnion`) and an executed counterexample (retraction
  re-keys hash-based ids and dangles relation-edges, refuting monotone growth). **Direct, sourced
  negative answer to Phase K/§15's lattice/matroid question**, for this specific claimed structure.
- **A claimed matroid structure for the `Zero`/eliminability construct** (`KR-REP-REDUCTION` cluster's
  own "frozen and refuted register") is **explicitly refuted across multiple axioms**
  (downward/upward/union/intersection-closure, and "generated by its minimal elements" all `[NEG]`).

**Net finding for D4**: outside `kernel-reduction/`'s own already-established `Reach`, **no genuine
closure operator, lattice, or matroid structure was found to hold for any K/Kernel-adjacent object in
either folder** — every structural claim actually tested was either refuted or left explicitly open.
