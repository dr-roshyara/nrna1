2. **Is `Observation` as a primitive distinct from observation functions?** Yes, confirmed, source-
   established via the Sañjaya-layer formalization (`W --Ω--> O`) — `Observation` is the codomain `O` of
   a function `Ω`, not the function itself. `Ω` has no executable body anywhere in the corpus.
3. **Is the 3-of-8 observation gap a genuine mathematical gap or a type-mapping gap?** Both, precisely
   separated: it is NOT a gap for trivial structural projections (definable for all 8 primitives once a
   carrier exists); it IS a genuine gap for semantically-loaded, distinguishing observations (the sense

   corrected statement replaces KSME-11's blunter framing without erasing the underlying gap.
