## 1. CRITICAL DEFECTS (block freeze)

### V-C1 [A+I] — The researcher chooses the population that makes SUPPORTED possible (§13.9a condition 2, §13.10)

The text: "B and D performed with `completeness: EXHAUSTIVE` over the **stated** population". The population is
whatever the researcher lists, so an exhaustive contradiction search over three hand-picked files satisfies condition
(2). **[I]** Second loophole: nothing freezes the population or `temporal_scope` at TEST-DEFINED. After finding a
counterexample, the researcher can narrow either one to avoid REFUTED ("within H's temporal scope"). RC-3 asked for
census scope, and "EXHAUSTIVE" hides that this was not delivered.

**Correction:**
- LEXICAL B/D must cover the full A.4 corpus lists and carry `NEGATIVE-CENSUS`;
- STRUCTURAL-ENUMERATION must cover the structure's full X;
- population and `temporal_scope` are recorded at TEST-DEFINED and frozen. Narrowing creates a **new** record that
  cites the counterexample it avoids.

### V-C2 [A+I] — The temporal-scope fix (C4) is only relabelled (§14.4c)

`WITHIN-SLICE [t₁, t₂]` lets the researcher choose the interval, so any ACROSS-TIME structure becomes
`WITHIN-SLICE [earliest, latest]` and may be described as "within [Aug, Sep] the corpus shows …". That is the
anachronism of T-H6. **[I]** `UNORDERED` leaves "within scope" undefined for REFUTED, and mixed dated/undated
participant sets have no value at all.

**Correction:** WITHIN-SLICE := **coexistence**. There must be a historical point t such that every participant's
first appearance is ≤ t, none is retracted or superseded before t, and all relation evidence is dated ≤ t (or lies in
one source). Otherwise ACROSS-TIME. Any UNORDERED participant ⇒ UNORDERED. The value is computed mechanically from
timelines, not chosen.

### V-C3 [I, partly A] — The control decision rule can be satisfied trivially (§9E.2 items 5–6; §13.9a condition 4; A.7)

"Candidate proportion **exceeds** control proportion" has no margin, and "similar rate" (the only trigger for
`CONTROL-UNDIFFERENTIATED`) is undefined. With k = 2, one candidate at 1 against controls at 0/2 already "exceeds".

Further gaps:
- The aggregation level (per candidate / generator / pass) is not stated.
- Condition (4) bars only `CONTROL-UNDIFFERENTIATED`, so an absent or unregistered comparison passes.
- **[A+I]** CORPUS findings "inherit" `control_comparison`, but S5b generates no controls, so recurrence has no
  baseline.
- **[I]** DESCRIPTIVE vs DISCOVERY is decided after the reveal, by the unblinded researcher.

**Correction:**
- pre-register, in the H-15 plan, the mapping (generator × structure class) → DESCRIPTIVE/DISCOVERY;
- pool the comparison per generator × class;
- require a pre-registered margin δ or test α. Anything else, including absent or unregistered comparisons, is
  `CONTROL-UNDIFFERENTIATED`;
- CORPUS `control_comparison` is derived from the participating findings' pooled S5a rates for that class;
- report counts, proportions, difference and number of available controls always. This also meets the external
  reviewer's point that proportion > proportion is not significance.

### V-C4 [I] — The independence counter drops a §9E.3 rule (A.8, G-13)

§9E.3 excludes "a DESCRIPTIVE finding for the structure in question". A.8 steps 1–3 and G-13 omit it, so a script
built to A.8 counts DESCRIPTIVE sets as recurrence, which is the C2 artefact again. The researcher also chooses the
"structure class" that decides pooling, and "greedily keep sets in `pair_id`/label order" is undefined because sets
have no pair_id (**[A]** found the ordering gap too).

**Correction:** add "drop DESCRIPTIVE" to A.8 and G-13; order sets by their sorted member tuples, lexicographically;
fix the structure-class vocabulary in the H-15 plan.

### V-C5 [A, flagged by I] — Text corruption and a stale freeze instruction

The literal `\1` at D-40 and §31 truncates D-40's rationale and a §31 sentence. §28 says "approve v1.2". Both are
integrity defects in a document meant to be frozen. The v1.4 K8 claim "checked mechanically" was **overstated**: the
check covered references, not text integrity.

**Correction:** restore the text; fix §28; add a text-integrity check (no literal backreferences, no version string
older than the document) to the pre-freeze procedure.
