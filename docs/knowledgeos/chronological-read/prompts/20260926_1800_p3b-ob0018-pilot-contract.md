1. **Inputs:** the production contract revision 3; this addendum; your plan entry (files); the slice. Nothing else.

    "partition": "<run_id>", "status": "CONSUMED" | "NOT-CONSUMED", "n_pages", "reading_step": 1 | 7,

    "birth_candidates": [{"kind", "anchor", "quote", "reason"}],
