# EKS-22 — Twenty-three documents of mathematics carry no status, no index and no stated relationship to the frozen baseline

**Status:** **BACKLOG · LIFECYCLE & AUTHORITY EXPOSURE** — registered from `P-61` (`docs/knowledgeos/theory-extraction/79-P61-…`). ⛔ **Not commissioned; activation requires a Human/PO-ARB authorization act.**
**Class:** operating-model problem — status and lifecycle of research output.
**Registered by:** Lane T (theory extraction), 2026-09-09.

> ### ⛔ **This item records a problem and a candidate requirement. It commissions nothing, proposes no status for the material, and questions none of its content.**

## 1 · The problem in plain terms

On **2026-09-06** the programme produced a **complete rewrite of its theory** — twenty-three documents,
written end to end as one coherent mathematical work, covering foundations, a type system, state
transitions, evidence, revision, identity, time, persistence, architecture and a worked example.

It is substantial, careful work.

⭐⭐⭐ **And nobody can tell what it is.**

| question a reader would ask | what the documents say |
|---|---|
| Is this the theory now, or a draft? | ⛔ **nothing** |
| Does it replace the frozen version? | ⛔ **nothing — the frozen version is not mentioned in any of the 23** |
| Which claims are proved, which are proposals, which are refuted? | ⛔ **nothing — no claim carries a status marker** |
| Where do I start reading? | ⛔ **no index** |
| Was it finished, or abandoned partway? | ⛔ **nothing** |

## 2 · Why this matters, and why it matters *now*

**a. Two days earlier, the programme adopted a rule that this material does not follow.** ⭐⭐ On
**2026-09-04** an epistemic status vocabulary was **formally adopted**, and the theory set written that
same day states the rule plainly: *"an untagged mathematical statement is a defect."* ⚠️ **The rewrite,
begun 48 hours later, contains no tags at all.** By the estate's own standard that is not a stylistic
gap.

**b. The material is already being read as if its status were settled.** ⭐⭐⭐ A separate review
recently treated one of its documents as the estate's standing theory of persistence and compared a
research result against it in detail. **That comparison may have been against a draft, or against an
abandoned branch. Nobody involved could tell.**

**c. It is the only place several important subjects are treated at all.** ⭐ Persistence, the
transition system, identity and provenance, temporal semantics — for some of these the rewrite is the
**only** treatment in the estate. ⚠️ **So it cannot simply be ignored, and it cannot safely be relied
on.** That is the worst of both positions.

**d. The confidence signal is inverted.** ⭐⭐ Because it reads as a finished, coherent work, it looks
**more** authoritative than material that carries honest status markers and admits what is open. **The
document set most likely to be provisional presents as the most complete.**

**e. Every week increases the cost.** ⭐ A status decision taken now is a short act by whoever wrote it.
Taken in six months it becomes an archaeology exercise, because the people who know the intent will no
longer remember which parts they meant as settled.

## 3 · What is *not* the problem

⛔ **The content is not in question.** This item makes no claim that the rewrite is wrong, premature,
unauthorized or redundant. ⭐ **It may well be the best theory the programme has.**

⛔ **This is not a request to tag twenty-three documents.** Retro-fitting a status to every statement
would be a large task and may not be the right remedy. **A single sentence about the set as a whole
would resolve most of the exposure.**

⛔ **Not a criticism of the author or the lane.** ⭐ Writing a theory end to end without stopping to
grade every line is a reasonable way to work. **The gap is that the grading never happened afterwards
either.**

⛔ **No status is proposed here.** Deciding whether the set is a successor, a parallel draft or an
abandoned branch is precisely the act this item is asking someone to take, and it is not this lane's to
take.

## 4 · Candidate requirement (a direction, not a design)

> ⭐ **A body of research output should carry, at the level of the set, a statement of what it is: its
> relationship to the current baseline, and whether it is intended to be relied on.**

⭐⭐ **The cheapest sufficient version is three lines at the front of the set** — *what this is · what it
supersedes, if anything · what may be relied on.* The theory set written two days earlier already does
exactly this, so the practice exists and is proven inside the estate.
