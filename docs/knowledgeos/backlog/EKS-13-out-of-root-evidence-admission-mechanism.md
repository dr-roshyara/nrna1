## 1 · The problem, as observed

The `three_model_convergence` KnowledgeOS reconstruction programme (`docs/knowledgeos/brainstorming/
three_model_convergence/`) established its corpus root as `docs/knowledgeos/brainstorming/` (MD-010/
MD-011, 2026-09-01). `docs/knowledgeos/research/` sits outside that root and was never classified.

**The same pattern has now recurred at least twice**, each time reinvented from scratch rather than
following a reusable procedure:

| Occurrence | What happened |
|---|---|
| `06-composition-rules.md` (`docs/knowledgeos/research/kernel-reduction/`) | Discovered by citation (MD-025), corpus-boundary-adjudicated (MD-026), adversarially audited (MD-027), packaged as a decision (MD-028), decided via an **ad hoc "SESSION-LEVEL HUMAN RESEARCH-GOVERNANCE DECISION"** with 12 hand-written conditions (MD-028-DQ-1), then re-put to the human formally a second time for a related file and decided again the same improvised way (MD-032) |
| `12-randomized-results.md` (same directory) | MD-034 (this session) independently rediscovers the identical shape of question — same directory, same "cited by an already-admitted file, itself unadmitted" situation — and recommends the same kind of decision be made a **third** time |

**`00_control/protocol.md` — the programme's own governing document — has no admission mechanism at
all.** Every instance to date has required: (a) a full corpus-boundary/provenance study, (b) an
adversarial audit of that study, (c) a hand-built, narrow decision package, (d) a human decision act
whose exact scope and 12-condition template is retyped from the previous instance rather than
referenced from a reusable procedure. Nothing in `protocol.md` names this as a recognized situation
or points to a standing procedure for it.

## 2 · Candidate requirement — a candidate, not a decision

> **`protocol.md` (or a sibling governance document) should name "admission of out-of-corpus-root
> evidence" as a recognized situation, with a standing, reusable decision template** (scope
> statement, non-effects list, provenance-tier check, the "does not establish canonical/ratified/
> proven/sufficient" disclaimer set) — so each future instance references the template instead of
> re-deriving its own wording.

This would not remove the human decision step, and would not pre-authorize any future admission — it
would only save each instance from re-inventing the same governance machinery MD-028-DQ-1 and
MD-032 already independently converged on.

## 3 · What this item explicitly does NOT claim

⛔ It does not claim any of the four admission decisions made so far (`03`, `04`, `06`) were wrong —
each was carefully reasoned and remains sound.
⛔ It does not propose specific template wording, a specific document location, or a specific
governance body change — those are for Governance/ARB to decide, if this item is disposed toward
action at all.
⛔ It does not claim the manual, phase-gated character of this research programme is itself a
problem — the deliberateness is a deliberate design choice (MD-021's own "separate authorization per
phase" discipline) and this item does not question it. The gap named here is narrower: the *absence
of a reusable template* for one specific, now-recurring situation within that deliberate process.
⛔ It does not affect any frozen MD-0xx artifact, `classification-register.tsv`, or any Model
membership determination.
