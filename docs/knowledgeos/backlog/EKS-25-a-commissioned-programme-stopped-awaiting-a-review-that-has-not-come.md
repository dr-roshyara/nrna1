# EKS-25 — A commissioned verification programme stopped itself for supervisory review, and the review has not come

**Status:** **BACKLOG · BLOCKED-WORK EXPOSURE** — registered from `P-72` (`docs/knowledgeos/theory-extraction/90-P72-…`). ⛔ **Not commissioned; activation requires a Human/PO-ARB authorization act.**
**Class:** operating-model problem — completed work awaiting a decision that has no owner in motion.
**Registered by:** Lane T (theory extraction), 2026-09-09.

> ### ⛔ **This item records a problem and a candidate requirement. It commissions nothing, requests no review, and criticises no work.**

---

## 1 · The problem in plain terms

On **2026-08-28** the programme formally commissioned an **independent verification track** over its own
theory — mathematical, statistical, computational and formal-consistency — with an unusually strict
mandate: **read, verify, falsify; never repair.** It even fixed the vocabulary its verdicts were allowed
to use, and **forbade the word *"validated."***

⭐⭐ **The work was then done, and done well.** Eleven registers were completed: an inventory, an
assumption register, definitions, derivations with their **non-consequences** written out, propositions,
statistical and computational feasibility, a three-way matrix, a counterexample register — ⭐ **which
records not only the counterexamples found but the searches that failed** — a contradiction register
holding **sixty-one** typed contradictions, and a measurement register. Nineteen findings were produced
across three waves.

⭐⭐⭐ **And then, exactly as its own protocol required, it stopped:**

> **"session STOPPED pending supervision"** · **"STOP for supervisory review; the final theory is NOT
> being written yet."**

⚠️ **That was eleven days ago. The review has not happened.**

## 2 · Why this is a business problem

**a. The work is finished and unusable.** ⭐⭐ This is not work in progress. It reached its own designed
stopping point and is **waiting on a decision**, not on effort. Every day it waits, the estate holds a
completed asset it cannot draw on.

**b. Two other work streams proceeded past it.** ⭐⭐⭐ In the eleven days since the stop, two separate
streams continued to work on the same subject matter — and **both re-derived conclusions the stopped
programme had already established**, including a refutation it had already recorded and a dependency it
had already named as a pending decision. **The estate paid three times for one result.**

**c. It is the most disciplined artifact in the estate, and therefore the most expensive to leave
idle.** ⭐ A programme that forbids itself the word *"validated"*, writes down what does **not** follow
from its own results, and records its **failed** searches is exactly the kind of evidence a theory
decision needs. **Leaving it unread is not a neutral delay.**

**d. Nothing is chasing it.** ⚠️ The stop is recorded inside the programme's own reports. **There is no
place where a reader would see that a completed programme is waiting** — no queue, no standing item, no
owner in motion. It stopped politely and became invisible.

**e. It will decay in a specific way.** ⭐⭐ Its findings are about a theory version that is still frozen
today. **If the theory moves before the review happens, the review becomes archaeology** — and its
conclusions will have to be re-established against whatever replaced them.

## 3 · What is *not* the problem

⛔ **The programme did nothing wrong** — stopping for supervision was its instruction, and stopping was
correct. ⛔ **No criticism of anyone is implied**; a review that has not been scheduled is not a review
that has been refused. ⛔ **No review is requested here** — that would be commissioning work, which a
backlog item may not do. ⛔ **And nothing about the programme's findings is asserted or endorsed** by this
item; whether they hold is exactly what the pending review would establish.

## 4 · Candidate requirement (a direction, not a design)

> ⭐ **When a piece of work stops by design awaiting a human decision, the fact that it is waiting
> should be visible somewhere a reader will look — not only inside the stopped work's own reports.**

⭐⭐ **The distinction that matters is between *paused* and *forgotten*, and today they look identical.**
A programme that stopped correctly and a programme that was abandoned leave the same trace.
