# EKS-46 — A third independent classification/governance pipeline exists in the corpus, previously
unknown to this reconstruction, and remains unread at full depth

## Problem, in business language

This reconstruction (the "three_model_convergence" programme, MD-057 onward) has spent five weeks
building its own methodology for reading this research corpus chronologically and tracking how its
theory evolved. During a controlled extension pass (MD-072), it discovered — by simply reading two
directories it had never opened before (`kernel/` and `phase_measure_theory/`) — that **at least two
other, entirely separate research-governance efforts already existed inside this same corpus**, each
with its own classification scheme, its own concept register, its own contradiction log, and its own
ratified decisions, none of them aware of the other two (this reconstruction's own
`three_model_convergence/` apparatus; `kernel/`'s own `classification/`+`corpus/`+`synthesis/`+
`falsification/` pipeline with a `KCON-001..025` register and `00_INDEX.md`; and `phase_measure_
theory/`'s own `how_to_combine/` governance chain with `GN-nn`-numbered rulings). A fourth-sense
version of the corpus's own "K_t naming register" problem, but at the scale of entire research
programmes rather than individual symbols.

## Why this matters

- Real, substantive work exists in `kernel/`'s own classification pipeline (an 11-model Knowledge-
  definition census, `K-1..K-11`; a formal Kernel-boundary falsification chain reaching its own
  conclusions; a 3,217-line principal-architect synthesis) that this reconstruction has now only
  skimmed at Level-1 census depth (MD-072) — not read to the depth its own F4-chain work received.
- If any of that pipeline's own conclusions bear on questions this reconstruction still treats as open
  (most plausibly: whether "Knowledge" itself belongs inside a Kernel object, directly adjacent to the
  F4 chain's own `Sat`/`EC_t` boundary question), this reconstruction has been working without access
  to relevant, already-produced evidence for as long as it has existed.
- This is the same class of problem `EKS-23`/`EKS-28`/`EKS-36`/`EKS-45` already catalogue at smaller
  scale (two independently-invented vocabularies, two uncited governance commissions, two same-day
  sibling threads) — here at the scale of three entire, multi-week research-governance *programmes*
  running in parallel inside one repository, none aware of the other two.

## Recommended resolution (not performed by this ticket — governance decision required)

A dedicated, bounded reading pass over `kernel/`'s own terminal synthesis artifacts — named precisely
in MD-072 §07 as `GAP-008` (`00_INDEX.md`'s full register, `EXTRACTION-LEDGER.md`'s `KCON-001..025` in
full, `KNOWLEDGE-CONCEPT-EXTRACTION-001`'s `K-1..K-11` table in full, and the 3,217-line
`principal-architect-review` file in full) to determine whether that pipeline's own conclusions bear on
this reconstruction's own still-open questions. This ticket does not perform that reading — it records
the discovery and its scope so a future, separately-authorized phase can act on it.
