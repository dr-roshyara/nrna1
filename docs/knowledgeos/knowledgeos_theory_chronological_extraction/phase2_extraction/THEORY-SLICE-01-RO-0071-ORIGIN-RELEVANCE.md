## 1 · The knowledge system — ⛔ silent, not negative

**The Constitution's AI principles are four lines, and every one is an ENTRY gate:**

| Line | |
|---|---|
| **84** | AI-generated knowledge **enters as** `authority: generated` |
| **85** | AI output **never becomes** `authoritative` without human review |
| **86** | AI working material is segregated and **only migrates into** authoritative knowledge **after review** |
| **87** | reusable AI context is **packaged** as Knowledge Packages |

> ### ⭐⭐ **All four govern the way IN. Not one says anything about the artifact once it has arrived.**

**Searched across the Constitution and `lifecycle.md`:**

| Phrase | Hits |
|---|---|
| *"once reviewed"* · *"regardless of origin"* · *"who produced"* · *"how it was produced"* · *"machine-generated"* · *"human-authored"* | ⛔ **0 each** |
| *"after review"* | **1** — line 86, ⭐ **the migration event itself, i.e. still the entry** |

> ### ⛔ **VERDICT: the governing text does not say origin stops mattering. It does not address the question at all.**
>
> ⭐ **The design has a complete pre-authoritative control and an EMPTY post-authoritative position.** *That is a different finding from "origin is intentionally irrelevant", and it must not be recorded as one.*

## 2 · ⭐⭐ But the research practice answers YES — and built the mechanism

**`T-0047`** *(`[C]`, `F0002`, `DIRECT_QUOTED`)*:

> **Every external source is graded `[FETCHED]` or `[CANONICAL]`, and *"⛔ No source is cited beyond what its grade supports."***

⭐ **That is a production-origin record**: it states *how the knowledge was obtained* — read this session, or held to a remembered thesis — **and binds what may be claimed from it.**

⭐⭐ **And it PERSISTS.** The grades sit **in the document body**, not in frontmatter: 7 occurrences in `F0002`'s reconstruction record, 2 in its dossier. ⛔ **No status or authority transition can erase them, because no field holds them.**

> ### ⭐ **So one part of the programme treats production origin as remaining relevant after the fact — strongly enough to invent a discipline for it and to forbid citation beyond it.**

⚠️ **Scope:** `T-0047` governs **external sources cited by a document**, not the document's own production. ⛔ **Adjacent, not identical.** But it establishes the *principle* — that how knowledge was obtained constrains what may be claimed from it — which is exactly what `RO-0071` asks about.

## 3 · The two competing models, tested

| | Model | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **M1** | **origin becomes irrelevant once a human assumes responsibility** | ⚠️ **INDIRECT ONLY** — `reviewers`/`owner` record the transfer; §86's *"migrates into"* implies leaving something behind. ⛔ **Never stated** |
| **M2** | **origin is part of epistemic provenance and must survive** | ⭐ **STATED, in practice, by `T-0047`** — and the Constitution's own goal is retrieval *"over a **10+ year horizon**"*, a durability claim ⛔ that says nothing about what must endure |

> ### ⛔ **Neither model is established by the governing text. `M2` has an implemented instance; `M1` has an inference.**

⚠️ **That asymmetry is evidence, not proof:** `T-0047` is one discipline in one corpus, and `RA-15` status D is unreachable *(`RO-0051`)*.

## 4 · What the split actually means

⭐ **Two parts of the same programme diverge:**

| | |
|---|---|
| ⛔ **The knowledge system** | records origin **only while an artifact is pre-authoritative**, in a field it then reuses for trust |
| ⭐ **The research corpus** | records origin **permanently, in prose**, and forbids claims beyond it |

> ### ⭐⭐ **The mechanism that survives is the one that was never put in a schema.**
>
> ⛔ **`T-0053` warns of exactly this from the other direction — *"paths change; ids never do."* Here it is prose that endures and the field that gets overwritten.**

## ⭐ `RO-0071` in one paragraph

**The question was whether production origin stays epistemically relevant after authority transfer, and the governing text does not answer it — the Constitution's four AI principles are all entry gates, and every phrase that would signal post-review relevance returns zero.** ⛔ **That is silence, not a negative, and recording it as *"origin is intentionally irrelevant"* would manufacture a decision nobody made.** ⭐⭐ **But the research practice answers yes in the affirmative and built a mechanism: `T-0047` grades every external source and forbids citation beyond its grade — and because the grades live in the document body rather than a field, no authority transition can erase them.** ⭐ **So the programme contains both an empty position and a working discipline, and the discipline that survives is the one that was never put in a schema.** ⛔ **Whether `M1` — origin becomes irrelevant once a human takes responsibility — is a deliberate design or an unconsidered gap cannot be settled from the text, and `RO-0073` puts it to the only party who can answer.**
