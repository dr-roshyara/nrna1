# KnowledgeOS — 10-Minute Snapshot

⚠️ **Candidate theory v0.1.** Not final, not validated. Full version: `CANDIDATE-KNOWLEDGEOS-THEORY.md`.

## 1. What it appears to be

**A governance platform for engineering knowledge.** Its subject is the *authority* of knowledge — where it came from, how far it should be trusted, who may change that, and what must happen before someone else can reuse it.

⚠️ The corpus reached this conclusion and **declined to adopt it**, because the mission it would serve is unratified. We inherit that refusal.

## 2. The central mechanism

> **Nothing becomes reusable engineering knowledge unless BOTH a produced verdict and an accepted decision exist.**
>
> *Evidence earns; governance grants; promotion requires both.*

⭐ **And we found why it holds** — a reason the corpus never gives. Two actors are each structurally incapable of the other's act: an instrument **produces** a verdict and is **forbidden to interpret** it; an authority **accepts** and cannot produce. **A separation of powers.**

## 3. Main concepts

Knowledge · Evidence · **Provenance** (immutable) · **Standing** (act-changed) · **Authority = provenance × standing** · Qualification · Decision Authority · Promotion · Product Knowledge Space · Bounded context.

⛔ *Knowledge Space* is used throughout and **never defined**.

## 5. Strongest evidence

⭐ **The authority schema cannot represent its own worked example.** Two dimensions need six states; the schema stores one single-valued field with five — and ranks them 1–5 as if they were one axis. **Tested: 39 governed documents, zero carry both dimensions.** Ten had to choose provenance and forfeit standing.

The only claim here that is both formally derived and empirically tested.

## 7. Biggest unresolved question

> ## **Is the mechanism real, or only prescribed?**

It has a formal shape, a structural reason for its independence — and **zero observed instances**. Seven files say the loop has never closed; one independent review says it ran three times; the records that would settle it are **outside reach**.
