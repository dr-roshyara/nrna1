## 3. The central candidate mechanism

> ## **Nothing becomes reusable engineering knowledge unless *both* a produced verdict and an accepted decision exist.**

The corpus states it as: ***evidence EARNS; governance GRANTS; promotion requires BOTH.*** *No ruling can make an observation replicated. No amount of evidence can make a document frozen.*

⭐ **And we found why it holds — a reason the corpus never gives.** The qualification standard says: *"**instruments produce, the authority accepts**"* and *"the AI evaluates, **governance decides**."* A companion rule forbids the instrument to interpret: *"the measuring instrument runs → captures → returns PASS/FAIL → stops; **no interpretation inside the instrument**."*

**So the two are independent because two different actors are each structurally incapable of the other's act.**

| Role | Output contract | May accept? | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Measuring instrument** | `PASS/FAIL`, ⛔ no interpretation | ⛔ no | `ES-003.3` `[C]` |
| ⭐ **Analyst** *(recovered v0.4)* | ***recommends, never declares*** — recommendation + evidence + `PENDING` | ⛔ no | `ES-004.1` `[C]` |
| **Authority** | accepts · defers · refuses | ✅ | both `[C]` |

> ### ⭐ **v0.4 — the corpus has THREE roles below promotion, not two.**
>
> Recovered from `ES-004.1` *"Retrospectives Recommend; They Never Declare"*: **"The AI is researcher/recorder/analyst; the ARB is the authority… A decision becomes PROMOTED only when the ARB confirms."**

> **And the chain is five stages, not four:**
>
> ```
> implementation → evidence → analysis → ARB decision → standard        [C]
> ```

# Part III — What has actually been demonstrated

## 9. ⭐ The one empirically tested result

> ### **The authority schema cannot represent its own worked example.**

**What the source says** `[A]` — the authority field asks two questions at once: *"how much should I trust this, **and** where did it come from?"* Its five values answer different ones. A signed financial statement is *derived* in provenance and *authoritative* in standing, **simultaneously and without contradiction**.

**What we derived** `[D]` — two dimensions of size 2 and 3 need **six** states. The schema stores **one field with five values**, and the field is declared `type: enum`, single-valued — while the same schema *has* a `list-enum` type and uses it elsewhere. No other field carries provenance. ⭐ **And each value carries a `rank` from 1 to 5, imposing a total order across both dimensions** — something the source never mentions. So the source's own example requires rank 1 **and** rank 2 in a single-valued field. Unsatisfiable.

**What we tested** `[E]` — all governed documents were scanned. **39 carry an authority value. Zero carry both a provenance and a standing value.** And both dimensions are in active use (`derived` 10×, `authoritative` 15×), so **ten documents had to choose provenance and thereby forfeit standing**.

> This is the **only** claim in this document that has been both formally derived and empirically tested. ⚠️ **It establishes a defect in an artifact — not a theory of authority.** Whether provenance × standing is the right algebra remains an open governance question.
