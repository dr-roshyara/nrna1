## The four results that matter

### 1 ⭐ `OQ-L4` answered — **NO**

`STATE.model` started **empty**. Construction demanded **6 primitives absent from the protocol's iteration-1 list** — `Competition`, `ComputedMaturity`, `EmergentRecord`, `OpenTerm`, `SearchRecord`, `Structure` — and **did not use 3** of it.

> **The list did not survive contact with construction.** That is the READ → CONSTRUCT → IMPLEMENT principle producing its first empirical result.

### 2 ⭐ Evidence overrode an architectural judgement

`Competition` was adjudicated **PREMATURE** two passes ago, on the ground that the corpus never calls these things competitions. **Construction demanded it anyway** (`RG-0002`): `CMP-0001` holds *three* rival readings with a conjunctive discriminator, which no edge can carry.

The old judgement is preserved verbatim in `EV-0004`, not deleted.

### 3 ⭐ Two structures were discovered, not carried

`STR-0003` **check-before-admit** as a total function `check(X,M) → {ALREADY_IN_M, SPLITS_M, GENUINELY_NEW}` — five instances, four files, **named by none of them**.
`STR-0004` **append-only with supersession edges** — four corrections preserve what they correct; the one destructive edit produced the window's only unrecoverable text.

**Neither appears in §9.1's `S-1..S-5`.** Phase F.1's search step found them.

### 4 ⭐ A schema that cannot hold its own example

`SI-0007`: `|P × S| = 6` states required; `authorities.yaml` stores **one field with 5 values**. F0018's own worked example — *"derived in provenance **and** authoritative in standing, simultaneously"* — is **not representable in the schema F0018 is analysing**.

F0018 says the field *conflates* two questions. It does not say the field *cannot express their conjunction*. **That follows by counting.**
