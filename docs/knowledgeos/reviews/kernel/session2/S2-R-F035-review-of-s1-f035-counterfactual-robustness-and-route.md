## 5 · Challenged

**`.4` *Route* is a third carrier — but the three-carrier composite needs testing, not just listing**
Session 1 assembles: **route** (this), **acquisition procedure** (`S1-F030`), **role** (`S1-F029`) — *"three separate things an evidence/claim reference may need to carry"*, flagged as its own inference.

⚠ Two of the three may not be separate. ⟦L⟧ `EvidenceLinks` (l. 209) carries *"evidence references + **acquisition method** + reliability conditions."* **Acquisition method is already there.** And *route* — *by what epistemic means was this obtained* (`pratyakṣa` vs `upamāna`) — is very close to *acquisition method*. Whether they are one carrier or two is the same **semantic-denotation** question I declined to settle at `S2-R-F029` and `S2-R-F030`.

**So the defensible composite is: one carrier already in law (acquisition/route, possibly one thing), plus one genuinely absent (role).** That is a smaller and more actionable claim than three.

**`.5` The completeness/certainty trade is single-sourced but its consequence is real**
*"The knowledge state became more complete, but less certain."* Session 1 is right that this appears **once in 93 documents** and right about the consequence: Confidence cannot be monotone in evidence volume, which forecloses the simplest derivations. ⟦L⟧ Consistent with `Confidence` being *"a structured epistemic attribute — never a scalar"* (l. 215): a non-monotone quantity is not a score. **Recorded as a constraint on any future `W:C-11` answer, not as an answer.**

**`.6` The inter-level genus is a challenge to containment, not a seventh genus**
*"Knowledge is not located at one level of representation. It emerges from relationships between levels."* Session 1 files it as a **seventh genus**. ⚠ It is not a rival *definition* of knowledge — it is a claim that whatever knowledge is, **no single altitude contains it**. That is an **altitude** claim, and it belongs with `S1-F020`'s three-altitude separation rather than in the genus series. Single-sourced (Hofstadter), and the corpus's genus count is already exposed to `M-6` (`S2-R-F017.3`).
