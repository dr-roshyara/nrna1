## 5 · Level 2 — analytical validity

### `.1` Fourteen lenses are not fourteen votes — **CHALLENGE, and it is the primary finding**
One model, one document, one sitting, applying fourteen lenses **it selected**, to a hypothesis stated in the prompt. A 13–1 tally under those conditions is **one reasoner's position expressed fourteen times**, not a 13–1 majority. This is `S2-R-F010 §9`'s three-theory-bases problem at scale 14.

And 13–1 is *the shape you get* when a reasoner elaborates a hypothesis it already favours through multiple framings: each lens is asked whether it supports the hypothesis, and the same underlying judgement answers fourteen times.

⚠ **So the informative datum is not the thirteen — it is the one.** A dissent that survives an exercise designed to elaborate the hypothesis is evidence; thirteen concurrences from a single reasoner are not. **Session 1's headline is right and its stated reason is wrong** — it treats the dissent as significant *because it is 1-of-14*, when the significance is that it **survived an elaboration exercise.**

⚠ Session 1 applies the correct discipline to *"two models answering one shared prompt"* (`S1-F011`) and to *"two passes in one file"* (`S1-F015`) but not to fourteen lenses in one document. **Third instance of the lineage discipline applied selectively** (after the Projection tally and the Gaṇeśa self-critique).

### `.2` *"Three independent arrivals at the lifecycle objection"* → two at best
`S1-F010` (11:16) and `S1-F014` (11:36) both sit in the morning block whose shared prompt lineage Session 1 itself established. Two lenses **within** this document are not two arrivals. Defensible count: **the morning lineage (one) plus this evening document (possibly one)** = **2**, and the second is only possibly independent — it reasons from F-1…F-5 like the rest.

⚠ **The objection is nonetheless the most durable in the corpus**, and the reason is better than the count: it recurs across a **10-hour gap and a different session**, and it is the **only** dissent an elaboration exercise could not absorb.

### `.3` The conditional exclusions are the corpus's methodological high point — **CONFIRMED**
*"Confidence (**if derivable**)"* and *"Supersession/contestation (**if separate lifecycle**)"*. Session 1: *"this member list is **not a list** — it is a list **parameterised by two open questions.** No earlier list states its own dependencies."*

✅ **Confirmed, and it is the most sophisticated single move in the Session-1 record.** It directly answers `S2-F024.1`'s criticism of `S1-F009`: F009's criterion contained a **hidden** free variable and therefore appeared to decide what it could not; this list **declares** its free variables and is honest about deciding less. That is a genuine methodological advance, and it arrives from inside the corpus.

### `.4` *Established fact* 6 defeats itself — **CONFIRMED**
*"Confidence is assigned at admission (**but derivation is unknown**)"* is listed among *established facts*. Session 1: the corpus *"records **that** confidence is assigned without knowing **from what**."* Correct. Law is consistent with this: ⟨R-1⟩ (l. 215) says **where** Confidence is assigned — inside the boundary — and not from what. So the gap is real and it is `W:C-11`, open in law too.

### `.5` *"Enforcement mechanism of the boundary, not the boundary itself"* — validates `S2-F024.2`
Session 1 records this as a third ontological category that *"contradicts F009 directly."*

`S2-F024.2` found F009 internally ambivalent: it **asserts** *"a domain boundary, not a component"* and then **elaborates** *"the Kernel is **the part of** the aggregate…"* — component language. I recorded then: *"Any convergence claim across the three needs to say which half it converges on."*

That is exactly what happens here. *Enforcement mechanism of a boundary* **contradicts F009's assertion** and **agrees with F009's elaboration.** So this cannot be scored as a genuine contradiction until F009 is disambiguated — the contradiction is with an artifact that holds both positions. **Classification: APPARENT CONTRADICTION — target internally inconsistent.** Eighth mechanism.

### `.6` Re-asserting a falsified hypothesis — **CONFIRMED, and Session 1 caught it**
*"⚠ the document does not acknowledge that result, though it postdates it by ~10 hours"*, and confidence rated **medium** partly for that reason. ✅ This is the strongest single piece of evidence in the record for `S2-F020`: an evening document re-asserts the aggregate that the morning's adversarial commission falsified, without engaging it. Documents in this corpus demonstrably do not share a knowledge base.

### `.7` *"Each hypothesis is supported by a different evidence base"* — **CONFIRMED as the reframing it claims to be**
Session 1: *"the disagreement is not about reasoning quality but about **which base is authoritative.** That reframes the whole competition."* ✅ And it converges with `S2-F020` from the corpus's own side: if the formulations differ because their bases differ, then the eight formulations are not eight theories of one domain — they are readings of **different evidence sets.**
