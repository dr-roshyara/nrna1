## FINDING

S1-F002 claims corroboration by independence:

> *"the same limit as KCON-018 (**"no authority manufacture"**) reached independently, and the same limit as KCON-013 … **Three independent arrivals** at: *the system can record, not manufacture.*"*

**Independence is asserted, not shown.** All three appear to derive from the same system inspection, of the same mechanism, on the same day (2026-08-21) — the same day as the source document itself. If so they are **three statements of one observation**, and the corroboration is nominal.

## EVIDENCE

- S1-F002's own provenance gives the source as a single document, `20260821-2033-…`, Phase 1, dated **2026-08-21**.
- S1-F002 elsewhere notes that KCON-017 was recorded *"`20260821-2032`, **the same day**"* — one minute apart by filename.
- The three claims are not merely compatible; they are **the same proposition** in different words: *records rather than creates* · *cannot create a Human Act* · *no authority manufacture*.

**Three restatements of one proposition, from one inspection, on one day, is one arrival.**

## WHY IT MATTERS

"Independent arrival" is load-bearing in this programme's evidence discipline — it is the reason convergence counts for anything. If restatement can be recorded as independent arrival, the strongest corroboration signal available becomes the easiest to manufacture, and *by accident*: a finding restated in three places looks three times as well-supported.

**The standing rule already established for this programme applies directly:** *a model's own previous reasoning cannot become independent evidence merely because it has been saved as a document.* This is the same failure in a different dress — **restatement laundered as convergence** rather than output laundered as evidence.

## POSSIBLE IMPACT

Corpus methodology, and it generalises: **any "N independent arrivals" claim in either session's register needs a stated independence basis** — different observer, different method, different date, or different underlying data. Absent one, the count should be recorded as *N restatements* and weighted as one.

Worth noting the converse: genuine independent arrival is real and valuable elsewhere in this programme's records. The challenge is to *this* instance, not to the criterion.

## PROVENANCE

Derived solely from `session1/S1-F002` and its internal citations. KCON-013/017/018 were **not read** — outside this review's read scope, which is itself why independence could not be confirmed here.

## STATUS

**OPEN — CHALLENGE.** Remedy is cheap: state the independence basis, or downgrade to *one observation, three statements*.

## EXTENSION — the defect recurs in `S1-F003`, and Session 1 gets it right once and wrong once in the same artifact

**Right, on A→B:** S1-F003 states plainly that *"B is **not** an independent arrival — it explicitly revises A, **36 minutes later**"*, and identifies *"the same author-context"*. That is exactly the analysis this finding asks for, applied unprompted. **Session 1 has the criterion.**

**Wrong, on Rule Port → Verification Port,** in the same document:

> ⟦I⟧ *"…structurally the same move ⟦L⟧ v1.1 makes with the **Verification Port** … Reached here in Phase 1, one day earlier, **independently**. Classification: CONSISTENT."*

**Independence is asserted with no basis, and the basis available points the other way:** same project, same author-context, and **v1.1 postdates the Phase-1 proposal by a day**. If the Verification Port was formulated with the Rule Port proposal in view — or by the same person who wrote it — that is **descent, not convergence**. Descent proves continuity of thought; convergence would prove that the port shape is forced by the problem. **They carry opposite evidential weight, and only one of them supports "a convergence worth accumulating."**

## Why the recurrence matters more than either instance

Session 1 applies the independence test **when the relationship is explicit** (B says it revises A) and **omits it when the relationship is merely plausible** (v1.1 may descend from the Phase-1 proposal). That is a systematic blind spot with a predictable shape: **undeclared descent reads as independent arrival.**

Since S1-F003 itself labels the Rule Port convergence *"worth accumulating"*, the count is already being kept — so the correction should land before the tally grows.

**Remedy, unchanged from the original finding:** state the independence basis — different observer, method, date, or underlying data — or record as descent. For this instance the question is narrow and answerable: *was the Verification Port formulated in knowledge of the Phase-1 Rule Port?*

## EXTENSION 2 — a third mechanism: a commissioned review inherits its commission's framing (`S1-F004`)

`S1-F004` Finding 3 claims: *"this is the **third independent arrival** at *over time* as the load-bearing dimension"* — citing `S1-F001` (measured, 0/20 grants) and the Phase 2 temporal thread.

**Session 1 has improved here and should be credited: it supplies a partial basis** — *"Two of the three are Phase 1, one is measured, one is external."* Measured-versus-external is a genuine independence argument: different method, different data. That is exactly what this finding asks for.

**The residual problem is framing inheritance, a third mechanism distinct from the two already recorded here.**

The external review is **`P2` EXTERNAL_RESEARCH by source but project-commissioned by origin** — S1-F004 quotes its self-description: *"an independent evidence base intended for later comparison against reconstructed EKS, PKS, and AIP architectures."*

> **Independence of *source* is not independence of *question*.** A review commissioned to assemble evidence for comparison against this project's architecture inherits the project's framing in what it looks for. If the commission's questions foregrounded temporal aspects of organisational knowledge, then the review finding temporal salience is **responsive, not convergent.**

## The three mechanisms now recorded in this finding

| # | Mechanism | Instance |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **restatement** — one observation stated three times | `S1-F002`, the "records not creates" triple |
| 2 | **undeclared descent** — a later artefact reads as an independent arrival | `S1-F003`, Rule Port → Verification Port |
| 3 | **framing inheritance** — commissioned evidence agrees with its commission's question | `S1-F004`, the third temporal arrival |

**All three inflate the same signal, and all three are invisible in a count.** A tally of "N independent arrivals" cannot distinguish them from genuine convergence without a stated basis.

**Remedy, extended:** the independence basis should name which of the three it excludes. For instance 3 the answerable question is narrow: *did the commissioning brief for the external review foreground temporality?* If it did, the arrival is responsive; if it did not, this is genuine convergence and unusually strong.
