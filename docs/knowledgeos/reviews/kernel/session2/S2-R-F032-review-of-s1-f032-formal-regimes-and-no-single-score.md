## 4 · Challenged

**`.1` Six formalisms, one question — the convergence is weaker than claimed but stronger than `S1-F019`'s**
The *sources* genuinely differ. The *question put to them* does not: `S1-F026` records the intake inversion — *"What would this book make us **stop believing** about KnowledgeOS?"* — and `S1-F024` records the uniform disposition *take the distinction, refuse the ontology*. Asking six books what must stay **outside** and receiving six answers saying *this stays outside* is **one disposition applied six times**, not six independent arrivals.

⚠ **But — and this matters — one source dissented.** The HSMM reading says KnowledgeOS *"should probably **contain** a Semi-Markov epistemic layer."* By `S1-F014`'s own asymmetry principle (*disagreement under a shared prompt is the informative case*), that dissent is **positive evidence the prompt did not fully determine the answers.**

So the honest ranking: **stronger than `S1-F019`'s 13–1** (there, one reasoner; here, genuinely independent sources that could have resisted, and one did) and **weaker than "six independent arrivals"** (one question shape, pre-loaded in direction).

⚠ Session 1's comparison — *"stronger even than KCON-012's six arrivals"* — measures against a number `S2-R-F016 §8` already reduced to two or three. The comparison inherits that defect.

**`.2` *"There is no single Knowledge Score"* is already law — CORRECTION**
