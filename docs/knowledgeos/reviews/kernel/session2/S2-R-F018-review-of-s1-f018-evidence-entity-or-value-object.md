## 5 · Level 2 — analytical validity

### `.1` The decisive dilemma is a **false dichotomy** — law takes a third route
**Duplicate-control check run, and it settles the question.** The dilemma's two horns are *Entity ⇒ cannot be inside the aggregate* and *Value Object ⇒ inside but unshareable and possibly unbounded*. Verified in law:

| Law | Text |
|---|---|
| `EvidenceLinks` (l. 209) | **Collection** — *"the justification: evidence **references** + acquisition method + reliability conditions; pseudo-evidence never admitted. **⟨C-3⟩** The core owns the **links**, never the evidence *content* — **external systems own the artifacts.** Holding content would drift the core toward the Ch IV **database** refusal"* |
| Register row (l. 109) | `Evidence` → **KEEP**, realised as the `EvidenceLinks` member |
| `EvidenceAdded` (l. 267) | *"new authentic evidence linked — EvidenceLinks extended"* |

Law holds **neither an Entity nor a Value Object of evidence**. It holds a **Collection of references plus acquisition method plus reliability conditions**, with content external. That route answers both horns:

- **Entity horn** (*grounding becomes procedural, not atomic*): the **links** are atomic within the aggregate; the *content*'s integrity is an external concern by design, and ⟨C-3⟩ gives the reason — holding content would collapse the core into a database.
- **VO horn** (*unshareable, unbounded*): references + acquisition method + reliability conditions is **bounded metadata**, and references are shareable by nature.

**So the question that *"determines the Kernel's shape"* does not determine it, because its two options are not exhaustive.** ⚠ Session 1's extraction is faithful — the dilemma is the source's — but the artifact presents an unresolved critical question where law has taken a documented position with a stated rationale. **Seventh document behaving as though it lacked v1.1** (`S2-F020`).

⚠ **What remains genuinely open, narrowly:** l. 635 `OQ-2` — *"May SNF-equivalence be recorded as an EvidenceLink supporting an identity-assignment act, without itself becoming an identity mechanism?"* — is open **in law itself**, and it is about what may *become* a link, not about evidence's DDD type. That is the surviving question, and it is `W:OQ-2`, not this dilemma.

Session 1 says the lens *"sharpens the dilemma rather than resolving it"*; in fact it **describes law's solution** without recognising it.
