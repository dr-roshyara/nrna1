## FINDING

S1-F004 classifies the determinism clash as **TRUE CONTRADICTION (candidate)**, and — commendably — refuses its own reconciliation because *"neither document draws the distinction."*

**But the two statements are in different grammatical moods, and statements in different moods cannot contradict.**

| | Statement | Mood | About |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** (`20260821-2032`) | the kernel *"is closer to: a **deterministic runtime** for governed knowledge state…"* | **proposal / design intent** | a thing to be built |
| **B** (`20260821-2351`) | *"Knowledge systems **lack**: deterministic execution, strict process isolation, universal semantics"* | **empirical observation** | things that exist |

**A proposal to build X and an observation that existing things lack X are not contradictory.** The observation may make the proposal *hard*, *novel*, or *unprecedented* — it does not make it false. B is evidence about a **population**; A is a claim about an **intended artefact** not in that population.

## EVIDENCE

Internal to S1-F004, using its own quotations:

1. B's own framing is comparative and descriptive: it appears under *"**Weak Analogy Areas**"* in an assessment of the Unix/Linux analogy — i.e. B is scoring an analogy, not stating a constraint on what can be built.
2. B's list is of properties **knowledge systems lack**, not properties **a knowledge kernel cannot have**.
3. A's own wording is hedged and directional — *"closer to"* — which is proposal language, not assertion of an achieved property.

## WHY THIS DIFFERS FROM SESSION 1'S OWN RECONCILIATION

S1-F004 sketches a reconciliation on **scope**: A may mean *deterministic governance mechanics*, B *deterministic execution*. It then rightly declines it, because adopting it *"would be my inference, not the corpus's position."*

**The modal reading requires no such inference.** It does not assign either document a meaning it did not state; it observes the mood in which each already speaks. Session 1's caution was appropriate to a scope reconciliation and does not apply here in the same way.

## WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH

- It **weakens** the classification from *TRUE CONTRADICTION* toward *tension between a design intent and an empirical observation about neighbouring systems.*
- It **does not** establish that determinism is achievable, desirable, or a Kernel property. **Nothing here bears on whether determinism should be adopted.**
- It **does not** resolve `W:C-2`, and **must not be cited in that adjudication.** The corpus is sealed for that purpose; this is a review record.

**The residual force of B is preserved and should be recorded as such:** even under the modal reading, B is evidence that determinism would be **unprecedented in this class of system**, which is a real burden on any proposal claiming it — just not a contradiction of one.
