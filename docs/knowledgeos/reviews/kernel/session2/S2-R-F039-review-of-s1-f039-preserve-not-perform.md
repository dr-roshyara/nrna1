## 4 · Confirmed — and the strongest item is not the convergence

**`.1` Brachman & Levesque supply the first *technical* argument for minimality.** *"There is an unbounded/infinite Knowledge Space, but every person or machine can only capture a **finite projection** of it"*, and reasoning bridges representation to belief.

✅ Session 1 is right that this is *"the first technical argument in the corpus for why the Kernel must be small"* — and it is a different **kind** of ground from everything preceding it. `S1-F021`'s *boring Kernel* asserts minimality; `S1-F016`'s falsification demonstrates non-atomicity; **this gives a reason**: a Kernel attempting the closure would be attempting the infinite. That is the only non-assertive, non-philosophical argument for minimality in 40 artifacts.

**`.2` The formulation is the positive complement to a prohibition law already states.** ⟦L⟧ §16: *"the kernel does not reason… engines at its ports do the reasoning."* Law says what the Kernel must **not** do; this says what it does **instead** — *preserve what makes reasoning possible.* ✅ **CONSISTENT**, and genuinely additive as a formulation, not as a capability.

**`.3` Williamson closes the genus programme negatively.** *"KnowledgeOS should not attempt to define knowledge by reducing it to information + truth + belief + evidence + justification."* ✅ Read with `S1-F037`'s *don't solve a primitive by renaming it* and `S1-F026`'s *the books do not prove our Kernel architecture*, the corpus's own verdict is that **Knowledge was not defined and may not be definable by decomposition.** Session 1 correctly marks this as a summary of corpus positions, not its own conclusion.

⚠ **But the conclusion is well-supported for a different reason**, and Session 1 supplies it without leaning on it: the formulation is **⟦L⟧-consistent with §16**, and `.1` gives it an argument. **Conclusion survives; the arrival count does not.**

**`.5` Institutional counting-as is a fourth position on context, and the four are not obviously compatible**
`S1-F022` (context may *constitute* knowledge) · `S1-F031` axis 2 (identity is *relational to* context) · ⟨Z-1⟩ (context is a *constitutive prerequisite*) · and now Searle (*institutions determine what something **counts as***).

✅ Session 1 records it as distinct and unreconciled. ⚠ Counting-as differs from the other three in **who confers**: not the context, but **collective acceptance**. That makes it an **authority** claim wearing a context label — and it bears on `S2-R-F001`'s cluster, since institutionally-conferred status has a validity period by construction. Recorded; **not routed.**

**`.6` Brandom vs `S1-F038` on projections — a genuine tension, and it points both ways**
