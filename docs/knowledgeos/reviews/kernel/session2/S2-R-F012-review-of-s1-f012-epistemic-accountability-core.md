## 9 · What is challenged

### `.1` *"The same three-register discipline"* is an identity claim from similarity — **CHALLENGE**
Session 1: *"This is the same three-register discipline this extraction uses (⟦C⟧/⟦I⟧/⟦L⟧), reached inside the corpus."*

The two triads are not the same instrument:

| The document | Session 1's markers |
|---|---|
| Fact from **Existing Law** | ⟦C⟧ = **quotation** (a provenance marker) |
| **DDD Interpretation** | ⟦I⟧ = **inference** |
| **Speculative Hypothesis** | ⟦L⟧ = **comparison to law** |

`⟦C⟧` marks *where a statement came from*; the document's first register marks *what kind of warrant it has*. And *Speculative Hypothesis* has no inline Session-1 counterpart — Session 1 carries hypothesis status in its classification block, not in the markup. **Both are three-way separations of evidence class; that is a family resemblance, not the same discipline.** Lens 8: similarity is not identity.
