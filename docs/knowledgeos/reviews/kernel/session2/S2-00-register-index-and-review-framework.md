## 1 · The adopted discipline, in one line each

| # | Lens | The question it forces |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **DDD** | is this one concept, two concepts, or one concept at two altitudes? |
| 2 | **Zero** | what does the absence *mean* — exclusion, unknown, unrepresented, or unmentioned? **Never convert "not mentioned" into "does not exist."** |
| 3 | **Provenance** | independent arrival, restatement, declared descent, or undeclared inheritance? Reporting an observation, or verifying it? |
| 4 | **Temporal** | state · event · lifecycle transition · validity interval · historical fact · or merely a timestamp? |
| 5 | **Authority** | who claims it, who may claim it — and is this evidence, interpretation, proposal, decision, or law? |
| 6 | **Evidence / justification** | what is observed · inferred · claimed · still hypothesis? |
| 7 | **Boundary / altitude** | *domain meaning · protected concern · state · aggregate · member · event · process · mechanism · pipeline stage · port/protocol · infrastructure* — **nothing is a contradiction until both propositions are shown to sit at the same altitude** |
| 8 | **Identity** | same object under two names, or two projections mistaken for two realities? **Never infer identity from similarity.** |
| 9 | **Claim / hypothesis** | FACT · CLAIM · HYPOTHESIS · INTERPRETATION · PROPOSAL · CONTRADICTION · OPEN QUESTION |
| 10 | **Knowledge / Knowledge Space** | Knowledge · Element · Product · Space · projection · knowledge-at-time · epistemic state · evidence · justification · history · retraction · identity — **test for levels/dimensions before assuming rival definitions** |

### Two standing rules that override convenience

**On contradictions.** Test first whether the propositions are *different questions · different types · different altitudes · different contexts · different temporal scopes · different senses of one word · proposal versus observation · evidence versus interpretation.* Only then call it genuine.

**On reconciliations.** Finding a plausible reconciliation **does not license resolving the issue.** Record it as HYPOTHESIS or ALTERNATIVE INTERPRETATION; **the original unresolved status stands.**

**On convergence.** Do not count as independent: restatement · copied reasoning · inherited framing · undeclared descent · response to the same source.

## 3a · The three levels of review — every artifact, kept separate

| Level | Question | Session 2's position |
|---|---|---|
| **1 · EXTRACTION FIDELITY** | is Session 1 representing the source accurately? | **structurally UNVERIFIED** — see §3b |
| **2 · ANALYTICAL VALIDITY** | does the inference follow from the evidence *in the artifact*? | testable, and the core of this session's work |
| **3 · CROSS-ARTIFACT SIGNIFICANCE** | new · reinforcement · recurrence · persistence · revision · contradiction · vocabulary collision · another instance | testable against the register |

**A successful Level 2 or Level 3 NEVER implies Level 1 was verified.** Stated in every per-artifact review.

## 3c · Implementation relevance — the second question

Analytical soundness and build-worthiness are **separate judgments**. For every material finding: **need? · altitude? · status? · why? · what would we lose? · smallest thing?**

Status vocabulary: `IMPLEMENT` · `IMPLEMENT LATER` · `PRESERVE AS KNOWLEDGE ONLY` · `DO NOT IMPLEMENT` · `NEEDS FURTHER EVIDENCE`.

⚠ *Interesting · consistent · mathematically valid · externally supported · extracted by Session 1* are **never** reasons to implement. A finding may be **TRUE + IMPORTANT + DO NOT IMPLEMENT**. Where the protection a finding asks for **already exists in law**, the answer is `DO NOT IMPLEMENT (already protected)` — `ES-005.4`, never a second concept.

⚠ **A recommendation is not a decision.** Session 2 records implementation relevance as a review finding for later adjudication. It never becomes a Kernel decision here.

## 3b · Standing scope limit — what Session 2 structurally cannot test

Recorded at `S2-F021.1`. Session 2 reads **artifacts**, never the corpus. It can test reasoning, classification, altitude, provenance logic and cross-artifact consistency. It **cannot** test:

- **fidelity** — whether a quotation matches its source;
- **selection completeness** — an accurate quotation of an unrepresentative fragment is invisible from here.

So *"Session 2 reviewed it"* must never be read as *"the extraction was verified against the source."* This is a property of the two-session design, not a defect in Session 1.
