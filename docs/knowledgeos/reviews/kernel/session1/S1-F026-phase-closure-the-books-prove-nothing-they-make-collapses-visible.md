reversal. That is a provenance-relevant observation for anyone weighing the later material.

## Finding 5 · Two substantive contributions from this batch's extractions ⟦CLAIM⟧

**Logitica** (`013116`, `P3`, 1,849 lines): ⟦C⟧ *"Knowledge should preserve the **structure and reasoning
required to reconstruct a solution**, rather than merely preserve the solution itself."* ·
⟦C⟧ *"**A candidate that fits the current observations is not necessarily uniquely established**"* —
*"different explanations compatible with the current observational boundary."*
⟦INFERENCE⟧ The second is **underdetermination stated as an admission problem**: fitting the evidence is
not sufficiency. It is independent support for `W:F-CM-1b`'s plural-interpretation question, from a
problem-solving source rather than a philosophical one.

**Philosophy of Complex Systems** (`013721`, `P3`, 3,374 lines): ⟦C⟧ *"**The object being known may itself
be dynamic, relational, emergent, and only pragmatically bounded**"*; knowledge as *"dynamical
condition-structured"*; a bounded context read as ⟦C⟧ *"a **deliberately selected boundary** for a
particular model, language, responsibility and interaction regime."*
⟦INFERENCE⟧ This is the corpus's strongest challenge to a *stable referent*: if the known object is only
pragmatically bounded, identity-of-what becomes unstable — which is `S1-F022`'s *identity ≠ continuity* and
`S1-F015`'s content-mutability question arriving from complexity science. **Third route to the same
question.**
