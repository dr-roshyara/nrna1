## Finding 1 · The recurring placement verdict ⟦CLAIM ×n — one pattern⟧

⟦C⟧ Rescorla: *"**Bayesian inference should be a supporting epistemic mechanism, not the KnowledgeOS Kernel
itself.**"*
⟦C⟧ Mathematical modelling: *"**There is no single 'Knowledge Score'**"* · *"KnowledgeOS therefore needs
**multiple** mathematical representations."*
⟦C⟧ Shieber: *"This book substantially strengthens the Kernel, but it also tells us **what must remain
outside** the Kernel."*

⟦INFERENCE⟧ **Third and fourth independent arrivals at the regime-not-ontology verdict**, after
`S1-F005`'s Phase 1 statement (*"the kernel protects the rules; the mathematics describes the behavior"*)
and the Fagin resolution. ⟦INFERENCE⟧ The corpus is now **uniform** on this: every formal apparatus it has
read — Bayesian inference, mathematical modelling, measure theory, possible worlds, information theory — is
placed as a *supporting regime*. That uniformity across six independent readings is the strongest
convergence in the entire corpus, stronger even than KCON-012's six arrivals, because these are six
*different* formalisms reaching the same placement.

⟦INFERENCE⟧ *"There is no single Knowledge Score"* is the sharpest form: it forecloses the quantification
programme the corpus repeatedly attempts (`S1-F006` entropy, the measure-theory thread) **at the level of
type**, not of accuracy — no scalar can carry it, so the question is not *which* scalar.

## Finding 2 · The dissent: a semi-Markov **layer** ⟦DESIGN PROPOSAL⟧

⟦C⟧ HSMM: *"**KnowledgeOS should probably contain a Semi-Markov epistemic layer.**"* · ⟦C⟧ *"We are
inferring **hidden states from observations**."*

⟦INFERENCE⟧ **This is the only document in the block proposing that a formalism be *contained*** rather than
supported. ⚠ Recorded as a **DESIGN PROPOSAL in tension with the block's uniform verdict** — and note it
says *layer*, not Kernel, so the tension may be dissolvable by `S1-F028`'s extent/contents distinction: a
layer inside KnowledgeOS need not be inside the Kernel's extent. Neither document says so.
⟦INFERENCE⟧ Its substantive contribution is *hidden state inferred from observation*, which is the same
structure as `S1-F014`'s derived-states hypothesis arriving from time-series statistics rather than DDD.
**Second route to derived epistemic state.**

## Finding 3 · Two new distinctions ⟦DISTINCTION⟧

⟦C⟧ Ammerman & Singer: *"**Inquiry is not the same thing as Knowledge.**"* · ⟦C⟧ *"**KnowledgeOS should not
become a decision engine merely because knowledge influences decisions.**"*

⟦INFERENCE⟧ The first completes a chain the corpus has been assembling piecemeal: `Question`/`Inquiry`
(`S1-F024`, from the critical-thinking source) is now explicitly separated from Knowledge. The second is a
**scope-creep prohibition of a form not yet recorded**: influence does not confer membership. ⟦INFERENCE⟧ It
is the same shape as `S1-F020`'s consumption rule (*outputs consumed ⇏ Kernel responsibility*) applied to
*decisions* rather than contexts — **second arrival at the influence-is-not-membership principle.**

⟦C⟧ Shieber also contributes an admission test in one line: *"**Was this input allowed to participate?**"*
⟦INFERENCE⟧ A participation/authorisation question distinct from sufficiency — it asks about *standing*, not
*strength*. Adjacent to `W:C-3` and to `S1-F029`'s evidence-role constraint, and not reducible to either.

## Finding 4 · Semantic grounding must attach to epistemic quantities ⟦CLAIM⟧

⟦C⟧ Rescorla: *"**Semantic grounding must be attached to epistemic quantities.**"*

⟦INFERENCE⟧ This is the constructive counterpart to `S1-F006`'s finding that semantic entropy is
knowledge-base-relative and to ⟦L⟧ ⟨r4⟩'s *low entropy ≠ certainty*: if a quantity must carry its semantic
grounding, then a bare number **cannot** cross a boundary. ⟦INFERENCE⟧ That is a **representation
requirement**, and it is the most implementable item in this block: it bears on unruled `W:C-11`
(Confidence derivability) by saying that whatever Confidence is, it may not travel ungrounded.
⟦L⟧ Comparison only: v1.1 ⟨r4⟩ makes interpretation probabilities *port-contract vocabulary, never aggregate
members* — **CONSISTENT in direction**, and this supplies the reason.
