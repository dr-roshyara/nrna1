## Why it matters

⟦I⟧ **The corpus anticipated its own vocabulary-collision problem on 2026-08-21** and stated the
correct discipline for it — *preserve, do not normalize* — four days before the collisions this
programme later found independently (`projection` in three senses, `agent` in two, `UNKNOWN` at two
altitudes). §5.1 is the general rule those specific cases instantiate.

⟦I⟧ Two entries bear directly on authority: **the mechanism records authority rather than creating
it**, and **cannot create a Human Act**. That is the same limit as KCON-018 (*"no authority
manufacture"*) reached independently, and the same limit as KCON-013 (recorded difference ≠ actual
independence). **Three independent arrivals** at: *the system can record, not manufacture.*

⟦I⟧ `Artifact` *"not equivalent to knowledge"* is an early statement of representation ≠ knowledge,
which ⟦L⟧ v1.1 carries as *Representation ≠ Identity* / *Expression ≠ Meaning* (§15). **CONSISTENT.**
