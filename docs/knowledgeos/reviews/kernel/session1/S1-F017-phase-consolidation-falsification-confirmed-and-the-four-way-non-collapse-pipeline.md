## Finding 1 · The falsification is confirmed at phase level ⟦FACT about the corpus⟧

⟦C⟧ *"The initial intuition was that KnowledgeOS might require a large `KnowledgeAggregate` containing
identity, evidence, justification, epistemic state, confidence, and history. **That hypothesis has now
been falsified as a default aggregate boundary.**"*

⟦C⟧ The generalised reason — the phase's sharpest sentence:

> ⟦C⟧ *"**Semantic relatedness, traceability, constitutional responsibility, and historical coherence do
> not by themselves imply transactional atomicity.**"*

⟦C⟧ And the limit of the result: *"The current research therefore does **not** yet define the Kernel or
its aggregate boundary."*

⟦INFERENCE⟧ This **ratifies `S1-F016`** at phase level and extends it: F016 falsified five *pairs*; this
generalises to **four grounds** (relatedness, traceability, constitutional responsibility, historical
coherence) none of which implies atomicity. ⟦INFERENCE⟧ It is also the fourth independent statement of
KCON-012, and the first to name *constitutional responsibility* as an insufficient ground — which
matters, because "the constitution requires it" was the warrant behind several member lists.

⚠ Note the careful wording: falsified **as a default** boundary. Not falsified as *a possible* boundary.
The document does not claim the aggregate is wrong, only that it cannot be assumed.

## Finding 2 · The phase's strongest positive result — a four-way non-collapse pipeline ⟦MODEL⟧

⟦C⟧ *"One of the strongest results of the brainstorming phase is the emergence of a set of distinctions
that must not be collapsed."*

⟦C⟧ **`Expression ≠ Meaning ≠ Candidate ≠ Knowledge`**, as a pipeline:

```
Expression → Interpretation → Meaning / Candidate Meaning → Governance / Admission → Knowledge
```

⟦C⟧ With the consequences stated as six separate non-collapses:
*"an expression is not knowledge; an interpretation is not knowledge; a candidate is not knowledge; a
mechanism's output is not knowledge; **mechanism confidence is not domain confidence**; **semantic
agreement is not truth**."*

⟦C⟧ And a definition by contrast: *"A candidate is an interpretation or proposed semantic object.
Knowledge is a **ratified domain commitment**."* → `Candidate → Admission boundary → KnowledgeClaim`.

⟦INFERENCE⟧ Two things here are new to Session 1:
1. **`Knowledge` defined as a *ratified domain commitment*** — a **sixth genus** for Knowledge, after
   *durable understanding*, *relationship*, *product with members*, *justified state evolution*, and
   *capacity*. It is the only genus that makes ratification (an act by an authority) constitutive.
2. **`KnowledgeClaim`** appears as the post-admission object name — ⟦INFERENCE⟧ note this is the term the
   corpus census flagged as a **recurring architectural temptation** (proposed, rejected, re-proposed
   under other names). Here it returns without acknowledgement of that history.

⟦L⟧ Comparison target only: v1.1 §15 carries *Expression ≠ Meaning* and *Representation ≠ Identity*;
⟨r4⟩ carries *Probability ≠ Truth*. ⟦INFERENCE⟧ The four-way pipeline is **CONSISTENT** with those and
**finer-grained** — it inserts *Candidate* as a distinct stage between meaning and knowledge, which v1.1
handles as candidate-side port vocabulary rather than as a pipeline stage.
