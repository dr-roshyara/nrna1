## Finding 1 · The complete cycle ⟦FALSIFICATION SEQUENCE⟧

⟦INFERENCE⟧ **This is the only place in 158 documents where the corpus proposes an architecture, attacks it
from multiple independent angles, and reaches a stated resolution — all within 73 minutes.** Every other
proposal in the corpus either stands unchallenged (`S1-F009`…`F012`) or is challenged without resolution
(`S1-F016`'s falsification, which the corpus later re-asserted against at `S1-F019`).

⟦C⟧ **Refutation 1:** *"'knowledge projection = measure' is probably **not** right"*; the definitions are
*"too loose"*; one construct *"cannot be both"* of the two things the proposal needs.
⟦C⟧ **Refutation 2 (v0.2):** records the correction as *"**Knowledge ≠ family of measures**"* — knowledge
projections *possess* measurable properties while the Kernel preserves something else.
⟦C⟧ **Refutation 3:** *"the synthesis has moved from 'measurements of knowledge' to **identifying knowledge
itself with conditional expectation, filtrations, probability measures, martingales**"* — and the book
*"does not establish that every knowledge state is a conditional expectation."*
⟦C⟧ **Refutation 4:** *"the synthesis still makes **six important category errors**"*, headed by
*"**Knowledge Space is not automatically a probability space**."*
⟦C⟧ **Refutation 5:** *"I would **not accept its 'definitive mathematical synthesis' or 'complete
mathematical framework' claims** yet."*

⟦INFERENCE⟧ The refutations are **not redundant** — they attack at four distinct levels: the
projection=measure identification (1), the family-of-measures ontology (2), the conditional-expectation
identification (3), and the space-type assumption (4), with (5) attacking the completeness *claim* rather
than the content. That is exactly the *perspective-diverse* attack pattern the corpus never applied to its
Kernel formulations.

## Finding 3 · What the supporting sources actually license ⟦DISTINCTION⟧

⟦C⟧ Roberts, *Measurement Theory*: measurement as *"a **homomorphism** from an empirical relational system
to a numerical relational system"*, with **meaningfulness** and **scale-type** conditions.
⟦C⟧ Aggoun & Elliott: measurable spaces, filtrations, conditional expectation, change of measure, filtering
— ⟦C⟧ described as *"a mathematical **regime for modelling uncertain state estimation from observations**"*,
which *"does not establish that every knowledge state is a conditional expectation."*

⟦INFERENCE⟧ **Roberts is the finding worth keeping.** A homomorphism requires an **empirical relational
system to exist first** — i.e. measurement is only meaningful if the qualitative structure is already
there. ⟦INFERENCE⟧ That is the formal statement of `S1-F038`'s *store the substrate; compute the measure*,
and it retro-justifies it: **you cannot measure what you have not first structured.** Two independent routes
to the same principle, one informal and one from measurement theory.
⟦INFERENCE⟧ It also supplies the criterion the corpus's earlier quantification attempts lacked
(`S1-F006` entropy, Confidence at `S1-F008`/`F019`): **meaningfulness and scale type**, not accuracy.
