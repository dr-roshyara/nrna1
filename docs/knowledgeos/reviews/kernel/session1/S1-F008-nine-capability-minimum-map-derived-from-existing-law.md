## The argument against a minimal rule-evaluator Kernel

⟦C⟧ A prior formulation — *"the Kernel is simply `(command, state, rules, evidence) → admissibility
verdict`"* — is judged **too small**, because the domain already holds authoritative responsibility
for identity assignment, evidence admission, justification preservation/evaluation, epistemic-state
determination, confidence, history and single-gate admission.

⟦C⟧ *"So the Kernel **cannot simply be a generic rule evaluator**. It has to protect an **actual domain
boundary**."*

⟦C⟧ A specific ownership split is quoted: *"F-5 explicitly establishes that the **domain owns the
admitted JustificationPath and its sufficiency evaluation**, while the reasoning mechanism owns only
production…"*

⟦INFERENCE⟧ This is the corpus's clearest statement of the **anti-minimalist** direction: elsewhere the
pressure runs toward a smaller Kernel (S1-F007's god-object critique; the Zero lens). Here the pressure
runs the other way — *too small* is also a failure mode. **Both directions are now on the record and
they are not reconciled.**
