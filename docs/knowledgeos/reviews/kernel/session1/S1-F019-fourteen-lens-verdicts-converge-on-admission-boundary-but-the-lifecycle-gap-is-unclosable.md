## Finding 3 · The smallest sufficient boundary — with two *conditional* exclusions ⟦MODEL⟧

⟦C⟧ *"**The Kernel is the Admission Boundary of the KnowledgeAggregate.**"*

⟦C⟧ **Must own (7):** identity assignment · constitutional evaluation · epistemic state determination ·
evidence **reference** preservation · justification preservation · history recording ·
**contradiction detection**.

⟦C⟧ **Must not own (8):** semantic interpretation · evidence content · reasoning · workflow · projection ·
rule definition · **"Confidence (if derivable)"** · **"Supersession/contestation (if separate lifecycle)"**.

⟦INFERENCE⟧ Two items are excluded **conditionally**, which is new and analytically important: the
exclusion of Confidence depends on unruled `W:C-11` (is it derivable?), and the exclusion of
supersession/contestation depends on the unresolved lifecycle question. ⟦INFERENCE⟧ **So this member list
is not a list — it is a list *parameterised by two open questions*.** No earlier list states its own
dependencies.

⟦INFERENCE⟧ Also: **contradiction detection** enters a must-own list for the first time (absent from all
four earlier lists), while **evidence reference** (not content) is retained — consistent with `S1-F014`'s
reference/content split and with `S1-F018`'s decisive Entity/VO question.
⟦INFERENCE⟧ **Projection is excluded for the fourth time**, still without argument.

⟦C⟧ Maximum bound: *"The Kernel must not own any responsibility that is not essential to protecting the
aggregate invariant."* ⟦INFERENCE⟧ A third equilibrium statement, matching `S1-F009`'s wording more
closely than `S1-F012`'s.

## Finding 4 · Ten "established facts" — and one carries its own defeat ⟦CLAIM⟧

⟦C⟧ 1 single-gate admission · 2 *"Identity is assigned at admission and is **immutable**"* · 3 *"Evidence
is admitted… and preserved as **references**"* · 4 justification preserved · 5 epistemic state determined
at admission · 6 ⚠ *"**Confidence** is assigned at admission (**but derivation is unknown**)"* · 7 history
recorded · 8 constitutional evaluation at admission · 9 *"semantic interpretation remains outside"* ·
10 *"**Mechanisms never become identity authority**."*

⟦INFERENCE⟧ Item 6 is labelled an *established fact* while conceding its mechanism is unknown — i.e. the
corpus records *that* confidence is assigned without knowing *from what*. That is precisely unruled
`W:C-11`, and it is the second time a document has listed confidence as settled-yet-underivable
(`S1-F008` did the same). ⟦INFERENCE⟧ Item 10 is the corpus's clearest statement of the
mechanism/authority prohibition, matching ⟦L⟧ v1.1 obligation 4 — **CONSISTENT**.

⟦C⟧ Three hypotheses are then ranked by which evidence base supports them: *"Admission Boundary (most
supported by F-1…F-5)"* · *"Epistemic Lifecycle Manager (most supported by lifecycle reasoning)"* ·
*"Constitutional Transition Engine (most supported by constitutional reasoning)"*.
⟦INFERENCE⟧ **Each hypothesis is supported by a different evidence base** — so the disagreement is not
about reasoning quality but about which base is authoritative. That reframes the whole competition.
