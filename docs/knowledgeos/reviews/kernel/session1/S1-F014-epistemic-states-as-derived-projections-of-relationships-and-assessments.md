## Finding 1 · The central hypothesis — states are derived, not managed ⟦HYPOTHESIS⟧

⟦C⟧ Tested per concept:

| Concept | As state | As relationship | ⟦C⟧ Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| `SUPERSEDED` | in a state of being superseded | superseded **by** another claim | **Relationship + State** |
| `RECONCILED` | in a state of being reconciled | reconciled **with** another claim | **Relationship + State** |
| `CONTESTED` | in a state of being contested | contested **by** another claim | **Relationship + State** |
| `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` | having insufficient evidence | insufficient **relative to** a threshold | **Assessment + State** |

⟦C⟧ **Key insight:** *"**Epistemic states are not independent properties of a claim. They are
projections of relationships and assessments.**"*

⟦C⟧ Consequences drawn: *"The Kernel should preserve **relationships** … and **assessments** … Epistemic
states can be **derived** … The Kernel does **not need to 'manage' states**—it needs to manage the
relationships and assessments from which states are derived."*

⟦C⟧ With an explicit own/not-own table: Kernel **owns** relationship preservation and assessment
preservation; **does not own** deriving states from relationships, nor applying assessments itself.

⟦INFERENCE⟧ **This directly answers the blocker `S1-F013` identified** (*state vs relationship vs event*)
— and answers it *"both, with the relationship primary"*. ⚠ The two documents are 2 minutes apart and
reach **opposite conclusions about answerability**: `113410` says UNRESOLVED for all eight layers;
`113645` resolves the same question with a verdict per concept. **Recorded as a genuine disagreement, not
reconciled.**

⟦INFERENCE⟧ **Note the vocabulary collision.** This document uses *"projections of relationships and
assessments"* — a **fifth** sense of *projection*, distinct from CQRS read-model, epistemic projection of
a Knowledge Space, participant projection, and measure-theoretic projection. Here it means *derived
attribute*. ⚠ **Do not merge with any earlier sense.**

⟦L⟧ Comparison target only: v1.1 §9 treats the seven epistemic states as **first-class and enumerated**,
with *"the seven states are distinct and none is a degree of another"*, and transitions as *named,
governed, forward-only*. ⟦INFERENCE⟧ **This hypothesis is in tension with that**: derived states would
not be stored, transitioned or enumerated at all. ⚠ Classified **POTENTIAL TENSION**, recorded; **not a
defect claim, not adjudicated** — and note the document never references v1.1.

## Finding 2 · Six named internal contradictions ⟦FACT about the corpus⟧

⟦C⟧ §11 *"CONTRADICTIONS DISCOVERED"* — the corpus's first explicit self-contradiction register:

| ⟦C⟧ Contradiction | Statement |
|---|---|
| **Identity vs Epistemic State** | *"Identity is immutable; epistemic state changes. If they are in the same aggregate, how are immutable and mutable concepts reconciled?"* |
| **Evidence vs Justification** | *"Evidence is outside the Kernel; justification is inside. If evidence is outside, how can justification be evaluated inside?"* |
| **State vs Event** | *"States are derived from events. If the Kernel manages states, does it need to manage events? If it manages events, why manage states?"* |
| **Relationships vs Properties** | *"Contradiction, supersession, contestation are relationships. If the Kernel treats them as properties, it loses the relationship."* |
| **Admission vs Lifecycle** | *"Admission is the first transition. If the Kernel owns only admission, who owns the rest of the lifecycle? If it owns the lifecycle, is it a God Aggr[egate]?"* |
| **Confidence vs Evidence** | *"Confidence is derived from evidence strength. If evidence is outside the Kernel, how is confidence assigned?"* |

⟦INFERENCE⟧ Three of these hit earlier Session-1 findings squarely:
- *Admission vs Lifecycle* is precisely the `S1-F009` ↔ `S1-F010` disagreement, **and it names the
  God-Aggregate horn** — so the corpus itself frames F009/F010 as a dilemma, not a mistake.
- *Confidence vs Evidence* is a mechanism-level statement of unruled `W:C-11`.
- *Identity vs Epistemic State* asks how one aggregate holds immutable and mutable members — which
  `S1-F013`'s Model A answered by making everything immutable except History. ⚠ Two adjacent documents,
  two incompatible treatments of mutability.

## Finding 3 · The strongest hypothesis, and the project's refusal ⟦HYPOTHESIS + REFUSAL⟧

⟦C⟧ §13: *"**The KnowledgeAggregate is the smallest consistency boundary** required to preserve the
constitutional integrity of a knowledge claim across its epistemic lifecycl[e]"* — where ⟦C⟧ *"the
smallest consistency boundary is the boundary that protects the aggregate invariant: Identity, Evidence
References, Justification, Epistemic State, Confiden[ce]…"*

⟦C⟧ Project response: *"this is a strong discovery round, but **I would not accept its strongest
architectural conclusion yet**."*

⟦INFERENCE⟧ Second instance of the pattern first seen in `S1-F007`: **diagnosis accepted, conclusion
refused.** ⟦INFERENCE⟧ Note the internal strain — §13 says the Kernel *is* the KnowledgeAggregate holding
Epistemic State, while §7 says the Kernel should **not** manage states. The document contains both.
⚠ Recorded as an internal inconsistency, unresolved.

⟦INFERENCE⟧ Also notable: it distinguishes **Evidence References** (inside) from **evidence content**
(outside) — ⟦C⟧ §12: *"The aggregate must preserve evidence references, but evidence content may be
outside. What is the boundary between refer[ence and content]?"* That reference/content split is new to
Session 1 and bears on unruled `W:C-10` (evidence-reference resolvability).

## DDD interpretation

- `SUPERSEDED` / `RECONCILED` / `CONTESTED` → **CANDIDATE RELATIONSHIPS** (not states).
- `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` → **CANDIDATE ASSESSMENT**.
- Epistemic state → **CANDIDATE DERIVED ATTRIBUTE / read-side projection**, not a stored member.
- `EvidenceReference` vs evidence content → **CANDIDATE VALUE OBJECT vs external artefact**.
- KnowledgeAggregate → **CANDIDATE AGGREGATE** = the smallest consistency boundary (§13), contradicted
  by §7 within the same document.
