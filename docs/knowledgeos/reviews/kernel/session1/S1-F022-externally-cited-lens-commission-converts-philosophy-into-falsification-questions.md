## Finding 1 · The method: every philosophical result becomes a question against an existing item ⟦METHOD⟧

⟦INFERENCE⟧ The project review does not import a single concept. For each philosophical challenge it (i)
states the challenge, (ii) converts it to a falsification question, (iii) **routes it to an existing
workbook item**, and (iv) refuses the architectural conclusion. Verbatim pattern:

⟦C⟧ *"So I would extract this as a **falsification question, not a new model**."*
⟦C⟧ *"So this is **not a new philosophical feature we should add**. It is **independent evidence that C-15
deserves serious adjudication**."*
⟦C⟧ *"Therefore I would **add this to our C-8/C-18/C-4 investigation, rather than create a new
capability**."*

⟦INFERENCE⟧ **This is the discipline the whole programme has been trying to enforce, executed inside the
corpus on 2026-08-23.** It is the strongest counter-evidence to reading Phase 2 as unconstrained
philosophical accumulation.

## Finding 2 · `identity ≠ continuity` — the strongest challenge ⟦QUESTION⟧

⟦C⟧ *"DeepSeek repeatedly challenges the assumption that: **an identified knowledge object remains 'the
same thing' because its identity persists**."*
⟦C⟧ Derived question: *"**What exactly makes an evolving epistemic object the same Knowledge identity
rather than a new Knowledge identity?**"*
⟦C⟧ Disposition: *"This **strengthens C-8 (identity continuity)** considerably. And importantly, it does
**not invalidate** our 'identity assigned, never derived' principle. It asks whether we have sufficiently
defined the **conditions** under which that assigne[d identity persists]."*

⟦INFERENCE⟧ **This is the exact question I recorded four times as unasked, and `S1-F015` first posed as
content-mutability.** Here it is stated at identity altitude and routed to `W:C-8`. ⟦INFERENCE⟧ Note the
precision of the refusal: the challenge is admitted against the *conditions* of identity persistence,
**not** against the assignment principle. That distinction preserves ⟦L⟧ INV-KOS-IDENTITY-001 while
opening `W:C-8`.

## Finding 3 · Retraction: `absence ≠ withdrawal`, and withdrawal may be a **process** ⟦QUESTION⟧

⟦C⟧ *"DeepSeek distinguishes: **absence ≠ withdrawal** — and describes withdrawal as potentially a
**process rather than merely an event**."* ⟦C⟧ *"how a Kernel should distinguish **discrete retraction from
gradual transformation**."*
⟦C⟧ Derived question: *"**Is retraction a change in epistemic state, a relationship to prior knowledge, or
a distinct historical act — and what must remain invariant across it?**"*
⟦C⟧ Disposition: *"independent evidence that **C-15 deserves serious adjudication**."*

⟦INFERENCE⟧ **A sixth formulation of the retraction question**, and the only one raising *gradual*
transformation — every earlier formulation (`S1-F010`, `F014`, `F015`, `F018`, `F021`) assumes a discrete
act. ⟦INFERENCE⟧ `absence ≠ withdrawal` is also a **new non-collapse**, adjacent to but distinct from
⟦L⟧ v1.1's *unknown ≠ absent ≠ false*.

## Finding 4 · Context may be **constitutive**, not metadata — *"the most dangerous challenge"* ⟦QUESTION⟧

⟦C⟧ *"**This is probably the most dangerous challenge to the current Kernel.** … context may not merely
modify knowledge; **context may constitute what the knowledge is**."*
⟦C⟧ Derived question: *"**Does the Kernel merely preserve context as evidence/metadata, or is some context
constitutive of the identity of the KnowledgeAggregate itself?**"*
⟦C⟧ Routed to *"our **C-8/C-18/C-4** investigation, rather than create a new capability."*
⟦C⟧ And it notes the Constitution *"already says context is constitutive of identity"*, connecting to the
`agency/context absent` problem.

⟦INFERENCE⟧ ⟦L⟧ v1.1 ⟨Z-1⟩ indeed makes context a **constitutive prerequisite** — absence of context
yields *pre-domain refusal*, no `KnowledgeId`. ⟦INFERENCE⟧ So the challenge is **not** that v1.1 treats
context as metadata; it is the unanswered follow-on: *what is the identity of the context itself?*
Recorded as a question against `W:C-8`/`W:C-18`/`W:C-4`. **Not adjudicated.**

## Finding 5 · Two explicit refusals ⟦REJECT⟧

⟦C⟧ **Contradiction/perspectivalism:** *"we must **reject** DeepSeek's architectural conclusion… we must
**not** infer: therefore `CONFLICTED` should be replaced by perspective-relative truth."* Converted to:
*"**Does the Kernel distinguish semantic contradiction from perspectival disagreement before admitting
something as a contradiction?**"* — and noted as *"a very strong convergence"* with **`F-CM-1`:
ambiguity ≠ contradiction**.
⟦C⟧ **knowing/acting (`zhi`/`xing`):** *"Interesting question. But I would **not put this inside the Kernel
yet**."*
⟦C⟧ **continuous transformation:** *"The 'continuous transformation' claim should **NOT** be imported."*

⟦INFERENCE⟧ **The perspectival-disagreement question is independent corpus-side support for `W:F-CM-1a`'s
subject matter**, reached from Chinese philosophy rather than from the ambiguity thread — and it arrives
at *distinguish before admitting*, which is a **pre-admission** test rather than a state question. That is
a genuinely different angle on F-CM-1 than anything in the adjudication record.
⟦INFERENCE⟧ The rejection of *continuous transformation* also pre-empts, on 08-23, the Phase 2
*continuous change* position later recorded in the Knowledge-definition thread.
