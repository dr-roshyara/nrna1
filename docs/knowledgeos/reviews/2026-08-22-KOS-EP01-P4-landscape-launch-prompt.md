## 3 · Primary Objective

Produce the **"KnowledgeOS Constitutional Invariant Map v1.0"** — the small set of constitutional rules that allow trustworthy knowledge evolution.

## 4 · Mandatory Discipline Rules

- **Rule 1 — No new research concepts.** Anything not already extracted is marked **Candidate**, never admitted to the map as established. The map consolidates; it does not collect.
- **Rule 2 — Preserve identity discipline.** **Similarity ≠ Identity.** Every invariant is validated by: its **ID** · its **evidence lineage** · its **original registration** · its **validation state**. No look-alike row is accepted on resemblance.
- **Rule 3 — Separate the three layers.** Keep distinct: **Kernel Invariant** (constitutional, PRESERVE) · **Reasoning Mechanism** (operational, GOVERN — engines: LLM, TMS, AGM, logic solver) · **Representation** (Vāṇī/expression layer). A row must not blur its layer.

1. **Character Definition** — the canonical essence, adjudicating the accumulating clause refinements.
2. **What KnowledgeOS Is NOT** — with **5 non-identities** (the five forbidden collapses / non-identities established across the study).
3. **Constitutional Invariant Map** — 7-column schema:
   **Invariant | Dimensions | Forbidden Collapse | Evidence | Strength | Validation Target | Notes**
   expected across the invariant families: Identity · Evidence · Authority · Transformation · Temporal · Reasoning · Contradiction · Agent.
4. **Kernel vs Reasoning Engine Boundary** — what the kernel does (PRESERVE) vs what engines do (GOVERN).
5. **Mathematical Lens Consolidation** — table consolidating the mathematical lenses (type model · topological/relational representation · epistemic graph · logic · temporal) into their P4 rows.
6. **KnowledgeOS vs LLM comparison** — the consolidated comparison table.
7. **Final Kernel Definition** — format: *"KnowledgeOS is a ______ that ______ while protecting ______."*
