## 1 · Role

**Principal Epistemic Architect** — responsible for **validating the KnowledgeOS Constitutional Invariant Map v1.0.** The research phase is complete. The responsibility is **no longer discovery** — it is to **test, challenge, validate, reject, consolidate** the candidate constitutional material.

## 2 · Context

The P4 Map defines the 8-family landscape (Identity · Evidence · Authority · Transformation · Temporal · Reasoning · Contradiction · Agent). Canonical character: *"KnowledgeOS is a constitutional epistemic operating system that enables trustworthy truth discovery by preserving the identity, context, evidence, reasoning, uncertainty, agency, and historical evolution of knowledge across time."* Negative boundary: not a repository, not an LLM, not a truth oracle, not a similarity engine, not an autonomous decision maker.

## 3 · Objective

Determine **which candidates survive as true constitutional invariants** — *"Which rules are necessary for KnowledgeOS to remain KnowledgeOS."*

## 4 · Validation Principle

`Evidence + Domain Independence + Necessity Test + Failure Test` — *"A candidate becomes constitutional only if removing it causes epistemic corruption."*

## 5 · Validation Questions (per candidate)

1. **Identity Test** — if this rule disappears, does KnowledgeOS lose the identity of knowledge? (e.g. Representation≠Meaning → Document=Knowledge)
2. **Evidence Test** — can unsupported claims become knowledge? (e.g. Evidence≠Authority)
3. **Reasoning Test** — can generated conclusions become facts? (e.g. Observation≠Inference → AI output=reality)
4. **Transformation Test** — can knowledge change without preserving history? (e.g. Revision≠Deletion)
5. **Agent Test** — can knowledge lose accountability? (e.g. "Who knows?" → knowledge exists anonymously)

## 6 · Required Classification (every candidate must receive one)

`KERNEL / ENGINE / MECHANISM / REPRESENTATION / DOMAIN-SPECIFIC / REJECTED / OPEN`

## 7 · Mandatory Separation

Do not confuse **Constitutional Principle** (e.g. "Relations must preserve meaning") with **Implementation** (e.g. "Use a graph database"). The P4 rule remains: *"Mathematics describes, never defines."* Topology, type theory, graphs, TMS, AGM, LLMs are **mechanisms or explanatory lenses, not automatically kernel laws.**

## 8 · Special Validation Areas

1. **Navya-Nyāya contributions:**
   - **Avacchedaka** — is context-boundary preservation **universally required** or only a **semantic modelling technique**?
   - **Abhāva** — is structured absence a **universal epistemic requirement** or a **reasoning mechanism**?
   - **Coextension without Identity** — is `Similarity ≠ Identity` **constitutional**? Could KnowledgeOS exist without this?
2. **LLM Comparison** — validate the architectural distinction (LLM: generate likely response vs KnowledgeOS: preserve justified epistemic state). *"What capability does an LLM fundamentally lack?"* Candidates: persistent epistemic identity · historical belief evolution · provenance preservation · contradiction management · justification accountability — **validate, do not assume.**
