## 1 · The question

**Reference-Architecture-altitude question:** does the EP-01 Port Contract vocabulary refinement — the T-2 slice (`08841729`, EP-02 ✅ APPROVED → ACCEPTED) — require **Reference Architecture v1.1** to change?

**What the refinement is (for the assessment):** the Expression↔Meaning Port Contract's single flat `declared insufficiency` (obligation 3) became **two sibling candidate-side declarations** (option C):
- `declared insufficiency` = `FILLER_UNKNOWN` (determinate insufficiency · abstention) **+** `reason` (`PARSE_UNAVAILABLE` | `READING_UNDERDETERMINED`) → routes to **UNKNOWN** (⟨C-5⟩ · obligation 3);
- `declared determination` = `NO_FILLER` (determinate negative — *"the role/slot is not expressed"*) → the **core evaluates** (obligation 2), never auto-mapped to ABSENT, never emitted by the mechanism (obligations 1 · 6).

**The question is not** "how do we improve SNF?" — SNF is closed. It is strictly: does this **boundary refinement** force an **architecture** change?

## 4 · The one observation (recorded, NOT a required change)

**OBS-T5-1 — v1.1 §10 diagram wording is under-inclusive under the refined vocabulary (non-material).** The §10 boundary diagram names the meaning candidate as *"a PROPOSAL about what an expression means, + its justification path, + **its declared insufficiency**."* Under the now-two-declaration vocabulary, this altitude-note names only the `declared insufficiency` sibling and omits `declared determination` (`NO_FILLER`).

**Why it is non-material (does not require a v1.1 change):**
1. The sentence sits in a **boundary sketch**, not in the normative ⟨A-2⟩ obligations table — and the obligations table (obligation 3: *"abstention is a first-class output; 'I did not determine this' is a valid answer"*) is **fully consistent** with the refinement: insufficiency is a *valid answer* for the mechanism, not a mandatory payload for every candidate (a `NO_FILLER` determinate candidate carries obligation 2's justification path + its declaration — the refined Q2 row makes this "present when a determinate claim is made").
2. v1.1 **explicitly delegated** the port vocabulary to the Port Contract (⟨A-2⟩ *"named here, not authored here"* · ⟨r4⟩ payload vocabulary *"defined by the Expression↔Meaning Port Contract"* · §20 DEF-2 · OQ-1). The refined contract **is** that delegated deliverable; an altitude-note that delegates cannot be contradicted by the delegatee's own vocabulary.
3. It does not alter any invariant, state, boundary, obligation, or routing — it is a **prose-level under-inclusiveness** of a sketch the architecture itself deferred.

**Disposition — recorded, NOT incorporated, NOT a v1.2 seed.** Reconciliation of this altitude-note's wording (e.g., *"+ its declared insufficiency or determination"*) belongs to a **future, separate act**: when/if the HPA ratifies the Port Contract as authoritative, the same ratification process may re-examine this altitude-note. This reassessment **must not** edit v1.1 (T-5 is read-only), and the observation does not open a v1.2 (plan §6: only a **material** impact would open a controlled v1.2).

## 5 · Verdict — **v1.1 REMAINS (NO CHANGE REQUIRED)**

**Evidence-based conclusion:** the evidence-backed Port Contract refinement does **not** require a change to Reference Architecture v1.1.

- The refinement is the **expected consumption** of v1.1's own deferrals — ⟨A-2⟩ (Port Contract named, not authored), DEF-2 (Port Contract opened as the first Logical-Architecture deliverable), OQ-1 (deferred to the Logical Architecture — now answered there), ⟨r4⟩ (candidate payload vocabulary is port-contract vocabulary, never aggregate members).
- The refinement **renders existing law** (obligations 2 & 3 → INV-KOS-VERIFICATION-001, INV-KOS-UNKNOWN-001), introducing **no new invariant, no new state, no new member, no new event, no register row** — verified independently (T-3 gates G-1…G-8, EP-02 re-derived, HPA ACCEPTED).
- **v1.1's structural inventory is untouched:** one core domain · five aggregates · twelve members · ten events · eleven invariants · eleven constitutional articles · register **25+4** · seven states. **No v1.2.**

**Conditionality (stated honestly):** this verdict is measured against the refinement **as delivered**, with the Port Contract still **PROPOSED · NON-AUTHORITATIVE**. It does **not** ratify the contract. If/when the HPA ratifies the Port Contract as authoritative (a separate act), the §10 altitude-note (OBS-T5-1) should be re-examined as part of that ratification — not now.

**Milestone (per the HPA's framing):** the reassessment confirms the refinement has not disturbed the Reference Architecture — one more step toward architecture stability, and **toward** — but not yet at — the point where a serious Kernel decision becomes well-founded. **The Kernel still waits.**
