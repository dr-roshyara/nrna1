| **the book's final acceptance act** | all of the above | the completed chain | **No** |

**Wording discipline (mandate §8), binding on every entry above.** Where something has never been
defined or ratified, the text reads **NOT ESTABLISHED** — never *"still being implemented"* and never
*"the implementation remains to be completed"*, both of which assert that a canonical design already
exists. And *"not yet ratified"* may not be written where the truth is *"never defined"*: **"not yet
ratified" ≠ "ratification pending for an already-established design."**
