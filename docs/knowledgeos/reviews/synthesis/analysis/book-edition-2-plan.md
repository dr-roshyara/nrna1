## 1 · Executive recommendation

Commission **Edition 2 as a HYBRID full-depth technical book** (form D, §3): the structure of a
technical monograph carrying research material under explicit, non-negotiable research-status
marking. Build it **on top of the frozen Edition 1**, whose 25 essays become the chapter abstracts.
Scope decision recommended: **OPTION A** — commission Parts III→II→IV now (sources already deep),
run the kernel full-read research as a parallel commissioned track, and gate Part I's expansion on

production authorization; the proven producer+independent review pattern repeats per part.
The purpose is not length: it is that a sophisticated reader can **understand, interrogate,
reproduce, and distinguish fact → definition → derivation → evidence → interpretation →
hypothesis → open question** on every page.

## 2 · Edition 1 assessment (baseline)

Edition 1 is a rigorous, provenance-complete condensed architecture monograph: 9,257 words,
25 chapters, terminology/provenance discipline independently verified (GN-39), corrected under
ruling (GN-40), accepted (GN-41). What it teaches: the shape of the architecture, its grades, its
boundaries, its history's outline, its open edge. What it merely names (DEPTH §6–7): M₄₉,
BC_Governance, the 8 K_t primitives, the 4 Zero arms, the 7 EXP-01 properties, the 6 DC
components. What it defines: nothing in block form — signatures are quoted, not exposited. What it
derives: nothing — five derivations are reproducible from sources but none is reproduced. What it
only cites: nearly all evidence detail (in evidence-maps, not prose). What is genuinely unknown:
the twelve OQs, plus the five NOT-ESTABLISHED derivations (DEPTH §7–9). **Edition 1 is not
reinterpreted as defective — it is the correct artifact of its ratified specification, and it is
the wrong artifact for the full-depth goal.**

## 3 · Recommended Edition 2 identity: HYBRID (form D) — and why

- **Not A (pure technical monograph):** "monograph" conventions imply settled theory; this
  architecture's ratified openness (OQ-1…12 as part of the architecture) would read as weakness
  in that form instead of as the designed property it is.
- **Not B (engineering reference):** the implementation layer is thin (CF-015); a reference book
  would either be mostly empty or would commit the cardinal sin of presenting intent as fact.
- **Not C (pure research monograph):** would serve researchers and lose the architects/engineers/
  maintainers the book is for (BA-0 §1 audience stands).
- **D (hybrid):** monograph structure + research-status rigor. Every section carries its status;
  unresolved research is taught AS unresolved, with the acts that would close it. This is also the
  only form the constitutional maxim (Art. I.3) and GN-14 allow without daily friction.

## 8 · Running-example proposal (one example threading Part III)

**"Certifying an election result"** — chosen because it is native to this repository's domain,
rich enough to exercise every object, and cleanly separable from product specifics.
Goal G: *"establish that election E's published result is trustworthy."* IdealState: the Knower's
sufficiency standard (every ballot counted once; tallies independently reproducible; chain of
custody unbroken). EC = η(G, IdealState): the derived requirement set (enumerated). K_t: what is
currently held across the eight primitives. Zero(K,EC): typed gaps — *unknown* (regional turnout),
*conflicting* (two tally reports), *missing* (one audit log), *invalid* (an unsigned record).
Evidence arrives: duplicates (three copies of one report must not triple confidence — I-5),
corroboration (an independent recount may — I-5), dependency (a summary derived from a tally is
not a second witness — I-6). The claim "result R is correct" walks the ladder: Candidate →
Supported (evidence) → Determination under the in-force AcceptancePolicy → Accepted — and stops.
Committed only by the electoral authority's act (A6). DC for "publish certification": Pre, Inv,
Auth (the authority), Post, Temporal (validity window), Evidence. Governance: what happens when
the AcceptancePolicy itself must change mid-election (I-11 stratification). Action: publish →
new observations → K_{t+1}. **Three-voice discipline at every step:** CONCEPTUAL MODEL (this
walk) · ARCHITECTURE (which L3 service would host each step — Verification Gate, Authority
Service, Decision Interlock) · IMPLEMENTATION ("none of this is executable today except the
authorization boundary pattern" — CF-015 honesty, restated wherever a reader could forget it).
Classification of the example itself: [INTERPRETATION]/[EDITORIAL] — an illustration of ratified
machinery, never evidence for it.
