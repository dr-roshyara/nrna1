**Prepared at the Book Architecture Authorization gate (GN-32 preparation). This packet PROPOSES;
it authorizes nothing. No book folder, chapter, or prose exists or is created by it. The ratified
Final Architecture (GN-31) is the sole architectural authority behind every proposal below.**

## 1 · Purpose and audience
Purpose (per GN-12/GN-18, the governing sentence): *the old corpus tells us how KnowledgeOS was
discovered; the canonical architecture tells us what KnowledgeOS became; the book explains both —
without pretending the destination was known at the beginning.* The book is an **explanation
layer**: it creates no architecture and carries no authority. Audience: engineers, architects, and
governance practitioners who must build on or evaluate KnowledgeOS, and future contributors who
need the discovery history to avoid re-walking dead branches.

## 2 · Proposed structure (titles PROPOSED, not final — GN-15/16 stand)
Architecture-first organization (GN-18), four parts (the HPA's illustrative Part I–IV sketch,
GN-20 note, now grounded in the ratified material):

```
Part I   DISCOVERY        the journey: eras (AF-001), crises, lenses, alternatives, experiments
Part II  RECONSTRUCTION   the method: archaeology→evidence→falsification→ruling→ratification
Part III ARCHITECTURE     the ratified Final Architecture: five layers, v0.2 core, D-FA-1…7
Part IV  IMPLICATIONS     engineering, AI agents, governance, what follows — and what stays open
```
Each chapter = one folder with `chapter.md` + `evidence-map.md` + `claims.md` + `unresolved.md`
(GN-15/16) — **created only after book-production authorization, not now.**

## 4 · Which archaeology becomes narrative
Part I/II consume, as [H] material with provenance: AF-001…011 · ALT-01…09 · HD-1…7 ·
concept-evolution (10+4 terms) · experiments (EXP-01/02 + the micro-falsifications) · the regime
timeline R1–R5 · AF-009 as the framing fact (discovery preceded governance — history, never
authority). PROCESS/PROMPT artifacts and duplicates appear only in the coverage appendix, never as
sources (GN-25/GN-01).

## 5 · Alternatives and failed experiments — presentation rule
Presented as **history with dignity and classification**: what was proposed, why, what evidence
challenged it, what happened (ALT format) — never as defects, never as still-live options, never
erased. The corpus's self-corrections (IdealState flaw-in-an-hour; measure-theory crisis; Step 121)
are narrative centerpieces of Part I, because they SHOW the method the book claims.

## 6 · Unresolved questions — representation rule
OQ-1…12 appear (a) in the dedicated Part IV chapter, each with its resolution prerequisite from
FA-6, and (b) in every chapter's `unresolved.md` where relevant. Presented as **ratified openness**
(GN-31: part of the architecture, not incompleteness). The book may NOT resolve, soften, or
editorialize any OQ toward a preferred answer.

## 7 · Terminology rules (binding, from FA-4)
Unqualified "Zero" = `Zero(K,EC)` only; the meta-principle is always "Z-KOS-001"; absence-lens Zero
appears only in Part I with dates. `K_t` unqualified = the formal state; ALT-09 and the engineering
usage always qualified. "Lord" appears only as history with the collision documented. "Committed"
only in the A6 decision-boundary sense. Sanskrit terms carry their lens origin on first use. A
terminology conformance table ships with every chapter's `claims.md`.

## 8 · Provenance / evidence rules
Every substantive claim carries: source (corpus file / registry / GN act) · grade
([E]/[IN]/[RC]/[H]/[R]/[U], surfaced in the text or evidence-map, never hidden) · date where the
claim is chronological. GN-14 is the master rule: **no sentence may silently upgrade the epistemic
status of the underlying evidence.** GN-01: synthesis echoes are zero evidence. Copies never count
as corroboration. Statistics carry their as-of dates (GN-03).

## 9 · Authority vs evidence separation (structural)
Only Part III speaks with architectural authority, and only by citing the ratified FA. Parts I/II/IV
speak historically or prospectively and must say so. A sentence pattern check enforces it: "is/must"
claims about the architecture cite FA; "was/became/tried" claims cite corpus provenance.

## 10 · The named tensions — how each is treated
Three Zero senses → told as evolution (AF-004 chronology), never merged. Three kernel traditions →
layered per D-FA-4; the book never crowns one. Lord collision → documented as unresolved naming
history (OQ-7). Ātma-Kernel → presented exactly as D-FA-5: a one-morning hypothesis cluster,
unresolved lens, no architectural role. Rich state vocabulary → the INHERITANCE story (older
tradition absorbed as adjacent layer, D-FA-1/AF-006).

## 11 · What the book MUST NOT claim
Never "validated" (GN-10 vocabulary stands) · never "designed from the beginning"/"always was"
(hindsight ban) · never that an OQ is resolved · never RA v1.1 content as architecture · never a
Zero/K_t sense merge · never that formal objects have executable implementations (3C CF-015 says
otherwise) · never that the corpus authorizes anything (AF-009) · never a statement stronger than
its evidence (the final rule).
