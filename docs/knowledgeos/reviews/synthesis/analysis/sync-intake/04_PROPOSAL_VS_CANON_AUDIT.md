another artifact `[AUTHORITATIVE REVISION & CONSOLIDATION]` — **with no ledger basis whatever.** A

Supervisory]` inside an ASCII box whose adjacent lines read `OVERALL: ✅ READY FOR RATIFICATION` and

companion concurs against its own closure graph: *"**δ** — Still open"*, *"**Equality** — Still
open"*. **A thing awaiting ratification is not ratified.**

## §8 — `kernel-reduction/`: specification exists; it is not law

**04-operator-contracts · 05-operator-capability-matrix · 06-composition-rules · 07-ablation-design
carry NO status line of their own.** Their status is inherited from the tree banner, which is
unambiguous on three counts: `[EXP] research experiment — **NOT canonical, NOT architecture, NOT
governance**`; an authorization boundary — *"This lane was **not authorized to canonize a
KnowledgeOS kernel** and does not"*; and an explicit conversion prohibition —

> *"No `[PROP]`, `[INF]` or `[EXP]` item in this directory may be converted into an architecture
> decision, governance rule, invariant, theorem or canonical KnowledgeOS law **without a separate,
> authorized act**."*

Every operator status is drawn from a deliberately provisional vocabulary
(`IRREDUCIBLE-CANDIDATE`, `UNRESOLVED`, `UNSUPPORTED`, `COUPLED`, `DERIVABLE-CANDIDATE`), and the
tree's own candidate kernel is qualified as *"**a research recommendation, not a canonization**"*.
The headline is additionally **self-superseded**: §19 replaces the `FINAL` report's framing, so that
framing classifies **F** while §19 stands. **A formal specification exists here. It is not the formal
specification of anything canonical.**
