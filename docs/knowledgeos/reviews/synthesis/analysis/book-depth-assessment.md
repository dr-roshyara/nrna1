## 1 · Executive Summary

The accepted GN-41 artifact is, on the evidence of a full structural audit, a **condensed
architecture monograph**: 25 provenance-disciplined essays of 285–454 words (9,257 words total),
structurally exact against the ratified BA-1…BA-6, twice reviewed, corrected under ruling, and
accepted. A full-depth technical book on the same ratified architecture would run an estimated
**90,000–140,000 words**, add ~25–35 diagrams and a complete editorial apparatus, and would expand
along five missing dimensions: formal definition blocks, reproduced derivations, worked examples,
architectural exposition (component/state/flow), and connected historical narrative. **~80% of the
expansion is [EDITORIAL]/[DERIVED] work over already-ratified or already-evidenced material and
requires no architecture change.** The remainder is bounded by: the kernel-era read debt (new
evidence needed for Part I depth), and seven chapters whose full formalization is capped by open
questions (they must teach *around* OQ-1/2/3/4/9, visibly). A small set of external mathematical
interpretations (DeepSeek) is flagged as [EXTERNAL RESEARCH CANDIDATE] at six locations — none may
enter without its own validation-and-intake gate. Expansion cannot proceed under the current
BA-1…BA-6 (which specify no depth); it requires a BA amendment plus a new production authorization.

## 2 · Current Book Assessment

**A. Current identity (supported by the audit):** a condensed architecture monograph / executive
layer — each chapter an abstract-grade treatment with full provenance. **What it does well:**
grade honesty, terminology discipline, OQ visibility, citation fidelity (independently verified).
**What it structurally lacks:** depth in every conventional book dimension; no reader apparatus;
no cross-reference fabric; audit artifacts carry material readers need but will not find.
