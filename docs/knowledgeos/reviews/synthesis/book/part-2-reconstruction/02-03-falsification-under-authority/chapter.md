# II.3 · Falsification Under Authority

[M] When the reconstruction had produced a canonical model (v0.1), the programme did not proceed
to celebrate it. It commissioned an attack: can the synthesis be proven wrong from its own
materials? The falsification pass returned four findings — a self-referential admission policy
(the model's own Step-121 disease, F-1), an unestablished residual of the goal inside the gap
function (F-2), a decision status quietly living inside an epistemic ladder (F-3), and a no-skip
rule practiced but nowhere stated (F-4).

[M] What happened next is the programme's governance in its purest form — including its one
recorded breach. The findings came with *proposed* repairs, and the discipline held that **finding
≠ proposed repair ≠ authorized repair**: nothing may be applied without a ruling. At exactly this
gate, the programme's synthesis engineer misread a hedged recommendation ("I would now make the
decision, e.g.: …") as the ruling itself, and applied the repairs prematurely. The error was
caught and corrected the same day — the ledger carries both dated entries — and the correction is
a permanent part of the record:
the premature entry re-graded PROVISIONAL, the applied model re-bannered PREPARED-UNRATIFIED, the
original text retained unmodified beneath the correction — errors are recorded, not hidden, in the
governance ledger exactly as in the corpus.

[M] The actual ruling (GN-19) then arrived in explicit form: ACCEPT all four — F-2 with a
specific, evidence-conservative resolution (simplify the gap function's signature; hold the
totality assumption open and revisitable rather than inventing a residual or declaring a theorem).
The repairs were applied *exactly as ruled*, with a change ledger in which every alteration
carries its finding, its ruling, and its grade — and nothing else changed. v0.1 stands unedited as
the pre-falsification record; v0.2 became the authorized model *only* at the moment of the ruling.

[M] The reader should take three things from this chapter. The synthesis was attacked before it
was trusted. The attack's repairs entered only under explicit authority, through a recorded error
and its correction — the discipline proved itself precisely by being violated once and holding.
And the sharpest finding (F-1) was the fourth appearance of a defect the corpus itself had
discovered first — the programme's methods kept converging on the same truth from independent
directions, which is the only kind of confidence this book offers anywhere.
