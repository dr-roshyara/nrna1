# IV.4 · What Remains Open

[FA/R] Twelve questions are open in the ratified architecture — open **by ruling**, not by
neglect. The ratifying act said it in so many words: their unresolved status *is part of the
ratified architecture* and must not be treated as accidental incompleteness or silently resolved
downstream. This chapter is their registry in prose; none is resolved here.

**OQ-1 · η-totality.** Does the goal retain any residual role inside the gap function, or do the
contract-derivation η capture everything? No evidence either way; the ruled signature is the
conservative one; a formal synthesis of η, or evidence of a residual consumer, would close it.

**OQ-2 · Kernel membership.** Which formal objects are irreducible? The traditions are layered
(III.9); the membership act — an irreducibility analysis or a formal-side selection ruling — has
never happened.

**OQ-3 · The aggregation operator.** The one genuine experiment ended in a principled negative;
two candidates survived; none was selected. A successor experiment designed dependency-first would
close it.

**OQ-4 · Action semantics.** The boundary between knowing and doing is firm; what an authorized
action formally *is* — its effects, its failures, its attribution — is specified only in parts.

**OQ-5 · The unexecuted suites.** Formal test suites exist as specifications (EG-05). Running them
is the whole remaining act.

**OQ-6 · The Ω-b record gap.** Unboundedness was dropped by disuse, never by act. A one-line
historical annotation would close the record; the annotation is HELD.

**OQ-7 · The Lord name.** Two historical referents, never reconciled; a terminology ruling —
book-relevant, architecture-open.

**OQ-8 · Ātma-Kernel.** A complete hypothesis arc without a disposition act; held as an unresolved
lens by explicit decision (D-FA-5); an adoption or rejection act would close it either way.

**OQ-9 · The v1.1 lane.** Intake deferred pending a dedicated conformance pass (IV.3).

**OQ-10 · The constitution banner.** The ratification act is real; the document's banner is stale;
the one-line synchronization is HELD.

**OQ-11 · EKS.** The oldest question in the record: what is KnowledgeOS *to* the engineering
knowledge system it grew beside? A scoping act would close it.

**OQ-12 · The missing ordering sentence.** Dependency-first (I-6) is TESTED in the model and
stated nowhere in the repository. One sentence, in the right governed place, would close it.

[FA] Read as a set, the twelve are not debris — they are the architecture's honest boundary: two
mathematical (1, 3), two formal-semantic (2, 4), two executional (5, 12), two historical-record
(6, 10), two naming (7, 8), two relational (9, 11). The programme's last lesson is that an
architecture which can *enumerate* what it does not know, with the acts that would change each
item, is stronger — not weaker — than one that cannot.
