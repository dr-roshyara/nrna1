# IV.5 · How to Read This Book's Evidence

[M] This closing chapter is the book's own provenance contract — what its claims rest on, what was
excluded, and where its coverage is thinner than its prose.

**The claim types.** Every substantive sentence in this book is one of four: [FA] — the ratified
architecture speaking, always traceable to the authorized model or a ratified determination; [H] —
history, traceable to a dated corpus document or an archaeology finding; [M] — the method and
governance record; [P] — prospect, which binds no one. Interpretations are additionally marked
[IN] where they occur, and open items [U]. Where a claim's underlying grade is weaker than its
sentence could suggest (COMPOSITION, SPECIFIED, absence-derived), the grade is stated inline. The
master rule is GN-14: no sentence may silently upgrade its evidence.

**What was excluded, deliberately.** Saved prompts and prompt-delivery files inside the historical
tree (eight of them, one a verbatim echo of the programme's own output); md5-duplicate documents
(copies are never corroboration); derivative session indexes (maps, not sources); the parallel
v1.1 lane's content; three binaries and one third-party PDF. Their exclusion is itself recorded
governance (the PROCESS/PROMPT rule), not editorial taste.

**Where coverage is thin, stated plainly.** The kernel-era corpus (170 documents) was processed at
extraction depth — roughly one document in twenty read in full; a 39-document full-read worklist
stands at one completed. Every kernel-era claim in this book carries that caveat by reference. The
root era is thinner still. The formal era (413 documents) is the deep layer — multi-pass read, and
the source of most of Part III's lineage. Counts are as of 2026-08-28; the corpus was growing
while it was studied, and the dates matter.

**What this book is not.** It is not the architecture (Part III *cites* the authority; the
authority lives in the governed model and its ledger). It is not a validation — that word appears
in this book only inside quotation of a state-name and inside this sentence. And it is not
finished truth: twelve open questions are part of what was ratified, and the honest expectation is
that closing them will change some sentences here. The book's own status, like everything else in
the programme, is exactly what the record says: produced under authorization, pending review —
*authorized, evidence-supported, and open where it is open.*
