# III.1 · The Five Layers

[FA] KnowledgeOS, as ratified, is not one artifact but a **discipline of five layers**, each with
its own kind of authority, plus a body of material that sits deliberately outside all of them
(FA-1 §1, ratified GN-31).

**L1 — the constitutional layer.** Eleven articles — Identity, Dimension, Authority, Decision,
Projection, Verification, Failure-State, Contradiction, Unknown, Agency, History — each a negation
that forbids a specific epistemic collapse. [FA] The articles are boundary laws: they state what
must never happen, not how anything works. Their ratification is carried by the retrospective
ratification act of 2026-08-22 (GN-27 resolution of finding CF-003).

**L2 — the formal model layer.** The canonical synthesis model v0.2: the Knower and the goal G,
the IdealState, the EpistemicContract `EC = η(G, IdealState)`, the knowledge state `K_t`, the
goal-gap `Zero(K, EC)`, the three-status admission ladder with its decision boundary, the Decision
Contract, Governance, and the action loop. [FA] L2 is a *model of epistemic process* — objects and
transitions — where L1 is a set of prohibitions. The two kinds do not compete.

**L3 — the repository architecture.** The Reference Architecture v1.0: eleven kernel services
(gates and records, never reasoners), an engine layer that proposes but never admits, and
representation models. [FA] L3 realizes L1 by construction: each article maps to one boundary,
one kernel service, one structurally impossible forbidden flow.

**L4 — the executable implementation.** [FA, stated at its true strength] Thin. The conformance
pass (3C, finding CF-015) established that no L2 formal object has an executable counterpart; what
runs today is governance tooling — a read-only session bootstrap that fails closed, an
operating-model presenter, observation diagnostics. The book states this plainly because the
architecture's own final rule demands it: the strongest statement made never exceeds the evidence.

**L5 — the governance record.** The GN ledger and the review-side acts. [FA] This is where
authority actually lives: every promotion in this book's history — v0.2, the Final Architecture,
this book's own design — became authoritative through a recorded human ruling, never through
content alone. That is L1's Article 3 operating on the architecture itself.

**Outside all layers: the historical corpus.** Six hundred eighty-eight documents of exploration.
[H] They are evidence of how the architecture was found (Part I tells that story). [FA] They carry
no authority — a fact the archaeology itself established (finding AF-009: the corpus contains not
one externally ratified decision) and governance then preserved as a standing rule.

The inter-layer relations are themselves graded claims. [FA/E] That L2 does not contradict L1 was
tested: the conformance pass found no CONTRADICTS relation anywhere. [U] That every L2 object
correctly realizes L1 at object level is NOT ESTABLISHED — an honest gap, held open on purpose.
