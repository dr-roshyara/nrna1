# III.9 · Three Kernels

The word "kernel" is never used bare in this book, because the programme's history produced three
different things the word honestly denotes — and the ratified architecture **layers them instead
of crowning one** (D-FA-4).

**The constitutional kernel.** [FA←E] The eleven articles, selected from twenty-nine registered
rows by an explicit human ruling and formalized as the constitution — *fewer laws, not fewer
truths*. Its identity is prohibitive: each article forbids a named collapse, and each was
failure-tested (remove it, and a named corruption opens). This kernel governs; it does not
compute.

**The formal kernel candidate.** [FA] The v0.2 object model — the tradition the corpus called M₄₉:
Knower, contract, state, gap, ladder, decision contract, governance, loop. It computes — it is a
model of epistemic *process* — and it is a **candidate** at its layer: the conformance pass found
no contradiction between it and the articles, but object-level realization was never established,
and the ratified architecture says so rather than assuming it.

**The historical formulations.** [H] Eight competing kernel formulations from the discovery
corpus's kernel era, none ever selected within that corpus. They are evidence of the search — the
shape of the space the two surviving kernels were carved from — and Part I.3 gives them their
history. (Coverage honesty from the archaeology applies: the kernel-era corpus was processed at
extraction depth, so the eight are inventoried with a recorded caveat, not exhaustively exposited.)

**What remains open.** [U — OQ-2] *Kernel membership at the object level*: which formal objects
are irreducible — the kernel of the kernel — was never decided by any act. The layering resolves
how the three traditions relate; it deliberately does not answer which L2 objects would survive a
step-⑤-style selection on the formal side. The architecture holds the question open and names the
two acts that could close it.
