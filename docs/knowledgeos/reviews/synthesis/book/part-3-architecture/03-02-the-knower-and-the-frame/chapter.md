# III.2 · The Knower and the Frame

[FA] Everything in the formal model begins with an owner. The **Knower** owns the problem, the
purpose, the IdealState, and final authority — invariant I-1, the only invariant that survived
three entire methodological regimes of the discovery corpus unchanged. Whatever else was torn down
and rebuilt (and Part I shows nearly everything was), ownership of the frame never moved.

**The goal G.** [FA] G is Knower-owned intent. It does not act; it *induces requirements*. In the
ratified model G has exactly one direct consumer — the Proposal selector — a deliberately narrow
wiring established by evidence (source 025g) when the falsification pass asked whether G leaks
into other objects and found no support for it.

**The IdealState.** [FA] The epistemic understanding the Knower would consider sufficient. Only
the Knower may change it. It is not a picture of the world; it is a *standard of sufficiency* —
which is why the model needs no access to any ideal world, only to the Knower's declared standard.
[H] Its history is instructive and typical: introduced in the corpus on 2026-08-26 at 00:08, it
was broken by a worked example at 00:17 and repaired the same night — the discovery process
attacking its own newest concept within the hour (Part I.6 tells this in full).

**The EpistemicContract.** [FA] `EC = η(G, IdealState)`: the total derivation of *what must be
known or satisfied* from the goal and the sufficiency standard. EC is the carrier of requirement
content in the ratified model — the answer to a question the discovery corpus struggled with for
days: where do an inquiry's obligations live? Not in the goal (a goal is not a checklist), not in
the world (the world does not know what you need), but in a derived contract the Knower's frame
fully determines.

**η-totality is an open assumption.** [U — OQ-1, held open by ruling] The model assumes η is
total: that the goal and the standard fully determine the contract, with no residual role for G
inside the gap function. No evidence establishes a residual; none proves totality. The ratifying
ruling made this openness explicit and revisitable rather than hiding it in a definition — the
reader should treat `EC = η(G, IdealState)` as the evidence-conservative formulation, not as a
theorem.

**Convergence with the repository side.** [E] The Knower is the one concept found on both sides of
the programme under the same lineage: the formal model's Knower (I-1) and the repository
constitution's protected first-class epistemic agent (H-KOS-Agent-001, "the Knower of the Field").
[FA, stated precisely] The convergence is PARTIAL: both make the Knower constitutive and
inseparable; ownership of goal and IdealState is established only on the formal side.
