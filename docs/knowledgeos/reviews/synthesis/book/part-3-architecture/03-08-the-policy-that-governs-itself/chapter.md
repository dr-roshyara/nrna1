# III.8 · The Policy That Governs Itself

[FA] The deepest defect the programme ever found — four separate times — was the same one: a
system whose admission machinery is governed by a policy that nothing governs. The ratified
answer is **stratification**:

```
policy-as-content          a policy is knowledge content AT REST inside K_t —
                           admissible, discussable, revisable like any claim
policy-in-force            the approved, versioned policy that actually governs admission
change between them        a DECISION under DC, approved through governance (BC_Governance),
                           producing version N → N+1
```

[FA] Invariant **I-11**: no in-force policy — the AcceptancePolicy and the constitution included —
changes without a governed, versioned approval decision. Self-modification of the in-force policy
is excluded *by construction*, not by exhortation. And I-11 closes the hole the falsification pass
found in I-1: the Knower's ownership now reaches Determination through the governed policy chain,
instead of stopping short at an unowned policy object.

**The four sightings.** [E/H] The book reports the pattern because it is the programme's best
argument for the repair: (1) the corpus's own Step 121 flagged a "critical governance gap" — the
admission constitution unversioned and unowned; (2) the corpus's freeze decisions modified the
model's own rules mid-flight with no governing act; (3) the falsification pass caught the
synthesized model repeating the pattern (finding F-1); (4) the conformance pass found the
implementation side's Constitution asserting ratification downstream of a stale PROPOSED banner —
the mildest form, a tracking gap, later resolved when governance located the actual retrospective
ratification act. Four independent appearances of one failure mode, on both sides of the
programme. [FA] The stratification is REQUIRED-BY-COHERENCE with corpus-adjacent support — the
grade is shown because the reader should know this invariant was *argued into* the model under
ruling, not read out of a single source.

[E] The constitutional layer's Chapter V is the same idea in constitutional form: engineering
evolution free below the articles; amendment of the articles only by ratified act; no weakening.
Two traditions, one stratification — arrived at, once again, independently.
