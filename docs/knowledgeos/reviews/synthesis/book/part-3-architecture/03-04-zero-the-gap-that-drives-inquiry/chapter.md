# III.4 · Zero — the Gap That Drives Inquiry

[FA] **`Zero(K, EC)`** is the model's engine of motion: the typed discrepancy between what the
knowledge state holds and what the EpistemicContract requires. It answers *why the system does
anything at all*. Zero's output is not a number but a typed structure — unknown, conflicting,
missing, invalid — because the model's constitution-side sibling forbids exactly the collapse a
scalar would commit (no single score may replace epistemic structure), and because invariant I-9
requires the four-way non-satisfaction typology to survive un-Booleanized.

**The signature is the ruled one.** [FA/R] The historical formulation was `Zero(K, G, EC)`. The
falsification pass could not establish any residual role for G inside the gap once EC carries the
requirement content, and the ratifying ruling adopted the simplification `Zero(K, EC)` explicitly
*as the evidence-conservative resolution*, retaining the old signature as lineage and holding the
question open (OQ-1) rather than declaring it settled. The reader should hear that precision: the
architecture does not claim G is absent from the gap; it claims no evidence establishes its
presence, and it reserves the right to be corrected.

**One name, three meanings — kept apart on purpose.** [H/R] "Zero" is the most collision-prone
word in the whole programme, and the ratified terminology register (D-FA-3) is strict about it:

- **`Zero(K, EC)`** — this chapter's object: the formal goal-gap function (the *latest* of the
  three senses to arrive, 2026-08-27).
- **Z-KOS-001** — the repository side's ratified *meta-principle*: the neutral reference, the
  refusal to reward premature assertion; explicitly "not a kernel object, not an epistemic status,
  not implementation." It explains why boundaries exist; it is not a component.
- **The absence-lens Zero** — the earliest sense (2026-08-22), from the Vedic-zero reading of
  absence as information; historical only.

[E] The archaeology fixed the chronology precisely because hindsight would invert it: the
meta-principle and the absence-lens are the *older* siblings; the computable gap function is the
youngest. No merge of the three is authorized, and this book performs none. What can honestly be
said [IN, marked as interpretation]: all three refuse the same temptation — treating not-knowing
as either nothing or as falsehood — and that shared refusal, not a shared definition, is why one
word kept being chosen.
