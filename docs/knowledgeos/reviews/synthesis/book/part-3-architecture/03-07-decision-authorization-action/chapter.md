# III.7 · Decision, Authorization, Action

[FA] The model's second boundary system separates knowing from doing, in three non-collapsing
steps (invariant I-3): **SufficientKnowledge ≠ ValidDecision ≠ AuthorizedAction.** Knowing enough
is not deciding; deciding validly is not being allowed to act.

**The Proposal selector.** [FA] From `(K_t, Z_t, G)` the selector proposes the next epistemic
action — and holds **no authority** (I-2). This is where G's single consumer sits: the goal shapes
what is proposed, never what is admitted and never what is executed. [H] The corpus's Q17 ruling
put it memorably during the era when the selector still wore a borrowed divine name: the role is
analytical, not transformational. The naming history is Part I.5's story; the architecture keeps
only the deflated, authority-free role.

**The Decision Contract.** [FA] Every decision is a six-part contract:
`DC(d) = (Pre, Inv, Auth, Post, Temporal, Evidence)` — preconditions, invariants preserved, the
authorization requirement, postconditions, temporal validity, and the evidence the decision rests
on. Authorization is a *precondition constraint filled via governance* — never a processing step a
pipeline can perform on its own (a COMPOSITION-grade element, and the model says so).

**Action and the loop.** [FA] Authorized action produces new observation; the loop closes back
into `K_t`. [FA, at true strength] The *boundary* is firmly established — knowledge informs and
never executes, and the constitutional side hardens the same line ("the Decision Interlock
terminates knowledge flows at recommendation"). The *semantics* of execution beyond that boundary
are *specified only* in parts and remain an open question (OQ-4): what exactly an action is, how
its effects are attributed back, what failure of an authorized action does to the record — the
architecture currently answers with the boundary, not with a theory of acting.

[E] The lived system already runs the triple: the session bootstrap refuses to conflate six
different things a process might mean by "I may act" — identity, role, eligibility, authorization,
ownership, continuation — and fails closed to *not authorized* whenever the record cannot resolve
them. That is I-3 as running code, and it is the strongest implementation-side evidence the
programme found for any formal element.
