# III.6 · States and Admission

[FA] How does anything become knowledge here? Through a **three-status admission ladder** governed
by an explicit policy, with one boundary that no amount of evidence can cross on its own.

**The ladder.** `Candidate → Supported → Accepted`. [FA] Transitions form a covering relation —
each status reachable only from its immediate predecessor (I-12); skipping is formally excluded.
The `Supported → Accepted` transition has a name, **Determination**, and a governor, the
**AcceptancePolicy** — which decides what *may be admitted*, a different question from what the
evidence supports.

**The boundary.** [FA] `Committed` is **not** a fourth epistemic status. It is a decision-boundary
status attached to an Accepted item by authority, under the model's sharpest rule, A6: **authority
determines commitment, not evidential truth.** Evidence can make a claim Accepted; only an
authorized act can make anyone bound to it. The falsification pass caught the earlier formulation
quietly treating Committed as a rung of the same ladder; the ratified repair re-typed it, and the
lived governance record turns out to enact exactly this discipline — adoption and authorization as
separate human acts, an implemented-and-green asset held back solely because independent
verification had not yet occurred.

**The policy governs itself — governedly.** [FA] The AcceptancePolicy is itself a governed,
versioned object: policy-as-content (admissible like any claim, at rest inside `K_t`) is
distinguished from policy-in-force (the approved version that actually governs admission), and no
in-force policy changes without a governed decision (I-11). Chapter III.8 gives this its own
treatment; it is mentioned here because admission is where its absence would corrupt first.

**The adjacent states — an inheritance.** [FA, per D-FA-1] The ladder answers *how far admitted*.
It never answered *in what condition held*. The ratified Final Architecture layers the
constitutional state vocabulary beside it: **REJECTED** — failed reasoning preserved as an
explicit state, never silently discarded; **CONFLICTED** — contradictory knowledge coexisting
until governed resolution, the conflict record surviving the resolution; and the evidence-layer
triple **UNKNOWN ≠ ABSENT ≠ FALSE**. [H] These are not extensions bolted on: they are the *older*
tradition (the archaeology dated them to the programme's first weeks, before the ladder existed),
inherited from the constitutional side rather than invented to fill the gap. The admission axis
stayed untouched; the model simply stopped pretending rejection, conflict, and ignorance were out
of scope.
