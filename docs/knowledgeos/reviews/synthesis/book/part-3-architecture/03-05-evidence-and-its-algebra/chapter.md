# III.5 · Evidence and Its Algebra

[FA] Evidence in the ratified model is structured, not scalar: it carries **dependency structure
and roles**, and two invariants — the only two that are computationally TESTED rather than read —
govern how it may combine:

- **I-5.** Duplicate evidence must not increase confidence; independent corroboration must.
- **I-6.** Dependency resolution precedes aggregation.

[E] Both come from the corpus's one genuine experiment, EXP-01: four candidate aggregation
operators (MAX, weighted mean, saturating, Bayesian-like) run against seven adversarial properties
on a shared normalized evidence universe. The recorded verdict was negative and precise: **no
simple scalar operator is sufficient as the epistemic foundation**, and the strongest single
lesson was I-6 — three apparent pieces of evidence may be one observation and two derived
representations, so dependencies must be resolved before anything is combined.

**What the experiment did not do.** [E, at true strength] Two candidates survived all seven listed
properties. Survival is not selection: the model deliberately **selects no aggregation operator**
(OQ-3, open by ruling). Any implementation claiming "the KnowledgeOS evidence formula" is claiming
something the architecture refuses to claim.

**An adjudicated discrepancy, disclosed.** [E/M] The repository contains a CSV export of the
EXP-01 property matrix that disagrees with the experiment's own prose record in three cells. The
governance track adjudicated it (GN-27): the prose record prevails — it is internally consistent
under its own normalization footnote, while the CSV is inconsistent under any single reading and
carries no generator or provenance. The CSV is graded unreliable-standalone; the negative verdict
is unaffected under either source. The discrepancy is reported here because the book's provenance
rules require the reader to be able to find every seam the programme found.

**Convergence across traditions.** [E] The constitutional layer arrived at the same prohibition
from a different direction: a "knowledge quality" scalar is a forbidden surrogate (Article 2's
failure signature is literally `Knowledge Quality = 0.87`). One prohibition, two independent
derivations — the kind of convergence this architecture treats as evidence, while still refusing
to call it proof.
