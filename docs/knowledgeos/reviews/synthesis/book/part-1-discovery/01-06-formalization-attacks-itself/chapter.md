# I.6 · Formalization Attacks Itself

[H] The final regime of the discovery corpus (2026-08-27 to 08-28) is the Step series: one hundred
fifty-eight numbered steps that built the formal model Part III describes — and, in the same
breath, the corpus's habit of attacking its own newest work reached its mature form.

**The hour-long life of IdealState v1.** Introduced 00:08, broken by its own author's worked
example at 00:17, repaired the same night. [H] The episode is the programme's method in miniature:
propose precisely enough to be breakable, break it yourself, keep the repair and the wreckage
both.

**The experiment.** [H/E] EXP-01 (Part III.5) belongs to this era: four aggregation operators, seven
adversarial properties, a negative verdict quoted at its exact strength — *no simple scalar
operator is sufficient* — and the dependency-first lesson that became I-6. The corpus's largest
self-audit (Step 050: fifty attacks on the kernel's consistency) is equally careful to call itself
*not a proof*. The era's evidential temper — test hard, claim little — is the reason the final
architecture can show TESTED grades at all.

**The gap that kept returning.** [H] Step 121 found it first on the formal side: the machinery of
admission was governed by a policy that nothing versioned and no one owned — "critical governance
gap," the record says. The era's own freeze decisions — stopping parts of the model mid-flight
with no governing act — were the same disease in behavioral form. The falsification pass would
later catch the synthesized model repeating it, and the conformance pass would find its mildest
cousin on the repository side. Four sightings; III.8 tells what the ratified architecture did
about it.

**What the era did not do.** [H] It never selected an aggregation operator; never resolved the
Lord name; never adopted or rejected Ātma-Kernel; never wrote down the no-skip rule it mostly
practiced; and — the archaeology's sharpest structural finding — it produced its central concepts
*late and narrow*: the EpistemicContract enters at Steps 023/024; the AcceptancePolicy appears in
exactly four documents. The concepts Part III leans on hardest are among the corpus's youngest.
Their weight is formal depth and the testing they attracted, not breadth of occurrence — a fact
the reader deserves to weigh, and the final rule requires us to hand over.
