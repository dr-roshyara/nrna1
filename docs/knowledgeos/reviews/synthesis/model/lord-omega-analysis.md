## 4 · Can the two concepts coexist? Does DDD require separation?

⟦INT⟧ on the evidence: **yes, they can coexist, and they never actually competed** — R5-Lord consumes
`EC`, which is Ω-a's descendant. The collision is purely **nominal**: the *name* Lord moved from Ω-c
(observer) to the selector while Ω-a travelled under different names (IdealState → EC).
⟦INT⟧ DDD reading: two concepts, two responsibilities, one label — a **ubiquitous-language collision
across the R4→R5 boundary**, resolvable by naming, *not* by redesign, because the responsibilities are
already separated in the formal model. ⚠ **No rename is proposed here** (reserved to HPA); the gate's
candidate decomposition (Observation → Proposal → Decision → Action) is *consistent* with this
evidence but not forced by it.
