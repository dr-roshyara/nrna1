# Regime Transition Analysis (R1→R2→R3→R4→R5)

**Commissioned at the review gate (2026-08-28).** Nine questions per transition; R3→R4 and R4→R5
carry the emphasis. Grounded in the Phase-1/2A reads; ⟦TITLE⟧ marks title-level grounding;
`NOT ESTABLISHED` is used rather than invention.

**Governing model (fixed at the gate):** three separate trajectories —

| Regime | OBJECT (what is it?) | METHOD (how do we know?) | EVIDENCE (how strongly?) |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | KnowledgeOS itself | argument | low |
| R2 | knowledge state | questions / lenses | low |
| R3 | Knower / Kernel | analogy (guarded) | guarded |
| R4 | epistemic primitives | computational testing | stronger |
| R5 | whole system + repository | construction + audit | strongest **declared** |

**Central hypothesis carried forward:** ⟦C-gate⟧ *"KnowledgeOS was not discovered by progressively
adding features to a fixed architecture. It emerged through successive changes in the way the problem
itself was represented, formalized, tested, and confronted with evidence."*
And the rule: `R(i+1) changes the question and/or evidence available to R(i)` — never "R(i) was
wrong, R(i+1) was better."

---

## T1 · R1 → R2 (2026-08-26, night → morning)

1. **Object change:** *KnowledgeOS as a whole* → *the knowledge state and its dimensions*. The
   unanswerable definitional question is replaced by a decomposable one.
2. **Vocabulary change:** measure/tuple/determination/conditional-evidence → dimension, question,
   atom, projection, Ω, Zero, Lord.
3. **Method change:** prose argument + source extraction → **enumeration** (24 named questions) +
   lens construction.
4. **Assumptions surviving:** Ω as unbounded knowledge space (C-009 explicitly carries over);
   knowledge as temporally situated (C-005); the refusal to freeze contracts (MD-03).
5. **Assumptions disappearing:** measure theory as candidate foundation (rejected in R1's own
   inherited verdict); the search for a single defining tuple.
6. **New evidence available:** none in kind — R2's standard is still assertion. What is new is
   *coverage bookkeeping* (numbered questions make gaps visible: Q1/Q3/Q21 never existed).
7. **New epistemic risk:** enumeration creates an illusion of completeness — realized in
   `the-complete-mathematical-model` (C-011, later superseded).
8. **Actually validated:** nothing — no test artifact exists in R2.
9. **Unresolved carried forward:** *what is Knowledge itself?* (Q7 addressed it, did not settle it);
   how conditional evidence combines (OQ-02); Determination's formal home (OQ-03).

## T2 · R2 → R3 (2026-08-26, evening)

1. **Object change:** *the knowledge state* → **the Knower** in relation to the knowledge system —
   the first appearance of agency as the object.
2. **Vocabulary change:** dimension/question/atom → chapter, Ātman, Knower, Sārathi, epistemic OS.
3. **Method change:** enumeration → **guarded analogy** (scripture read chapter-by-chapter with
   explicit correction documents).
4. **Surviving:** Ω (as the Lord-lens object, with the R3 guard *Krishna/Ω is a lens-derived
   abstraction*); the Zero lens; the Knower-owns-the-frame principle is born here and survives to
   the end (C-014 → 025h).
5. **Disappearing:** the Question programme — **abandoned mid-R3 without a closure document**
   (MD-04 partially superseded); the Knowledge Atom is never mentioned again after R2.
6. **New evidence:** none in mechanism; but R3 introduces **discipline documents** (corrections:
   *chapter 3 is not primarily epistemic*, *don't map chakra→layer*, *Sārathi does not own the
   frame*) — the first systematic self-correction practice in the corpus.
7. **New epistemic risk:** ⟦C-gate⟧ *scriptural concept ≠ architectural component* — the mapping is
   interpretive unless independently justified. Partially realized: the Ātma-as-Kernel escalation
   (4 documents, 14 minutes) reached *"can be the Kernel"* without any test (C-012, still OPEN).
8. **Actually validated:** nothing formally; the guards are quality controls, not validations.
9. **Unresolved:** whether Ātma-as-Kernel means anything testable; the fate of the 24 questions.

## T3 · R3 → R4 (2026-08-27, ~09:30 → 11:43) — **first emphasized transition**

1. **Object change:** *the Knower/Kernel relation* → **the epistemic primitives themselves** —
   from *who knows* to *what must be computationally closed*.
2. **Vocabulary change:** Ātman/Knower/chapter → closure, pathway, algebra, property, invariant.
   ⟦OBS⟧ The R3 vocabulary does not appear in R4's closure documents at all.
3. **Method change:** the decisive one — ⟦C⟧ *"close the epistemic primitives"* (MD-06) and
   ⟦C⟧ *"we do not choose mathematics because it looks useful; we test candidate mathematics
   against the semantics and invariants"* (MD-07). Analogy is replaced by **falsifiable property
   testing**.
4. **Surviving:** the three-pathway input insight (plural input universe, C-015); the Knower
   authority principle (silently — it re-emerges in 025h); Zero and Lord **as names**.
5. **Disappearing:** Ātman as an architectural term (until a trace in `025s`); scripture as source;
   ⟦OBS⟧ **Q17's role assignment** — R2 had ruled *"Zero is analytical, not transformational; Lord
   is analytical, not transformational"*; R4/R5 rebuild both as operational functions without citing
   Q17. **This is where CON-01/CON-02's displacement actually happens** — the transition, not the
   algebra documents, is the site of the semantic break.
6. **New evidence available:** the **property-test artifact** (EXP-01, CSV) — the corpus's first
   recorded test of anything.
7. **New epistemic risk:** formalization-by-stipulation — a definition can silently replace the
   concept it names (realized: CON-01, CON-02).
8. **Actually validated:** the negative result (no simple scalar suffices) + duplicate/corroboration
   property requirements + dependency-first ordering — **within the tested property set** (¬∃x∈T,
   not ¬∃x∈U, per the gate's standing principle).
9. **Unresolved:** selection between the two surviving algebras (OQ-11); whether the closure
   numbering conflict (CON-05) matters.

## T4 · R4 → R5 (2026-08-27, ~14:00) — **second emphasized transition**

1. **Object change:** *individual primitives* → **the whole system**, then (Step 109) **the actual
   repository**. R5 itself contains an internal object shift at Step 100/109: theory → reality.
2. **Vocabulary change:** closure/pathway → step, kernel, audit, conformance, drift, golden trace,
   vertical slice.
3. **Method change:** closure-per-primitive → **serialized construction with numbered steps**, then
   deliberate attack (Step 50: ⟦C⟧ *"stop extending the theory and try to break it"*), then
   evidence-register reconstruction (Step 109: `Observed/Inferred/Specified/Claimed`).
4. **Surviving:** R4's evidence algebras (into 025c/025n); the action/decision/Knower triad (into
   025g/025h with C-014 intact); the property-testing ethos (Step 50 generalizes EXP-01's
   adversarial stance).
5. **Disappearing:** ⟦OBS⟧ **all three R2 core objects** — atom: 0, dimension: 0, knowledge space: 0
   occurrences in the Step-49 reduction (EG-06). And **Knowledge itself is not among the 8
   primitives** — R1's opening hypothesis (*knowledge is probably not the Kernel object*) is
   vindicated by construction, unacknowledged.
6. **New evidence:** the 50-attack audit (EXP-02, survival-within-scope); Band-1/2 repository
   observations including the ⟦C⟧ `CRITICAL GOVERNANCE GAP` finding (Phase-2A P1).
7. **New epistemic risk:** **generative-rate production** (up to 76 docs/hour) — self-administered
   tests inside a single run (Step 100: 36/36 PASS) carry limited independent weight (PQ-02).
8. **Actually validated:** M₄₉ survives the tested attack set; the repository's directory topology
   and a real governance gap were observed. **Not validated:** rule-by-rule conformance, the
   self-verification suite, the implementation chain (EG-04/05).
9. **Unresolved:** everything in `phase-2a-findings.md` Band 3; the homeless Ω; the Lord naming
   decision.

## Cross-transition observations

- **The two semantic breaks (Zero, Lord) are located at T3**, not inside R5 — the algebras merely
  inherit the break. Any repair must therefore address the transition, not just rename a step.
- **Discipline documents cluster at transitions** (R3's corrections, R4's method declaration,
  Step 50, Step 109) — the corpus's self-correction instinct activates when the method changes.
- **Each transition abandons bookkeeping silently** (24 questions; R2 objects; freeze decisions).
  The corpus never writes a closure document for a regime. ⟦INT⟧ For the book: regime *endings* are
  reconstructions, not events the corpus recorded.
