## The four completeness conclusions, and the fifth (mandate §11 — never one answer)

**C1 · DERIVATION completeness.** Substantial and real. Σ derived-not-stored **by proof**; replay
proven **under KA4 + history validity**; the no-scalar result executed and independently confirmed;
`Q_t` computationally closed 10/10 and 7/7; the operation-necessity search executed by **real bounded
BFS**. **But** derivation is not uniform: qualification is not derived at all (*"a verifier
construction"*), measurement assigns a scale to a quantity **not shown measurable** (MT-5), and Σ's
minimality carries an **unreconciled same-date refutation** (AF-F-38). Three widely-cited results
compute over **hand-authored tables or loops that cannot fire** (AF-F-33, AF-F-34).

**C2 · DEFINITION completeness.** Weakest of the five. **≥26 mutually distinct knowledge-state
forms**; ≥12 Σ forms; three rival Evidence definitions; two non-identical lineage formulas; two
opposite identity repairs; `Q_t` binding **three** incompatible objects. **Absent outright:** state
equality, `Qualify`'s body, pre/postconditions, rejection semantics, persistence.

**C3 · ARCHITECTURAL completeness.** The governed surface is **small, coherent, and consists almost
entirely of constraints.** Present: the eight primitives (named, not typed), `K_t`, I-1…I-12, the
ladder + I-12, A6/I-4, DC, the interlock, policy stratification + I-11, `Committed`-as-status,
REJECTED/CONFLICTED via D-FA-1, the Art. 9 triple, Determination narrowly. **Absent:** every
construction those constraints presuppose — operations, transformations, identity, equality, lineage,
replay, measurement, Σ, `Q_t`, the Evidence object.

**C4 · GOVERNANCE completeness.** **Exactly one construct carries a real governance act: Policy**
(GN-19 / R-1 / I-11). Determination and Authority are authorized **narrowly**; REJECTED/CONFLICTED
are ratified **via D-FA-1 while occurring zero times in v0.2/v0.1**. Everything else: **NO ACT.**
Against that, research artifacts carry `Authority: HPA` ×12, `HPA Ruling` ×28/12, `RATIFIED` ×54,
`ACCEPTED` ×97 — **none with a ledger counterpart.** And the **Constitution's own status is
contradictory across three records**, which places the ground under Articles 3/4/6/7/8/9/11 in
question.

**C5 · IMPLEMENTATION READINESS — what an engineer can build without inventing semantics.**
> A state over the eight ratified primitives, carrying I-1…I-12 as constraints at their stated
> grades, enforcing the policy stratification (I-11) and the authority boundary (A6/I-4/DC.Auth),
> with evidence composition obeying I-5 and I-6.
> **Nothing that changes that state.** No legal operation and no legal transformation is canonically
> defined; postconditions are undecidable without state equality; and `commit` executes as the
> identity function.

## Does the programme need to return to DERIVATION?

**No — and this is the mandate's decisive question.** The remaining work is **definition,
architecture and governance**, in that dependency order. Derivations exist in abundance; what does
not exist is **a decision admitting any of them**, and in three cases a decision about which of two
same-date derivations stands (Σ 3-vs-4; identity's two repairs; policy in/out of `K`). The one
genuine derivation debt is **measurability prior to scale typing** (MT-5) — and it is not on the
critical path to operations.
