## The uniform pattern

**The governed surface supplies constraints and boundaries — I-5, I-6, I-12, A6, the non-collapse
distinctions, the eight primitive names — and no constructions.** Sharpest formulation on record:
*"the ratified canon defines when a transition would be ILLEGAL … without ever defining what a
transition IS."* The same shape holds for identity, equality, lineage, replay and measurement: each
is **required by something ratified and defined by nothing ratified.**

## The structural fact that no prior account states

**`Assertion` is not one of the eight ratified primitives.** The governed `K_t` carries
`Proposition`, `Relation` and `Observation` — and no `Assertion`. The verification lane's `K=(𝒜,ℛ)`
is built **on** `Assertion` and carries neither `Policy` nor `Action`. **The two lanes' state objects
do not share a base object**, and nothing in the record binds them. *(Registered as AF-F-42.)*
