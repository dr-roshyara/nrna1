## X · What primary-text evidence establishes

**X-1 · A6 and I-4, verbatim.** Primary source step 008 §A6 (PROPOSED-OPEN):
*"Authority determines commitment, not evidential truth."* Amended into v0.2 §1 by R-3
(AUTHORIZED): *"`Committed` is a decision-boundary status attached to an Accepted item **by
authority**. A6 … is now the **explicit boundary-crossing rule**, not an intra-ladder step."*
I-4 (AUTHORIZED): *"Authority determines commitment, not evidential truth — READ (008-A6)."*
Rendered RATIFIED in FA-1 §2 as `Candidate ⋖ Supported ⋖ Accepted ─ A6 boundary ─▶ Committed`.
**A6 occurs 0 times in FA-3 — no D-FA-n decision addresses it.** The only ratified artifact binding
A6 to a constitutional article is **FA-5, and it binds A6 to Article 3, not Article 8.**

**X-2 · Nothing crosses from evidence into authority.** Art. 3.1–3.2 (RATIFIED per the caveat
below): *"Evidence SHALL NOT constitute authority. Assessment SHALL NOT constitute authority. A
source SHALL NOT constitute authority."* · *"Authority SHALL be **assigned** — a recorded reference
to a human act — never intrinsic, never emergent from content."*

**X-3 · What evidence alone causes: exactly one rung.** `Candidate → Supported` is evidential;
`Supported → Accepted` is **gated on an in-force AcceptancePolicy**, and I-11 (AUTHORIZED) forbids
changing an in-force policy without a governed, versioned approval decision. Negative limits
(RATIFIED): Art. 9.2 *"Absence of evidence SHALL NOT pass"* · Art. 6.3 *"**Generation SHALL NOT be
justification**"* · Art. 6.4 *"Evidence SHALL NOT become a conclusion without a preserved
inference rule."*

**X-4 · An operation cannot produce an authority-bearing state by anything it computes.** Art. 4.2
*"Knowledge SHALL inform action; it SHALL **not execute** it"* · v0.1's concept row *"Authorization
— precondition constraint filled via governance, **never a processing step**"* · I-2 *"Proposal ≠
decision; the selector holds no authority."* **FORMAL-DERIVED:** authority may only be *recorded*
where an assigned act is among an operation's **inputs**.

**X-5 · Six transitions require an authority or governed act; only one has a rule.** The A6
crossing (AUTHORIZED) · reaching REJECTED · reaching CONFLICTED · resolving CONFLICTED (all
RATIFIED, FA-1 §2 / Art. 8.1) · changing an in-force policy (I-11 / Art. 2.2) · constitutional
amendment (Ch. V). **Only the A6 crossing has an explicit boundary rule and a position in a
covering relation.**

**X-6 · Article 8.3, verbatim and located.** Constitution v1.0, **line 92**, Art. 8 clause 3:
*"Resolution SHALL be forward-only: the conflict record SHALL survive resolution."*

**X-7 · ⚠ A CORRECTION TO THE COMMISSIONER'S OWN EARLIER REPORT.** The "parallel line" in

**X-8 · AF-F-32 verified verbatim, and sharpened.** Step 025a-2 §36: *"For the first reference

TemporallyValid ∧ SufficientSupport ∧ NoBlockingConflict ∧ ProvenanceAvailable`. This is

## Y · What is therefore constrained

**Y-1** Article 8.3 **does not constrain the A6 crossing**: both its clauses concern *resolution* of
CONFLICTED, and the only ratified A6↔article binding is to **Article 3**.

**Y-2 · Article 8.3 does not answer the return-rung question.** Two readings are textually
available. **Reading A (record-preservation)** is supported by the Constitution's own structure:
"forward-only" occurs exactly twice, and in **both** places the colon clause names *what must
survive* — 8.3 → *the conflict record*; 11.3 → *the old state … remain history*. RA v1.0's own
gloss reads it the same way (*"keeps the conflict record after resolution"* — no rung).
**Reading B (ordinal monotonicity)** has **no supporting clause**, and would require an L1 article
to constrain an **L2 rung ordering the Constitution never names** — the Constitution contains no
ladder at all — against FA-1 §1's ratified *"layers that must never be conflated."*
**FORMAL-DERIVED:** 8.3 constrains **the record, not the destination**. Marked inference; the
referent of a three-word phrase is an HPA matter, not an extractor's.

**Y-3 · The return-rung question is not an I-12 question at all.** FA-1 §2 scopes I-12 to the
admission axis; CONFLICTED sits off it. So the alleged `Candidate → Conflicted → Supported`
"I-12 bypass" is **an Article 8 question about what a governed resolution does** — and no ratified
text answers it. **Two questions have been running together and are separable.**

**Y-4 · AF-F-32 stands, with scope attached.** The crossing is real and reproducible **at the
PROPOSED-OPEN corpus layer**, by a rule its own author labelled *experimental, not final*. It is
**not** evidence that the ratified or authorized surface breaches A6. GN-29's rule governs:
*"archaeology outputs are historical evidence, never authority."*

**Y-5 · ⚠ A PROVENANCE CONTRADICTION THAT CONDITIONS EVERY CONSTITUTIONAL CITATION ABOVE.** Three
records disagree about the Constitution's status: **its own banner** — *"PROPOSED ·
EVIDENCE-BASED · NON-AUTHORITATIVE — pending HPA ratification"*; **GN-27/CF-003** — *the
Constitution WAS ratified, the defect is a stale banner only*; **the instrument GN-27 cites**
(`20260822-1028`, line 121) — *"Constitution v1.0: **PRODUCED, PROPOSED**."* OQ-10 is
ratified-as-open and *"still unperformed."* Every citation of Arts. 3/4/6/7/8/9/11 in this
document rests on the GN-27 determination plus FA-1 §1's parenthetical — **not** on the
Constitution's banner, and **not** on the 1028 record's own status list, which says the opposite.
**Reported, not resolved.**
