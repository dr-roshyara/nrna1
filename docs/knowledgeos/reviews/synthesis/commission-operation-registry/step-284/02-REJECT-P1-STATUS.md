## X · What primary-text evidence establishes

**X-1 · The ratified requirement, and its exact wording.** D-FA-1 (RATIFIED, GN-31) supplies the
non-admission states; FA-1 §2 states the reachability requirement verbatim:
> *"REJECTED/CONFLICTED are reachable from any pre-boundary status **by a governed act, never
> silently**; both are preserved states, not deletions."* · REJECTED = *"preserved failure state
> (Art. 7) [terminal, never deleted]"*.
Two precisions the earlier summary flattened: the ratified text **already types the reaching act as
*governed***, and **`REJECTED` does not occur in v0.2 at all** (0 occurrences of "reject" in v0.2
and v0.1) — it enters the Final Architecture **only** through D-FA-1, sourced [E] to Arts. 7–9.

**X-2 · I-12's ratified scope.** v0.2 §1/§4 (AUTHORIZED) state the covering relation and no-skip.
**FA-1 §2 (RATIFIED) scopes it:** *"no-skip (I-12) governs **the admission axis only**"* — and
places REJECTED and CONFLICTED **off** that axis.

**X-3 · Article 7 states no guard.** Verbatim: REJECTED must be *"preserved as explicit epistemic
states"*, *"never be knowledge"*, *"never silently discarded"*, and *"the history of error SHALL

## Y · What is therefore constrained

**Y-1** Any registry claiming to cover the ratified state model **must contain at least one
operation reaching REJECTED**, and that act must be **governed and never silent**. This is
derivable **now**, without P-1 — and it explains why `Reject` appeared in the core of all six
candidates without licensing any of them.

**Y-2 · The conflict AF-F-31 alleges does not obtain.** AF-F-31 reads *"its specification violates
I-12 and Article 8."* That presupposes §256.11 **is** a specification; **the primary text denies
that in its own words, twice** (X-4). And neither ratified constraint reaches: **I-12 is
RATIFIED-scoped to the admission axis** and REJECTED is off it (X-2); **Art. 8.1 demands a
governed resolution**, which FA-1 §2 independently already demands (X-1). Nothing ratified
authorizes an ungoverned `Conflicted → Rejected`.
**Operative classification: SEMANTIC UNDERDETERMINATION with a governance consequence — NOT a
conflict between ratified propositions.**

**Y-3 · The blockage is bounded, and the boundary is precise.** P-1 does **not** block the
*existence* of a REJECTED-reaching obligation (Y-1). It **does** block **every minimality,
uniqueness, core-membership and invariant-compatibility claim that quantifies over `Reject`'s
arity, codomain, source-status guard or authority-typing** — which, since `Reject` sits in the core
of all six candidates, is load-bearing for the entire family.
