## 1 · What an invariant is here — and five distinctions that govern this chapter — *status: fact [FA] + method [M]*

[FA] The twelve invariants are the constraints that hold the formal model together: each one
forbids a specific collapse the discovery corpus either suffered or deliberately attacked. They
are the model's counterpart of the constitutional articles — narrower, object-level, and carried
with explicit epistemic grades. [M] Five distinctions govern every sentence below, and the reader
should refuse any account of this architecture that blurs them: **an invariant EXISTS** (it is
ratified text) ≠ **an invariant is FORMALLY PROVEN** (none is — no consistency proof exists);
**STATED** ≠ **EXECUTABLE/TESTED** (two are computationally tested; three more execute at
boundary level; the rest are read-supported text); **absence of observed contradiction** ≠
**proof of consistency** (two adversarial passes found no violation — that is evidence, not
proof); **coherence** ≠ **completeness** (the set coheres; the disposition analysis lists what it
does not yet specify); **mathematical soundness** ≠ **computability** (sound constraints over
objects that are mostly not computationally realized). The chapter never uses the word this
programme forbids; its strongest available claim is the ruled formulation: *no demonstrated
contradiction or invariant violation; several formal under-specifications remain.*

## 2 · How to read the twelve — *status: method [M]*

[M] Every invariant below carries: its **ratified wording verbatim** (the D-2 rule — the grade
annotations included, exactly as the ratified table has them, a discipline this very chapter
failed once and was corrected under GN-45); meaning; why it exists; lineage; grade; what it
constrains and what it deliberately does not; a positive example from the running certification;
an attempted violation where the sources supply one; conformance status where established; and
open questions. Format note: the ratified table's row text is quoted inside ❝…❞; everything else
is exposition.

## 3 · The ownership and boundary invariants (I-1…I-4) — *status: definitions over ratified text [FA]*

**I-1** ❝The Knower owns problem, purpose, Ideal State and final authority❞ — grade ❝READ,
3-regime chain — the strongest❞. *Meaning:* the frame has exactly one owner (III.2). *Why:* every
ownerless design in the corpus failed in a recorded way. *Lineage:* 151244 · Q11/Q18 · 008-A6 —
the only invariant that crossed all three methodological regimes. *Constrains:* who may set G and
IdealState; where final authority terminates. *Does NOT constrain:* who does the epistemic labor
(fully delegable); who may BE a Knower (outside L2). *Positive:* the returning officer's standard
governs certification sufficiency. *Violation attempt:* let evidence pressure adjust the standard
— the 00:17 IdealState flaw is the recorded in-source failure of exactly that. *Conformance:*
PARTIAL (repository Knower is protected first-class; goal-ownership formal-side only — CF-012).
*Open:* frame equivalence (R2).

**I-2** ❝Proposal ≠ decision; the selector holds no authority❞ — grade ❝READ (Q17 + 025g/h)❞.
*Meaning:* what suggests never decides. *Why:* the Q17 deflation — the selector role is
analytical, not transformational. *Constrains:* every selector output to candidate status. *Does
NOT constrain:* selector quality (utility/risk models unratified — PF-8). *Positive:* the
selector's "run the tally comparison" binds nobody. *Violation attempt:* wire the selector's
output directly to execution — blocked twice (I-2 here; the Decision Interlock at L3). *Open:*
the MV-F-6 lift must preserve this invariant, and does by construction — but the lift itself is
undefined (taught in III.9 §8, pending disposition).

**I-3** ❝SufficientKnowledge ≠ ValidDecision ≠ AuthorizedAction❞ — grade ❝READ (042)❞. *Meaning:*
the three questions of 042 §42.1 never merge. *Why:* every shortcut a system takes is one of
these merges. *Constrains:* the whole decision chain's typing. *Does NOT constrain:* what
sufficiency IS (that is Q's business — and Q is exactly what PF-7 shows the ratified contract
lacks a carrier for; the invariant is threatened in EXPRESSIBILITY, not violated). *Positive:*
III.7 §10's counterfactual — abundant knowledge, inadmissible decision. *Violation attempt:*
"we know enough, so act" — the stress table (§11) runs it. *Conformance:* the strongest — the
six-way non-collapse in running code.

**I-4** ❝Authority determines commitment, not evidential truth❞ — grade ❝READ (008-A6)❞.
*Meaning:* A6, the model's sharpest line (III.6 §6). *Why:* 008 §21's argument — "a manager's
authority does not make evidence more truthful." *Constrains:* the Accepted→Committed crossing to
acts. *Does NOT constrain:* epistemic status (authority cannot make claims true — the same
sentence, other direction). *Positive:* the officer's signature, not the evidence volume,
certifies. *Violation attempt:* F-3 — the first synthesis stacked Committed as a fourth epistemic
rung; caught by falsification, repaired by ruling (R-3). *Conformance:* enacted (adoption ≠
authorization as separate acts; AST-019 held). *Open:* the untyped adjudication operator's I-4
relation (MV-F-10 — unexamined, no violation constructed).

## 4 · The tested pair (I-5, I-6) — *status: fact [FA←TESTED]*

**I-5** ❝Duplicate evidence must not increase confidence; independent corroboration must❞ — grade
❝**TESTED** (EXP-01)❞. **I-6** ❝Dependency resolution precedes aggregation❞ — grade ❝**TESTED**
(EXP-01)❞. *Meaning & why:* III.5's whole subject — the anti-amplification laws. *Lineage:* the
statistics lenses' intuitions → EXP-01's adversarial design → the recorded verdict → ratification;
then, uniquely in this list, **independent re-derivation**: the GN-46 audit re-implemented the
operators and reproduced the results, showing besides that scalar contradiction-preservation is
impossible (an impossibility argument, stronger than the original test) and that both laws are
**pipeline properties** — no operator alone carries them. *Constrains:* any evidence combination.
*Does NOT constrain:* operator choice (the ratified non-claim; OQ-3). *Positive:* the forty-one
items collapsing to one (III.5 §9). *Violation attempt:* the 100-duplicates attack — survives at
pipeline level; raw operators fail it, which is the point. *Conformance:* NO repository statement
of I-6 exists (OQ-12) — the model is ahead of its ecosystem here. *Open:* I-5 is well-defined
only relative to the undefined equivalence ~ (MV-F-22/R2); the TESTED grade describes a prose
record plus the new independent corroboration — system-level replication is OQ-5.

## 5 · The world and method invariants (I-7, I-8) — *status: fact [FA←E]*

**I-7** ❝X_t ≠ Observed(X_t) — model ≠ reality, structurally❞ — grade ❝READ (Step 66)❞.
*Source, verified:* 066's boxed `O_t ≠ X_t` with observation as `O_t ~ P(O_t | X_t)` — the
inequality is the survivor of Ω's dismantling (Part I.4). *Constrains:* every consumer of
observations; forbids the state ever standing in for the world. *Does NOT constrain:* how good
observation can get (it may approach; never equal-by-construction). *Positive:* the certification
never claims the election WAS clean — it claims the knowledge state satisfies the contract.
*Violation attempt:* "the dashboard is green, therefore the system is healthy" — the constitutional
twin (Art. 6.5) and this invariant both refuse it. *Open:* the world-symbol register (AF-F-12,
apparatus work).

**I-8** ❝No architectural assertion without evidence (method invariant)❞ — grade ❝READ (109),
partially executed❞. *Source, verified:* Step 109's boxed governing principle, with its
separation of Observed / Inferred / Specified / Claimed — and, the source adds, "Not yet
evidenced": five categories, not four (arity corrected under GN-54, AF-F-26) — **the direct
ancestor of this book's own grade discipline.** *Meaning:* the model polices its own assertions. *Why the
annotation says "partially executed":* 109 began an evidence-based reconstruction it did not
finish — the ratified grade records that honestly, and this chapter's first edition dropped that
annotation and was corrected (AF-F-7/GN-45; the wound is now part of the teaching). *Constrains:*
the meta-level — including this book. *Positive:* every status line in Part III. *Violation
attempt:* this chapter's own corrected census IS the recorded violation-and-repair. *Open:* none
beyond its own ongoing execution.

## 6 · The structure-preservation invariant (I-9) — with its finding, in the open — *status: fact [FA] + finding [M]*

**I-9** ❝Zero's four-way non-satisfaction typology must not collapse to Boolean❞ — grade
❝READ (025d)❞. *Meaning:* the gap is typed, never a bit and never a score (III.4 §9). *Why:* the
source's own Sat ≠ Boolean argument; one bit destroys the operational differences between
not-knowing, contest, absence-of-an-act, and invalidation. *Normative content:* independently

without concealment:* the descriptive clause "four-way" is **false of its cited source** — 025d's
satisfaction set is nine-valued, and the compression sits inside the invariant's own ratified
wording (AF-F-9 = MV-F-2, classified a wording-level demonstrated defect, pending an annotation
ruling; the load-bearing three of the compressed statuses — Insufficient, Stale, Prohibited — are
the PF-1 architectural candidate). *Constrains:* every representation of Zero's output. *Does NOT
constrain:* the exact arity of the typology — which is precisely what the pending disposition must
decide. *Positive:* III.4 §14's five-status vector and its two forbidden collapses. *Violation
attempt:* "readiness 0.62" — run and refuted there. *Open:* the PF-1/AF-F-9 disposition.

## 7 · The governance invariants (I-10, I-11) — *status: fact [FA] + lived history [E/M]*

**I-10** ❝Constitution changes require approval (governance)❞ — grade, verbatim including its
extraordinary annotation: ❝READ (042) — **violated in the real repo per Step 121**: live
finding❞. *Source, verified:* 042 §42.15's governance invariant `ProductionChange →
RequiredApproval`. *The annotation's history — the reader deserves the whole arc:* Step 121 found
the corpus's own constitution unversioned ("can be silently weakened" — the live finding the
ratified table preserves); the conformance pass later found the implementation side's mildest
cousin (a stale banner); and governance investigation then located the actual retrospective
ratification act (GN-27) — so the *repository-side* instance resolved, while the ratified
annotation stands as the historical record of the formal-side finding. Nothing here was rewritten
to look better; the table says "violated… live finding" because at ratification time that was the
truth, and dated later evidence sits beside it, not over it. *Constrains:* constitutional change
anywhere in the model's reach. *Positive/violation:* the four-sightings story (III.8 §1).

**I-11** ❝No in-force policy — AcceptancePolicy and constitution included — changes without a
governed, versioned approval decision❞ — grade ❝REQUIRED-BY-COHERENCE; corpus-adjacent (042
governance invariant + 121.47)❞. *Meaning:* the stratification's seal (III.8). *Why:* F-1 — the
first synthesis's admission machinery was governed by an ungoverned policy; the repair was argued
into the model under ruling, which is exactly what its RC grade announces. *Constrains:* the
content→force crossing. *Does NOT constrain:* single- vs multi-authority resolution — and there
sits its finding: under multiple applicable authorities, "THE in-force policy" is unique by
presupposition only (MV-F-7/PF-9 — evaluability threatened, not violated; the source's governance
algebra that would close it is mathematically sound and architecturally unauthorized). *Also
open:* the loop's base case (MV-F-8 — genesis) and its dynamics (AF-F-4). *Positive:* III.8 §9's
worked version transition. *Conformance:* content-level (Constitution Ch. V), with the L5 record
practicing the discipline the L2 text under-specifies.

## 8 · The order invariant (I-12) — *status: fact [FA/R]*

**I-12** ❝Status transitions follow the covering relation; no skipping❞ — grade
❝REQUIRED-BY-COHERENCE (one axiom)❞. *Meaning:* `Candidate ⋖ Supported ⋖ Accepted`, and

transition (the calculus — AF-F-3, open by design as policy content). *Positive:* the three

Accepted — refused at Determination; the covering relation executes in the reference
implementation — one of the boundary rules carrying a machine witness (with the tested pair, and
the executed A6 and Unknown→Block witnesses of §1's census; the earlier "the one invariant"
parenthetical was false and is corrected under GN-54, AF-F-25).
*Conformance:* enacted in the L5 record ("reached in order, by separate acts"; AST-019 held).

## 15 · Conclusion — Part III closes — *status: fact [FA], summary*

[FA] Part III ends where it began, but with everything visible: an architecture of one owner, one
contract, one typed gap, two tested evidence laws, a short ladder with a hard ceiling, a
conjunction that never averages, a policy that governs itself under guard, three kernels layered
without a crown — and twelve constraints whose grades, geneses, repairs, findings, and open edges
are now all on the page. The reader who wants the architecture has it; the reader who wants to
know exactly how much of it is tested, read, composed, ruled, or still open has that too — and
the distinction between those two readers is, in the end, the discipline this system exists to
protect. What remains for this book is the story of how all of it was found (Parts I–II), what it
implies (Part IV) — and, first, a review gate that will hold this Part to every standard it has
just taught.
