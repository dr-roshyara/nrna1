## 2 · Status vocabulary (GN-10 discipline)
Allowed: authorized · ratified · evidence-supported · conformant · adjudicated · resolved (only of
CF-001/CF-003) · open/deferred/held. Forbidden: **validated** (any conjugation, as status) ·
proven · final truth · "the architecture always…".

## 3 · Enforcement design
Each chapter's `claims.md` carries a terminology-conformance table; the BA-5 checklist runs a
mechanical grep for forbidden tokens (bare "kernel" in Part III, "validated", "Lord" outside
Part I/IV.4, unqualified second-sense usages) before a chapter may pass.
