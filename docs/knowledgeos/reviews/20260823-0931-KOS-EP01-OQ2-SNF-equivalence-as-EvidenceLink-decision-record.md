## 3 · The Port Contract's position at contract altitude (§Q8)

The Expression↔Meaning Port Contract answers the open remainder at the altitude where the question was deferred (Logical Architecture — the contract is that deliverable):

- **What a collision is:** when two distinct expressions normalize to the same candidate, the port reports a **candidate-equality observation** — in measurement vocabulary, a **collision**. **The collision rate is a critical failure metric** (HPA strong-evidence item 9), not a quality nicety.
- **Collisions are evidence, never admission** — *"they may feed an **EvidenceLink** (a candidate-side observation offered to an identity-assignment act — the contract's position on **OQ-2**, which remains subject to HPA rule), and they are **never** read as identity, truth, equivalence, or certainty."*
- **No state change:** collision reports accompany the candidate and are preserved; they **never by themselves change any knowledge state** — reporting a collision is **not a domain event** (⟨A-3⟩; nothing in the aggregate changes).
- **The contract's §6 record:** *"OQ-2 — may SNF-equivalence be recorded as an EvidenceLink? The contract's position: **yes, as a candidate-side evidence observation, never as the assignment itself** (Q8). **HPA rule still required.**"*
- **The SNF role line (contract §7 · invariant 6):** SNF equality therefore means *"a candidate for the same admitted meaning, offered to an authorized identity-assignment act, which may accept or refuse it (⟨C-1⟩). The SNF family may support identity **reasoning** (as evidence, §8); it never performs identity **assignment**."*

## 5 · The proposed ruling (RECOMMENDATION — the HPA decides)

The ruling is the HPA's alone (v1.1 §20 · OQ-2: *"the ruling remains the HPA's"*; the Port Contract §6: *"HPA rule still required"*). The options, with consequences:

| Option | Ruling | Consequences |
|---|---|---|
| **ACCEPT** *(recommended)* | **Yes** — SNF-equivalence may be recorded as an EvidenceLink supporting an identity-assignment act, **as a candidate-side evidence observation, never as the assignment itself** — subject to the two §Q8 conditions: (1) it is a **reference** (evidence references + acquisition method + reliability conditions), never the evidence content (⟨C-3⟩); (2) it **never by itself changes any knowledge state** and never assigns/deduces identity (obligations 1 · 4 · 6 · ⟨A-3⟩ · ⟨C-1⟩ ✅-route) | Records **OQ-2 RESOLVED** with the contract's §Q8 position ratified as the HPA's rule. **No architecture change** — no new invariant/state/member/event/register row · no mechanism · no v1.1 edit beyond a status annotation · no Constitution change · no Kernel authorization · register **25+4 unchanged** · the EvidenceLink path is confirmed safe (collisions feed evidence, never admission) |
| **REJECT** | **No** — SNF-equivalence may **not** be recorded as an EvidenceLink | The §Q8 position is overruled; the collision-reporting protocol is restricted to the candidate itself and **never feeds an EvidenceLink**. Consequences: a candidate-side evidence channel is closed; **every identity guard still holds** (the rejection is a *stricter* reading, so it also changes no architecture); recorded as a note; the §Q8 wording would need reconciliation at Port Contract ratification |
| **ACCEPT with modification** | **Yes**, with conditions beyond the two §Q8 conditions | Any added condition must **trace to an existing invariant** (no new law — G-7 discipline); the modification is recorded with its invariant trace; otherwise identical to ACCEPT |

**Recommendation rationale (evidence, not preference):** the §Q8 position is the **only** reading consistent with all existing law at once — it preserves the ⟨C-1⟩ ✅-route verbatim ("a candidate for the same admitted meaning, offered to an authorized identity-assignment act"), keeps EvidenceLink as reference-only (⟨C-3⟩), keeps admission through the Verification Port alone (INV-KOS-VERIFICATION-001), and keeps the collision's non-state-changing nature (⟨A-3⟩). REJECT would close a channel the architecture already renders safe; ACCEPT-with-modification would add conditions to an already-guarded position. ACCEPT is therefore the minimal, law-consistent ruling.
