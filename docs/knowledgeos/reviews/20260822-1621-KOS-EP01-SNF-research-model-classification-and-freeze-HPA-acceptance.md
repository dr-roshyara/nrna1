## 0 · The act (HPA, in substance)

> *"I would therefore accept Claude's architectural direction and freeze our current SNF formula as a research candidate rather than refine it further right now. The formula can be revisited after the port contract is ratified."*

Three decisions in one act:

1. **ACCEPT the architectural direction** — *"the port, not SNF, is architectural"* is the architectural consequence of the whole SNF measurement exercise. The chain no longer asks *"what is the best SNF formula?"* but the much better architectural question: *"what must any semantic mechanism prove before KnowledgeOS is willing to admit its output?"*
2. **CLASSIFY the formula** — the SNF formula discussed so far is **SNF Measurement Research Model v0.x**, **not** a *"KnowledgeOS Architecture Formula."* The architecture does not need to know which semantic theory wins.
3. **FREEZE the current formula** as a research candidate. No further refinement now. Revisit **after the port contract is ratified**.

**The two separate questions (HPA):**

| Question | When | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| **Architectural** — what must *any* semantic mechanism provide when crossing into KnowledgeOS? | **now** | the **Expression↔Meaning Port Contract** |
| **Research** — which mechanism provides those obligations *best*? | **later** | SNF formula · entropy · NSID · Sanskrit/Pāṇinian-inspired grammar · Bayesian interpretation · … |

> *"That separation is excellent DDD."*

**Reference to the current formula (the HPA's pointer):** the current SNF formula lives in the research artifact `docs/knowledgeos/brainstorming/# Semantic Normal Form (SNF) Formula: Re` (untracked, main checkout) — a research review and improvement analysis of the formula's components (Gödel fingerprint · semantic entropy · convergence · non-collapse · transformation stability), with refinement and senior-mathematical-review sections. Its final discipline statements (SNF should not be one giant formula · reject a single composite SNF score · the measurement framework with convergence · non-collapse · false collapse · stability · abstention) **corroborate** the architecture's §15/§17/§19 and the Port Contract's Q8 — this instrument's job is to classify the formula at the research altitude, not to evaluate its content.
