## 4 · What this supplies for `R3`

⛔ **The ARB rules `R3`; this only supplies evidence.**

The ARB asked whether the two verdict sets are *the same concept, different bounded-context concepts, or one superseding the other.* Three observations bear on it:

1. **Different questions.** `Verdict` answers *did a mechanical check pass?* — `PASS/FAIL/WARN/INCONCLUSIVE`. `AssessmentService` answers *did an accepted recommendation measurably help?* — `SUPPORTED/PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED/NOT_SUPPORTED/INCONCLUSIVE`.
2. **Different authority postures.** `Verdict` **reserves** three values to human review acts and enforces the reservation. `AssessmentService` reserves nothing — all four values are machine-emitted from thresholds (`MIN_COMMITS=3` · `≥20%` · `≥2%`).
3. **No shared type, no shared import, no shared guard.**

> ⭐ **The evidence points toward *different bounded-context concepts*, not a collision** — which is the reading the ARB's own DDD framing anticipated: *same word does not imply same concept.*
>
> ⚠️ **But that is `INFERRED`, and the ARB rules it.** The one thing the evidence does *not* support is a blind merge — `Verdict`'s reservation of `CERTIFIED`/`EMERGENT`/`PASS AFTER CORRECTION` to human acts would be lost in any union with a set that reserves nothing.
