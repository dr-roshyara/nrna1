🔴 Category 2: Token Inconsistency

Some pages use neutral-*, others use gray-* — these are DIFFERENT colors

✅ neutral-* (Login, Admin, Election)

Slate-based neutrals (Tailwind gray-900 mapped to neutral)

❌ gray-* (Committee, old pages)

Cool-gray neutrals — warmer, pink-ish tint vs slate-blue

Impact: text-neutral-600 is #475569 (slate). text-gray-600 is #4b5563 (cool gray). They look different side-by-side. Pages using gray-* appear warmer, pages using neutral-* cooler.
